AI Video Production for Brands — How It Works

Tabiji Media Studio is an AI-powered short-form video production service built for brands that need consistent, high-quality content without the cost and complexity of traditional production. We run a fully automated pipeline that goes from brief to finished file in 24–48 hours — handling everything from cinematic video generation and branded text overlays to custom AI-composed music and cross-platform formatting.

Our pipeline is built on the best AI models available today: Google Veo 3 for cinematic video generation, Gemini for visual concept ideation and image creation, MiniMax Hailuo for alternative video styles, and a custom AI music generation layer for background audio. Every model in the stack was evaluated head-to-head across dozens of real productions — we only use what consistently delivers results at scale. The output looks like a film crew showed up, without the crew, the travel budget, or the six-week lead time.

Tabiji Media Studio is designed for brands, agencies, and content teams that need volume without sacrificing quality. Whether you're a travel brand posting daily Reels, an e-commerce brand launching seasonal campaigns, or a creator studio delivering content for multiple clients, our pipeline scales with you. You send us a brief. We deliver ready-to-post files. That's it.

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about AI video production.

Tabiji Media Studio offers content packages starting at $1,500/month. Pricing scales based on volume (number of videos per month), platforms, and content complexity. At that starting price point, you get significantly more content than you'd get from a traditional production agency or freelancer — and it's delivered in 24–48 hours rather than weeks. We discuss exact pricing during your discovery call so we can match a package to your specific needs and volume. Fill out the form below to get started.

We use a curated stack of the best AI models available, selected after extensive head-to-head testing. For video generation, we primarily use Google Veo 3 — currently the leading model for cinematic, realistic short-form video. For image creation and concept generation, we use Gemini (Google's image generation via Imagen). For alternative video styles and motion effects, we use MiniMax Hailuo. For background audio, we use a custom AI music generation pipeline that produces original, royalty-free tracks tuned to the mood and pacing of each video. Every tool in our stack was chosen because it produces the best real-world results — not just the best benchmark scores.

Most content is delivered within 24–48 hours of receiving your brief. For standard short-form video packages, we typically turn around the first batch the next business day. Larger volume orders or campaigns with multiple brand guidelines may take 48–72 hours for the initial delivery. After the first batch, you'll also have an established brief template that makes future orders faster. This is one of the biggest advantages over traditional production — no location scouting, no crew scheduling, no post-production delays.

We deliver ready-to-post files directly to you — optimized for each platform's specs (aspect ratio, resolution, format). You maintain full control of your social media accounts. We don't require access to your accounts, and there's no proprietary platform lock-in. Files are delivered via a shared link or directly to your email. Each file includes the correct format for Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, X, and Pinterest, so you can post across all platforms without any additional editing.

Traditional video production requires a crew, physical locations, scheduling, filming days, and a lengthy post-production process. A single short-form video can take 1–4 weeks and cost thousands of dollars. AI video production eliminates all of that. Our pipeline generates cinematic visuals entirely from text prompts and parameters — no crew, no travel, no physical shoot required. The result is 10x faster delivery, a fraction of the cost, and the ability to produce high volumes of consistent content on demand. The trade-off is that AI video works best for certain styles (cinematic, lifestyle, travel, product showcases) and isn't a replacement for content that requires real human presence or documentary footage. For brands that need consistent volume, AI video is a no-brainer.

Our primary focus is travel and lifestyle content — that's where our pipeline is most proven, and it's the niche we built Tabiji in. Beyond travel, we create content for food and restaurant brands, real estate (property tours and neighborhood highlights), e-commerce (product showcases and lifestyle campaigns), wellness and fitness brands, hospitality, and agencies managing content for multiple clients. If your brand relies on visually engaging short-form video, our pipeline can adapt to your vertical. Reach out and we'll let you know if we're a good fit for your specific category.
