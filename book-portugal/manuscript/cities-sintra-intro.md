Sintra — UNESCO palace town 40 minutes from Lisbon by CP urban train, host to Pena Palace, Quinta da Regaleira, Castelo dos Mouros, and Monserrate. Seven scams affect nearly every day-tripper. The Headout and clone-reseller "skip-the-line" ticket fraud is the canonical 2025 Sintra scam, documented in r/Europetravel's "Is Headout legit for Skip the line in Sintra?" thread. Tuk-tuk "full palace tour" overcharges run €60+ when the Scotturb 434 bus day-pass is €13.50. Lisbon-based bus-tour packages bundle Sintra with commission-driven shopping stops at Cabo da Roca. Sintra town restaurant tourist-menu inflation on Volta do Duche, Pena + Quinta da Regaleira "combined pass" markup, Booking.com property-inbox phishing targeting Sintra guesthouses, and unlicensed "guide" pressure at each palace entrance round out the seven.
