If you are victimized, consult local authorities (dial **112** for emergency services nationwide; **PSP Turismo Lisbon** at **+351 21 342 1634** for tourist-police assistance 24/7 with English support; your nearest PSP or GNR station for denuncia filing — direct phone numbers are listed in Appendix C), your embassy or consulate, your credit card issuer, and your travel insurance provider. Do not rely on this book as your sole source of guidance.

**A special note on Multibanco ATMs and card payments.** Portugal's domestic bank-ATM network is called **Multibanco**, operated by SIBS. Standalone machines branded **Euronet** or **Travelex** in tourist areas are NOT bank ATMs — they are independent processors that charge 8–15% "Dynamic Currency Conversion" (DCC) fees and are the primary sites for skimming-device installation. Throughout this book we will repeatedly recommend that you use Multibanco machines inside bank branches (Caixa Geral de Depósitos, Millennium BCP, Santander, Novo Banco, BPI) during business hours, and that you pay by credit card — not bank transfer, not WhatsApp link, not off-platform "deposit" — for any accommodation, excursion, or tour. Your credit card's 30–60 day chargeback window is the single most important consumer-protection tool for travel in Portugal.
