Nazaré — the Silver Coast surf town famous for 80-foot winter big waves at Praia do Norte. Six scams catch both day-trippers from Lisbon and longer-stay big-wave-season visitors. The dominant 2025 Nazaré story is logistical: **the Ascensor da Nazaré funicular, which connected Praia beach to Sítio cliff top since 1889, was closed indefinitely in 2025 following the September 2025 Lisbon Elevador da Glória derailment that killed 15 people**. Taxi drivers, tuk-tuk operators, and unlicensed "shuttle" vans are now exploiting the resulting transport vacuum with inflated Praia-to-Sítio fares. The Sítio photo-tout and "seven skirts" widow-costume scam, Nazaré beachfront restaurant fresh-fish weighing trap, Praia do Norte big-wave-viewing unofficial-parking scam, Nazaré accommodation fraud (Booking.com phishing + Idealista SEPA bait), and Lisbon-based Nazaré day-trip package commission-bundling with Óbidos and Fátima complete the Nazaré six.
