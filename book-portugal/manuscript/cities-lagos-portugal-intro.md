Lagos (Portugal) — the Algarve's westernmost major resort, famous for the sandstone sea caves at Ponta da Piedade. Six scams affect both independent travelers and day-trippers arriving from Faro Airport or Portimão cruise port. The Ponta da Piedade grotto-boat-tour reseller markup — where Old Town tour offices and cliff-top touts price €15–€25 marina-direct tours at €40–€70 — is the flagship Lagos scam documented in r/travel's "10 days in portugal — a review" 2025 thread. The "fake charity / orphans perfume" street scam, Old Town couvert and tourist-menu inflation, FAO-to-Lagos transfer overcharge (€160–€220 when Bolt is €100–€120), Rua Cândido dos Reis bar-strip drink-tout pressure, and Klass Wagen fraud at the Lagos depot complete the six-scam Lagos pattern.
