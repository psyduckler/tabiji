Lisbon — Portugal's flagship city and the densest scam ecosystem in the country. Seven specific scams trip up visitors with reliable regularity, and the Tram 28 pickpocket-team pattern in particular is the single most-reported tourist incident in Portugal. Every scam below has been documented by Lisbon PSP Turismo or by the Portuguese-language r/lisboa and r/LisbonPortugalTravel communities in 2024–2026. Lisbon Airport (LIS) taxi overcharges, Alfama and Baixa restaurant couvert inflation, tuk-tuk "full city tour" commission bundling, fado-house commission touts, Metro ticket-machine "helper" distraction scams, and the Rossio counterfeit-drug tout scheme round out the top seven.
