Cascais — a wealthy coastal town 30 km west of Lisbon, reachable by a €2.40 CP urban train ride in 40 minutes. Six scams affect Lisbon day-trippers and longer-stay visitors. The Lisbon-Cascais day-trip bus-tour bundling — which compresses Cascais into a 30-minute marina photo stop inside a rushed "Sintra + Cascais + Cabo da Roca" itinerary — is the most commonly flagged Cascais scam, per r/Lisbon's 2025 "Daily trip Cascais" thread where locals overwhelmingly recommend the train-first approach. Cascais Marina boat-tour reseller overcharge, Cascais restaurant tourist-menu inflation at Baía marina-adjacent restaurants, Cascais-Lisbon train-and-taxi transfer scams, Praia do Guincho unofficial-parking-attendant scams, and Cascais accommodation phishing round out the six.
