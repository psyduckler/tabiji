Coimbra — UNESCO university city 180 km north of Lisbon, home to the University of Coimbra (founded 1290) and the Joanina Library (built 1717). Six scams affect both Lisbon-to-Porto day-trippers and longer-stay visitors. The University of Coimbra "combined ticket" reseller markup — where Google Ads-driven third-party sites inflate €15 genuine tickets to €25–€45 — is the flagship Coimbra scam. Baixa de Coimbra restaurant couvert and tourist-menu inflation, Coimbra-B train-station taxi / Uber overcharge (the "Aggressive Uber driver Coimbra" 2025 r/portugal anchor documents a 9-minute ride that escalated to cash-demand aggression), fado de Coimbra "show + dinner" combined-ticket markup, Coimbra accommodation phishing + student-housing fraud, and the Baixa + Coimbra-B + university-hill pickpocket hotspot pattern round out the six.
