# The Scams: City by City {-}

The chapters that follow document every scam reported in ten Portuguese destinations. Cities are ordered by how most visitors actually travel: starting with Lisbon, the flagship destination and densest scam ecosystem, then up to Porto, out to the UNESCO palace town of Sintra as a day trip, south to the Madeira archipelago capital at Funchal, across the Algarve coast from Faro through Albufeira to Lagos, back along the Lisbon Riviera to Cascais, inland to the UNESCO university city of Coimbra, and finishing at the big-wave surf town of Nazaré on the Silver Coast.

If you're hitting only a few of these, skip to the chapter you need. If you're doing a longer loop, skim them all — the same core scams recur with local variations. The LIS / FAO / FNC airport "meter-off" taxi pattern runs identically at every international arrivals hall. The Booking.com property-inbox phishing pattern runs at every Portuguese accommodation city. The couvert + tourist-menu inflation runs in every restaurant chapter in this book. The Klass Wagen duct-taped-car damage-claim pattern runs at Faro Airport and extends to Lagos and Lisbon depots. Learn the pattern in one city, and you'll spot it the instant you arrive in the next.

## The Ten Cities at a Glance {-}

**Lisbon** — The flagship. The Tram 28 team-based pickpocket ring along the Alfama / Portas do Sol / Sé loop (the most pickpocket-heavy tourist route in Portugal); the LIS airport "meter-off fixed price" €40–€80 taxi quote that Bolt charges €12–€16; the Alfama and Baixa couvert + tourist-menu inflation that runs higher-volume than anywhere else in Portugal; the Praça do Comércio tuk-tuk tout that inflates a €6.80 day-pass route into a €120–€200 "full city tour"; the fado-house commission tout that prices a genuine €14–€22 concert at €65–€85 through hotel concierges; the Metro ticket-machine "helper" scam that either skims cards or pockets change; and the Rossio / Restauradores late-night drug-tout scam that sells fake hashish and follows up with a fake "plainclothes police" shakedown.

**Porto** — Portugal's second city and its wine-export capital. The Vila Nova de Gaia port-cellar commission upsell that prices €15–€25 direct tastings at €60–€150 through Old Town tour offices; the Praça da Ribeira tuk-tuk "no-fixed-route" overcharge; the Euronet / Travelex ATM skimming pattern (the rule: use Multibanco inside bank branches); the Ribeira restaurant couvert and francesinha markup (€18–€28 waterfront versus €13–€15 at the Café Santiago original); the Livraria Lello "skip-the-line" reseller markup (the real €8 ticket is redeemable against any book); the Porto fado-show commission pattern; and the Douro riverboat cruise hidden-extras + Ribeira accommodation off-platform fraud.

**Sintra** — UNESCO palace town 40 minutes from Lisbon by CP urban train. The Headout-and-clone-reseller "skip-the-line" ticket fraud that inflates €15 Pena Palace tickets to €35–€55; the tuk-tuk "full palace tour" overcharge at €60+ when the Scotturb 434 bus day-pass is €13.50; the Lisbon-based bus-tour package that bundles Sintra with Cabo da Roca and a commission-driven shopping stop at 40% of time in Sintra; the Volta do Duche restaurant tourist-menu inflation that adds silent couvert charges and inflated "traditional" pricing; the Pena Palace + Quinta da Regaleira "combined pass" that is 30–80% above direct booking; the Booking.com property-inbox phishing that targets Sintra guesthouses; and the unlicensed "guide" pressure at each palace entrance.

**Funchal** — Madeira's capital, one of Portugal's top cruise-ship destinations. The Mercado dos Lavradores fruit-vendor overcharge that bills €17–€72 for produce worth €3–€8 at Pingo Doce; the Funchal taxi overcharge on the Monte-Funchal corridor (€20 quoted, €13.50 Bolt); the FNC airport rental-car damage-claim pattern; the levada hiking tour reseller markup that charges for access to legally-free public trails via the Madeira.fun aggregator; the cruise-day excursion shopping-stop bundling; the Monte toboggan photo-operator surprise surcharges; and the Funchal Old Town "free poncha" tout pressure.

**Faro** — The Algarve's main gateway airport and a walled-old-town destination in its own right. The Klass Wagen damage-claim + rigged-fuel-gauge rental scam (the canonical 2025 r/Algarve anchor "well-rehearsed scam"); the FAO airport "entry-point transfer" scam with sign-holders outside arrivals; the Faro Old Town couvert + "fresh fish market price" weigh-and-bill pattern; the Ria Formosa boat-tour reseller markup with operator shell games; the Portugal-wide caller-ID spoofing scam that targets tourists mid-trip; the Faro pickpocket and Euronet / Travelex ATM skimming pattern; and the Algarve Via Verde toll double-billing trick on rental-car contracts.

**Albufeira** — The Algarve's most concentrated package-tourism resort. The Oura Strip "public relations" scratchcard + bar-ushering scheme (canonical 2025 r/portugal anchor "Possible scam and snowball effect?"); the Albufeira Old Town pickpocket team pattern; the British-pub-strip restaurant tourist-menu inflation; the Vilamoura / Algarve timeshare presentation pressure (regulated under Decreto-Lei 37/2011 with a 14-day cooling-off); the FAO-to-Albufeira transfer overcharge at €80–€120 when Bolt is €45–€55; and the beach-operator jet-ski damage-deposit fraud pattern.

**Lagos (Portugal)** — The Algarve's westernmost major resort, famous for the sea caves at Ponta da Piedade. The Ponta da Piedade grotto-boat-tour reseller markup at €40–€70 when marina-direct operators charge €15–€25; the "fake charity / orphans perfume" street scam on Praça Gil Eanes; the Old Town couvert + tourist-menu inflation on Rua 25 de Abril; the FAO-to-Lagos transfer overcharge at €160–€220 when Bolt is €100–€120; the Lagos bar-strip (Rua Cândido dos Reis) drink-tout pressure and card-skimming risk; and the Klass Wagen rental-car fraud at the Lagos depot (same operator, same duct-tape pattern as Faro).

**Cascais** — A wealthy coastal town 30 km west of Lisbon on the CP urban train. The Lisbon-Cascais day-trip bus-tour bundling that compresses Cascais into a 30-minute marina photo stop; the Cascais Marina boat-tour reseller overcharge; the Cascais restaurant tourist-menu inflation, particularly at marina-adjacent Baía restaurants; the Cascais-Lisbon train / taxi day-trip transfer scam at the CP Cais do Sodré and Cascais stations; the Praia do Guincho unofficial-parking-attendant scam; and the Cascais accommodation phishing + Booking.com off-platform fraud.

**Coimbra** — UNESCO university city 180 km north of Lisbon. The University of Coimbra "combined ticket" reseller markup that inflates €15 genuine tickets to €25–€45 through third-party sites; the Baixa de Coimbra restaurant couvert and tourist-menu inflation; the Coimbra-B train-station taxi / Uber overcharge (the infamous 2025 r/portugal "Aggressive Uber driver Coimbra" anchor); the fado de Coimbra "show + dinner" combined-ticket markup; the Coimbra accommodation phishing + Booking.com off-platform fraud; and the Baixa + Coimbra-B + university-hill pickpocket hotspot pattern.

**Nazaré** — The Silver Coast surf town famous for 80-foot big waves at Praia do Norte. The Sítio da Nazaré transport overcharge following the 2025 closure of the 1889 Ascensor da Nazaré funicular; the Sítio photo-tout and "seven skirts" widow-costume scam; the Nazaré beachfront restaurant tourist-menu inflation (fresh-fish weighing trap); the Praia do Norte big-wave-viewing unofficial-parking "attendant" scam; the Nazaré accommodation fraud via Booking.com phishing and Idealista SEPA-transfer bait; and the Lisbon-based Nazaré day-trip package that bundles with Óbidos and Fátima commission-driven shopping stops.
