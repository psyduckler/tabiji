Funchal — Madeira's capital and one of Portugal's top cruise-ship destinations, home to the art-deco Mercado dos Lavradores, the Monte toboggan ride, and the island's 400+ km levada-hiking network. Seven scams catch cruise-day tourists and independent Madeira visitors. The Mercado dos Lavradores fruit-vendor overcharge is the canonical Funchal scam — r/Madeira has documented €17–€72 bills for handfuls of fruit that cost €3–€8 at any Pingo Doce. Funchal taxi overcharge on the Monte-Funchal corridor, Funchal Airport (FNC) rental-car damage claims (Madeira Rent-a-Car / RentCarMadeira flagged specifically), levada hiking-tour reseller markup via the Madeira.fun aggregator, cruise-day shopping-stop bundling, Monte toboggan photo-operator surcharges, and Old Town "free poncha" tout pressure complete the Funchal ecosystem.
