Melaka is the UNESCO heritage capital of the straits, a two-hour drive south of KL that packs six centuries of Portuguese, Dutch, and British colonial layering into a riverfront grid centered on Christ Church Melaka, the Stadthuys, and the ruined A'Famosa gate. Jonker Street runs the Friday-to-Sunday Jonker Walk night market through the old Chinatown; the Menara Taming Sari gyro-tower spins slowly above it; and the Melaka River Cruise threads the painted shophouse walls from Taman Rempah to Kampung Morten. The scam density tracks the foot traffic. The QR-code receipt fake-payment scam hit Melaka Tengah in April 2026, with police arresting operators who showed forged PayNet screens to tour-group leaders collecting cash for "settled" bills. The Hello Kitty trishaw is the city's most photographed vehicle and its most reliable bait-and-switch, with quoted RM 40 rides escalating to RM 150 "per person per landmark" once the pedaling starts. Jonker Street food and durian stalls overcharge by weight-switch and uncalibrated scales; unofficial Melaka River Cruise touts sell already-used tickets at the Muara jetty; Spider-Man and Frog costumed beggars around Dutch Square were tied to a February 2026 child-exploitation ring; and the Melaka police chief issued an April 2026 warning about fake-donation charity solicitors working Jonker Walk with laminated NGO badges.
