Langkawi is a duty-free archipelago of 99 islands in the Andaman Sea off the Kedah coast, Langkawi International Airport (LGK) on the main island feeding the Pantai Cenang beach strip and the Kuah ferry terminal handling the passenger runs to Penang and Kuala Perlis. Eagle Square watches over the Kuah waterfront; the Oriental Village at the base of Gunung Mat Cincang holds the SkyCab cable car that climbs to the Langkawi Sky Bridge; the Kilim Geopark runs mangrove-and-eagle-feeding boat tours out of Tanjung Rhu; and Pulau Payar Marine Park pulls the day-trip snorkel crowd. The scam density is highest on the beach and at the airport. The Pantai Cenang jet-ski damage-compensation shakedown — PRC visitors charged thousands of ringgit for pre-existing scratches on rental skis — went viral in November 2025. LGK coupon-taxis quote inflated one-way rates from the terminal, and the May 2025 LIMA 25 airshow drew a wave of fake-discount booking scams. August 2025 police raids uncovered duty-free counterfeit alcohol alongside fake RM 100 notes in Kuah change circulation; SkyCab and Sky Bridge ticket touts outside Oriental Village sell already-scanned QR codes; Pantai Cenang beach hawkers run pressure-upsell massage, henna, and hair-braid routines; and island-hopping operators bait-and-switch boats mid-trip, with September and October 2025 drownings traced to unlicensed vessels.
