Information is current as of **2026 Edition**. For newly reported scams and live updates, scan the QR code on the back cover or visit **[tabiji.ai/scams](https://tabiji.ai/scams)**.

If you are victimized, consult local authorities — dial **999** for emergency services nationwide; **997** for the NSRC (National Scam Response Centre) to report financial and online scams; **1300-88-5050** for the MOTAC Tourist Helpline; file a PDRM *laporan polis* in person at the nearest *balai polis*; file a KPDN consumer complaint at **eaduan.kpdn.gov.my**; and contact Bank Negara Malaysia's BNMTELELINK at **1-300-88-5465** for financial-sector disputes — as well as your embassy or consulate, your credit card issuer, and your travel insurance provider.
