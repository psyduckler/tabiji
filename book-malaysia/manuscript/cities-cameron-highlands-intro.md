Cameron Highlands is Malaysia's colonial-era hill station, a three-and-a-half-hour climb from KL via the Tapah or Simpang Pulai turnoffs, where the thermometer drops into the high teens and the hillsides roll in green terraces of tea and strawberries. Tanah Rata is the backpacker hub; Brinchang holds the night market and the bus station; the BOH Sungai Palas and BOH Habu tea estates run the two photogenic factory tours; the Mossy Forest crowns Gunung Brinchang at 2,032 meters; and Big Red Strawberry Farm and Raju's Hill pull the Instagram trade. The scams cluster around the tour-van economy. The Tanah Rata tour-van RM 1,470 overcharge, surfaced on AsiaOne in viral form, showed a family quoted RM 100 and billed fourteen times the figure at the Mossy Forest turnaround. A December 2025 JPJ operation seized school buses ferrying tourists on illegal charter runs between Tapah and Brinchang. Strawberry farms switch per-kg prices between the entrance sign and the checkout counter; unofficial "conservation fees" of RM 30–50 are collected at the Mossy Forest entrance by touts in lookalike SPFN vests; Brinchang homestay impersonation — fake listings under the Heritage Hotel Cameron Highlands name — drew an April 2026 property-owner alert; and unofficial BOH Sungai Palas guides offer "factory insider tours" at RM 80 that the estate itself runs free of charge.
