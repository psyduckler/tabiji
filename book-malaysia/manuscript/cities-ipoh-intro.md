Ipoh is the Perak state capital and the midway stop on the KL-Penang ETS rail corridor, a city built on tin money and sustained by a food scene that draws Singaporeans up for the weekend. The ETS Ipoh Railway Station — Ipoh's "Taj Mahal" in white Moorish plaster — opens onto Old Town with its kopitiam shophouses, Concubine Lane (Lorong Panglima) with its crowded photo-wall alleys, and New Town with AEON Ipoh Station 18 and the Kinta Riverfront walk. The limestone hills ring the city with cave temples — Kek Lok Tong, Sam Poh Tong, Perak Tong — and the Gua Tempurung show-cave sits 25 km south toward Gopeng, while Kellie's Castle moulders out past Batu Gajah. The scam profile is split between tourist-strip hustles and the phone channel. Unlicensed photographers around Concubine Lane pressure visitors into RM 800 "photo-fee" settlements after snapping unasked. October 2025 brought a PRC-linked distraction-theft ring working AEON Ipoh Station 18 with coordinated bump-and-lift teams. The Macau-scam fake-PDRM phone impersonation hit an Ipoh trader for RM 1.2 million in 2025 — callers posing as Bukit Aman officers alleging money-laundering warrants. Concubine Lane food stalls run tourist-price menus; November 2025 saw a fake-traffic-police conviction for roadside "fine" extraction near Gunung Rapat; and cave temple donation touts work Kek Lok Tong and Gua Tempurung with laminated receipt books.
