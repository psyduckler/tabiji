Kota Kinabalu is Sabah's state capital, Kota Kinabalu International Airport (KKIA / BKI) landing the dive-and-mountain traffic on Borneo's northwest coast at a city hemmed between the South China Sea and the 4,095-meter bulk of Mt Kinabalu. Jesselton Point ferry terminal shuttles day-trippers out to Tunku Abdul Rahman Marine Park — Gaya, Sapi, Manukan, and Mamutik islands — and Gaya Street hosts the Sunday tamu market under Signal Hill. Suria Sabah anchors the mall circuit, the Waterfront Esplanade runs the sunset bar strip, and the onward routes lead east: Sepilok for orangutans, Sandakan for the Kinabatangan River, and Semporna for the Sipadan dive boats. The scam ecosystem tracks Sabah's premium adventure tourism. Mt Kinabalu fake climbing-packages cost one victim RM 18,000 in 2025 — Sabah Parks is the only official permit channel, and every other "guaranteed summit" booking is a resale at best. KKIA airport taxi-coupon and Grab-cancellation routines match the Peninsular pattern. Jesselton Point illegal island-hopping touts drew November 2025 Borneo Post coverage and arrests for unlicensed boat operation. The Semporna lobster-switch — live lobster quoted, frozen substitute billed — went viral in October 2025; KK-wide online-fraud losses totaled RM 11.1 million in Q1 2026; and Sabah interior day-trips to Kiulu, Tuaran, and Kundasang mark up van-and-driver rates by 200% on walk-in bookings.
