Halifax is Nova Scotia's capital, a major East Coast cruise-ship port, and the Maritime gateway to Peggy's Cove, Mahone Bay, and Lunenburg. The scam ecosystem is almost entirely cruise-day-adjacent: most Halifax tourists arrive by ship for a single-day visit, and the short 6–8-hour window between embarkation and departure is what the scam ecosystem targets. The *Halifax cruise-day shore-excursion markup* is the flagship: tout-operators at the Halifax Seaport cruise terminal sell "Peggy's Cove + Mahone Bay + Cape Breton day tours" at 180–240 CAD per person for routes that the Halifax Waterfront Tourism Bureau (on the boardwalk, a 5-minute walk from the cruise terminal) sells at 90–130 CAD per person on the same buses. The defense is to walk past the pier touts and book at the Tourism Bureau booth or through the cruise line's onboard excursion desk — both route through the same vetted tour-operator network.

Five further scams shape the Halifax visit. The *YHZ Halifax Stanfield Airport taxi and Uber cancel-and-cash scam* is the standard Canadian airport pattern — real YHZ-to-downtown is 65–80 CAD on Uber or the metered Casino Taxi / Yellow Cab; decline any flat-rate $130+ cash quote. The *Halifax Waterfront restaurant tourist-trap pricing* at the Bluenose II and harbour-front seafood docks quotes 40–55 CAD for lobster rolls and 38–48 CAD for fish-and-chips that run 22–32 CAD at Hydrostone Market, Quinpool, or Argyle Street restaurants; for a fair Maritime seafood meal, pick The Wooden Monkey, Five Fishermen, or Your Father's Moustache rather than the waterfront. The *Maritime Museum and Citadel Hill skip-the-line tout pressure* on cruise-ship days has men with folders offering "fast-track" passes at 30–50 CAD — the real lines at the Maritime Museum of the Atlantic and Halifax Citadel National Historic Site are rarely long (20–30 minutes max), the tickets are inexpensive (~10 CAD), and no skip-the-line exists. The *Halifax vacation-rental and long-stay rental fraud* on Craigslist and Kijiji mirrors the national pattern — "furnished downtown apartments" that take Interac e-Transfer deposits and don't exist; book only on Booking.com, Airbnb, or VRBO. Finally the *Lunenburg and South Shore tour bundle reseller markups* follow the cruise-day excursion pattern at a smaller scale — "Lunenburg + Blue Rocks + Mahone Bay photography tour" at 220 CAD per person that the Lunenburg Tourism Bureau sells for 120 per person; go through official channels or self-drive the South Shore (a 1-hour drive from Halifax).
