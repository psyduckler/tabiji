Calgary is Alberta's economic capital — the Calgary Tower, Heritage Park, and the Calgary Stampede every July that draws 1.4 million visitors over ten days. The scam ecosystem follows the Stampede peak and the airport-to-downtown / airport-to-Banff taxi corridor. The *Calgary Stampede ticket scalper online fraud* is the flagship: fraudulent Facebook Marketplace, Craigslist, and Twitter-DM ticket sellers charge 300–1,500 CAD for rodeo, concert, and "VIP wristband" tickets at above-face-value prices, then either send fake QR codes or nothing at all. The *Calgary Herald* and Calgary Police Service have repeatedly flagged the pattern, which spikes every June as Stampede week approaches. The defense is to buy only through *calgarystampede.com* and the official Ticketmaster resale channel; any seller asking for Interac e-Transfer or wire transfer is a fraud.

Five further scams shape the Calgary visit. The *Stampede festival vendor and counterfeit "VIP wristband" scam* sells laminated-paper "wristbands" at 200–600 CAD outside Stampede Park gates that don't scan at the actual entry; real wristbands come through the Stampede website with a scannable QR linked to the named purchaser's Stampede account, and vendors at the gate are never authorized. The *YYC Calgary International Airport taxi and Uber cancel-and-cash scam* follows the standard Canadian airport pattern — real YYC to downtown is 50–60 CAD on Uber or the metered Checker Cabs; decline any flat-rate $110+ cash quote. The *Calgary-to-Banff hotel-concierge transfer and "private tour" markup* is the most expensive non-Stampede Calgary scam: hotel concierges and airport-curb touts sell "private Banff transfer" at 400–700 CAD for what Banff Airporter and Brewster Express bus services run at 75–125 CAD per person and what a private-hire car (Klook, Viator) runs at 180–260 CAD. Book the Banff Airporter or Brewster Express bus in advance and save the markup. The *Calgary vacation-rental and Stampede-week apartment fraud* spikes every June — Facebook Marketplace listings for "furnished Stampede-week apartments" that take 100% non-refundable Interac e-Transfer deposits for nonexistent properties; book only on Airbnb, Booking.com, or VRBO. Finally the *Calgary Tower and Heritage Park skip-the-line tout pressure* mirrors the Halifax pattern — men with folders at the base of the Calgary Tower quote "fast-pass" tickets at 60 CAD when the real ticket is 22 CAD and lines are short; buy at the actual Calgary Tower ticket desk or online at *calgarytower.com*.
