Victoria is British Columbia's capital, Vancouver Island's largest city, and the Inner Harbour / Fairmont Empress / BC Parliament Buildings corridor. The city is reached by BC Ferries from Vancouver, by seaplane from Coal Harbour, or by the YYJ airport, and its scam ecosystem has two distinct categories: ferry-and-transport frauds, and a Vancouver-Island-specific safety warning for solo young female travelers. The *BC Ferries "Experience Card" and prepaid-resale fraud* is the flagship: BC Ferries' Experience Card is a discount-fare prepaid card that is non-transferable between users, but Craigslist and Facebook Marketplace listings sell "unused prepaid Experience Cards" at face-value discounts that the buyer cannot use because the card is registered to the original purchaser. Use only *bcferries.com* for ferry bookings; refuse any Craigslist/Marketplace Experience Card offers.

Five further scams shape the Victoria visit. The *Inner Harbour whale-watching operator variance and safety concerns* is the most serious Victoria scam and the most consequential public-safety issue in this book — some unlicensed or lower-tier whale-watching operators on the Inner Harbour cluster run boats without Transport Canada passenger certificates, without life vests for all passengers, and without current marine-radio equipment. The defense is to book only operators on the Transport Canada-certified list (Prince of Whales, Eagle Wing Tours, Orca Spirit Adventures, and SpringTide Whale Watching are the four largest licensed operators) and to visually confirm life-vests-for-all before boarding. If the crew cannot produce a life vest for every passenger, leave. The *Butchart Gardens tour-bundle markups and cruise-day shortages* is the Victoria cruise-day version of the Halifax/Quebec pattern — "Butchart Gardens + Craigdarroch Castle + Victoria city tour" bundles sold at 220–280 CAD per person at the Inner Harbour pier touts, when the Butchart Gardens direct bus (CVS Tours or Gray Line) plus Uber Castle/city is 80–110 CAD. Book at the Victoria Tourism booth (Wharf Street) or through your cruise line's onboard excursion desk. The *Vancouver Island trafficking and stranger-car safety warnings* is Vancouver Island's most-serious public-safety category: RCMP E Division and the Vancouver Island Integrated Major Crime Unit have issued repeated warnings about late-night ferry-to-Victoria arrivals (9 PM – 1 AM sailings from Swartz Bay), particularly targeting solo female travelers with "offered rides" from strangers. Use only pre-booked Uber, BC Transit bus 70 or 72 to Victoria, or the official BC Ferries Connector bus; never accept a ride from a stranger at Swartz Bay terminal regardless of how friendly the approach seems. The *YYJ Victoria airport and downtown taxi overcharge* follows the standard Canadian airport pattern — real YYJ-to-downtown is 60–75 CAD on Uber or the metered Bluebird Cabs; decline any flat-rate $130+ cash quote. Finally the *Victoria apartment rental and online romance / donation scams* on Kijiji and Facebook Marketplace mirror the national rental-fraud pattern with a Victoria-specific online-romance variant that targets cruise-ship-day or short-stay visitors — book on-platform and refuse any "spiritual donation" or "emergency help" request from online contacts.
