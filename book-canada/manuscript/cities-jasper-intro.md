Jasper is the northern Rocky Mountains park — Maligne Lake, Spirit Island, the Jasper SkyTram, and the Icefields Parkway that connects it to Banff 230 km south. The scam ecosystem mirrors Banff's (Pursuit Collection pricing, vacation-rental fraud, captive-market inflation) at a smaller scale, with one Jasper-specific category: wildlife-tour and bear-spotting operator markups. The *Pursuit Collection cross-park combo-pass pricing* is the flagship: Pursuit sells a bundled "Jasper SkyTram + Maligne Lake Cruise" pass at a confusing USD premium that overcharges American tourists 120–180 USD more than the separate Canadian-rate tickets (SkyTram: 60 CAD; Maligne Lake Cruise: 98 CAD; combo Canadian rate: 140 CAD). Book separately if you want both, or confirm the Canadian-dollar bundle rate at the Pursuit website before paying USD.

Five further scams shape the Jasper visit. The *Maligne Lake cruise booking and "Spirit Island Premium" upsell* sells a standard Maligne Lake cruise at 98 CAD and a "Spirit Island Premium" add-on at 60 CAD more — the Premium upsell is a slightly-longer cruise, not a different destination (Spirit Island is the endpoint of every Maligne Lake cruise regardless of tier), and the additional time is 30 minutes. Skip the Premium unless you specifically want the extended photo time. The *Jasper town hotel parking and restaurant pricing inflation* is the same captive-market pattern as Banff — parking 35–55 CAD per night at hotels, restaurant pricing 30–50% above Edmonton equivalents — the defense is simply to budget for it or to stay in Hinton (45 minutes east) for lower rates. The *YEG Edmonton Airport-to-Jasper rental car and one-way drop fees* catches arrivals who fly into Edmonton International and drop the rental in Vancouver or Calgary — one-way drop fees can run 300–800 CAD on top of the rental rate; round-trip Edmonton and bus-transfer to Jasper (Sundog Transportation, Brewster Express) is often cheaper. The *Jasper wildlife tour and "bear-spotting" operator markups* are a Jasper specialty — operators quote 150–250 CAD per person for "guaranteed bear spotting" 3-hour tours that are simply drives down the Icefields Parkway where anyone can self-drive and spot wildlife for free; book licensed photography-and-wildlife operators (Sundog, Jasper Adventure Centre) only if you want guided expertise, not "guaranteed" spotting. Finally the *Jasper town vacation-rental fraud and wildfire recovery scams* are the regional-specific variant — Jasper's 2024 wildfire destroyed or damaged a significant share of the town's short-term rental stock, and a related fraud cycle has targeted tourists booking "wildfire-recovery cabins" and "post-fire discount rentals" on Facebook Marketplace. Book only on Booking.com, Airbnb, or VRBO; verify any "wildfire discount" with the hotel or property owner directly through the platform's messaging.
