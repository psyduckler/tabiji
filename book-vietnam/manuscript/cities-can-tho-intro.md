Can Tho is the Mekong Delta's largest city and the launching point for the Cai Rang floating market — a pre-dawn sampan economy that is the country's most photographed river scene and also a routine kickback circuit for the HCMC day-tour pipeline. The *HCMC-based Mekong Delta day tour kickback and tourist-restaurant scam* is the flagship: a cheap-looking "Saigon Happy Tour" day trip from District 1 stops for lunch at an unmarked "typical local restaurant" where rice plates and simple pho arrive at 800,000 VND per person; the tour operator pockets a kickback, the restaurant overcharges, and the guest has no menu to dispute. The defense is to eat only at restaurants with posted menus, to photograph the posted menu before ordering, and to book Mekong Delta tours through operators with visible VNAT licenses (*vietnamtourism.gov.vn* has the list) rather than street-corner offices.

Five further scams shape the delta visit. The *Cai Rang floating-market declining authenticity and boat overcharge* splits in two — first, the market itself has thinned dramatically since 2023 as wholesale traders shift to Can Tho city's ground markets, so "the floating market" a tourist expects is smaller than photographs suggest; second, private-boat operators quote 1,500,000 VND for solo charters when a group sampan at the Ninh Kieu pier is 150,000 VND per person. The defense is to join a group sampan at the official pier and go at 5:30 a.m. when the remaining trade activity is visible. The *Can Tho street-taxi overcharge* quotes 500,000 VND for a hotel run that Grab prices at 60,000; Grab is fully available in Can Tho and defeats it. The *Mekong Delta homestay fake-listing and kickback commission* pattern runs through off-platform WhatsApp contacts — a booking that "cancels" on arrival and a driver who "happens to know" a partner homestay at a marked-up rate; Booking.com-listed homestays with public reviews are the fix. The *Mekong Delta "coconut candy factory" and "honey bee farm" commission stops* are not themselves scams — they are tour-itinerary kickback stops where prices are double the town shops — and the polite defense is to enjoy the demonstration and decline the purchase. Finally the *Can Tho tour operator license fraud* — unlicensed operators running "day tours" without insurance or emergency equipment — is the serious one; if a tour office cannot produce a VNAT license on request, walk to the next one.
