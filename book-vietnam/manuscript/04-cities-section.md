# The Scams: City by City {-}

The chapters that follow document every scam reported in eleven Vietnamese destinations. Cities are ordered by how most visitors actually travel: starting with Hanoi, the capital and the country's densest scam ecosystem; then out to Ha Long Bay's karst cruises and up into the northern mountains of Sapa; down the central coast through the imperial city of Hue, the lantern-lit trading port of Hoi An, and the beach resort of Da Nang; further south to Nha Trang's coastline and Dalat's highland retreats; across to Ho Chi Minh City and the Mekong Delta hub of Can Tho; and finishing on the Gulf-of-Thailand island of Phu Quoc.

If you're hitting only a few of these, skip to the chapter you need. If you're doing a longer loop, skim them all — the same core scams recur with local variations. The fake-Grab airport approach that intercepts arrivals at Hanoi's Noi Bai runs identically at Tan Son Nhat in HCMC, Cam Ranh near Nha Trang, Da Nang, Hue Phu Bai, Dalat, and Phu Quoc. The off-platform Booking.com payment-fraud pattern runs in every Vietnamese city with a tourist economy. The motorbike passport-hostage damage charge — a shop holds your passport until you pay an invented repair bill — runs in Hoi An, Hue, Da Nang, and on Phu Quoc. And the commission-stop-on-a-day-tour pattern (coconut-candy factory, pearl farm, silk-weaving village) threads through the Mekong Delta, Phu Quoc, Sapa, and the Hoi An cooking-class circuit. Learn the pattern in one city, and you'll spot it the instant you arrive in the next.

## The Eleven Cities at a Glance {-}

![Hanoi — Old Quarter lanterns and One Pillar Pagoda at golden hour](assets/cities/hanoi.jpg)

**Hanoi** — The capital, the Old Quarter, and Vietnam's densest tourist-scam ecosystem. The Noi Bai Airport (HAN) fake-Grab script that r/VietNam has filmed in real time; the Old Quarter shoe-shine aggressive upcharge that starts with a surprise swipe of polish and ends with a 500,000-VND demand; the Hoan Kiem Lake motorbike-pickpocket pair documented across dozens of Tourist Police reports; and the Tinder / overpriced bar extortion circuit on Ma May and Ta Hien streets that has hit travelers with 4–8 million VND tabs.

![Ha Long Bay — Karst islands and junk-boat sails at golden hour](assets/cities/ha-long-bay.jpg)

**Ha Long Bay** — UNESCO karst seascape and a booking ecosystem shot through with fake operators. The fake-cruise-booking websites with ghost itineraries that disappear the morning of embarkation; the Tuan Chau pier flat-rate taxi cartel that quotes 800,000 VND for a 180,000-VND ride; onboard upcharges for "mandatory" kayak and cave fees that are actually included; and the Booking.com / Agoda off-platform payment fraud that hits every major cruise operator.

![Sapa — Terraced rice fields and Fansipan peak in mountain mist](assets/cities/sapa.jpg)

**Sapa** — Hmong highlands, Fansipan cable car, and a trekking-and-homestay economy built on kickbacks. The Lao Cai station shuttle-fraud that quadruples the 50,000-VND official price; the Hmong village-shop handicraft pressure sale; hotel "upgrade" scams on arrival that demand 800,000 VND for a room the guest already booked; and Fansipan cable car "closed today" upsells from touts at the station.

![Hue — Perfume River and Thien Mu Pagoda at sunset](assets/cities/hue.jpg)

**Hue** — Imperial City, Perfume River dragon boats, and a cyclo network that runs mid-ride extortion. The Phu Bai Airport 700,000-VND taxi overcharge when Grab shows 250,000; cyclo riders who agree 100,000 VND and demand 500,000 halfway through; the Imperial City "single-entry re-entry" trap where a fake officer tells you your day ticket is invalid; and the "bicycle pusher and granddaughter-guilt" donation scams around the Citadel.

![Hoi An — Lanterns, canal boats, and the Japanese Covered Bridge at night](assets/cities/hoi-an.jpg)

**Hoi An** — UNESCO ancient town, tailor-shop economy, and a lantern-river evening that draws pressure sales. The Hoi An tailor "exclusive fabric" markup that charges 3,000,000 VND for what sells at 800,000 two doors down; the lantern-boat fake monk who demands donations for "blessing" bracelets; the Ancient Town fake-ticket-checker scam that invents 500,000-VND "fines" on valid tickets; the fruit-lady forced-weight overcharge where tourists get loaded up for a "photo" and then billed per shutter click; and bicycle / motorbike rental damage claims worth 2,000,000 VND.

![Da Nang — Dragon Bridge, My Khe crescent, and Marble Mountains](assets/cities/da-nang.jpg)

**Da Nang** — Central-coast beach city with airport-scam traps and a self-drive rental scene that's unusually aggressive. The DAD post-midnight fake-Grab taxi script; the Ba Na Hills "cable car closed / ruined my trip" tout that diverts to overpriced private drivers; the My Khe beachfront "where are you from?" tea-scam with 6-million-VND tabs; the Dragon Bridge weekend fire-show pickpocket teams; and the self-drive rental-car damage scam with 40-million-VND "invisible" damage claims.

![Nha Trang — Long crescent beach and Po Nagar Cham Towers](assets/cities/nha-trang.jpg)

**Nha Trang** — South-central coast resort with Russian-tourist-area pricing discrimination and a late-night bar economy. The Cam Ranh Airport (CXR) 1,200,000-VND taxi quote for a 400,000-VND ride; beachfront iPhone thefts documented across Tran Phu in 2024–2025; late-night massage and bar solicitation scams ending at 5-million-VND "service" tabs; dual-menu price discrimination where the English menu shows three times the Vietnamese menu; and hotel off-platform Booking.com fraud that takes the deposit before the guest arrives.

![Dalat — Misty pine-forest highlands, pastel railway station, Xuan Huong lake](assets/cities/dalat.jpg)

**Dalat** — Central-highland retreat with adventure tourism that runs to unlicensed operators and a fake-resort ecosystem. The Lien Khuong Airport (DLI) "regulated price officials" scam that quotes 1,500,000 VND when Grab is 300,000; unlicensed canyoning operators running unsafe equipment in the waterfalls near the city; the "Valley of Love" flower-garden scalper with ticket-overcharges; the Dalat fake-resort listing that leads to a vacant lot; and "Easy Rider" motorbike-tour guides quoting 3,000,000-VND day rates when the real local rate is 700,000.

![Ho Chi Minh City — Notre-Dame spires and Saigon Central Post Office at dusk](assets/cities/ho-chi-minh-city.jpg)

**Ho Chi Minh City** — Southern commercial capital, District 1, and the most industrial-scale nightlife-scam economy in Vietnam. The Tan Son Nhat (SGN) fake-Grab script; the Vinasun-copycat taxi meter-tampering circuit; the Bui Vien walking-street bar "hostess" 4,000,000-VND extortion that matches Bangkok's Patpong scale; the District 1 motorbike bag-snatch and phone-grab teams; the Ben Thanh Market foreigner-price gap (with Saigon Square next door at a third the price); and HCMC-wide Booking.com / Agoda off-platform payment fraud.

![Can Tho — Cai Rang floating market at dawn on the Mekong Delta](assets/cities/can-tho.jpg)

**Can Tho** — Mekong Delta hub with a floating-market economy built around tour kickbacks. The HCMC day-tour tourist-restaurant 800,000-VND-per-plate kickback stop; the Cai Rang floating-market private-boat overcharge; the Can Tho street-taxi overcharge with Grab quoting 60,000 for a 500,000 street quote; Mekong Delta fake-homestay listings; commission stops at the "coconut candy factory" and "honey bee farm"; and unlicensed tour-operator scams documented by VNAT.

![Phu Quoc — Sao Beach sunset and basket-boat silhouette](assets/cities/phu-quoc.jpg)

**Phu Quoc** — Gulf-of-Thailand resort island with airport-scam entry and a pearl-farm-pitch tourism pipeline. The PQC airport "Grab doesn't work here" script when the real Grab zone is fifty meters away; the pearl-farm "educational tour" hard sell with 15-million-VND single-pearl quotes; the jet-ski deposit-damage scam at beachfront rentals; spa add-on pressure mid-massage; the cable-car 4-island package upsells; and Airbnb / Booking.com off-platform payment fraud.
