Phu Quoc is Vietnam's largest island — Gulf of Thailand, west-coast beaches, pepper plantations, and a fast-growing resort corridor with its own airport (PQC) in the south. Because the tourism economy is young and the airport-hotel distance is large, almost every visitor is exposed to two Phu Quoc signature scams: the *PQC airport "Grab doesn't work here" script* and the *pearl-farm hard sell*. At the airport, drivers insist Grab "doesn't work on Phu Quoc" and quote 1,000,000 VND to a hotel the app would price at 220,000. Grab works on Phu Quoc. The designated pickup zone is fifty meters from the arrivals exit, past the taxi queue. The rule is the same as every other Vietnamese airport — book in the app, walk to the zone, ignore the driver who speaks to you first.

Five further scams structure the Phu Quoc week. The *pearl-farm "educational tour" hard sell* runs a free or nominally cheap day-tour bus to the island's south — the "tour" is a forty-five-minute sales pitch ending at the shop, where the pressure closes on 15,000,000-VND single-pearl necklaces on a "once-in-a-lifetime price"; the defense is simply to say "I am not buying today" and board the return bus. No Phu Quoc pearl is a special-edition item that exists only in that shop. The *jet-ski and water-sports deposit-damage scam* at Sao Beach, Long Beach, and Ong Lang scales the Koh Tao / Patong script — a 10,000,000-VND deposit, an invented 50,000,000-VND scratch claim on return, a passport held until payment; the defense is a two-minute phone video walkaround of the jet ski before riding off and a refusal to hand over the real passport. The *massage / spa upcharge and add-on pressure* runs beachfront resort spas — a menu 500,000-VND massage becomes a 1,500,000-VND "add-on" pitch mid-session; politely saying "just the massage on the menu, thank you" ends it. The *cable-car 4-island tour package upsell* at the Hon Thom cable car station adds "lunch upgrade" and "premium boat" fees that are included in the published Sun World price; the defense is the Sun World website print-out. Finally the *hotel and Airbnb off-platform booking fraud* is especially active on Phu Quoc — the "host" asks for direct bank transfer for a "20% discount"; the listing disappears or the host denies the booking on arrival. On-platform payment only, every time, closes the loop.
