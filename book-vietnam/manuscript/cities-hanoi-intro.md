Hanoi runs the densest tourist-scam ecosystem in Vietnam, and almost all of it converges on a single radius: the Old Quarter loop between Hoan Kiem Lake, St. Joseph's Cathedral, and Bia Hoi Junction. The *fake-Grab driver* gambit — a man in a polo shirt standing in the Noi Bai Airport (HAN) arrivals hall who flashes a phone screen that looks like the Grab app, tells you "your car this way," and walks you out to a private car waiting at the car-park exit — has been running long enough that Grab and Be now station their own uniformed staff inside the official pickup counters. r/VietNam has repeatedly documented the choreography, including a 2025 thread titled *"Robbed at HCM airport by fake Grab driver. Be…"* and its Hanoi equivalents — the pattern is to offer a fare that sounds close to legitimate (500,000–700,000 VND vs the real 230,000–300,000 on the app) so jet-lagged arrivals don't feel obviously scammed. Once in the car the meter doesn't exist; the final demand is 1,000,000–1,200,000 VND at the hotel door, with a driver who suddenly has no change.

The risk radiates from the airport into three Old Quarter corridors. The *shoe-shine aggressive upcharge* works the Hoan Kiem Lake perimeter, the St. Joseph's Cathedral square, and the Old Quarter café strips along Ly Thai To — boys spray polish on a tourist's sandal strap before being asked, then demand 500,000 VND for a "repair," with the polite defense being to firmly decline, place 20,000 VND on the ground if anything at all, and walk on. The *Hoan Kiem Lake pickpocket pair* — one teenager bumps into a tourist while apologizing, the other goes for the bag from behind — works the weekend night-market crowds on Hang Dao street and the Bia Hoi Junction evening beer-benches, and defeats cross-body-worn zipped bags with a hand on the zipper. The *xe-om "free motorbike tour"* runs out of Old Quarter hotel doorways — a "friendly local" offers a free city tour and three hours later demands 2,000,000 VND — and pairs with the *Tinder / overpriced bar extortion* circuit on Ma May, Hang Buom, and Hang Be streets, where a matched local picks the bar and the tab arrives at 8,000,000 VND. A safe default: book Grab in the app and walk to the official pickup zone at every airport (ignore every "driver" who approaches first); never accept a drink, ride, or "tour" from a stranger who picks the venue; wear your bag cross-body in front, zippers toward your chest, in any evening crowd; and if a bar bill inflates past what the menu shows, ask for the menu and call **113** from speakerphone — most venues fold at the dial tone.
