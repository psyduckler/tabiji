Why This Matters

Most Americans assume their health insurance covers them when they travel internationally. It usually doesn't — at least not the way you'd expect. Coverage varies wildly between carriers, plan types (PPO vs HMO), and even specific employer groups.

An emergency room visit abroad can cost $5,000-$50,000+. Medical evacuation can run $50,000-$250,000. Knowing what your carrier covers before you leave is not optional — it's essential.
