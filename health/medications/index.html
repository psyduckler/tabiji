⚠️ Important Notes

  • Always carry documentation. Bring your prescription, a doctor's letter, and any required import certificates for controlled medications.
  • Rules change frequently. Verify current restrictions with the destination country's embassy or health ministry before traveling.
  • "Restricted" means extra steps are needed — you may need advance approval, import permits, or official documentation to bring the medication legally.
  • "Banned" means prohibited entry — the medication cannot be brought into the country, even with a prescription.
  • OTC does not mean universal. Medications sold over-the-counter in one country may be controlled or illegal in another.
