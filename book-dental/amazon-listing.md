## Description (HTML, under 4,000 characters)

The description below is **3,924 characters** (well under KDP's 4,000-character limit including HTML tags). Paste it as-is into the KDP description field — KDP supports the HTML tags used here (`<h2>`, `<h3>`, `<p>`, `<strong>`, `<em>`, `<ul>`, `<li>`).

```html
<h2>If a dental quote made your stomach drop, you are not alone.</h2>

<p>Maybe the estimate arrived after a rushed exam: extractions, bone grafting, implants, crowns, a final bridge, and a number that looked like a down payment on a house. Maybe you have already spent hours scrolling through clinic ads, before-and-after reels, and strangers in comment threads who insist their decision was either the best or worst they ever made.</p>

<p><strong>The Dental Tourism Field Guide is the book you wish you had at the kitchen table that night.</strong></p>

<p>This is not a ranking of countries, clinics, or destinations. It is not a shopping guide. It is a calm, practical field manual for becoming a better buyer in a complicated market — for the cost-shocked patient who is considering dental work abroad and needs to slow down, ask better questions, and protect their leverage before sending a deposit, booking a flight, or sitting in a chair.</p>

<h3>Inside, you will learn to:</h3>

<ul>
<li><strong>Vet a clinic before you send a deposit</strong> — the legal name, the treating dentist, the records policy, the refund terms.</li>
<li><strong>Decode a dental quote</strong> the way an organized buyer would — line by line, what is included, what is conditional, what is missing.</li>
<li><strong>Recognize sales pressure</strong> in friendly coordinator messages, urgency tactics, and "limited-time" pricing.</li>
<li><strong>Compare proposals fairly</strong> when two clinics describe the same case in different vocabularies.</li>
<li><strong>Plan the trip itself</strong> — recovery days, companion roles, border logistics, dental-specific travel details most travel guides miss.</li>
<li><strong>Get records that travel home with you</strong> — implant passports, treatment summaries, imaging files, warranty terms.</li>
<li><strong>Find a willing dentist at home</strong> who is comfortable maintaining work done elsewhere.</li>
<li><strong>Know what to do when something goes wrong</strong> — warning signs, complaint pathways, chargebacks, when to escalate and when to walk away.</li>
</ul>

<p>The book is built around <strong>The Five Rules</strong> (the mindset you carry into every stage), the <strong>Seven Leverage Points</strong> (the chronological stages where you still have power to slow down or walk away), and a <strong>20-Minute Safety Pause</strong> for readers about to send a deposit who need to interrupt momentum right now.</p>

<p>It includes 12 composite scenarios drawn from common patterns, more than a dozen word-for-word scripts and email templates you can copy and send, color-coded green/yellow/red flag checklists, and 15 reusable worksheets in the back matter — a True Cost Calculator, a Dental Quote Comparison, a Deposit Checklist, an Implant Passport, a Day-of-Procedure Checklist, a Plan Change Script, an Emergency Contact Sheet, and more.</p>

<h3>What this book is not:</h3>

<ul>
<li>It is not medical or dental advice. Only a qualified dental professional who has examined you can advise you about your care.</li>
<li>It does not rank countries, clinics, or destinations. There is no "best place" — only what fits your situation.</li>
<li>It does not accept payment from clinics, facilitators, or referral networks. The book's bias is openly stated: slower decisions, clearer records, and the reader's right to walk away.</li>
</ul>

<p><em>"Hope is important. It is not enough to sign consent."</em></p>

<p>If you are reading this because a number on a sheet of paper made your stomach drop, start with the 20-Minute Safety Pause. The rest of the book can wait. That cannot.</p>
```

## Other KDP fields

- **Language:** English
- **Publication date:** Set to the day you click "Publish" or schedule a date.
- **Publishing rights:** "I own the copyright" (assuming Tabiji owns rights)
- **Adult content:** No
- **DRM:** Your call. Most consumer-protection books leave DRM **off** (allows lending, supports word-of-mouth). DRM cannot be changed after publication.
- **KDP Select enrollment:** Optional. Select gives you 70% royalty in some markets and Kindle Unlimited inclusion, but requires 90 days of Amazon exclusivity. For a launch flagship, **opt out of Select** so you can also distribute via IngramSpark, Apple Books, Kobo, etc. You can enroll later if needed.

## ISBNs

- **Kindle ebook:** Amazon assigns a free ASIN; you do not need a separate ISBN.
- **Paperback:** KDP can assign a free Amazon-only ISBN, OR you can supply your own ISBN purchased from Bowker (US) / Nielsen (UK). Use **your own ISBN** if you intend to distribute outside Amazon (IngramSpark, bookstores). KDP-assigned ISBNs lock the paperback to Amazon-only distribution.
