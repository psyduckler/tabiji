Excerpt · Barcelona

The La Rambla Pickpocket Cluster

You step off the Liceu metro stop and turn onto La Rambla, and a friendly stranger bumps your shoulder apologetically, pointing to a “bird-poop” stain on your jacket. While they help you wipe it off, a second pair of hands is inside your bag. Mossos d’Esquadra logs more than 140,000 pickpocket reports a year in Barcelona — the overwhelming majority concentrated on Line 3 of the metro, at Sagrada Família, and along the 1.2 km stretch from Plaça Catalunya to the port…

Red flag: Any unsolicited physical contact — a shoulder bump, a pointing finger, a “let me help” offer — on La Rambla or the metro.
Full pattern, the three working cluster types & the phrase that ends it — inside.
Excerpt · Granada

The Alhambra “Skip-the-Line” Ticket

You arrive at Plaza Nueva and a well-dressed man with a clipboard offers a guaranteed-entry Alhambra ticket for 55–80 € — the official Patronato site sells the same adult ticket for 19.09 € and is sold out for the next three weeks. What you get is either a ticket for the wrong day, a ticket that covers only the Generalización gardens, or nothing at all. Granada Policía Local has named the practice repeatedly; r/Granada threads collect dozens of victim reports from the 2024–2025 seasons…

Red flag: Any Alhambra ticket sold anywhere except tickets.alhambra-patronato.es.
Full pattern, the Plaza Nueva tout circuit & the lead time you actually need — inside.
Excerpt · Ibiza

The Playa d’en Bossa Scooter Damage Deposit

You rent a scooter on Playa d’en Bossa for 40 € a day and hand over 400 € in cash “in case there’s damage.” When you return it, the operator points to a mirror-crack or an underside-scratch that nobody inspected at pickup and keeps 300 € for “repair.” r/Ibiza’s 2024–2025 threads collect dozens of forfeitures; the Policía Local on Ibiza has issued recurring bulletins naming specific shops…

Red flag: Any rental shop demanding a cash deposit or your passport rather than a credit-card hold.
Full pattern, the pre-rental photo protocol & the credit-card-only rule — inside.
