Chapter · BBL Patterns

The BBL With Intramuscular Fat Injection in a Non-Accredited Facility

One of the eight documented patterns this book recommends against. The CDC's MMWR 73(3) documents 93 U.S.-citizen deaths from cosmetic surgery in the Dominican Republic from 2009 to 2022 — 92 percent of them involved gluteal fat transfer. The ASPS issued urgent safety guidance in 2018 and again in 2019. A South Florida case series reports a mortality rate between 1 in 3,000 and 1 in 6,241. The protective answer here is not "ask better questions." It is the pattern, not the destination…

Red flag: intramuscular fat injection, no ultrasound guidance, non-accredited facility.
Cites ASPS 2018/2019 guidance and CDC MMWR primary source. The line between "high-risk done well" and "high-risk done in a package mill."
Chapter · Demonstration Case

The Demonstration-Case Discount Waiver

Reduced procedure cost in exchange for the clinic's use of the patient's before-and-after photos in marketing. A single page. Friendly language. Significant rights given up — image use in perpetuity, across territories, on platforms not yet invented, with no revocation clause and no compensation for downstream use. The patient signs it at the same desk where the deposit moves. Read this waiver line by line before signing — the book annotates a sample paragraph by paragraph…

Pattern: The price discount and the rights surrender are sold as one transaction.

Eight patterns we recommend against

A field guide takes editorial positions where the data demands them. Eight specific procedure-context combinations where the protective answer is not "how to choose well" but "do not have this procedure under these conditions." Each is grounded in cited public-health data.

⚠️ BBL with IM fat injection in non-accredited facilities
⚠️ Stem-cell, regenerative, exosome, peptide & longevity tourism
⚠️ Unapproved alternative cancer therapies abroad
⚠️ Surgery in non-accredited facilities at Step 3 and above
⚠️ Unverifiable surgeon credentials
⚠️ Unverifiable anesthesia provider credentials
⚠️ Surgery within 12 months of major adverse life events
⚠️ Combination procedures stacked on Step 4+ procedures
