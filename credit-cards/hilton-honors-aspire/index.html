Travel Benefits Breakdown

Here's exactly what you get — and what you don't — when you use the Hilton Honors American Express Aspire Card for travel.

✈️ Lounge Access

Network: Priority Pass Select (1,300+ airport lounges worldwide)

Guest policy: Up to 2 complimentary guests per visit

Complimentary Priority Pass Select membership with enrollment required. Includes 2 complimentary guest passes per lounge visit. Does not include Amex Centurion Lounge access.

🛂 Global Entry / TSA PreCheck Credit

Get a $100 statement credit every every 4 years when you pay for Global Entry, TSA PreCheck with this card.

Global Entry ($100) includes TSA PreCheck and lets you skip customs lines when returning to the US — worth every cent for international travelers.

⏱️ Trip Delay Insurance

If your trip is delayed by 6+ hours, you're covered for up to $500/person in expenses.

Covers: meals, lodging, toiletries, medication, other personal use items

Up to $500 per covered trip for delays of 6+ hours. Maximum 2 claims per 12-month period.

Must book travel with this card. Keep all receipts for reimbursement.

🚫 Trip Cancellation & Interruption

Reimburses up to $10,000/person (max $20,000/trip) if you must cancel or cut short your trip.

Covered reasons: illness (medically inadvisable per physician), severe weather, jury duty, change in military orders

Maximum $10,000 per covered trip, $20,000 per eligible card account per 12 consecutive months.

🧳 Baggage Insurance

Lost/stolen/damaged baggage: Up to $3,000/person

Checked bags: up to $2,000 | Carry-on: up to $3,000

High-risk items (jewelry, electronics) capped at $1,000 per person per trip.

Baggage delay coverage: not included.

🏥 Emergency Medical & Evacuation

✅ Emergency evacuation coordination covered (transportation costs covered).

Premium Global Assist Hotline: 24/7 emergency assistance coordination including medical evacuation transport (Amex covers transport costs). Does not cover medical treatment costs.

🚗 Rental Car Insurance

Secondary coverage — you must file with your personal auto insurance first, then this card covers the remainder.

Coverage amount: Up to $75,000

Covers: collision damage, theft

Excludes: liability, personal injury

Eligible countries: Worldwide except Australia, Italy, New Zealand, and OFAC-sanctioned countries.

Secondary car rental coverage. Must decline rental company's CDW/LDW. Up to 30 consecutive days.

💡 Credit card travel insurance has limits

Credit card trip insurance typically doesn't cover medical emergencies abroad. For international trips, dedicated travel health insurance is recommended.

🌍 Foreign Transaction Fees

✅ No foreign transaction fees — use this card internationally without paying an extra 2–3% on every purchase.

💰 Annual Credits & Perks

  • Airline fee credit: $250/year — Up to $250 in statement credits per year for eligible airline incidental fee purchases (baggage fees, seat upgrades, in-flight purchases) — NOT airfare itself.
  • Hotel credit: $200/year — Two $100 Hilton resort credits semi-annually ($200 total per year). Valid at Hilton Resort properties within the US and Puerto Rico. Applicable to room charges, spa, dining, and recreational activities at eligible resorts.
  • Free Night Reward: — One complimentary Weekend Night Reward each year automatically upon card renewal. Additionally, earn a second free night reward after spending $60,000 on the card in a calendar year.
