Travel Benefits Breakdown

Here's exactly what you get — and what you don't — when you use the The Platinum Card from American Express for travel.

✈️ Lounge Access

Network: Amex Global Lounge Collection (1,550+ lounges in 140 countries)

Guest policy: Up to 2 complimentary guests per visit ($50 fee per additional guest)

  • American Express Centurion Lounges (15 US locations)
  • Delta Sky Club (limited to 10 visits/year when flying Delta)
  • Priority Pass Select (enrollment required)
  • Aspire Lounges
  • Escape Lounges — Centurion Studio Partner
  • Plaza Premium Lounges
  • Select Lufthansa Lounges

Centurion Lounges: up to 2 guests at $50/guest. Priority Pass: enrollment required, guest policy varies by lounge. Delta Sky Club: limited to 10 complimentary visits per year as of Feb 2024; must be flying on Delta-marketed/operated flight. Note: Amex Platinum still has Priority Pass Select as of 2026 (not removed).

🛂 Global Entry / TSA PreCheck Credit

Get a $100 statement credit every every 4 years when you pay for Global Entry, TSA PreCheck with this card.

Global Entry ($100) includes TSA PreCheck and lets you skip customs lines when returning to the US — worth every cent for international travelers.

⏱️ Trip Delay Insurance

If your trip is delayed by 6+ hours, you're covered for up to $500/person in expenses.

Covers: meals, lodging, toiletries, medication, personal use items

Maximum 2 claims per 12-month period.

Must book travel with this card. Keep all receipts for reimbursement.

🚫 Trip Cancellation & Interruption

Reimburses up to $10,000/person (max $20,000/trip) if you must cancel or cut short your trip.

Covered reasons: illness (medically inadvisable per physician), severe weather, jury duty, change in military orders

Maximum $20,000 every 12 months across all claims.

🧳 Baggage Insurance

Lost/stolen/damaged baggage: Up to $3,000/person

Checked bags: up to $2,000 | Carry-on: up to $3,000

High-risk items (jewelry, electronics) capped at $1,000 per person per trip. Delayed baggage is NOT covered — only lost/stolen/damaged.

Baggage delay coverage: not included.

🏥 Emergency Medical & Evacuation

✅ Emergency evacuation coordination covered (transportation costs covered).

Premium Global Assist: 24/7 hotline for emergency medical evacuation (no cost to cardholder for evacuation transport), repatriation of mortal remains, and care for unattended minor children. Does NOT cover medical treatment costs — only coordinates and covers the transport itself.

🚗 Rental Car Insurance

Secondary coverage — you must file with your personal auto insurance first, then this card covers the remainder.

Coverage amount: Up to $75,000

Covers: collision damage, theft

Excludes: liability, personal injury

Eligible countries: Worldwide except Australia, Italy, New Zealand, and OFAC-sanctioned countries.

Secondary coverage — file with personal auto insurance first. Up to 30 consecutive days.

🌍 Foreign Transaction Fees

✅ No foreign transaction fees — use this card internationally without paying an extra 2–3% on every purchase.

💰 Annual Credits & Perks

  • Airline fee credit: $200/year — $200 annual airline fee credit for one selected qualifying airline. Covers incidental fees (baggage fees, seat upgrades, etc.) — NOT airfare.
  • Hotel credit: $200/year — $200 annual hotel credit for prepaid Fine Hotels + Resorts or The Hotel Collection bookings through Amex Travel (minimum 2-night stay for The Hotel Collection).
  • Uber credit: $200/year — $15/month Uber Cash credit ($35 in December) for US Uber rides and Uber Eats. Must add card to Uber app.
  • CLEAR Plus Credit: $189/year — Covers annual CLEAR+ membership for expedited airport security.
  • Equinox Credit: $300/year — $25/month ($300/year) for eligible Equinox memberships.
  • Walmart+ Credit: $155/year — Covers monthly Walmart+ membership fee ($12.95/month).
  • Digital Entertainment Credit: $240/year — $20/month for eligible streaming/digital services (Disney+, Hulu, ESPN+, Peacock, NYT, etc.).
  • Saks Fifth Avenue Credit: $100/year — $50 Jan-Jun + $50 Jul-Dec at Saks Fifth Avenue.
