Travel Benefits Breakdown

Here's exactly what you get — and what you don't — when you use the Capital One Venture X Rewards Credit Card for travel.

✈️ Lounge Access

Network: Priority Pass Select

Guest policy: Up to 2 complimentary guests per visit ($35 fee per additional guest)

  • Capital One Lounges (DFW, DEN, IAD)

Unlimited Priority Pass visits worldwide (1,300+ lounges). First 2 guests complimentary at Priority Pass locations; $35/guest after. Capital One Lounge access included. Additional authorized cardholders also receive lounge access.

🛂 Global Entry / TSA PreCheck Credit

Get a $100 statement credit every every 4 years when you pay for Global Entry, TSA PreCheck with this card.

Global Entry ($100) includes TSA PreCheck and lets you skip customs lines when returning to the US — worth every cent for international travelers.

⏱️ Trip Delay Insurance

If your trip is delayed by 6+ hours, you're covered for up to $500/person in expenses.

Covers: meals, lodging, toiletries, essential items

Covers cardholder, spouse, and dependent children under 22. Must file within 30 days at eclaimsline.com or call 800-825-4062.

Must book travel with this card. Keep all receipts for reimbursement.

🚫 Trip Cancellation & Interruption

Reimburses up to $2,000/person (max $2,000/trip) if you must cancel or cut short your trip.

Covered reasons: death, injury or illness of cardholder or immediate family member, financial insolvency of common carrier

IMPORTANT: Only covers airline/bus/train/ferry tickets — does NOT cover prepaid tours or hotel reservations. Very limited covered reasons compared to Chase/Amex.

🧳 Baggage Insurance

Lost/stolen/damaged baggage: Up to $3,000/person

Delayed baggage: Up to $500/person after 6+ hours. Covers essential items, toiletries, clothing.

🏥 Emergency Medical & Evacuation

No dedicated emergency medical evacuation benefit. Visa Infinite provides some assistance services but does not cover evacuation costs.

🚗 Rental Car Insurance

✅ Primary coverage — this means you do NOT need to file with your personal auto insurance first. You can decline the collision damage waiver (CDW) at the counter and save $10–30/day.

Covers: collision damage, theft

Excludes: liability, personal injury

Eligible countries: Most countries worldwide.

Primary coverage — Venture X is the only Capital One personal card with primary rental coverage (vs secondary for regular Venture).

🌍 Foreign Transaction Fees

✅ No foreign transaction fees — use this card internationally without paying an extra 2–3% on every purchase.

💰 Annual Credits & Perks

  • Annual Travel Credit: $300/year — $300 in statement credits for bookings made through Capital One Travel portal.
  • Anniversary Bonus: — 10,000 bonus miles every account anniversary (worth ~$100 toward travel).
