🏥 Healthcare Summary

Good quality

Santiago has excellent private hospitals including Clínica Alemana and Clínica Las Condes with modern facilities and English-speaking doctors. Outside Santiago, healthcare quality is adequate but more limited in remote areas like Patagonia.

Insurance: Travel insurance with medical coverage is strongly recommended. Evacuation coverage is important for travelers heading to Patagonia or the Atacama Desert, where medical facilities are distant.

💊 Medication Restrictions

Chile follows INCB international conventions. Carry certified prescriptions for controlled substances — ideally apostilled or with official translation. Keep all meds in original packaging.

  • Cannabis / CBD — BANNED: Medical cannabis exists domestically but tourists cannot import. CBD products prohibited.
  • Adderall / amphetamines — RESTRICTED: Require original prescription plus certified doctor letter. Quantities limited to trip duration.
  • Methylphenidate (Ritalin) — RESTRICTED: Allowed with valid prescription. Carry original packaging and medical documentation.
  • Benzodiazepines (Xanax, Valium) — RESTRICTED: Prescription required. Declare at customs for quantities over 30-day supply.
  • Opioids / codeine — RESTRICTED: Strict prescription requirements. Notify Chilean health authority (ISP) for imports of Schedule I substances.

🙏 Cultural Tips

Tipping: 10% at restaurants is customary. Not expected at cafes. Round up for taxis. Tip tour guides CLP 5,000-10,000/day.

Cultural taboos to avoid:

  • Comparing Chile unfavorably to Argentina (strong rivalry)
  • Being overly direct or confrontational
  • Skipping the greeting ritual — always say hello first
  • Eating on public transport
  • Discussing Pinochet unless the other person brings it up
