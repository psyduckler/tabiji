🏥 Healthcare Summary

Adequate quality

St. Luke's, Makati Medical Center, and other top Manila hospitals provide international-standard care with English-speaking staff. Outside Metro Manila and Cebu, hospital facilities drop off significantly in quality and availability.

Insurance: Comprehensive travel insurance with medical evacuation coverage is essential, especially for island-hopping or travel outside major cities where facilities are limited.

💊 Medication Restrictions

The Philippines has strict anti-drug laws with severe penalties including long prison sentences. Common medications are affordable and widely available at pharmacies like Mercury Drug. Carry prescriptions for controlled substances in original packaging with a doctor's letter. Some medications available OTC elsewhere may be restricted — check with the Philippine FDA if unsure about specific drugs.

  • Benzodiazepines (Xanax, Valium) — RESTRICTED: Controlled under Comprehensive Dangerous Drugs Act (RA 9165); prescription required. Import with prescription and BOR (Board of Registration) documentation
  • Adderall / amphetamines — BANNED: Amphetamines (shabu/meth) are primary drug war targets; Adderall is not recognized as legitimate and is effectively banned. Extreme risk—do NOT bring
  • Cannabis / CBD — BANNED: Cannabis is illegal under RA 9165 with severe penalties; medical cannabis bill pending but not yet law. CBD is also prohibited
  • Methylphenidate (Ritalin, Concerta) — RESTRICTED: Controlled dangerous drug; available by prescription from licensed physicians. Import with full prescription documentation and PDEA clearance recommended
  • Codeine / opioids — RESTRICTED: Controlled under RA 9165; prescription required. Carry original packaging and doctor's letter. Avoid large quantities

🙏 Cultural Tips

Tipping: 10% at restaurants if no service charge. Round up for taxis. Tip tour guides and drivers PHP 200-500/day.

Cultural taboos to avoid:

  • Raising your voice or showing anger publicly (causes 'loss of face')
  • Refusing food offered by a host
  • Being overly direct with criticism
  • Pointing with your finger (use lips or eyes to indicate direction)
  • Arriving exactly on time to social events ('Filipino time' is 15-30 min late)
