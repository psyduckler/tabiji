🏥 Healthcare Summary

Moderate quality

Spitalul Clinic de Urgenta (Emergency Clinical Hospital) is Bucharest's main trauma center. For elective care, private hospitals like Medicover and Regina Maria offer modern facilities.

Insurance: EU citizens should carry EHIC for access to public healthcare. All other visitors should have comprehensive travel health insurance. Private hospitals are significantly better and require payment upfront.

💊 Medication Restrictions

Most common European medications available in Romanian pharmacies. Carry prescriptions for controlled medications and ensure they are in original packaging.

  • Opioids / narcotics — RESTRICTED: Carry original prescription and sufficient documentation. Romanian customs may inspect quantities.
  • Cannabis — BANNED: Illegal in Romania. Possession can result in fines and imprisonment.
  • Benzodiazepines — RESTRICTED: Available on prescription. Carry documentation for quantities beyond personal use.

🙏 Cultural Tips

Tipping: Expected in restaurants: 10-15%. Leave cash on table or hand directly to waiter. Hotel staff: 5-10 RON per service. Taxi drivers: round up. Not obligatory but greatly appreciated.

Cultural taboos to avoid:

  • Disrespecting Orthodox religious customs in churches
  • Discussing Romani people using the R-slur (offensive)
  • Excessive complaints about Romania in front of locals can be perceived as rude
  • Assuming Romanians speak Hungarian (separate culture and language)
