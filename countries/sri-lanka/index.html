🏥 Healthcare Summary

Adequate quality

Private hospitals in Colombo offer reasonable standards with English-speaking doctors. Outside major cities, medical facilities are basic, and rural areas may lack reliable emergency services.

Insurance: Comprehensive travel insurance with medical evacuation coverage is strongly recommended, as serious conditions may require transfer to Singapore or India for specialist treatment.

💊 Medication Restrictions

Sri Lanka has severe penalties for drug offences including lengthy prison sentences. Carry all prescription medications in original packaging with a valid prescription and doctor's letter. Airports use technology to detect illegal items including in transit baggage. Always carry your passport as identification.

  • Benzodiazepines (Xanax, Valium) — RESTRICTED: Prescription controlled drugs under Poisons, Opium and Dangerous Drugs Ordinance; carry prescription for import
  • Adderall / amphetamines — BANNED: Amphetamines are prohibited dangerous drugs in Sri Lanka; Adderall is not available. Severe penalties for possession
  • Cannabis / CBD — BANNED: Cannabis is illegal under the Poisons Ordinance; CBD products are also prohibited. Strict enforcement
  • Methylphenidate (Ritalin, Concerta) — RESTRICTED: Controlled psychotropic; limited availability in Sri Lanka. Import with prescription and doctor's certificate
  • Codeine / opioids — RESTRICTED: Codeine is controlled under the Dangerous Drugs Ordinance; prescription required. Carry doctor's letter and keep in original packaging

🙏 Cultural Tips

Tipping: 10% at restaurants if no service charge. Tip drivers LKR 500-1000/day, guides LKR 1000-2000/day.

Cultural taboos to avoid:

  • Posing for photos with your back to a Buddha statue
  • Wearing clothing with Buddha images (can be confiscated/deported)
  • Touching someone's head
  • Pointing feet at people or religious objects
  • Using left hand for eating or passing items
