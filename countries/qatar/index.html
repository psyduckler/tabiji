🏥 Healthcare Summary

Excellent quality

Hamad General Hospital is the main public hospital. Sidra Medicine and Aspetar are world-class specialized hospitals. English is widely spoken at all medical facilities.

Insurance: Travel insurance recommended. Healthcare costs are significant. Hamad Medical Corporation provides emergency care regardless of insurance.

💊 Medication Restrictions

Qatar has strict drug laws. Carry all medications in original packaging with prescriptions. Apply for prior approval for controlled substances at qatar.gov.qa before travel.

  • Opioids / narcotics — RESTRICTED: Require prior approval from Qatar Ministry of Public Health. Carry original prescription and doctor's letter. Maximum 3-month supply.
  • Cannabis / marijuana — BANNED: Strictly illegal. Zero tolerance. Possession can result in imprisonment and deportation.
  • Benzodiazepines — RESTRICTED: Require documentation from prescribing physician. Carry in original packaging.
  • Pseudoephedrine (in cold medicines) — RESTRICTED: Many common cold medications containing pseudoephedrine may be restricted. Check current list with Qatar Ministry of Public Health.

🙏 Cultural Tips

Tipping: Not culturally required but appreciated. Restaurants: 10-15% if not included. Hotel staff: QAR 10-20 per service. Taxis: round up to nearest riyal.

Cultural taboos to avoid:

  • Public displays of affection (kissing or cuddling in public can result in fines or arrest)
  • Drinking alcohol outside licensed venues (hotels, licensed restaurants)
  • Loud or disruptive behavior in public
  • Disrespecting Islam, Qatar's ruling family, or government
  • Same-sex public displays of affection
