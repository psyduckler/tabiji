What is a compare page?

Each compare page puts two destinations side-by-side across cost, safety, weather, food, transit, and honest verdicts from real travelers — a decision-focused answer to "which one should I book?" rather than a generic listicle.

How are pages ranked here?

Ranking blends internal compare-page links, destination demand across the catalog, and freshness. The goal is to surface the clearest next reads first.
