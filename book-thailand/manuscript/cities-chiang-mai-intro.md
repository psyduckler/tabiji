Chiang Mai feels gentler than Bangkok — the 700-year-old moat, the slow pace of the Old City, the jazz bars around North Gate — and that softness is exactly what the scam ecosystem exploits. The signature Chiang Mai operation is the passport-hostage scooter-rental scam, run out of shops just inside Tha Phae Gate and along Moon Muang Road. You rent a 125cc Honda Click for 200 baht a day, hand over your passport as “deposit” because every shop on the block requires it, spend a great week riding up to Doi Suthep and out to Sticky Waterfalls, and on return the owner’s wife kneels down and points to a hairline scratch on the underside of the fairing. The repair quote is 13,500 baht. A 2025 r/ThailandTourism thread titled “Just payed 13500 baht for this damage” documents exactly that number and exactly that leverage — your passport is in a locked drawer in the back office, your flight is in 36 hours, and refusal is effectively impossible.

The second layer is nightlife. In July 2025, a 27-year-old British tourist tried to walk out of Diamond Karaoke Bar on Sri Don Chai Road with a 20,000-baht bill in dispute; nine staff aged 18–28 physically restrained him, dragged him into the street, and kicked him while a crowd watched. The footage ran internationally on *The Sun*, *Metro*, and *VnExpress International*, and the venue was shut down for operating without a license. Sri Don Chai sits two blocks from the Night Bazaar. Around the Night Bazaar itself, the tailor bait-and-switch (shops known on r/chiangmai as “Tony Tailors,” “Tom Tailor,” “Siam Tailor”) and the *tuk-tuk* gem-shop loop — built around the fiction that Wat Chedi Luang is “closed for cleaning” mid-day — both run continuously. In November, Yi Peng brings a parallel ticket-fraud season, with lookalike websites impersonating the legitimate CAD Khomloy Mae Jo event. A safe default: never hand over your original passport for a scooter — offer 3,000–5,000 baht cash plus a photocopy. Use Grab for transport (app-fixed pricing kills the *songthaew* foreigner rate), verify temple hours on Google Maps before listening to any driver, and for nightlife stick to Nimmanhaemin, North Gate jazz, or Tha Phae East Gate rooftops. The Chiang Mai Tourist Police line is +66 53 247 318, or dial 1155 nationally.
