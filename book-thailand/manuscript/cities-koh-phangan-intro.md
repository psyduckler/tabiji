Koh Phangan’s scam ecosystem orbits the Full Moon Party at Haad Rin, which draws 30,000-plus tourists a month and a parallel economy of undercover drug stings. The mechanic is consistent: you buy mushroom shakes or cannabis from a beach seller and within minutes are stopped by officers who produce the same substance from your pocket and demand 30,000–100,000 baht cash “or jail.” The *Bangkok Post* reported in October 2025 that Israeli soldiers had been arrested on drug charges at a Phangan party, and *Khaosod English* covered the same incident. Cannabis requires a medical prescription in Thailand post-2025; street cannabis is illegal, and the Haad Rin beach sellers are tied directly to the extortion rotation.

The secondary layer is familiar island-Thailand: the passport-hostage scooter-rental scam (11,000–13,000-baht damage demands for scratches on the underside fairing, documented on r/ThailandTourism where three friends were hit on the same day), Thong Sala Pier *songthaew* drivers quoting 300 baht for the 20-km Haad Rin run that locals pay 100 for, and cannabis dispensaries advertising “70% THC” flower at 3,000 baht a gram when the theoretical maximum is around 34 percent. Before the Full Moon, hosts send “water pipe broken, cancel your booking, pay me 8,000 baht cash” messages designed to pull you off Airbnb or Agoda and off any refund protection. A safe default: do not carry or consume any recreational drug on Phangan, stay on the Haad Rin main strip rather than isolated beaches at night, never cancel a booking on a host’s request (make them cancel, they incur the penalty), rent a scooter only against a cash deposit and never an original passport, and save Tourist Police 1155 before the ferry leaves Thong Sala.
