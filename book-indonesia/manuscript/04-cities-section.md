# The Scams: City by City {-}

The chapters that follow document every scam reported in twelve Indonesian destinations. Cities are ordered by how most visitors actually travel: starting with Jakarta, the capital; west to Yogyakarta and the Java cultural corridor; up to the Mount Bromo and Ijen Crater volcano pair; across to Bali and Ubud, the country's flagship tourist corridor; south to the Seminyak beach strip and the offshore cluster of Nusa Penida; east to the Gili Islands, Lombok, and its Rinjani trek; across to Labuan Bajo for Komodo; and finishing at the Singapore-adjacent duty-free island of Batam.

If you're hitting only a few of these, skip to the chapter you need. If you're doing a longer loop, skim them all — the same core scams recur with local variations. The fake-Grab airport approach that intercepts arrivals at Bali's Ngurah Rai runs identically at Jakarta's Soekarno-Hatta, Lombok's Praya, Labuan Bajo, Yogyakarta, and Batam. The scooter-rental pre-damage claim that runs on Bali's Kuta and Canggu repeats in Ubud, Nusa Penida, Seminyak, and Kuta Lombok. And the commission-stop-on-a-day-tour pattern (batik gallery, silver factory, kopi luwak plantation, kris workshop) threads through Yogyakarta, Ubud, Nusa Penida, and the Bromo–Ijen combo circuit. Learn the pattern in one city, and you'll spot it the instant you arrive in the next.

## The Twelve Cities at a Glance {-}

![Jakarta — Kota Tua colonial facades and the Jakarta History Museum at golden hour](assets/cities/jakarta.jpg)

**Jakarta** — The national capital, Kota Tua (Old Town), and the gateway airport for most first-time arrivals. The Soekarno-Hatta Airport (CGK) "airport shark" taxi scam that runs 600,000-rupiah quotes on real 200,000 metered rides; the Blue Bird impersonator and meter-tampering script in central Jakarta; the Grab/Gojek off-app cash-negotiation trick on high-volume night rides; the Kota Tua becak (pedicab) "free tour" commission-stop loop; ATM skimming and card-cloning fraud concentrated on Jalan Jaksa and Kemang bank rows; and the Blok M / Kemang nightlife "honeypot bar" Tinder-extortion circuit that has hit solo male travelers with 7-million-rupiah tabs.

![Yogyakarta — Borobudur sunrise and Mount Merapi in the distance](assets/cities/yogyakarta.jpg)

**Yogyakarta** — Java's cultural capital, gateway to Borobudur and Prambanan, and the center of the batik commission-kickback circuit. The Malioboro batik-gallery tout-led scam that ends at 3–8 million rupiah "hand-drawn" purchases; the YIA airport and Kulon Progo transfer overcharge; the becak and andong pedicab "free orientation" commission loop; the Borobudur sunrise fake-ticket and Manohara Hotel upsell fraud; the Prambanan "mandatory guide" and skip-the-line invention; and the Mount Merapi jeep lava-tour unlicensed-operator scam.

![Mount Bromo — Volcanic caldera at sunrise with Mount Semeru in the distance](assets/cities/mount-bromo.jpg)

**Mount Bromo** — East Java's iconic sunrise-volcano tour, built around Cemoro Lawang jeep cartels that control every traveler who arrives. The Cemoro Lawang jeep-tour kickback ring that quotes 700,000 rupiah and ends at 1,500,000 with viewpoint fees; the national park entry-fee confusion and fake-ticket fraud; the crater-approach horse rental forced-tip shakedown; the Bromo–Ijen combo sleep-deprivation overpromise; the King Kong Hill viewpoint fee padding and parking tout ring; and the altitude-sickness fake-medical upsell that targets travelers struggling with the 2,300-meter elevation.

![Ijen Crater — Turquoise acid lake at dawn with sulfur miners](assets/cities/ijen-crater.jpg)

**Ijen Crater** — The blue-flame volcano near Banyuwangi in East Java, and the "mandatory guide" shakedown at the Paltuding base station. The Paltuding mandatory-guide fee that runs 800,000–1,500,000 rupiah for a walk any fit traveler can do alone; the gas mask "professional grade" rental overcharge with counterfeit models; the Banyuwangi Airport (BWX) and ferry terminal taxi overcharge; the "just missed the blue flame" fraud pushed by late-arrival tour guides; Banyuwangi ATM skimming on the short bank row; and the sulfur-miner photo forced-tip scam at the crater rim.

![Bali — Tanah Lot sea-temple at sunset](assets/cities/bali.jpg)

**Bali** — The island flagship, the Kuta–Seminyak–Canggu corridor, and Indonesia's most developed tourist-scam ecosystem. The Ngurah Rai Airport (DPS) fake-Grab and taxi-mafia script; the ATM skimmer network across Jalan Legian and Kuta bank rows; the money-changer sleight-of-hand at "BEST RATE" booths on Legian; the scooter rental "spare key theft" and pre-damage claim scam; traffic-police roadside bribe shakedowns for scooter riders; the "Bali tourist levy" / tourist tax confusion and fake-tax scam sites; and organized drink spiking at Kuta and Seminyak beach clubs.

![Ubud — Tegallalang rice terraces at golden hour](assets/cities/ubud.jpg)

**Ubud** — Bali's spiritual-tourism hub, rice-terrace country, and the Sacred Monkey Forest. The Sacred Monkey Forest theft and aggressive-monkey grabs on sunglasses, phones, and hats; the kopi luwak "coffee plantation" animal-cruelty tour with counterfeit civet-coffee product; the fake-guru yoga-retreat and "spiritual healing" scam; the Ubud taxi-mafia and anti-Grab blockade; pushy massage and "extended service" upcharge pressure; and the Tegallalang rice-terrace "mandatory guide" and photo-spot fee padding.

![Seminyak — Beach-club sunset with Mount Agung silhouette](assets/cities/seminyak.jpg)

**Seminyak** — Bali's upscale beach-club strip on Jalan Kayu Aya and the Legian corridor. The money-changer sleight-of-hand scaled to the richer-tourist demographic; beach-club drink spiking at Finns, Atlas, and Potato Head; villa rental fake-listing and off-platform Instagram booking fraud; beauty-salon "add-on service" overcharge and card-fraud overlay; tailor-shop "custom suit" bait-and-switch; and massage/spa "extended service" pressure that segues into card fraud.

![Nusa Penida — Kelingking Beach cliff viewpoint at golden hour](assets/cities/nusa-penida.jpg)

**Nusa Penida** — Bali's offshore island, reached by a 45-minute fast boat from Sanur. The Sanur–Nusa Penida fast-boat ticket overcharge and fake-operator fraud; the Kelingking Beach, Broken Beach, and Angel Billabong "park fee" padding and parking-tout shakedowns; the scooter rental + dangerous-road injury-scam cycle on the island's steep western descents; full-day driver tour commission stops; ATM scarcity and hotel cash-exchange padding; and the Manta Point snorkel-tour unlicensed-boat safety scam.

![Gili Islands — Sunset jetty view toward Mount Rinjani](assets/cities/gili-islands.jpg)

**Gili Islands** — The Gili T / Meno / Air trio off Lombok's west coast, a car-free party-and-snorkel destination. The Gili fast-boat ticket overcharge and fake-operator fraud between Bali and the Gilis; the Gili Trawangan snorkeling and turtle-tour unlicensed-boat scam; the Gili T magic-mushroom and spiked-drink "party island" scam; the cidomo (horse cart) overcharge monopoly; the dive-shop certification-shortcut and gear-refill scam; and the Gili pier arrival porter forced-tip luggage shakedown.

![Lombok — Mount Rinjani caldera at dawn](assets/cities/lombok.jpg)

**Lombok** — Bali's quieter eastern neighbor, Mount Rinjani trekking, and the Kuta Lombok surf strip. The Praya Airport (LOP/BIL) taxi mafia and Kuta-Lombok transfer overcharge; the Bali–Lombok fast-boat Padang Bai taxi-mafia blockade; the Mount Rinjani trek unlicensed-operator and porter-overload scam; the Kuta Lombok scooter rental pre-damage and "road police bribe" shakedown; Senggigi Beach tout and restaurant menu-swap bill padding; and ATM skimming, money-changer shortchange, and currency fraud concentrated on Senggigi and Mataram.

![Labuan Bajo — Komodo park bay with Phinisi schooners at golden hour](assets/cities/labuan-bajo.jpg)

**Labuan Bajo** — The Flores gateway to Komodo National Park, built around a boat-tour economy. The Komodo Airport (LBJ) and hotel-transfer taxi overcharge; the Komodo National Park entry-fee confusion ("$500 pass" vs the real 500,000-rupiah pass); the day-tour boat operator safety and overcrowding scam; the liveaboard overpromising and quality-mismatch scam; the Pink Beach / Padar / Rinca "mandatory ranger" and photo-spot fee padding; and the Labuan Bajo ATM skimming and remote-area cash-fraud concentrated on the short main-road bank row.

![Batam — Barelang Bridge toward Singapore's skyline at dusk](assets/cities/batam.jpg)

**Batam** — The Riau Archipelago island 40 minutes by ferry from Singapore, built as a weekend getaway and duty-free strip. The ferry-from-Singapore Harbourfront/Tanah Merah overcharge and "tourist tax" scam at Batam Center; the Batam Airport (BTH) and hotel-transfer taxi overcharge; the massage parlour / KTV "plus plus" extras extortion scam; the money-changer sleight-of-hand and street-corner exchange fraud on Nagoya Hill; the duty-free shopping-mall street-tout commission scam; and the Nagoya ATM skimming and credit-card fraud network.
