Ijen Crater is East Java's second volcano day-trip — the turquoise acid lake and the famous blue-flame sulfur vents near Banyuwangi, reached through the Paltuding base station for a 3-km predawn hike. The scam ecosystem is unusually specific because almost every traveler arrives through a Bromo-Ijen combo package and is funneled through Paltuding's single entrance gate. The *Paltuding mandatory-guide shakedown* is the flagship: a man in a volunteer vest tells arrivals the park now requires a guide at 800,000–1,500,000 rupiah per person, citing "safety regulations," "blue-flame permits," or "insurance." The real rule as of 2025 is that independent walking is permitted on the main trail — an optional guide costs 150,000–250,000 per group of up to four, arranged at the park-office desk inside the gate. The defense is to walk past the Paltuding outer touts, pay the entrance fee (150,000 weekday / 225,000 weekend foreign-visitor rate) at the official booth, and arrange an optional guide at the inside desk only if you want one.

Five further scams shape the Ijen visit. The *gas mask rental overcharge and fake-model fraud* quotes 150,000–400,000 rupiah for a "professional grade" mask that is a cheap paint-respirator cartridge, which is *not* rated for sulfur dioxide at the Ijen crater fumes; the real N95/N99 industrial-respirator rental at the Paltuding park office costs 60,000 rupiah, and you should insist on that model or bring your own N95 from home. The *Banyuwangi Airport (BWX) and ferry terminal taxi overcharge* quotes 500,000–1,000,000 rupiah for the 40-km run to Paltuding when Gojek is 250,000–350,000; book Gojek, or use the airport shuttle service to Banyuwangi town and Grab from there. The *"just missed the blue flame" timing fraud* is pushed by late-arrival tour guides — the blue-flame vents are visible only between 2:30 AM and 4:00 AM before sunrise, and guides who arrive late deliberately tell groups "you just missed it" to avoid the crater descent (which they know they are not qualified to lead); the defense is to book a pre-dawn departure (midnight to 1 AM from Banyuwangi) and verify your guide's descent-to-crater capability before you pay. The *Banyuwangi ATM skimming and remote cash-exchange fraud* works the short bank row on Jalan Kapten Piere Tendean — use ATMs inside the Banyuwangi Mall or inside BCA's main branch, never freestanding machines. Finally the *sulfur-miner photo forced-tip and commission selfie scam* works the crater rim — a friendly-looking sulfur miner with baskets of yellow rock offers to "pose for photo, no problem" and then demands 100,000 rupiah per shutter click; agree a single total amount (20,000–50,000 rupiah) in writing or on your phone before the first photo, or politely decline.
