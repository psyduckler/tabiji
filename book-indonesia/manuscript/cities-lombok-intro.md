Lombok is Bali's quieter eastern neighbor — a volcanic island with the 3,726-meter Mount Rinjani (Indonesia's second-highest volcano), the Kuta Lombok surf strip, and the Senggigi beach corridor. The scam ecosystem concentrates on the airport and port taxi mafia, the Rinjani trekking operators, and the Kuta Lombok scooter-rental and fake-police circuit. The *Praya Airport (LOP/BIL) taxi mafia and Kuta-Lombok transfer overcharge* is the first contact: at LOP, drivers quote 400,000–900,000 rupiah for the 60-km run to Senggigi or Kuta Lombok when the real Grab (or Gojek) rate is 180,000–280,000. Grab works at the designated pickup zone 150 meters from the arrivals exit, past the taxi queue — walk the 150 meters and book in the app. The *Bali–Lombok fast-boat Padang Bai taxi-mafia blockade* runs at the Padang Bai fast-boat port on the Bali side — a visible taxi cartel physically blocks Grab drivers from entering the port pickup zone, and quote 400,000–700,000 rupiah for rides Grab prices at 100,000; the workaround is to walk 300 meters outside the port gate toward the main road and re-book.

Four further scams shape the Lombok visit. The *Mount Rinjani trek unlicensed-operator and porter-overload scam* is the most serious one on the island — the real Rinjani trek is a 2-to-4-day expedition to the Segara Anak caldera lake; unlicensed operators in Senggigi and Kuta Lombok quote 2–4 million rupiah for packages that use overloaded porters (30+ kg per porter for 3 days), skip safety briefings, and have no emergency equipment. The Lombok trekking association publishes a vetted operator list through the Rinjani Trekking Club and the Rinjani National Park office; fair market price for a well-run 3-day trek is 4–6 million rupiah per person all-in, with porters limited to 25 kg per person. The *Kuta Lombok scooter rental pre-damage and "road police bribe" shakedown* is the Kuta Lombok signature scam — rental shops quote 80,000–100,000 rupiah per day for scooters with pre-existing damage, and an accomplice in a yellow vest stops riders 3–5 km down the road to demand a 300,000-rupiah "road police bribe" to "avoid a real ticket." The defense is the Bali scooter protocol (walk-around video, passport photocopy only, 1-million-rupiah cash deposit) plus the fake-police protocol: ask "*saya mau tilang*" ("I want the official ticket, please"); if the "officer" has no printed tilang form, he's a confederate and will wave you on. The *Senggigi Beach tout and restaurant menu-swap bill padding* works the Senggigi tourist strip — touts lead visitors to restaurants where the posted English menu prices are replaced at the table with a higher "peak hour" menu; always request the original posted menu in writing, and pay only the posted amount. Finally the *Lombok ATM skimming, money-changer shortchange, and currency fraud* concentrates on Senggigi and Mataram's bank rows — use ATMs only inside BCA or BNI bank lobbies, and use PT Central Kuta or Dirgahayu Valuta Prima for currency exchange, counting every note before handing over your dollars/euros.
