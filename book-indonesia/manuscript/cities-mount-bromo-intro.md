Mount Bromo is East Java's iconic sunrise-volcano tour — a 2,329-meter active caldera in the Bromo Tengger Semeru National Park, reached through the village of Cemoro Lawang and viewed from either King Kong Hill or the Penanjakan viewpoint. Almost every tourist arrives through a jeep-tour package that the *Cemoro Lawang jeep cartel* controls end-to-end: the cartel is an informal coordination of about sixty 4WD-jeep owners who quote a starting price of 700,000 rupiah per jeep for a "sunrise-and-crater" combo and then quietly layer additional fees at each stop — King Kong Hill viewpoint parking (120,000), Bromo crater horse ride "not included" (400,000 one-way plus return), and an unannounced stop at a "traditional Tengger village" where a purchase of "volcanic sand souvenirs" is expected. The *Bromo Tengger Tourism Office* has published a licensed-operator list; the defense is to book through a Probolinggo-based operator with a visible license number (typical fair rate: 750,000–900,000 rupiah all-in for a private jeep with four passengers) rather than through a Cemoro Lawang guesthouse, which takes 30–40% commission.

Five further scams shape the Bromo visit. The *national park entry-fee confusion* quotes foreign visitors 620,000 rupiah on weekdays or 765,000 on weekends — these are the real weekday/weekend foreign-visitor rates, but scalpers outside the gate sell "skip-the-line" tickets at 1,000,000–1,500,000 that are fake paper stubs; buy only at the official Cemoro Lawang, Ngadas, or Wonokitri gates, cash only, and keep the printed ticket. The *horse rental forced-tip and crater-approach shakedown* works the final 1-km walk from the parking area to the Bromo crater rim — horse owners quote 150,000 rupiah "to the crater" and then demand 300,000 "return tip" at the top, blocking the only path back down; fix the round-trip price in writing before mounting, and budget 400,000 rupiah total. The *Bromo–Ijen combo overpromise* sells a "magic 2-day sunrise at Bromo, blue flame at Ijen" package that pushes travelers through 36 hours with three hours of sleep — the defense is to book the two separately with a rest day in Banyuwangi between. The *King Kong Hill viewpoint fee padding* adds 50,000–100,000 "parking tout" fees that aren't part of the park fee — either walk the marked trail from Penanjakan (free) or pay the posted Cemoro Lawang parking rate (15,000). Finally the *altitude-sickness fake-medical upsell* works travelers struggling with the 2,300-meter elevation — a "park medic" offers "oxygen and aspirin" at 300,000–500,000 rupiah; the defense is to bring basic altitude medication (acetazolamide) in your travel kit, drink water, and descend if symptoms persist.
