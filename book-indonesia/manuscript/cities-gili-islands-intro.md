The Gili Islands are a three-island cluster — Gili Trawangan (the party island), Gili Meno (the quiet one), and Gili Air (the middle ground) — off Lombok's west coast, a 90-minute fast-boat ride from Bali. Each island is car-free: transport is by cidomo (horse cart) and bicycle, snorkeling is the main daytime activity, and Trawangan's nightlife is the core of the scam economy. The *Gili fast-boat ticket overcharge and fake-operator fraud* is the first-contact scam at Padang Bai (Bali side) — operators quote 600,000–900,000 rupiah one-way on boats that are either unlicensed or non-existent; the real licensed operators (Eka Jaya, Gili Getaway, Wahana Gili Ocean, BlueWater Express) run at 400,000–700,000 round-trip, and the Ministry of Transport publishes a licensed fast-boat operator list at *perhubungan.go.id*.

Five further scams shape the Gili visit. The *Gili Trawangan snorkel and turtle-tour unlicensed-boat scam* runs out of the island's beachfront — operators quote 150,000 rupiah for a "turtle spot and three-island snorkel" on boats without life vests or emergency radios; the defense is the same as Nusa Penida's — book only at the Gili Trawangan Tourism Information Desk (near the harbor) with a licensed operator, pay 200,000–300,000 rupiah for the safe boat version, check for life jackets before boarding, and stick to daylight snorkel times. The *Gili T magic-mushroom and spiked-drink "party island" scam* is the highest-stakes scam here — bars along the Trawangan night strip openly advertise "magic mushroom shakes" that are either genuine (illegal under Indonesian law, with serious penalties including deportation and prison) or a spiked decoy whose purpose is to incapacitate the customer for theft; never consume unmarked liquids, never accept an opened drink from a stranger, and if in doubt go to the known-reputable bars (Pink Coco, Sama Sama, Lava Bar on Trawangan). The *cidomo (horse cart) overcharge monopoly* is the island's only cart transport — drivers quote 200,000–500,000 rupiah for short island runs that the posted rate board at the harbor shows as 50,000–100,000; use the posted rate or walk (the islands are 2–3 km across). The *Gili dive-shop certification-shortcut and gear-refill scam* targets open-water certification tourists — some Trawangan dive shops compress 4-day Open Water courses into 2 days, skip nitrox or equipment-safety briefings, and sell "certification cards" that are not valid PADI or SSI credentials; book only at shops listed on *padi.com* or *divessi.com* (Blue Marlin, Trawangan Dive, Manta Dive are verified). Finally the *Gili arrival porter forced-tip and luggage carry shakedown* hits at every fast-boat arrival — a porter grabs your bag as you disembark, carries it four minutes to your bungalow, and demands 500,000 rupiah; the fair tip for a carried bag is 20,000–50,000 rupiah total, paid only after you agree in advance, and you are always within your rights to carry your own bag (the islands are flat).
