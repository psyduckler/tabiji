Lanzarote is the quietest of the Canary Islands' four major tourist islands, and its scam scene reflects the lower volume. The ochre volcanic landscapes of Timanfaya, César Manrique's architectural interventions at Jameos del Agua and Mirador del Río, and the slower pace of Playa Blanca and Costa Teguise produce a meaningfully gentler tourist-approach environment than Ibiza or Tenerife. What happens, happens in four narrow clusters: buggy-rental underside-damage gambits in Playa Blanca, Costa Teguise scooter-scratch variants of the Ibiza script, Arrecife taxi flat-fares from ACE Airport, and Timanfaya "private-guide" approaches.

The **buggy-rental underside-damage gambit** is the volume scam. Shops on the Playa Blanca promenade rent beach-buggies — the open-top, two-seat off-roaders — for 70–100 € a day, with a 400 € cash deposit. On return, the shop owner crawls underneath the buggy and "discovers" a dent or an oil leak that nobody inspected at pickup. The defense is a two-minute iPhone video of the entire underside at pickup, date-stamped and time-stamped by the phone's own metadata.

The risk radiates out in three corridors. **Costa Teguise scooter-scratch** returns against the 400 € deposit match Ibiza's. **Arrecife airport (ACE) flat-fare taxis** quote 25 € into the center for a regulated 15 € metered ride. And **Timanfaya "private-guide" approaches** at the park entrance offer 25 € walking tours that bypass the official national-park *ruta de los volcanes* coach (which is the only legal way to drive inside the protected zone, and is included in the 12 € park entry). A safe default: video-record buggies and scooters at pickup for a full minute; take the official airport taxi rank or **Bus 22 / Bus 23** into Arrecife at 1.40 €; ignore every Timanfaya "private-guide" offer and take only the official park coach from the Islote de Hilario visitor center; pay rentals by credit card; and call **091** or **112**. Lanzarote's municipal tourist-help is in Arrecife on Avenida de la Marina.
