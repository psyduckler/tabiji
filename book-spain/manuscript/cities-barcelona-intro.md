Barcelona is, by Mossos d'Esquadra's own published figures, the pickpocket capital of Europe. In 2024, the force logged roughly 140,000 pickpocket-related reports, with the overwhelming majority concentrated on La Rambla, at Sagrada Família, in the Gothic Quarter, and on Line 3 of the metro between Liceu and Passeig de Gràcia. Coordinated teams of three to eight operate in tight clusters — one creates a distraction (the "bird-poop" wipe, the "spilled" drink, the photo request, the map-reading ask), another lifts, and a third receives and walks the stolen item out of sight. r/Barcelona's pinned sticky, updated quarterly since 2022, tracks individual teams and posts photos after arrests; r/AskBarcelona threads from March 2025 capture the exact La Rambla approach: "a pickpocket tried to reach into my dress pocket and grab my cell phone. It was so shocking."

The risk radiates out across four corridors. The **Sagrada Família rosemary-sprig (*clavel*)** women press a sprig into your hand "for good luck" and transition into a forced palm-reading that ends with a 20 € demand; refusing cleanly means not accepting the sprig in the first place. The **Gothic Quarter "friendship bracelet" tie-on** — someone grabs your wrist while "admiring" your watch and knots a cord you now "owe" 10–20 € for — concentrates on Carrer Ferran and at Plaça Sant Jaume. The **Plaça Catalunya fake-ticket reseller** sells "skip-the-line" Sagrada Família tickets at triple the official price, often for the wrong day. And the **El Prat Airport freelance taxi** quotes a flat 80 € for a regulated run that should be 30–40 € metered. A safe default: keep your phone in a zipped inner pocket on the metro and Las Ramblas, never a back pocket; refuse every unsolicited approach near Sagrada Família or in the Gothic Quarter with a firm "*no, gracias*"; buy Sagrada Família tickets only at **sagradafamilia.org** and Alhambra tickets only at **tickets.alhambra-patronato.es**; take the official taxi rank or the Aerobús to and from El Prat; and call **091** (Policía Nacional), **112** (pan-European emergency), or the Mossos at **088** the moment a theft occurs — reports filed the same day in English are standard procedure at the **Les Rambles Mossos station** at Nou de la Rambla 76–78.
