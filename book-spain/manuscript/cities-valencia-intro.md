Valencia sits in an odd sweet spot: the Mediterranean's third-largest Spanish city, paella's birthplace, architecturally stunning at the Ciudad de las Artes y las Ciencias, and yet much lower-volume on scam reports than Barcelona or Madrid. What happens, happens in four tight clusters: tourist-strip restaurants charging for "authentic paella" that arrives frozen and reheated, Mercado Central "free sample" pressure-sells, fake skip-the-line ticketing at the Lonja and Torres de Serranos, and bicycle-rental deposit-holds along the Turia gardens.

The **paella-overcharge trap** is the volume scam. Tourist-strip restaurants on Calle de la Paz, near the Plaza de la Reina, advertise "paella valenciana auténtica" at 28 € per person — roughly three times the menu-del-día price at a family restaurant in Ruzafa or Russafa — and serve a reheated, individually-portioned, pre-made paella that never touched a wood fire. Paella is a Sunday-lunch, cook-to-order dish that typically takes 30–40 minutes; if it arrives in under 10, you were scammed. r/Valencia has a pinned list of "paella that is not paella" offenders, updated seasonally.

The risk radiates out in three corridors. **Mercado Central "free sample" pressure-sells** offer jamón or cheese samples and transition into a 30–60 € pressure-sell of vacuum-packed product, with the hard ask coming after you've already accepted two bites. **Plaza del Ayuntamiento fake-ticket "skip-the-line"** resellers sell entry to the Lonja (a UNESCO site with a 2 € municipal entry) for 15–20 €. And **Turia-park bike rentals** demand a 100 € deposit and then claim a frame scratch on return to keep the deposit. A safe default: eat paella only at places where it's cooked to order and stated on the menu as a two-person minimum — **La Pepica** on Las Arenas beach is the traditional standard, though any place that says "30 minutos" on the paella line is signaling legitimacy; accept no market samples; buy Lonja and Torres de Serranos tickets only at **valencia.es/turismo**; photograph rental bikes at pickup; and call **091** or the Valencia SATE at **+34 963 539 539**.
