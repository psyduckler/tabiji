Palma de Mallorca — the Balearic capital and Mediterranean cruise magnet — handles roughly 11 million airport passengers and 2 million cruise arrivals each year, and its scam scene reflects the throughput: airport-taxi flat-rate variants, Paseo Marítimo scooter-rental damage claims, Santa Catalina and Borne restaurant terrace-price surprises, and Cala Major sunset-cruise "all-inclusive" supplements.

The **Palma Airport "meter forgotten" variant** is the volume scam. The regulated airport-to-central-Palma fare is roughly 20–30 € metered with the mandated 3 € airport supplement. A small number of drivers "forget" to start the meter and collect a cash 40 € on arrival; Policía Local's 2024 "Un taxi legal, un viaje seguro" campaign posted warnings at Terminal arrivals in four languages. The **Paseo Marítimo scooter-rental damage claim** — *"ha rayado el espejo, 300 € por la reparación"* ("you've scratched the mirror, 300 € for repair") — runs against the 400 € cash deposit most shops demand up front, with photo-of-the-intact-mirror-at-pickup defenses working exactly as on Ibiza.

The risk radiates out in three corridors. **Cala Major sunset-cruise "all-inclusive" champagne supplements** turn out to be supplement-only: the "open bar" is sparkling wine at a 12 € per-glass surcharge. **Santa Catalina market tapas-bar tourist menus** aren't always on the posted board — the kitchen produces a "special" at double price when the waiter recognizes foreign accents. And **Borne-street jewelry shops** selling "zafiros" (sapphires) as untreated natural stones are often dyed-glass; the visible inclusions you'd expect in a real sapphire are absent. A safe default: confirm the meter before the airport taxi pulls away; photograph scooter rentals at pickup with date-stamp visible and pay with a chargebackable credit card; read sunset-cruise packages' alcohol policy before booking; ask Santa Catalina menu prices in writing; buy jewelry only from shops with visible EU gemological-certification paperwork; and call **091** (Policía Nacional) or the Palma municipal police on **971 225 500**.
