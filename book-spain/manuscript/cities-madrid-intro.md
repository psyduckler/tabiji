Madrid runs the most theatrical approach-scam scene in mainland Spain, and almost all of it converges on three addresses: Puerta del Sol, Gran Vía, and the Plaza Mayor. The **three-card *trile*** game on Sol, worked by four- to five-person teams since at least the 1990s, is so well-rehearsed that Policía Nacional issued a 2024 public-awareness campaign naming the exact choreography — a player, a tout, and three spotters who crowd winners on the way out and lift wallets in the scrum. *El País* and *La Vanguardia* have documented the script repeatedly, most recently in coverage of the pre-summer 2025 Policía Municipal sweep that detained 19 operators in a single afternoon. The **Plaza Mayor "deaf-and-mute petition"** — a man in a sports jersey thrusting a clipboard under the pretext of a "charity for children with disabilities" — runs as a distraction-theft setup: while you read and sign, his partner is inside your back pocket.

The risk radiates out in three corridors. The **Gran Vía "counterfeit-bill check"** documented by *ABC* in repeated 2024–2025 coverage is the fake-plainclothes variant: two men flash laminated cards, claim they're checking bills after a "nearby bank report," and ask to see your cash — at which point they lift notes during the examination. The **Atocha- and Chamartín-station freelance-taxi flat-rate quote** triples the regulated 33 € airport fare on rides that should clock in under 12 € to central Madrid. And the **Prado "skip-the-line" and "private-guide" resellers** on Paseo del Prado sell tickets for the wrong day, or a nonexistent private tour that never leaves the plaza. A safe default: take the metro Line 8 from Barajas to Nuevos Ministerios or the official airport-taxi rank at the flat 33 € fare; verify Prado tickets only at **museodelprado.es**; ignore any clipboard, card-game, or unsolicited conversation at Sol, Gran Vía, or Plaza Mayor; and call **091** (Policía Nacional) or **092** (Policía Municipal) — or the Madrid SATE at **+34 902 102 112** — the moment a fake-officer tries to examine your wallet or an approach turns physical.
