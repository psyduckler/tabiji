# The Scams: City by City {-}

The chapters that follow document every scam reported in sixteen Spanish destinations. Cities are ordered by how most visitors actually travel: starting with Madrid, the capital and the country's densest approach-scam ecosystem, then east to Barcelona and the Catalan coast, south through the great Andalusian trio (Seville, Granada, Córdoba), on to the Costa del Sol (Málaga) and the Mediterranean coast (Valencia), north to the Basque Country (Bilbao, San Sebastián), west to Galicia (Santiago de Compostela), across the central plateau (Toledo), and finishing on the two big island groups — the Balearics (Mallorca, Ibiza) and the Canaries (Tenerife, Gran Canaria, Lanzarote).

If you're hitting only a few of these, skip to the chapter you need. If you're doing a longer loop, skim them all — the same core scams recur with local variations. The Las Ramblas pickpocket cluster that intercepts tourists at the Liceu metro exit runs identically at Puerta del Sol in Madrid and at Plaza Nueva in Seville. The rosemary-sprig *clavel* forced-reading documented at Sagrada Família repeats at Plaza de España in Seville and at the Mezquita entrance in Córdoba. The scooter passport-hostage damage charge — a shop holds your passport until you pay an invented repair bill — runs on Ibiza, Mallorca, Lanzarote, Gran Canaria, and Tenerife with identical choreography. The flat-fare airport-taxi overcharge starts at Barajas and El Prat arrivals and repeats at nearly every Spanish airport, train station, and pier. Learn the pattern in one city, and you'll spot it the instant you step out of the next metro.

## The Sixteen Cities at a Glance {-}

![Madrid — Puerta del Sol at dusk](assets/cities/madrid.jpg)

**Madrid** — The capital, the Prado, and the country's densest approach-scam ecosystem. The Puerta del Sol three-card *trile* game that teams up four to five people to fleece winners on the way out; the Gran Vía fake-plainclothes "counterfeit-bill check" that lifts notes under the guise of examination; the Plaza Mayor "deaf-and-mute petition" that your back pocket pays for; and the Atocha-station freelance-taxi flat-rate quote that triples the regulated fare into Sol.

![Barcelona — Sagrada Família spires over Eixample rooftops](assets/cities/barcelona.jpg)

**Barcelona** — Las Ramblas, the Gothic Quarter, and Europe's acknowledged pickpocket capital. Coordinated pickpocket teams of three to eight working La Rambla and the Line 3 metro between Liceu and Passeig de Gràcia; the Sagrada Família rosemary-sprig forced-reading; the "bird-poop" stain-and-swipe on Carrer Ferran; the Gothic Quarter "friendship bracelet" tie-on that demands 20 € once it's on your wrist; and the Plaza Catalunya skip-the-line ticket reseller that sells Sagrada Família tickets for a different day.

![Seville — Giralda tower above the Catedral](assets/cities/seville.jpg)

**Seville** — Alcázar, Catedral, and the warmest approach scene in Spain. The Plaza de España rosemary *claveles* pushed by women who insist the sprig is a gift until the palm-reading ends in a 20 € demand; a Barrio Santa Cruz fake tour-guide who collects a "deposit" for a group that never materializes; Santa Justa station freelance taxis quoting 30 € flat for a metered ride that should be under 12 €; and flamenco-tablao "show and dinner" packages that bill the wine separately at 18 € a glass.

![Granada — Alhambra silhouetted against Sierra Nevada](assets/cities/granada-spain.jpg)

**Granada** — The Alhambra, the Albaicín, and the Mirador de San Nicolás. Alhambra "skip-the-line" resellers in Plaza Nueva selling tickets at triple the Patronato price — or for the wrong day; the Mirador palm-reading that starts with 5 € and ends with 50 €; Sacromonte cave-flamenco shows that quote "no cover" and then add a 25 € per-person drink minimum; and the Albaicín fake-guide approach at the Puerta de Elvira.

![Córdoba — Mezquita arches and Roman Bridge](assets/cities/cordoba.jpg)

**Córdoba** — Mezquita, the Judería, and the Roman Bridge. A Mezquita entrance rosemary-sprig variant by the Patio de los Naranjos; Judería fake tour-guides offering "official" walks that include no ticket to anything; Roman-Bridge photo "helpers" who demand a tip after taking your phone for a photo you didn't ask for; and a Plaza de las Tendillas "charity petition" that lifts the pocket signing the clipboard.

![Málaga — Alcazaba above the Mediterranean port](assets/cities/malaga.jpg)

**Málaga** — Picasso's birthplace and the gateway to the Costa del Sol. Airport freelance-taxi quotes that double the regulated fare into downtown; beach-chair rentals on La Malagueta where "10 €" means 10 € per hour and the tally runs at sunset; car-rental fuel-gauge disputes at Málaga Airport that appear only on return; and Calle Larios "fake charity" clipboards that circulate in lunchtime crowds.

![Valencia — Ciudad de las Artes y las Ciencias](assets/cities/valencia.jpg)

**Valencia** — The Turia gardens, the futuristic Ciudad, and paella's birthplace. Restaurant "authentic paella" pitches on tourist strips that cost triple the menú-del-día price and arrive frozen-then-reheated; the Mercado Central "free sample" approach that transitions into a 30 € pressure-sell; Plaza del Ayuntamiento fake-ticket "skip-the-line" for the Lonja; and Turia-park bike-rental deposit-hold with a scratched-frame excuse at return.

![Bilbao — Guggenheim titanium curves on the Nervión](assets/cities/bilbao.jpg)

**Bilbao** — Guggenheim, Casco Viejo, and Basque food culture. The Casco Viejo *pintxo* bar "counter totals" that can't be verified because the tabs are verbal; fake Ertzaintza-vest "ID checks" near the Gran Vía Metro Bilbao entrance; airport flat-rate taxi quotes for the 20-minute ride into Abando; and a San Francisco street sketchy-bar "drink included" cover that transitions to a 200 € bottle-service bill.

![San Sebastián — La Concha bay and Monte Urgull](assets/cities/san-sebastian.jpg)

**San Sebastián** — The Parte Vieja, La Concha, and some of Europe's finest food. Parte Vieja *pintxo* overpricing of displayed (non-fridge) items on tourist-strip bars; la Concha beach-chair rentals that quote per-hour; a Monte Igueldo funicular area with freelance "guides" who attach to groups and demand tips; and Boulevard-promenade "charity" clipboard approaches during high season.

![Santiago de Compostela — Cathedral towers at the Obradoiro](assets/cities/santiago-de-compostela.jpg)

**Santiago de Compostela** — The Camino's finish and Galicia's medieval heart. Plaza do Obradoiro fake *Compostela* certificate offers at 20 € (the real Pilgrim's Office issues them free); Rúa do Franco restaurant per-person-minimum pitches that surprise two-person tables with four-person bills; knock-off pilgrim-shell jewelry sold as silver; and post-Mass cathedral-area "blessing" cash requests from non-clerical approaches.

![Toledo — walled city above the Tajo gorge](assets/cities/toledo.jpg)

**Toledo** — Three-cultures city, forty minutes from Madrid. Puerta de Bisagra freelance-taxi flat-rate quotes from the AVE station (regulated rate: 5.80 €; tout quote: 20 €); Damascene-souvenir shops pushing "handmade" steel that's machine-stamped; Santo Tomé photo-tour "helpers" angling for a tip; and a Plaza de Zocodover sketchy-guide approach that attaches to small groups at the Alcázar.

![Palma de Mallorca — La Seu Cathedral above the harbor](assets/cities/palma-de-mallorca.jpg)

**Palma de Mallorca** — Balearic capital and Mediterranean cruise magnet. Airport "meter forgotten" cash-only taxi variants; Paseo Marítimo scooter-rental damage claims (mirror-scratch, mirror-crack, mirror-cost-300 €); Cala Major sunset-cruise "all-inclusive" champagne supplements; Santa Catalina market *tapas*-bar tourist menus that aren't on the posted board; and a Borne-street jewelry variant selling dyed-glass "sapphires."

![Ibiza — Dalt Vila above the Mediterranean harbor](assets/cities/ibiza.jpg)

**Ibiza** — Clubs, sunsets, and some of the steepest rental-scam rates in the Mediterranean. Playa d'en Bossa scooter-scratch scams returning 400 € deposits minus "repair" fees; beach-club "free entry until 9 p.m." that turns into a 150 € per-person minimum; Platja des Cavallet jet-ski damage demands at 1,500+ €; and Dalt Vila restaurant terrace-price surprises where the indoor menu isn't the terrace menu.

![Tenerife — Mount Teide above a sea of clouds](assets/cities/tenerife.jpg)

**Tenerife** — Canary Islands flagship and package-holiday central. Los Cristianos timeshare-sales "free gift" lures that end in three-hour high-pressure sessions; Playa de las Américas jet-ski damage claims; Mount Teide "private-guide" approaches at the cable-car base that skirt the official park-ranger service; and Santa Cruz carnival-season taxi flat-fare hikes that aren't posted anywhere.

![Gran Canaria — Maspalomas dunes at sunset](assets/cities/gran-canaria.jpg)

**Gran Canaria** — Dunes, Atlantic resorts, and Las Palmas. Maspalomas beach-chair rentals that charge double if you don't ask for the per-hour number; Playa del Inglés jet-ski damage claims; Las Palmas taxi "special airport rate" quotes above the regulated flat; Puerto Rico catamaran-cruise alcohol-included-but-only-for-ten-minutes bait; and Playa del Inglés scooter-scratch shakedowns that match the Ibiza script.

![Lanzarote — Timanfaya volcanic badlands](assets/cities/lanzarote.jpg)

**Lanzarote** — Ochre volcanic landscapes and a quieter scam scene than Ibiza or Tenerife. Playa Blanca buggy-rental underside-damage gambits; Puerto del Carmen restaurant "fresh fish" priced at 18 € per 100 g without the size confirmed; Arrecife taxi flat-fares from the airport; and Timanfaya "private-guide" approaches that bypass the official national-park caravan.
