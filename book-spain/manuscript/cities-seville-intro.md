Seville's scam scene is warmer than Madrid's and softer than Barcelona's, but more persistent than either. Almost all of it is built on a single interaction pattern: the *clavel* (rosemary-sprig) approach, worked overwhelmingly by women on the approaches to the Catedral, inside the Plaza de España, and in the Santa Cruz quarter between the Archivo de Indias and the Alcázar. The script is identical every time: a smiling woman presses a sprig into your hand as "*un regalo, para la buena suerte*" ("a gift, for good luck"). You accept it. She takes your palm and begins reading. Two or three minutes later she asks for "a small donation" of 20 € — "for the Virgin," "for my children," "for the rosemary." Walking away without paying, once the reading has started, draws loud complaint that is designed to embarrass you into paying; the trick is to refuse the sprig in the first place, with a firm "*no, gracias*" before your hand rises to accept it.

The risk radiates out in three corridors. **Barrio Santa Cruz fake tour-guides** attach to small groups outside the Alcázar, offer a "group walk" at 15 € per head, and collect deposits for a tour that either never materializes or wanders aimlessly away from the advertised sites. **Santa Justa AVE-station freelance taxis** quote a flat 30 € for a metered run that should be under 12 € to Plaza Nueva or Triana; the official rank, with an inspector usually present, is directly outside the main exit. And **flamenco-tablao "show and dinner" packages** in Triana and Santa Cruz quote a headline 35 € per person but add 18 € a glass for wine that isn't on the pre-paid menu. A safe default: never accept a rosemary sprig; buy Alcázar and Catedral tickets only at **alcazarsevilla.org** and **catedraldesevilla.es**; take the official Santa Justa taxi rank and confirm the meter is running before the car pulls away; book flamenco tablaos through their own websites with the drink price published; and call **091** or the Seville SATE on **+34 954 502 080** for help in English during office hours, or dial **112** around the clock.
