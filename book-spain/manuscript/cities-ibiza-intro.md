Ibiza is the Mediterranean's club capital, the Balearic's youngest-demographic island, and — by per-tourist rate — the steepest-rental-scam destination in Spain. Playa d'en Bossa, Sant Antoni (San An), and the Marina Botafoch / Dalt Vila harbor line generate a reliable stream of scooter-deposit, jet-ski-damage, beach-club-minimum, and terrace-restaurant surprises from May through October.

The **scooter-scratch deposit-hold** is the volume scam. Shops on Playa d'en Bossa and Sant Antoni take 400 € cash deposits on a scooter rental — "*por si hay algún daño*" ("in case there's damage") — and then claim a mirror-crack or underside-scratch at return that nobody inspected on pickup. r/Ibiza's 2024–2025 threads collect dozens of 300–400 € deposit-forfeiture cases; the Policía Local on Ibiza has issued recurring bulletins naming specific shops.

The risk radiates out in four corridors. **Platja des Cavallet jet-ski damage demands** run at 1,500+ €, with the deposit usually held on a credit card that can be contested; passports as deposits are a hard red flag. **Beach-club "free entry until 9 p.m."** at venues on Platja de ses Salines and Playa d'en Bossa turns into a per-person 150 € drink minimum once you're inside. **Dalt Vila restaurant terrace-price surprises** — the indoor menu is not the terrace menu, and the terrace is 30–40% higher — concentrate on the Baluard de Sant Pere and the harbor-view restaurants along Carrer de Calvari. And **Sant Antoni "VIP club entry" touts** on the West End sell "skip the line" wristbands for 40 € at clubs that don't have a line. A safe default: pay scooter deposits only with a chargebackable credit card, never cash or passport; photograph the scooter from all sides including the underside before riding off; read beach-club minimums on the official door sign, not the promoter's pitch; ask for the terrace menu specifically if the restaurant has both; ignore every "VIP" wristband tout on the West End; and call **091** or **112**. Ibiza's SATE-equivalent tourist-help is at the municipal tourist office in Vara de Rey; office hours only.
