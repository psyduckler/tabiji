Granada is Spain's great Moorish capital and home to the Alhambra, the most-visited monument in Andalucía and the single most-scammed ticketing destination in the country. Tickets to the Alhambra sell out weeks in advance through the official Patronato de la Alhambra y el Generalife — **tickets.alhambra-patronato.es** — at a standard adult price of 19.09 € in 2026. The "skip-the-line" reseller industry in Plaza Nueva and at the Mirador de San Nicolás metabolizes this shortage: street touts and Facebook-ad operators sell "guaranteed entry" tickets at 55–80 € that either do not exist, arrive for the wrong day, or cover only the Generalife gardens without access to the Nasrid Palaces. Granada Policía Local has issued repeated 2024–2025 bulletins naming the practice; r/Granada and r/travel threads from April 2025 collect dozens of victim reports — including one from a traveler who paid 220 € for four tickets that turned out to be for a date three weeks in the past.

The risk radiates out in three corridors. The **Mirador de San Nicolás palm-reading** — a seated woman who starts a reading before you realize you're receiving one — pivots off a 5 € "gift" to a 50 € "blessing" as the conversation wears on; the defense is to walk past without stopping and enjoy the view from ten meters farther along. **Sacromonte cave-flamenco** venues off Camino del Sacromonte quote "no cover" and then require a per-person 25 € drink minimum that isn't on the door signage. And the **Albaicín fake-guide approach** at Puerta de Elvira attaches to tourists climbing toward San Nicolás, offering "the real Albaicín walk" at 20 € per head for a group that may or may not ever coalesce. A safe default: buy Alhambra tickets only at **tickets.alhambra-patronato.es** (or through the **Patronato's official app**) and buy them the day your trip is booked — weeks in advance for peak season; ignore every "skip-the-line" offer near Plaza Nueva, Puerta Real, or the Alhambra entrance; walk past the Mirador palm-readers without making eye contact; and call **091** or the Granada SATE at **+34 958 808 878** for help in English.
