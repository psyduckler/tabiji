# Scam Comic Canonical Cast

Four canonical protagonists shared across every country's scam comic set.
Rotate them by scam type so the series has cast variety while each individual
character stays visually consistent across countries.

**Paste the full paragraph verbatim** into the `CHARACTER:` section of every
prompt — do not paraphrase. The locked wording is what keeps the model
rendering the same person across panels and cities.

## Cast

### 1. Margaret "Margie" — elder woman (~62)

> A 62-year-old Western woman with shoulder-length silver-gray hair worn under a woven straw sun hat with a cream ribbon, warm blue eyes behind tortoiseshell reading glasses often perched on her head, light olive complexion with gentle laugh lines and a friendly curious expression. She wears a cream linen blouse, tan wide-leg travel pants, and white canvas sneakers, with a small tan leather crossbody bag and a coral scarf. Gracious, cheerful, a little too trusting.

### 2. Priya — mid-30s woman (~34)

> A 34-year-old South Asian woman with long dark wavy hair pulled into a low ponytail under a wide-brim navy sun hat, warm brown eyes, clear brown skin, athletic build and confident posture. She wears olive utility shorts and a terracotta cotton t-shirt, white running sneakers, and carries a charcoal compact hiking backpack with a reusable water bottle clipped to the side. Sunglasses pushed up on her head. Capable, lightly skeptical, a prepared traveler.

### 3. Harold "Harry" — elder man (~64)

> A 64-year-old Western man with close-cropped silver-white hair and a neatly-trimmed salt-and-pepper beard, warm hazel eyes with kind wrinkles, light tan complexion. He wears a pale-blue short-sleeve button-down, beige chinos, brown leather loafers, and a soft navy baseball cap, with a small brown leather sling bag and a simple silver watch. Affable, chatty, easily charmed.

### 4. Marcus — mid-30s man (~34)

> A 34-year-old man of mixed heritage with short dark-brown hair and light stubble, warm medium-brown skin, athletic build. He wears an olive-green polo shirt, dark khaki slim travel trousers, and brown trail sneakers, with a black sling bag and a DSLR camera on a woven strap around his neck, sunglasses hooked at his collar. Observant, curious, slightly guarded but warm.

## Scam-type pairing rules

Target demographic is older and slightly female-skewing, so Margie is the
headline protagonist and should appear in a higher share of scams. The others
provide variety without losing the demographic anchor.

| Scam type | Primary character | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Gold ring, friendship bracelet, rose seller, fake charity/tomb, fake Airbnb, trinket pressure, market upsell, complimentary tapas, souvenir overcharge, museum trap, candy shop, counterfeit shops targeting tourists | **Margie** | Gracious, trusting — the archetypal "easy mark" for charm and emotional-appeal scams. |
| Taxis, airport transfers, car/scooter/jet-ski rental damage, airport hot-zones, train/station helpers, Grab/Bolt cancel-switch, QR code fake parking, transit pickpocketing, fake SIM, fake public bus, Airbnb fraud, overbooking, fake-ticket-fine, Camino pilgrim office | **Priya** | Capable, prepared, pushes back — makes transit/rental scam aggression more visible. |
| Fake police, ATM distraction, petition pickpocket, tailor shops, fake guides, luxury watch grab, bird-droppings distraction, beach smoking fines, wine tasting, contactless skim, minimum-spend restaurants, lucky-face, counterfeit tickets | **Harry** | Affable, chatty, easily charmed — the believable target for authority-impersonation and verbal-con scams. |
| Nightlife (Patpong, Bangla, Khao San, Walking Street), three-card monte, beach theft, beach club menu-switch, ATV/scooter adventure rentals, boat tours, Full Moon parties, bucket drinks, clumsy-jogger bump, phone-snatch, fake festival credentials | **Marcus** | Young, curious, camera in hand — fits active/nightlife scams and photo-tourist targets. |

## Casting principles

- **One comic = one protagonist, four panels.** Don't mix two protagonists in the same comic.
- **Rotate to balance.** Across a city's ~7–14 scams, aim for a roughly-even split (e.g. 3-3-3-3 or a Margie-leaning 4-3-3-2).
- **Use demographic fit over variety.** If a scam targets a specific demographic in the real world (tailor → Harry, Full Moon Party → Marcus, fortune teller → Margie or Harry), honor that even if it makes the cast split uneven.
- **Never change a character's appearance between countries.** Margie's straw hat and coral scarf are the same in Bangkok as in Paris as in Seville.
