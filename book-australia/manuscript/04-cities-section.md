# The Scams: City by City {-}

The chapters that follow document every scam reported in fourteen Australian cities. Cities are ordered east-to-west across the five state capitals, then through the Australian Capital Territory, and finally into the iconic regional destinations — the Great Barrier Reef gateway cities, the coastal holiday towns, the Red Centre, the Whitsundays, and the tropical north.

If you are traveling to only a few of these cities, skip directly to the relevant chapters. If you are visiting more, skim them all — many scams (the airport taxi top-up, the fake Airbnb bank-transfer, the SIXT rental damage charge, the weekend-surcharge-plus-tip-pressure stack, and the cloned-site ticket fraud) appear in multiple cities with local variations. Knowing the pattern in Sydney makes you faster to spot it in Perth or Darwin.

## The Fourteen Cities at a Glance {-}

![Sydney — Opera House and Harbour Bridge at sunrise](assets/cities/sydney.jpg)

**Sydney** — The Harbour Bridge, the Opera House, Bondi Beach, and the country's largest single concentration of airport-taxi overcharge reports. The "top-up" scam at the T1 rank (1,700-upvote r/sydney thread); fake-surge Uber pricing at the rideshare bay; AUD $3,266 lost to a cloned-site Airbnb; bag theft from towels at Bondi; Opal card fare confusion; counterfeit BridgeClimb and Vivid Sydney tickets sold on Facebook Marketplace.

![Melbourne — Flinders Street Station clocks](assets/cities/melbourne.jpg)

**Melbourne** — Tullamarine's in-terminal fake-taxi bait operation is sophisticated enough that the airport has installed warning signs. The SIXT rental-damage phantom charge ($1,040 to $6,500 documented); fake QR-code parking tickets on CBD windshields; CBD and Southbank pickpocket clusters around Flinders Street Station; cloned Great Ocean Road booking sites; rooftop-bar card-reader "system errors."

![Brisbane — Story Bridge and CBD skyline](assets/cities/brisbane.jpg)

**Brisbane** — A long-route taxi scam from Brisbane Airport runs a $45 metered fare to $85 by skipping the Airport Link tunnel. Phantom DiDi and Uber toll charges for Clem7 and Go Between tunnels on trips that did not use them; Facebook Marketplace short-stay rental fraud; Queen Street Mall charity-clipboard pressure; Fortitude Valley tab-inflation and card-reader "tap errors"; opportunistic phone snatching at Eagle Street ferry terminals.

![Perth — Elizabeth Quay and Swan River](assets/cities/perth.jpg)

**Perth** — Australia's most isolated capital runs a distinct scam mix. Perth Airport "fake surge" coordinated Uber gouging during FIFO (fly-in-fly-out) hours; Facebook rental scams that documented r/perth threads name as "rife"; Rottnest Island ferry-package bait; Fremantle Markets mislabelled-provenance stalls; Northbridge nightlife tab inflation; WHV-targeted hostel prepay scams.

![Adelaide — Adelaide Oval and Torrens river](assets/cities/adelaide.jpg)

**Adelaide** — The DiDi "cancel mid-trip" scam (documented: drivers end the ride 100 meters after pickup and collect $15 for a $9 trip). Taxi short-run overcharge from the airport; fake Barossa wine-tour bookings from cloned sites; Facebook Marketplace Airbnb invoice scams; international-student visa fraud targeting MARA-unregistered agents; SA Tourism Voucher phishing emails.

![Hobart — Mount Wellington and Salamanca sandstone](assets/cities/hobart.jpg)

**Hobart** — A short-run taxi overcharge on the seventeen-kilometer airport drive; Bargain Car Rentals' phantom-damage pattern flagged by r/TasmaniaTravel; Salamanca Market "Tasmanian-made" mislabelling where imported goods are sold at artisan prices; fake short-stay rentals exploiting the 1,548-home Airbnb boom; AI-generated fake tour booking sites for Cradle Mountain, Bruny Island, and Port Arthur combos.

![Darwin — Mindil Beach sunset](assets/cities/darwin.jpg)

**Darwin** — The Blue Taxi rank's well-documented airport overcharge (specifically named on r/darwin). Cloned Kakadu and Litchfield day-tour booking sites; NT unsealed-road rental damage claims; Mindil Beach Sunset Market Indigenous-art mislabelling; fishing-charter deposit fraud at Cullen Bay; fake Cullen Bay short-stay apartments that don't exist at the listed addresses.

![Canberra — Parliament House and Lake Burley Griffin](assets/cities/canberra.jpg)

**Canberra** — ACT Cabs' "quoted price" overcharge from the airport (specifically flagged on r/canberra as universally applied). Cloned Booking.com and AllHomes rental listings using Domain.com.au photos; Uber fake-surge gouging during Civic post-midnight hours; AFP / ATO / Services Australia phone-impersonation scams specifically targeting international students; "Parliament House tour" ticket scams outside the free-entry building.

![Cairns — Esplanade and reef fleet terminal](assets/cities/cairns.jpg)

**Cairns** — The gateway to the Great Barrier Reef and the dense cluster of "cheap reef tour" bait-and-switch operators that take $99 full-day bookings to an inner-reef sandspit while outer-reef trips at $220+ visit the actual Ribbon Reefs. Backpacker hostel prepay scams in Edge Hill; 13cabs airport overcharge; FNQ rental-car phantom damage for Daintree and Cape Tribulation self-drives; cloned Daintree and crocodile-tour booking sites; Palm Cove short-stay rental fraud.

![Gold Coast — Surfers Paradise skyline](assets/cities/gold-coast.jpg)

**Gold Coast** — Home to the sharpest timeshare presentation in Australia: the Wyndham / Club Wyndham "free theme park tickets" recruitment on Cavill Avenue that r/AusFinance documents as 64-year contract lock-ins. OOL Airport's $360 ten-minute taxi overcharge; fake theme-park ticket discount sites; Cavill Avenue $60 multi-club wristband fraud; Surfers and Broadbeach short-stay rental scams; jet-ski and beach-toy damage-deposit scams on the Broadwater.

![Byron Bay — Cape Byron Lighthouse](assets/cities/byron-bay.jpg)

**Byron Bay** — The country's most aggressive weekend surcharge stack (15–20% surcharge plus 10% "service charge" plus card-reader tip prompt — a documented 30–35% premium over weekday equivalents). Post-liquidation Bluesfest ticket resale fraud (the festival was placed into liquidation in April 2026); fake villa listings exploiting the peak-season scarcity; WHV backpacker hostel prepay scams; unregulated beach surf-lesson overcharge; festival "private parking" fraud during Bluesfest, Splendour, and Falls weekends.

![Alice Springs — Uluru at sunset](assets/cities/alice-springs.jpg)

**Alice Springs & Uluru** — The Red Centre and Australia's highest concentration of fake Aboriginal art galleries. "Papunya-style" paintings sold as artist-provenanced work; cloned Uluru Sounds of Silence and Field of Light booking sites; outback rental-car unsealed-road damage claims (Mereenie Loop, Finke Desert, Lasseter Highway); ASP airport short-run taxi overcharge; Yulara "cheap hostel alternative" fraud; fake Uluru National Park "combo pass" ticket sites.

![Whitsundays — Hill Inlet at Whitehaven Beach](assets/cities/whitsundays.jpg)

**Whitsundays** — Overcrowded day-cruise operators with long-running TripAdvisor "scam alert" review histories (Whitehaven Xpress, Cruise Whitsundays, Spirit of Whitsundays). Bareboat sailing charter damage-deposit scams; Daydream Island resort-quality disputes that escalate into non-refundable complaints; Airlie Beach backpacker hostel and sailing-package prepay scams; stinger-season "exclusive safe beach" upsells for $180 that are just hotel pools; Hamilton Island short-stay Facebook listing fraud.

![Port Douglas — Four Mile Beach](assets/cities/port-douglas.jpg)

**Port Douglas** — Quicksilver Cruises' "Ocean Walk" helmet-dive upsell, targeted by TripAdvisor reviews as an overcharged onboard sale. Fake rental listings that cost one UK traveler nearly £1,500; Cairns Airport to Port Douglas private-taxi overcharges ($265 quoted against $58–$75 shuttle rates); Macrossan Street outdoor-seat surcharges and undisclosed weekend premiums; cloned Daintree River cruise booking sites; Great Barrier Reef "outer reef" tours that are actually inner-reef Low Isles trips.
