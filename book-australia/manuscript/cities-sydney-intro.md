Sydney is Australia's largest scam exposure zone for first-time visitors, and the single worst category is airport taxi overcharging. The Sydney Airport "top-up" scam, documented in a 1,700-upvote r/sydney thread, adds a fake toll or late-night surcharge to the metered fare at drop-off, and the fake-surge Uber variant — 1,100 upvotes on a separate r/sydney post — exploits coordinated driver cancellations to push the multiplier. Across the city, fake short-stay Airbnb listings have cost Canadian and European visitors AUD $3,266 in a single transaction, Bondi bag theft from the sand remains the #1 beach loss, and Opal card fare confusion costs unfamiliar visitors $60–$100 over a week.

The risk zones are Sydney Airport T1 arrivals, Bondi and Manly beaches during peak-season weekends, and the short-stay booking pipeline for Surry Hills, Darlinghurst, and Bondi. The safe default: pre-book Uber, DiDi, or Bolt before you clear customs; tap your contactless bank card on Opal readers for every train, bus, and ferry; book short-stays only through airbnb.com or booking.com via the app, and never pay by bank transfer outside the platform.
