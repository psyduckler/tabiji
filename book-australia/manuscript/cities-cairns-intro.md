Cairns is the gateway to the Great Barrier Reef, and that single fact shapes the scam landscape. The dominant exposure is the "cheap reef tour" bait-and-switch — an agent on the Esplanade offers a $99 full-day Great Barrier Reef tour, which on the day turns out to be an inner-reef sandspit visit to Green Island rather than the outer-reef trip tourists imagined. r/Cairns's "Honest opinions on Great Barrier Reef — Still worth seeing?" has the load-bearing local quote: "the outer reefs 2 hours out of Cairns and Port Douglas are still spectacular." Authentic outer-reef trips start at $220 per day. A second exposure is the backpacker hostel prepay scam — r/australia's "Backpacker Scam" documents WHV travelers losing $380–$800 to Facebook "landlords" demanding upfront bank transfers for sharehouses that do not exist.

The risk zones are the Cairns Reef Fleet Terminal booking booths for reef tours, the Cairns Esplanade for agent-sold day trips, and the Cairns WHV hostel market for prepaid accommodation. The safe default: book outer-reef trips directly with Passions of Paradise, Silverswift, Sunlover, Quicksilver, Reef Magic, or Ocean Freedom — ask for the specific site name (Moore Reef, Flynn Reef, Agincourt Ribbon Reefs) in writing; use HostelWorld or Booking.com for hostels with payment at reception; and film rental cars thoroughly at both pickup and drop-off, staying off the Bloomfield Track north of Daintree, where insurance is void.
