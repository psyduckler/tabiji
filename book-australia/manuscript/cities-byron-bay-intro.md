Byron Bay runs the most aggressive pricing-premium stack in Australia. r/AusFinance's "What are the rules around weekend surcharges?" is blunt: "In the Byron Bay area, there is a 20% weekend surcharge at every venue." Combined with a separate 10% "service charge" plus card-reader tip prompts starting at 10%, a Saturday breakfast routinely runs 30–35% above the weekday equivalent. That alone is not a scam — weekend surcharges are legal in Australia if disclosed — but in Byron the disclosure is often minimal and the stacking is never spelled out before you sit down. The 2026 landscape also includes post-liquidation Bluesfest ticket fraud: the festival was placed into liquidation in April 2026, and Facebook Marketplace is now flooded with "face-value resale" scams targeting ticket-holders.

The risk zones are Jonson Street cafés and every Saturday-morning restaurant in the CBD, the Facebook Marketplace villa-rental pipeline during Bluesfest, Splendour, and Falls festival weeks, and The Pass / Wategos / Main Beach for informal surf-lesson stacked charges. The safe default: check menu surcharge disclosure before sitting down, decline card-reader tip prompts, book festival tickets only through the festival's own site or licensed resellers (Ticketmaster, Moshtix, Ticketek); book villas only through airbnb.com / stayz.com.au; and use established surf schools (Let's Go Surfing, Black Dog, Surfing Byron Bay) with posted rates.
