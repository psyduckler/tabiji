Adelaide's scam signature is the DiDi "cancel mid-trip" overcharge. r/Adelaide's "Didi cancel scam" thread documents the mechanic precisely: "they claim to pick me up, then end the ride 100 meters down the road. Ride fare adjusts and they walk off with $15 out of my account." The driver exploits the structured cancellation-fee and part-trip-fare logic to collect a disproportionate payout on a trip the passenger never actually took. The second exposure is the fake Airbnb invoice scam — an emailed PDF that looks like an Airbnb confirmation but requests a bank transfer, documented on r/Adelaide's "Beware of this rental scam" post. A third is the Barossa wine-tour booking scam, where cloned sites outbid legitimate operators on Google Ads and vanish once payment clears.

The risk zones are Adelaide Airport taxi ranks for short-run overcharging, the CBD DiDi pickup spots, and the international-student corridor around the universities where MARA-unregistered "visa consultants" target vulnerable arrivals. The safe default: use JetExpress bus (J1, J2, J7, J8) from the airport for a fixed $5.40 fare; screenshot any anomalous DiDi ride immediately and request an in-app refund; book Barossa tours only through verified operators (Groovy Grape, Taste the Barossa, Barossa Daimler, Adelaide Wine Tours) with ABN verification at abn.business.gov.au.
