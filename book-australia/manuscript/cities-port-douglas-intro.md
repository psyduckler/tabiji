Port Douglas is a premium tourism town and the scam mix reflects it. The signature exposure is the Quicksilver "Ocean Walk" helmet-dive upsell — a $175-per-person on-pontoon add-on with consistent TripAdvisor "scam" reviews ("Beware of the Ocean Walk scam"), where visitors feel pressured to buy after having already paid $290 for the base Great Barrier Reef cruise. Quicksilver's base outer-reef cruise is genuinely excellent; the complaint is specifically the Ocean Walk upsell. The second exposure is the Cairns-to-Port-Douglas transfer overcharge — r/Cairns's "Travelling to Port Douglas from Cairns Airport" documents taxi quotes at $265 one-way when the Sun Palm shuttle runs the same route for $58–$75. A third cluster is fake Four Mile Beach villa rentals on Facebook, with one £1,500 AUD near-miss documented in the Aussie Backpacking community.

The risk zones are on-pontoon upsells during Great Barrier Reef day trips, Cairns Airport taxi ranks (for Port Douglas transfers specifically), Facebook Marketplace and cloned sites for Port Douglas accommodation, and Macrossan Street cafés for undisclosed outdoor-seat surcharges. The safe default: skip the Quicksilver Ocean Walk upsell — use the included snorkel or certified scuba dive instead; pre-book Sun Palm Transport, Exemplar Coaches, or CaPTA for Cairns → Port Douglas transfers ($58–$75); book accommodation through airbnb.com / stayz.com.au; and photograph Macrossan Street menus before ordering, since outdoor-seat surcharges are often disclosed only on signage inside the venue.
