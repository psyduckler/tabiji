Canberra is statistically one of the safest Australian capitals for violent and property crime, but two distinct scam patterns target tourists. The first is ACT Cabs' "quoted price" overcharge — r/canberra's "PSA: Avoid ACT Cabs" is unambiguous: "every time I've used a cab in Canberra they have tried the quoted price scam." The driver offers a flat rate above the legal metered fare, framing it as transparency. The second is cloned Booking.com rental listings — r/canberra's "Fake property Booking.com" thread cracked a Lyneham scam because the poster recognized the listing photos from a Domain.com.au real-estate ad. A third, AFP / ATO / Services Australia phone impersonation, targets international students around the universities with fake "warrant" threats demanding gift-card payments.

The risk zones are Canberra Airport (CBR) and Civic taxi ranks, AllHomes and Booking.com listings for Turner, Braddon, and Lyneham, and unsolicited phone calls claiming AFP affiliation. The safe default: use Uber from CBR's rideshare bay (typical CBD fare $18–$28) or Transport Canberra Route 3 ($5 with MyWay); insist on meter for any taxi — it is legally required; book rentals only through airbnb.com or booking.com via the app; and hang up on any caller claiming to be from a government agency demanding immediate payment.
