Brisbane's signature scam is the long-route airport taxi. A Brisbane Airport driver takes the surface road via Nudgee and Hamilton instead of the direct Airport Link tunnel, running a $45 metered fare to $85 or more. The r/brisbane thread "What's the biggest scam still running in Brisbane?" is blunt: "the biggest scam is when they accept the trip, and then just drive around in circles/take the wrong turns on purpose." A related exposure is the phantom DiDi and Uber toll charge — rideshare receipts show Clem7, Go Between, and Legacy Way tolls added to trips that did not use those tunnels, documented on r/brisbane as a recurring pattern worth $3–$8 per trip to scrutinize.

The risk zones are Brisbane Airport (BNE) international and domestic ranks, Queen Street Mall at lunch-hour for clipboard charity pressure, Fortitude Valley clubs at closing time for tab-inflation and card-reader "system errors," and the Facebook Marketplace pipeline for CBD and New Farm short-stay rentals. The safe default: take Airtrain ($21.90) or pre-booked Uber from BNE; check every DiDi/Uber receipt line-by-line for toll charges and request refunds in-app; never open a tab at a Valley club, paying round-by-round with card tap instead; and book short-stays only through airbnb.com or booking.com.
