The Gold Coast runs Australia's sharpest timeshare presentation scam. Street recruiters on Cavill Avenue offer "free" or "discounted" theme-park tickets in exchange for attending a 90-minute "holiday club presentation" — the presentation runs 4–5 hours, the sales close is relentless, and the contract is a vacation-club commitment that r/AusFinance documents as 64-year lock-ins with limited exit options. The second major exposure is the Gold Coast Airport (OOL) taxi rank, where r/GoldCoast's "Was I scammed by the taxi driver?" documents a $360 charge for a ten-minute ride to Surfers Paradise. Fake theme-park ticket discount sites (Thrillopilia, ticketstodo-style aggregators) and Cavill Avenue "multi-club wristband" promoters at $60 per person complete the cluster.

The risk zones are Cavill Avenue and Orchid Avenue for timeshare and wristband promoters, Gold Coast Airport taxi rank for rank overcharging, Google and Facebook Ads for counterfeit theme-park tickets, and Surfers Paradise / Broadbeach / Burleigh for Facebook Marketplace short-stay scams. The safe default: buy theme-park tickets only at themeparks.com.au (Village Roadshow Super Pass) or dreamworld.com.au; take Uber, DiDi, or Con-X-ion shuttle ($25–$55) from OOL, never a rank taxi quoting a flat rate; decline every "free ticket" street offer; and pay cover at club doors round-by-round, never through a pre-sold wristband.
