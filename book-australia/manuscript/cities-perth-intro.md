Perth's isolation from the rest of Australia produces two distinctive scam clusters. The first is the Perth Airport "fake surge" — r/perth's "Uber drivers artificially driving up prices early morning" thread documents drivers coordinating to let ride requests lapse until the surge multiplier triggers, then accepting at 2–3× the normal fare. The FIFO (fly-in-fly-out mining) traffic at 4am–6am and 5pm–7pm amplifies the effect. The second is the Perth rental scam — r/perth's "I just arrived in Perth and I've had a rent scam" post (with 58+ comments from recent victims) names Facebook Marketplace as the dominant fraud channel for sharehouses and short-stays, with "David was desperate for a Perth rental" documenting the same pattern in 2025.

The risk zones are Perth Airport T1 and T3/T4 rideshare bays during peak FIFO hours, Facebook Marketplace rental listings across Fremantle, Subiaco, and Northbridge, and the Rottnest Island aggregator booking pipeline. The safe default: take the Perth Airport train (under $5, every 15 minutes) or compare Uber / DiDi / Bolt before ordering; book rentals only through airbnb.com, booking.com, or flatmates.com.au with in-person inspection; and buy Rottnest ferry tickets only from Rottnest Express or Sealink directly, not from bundled "ferry + bike + lunch" sites.
