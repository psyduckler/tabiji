# Appendix C: Emergency Contacts {-}

Every phone number in this appendix was verified current at the time of publication. Phone numbers do change — if a number below fails to connect, search the organization's name plus *Australia phone* and you will almost always find the current line on the first page of results.

**Before you travel**, we strongly recommend you double-check your own embassy or consulate's current number directly on its official website (the US State Department's travel.state.gov, the UK's gov.uk, Canada's travel.gc.ca, etc.). Save the number and the after-hours emergency line on your phone before departure. Even a verified number can become a wrong number between editions.

## National (anywhere in Australia)

- **Emergencies (police, ambulance, fire):** **000** from any phone anywhere in Australia. English-speaking operators available 24/7. If you are calling from a mobile with no signal, dial **112** instead — it routes to 000 through whichever mobile network has coverage.
- **Non-emergency Police Assistance Line:** **131 444** (reaches the state police service in whichever state you are calling from).
- **State Emergency Service (for storms, flooding, cyclones, bushfires):** **132 500**.
- **Poisons Information Centre (suspected drink-spiking, accidental ingestion):** **13 11 26**.
- **Australian Federal Police (federal crimes including passport fraud, international scams):** +61 2-5126-0000 / [afp.gov.au](https://www.afp.gov.au).

## Card issuer emergency lines (callable from Australia)

When you call, have your card number (or the last four digits), the approximate transaction amount, and the time of the fraudulent charge ready.

- **Visa Global Customer Assistance (Australia toll-free):** 1-800-125-683. 24/7, English.
- **Mastercard (Australia toll-free):** 1-800-120-113. 24/7.
- **American Express Australia emergency card services:** 1-300-132-639. 24/7.
- **Diners Club Australia:** 1-300-360-060.

If your card was issued by a bank (not directly by Visa, Mastercard, or Amex), the fastest route is to call the number printed on the back of your card. That line routes you to your own issuer's fraud desk and they can freeze the card in under a minute.

## A note on calling 000

If you dial 000 from any Australian phone, you will be asked which service you need — police, ambulance, or fire. The operator speaks English 24/7 and will stay on the line. State your location as a landmark (e.g. "Bondi Pavilion," "Flinders Street Station," "Uluru sunset viewing area") or as a Google-Maps pin if you can. If you are calling from a mobile, the dispatcher can pull your approximate location automatically — a rough "I'm near the Opera House" is enough to get help moving.

If you are a non-English speaker, dial 000 and request TIS National (Translating and Interpreting Service) on 131 450 afterwards for follow-up support. The 000 call-taker can request an interpreter directly.

For pickpocket or property-theft situations where emergency response is not needed, dial **131 444** (Police Assistance Line) or file online through the state police URL above. You will be assigned an incident reference number that you will need for chargebacks and travel-insurance claims.
