Hobart's scam exposure is split three ways. The short-run airport taxi overcharge is the most immediate — r/hobart's "What's the deal with taxis?" thread documents drivers quoting $25 for a three-minute drive to West Hobart. The second is the Tasmanian rental-car phantom damage pattern, where r/TasmaniaTravel names Bargain Car Rentals specifically for "multiple bad reviews on poor car quality, scam business tactics"; the Tasmania-wide unsealed-road contract clause (which voids insurance across the Tarkine, west coast, and much of Cradle Mountain's back-road access) amplifies the risk. The third is the 2024–2026 boom in AI-generated fake Tasmanian tour booking sites for Cradle Mountain, Bruny Island, and Port Arthur combos — polished domains, no underlying operator.

The risk zones are Hobart Airport's single taxi rank, Salamanca Market for "Tasmanian-made" provenance-inflated prices on imported goods, and Google Ads searches for "Tasmania tour" or "Port Arthur ticket" where cloned sites outbid the real operators. The safe default: take SkyBus Hobart Express ($22) from the airport; rent only from Avis, Hertz, Europcar, Budget, or Thrifty — never budget-only operators with consistent bad reviews; book tours through Pennicott Wilderness Journeys, Bruny Island Cruises, Under Down Under, or Jump Tours directly, each with verifiable Tasmanian ABNs.
