The 4 Scams

Scam #1
Galway Taxi Card-Refusal and Cash-Only Padding
⚠️ High
📍 Eyre Square taxi rank, O'Brien's Bridge taxi rank, Salthill-to-Eyre-Square route, Galway nightlife corridor (Quay Street, Shop Street)

Rogue Galway taxi drivers refuse card payment, demand cash, and pad the meter on the Salthill-to-Eyre-Square run.

A 2024 Reddit thread with 137 upvotes documented two taxis on the same Salthill-to-Eyre-Square route on the same weekend: one cost 15 EUR on the meter, the other 33 EUR cash with a refused receipt. The next night a driver pulled up, refused to use the meter, and quoted a flat 25 EUR before speeding off when the rider asked for the meter to be turned on.

The trap is the cash-only quote at the rank and the post-fare card surcharge. A 2025 Reddit thread with 91 upvotes flagged Galway drivers charging an illegal extra fee on card payments — the National Maximum Taxi Fare Regulations require taxis to accept card with no surcharge, and the National Transport Authority will fine non-compliant drivers from 80 EUR to 250 EUR per offense. A 2024 Reddit thread with 100 upvotes documents an O'Brien's Bridge rank taxi tapping a card for a 50 EUR phantom charge on a 15-minute Renmore ride that should have cost 12 EUR.

The Connacht Tribune reported the National Transport Authority and Garda joint sting in Galway City that issued on-the-spot fines to more than 20 taxi drivers in a single operation for refusing card payment. Galway logged 37 NTA complaints in the first half of 2025 — the third-highest in Ireland after Dublin (580) and Cork (62) — and 57 percent of all national taxi complaints were for overcharging or fare disputes.

The defense is operational. Use the Free Now app or Bolt for Galway taxi rides — fares are app-locked and disputes filed from the trip history. Insist on the meter at every rank cab and refuse any flat cash quote. Photograph the meter at the start and end of every ride and check the card terminal total against the meter before tapping. Use the Free Now or Bolt app for Galway taxi rides — refuse any cash-only quote from rank cabs and report illegal card-fee surcharges to the NTA at 0818 064 000.

Red Flags

  • Driver refuses to use the meter and quotes a flat fare at the rank
  • Driver claims the card terminal is broken or has no signal
  • Cash demanded at a higher amount than the metered fare
  • Surcharge added to a card payment with no explanation
  • Driver refuses to issue a printed receipt at the destination

How to Avoid

  • Use the Free Now app or Bolt for Galway taxi rides with app-locked fares.
  • Refuse any rank-stand quote of a flat cash fare and insist on the meter.
  • Photograph the meter at the start and end of every ride.
  • Report illegal card-fee surcharges to the NTA at 0818 064 000.
  • Save your bank-app card transaction in case the printed receipt is refused.
Scam #2
Eyre Square Organized Begging and Cancer Sob-Story
🔶 Medium
📍 Eyre Square, Eyre Square shopping mall rear entrance, Shop Street pedestrian zone, Quay Street, Salthill Promenade

Organized street beggars work Eyre Square and Shop Street with rehearsed 5-EUR cash-need scripts that escalate into fake-cancer direct messages on Reddit.

A 2024 Reddit thread with 219 upvotes documented the seasonal pattern in detail — the same crews leave Galway after Christmas and return by Saint Patrick's Day, working in coordinated rotations with a meeting in Eyre Square each morning to assign pitches. A 2025 Reddit thread with 227 upvotes traced an identical fake-disabled and fake-injured-dog mould the operators run in Edinburgh, Dublin, and Galway with the same props.

The pivot is the fake-soldier or fake-injured variant. A camo-jacketed beggar at the back entrance of the Eyre Square shopping mall asks tourists in English for 5 EUR to 10 EUR to get home; a clown-costumed scammer on Shop Street targets children and teenagers, demanding money once a balloon is accepted. A 2025 Reddit thread with 80 upvotes warned about the Shop Street clown variant directly. Reddit commenters describe seeing the same operators driving 2022 Mercedes cars after the day's work, and several note 500 EUR per weekend is the documented haul.

The online variant is more sophisticated. A 2025 Reddit thread with 217 upvotes exposed a known Irish scammer working the Galway subreddit under the alias Julie, claiming stage-4 breast cancer and requesting Revolut transfers from sympathetic locals — the same person had previously been investigated by Garda fraud and named in the Crimeworld report. Several commenters confirmed they had transferred money for phone credit, hospital snacks, and Easter gifts before the scam was outed. The scammer DM-ed users who posted supportive comments, asking for money in private.

The defense is to refuse all unsolicited cash requests in central Galway and to never transfer money to a Revolut number based on a Reddit DM, no matter how detailed the medical story. Genuine homelessness in Galway is real but rare; the cash beggars on Shop Street and Eyre Square are documented as organized, not destitute. Refuse all unsolicited cash requests at Eyre Square, Shop Street, and Salthill — donate to the Galway Simon Community at galwaysimon.ie if you want to help genuine homelessness, and never Revolut money to a Reddit DM citing a medical emergency.

Red Flags

  • Approach is in English at Eyre Square or Shop Street
  • Beggar wears camo jacket or clown costume as a sympathy signal
  • Story involves urgent need to get home with no specific destination
  • Reddit DM asks for Revolut transfer to support a medical emergency
  • Same operator visible on the same pitch every morning at the same time

How to Avoid

  • Refuse all unsolicited cash requests at Eyre Square, Shop Street, and Salthill.
  • Donate to Galway Simon Community at galwaysimon.ie if you want to help.
  • Never transfer money via Revolut to a Reddit DM with a medical sob-story.
  • Steer children and teenagers away from any costumed character demanding cash.
  • Report aggressive begging to the Garda confidential line at 1800 666 111.
Scam #3
Galway Daft.ie Phantom-Landlord Deposit Fraud
🔶 Medium
📍 Galway city center rental listings, Salthill apartment blocks, Knocknacarra room shares, Corrib Park area, Westside student lets

Daft.ie phantom-landlord scams target Galway tourists, students, and short-term tenants with cloned listings, fake viewings, and wire-only deposit requests.

A 2025 Reddit thread with 142 upvotes documented a Galway Daft.ie ad where the email reply read like a Facebook Marketplace template — the supposed landlord was abroad, would not show the property, and asked for one-month deposit by transfer to release the keys. Garda national rental-fraud guidance at garda.ie warns the same pattern claims hundreds of victims across Ireland every year.

The pivot is the in-person variant. A 2025 Reddit thread with 91 upvotes documented an elaborate Knocknacarra room scam where a tenant showed at least 20 viewers around a single room across four 30-minute slots. Every viewer was told to email the landlord afterwards, and the landlord then asked all of them for a deposit to lock in their place. The phone number in the email belonged to an unrelated woman. Photographs in the listing turned out to be pulled from another country — air-conditioning units and non-Irish electrical sockets in the bathrooms gave it away.

The Irish Times documented a 2,500 EUR Galway loss to a phantom landlord using the alias Lauren O'Sullivan who claimed Covid-19 restrictions prevented an in-person viewing. The Garda warning is unambiguous: do not send a deposit to anyone who will not meet you at the property and show photo ID. Daft.ie's own safety page at support.daft.ie warns of the same pattern with the same prevention advice.

The defense is structural. Verify every Galway rental listing on daft.ie or rent.ie — refuse any landlord using a foreign mobile, foreign IBAN, or Telegram contact. Insist on an in-person viewing and photo ID. Never wire a deposit before signing a Residential Tenancies Board (RTB)-compliant lease. Pay deposits by card with chargeback protection, never by bank transfer or crypto. Refuse any Galway landlord who will not show the property in person — demand photo ID at the viewing and pay deposits only by card with chargeback protection, never by wire or crypto.

Red Flags

  • Landlord claims to be abroad and unable to show the property
  • Listing has photographs with non-Irish plug sockets or air-conditioning units
  • Phone number in the email belongs to someone who does not know the landlord
  • Deposit demanded by bank transfer, Revolut, or crypto only
  • Rent figure is well below the Galway market rate for the apartment type

How to Avoid

  • Refuse any Galway landlord who will not show the property in person.
  • Verify every Daft.ie or rent.ie listing has a Residential Tenancies Board (RTB) match.
  • Demand photo ID and a printed lease at the in-person viewing.
  • Pay deposits only by card with chargeback protection — never wire or crypto.
  • Report rental fraud to the Garda at garda.ie/en/crime/fraud/.
Scam #4
Aran Islands Ferry Reseller-Site Booking Scam
🔶 Medium
📍 Galway City Docks ferry departure, Rossaveel ferry terminal, Doolin ferry pier, online ferry booking searches

Lookalike Aran Islands ferry booking websites take credit-card payments, then send a midnight cancellation email before the sail and disappear with the money.

The visitaran.ie pattern is the documented anchor — actual Doolin Ferries staff at the pier told scammed customers to cancel their credit cards because the booking site was running a confirmed scam. Garda complaints about the third-party reseller pattern are on file. Customers who arrived at the dock with a visitaran.ie confirmation found no booking on the operator manifest and lost the deposit (110 EUR per ticket in one documented case).

The trap is the search-result hijack. Tourists searching for Aran Islands ferry tickets see a high-ranking lookalike URL, the page mimics the real Aran Island Ferries livery, and the booking flow runs through a Stripe checkout that issues a real receipt. The midnight cancellation email arrives 6 to 12 hours before departure, citing weather, safety, or operational issues. By the time the customer reaches the pier the next morning, the scammer has already taken the card payment and the website has rotated to a new domain.

The Aran Island Ferries operator at aranislandferries.com runs the only year-round service from Rossaveel — adult single 22 EUR, return 34 EUR — with a Galway City Docks seasonal route running March to September at 30 EUR adult one-way. Doolin Ferries at doolinferry.com is the alternative from County Clare. The Cliffs of Moher tourist attraction at cliffsofmoher.ie sells direct admission at 12 EUR online, and the Galway Express bus to the cliffs at galwaytourism.ie runs 45 EUR for the bus plus admission combo. Anything more expensive that does not match these operator URLs is a reseller markup or a scam.

The defense is strict URL verification. Type the operator domain into the address bar manually and confirm the URL matches aranislandferries.com, doolinferry.com, or cliffsofmoher.ie before entering card details. Reseller platforms (Viator, GetYourGuide, Tiqets) charge a markup but are legitimate; scam sites mimic the operator brand at the operator price. Pay by card with chargeback protection, never by bank transfer. If the confirmation arrives from an unfamiliar domain, dispute it within 24 hours and call the operator to verify the manifest. Book Aran Islands ferries only at aranislandferries.com or doolinferry.com, and Cliffs of Moher admission at cliffsofmoher.ie — never click a search-result link to an unverified reseller.

Red Flags

  • Booking site URL is a lookalike of the operator domain with extra words
  • Cancellation email arrives at midnight 6-12 hours before departure
  • Confirmation page does not match the operator livery character for character
  • Customer-service phone number on the booking page does not answer
  • Booking total is identical to the operator price with no service-fee disclosure

How to Avoid

  • Book Aran Islands ferries only at aranislandferries.com or doolinferry.com.
  • Buy Cliffs of Moher admission only at cliffsofmoher.ie.
  • Type the operator domain manually rather than clicking a search-result link.
  • Pay by card with chargeback protection, never by bank transfer or crypto.
  • Dispute any unfamiliar-domain booking within 24 hours via your card issuer.

🆘 What to Do If You Get Scammed

📋 File a Police Report

Go to the nearest An Garda Síochána station. Call 999 or 112. Get an official crime report — you'll need this for insurance claims. You can also report online at garda.ie.

💳 Cancel Your Cards

Call your bank immediately. Most have 24/7 numbers on the back of the card (keep a photo saved separately). Block any suspicious transactions before the thieves use your details.

🛂 Lost Passport?

Contact your nearest embassy or consulate. The US Embassy in Dublin is at 42 Elgin Road, Ballsbridge, Dublin 4. For emergencies: +353 1-668-8777.

📱 Track Your Device

If your phone was stolen, use Find My (iPhone) or Find My Device (Android) from another device. Don't confront thieves yourself — share the location with police instead.
