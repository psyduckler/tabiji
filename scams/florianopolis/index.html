Scam #1
The FLN Special Taxi Kiosk
🔶 Medium
📍 Hercílio Luz International Airport (FLN) arrivals, Centro / Lagoa da Conceição / Praia Mole / Jurerê transfer routes, FLN 'Special Taxi' kiosks, beach-zone hotel drop-offs

A "Special Taxi" kiosk inside FLN arrivals quotes R$250 to your Lagoa pousada while you're hauling beach bags from a summer flight. The real Uber fare is R$50–R$120 depending on which beach you're heading to, and the Consórcio Fênix bus to TICEN is R$5.50.

Florianópolis's Hercílio Luz Airport (FLN, 12 kilometers from Centro) is South Brazil's main coastal arrival point and hosts a 2025 taxi-overcharge ecosystem common to Brazilian airports — amplified during peak summer (December through March) and Réveillon when tourist demand surges and unlicensed drivers flood the curb. You walk out of FLN arrivals with two suitcases and a beach tote, jet-lagged, and a polo-shirted attendant at a "Special Taxi" kiosk quotes R$220 to your Lagoa da Conceição pousada. It sounds plausible until you check Uber.

Real fares anchor at R$50–R$80 FLN to Centro (15 minutes), R$70–R$100 to Lagoa da Conceição (25 minutes), R$80–R$120 to Praia Mole (35 minutes), and R$100–R$150 to Jurerê on the north island (40 minutes). The trap menu starts with "Special Taxi" kiosks inside FLN arrivals quoting R$150–R$300 to those same destinations. Sign-holders posing as "pousada transfer" agents intercept tourists whose hotels don't include shuttle service. Uber driver cancel-and-recontract works the curb-side handoff identical to São Paulo and Rio. "Beach circuit transfer R$400" bundles add commission stops to a route that's R$5.50 on the local bus or R$30–R$60 between beaches on Uber. "Meter broken" yellow-taxi demands materialize at hotel arrival with R$200+ flat-rate cash. And Reddit documents a GRU/GIG-side "cheap flight package to Floripa" tout offering expired airline credits or Bolivian-airline fraud as the upstream variant.

Book Uber or 99 on airport Wi-Fi after collecting your luggage — typical FLN to Centro is R$50–R$80, Lagoa da Conceição R$70–R$100, Praia Mole R$80–R$120, and Jurerê R$100–R$150. Meet your driver at the signposted "Aplicativos" pickup zone. Ignore every "Special Taxi" kiosk quoting above R$150 to Centro, and refuse every "beach circuit transfer R$400" bundle and "meter broken" R$200 cash demand at the hotel. The budget alternative is the Consórcio Fênix bus from FLN to TICEN central terminal at R$5.50 per person (40 minutes). Pre-book Uber the night before for the return trip; never hand a credit card to any taxi driver. Save DEATUR Florianópolis +55 48 3271 6390 (English available, R. Carlos Adolfo Wendhausen, 86).

Red Flags

  • A "Special Taxi" kiosk inside FLN arrivals quoting R$150–R$300 to Centro or the beaches — the real Uber fare is half that.
  • A sign-holder posing as a "pousada transfer" agent for a hotel that doesn't include shuttle service.
  • An Uber driver who accepts your in-app ride, cancels at the curb, and re-quotes cash.
  • A "beach circuit transfer R$400" bundle pitching package access — the local bus is R$5.50.
  • A GRU or GIG tout offering a "cheap flight package to Floripa" — that's the Bolivian-airline fraud.

How to Avoid

  • Book Uber or 99 on airport Wi-Fi — FLN to Centro R$50–R$80; Lagoa R$70–R$100.
  • Meet your driver at the signposted "Aplicativos" pickup zone, not curbside near kiosks.
  • The Consórcio Fênix bus to TICEN is R$5.50 per person (40 minutes) for budget travelers.
  • Don't hand a credit card to any taxi driver — pay in-app or cash only.
  • DEATUR Florianópolis +55 48 3271 6390 (English available).
Scam #2
The Praia Mole Réveillon Cancel
⚠️ High
📍 Florianópolis Airbnb listings (Booking.com, Hotels.com, Airbnb), high-season window (Dec–Mar peak summer + Réveillon), 'aluguel temporada' Instagram / WhatsApp dealers, fake-listing phishing sites

A confirmed Praia Mole Airbnb for Réveillon gets cancelled forty-five days out, you scramble for a replacement, and the same property is relisted at three to five times the price. WhatsApp "aluguel temporada Floripa" sellers demand 100% PIX upfront for properties that don't exist; a "Mega Model house" pitch is a separate trafficking risk.

Florianópolis accommodations spike four to ten times during peak summer (December through March) and Réveillon, and a 2025 cancel-and-relist plus phishing-site fraud ecosystem is widely documented across Brazilian traveler-community channels. You book a Praia Mole Airbnb in October at R$700/night for New Year's, get a confirmation, and forty-five days before arrival the host cancels with a vague excuse. The same unit is back on Booking.com under a slightly different name at R$2,800.

The variants share the same logic. Instagram and Facebook "aluguel de temporada Floripa" pages use stolen photos of luxury Jurerê or Praia Mole pousadas — you pay in advance via PIX, arrive, and find no property at the address. Confirmed-booking cancellations land 30–60 days before peak windows with the same unit relisted at three to five times the original price (the platform refunds the original payment, but you're stuck with prices doubled). WhatsApp "private Praia Mole apartment for January R$8,000/week" demands 100% PIX upfront — Brazilian PIX is irreversible the moment it clears, and the listing disappears the second payment confirms. A "beachfront pousada" booking turns out to be 500 meters to a kilometer inland with no view (always verify on Google Maps satellite before paying). On-arrival bait-and-switch downgrades a confirmed unit to an inferior room. "Cleaning deposit R$600 cash on arrival" never returns at checkout. "Mandatory parking fee R$200/night" gets layered onto street parking that's free. And separately, a "Mega Model" beach-scout offering "free week accommodation" in exchange for a photoshoot is a documented trafficking template — see scam #3.

For Florianópolis in peak season (December–March) and Réveillon, book six to nine months ahead via Booking.com or established hotel chains with platform-protected payment — reputable picks include Hotel Pousada dos Sonhos (Jurerê), Pousada do Pero (Centro), Costa Norte Ingleses Hotel, and Slaviero Hotel. Avoid Airbnb peak-season Floripa (cancel-and-relist is well-documented across Brazil), and never PIX-transfer to Instagram or WhatsApp "aluguel temporada" sellers — PIX is irreversible by design. Cross-reference any pousada or apartment name on Google Maps and Tripadvisor before paying — fake listings have no independent online footprint. Verify "beachfront" claims via Google Maps satellite view. Refuse "cleaning deposit cash on arrival" demands and "mandatory parking R$200/night" charges on free street parking. For budget travelers in peak season, basing in less-stressed Centro and Ubering to the beaches is half the cost of a Jurerê or Praia Mole rental. DEATUR Florianópolis +55 48 3271 6390 takes booking-fraud denuncias within 48 hours.

Red Flags

  • An Instagram "aluguel temporada Floripa" page with stolen luxury-pousada photos and PIX-only payment.
  • A WhatsApp "private apartment R$8,000/week" demanding 100% PIX upfront.
  • A confirmed Airbnb cancellation 30–60 days before peak with the unit relisted at 3–5×.
  • A "beachfront" listing that turns out to be 500 meters to a kilometer inland on Google Maps.
  • A "cleaning deposit R$600 cash on arrival" demand that wasn't on the booking.

How to Avoid

  • Book six to nine months ahead via Booking.com or hotel chains with platform-protected payment.
  • Reputable Floripa picks: Pousada dos Sonhos, Pousada do Pero, Costa Norte Ingleses, Slaviero.
  • Avoid Airbnb peak-season; never PIX-transfer to Instagram or WhatsApp sellers.
  • Cross-reference any pousada name on Google Maps satellite before paying.
  • DEATUR Florianópolis +55 48 3271 6390 for booking-fraud denuncia within 48 hours.
Scam #3
The Mega Model Beach Pitch
⚠️ High
📍 Praia Mole, Praia da Joaquina, Praia Brava, Jurerê Internacional beach club zone, Lagoa da Conceição evening promenade, summer-season tourist zones

A well-dressed man approaches your daughter at Praia Brava with "Excuse me, are you a model? Mega Model agency is doing a campaign." The pitch ends with "free week in our model house" — and Brazilian Federal Police anti-trafficking advisories flag this as a documented trafficking template. Refuse on sight.

Florianópolis hosts a documented 2025 "modeling scout" beach-recruitment scam targeting young female tourists with promises of "free accommodation and photoshoot" that actually carry trafficking and sexual-exploitation risk per Brazilian Federal Police anti-trafficking advisories. The scam runs at Praia Mole, Praia Brava, Jurerê Internacional, and Lagoa da Conceição evening promenade — the same beaches and zones where the family's relaxed vacation guard drops. Older travelers may be reading on a beach chair while their adult daughter or niece is approached fifty meters away.

The mechanic has eight stages. Stage one: a well-dressed individual (often male, sometimes pairs) approaches a young woman (typically 18–30, sometimes a traveler's adult daughter or niece) at a beach club or evening promenade. Stage two: the opening line is "Excuse me, are you a model? You have a great look — our agency Mega Model is doing a campaign and we'd love to feature you." Stage three: a business card or Instagram handle is exchanged with a photo-shoot offer. Stage four: "free accommodation in our model house in Praia Brava during the shoot week" — this is the trafficking flag. Stage five: "flexible payment — we cover your travel, free accommodation, R$2,000 day rate after Day 1." Stage six: once at the "model house," isolation, passport confiscation, and forced-prostitution or trafficking risk per Brazilian Federal Police advisories. Stage seven: the "photoshoot" setting requires "beach swimwear" or "lingerie" that escalates. Stage eight: target demographic is solo female travelers, often digital nomads or backpackers in their 20s and 30s, sometimes 60+ travelers' daughters during family holidays.

Never engage with "modeling scouts" approaching at any Florianópolis beach — legitimate Brazilian agencies do not scout at tourist beaches; they work via established casting calls in São Paulo and Rio. Refuse "Mega Model" specifically (the named recurring 2025 brand). Firm "não obrigada" and walk away — no business card exchange, no Instagram follow. "Free accommodation in model house" is a trafficking template; never accept. Brief any female travelers in your party (especially adult daughters or nieces) before the trip. Brazilian Federal anti-trafficking hotline is 100 (Disque 100) for any concerning approach. Polícia Civil Florianópolis +55 48 3251 0202 for immediate threats; DEATUR +55 48 3271 6390 for incident reports. The US Consulate Rio (+55 21 3823 2000) coordinates with Brazilian authorities on trafficking concerns. Florianópolis is broadly safe for older tourists — this scam targets younger demographics, but family parties should be aware. For a beach experience without recruit-scam exposure, base in Centro or Lagoa da Conceição (less beach-club tourist density than Jurerê).

Red Flags

  • A "modeling scout" from "Mega Model" approaching at Praia Mole, Brava, or Jurerê.
  • An opening line "you have a great look — agency campaign would love to feature you."
  • A business card and Instagram handle exchange with a photo-shoot offer.
  • A "free accommodation in model house in Praia Brava during the shoot" pitch.
  • Targets female travelers 18–30 — including adult daughters or nieces of older travelers.

How to Avoid

  • Don't engage modeling scouts on Floripa beaches — none are legitimate.
  • Refuse "Mega Model" by brand name per the 2025 community warning.
  • Firm "não obrigada"; do not exchange Instagram handles or business cards.
  • "Free accommodation in a model house" is a trafficking template — never accept.
  • Disque 100 (Federal anti-trafficking); DEATUR Florianópolis +55 48 3271 6390.

Scam #4
The Jurerê R$800 Cama Balinesa
🟢 Low
📍 Praia Mole / Praia da Joaquina (east-island surf beaches), Jurerê Internacional beach-club strip (north island), Praia da Galheta, Lagoinha do Leste, Praia da Armação

A polo-shirted attendant at Jurerê Internacional leads you to a four-poster canopy "VIP couples bed" with a R$1,800/day price tag — the regular chair-and-umbrella twenty meters away is R$150–R$300 with a food-and-drink minimum. All Brazilian beaches are public federal property (Lei 7.661/1988); no entrance fee, no chair-rental obligation.

Florianópolis's premier beaches — Praia Mole, Joaquina, Jurerê Internacional — host a 2025 beach-club and walking-vendor ecosystem with VIP upcharge patterns common to upscale Brazilian summer destinations. You walk onto Jurerê at noon and a polo-shirted attendant intercepts you at the sand line: "VIP couples bed for you, senhora? Best ocean view in Floripa, R$1,800 for the day." The four-poster canopy daybed twenty meters away looks the part.

The real numbers anchor honest service. Reputable Jurerê beach clubs — Café de la Musique (Google 4.4+), Donna Floripa, P12 (the original) — offer a chair, an umbrella, and table service at R$150–R$500 per couple with a R$200–R$400 food-and-drink minimum. The trap menu runs alongside it. "Mandatory parking R$100" at Jurerê beach-club entrances pads street parking that's free 100 meters away. Walking vendors with bracelets, sarongs, jewelry, and henna tattoos open with "special price my friend" pitches at three to four times fair market. "Açaí na tigela R$35" against R$12–R$18 from licensed beach kiosks. A "beach massage R$200/30 min" that becomes R$600 "extended deluxe" by minute fifteen. A "jet-ski rental R$600/30 min" at Jurerê against fair operator pricing of R$200–R$350. A "photo with Floripa flag or surfboard R$50" at tourist hot-spots. And a "private cabana R$1,800/day" at Jurerê Internacional when the standard chair-and-umbrella covers the same access to the same water.

All Brazilian beaches are public federal property (Lei 7.661/1988) — no entrance fee, no chair-rental obligation. Reputable Jurerê beach clubs (Café de la Musique, Donna Floripa, P12) charge R$150–R$500 chair plus minimum-spend; refuse every "VIP couples bed," "private cabana," or "exclusive zone" upsell above R$400. For older travelers wanting calm swimming, choose Jurerê (calm, family-friendly), Praia Daniela (north island, sheltered), Praia da Galheta (secluded, walkable from Mole), or Praia dos Ingleses (north island, calm shore) — Praia Mole is for surfers and the party scene. Buy açaí from licensed beach kiosks at R$12–R$18 and book massages at a hotel spa or Spa Costa Norte at R$250–R$400, never with walking vendors. For jet-ski rental, comparison-shop three operators on the beach — fair pricing is R$200–R$350 for 30 minutes. Lagoa da Conceição (the saltwater lagoon at the island's center) offers calm swimming with restaurants — gentler than the Atlantic-coast surf beaches.

Red Flags

  • A "VIP couples bed R$1,500–R$3,500/day" at Jurerê — the standard chair-and-umbrella is R$150–R$500.
  • A "mandatory parking R$100" charge at a Jurerê beach-club entrance — street parking is free 100 meters away.
  • A walking vendor opening with "special price my friend" at 3–4× fair market.
  • "Açaí na tigela R$35" from a walking vendor — the kiosk price is R$12–R$18.
  • A "beach massage R$200" that becomes R$600 "extended deluxe" by minute fifteen.

How to Avoid

  • All Brazilian beaches are public — no entrance fee is mandatory anywhere.
  • Reputable Jurerê beach clubs: Café de la Musique, Donna Floripa, P12 at R$150–R$500.
  • For calm-swim travelers, Jurerê / Daniela / Galheta / Ingleses beat Mole's surf scene.
  • Refuse "VIP bed," "private cabana," or "exclusive zone" upsells above R$400.
  • Açaí at licensed kiosks R$12–R$18; book massages at hotel spas R$250–R$400.
Scam #5
The Lagoa Couvert Trap
🟢 Low
📍 Lagoa da Conceição restaurant strip (Avenida das Rendeiras / Servidão João Manoel Gonçalves), Centro Florianópolis Mercado Público restaurants, Praia Mole beachfront dining, Jurerê Open Shopping restaurants

Lagoa da Conceição waterfront restaurants pad bills with R$25–R$60 "couvert artístico" for one-guitarist live music (legitimate is R$10–R$20), 13–15% "serviço" against the optional 10% standard (Lei 13.419/2017 makes it refuseable), and verbal "sequência de camarão R$200–R$400 per person" without weight disclosed. Jurerê Open Shopping runs 2–3× Centro prices for the same dishes.

Florianópolis's Lagoa da Conceição — the saltwater lagoon area at the island's center, and its main bohemian dining hub — hosts a 2025 restaurant couvert artístico plus serviço padding ecosystem common to Brazilian beach destinations. Jurerê Open Shopping runs two to three times Centro prices for the same dishes. You sit down on a Lagoa waterfront patio at sunset, the host hands you a leather-bound menu, and the bill an hour later has couvert charges, padded serviço, and a R$300 "sequência de camarão" line you didn't know cost what it costs.

The trap menu has nine recurring mechanics. Lagoa da Conceição waterfront restaurants charge "couvert artístico R$25–R$60 per person" for live music that's a single guitarist for 30 minutes — legitimate couvert for a real performer is R$10–R$20 disclosed before seating. "Serviço 13–15%" replaces the standard optional 10% — Brazilian Lei 13.419/2017 makes serviço explicitly optional, and you can refuse. "Taxa de couvert + serviço" double-charging is illegal under PROCON SC rules but appears regularly. Jurerê Open Shopping restaurants run two to three times Centro prices for similar Brazilian dishes. "Água mineral R$18" replaces the real R$5–R$10. "Caipirinha de cachaça artesanal R$45" is poured from house cachaça at R$18–R$30. "Sequência de camarão" (shrimp tasting) gets verbally quoted at R$200–R$400 per person without weight disclosed — the real fair price is R$100–R$180. Bill-switcheroo where the printed receipt total differs from the card-charged amount by R$30–R$100 is the closing variant. And Mercado Público (Centro) tourist-menu spots run 1.5–2× the working-class fishmonger prices for similar dishes.

Before sitting at any Lagoa, Centro, or Jurerê restaurant, ask for the menu and confirm in writing the couvert artístico amount per person (legitimate R$10–R$20), the serviço policy (10% standard and optional under Lei 13.419/2017), and any credit-card surcharge — photograph the menu. Reputable Lagoa picks: Restaurante Pirão on Avenida das Rendeiras (traditional Açoriana, Google 4.6+, R$70–R$150), Aboriginie at Lagoa center (regional, R$80–R$180), Manolo Restaurante (Brazilian-Italian, R$90–R$200). Reputable Centro: Mercado Público stalls — Box 32 for local seafood, Restaurante Kaffa for traditional — at R$50–R$120, plus Restaurante Ostradamus at Ribeirão da Ilha for oysters direct from the farm at R$120–R$250. For shrimp, agree weight × R$80–R$150 per kg in writing before cooking. Order "água sem gás copo" (free tap water) at most spots rather than the bottled R$18 markup. Caipirinhas at reputable Lagoa spots are R$18–R$30; anything above R$50 is tourist-trap pricing. Refuse padded serviço under Brazilian law and request the official PROCON SC complaint form if pushed.

Red Flags

  • A Lagoa restaurant charging "couvert artístico R$25–R$60 per person" for one guitarist (real R$10–R$20).
  • "Serviço 13–15%" presented as mandatory — the standard 10% is optional under Lei 13.419/2017.
  • "Taxa de couvert + serviço" double-charging — illegal under PROCON SC rules.
  • A "sequência de camarão R$200–R$400 per person" verbal quote without weight disclosed.
  • Jurerê Open Shopping pricing 2–3× Centro for similar dishes.

How to Avoid

  • Before sitting, photograph the menu and confirm couvert + serviço + card surcharge in writing.
  • Reputable Lagoa: Restaurante Pirão, Aboriginie, Manolo Restaurante at R$70–R$200.
  • Centro picks: Mercado Público stalls (Box 32, Kaffa); Ostradamus oysters at Ribeirão da Ilha.
  • "Serviço" is optional under Lei 13.419/2017 — refuse if the service was poor or padded.
  • For shrimp, agree weight × R$80–R$150 per kg in writing before cooking.
Scam #6
The Ilha do Campeche Schooner
🔶 Medium
📍 Canasvieiras pier (north-island schooner launches), Praia da Armação pier (Ilha do Campeche access), Praia da Daniela boat-rental zones, hotel-concierge boat-tour bookings

Canasvieiras pier touts sell "private schooner R$600 per person" trips when the reputable group rate via Escuna Príncipe Negro or Trip Floripa is R$80–R$150. "Private speedboat to Ilha do Campeche R$500 per person" replaces the legitimate small-boat at R$60–R$120 plus the R$25 conservation entry. Many of the "private" boats run without life jackets or Marinha registration in summer-storm waters.

Florianópolis is an island (Ilha de Santa Catarina) with multiple boat-tour categories — north-island schooner beach-hopping launched from Canasvieiras pier, Ilha do Campeche day-trips launched from Praia da Armação, and Lagoa da Conceição kayak rentals. The 2025 boat-operator ecosystem hosts overcharge plus unlicensed-operator patterns, including documented serious safety issues during summer storm windows.

Legitimate cost: Canasvieiras schooner beach-hopping (5 hours, 3–4 north-island bay stops with lunch buffet and snorkel gear) is R$80–R$150 per person via reputable operators Escuna Príncipe Negro and Escuna Trip Floripa. Ilha do Campeche day-trip via small boat from Praia da Armação is R$60–R$120 round-trip per person plus R$25 island-conservation entry (mandatory and legitimate). Lagoa da Conceição kayak rental is R$30–R$60 per hour. The trap menu starts with Canasvieiras pier-front touts selling "private schooner R$600 per person" against the group R$80–R$150. "VIP exclusive route to Praia do Forte R$400" — Forte is a standard schooner stop. Unlicensed boats without life jackets, VHF radios, or Marinha registration carry real safety risk during summer storms. At Ilha do Campeche, "private speedboat R$500 per person" replaces the real R$60–R$120 small boat. "Campeche island guide mandatory R$100" — no guide is required; the island is a small public archaeological park with R$25 entry. "All-inclusive open bar premium R$250" is the standard schooner with two extra beers. "Private islands access R$300" charges for islands that are actually public. And "banana boat for kids R$200" at Praia da Daniela inflates against R$50–R$80 from licensed operators.

Book Canasvieiras schooner tours only through Google 4.5+ operators with 50+ reviews — Escuna Príncipe Negro or Escuna Trip Floripa at R$80–R$150 per person for the 5-hour group tour. Verify life jackets for every passenger and the Marinha registration sticker before boarding. For Ilha do Campeche, book direct at the Praia da Armação pier (R$60–R$120 round-trip plus R$25 conservation entry; arrive by 9am for the 300-person daily quota). Refuse every "private schooner," "VIP exclusive route," or "private islands access" upsell — Florianópolis's island coast is mostly public-access. Pre-book online one to two days ahead in high season (December–March). Bring biodegradable sunscreen for Campeche's protected coral. Take Dramamine 30 minutes before departure if you're prone to motion sickness. For travelers preferring calmer water, Lagoa da Conceição kayak rental (R$30–R$60 per hour) is gentler than open-ocean schooner. DEATUR Florianópolis +55 48 3271 6390 for any boat-tour incident.

Red Flags

  • A Canasvieiras pier tout selling "private schooner R$600 per person" — the group tour runs R$80–R$150.
  • A "VIP exclusive route to Praia do Forte R$400" — Forte is a standard schooner stop.
  • A boat without visible life jackets, a VHF radio, or a Marinha registration sticker.
  • A "private speedboat to Ilha do Campeche R$500 per person" — the real small boat is R$60–R$120.
  • A "Campeche island guide mandatory R$100" — only the R$25 conservation entry is required.

How to Avoid

  • Book Google 4.5+ schooner operators: Escuna Príncipe Negro or Trip Floripa at R$80–R$150.
  • Verify life jackets and the Marinha registration sticker before boarding.
  • For Ilha do Campeche, book direct at Praia da Armação at R$60–R$120 plus R$25 entry.
  • Refuse every "private," "VIP," or "islands access" upsell — most of the island coast is public.
  • Campeche has a 300-person daily quota — arrive at Armação by 9am in peak season.

🆘 What to Do If You Get Scammed

📋 File a Police Report

Go to the nearest Civil Police (Polícia Civil) station. Call 190 (emergency) or 197 (civil police). Get an official crime report — you'll need this for insurance claims. You can also report online at delegaciaonline.rj.gov.br.

💳 Cancel Your Cards

Call your bank immediately. Most have 24/7 numbers on the back of the card (keep a photo saved separately). Block any suspicious transactions before the thieves use your details.

🛂 Lost Passport?

Contact your nearest embassy or consulate. The US Consulate General is at Av. Presidente Wilson, 147, Centro, Rio de Janeiro. For emergencies: +55 21 3823-2000.
