The 6 Scams

Scam #1
Brasília (BSB) Airport Taxi & Late-Night Transfer Overcharge
🔶 Medium
📍 Aeroporto Juscelino Kubitschek (BSB), arrivals hall, curbside pickup

BSB airport taxi/kiosk operators quote flat R$180–R$350 to Plano Piloto/Asa Sul (real Uber R$40–R$65), with hotel-arranged transfers at 30–50% markup and unlicensed late-night drivers (after 11 PM) demanding fake R$80 "airport surcharges."

Brasília's Aeroporto Juscelino Kubitschek (BSB) is 12 km from central Plano Piloto (the Lúcio Costa planned-city core) and 10 km from the Eixo Monumental + Esplanada dos Ministérios (the federal government monument corridor). Despite being a capital-city airport, 2025 late-night arrivals show a recurring taxi + rideshare overcharge pattern.

Legitimate 2026 cost: Uber / 99 / Cabify from BSB to Asa Sul hotels (Carlton, Kubitschek Plaza, Grand Mercure) R$40–R$65 (20 min); to Asa Norte R$50–R$75 (25 min); to the Setor Hoteleiro Sul (SHS) R$35–R$55 (15 min); to Lago Sul (upmarket residential + embassies) R$70–R$110 (30 min); to Plano Piloto's Eixo Monumental (Congresso Nacional, Palácio do Planalto) R$45–R$65 (20 min). Budget public: the Ônibus 'Executivo Aeroporto-Rodoviária' links BSB to the Rodoviária do Plano Piloto at R$5.00 per person (40 min), runs 5 AM–11 PM. The 2025 scam patterns: (a) 'Taxi Comum' rank outside BSB quotes 'flat R$180 to Asa Sul' (real Uber R$40–R$65); (b) 'Special Taxi' / 'Taxi Executivo' kiosks quoting R$200–R$350 to Plano Piloto; (c) late-night arrivals (after midnight) — BSB has many congressional / business flights arriving 11 PM–2 AM; Uber wait times spike to 15–30 min and unlicensed drivers fill the gap; (d) Uber driver refusing in-app price after pickup, demanding cash top-up R$50 'because airport surcharge' — Uber Brazil has no airport surcharge; (e) hotel-arranged transfer at 30–50% markup vs Uber (hotel R$140, Uber R$50); (f) fake 'official Brasília tourism airport tour-desk' selling combined 'airport transfer + city-tour R$400' — real DIY: Uber + DIY walking of Eixo Monumental = R$60; (g) for late-night arrivals, some BSB taxis demand a 'night surcharge R$80' — legitimate night-surcharge is limited to 20% over daytime meter; (h) mototaxi street offers at the arrivals curbside — do NOT use with luggage or as a tourist.

For travelers: (1) book Uber / 99 / Cabify on airport Wi-Fi AFTER luggage; (2) meet driver at the designated 'Aplicativos' pickup zone — at BSB this is on the Upper Level Pick-Up / Desembarque Nível Superior (follow 'Aplicativos' signs from the arrivals hall, NOT 'Taxi' ranks); (3) for late-night arrivals (after 11 PM), pre-book a transfer via your hotel or Brasília Transfers at R$80–R$150 fixed — worth avoiding Uber-wait frustration in an unfamiliar city; (4) Ignore every 'Taxi Comum' / 'Special Taxi' quote inside the terminal above R$100 — those are the overcharge operation; (5) Don't hand a credit card to any taxi driver; (6) if your Uber driver demands cash top-up for 'airport surcharge', refuse + cancel in-app + use in-app safety button (there is no Uber Brazil airport surcharge); (7) for return to BSB, pre-book Uber the night before from your hotel — saves 20 min of pickup friction; (8) DEATUR Brasília +55 61 3362 5522 (Polícia Militar Turística, some English); Polícia Militar 190, SAMU 192. Book Uber, 99, or Cabify on airport Wi-Fi after luggage — meet driver at the Upper Level "Aplicativos" pickup zone, NOT the "Taxi" rank. Refuse every "Taxi Comum" or "Special Taxi" quote above R$100 (real Uber to Asa Sul is R$40–R$65). For late-night arrivals (after 11 PM), pre-book Brasília Transfers R$80–R$150 fixed instead of fighting Uber wait times. Red Flags

  • 'Taxi Comum' rank quoting flat R$180 to Asa Sul (real Uber R$40–R$65)
  • 'Special Taxi' / 'Taxi Executivo' kiosks quoting R$200–R$350 to Plano Piloto
  • Uber driver demanding cash top-up R$50 'because airport surcharge' (none exists)
  • Hotel-arranged transfer at 30–50% markup vs real Uber
  • 'Night surcharge R$80' from BSB taxi (legitimate max 20% over meter)

How to Avoid

  • Meet driver at 'Aplicativos' zone on Upper Level Pick-Up (signposted).

  • DEATUR Brasília +55 61 3362 5522; Polícia Militar 190; SAMU 192.
Scam #2
Esplanada dos Ministérios & Congresso Nacional Pickpocket & 'Tourist Guide' Scam
🔶 Medium
📍 Esplanada dos Ministérios, Congresso Nacional, Praça dos Três Poderes, Catedral Metropolitana

Unlicensed "official tourist guides" on the Esplanada dos Ministérios and Praça dos Três Poderes charge R$80–R$150 (real Cadastur-licensed R$200–R$350); phone-snatch crews target older photo-takers; "mandatory" donations are demanded at the free Catedral; and "exclusive Palácio Alvorada tours" are wholly fraudulent (Alvorada is closed).

Brasília's Praça dos Três Poderes (Esplanada dos Ministérios, Congresso Nacional, STF supreme court, and Catedral Metropolitana) is the city's mandatory tourist circuit — and 2025 'tourist guide' scams plus phone-snatch incidents cluster around the Three Powers Plaza.

The 2025 scam patterns: (a) 'official tourist guide R$80–R$150' offered on the Esplanada by someone claiming Cadastur license — MOST are unlicensed; legitimate guides carry a visible Cadastur ID card, are listed in Brasília's tourism office database, and cost R$200–R$350 for a 2-hour tour (book via the official SETURC tourism office at www.turismobrasilia.com.br); (b) phone-snatch on the Esplanada walk — tourists take photos of the twin ministry buildings or Oscar Niemeyer's Congresso 'bowl' roof, a pedestrian or cyclist snatches the phone; (c) 'cadeira de roda acessível' (accessible chair) tourist-desk scam at the Catedral Metropolitana — offers to 'rent' a wheelchair R$100 for a 10-min cathedral visit (the Catedral has free accessibility assistance — request at reception); (d) 'mandatory Congresso Nacional tour R$60' offered in the parking area — the actual Palácio do Congresso guided tour is free (weekdays 10 AM, 11 AM, 3 PM; book at tours.congresso.leg.br 1–5 days ahead, bring passport); (e) 'exclusive Palácio da Alvorada tour R$200' — the Presidential residence Alvorada is currently closed to public tours per 2025 SFP security policy; 'exclusive' offers are fraudulent; (f) 'Brasília by night tour R$350 per person' with kickback commission stops at souvenir shops + overpriced Lago Paranoá restaurants (real DIY: Uber to Pontão Lago Sul sunset + Juscelino Kubitschek bridge photo R$40 round-trip); (g) Praça dos Três Poderes 'model photo' scam — 'take your picture with this historical flag R$50' using a regular Brazilian flag; (h) at the Catedral Metropolitana, 'mandatory donation R$30' demanded at the entrance — the cathedral is free; a voluntary donation box is inside the nave.

For travelers: (1) for guided Esplanada tours, book via official SETURC tourism office (www.turismobrasilia.com.br) at R$200–R$350 for 2 hours — Refuse every street-approach 'guide' offer; (2) the Congresso Nacional guided tour is free — book at tours.congresso.leg.br 1–5 days ahead (English tours available on request), bring passport for security check; (3) for the Palácio do Planalto, free guided tours on Sundays 9:30 AM–1:30 PM only — book at www.planalto.gov.br/presidencia/visita; (4) carry decoy phone + R$100 cash for the Esplanada walk; leave passport + cards in hotel safe; take photos QUICKLY then stow phone; (5) at the Catedral Metropolitana, entry is free (voluntary donation box inside nave) — Refuse every 'mandatory donation' at entrance; (6) for the Lago Paranoá sunset + Juscelino Kubitschek bridge (a genuinely beautiful Brasília experience), Uber R$40–R$60 round-trip from Asa Sul — skip every 'private night tour R$350' offer; (7) for travelers, the gold-standard 1-day Brasília circuit: morning Catedral (9 AM) + Itamaraty Palace (guided tour Wed/Thu/Fri 2:30 PM, free with booking) + Esplanada walk + Congresso tour + lunch at Setor de Clubes Sul (Veleiros, Asa Delta, Pontão waterfront) + afternoon Memorial JK (tomb of Kubitschek, R$10) + sunset at Pontão; (8) DEATUR Brasília +55 61 3362 5522; Polícia Militar 190; SAMU 192. Book Esplanada guides ONLY via the official SETURC tourism office (turismobrasilia.com.br) at R$200–R$350 for 2 hours — refuse every street-approach "guide" offer. The Congresso Nacional tour is FREE via tours.congresso.leg.br (English available, 1–5 days ahead, bring passport); Catedral entry is FREE (voluntary donation inside only). Carry a decoy phone + R$100 cash on the Esplanada walk; leave passport and cards in the hotel safe; take photos quickly and stow phone. Refuse every "exclusive Palácio Alvorada tour" or "all-inclusive Sep 7 parade viewing" offer — both are public-access free events.

Red Flags

  • 'Official tourist guide R$80–R$150' without visible Cadastur ID card
  • 'Mandatory Congresso Nacional tour R$60' (actual tour is free via tours.congresso.leg.br)
  • 'Exclusive Palácio da Alvorada tour' offer (Alvorada is currently closed to public)
  • 'Mandatory donation R$30' at Catedral Metropolitana entrance
  • Phone-snatch on the Esplanada when taking photos of the ministry buildings

  • Congresso tour free via tours.congresso.leg.br; Planalto free Sun 9:30 AM–1:30 PM.
  • Catedral entry free (voluntary donation inside only); Itamaraty guided Wed/Thu/Fri 2:30 PM.
  • Decoy phone + R$100 cash on Esplanada; passport + cards in hotel safe.
  • DEATUR Brasília +55 61 3362 5522; Polícia Militar 190.
Scam #3
Brasília Restaurant Couvert + Serviço Padding & 'Comida por Kg' Weighing Trap
🟢 Low
📍 Asa Sul / Asa Norte restaurant districts, Setor Comercial Sul / Norte lunch strips

Brasília restaurants pad bills via uncertified "comida por kg" buffet scales (10–25% weight inflation), drink-bundled-into-plate-weight tricks, R$40–R$100 inflated couvert artístico (legitimate R$20–R$40), and silent 10% serviço — Lei 13.419/2017 makes the serviço optional.

Brasília's dining culture is dominated by weekday 'comida por kg' (pay-by-weight) lunch buffets and upscale Asa Sul / Asa Norte dinner restaurants. The pay-by-weight format is a 2025 tourist-overcharge vector alongside the standard couvert + serviço patterns.

Brazilian restaurant law (Lei 13.419/2017) makes the 10% serviço charge OPTIONAL. INMETRO (the Brazilian metrology institute) regulates commercial scales — the 'comida por kg' buffet MUST display an INMETRO-certified scale with the certification sticker visible. The 2025 scam patterns: (a) 'comida por kg' buffet with UNCERTIFIED scale padding weight by 10–25% — legitimate Asa Sul lunch buffet R$70–R$110 per kg (reputable: Fogo de Chão Asa Sul, Porcão Brasília, Universal Diner); (b) 'bebida sem conta' (drink without itemised receipt) — R$15 bottled water or R$25 refresco added to plate-weight then billed opaquely; (c) couvert artístico R$40–R$100 per person at Asa Sul + Asa Norte upscale (legitimate R$20–R$40 for a genuine Brasília MPB / samba musician); (d) 'serviço 10%' added silently + menu shows 'serviço opcional' fine print; (e) credit-card surcharge 5–10% at bill-time ('we prefer PIX'); (f) at Pontão Lago Sul waterfront restaurants, 'view surcharge R$30 per person' added silently; (g) tourist-zone Setor Comercial Sul lunch kg-buffet charging R$180 per kg (double regional rate) — check posted R$/kg BEFORE sitting; (h) 'couvert + música ao vivo R$80' bundled as mandatory at bars like Bar Brasília (can be refused by leaving early).

For travelers: (1) at 'comida por kg' buffets, VERIFY the scale has a visible INMETRO certification sticker (valid 1 year, stamped Inmetro Calibração 2025/2026) — if missing, walk out; (2) BEFORE plating, check posted R$ per kg price (legitimate 2026 Brasília lunch R$70–R$110 per kg); reputable kg-buffets in Asa Sul: Universal Diner (lunch R$85/kg), Zuu aA (R$95/kg), Buffet Estoril (R$80/kg); (3) drinks are SEPARATE from the plate-weight — ask 'bebida está separada?' BEFORE you fill water / refresco; pay for drinks at the separate counter; (4) BEFORE sitting at dinner restaurants, confirm couvert artístico R$ + serviço policy + card surcharge IN WRITING; photograph menu; (5) reputable Asa Sul dinner: Mangai (Northeastern, Google 4.5+, R$100–R$180), Bar Brasília (iconic chope + petiscos, R$80–R$160), Fogo de Chão Brasília (rodízio, R$200–R$280); reputable Lago Sul: Veleiros (waterfront, R$100–R$200), Barranco Bar & Restaurante; (6) Refuse 'view surcharge' at Pontão — the view is free, the restaurant charges for food not scenery; (7) if serviço is added against your wishes for poor service, politely Refuse and cite Lei 13.419/2017; (8) PROCON-DF 151 for billing disputes; DEATUR Brasília +55 61 3362 5522. At "comida por kg" buffets, verify the visible INMETRO certification sticker on the scale (valid 1 year, stamped Inmetro Calibração 2025/2026) — walk out if missing. Check posted R$/kg before plating (legitimate R$70–R$110/kg in Brasília); ask "bebida está separada?" to keep drinks off plate-weight; pay drinks at the separate counter. Reputable kg-buffets: Universal Diner, Zuu aA, Buffet Estoril; reputable dinner: Mangai, Bar Brasília, Fogo de Chão. Refuse silent 10% serviço under Lei 13.419/2017 and refuse "view surcharge" at Pontão — call PROCON-DF 151 for disputes.

Red Flags

  • 'Comida por kg' buffet with no visible INMETRO certification sticker on scale
  • Drinks bundled into plate-weight rather than charged separately
  • Couvert artístico R$40–R$100 for a single MPB musician (legitimate R$20–R$40)
  • Setor Comercial Sul kg-buffet at R$180/kg (regional real R$70–R$110/kg)
  • 'View surcharge R$30 per person' at Pontão Lago Sul waterfront restaurants

How to Avoid

  • VERIFY INMETRO certification sticker on kg-buffet scale BEFORE plating.
  • Check posted R$/kg (legitimate Brasília lunch R$70–R$110/kg); ask drinks separate.
  • Reputable kg: Universal Diner / Zuu aA / Buffet Estoril; dinner: Mangai / Bar Brasília.
  • Confirm couvert + serviço + card surcharge IN WRITING; photograph menu.
  • Refuse serviço for poor service (Lei 13.419/2017); PROCON-DF 151.

Scam #4
Brasília Aluguel de Temporada (Airbnb) & Hotel Booking Fraud (Peak Dates)
⚠️ High
📍 Booking platforms, Airbnb / Instagram / WhatsApp 'aluguel temporada' sellers

Peak-date Brasília accommodation (Independência Sep 7, Réveillon, presidential inaugurations) faces Airbnb cancel-and-relist fraud at 2.5x markup, irreversible WhatsApp/Instagram PIX deposits to nonexistent units, and hotel overbooking that "relocates" guests 20–30 km out to Taguatinga the night of arrival.

Brasília's peak accommodation dates cluster around Independência (Sep 7 military parade on the Esplanada), Réveillon (the Pontão Lago Sul fireworks display), and government-calendar events (posses presidenciais every 4 years, large congressional sessions). The accommodation landscape mixes Plano Piloto hotels with Asa Sul / Lago Sul Airbnbs and Park Way suburban houses — all vulnerable to cancel-and-relist plus PIX-deposit fraud.

The 2025–2026 scam patterns: (a) Airbnb cancel-and-relist — host accepts your Independência booking 6 months out at R$350/night, cancels 30 days before the dates citing 'electricity emergency' / 'family illness' / 'host incapacitated' — re-lists the same unit at R$900/night immediately; Airbnb refunds the original payment but you are scrambling 30 days out with peak-season pricing; (b) Instagram / WhatsApp 'aluguel temporada Brasília' sellers demanding PIX-transfer deposit — Brazilian PIX is IRREVERSIBLE, and the listing disappears after payment; (c) Park Way / Lago Sul 'private house rental' via WhatsApp with PIX deposit — house doesn't exist, seller blocks your number; (d) hotel overbooking for Independência / Réveillon — Plano Piloto hotels oversell the peak week then 'relocate' paying guests to Taguatinga or Águas Claras (20–30 km from Plano Piloto) the night of arrival; (e) fake 'Brasília tourism board approved rental' Instagram accounts — no such approval body exists (SETURC is the official tourism office but does NOT approve individual rentals); (f) 'government-floor private apartment with Esplanada view R$2,500/night' — most Setor Hoteleiro (SHS / SHN) apartments have no Esplanada view and R$400–R$600/night is the legitimate range; (g) Réveillon 'Pontão VIP reserved beach-spot R$500 per person' — the Pontão waterfront is PUBLIC federal property; no reservation legitimately exists; (h) 'all-inclusive Esplanada civil-military parade viewing R$350 per person' — the Sep 7 parade is free public viewing.

For travelers: (1) BOOK accommodation 6–9 months ahead for peak dates (Sep 7, Dec 28–Jan 3, new-presidency January) via RECOGNIZED hotel chains ONLY — reputable Plano Piloto: Meliá Brasil 21, Royal Tulip Alvorada, Grand Mercure Brasília Eixo Monumental, Brasília Palace Hotel, B Hotel Brasília, Windsor Plaza Brasília, Kubitschek Plaza Hotel; (2) Avoid Airbnb for these dates entirely — cancel-and-relist pattern is well-documented per Brazilian traveler reports; (3) Never PIX-transfer to Instagram / WhatsApp 'aluguel temporada' sellers — payment is IRREVERSIBLE with no recovery recourse (only use credit card via Booking.com / Expedia / hotels.com which offer chargeback protection); (4) confirm your hotel booking 72 hours before arrival via direct phone call (NOT email); if overbooking is mentioned, demand written confirmation of the alternate property + the hotel chain's transfer commitment in writing BEFORE leaving the original reservation; (5) the Independência parade (Sep 7 at 9 AM on the Esplanada dos Ministérios) is 100% free public viewing — arrive by 7 AM to secure a spot along the barricades; Refuse every 'all-inclusive parade viewing R$350' offer; (6) the Réveillon Pontão Lago Sul fireworks are free public viewing — arrive by 9 PM; Refuse every 'VIP reserved beach spot' offer; (7) for travelers staying in Brasília long-term, reputable apart-hotels (daily / weekly rates with hotel-grade service): Royalty Brasília, Manhattan Plaza, Kubitschek Plaza — all in Plano Piloto; (8) PROCON-DF 151 for booking fraud disputes; DEATUR Brasília +55 61 3362 5522. Book Brasília peak-date accommodation 6–9 months ahead via recognized hotel chains only — Meliá Brasil 21, Royal Tulip Alvorada, Grand Mercure Eixo Monumental, B Hotel Brasília, Kubitschek Plaza. Avoid Airbnb for Independência (Sep 7) and Réveillon entirely — the cancel-and-relist pattern is well-documented. Scam #5
Lago Paranoá / Pontão Sul Pickpocket & Phone-Snatch Ring
🔶 Medium
📍 Pontão do Lago Sul, Ponte Juscelino Kubitschek, Orla do Lago Paranoá

Phone-snatch crews target the Pontão do Lago Sul sunset promenade, Ponte JK pedestrian span, and waterfront restaurant tables — passing motorbike riders lift phones held for sunset photos, "helpful local" photo-takers run with the device, and unlicensed R$200 "sunset cruise" speedboats lack life jackets and Marinha registration.

The Pontão do Lago Sul (Brasília's waterfront restaurant, bar, and sunset promenade on the southern shore of Lago Paranoá, 3 km from Plano Piloto across the Ponte Juscelino Kubitschek) is the city's default evening tourist destination — and 2025 shows a phone-snatch and pickpocket pattern mirroring the broader Brazilian urban-tourism risk.

The 2025 scam patterns: (a) Pontão promenade phone-snatch — tourists take sunset photos of the Ponte JK (Oscar Niemeyer's triple-arch bridge, Brasília's signature postcard view), a passing cyclist or motorbike rider snatches the phone held for the photo; (b) 'helpful local' offering to take your Pontão sunset photo — the 'helper' runs with the phone; (c) Pontão waterfront restaurant distraction — a vendor approaches your table with flowers / jewelry / trinkets while an accomplice lifts a handbag from the back of your chair (leave bag on lap or between feet, never on chair-back); (d) Ponte JK mid-span pedestrian walk phone-snatch — some tourists walk the JK bridge for photos (it's 1.2 km pedestrian lane); motorbike rider snatches from the cycle lane; (e) 'sunset cruise Lago Paranoá R$200 per person' — unlicensed Pontão speedboat operators with no life jackets / no Marinha registration; reputable sunset cruise via Escuna Lobão at Pontão R$80–R$120 per person (2 hours); (f) 'aplicativo fix' street approach near Pontão Uber pickup — 'your Uber app has an error, let me fix it on your phone' = phone-theft tactic; (g) at Pontão parking, 'safe parking R$40 per hour' offered by unofficial attendants — the Pontão has free public parking, paid only during Fri-Sat evenings via the municipal meter (Zona Azul R$3 per hour); (h) at Orla do Lago Paranoá walk-path near Hotel Nacional, after-dark robberies reported in 2025.

For travelers: (1) at Pontão sunset (the iconic Brasília experience), take photos QUICKLY from the promenade or a reputable restaurant's terrace then STOW phone; do NOT hold phone visibly while walking the cycle-path edge; (2) Refuse every 'photo helper' approach — use a selfie stick or ask a restaurant server for assistance; (3) at Pontão waterfront tables, keep handbags on your LAP or between your feet — Don't on the chair-back; Refuse every vendor approach with flowers / trinkets during meal; (4) for the Ponte JK photo, take it from the Pontão waterfront (you get a full side-view of the bridge + city skyline) — do NOT walk the pedestrian lane of the bridge itself (1.2 km with snatch risk); (5) for Pontão sunset cruises, book via Escuna Lobão R$80–R$120 at the Pontão kiosk (verify Marinha registration + life jackets BEFORE boarding); Refuse unlicensed speedboat R$200 offers; (6) at Pontão parking Fri-Sat evenings, use official municipal Zona Azul meter (R$3/hour) — Refuse 'safe parking R$40/hour' unofficial attendant offers; (7) Refuse every 'aplicativo fix' street approach near Uber pickup — no one should handle your phone other than you; (8) Avoid the Orla do Lago Paranoá walking path near Hotel Nacional after dark — use Uber for any transit after sunset in that area; (9) reputable Pontão restaurants: Veleiros (Google 4.4+), Quitinete, Asa Delta, Kobe, Berttô; reputable sunset bars: Sunset Café, Belini Bar; (10) DEATUR Brasília +55 61 3362 5522; Polícia Militar 190. 🆘 What to Do If You Get Scammed

📋 File a Police Report

Go to the nearest Civil Police (Polícia Civil) station. Call 190 (emergency) or 197 (civil police). Get an official crime report — you'll need this for insurance claims. You can also report online at delegaciaonline.rj.gov.br.

💳 Cancel Your Cards

Call your bank immediately. Most have 24/7 numbers on the back of the card (keep a photo saved separately). Block any suspicious transactions before the thieves use your details.

🛂 Lost Passport?

Contact your nearest embassy or consulate. The US Consulate General is at Av. Presidente Wilson, 147, Centro, Rio de Janeiro. For emergencies: +55 21 3823-2000.

📱 Track Your Device

If your phone was stolen, use Find My (iPhone) or Find My Device (Android) from another device. Don't confront thieves yourself — share the location with police instead.
