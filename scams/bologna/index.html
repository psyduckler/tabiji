The 7 Scams

Scam #1
The Via dell'Indipendenza "Cigarette" Pickpocket
⚠️ High
📍 Via dell'Indipendenza (Piazza del Nettuno to Bologna Centrale), Via Ugo Bassi, connecting porticoed streets

Pairs of women on Via dell'Indipendenza interrupt tourists asking for a cigarette, the time, or directions while a partner pickpockets the wallet or bag.

A June 2016 Il Resto del Carlino arrest documented two Roma women (ages 18 and 26) using this exact MO on a Japanese tourist; a September 2025 firsthand traveler post and an August 2025 CCTV arrest on Via Riva di Reno confirm the female-duo pattern is still active in 2025.

Red Flags

  • Approach happens in a high-traffic tourist area
  • Refusal triggers escalation, guilt-trip, or a follow
  • Cannot produce official credentials when asked

How to Avoid

  • On Via dell'Indipendenza, walk on the portico-wall side with your bag between you and the wall.
  • Reply to any 'posso fare una domanda' with a firm 'no, mi scusi' and keep walking.
  • Use a crossbody bag with the zipper facing your body.
  • Shout 'ladri!' if a theft is in progress — Bologna police actively patrol this street.
  • Never carry a wallet in a back pocket in the historic center.
Scam #2
The Due Torri "Spare Some Change" Arm-Grab
⚠️ High
📍 Piazza di Porta Ravegnana (Due Torri), Via Rizzoli, Piazza della Mercanzia

Beggars at Piazza di Porta Ravegnana — directly under the Torre degli Asinelli and Torre Garisenda — grab tourists' arms during a pretended plea for alms and empty pockets in the same motion.

An August 2016 Il Resto del Carlino arrest (44-year-old Bosnian woman with prior convictions, 6pm, Spanish tourist) established the MO; a Bolognatoday report of a Japanese tourist restraining a thief in the same piazza confirms it repeats. A 3am variant turns violent — a 43-year-old French woman was slapped and robbed in the historic center.

Red Flags

  • Stranger initiates physical contact (arm, shoulder, hand)
  • Cash payment demanded on the spot
  • Approach happens in a high-traffic tourist area
  • Refusal triggers escalation, guilt-trip, or a follow

How to Avoid

  • Compose your upward-looking tower photo before stopping — phone out, zip closed, bag in hand.
  • Step back one full pace if anyone making a begging gesture enters your body space.
  • Never let a stranger touch you in the historic center.
  • Carry a daily-use wallet with €40–€60 and keep cards, passport, and larger cash in a flat pouch under your shirt.
  • Call 112 immediately if robbed.
Scam #3
Bologna Centrale Platform & Taxi-Queue Pickpockets
⚠️ High
📍 Bologna Centrale platforms, underground passages, main entrance forecourt, taxi queue; Via Amendola; Via Matteotti

Coordinated groups work Bologna Centrale platforms, underground passages, ticket machines and the taxi queue.

Train staff now announce 'attenzione, pickpocket!' as trains pull in. A July 2024 incident saw a Business-class suitcase stolen from a Frecciarossa during the ~10-minute Bologna Centrale stop. Variants include 'helper' scams at FastTicket kiosks that photograph card PINs and three-to-five-person distraction teams that open outer luggage pouches on platforms.

Red Flags

  • 'Skip the line' pitch outside an official ticket window
  • Driver approaches you instead of using the official rank
  • A second person hovers nearby while one engages you
  • Approach happens in a high-traffic tourist area

How to Avoid

  • Exit via the main Piazza delle Medaglie d'Oro entrance, not Via Carracci (Bolognina/rear).
  • Take only official white taxis from the signed rank; ignore any 'taxi?' offers inside the hall.
  • Use only Trenitalia or Italo apps or their logo-marked machines for tickets.
  • Keep luggage on the facing end-of-carriage rack on Frecciarossa/Italo.
  • Never buy tickets from third-party resellers.
Scam #4
The Outdoor Restaurant Bag-Snatch
⚠️ High
📍 Outdoor tables on Via Volturno, Via dei Mille, Via de' Giudei (Jewish Ghetto), Via Pescherie Vecchie, Quadrilatero market streets

Bags hung on chair backs at outdoor Quadrilatero and Jewish Ghetto restaurants are snatched by passing pedestrians, followed by immediate ATM cashout before cards can be frozen.

A June 2017 Il Resto del Carlino arrest documented a 22-year-old Moroccan man snatching a 53-year-old American tourist's purse on Via Volturno; police caught him on Via dei Mille with her Gucci wallet, €70 cash, credit cards, and a $500 check. A Bolognatoday 'Furto nel Ghetto' report documents the ATM-cashout variant on Via de' Giudei.

Red Flags

  • Cash payment demanded on the spot
  • Approach happens in a high-traffic tourist area
  • Refusal triggers escalation, guilt-trip, or a follow
  • Cannot produce official credentials when asked

How to Avoid

  • Place your bag on the floor with one strap looped around your chair leg or ankle — never on the chair back or an empty seat.
  • Sit with your back to a wall facing foot traffic.
  • Leave your passport in the hotel safe.
  • Use in-app card blocks immediately if a bag disappears — the ATM variant depends on a 3–10 minute reaction lag.
  • Eat where locals eat; avoid the Piazza-Maggiore-to-Piazza-della-Mercanzia alleys flagged by locals as '95% tourist traps'.
Scam #5
The Bologna Taxi Overcharge
🔶 Medium
📍 Bologna Guglielmo Marconi Airport taxi rank, Bologna Centrale rank, radio-dispatched taxis from suburbs

Bologna Airport drivers quote flat fares above the posted €12.80 fixed fare (up to 6km) or switch the meter to a long-distance tariff inside the city.

A 2025 traveler thread documents a €24 starting fare — a fellow commenter calls it 'straight up scammed.' The true max radio starting fare is €6.80; per-km is €1.45 in normal daytime. Variants include the 'credit card reader is broken' scam that forces victims to withdraw cash at ATMs while the driver watches the PIN.

Red Flags

  • Cash payment demanded on the spot
  • Driver approaches you instead of using the official rank
  • Approach happens in a high-traffic tourist area
  • Refusal triggers escalation, guilt-trip, or a follow

How to Avoid

  • Use the Marconi Express monorail (€12.20, ~7 minutes) from the airport to Bologna Centrale — it is faster and has nothing to negotiate.
  • For taxis, use only white-painted Cotabo or Taxi Bologna at the official rank and confirm the €12.80 airport fixed fare before closing the door.
  • Use TaxiClick or Cotabo (051.372727) for radio calls.
  • Always demand a ricevuta with taxi number, time, and route.
  • Refuse any ATM detour.
Scam #6
The Quadrilatero "Ancient" Tourist-Menu Trap
🔶 Medium
📍 Via Pescherie Vecchie, Via Drapperie, Via Clavature, Via degli Orefici, Via Caprarie (Quadrilatero)

Quadrilatero restaurants add unmarked per-person coperto (often above €3.50), automatic 15% 'servizio' on foreign cards, unrequested pane/acqua/antipasti, and fish or steak priced 'al etto' that arrives at 400–600g.

A 2024 NYT opinion piece documents 'Ancient cold cuts butcher' chain shops that had actually 'been open for three months.' Locals name the Piazza-Maggiore-to-Piazza-della-Mercanzia alleys as '95% tourist traps.'

Red Flags

  • Cash payment demanded on the spot
  • Menu has no posted prices or sells fish 'al etto'
  • Approach happens in a high-traffic tourist area
  • Refusal triggers escalation, guilt-trip, or a follow

How to Avoid

  • Walk 3–5 minutes off the Piazza Maggiore axis — Via del Pratello, Santo Stefano, or Mercato delle Erbe are locally recommended.
  • Photograph the posted menu outside before sitting (Italian law requires prices).
  • Ask at seating: 'mi conferma il coperto e se c'è servizio?' Prefer trattorias/osterias with handwritten daily menus.
  • Trust Italian-language Google reviews sorted by Most Recent over English reviews.
Scam #7
The Fake Short-Term-Rental / Key Handoff
⚠️ High
📍 Apartment listings in the historic center, Fiera district, areas within 15 minutes' walk of Bologna Centrale

Fraudulent hosts on Airbnb, Booking and TripAdvisor direct guests off-platform for payment, then vanish on arrival day.

TripAdvisor Bologna Forum's 'Be aware of scams' thread documents a victim contacted by 'Bjarne' via off-platform email; a 'SCAM. DO NOT BOOK' review names a Fiera-district apartment that was locked and unresponsive on arrival. Side patterns: undisclosed credit-card surcharges and a fake 'city tax in cash' cover (Bologna's real €5/night tourism tax applies only to rooms over €120/night).

Red Flags

  • Cash payment demanded on the spot
  • Pressure to pay off-platform (wire, bank transfer, cash on arrival)
  • Approach happens in a high-traffic tourist area
  • Refusal triggers escalation, guilt-trip, or a follow

How to Avoid

  • Book only through Airbnb, Booking.com, or platforms with pay-through-platform guarantees — never wire or pay cash off-platform.
  • Check the property on Google Street View before arrival.
  • Require in-platform messages with photos of the entrance and keybox.
  • Budget for the €5/night tourism tax (rooms over €120/night only); refuse cash 'city fee' surprises.
  • Push back on credit-card surcharges — EU PSD2 bars them on most consumer cards and legitimate B&Bs don't ask.

🆘 What to Do If You Get Scammed

📋 File a Police Report

Go to the nearest Carabinieri / Polizia di Stato station. Call 112 (Carabinieri) or 113 (Polizia). Get an official crime report — you'll need this for insurance claims. You can also report online at poliziadistato.it.

💳 Cancel Your Cards

Call your bank immediately. Most have 24/7 numbers on the back of the card (keep a photo saved separately). Block any suspicious transactions before the thieves use your details.

🛂 Lost Passport?

Contact your nearest embassy or consulate. The US Embassy in Rome is at Via Vittorio Veneto 121, 00187 Rome. For emergencies: +39 06-4674-1.

📱 Track Your Device

If your phone was stolen, use Find My (iPhone) or Find My Device (Android) from another device. Don't confront thieves yourself — share the location with police instead.
