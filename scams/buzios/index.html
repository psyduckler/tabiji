The 6 Scams

Scam #1
The Rio–Búzios R$1,500 Transfer
🔶 Medium
📍 GIG airport tour-desk transfer counters, Rio Zona Sul hotel concierge, Búzios Rodoviária (bus station), 1001 Auto Viação routes, private-driver 'door-to-door' WhatsApp dealers

A GIG airport tour-desk quotes R$1,500 to drive you the 175 km to your Búzios pousada. The 1001 Auto Viação direct bus from Novo Rio Rodoviária is R$95–R$130 and a private door-to-door is R$350–R$500 with reputable operators. You'd be paying triple to a desk taking a 30% kickback.

Búzios is Rio state's premier weekend beach getaway — 175 km east of Rio, a 2.5–3 hour drive — and the transfer ecosystem hosts a 2025 overcharge pattern targeting foreigners unfamiliar with the budget alternatives. A GIG airport tour-desk shows you a glossy brochure of beachfront pousadas and quotes R$1,500 for the private transfer. Your hotel concierge in Copacabana will cheerfully match it.

The real fares set the right anchor. The 1001 Auto Viação direct bus from Rio's Novo Rio Rodoviária is R$95–R$130 (2.5–3 hours, 4–6 daily departures) — air-conditioned coach with reclining seats, restroom, and WiFi; book at 1001.com.br one to two weeks ahead in high season. A reputable private door-to-door (BR Transfers, Carioca Transfers, Rio Transfer Service) is R$350–R$500 per car for 1–4 passengers. Uber Rio-to-Búzios sometimes works but usually quotes R$600–R$900 because of the long inter-city ride. The trap menu starts with the R$1,200–R$2,000 GIG airport tour-desk "private transfer." Then "shared shuttle van R$250 per person" that's identical to the 1001 bus at twice the price. Then a "WhatsApp private apartment + transfer combo US$400 per person" bundling an inflated rental with the transfer markup. r/Brazil airport-transfer threads document all three running side by side.

For the return trip, the same desks stage urgency: "only R$1,800 available today, last bus sold out." The bus is rarely sold out and you can book at the Búzios Rodoviária box office on arrival. Take the 1001 Auto Viação direct bus from Novo Rio Rodoviária at R$95–R$130 (book 1001.com.br) — air-conditioned coach with reclining seats, restroom, and WiFi. For door-to-door, book BR Transfers or Carioca Transfers via direct website at R$350–R$500 per car (1–4 passengers). Refuse every GIG tour-desk or hotel-concierge "recommended transfer" above R$800. At Búzios Rodoviária, walk 5–10 minutes to Rua das Pedras or take a local taxi R$30–R$50 — Búzios is small (9 km long). Costa do Sol highway tolls (R$26 each way) should be included in any private quote, not a surprise add-on.

Red Flags

  • A GIG tour-desk quoting R$1,200–R$2,000 for a private transfer (real reputable rate R$350–R$500).
  • A hotel concierge "recommended driver" at R$1,500 — that's a kickback markup over the real R$350–R$500 rate.
  • A "shared shuttle van R$250 per person" that's identical to the 1001 bus at twice the price.
  • A "taxi to your pousada R$150" at Búzios Rodoviária for a sub-2-km trip.
  • A return-trip "last bus sold out, R$1,800 only option" urgency upsell — the bus is rarely sold out.

How to Avoid

  • Take the 1001 Auto Viação bus from Novo Rio Rodoviária to Búzios at R$95–R$130 (book at 1001.com.br).
  • For door-to-door, book BR Transfers or Carioca Transfers direct at R$350–R$500 per car.
  • Ignore every "recommended transfer" offer above R$800.
  • At Búzios Rodoviária, walk 5–10 minutes to the center or take a local taxi at R$30–R$50.
  • The Costa do Sol toll (R$26 each way) belongs in the private quote — it's not an add-on.
Scam #2
The Rua das Pedras VIP Schooner
🔶 Medium
📍 Praia da Armação main pier, Praia dos Ossos boat launches, Rua das Pedras tour-booking touts, Praia João Fernandes / Geribá / Ferradura schooner stops

Pier touts on Búzios's Rua das Pedras pitch "private VIP" schooner trips at R$600–R$1,500 per person; the reputable group tour with the same 12-beach route, snorkel gear, and buffet runs R$80–R$130 with operators like Buzios Trolley, Tour Shop, or Maravilhas do Mar. Many of the "private" boats run without life jackets, VHF radio, or Marinha registration.

The Búzios beach-hopping schooner tour (Passeio de Escuna) is a 5–6 hour boat trip stopping at 8–12 of the peninsula's beaches with snorkeling and a buffet — the destination's flagship excursion. A pier-front tout on Rua das Pedras steps into your path with a glossy brochure: "Special private schooner just for you, R$600 per person, all-inclusive, very exclusive." If two of you are walking together, that's R$1,200 before lunch.

A reputable group tour (Buzios Trolley at Pier Marina, Tour Shop on Rua das Pedras, Maravilhas do Mar at Praia da Armação) is R$80–R$130 per person for 5–6 hours including buffet lunch, snorkel gear, and soft drinks; add R$50 for cocktails or open bar. Private speedboat or yacht charter with a real operator is R$800–R$2,500 for a group of 4–8 people, not per person. The trap menu runs through the standard upsells. "VIP exclusive route to Praia das Caravelas R$1,200" — Caravelas is a public beach included in the standard route. "All-inclusive open bar premium R$350 per person" that's the standard tour with two extra beers. "Glass-bottom boat upgrade R$200" — glass-bottom boats exist, but the regular schooner stops at the same snorkel sites. "Private chef on-board R$800" — the included buffet is a generic Brazilian beach lunch served on every reputable tour. The genuine risk sits at the unlicensed boats: operators advertising "cheap schooner R$50 today only" lack life jackets, VHF radios, and Marinha do Brasil registration, and Búzios bay has documented serious safety incidents during summer afternoon storms.

Book schooner tours only through Google 4.5+ operators with 50+ reviews — Buzios Trolley (Pier Marina), Tour Shop (Rua das Pedras), or Maravilhas do Mar (Praia da Armação) — at R$80–R$130 per person for 5–6 hours. Verify life jackets for every passenger and the operator's Marinha registration sticker before boarding. Refuse every "private schooner," "VIP exclusive route," or "all-inclusive premium" upsell — the group tour visits the same 12 beaches (João Fernandes, Azeda, Azedinha, Tartaruga, Ferradura, Forno, Brava). Pre-book online 2–3 days ahead in high season (Dec–Feb, July) and bring biodegradable sunscreen for the coral. Take Dramamine 30 minutes before departure for motion sickness and sit center-boat (less rocking).

Red Flags

  • A pier-front tout selling "private schooner R$600 per person" — the real group tour runs R$80–R$130.
  • A "VIP exclusive route to Praia das Caravelas" at R$1,200 — Caravelas is on the standard route.
  • A boat without visible life jackets, a VHF radio, or a Marinha registration sticker.
  • An "all-inclusive open bar premium" at R$350 — the real package is the standard tour plus two beers.
  • A "private chef on-board R$800" pitch — every reputable tour includes the buffet.

How to Avoid

  • Book Google 4.5+ operators: Buzios Trolley, Tour Shop, or Maravilhas do Mar at R$80–R$130 per person.
  • Verify life jackets for everyone and the Marinha registration sticker before boarding.
  • Refuse every "private," "VIP," or "premium" upsell — the group tour is the same experience.
  • Pre-book 2–3 days ahead in high season (December–February, July) — the popular boats sell out.
  • The standard route is João Fernandes, Azeda, Azedinha, Tartaruga, Ferradura, Forno, Brava.
Scam #3
The Rua das Pedras Couvert Trap
🔶 Medium
📍 Rua das Pedras restaurant strip (cobblestone main street), Praia da Armação waterfront dining, Orla Bardot promenade restaurants, Centro Histórico tourist-menu spots

Rua das Pedras restaurants pad bills with USD-only menus at 2–3× local prices, R$80–R$200 "couvert artístico" charges (legitimate is R$15–R$40), silent 10% serviço (optional under Brazilian law), R$25 bottled water (real R$6–R$12), and verbal "lobster peso variável" quotes at R$800–R$1,500 per kg. Eat one block off the strip and the same dish costs half.

Búzios's Rua das Pedras (the cobblestone main street with most tourist restaurants) hosts a 2025 bill-padding ecosystem common to Brazilian beach destinations, plus a unique "couvert artístico" inflation pattern. You sit down at a waterfront table, the host hands you a leather-bound menu printed in USD, and the moqueca that cost R$80 a block away is now US$35.

The trap menu runs through seven moves. USD-only menus padded 2–3× the local R$ price (Brazilian moqueca dish at US$35 vs R$80–R$120 at non-tourist spots). "Couvert artístico" added at R$80–R$200 per person for "live music" that's a single guitarist playing for 30 minutes — legitimate couvert is R$15–R$40 and disclosed before seating. "Serviço 10%" presented as mandatory — Brazilian law (Lei 13.419/2017) requires this be optional and refuseable. "Água mineral R$25" against the real R$6–R$12. "Lagosta peso variável" (lobster, market price) verbal quotes at R$800–R$1,500 per kg without weight disclosed before cooking. A 5–10% credit-card surcharge added unannounced at the bill. And the bill-switcheroo where the R$ amount on the receipt differs from what's charged to the card by R$100–R$500.

Eat one or two blocks off Rua das Pedras at authentic Búzios spots — Cigalon (Orla Bardot waterfront, Google 4.7+, French-Brazilian), Sawasdee (Thai, Manguinhos), Bar do David (working-class Centro), or Fishbone (Praia da Armação seafood) — at R$70–R$180 per meal with transparent pricing. Before sitting, photograph the menu and confirm couvert artístico amount, serviço policy, and credit-card surcharge in writing. Refuse the silent 10% serviço under Lei 13.419/2017 (it's optional); for lobster, agree weight × R$400–R$700/kg in writing before cooking. Skip USD-priced menus and request the real R$ amount. For sunset cocktails, the free Orla Bardot promenade boardwalk beats overpriced "sunset bar" tables.

Red Flags

  • A Rua das Pedras restaurant with a USD-only menu at 2–3× local R$ prices.
  • A "couvert artístico" line at R$80–R$200 per person for a single guitarist (real couvert R$15–R$40).
  • "Serviço 10%" presented as mandatory — under Brazilian law (Lei 13.419/2017) it's optional.
  • "Água mineral R$25" — bottled water is R$6–R$12 anywhere reputable.
  • A "lagosta peso variável" verbal quote without weight or price-per-kg disclosed before cooking.

How to Avoid

  • Eat off Rua das Pedras: Cigalon, Sawasdee, Bar do David, or Fishbone at R$70–R$180 per meal.
  • Before sitting, photograph the menu and confirm couvert, serviço, and card-surcharge in writing.
  • "Serviço 10%" is optional under Lei 13.419/2017 — refuse if the service was poor.
  • For lobster, agree weight × R$400–R$700 per kg in writing before the cooking starts.
  • For sunset cocktails, the free Orla Bardot boardwalk beats any "sunset bar" table.

Scam #4
The Geribá VIP Beach Bed
🟢 Low
📍 Praia João Fernandes (high-end beach), Praia Geribá (surf beach), Ferradura, Praia da Tartaruga, beach-club VIP zones, walking beach-vendor strips

A polo-shirted attendant at Praia João Fernandes leads you to a four-poster canopy "VIP couples bed" with a R$800/day price tag — the regular chair-and-umbrella twenty meters away is R$80–R$200 with a food-and-drink minimum. All Brazilian beaches are public federal property (Lei 7.661/1988); no entrance fee, no chair-rental obligation.

Búzios's premier beaches — João Fernandes, Geribá, Ferradura — host a 2025 beach-club and walking-vendor ecosystem with VIP upcharge patterns common to upscale Brazilian beach destinations. You walk onto João Fernandes and a polo-shirted attendant intercepts you at the sand line: "VIP couples bed for you, senhora? Best ocean view in Búzios, R$800 for the day." The four-poster canopy daybed twenty meters away looks the part.

The real numbers anchor honest service. Reputable beach clubs at João Fernandes — Praia dos Amores, Sunset Lounge, Bardot Beach Club; at Geribá, Casa Geribá, Mestre Albino, Banca do Surf; at Ferradura, Pier 220 — offer a chair, an umbrella, and table service at R$80–R$200 per couple with a R$100–R$200 food-and-drink minimum. The trap menu runs alongside it. Walking vendors with bracelets, sarongs, jewelry, and henna tattoos open with "special price my friend" pitches at 3–4× fair market. "Fresh coconut water R$25" against R$8–R$15 from licensed beach kiosks. A "beach massage R$200/30 min" that becomes R$600 for the "extended deluxe" by minute fifteen. A "jet-ski rental R$600/30 min" at Geribá against fair operator pricing of R$200–R$350. A "photo with parrot R$80" — wildlife welfare concern plus scam. And a "private cabana R$1,500/day" at João Fernandes when the standard chair-and-umbrella covers the same access to the same water.

All Brazilian beaches are public federal property (Lei 7.661/1988) — no entrance fee, no chair-rental obligation. Reputable beach clubs at João Fernandes (Praia dos Amores, Sunset Lounge, Bardot Beach Club), Geribá (Casa Geribá, Mestre Albino, Pier 220 at Ferradura) offer chair + umbrella + service at R$80–R$200 with a food-and-drink minimum. Refuse every "VIP couples bed," "private cabana," or "exclusive zone" upsell above R$200; firm "não obrigado" to walking vendors. Buy coconut water from licensed kiosks (R$8–R$15) and book massages at a hotel spa (R$250–R$400 for 60 minutes), never with parrots, monkeys, or iguanas. For travelers, Praia da Tartaruga is calm and sheltered for swimming; Geribá is for surfers (waves).

Red Flags

  • A "VIP couples bed R$800/day" at João Fernandes — the standard chair-and-umbrella runs R$80–R$200.
  • A walking vendor opening with "special price my friend" at 3–4× fair market.
  • "Fresh coconut water R$25" from a walking vendor — the kiosk price is R$8–R$15.
  • A "beach massage R$200" that quietly becomes R$600 "extended deluxe" mid-rub.
  • A "photo with parrot R$80" — wildlife welfare concern and a scam at the same time.

How to Avoid

  • All Brazilian beaches are public — no entrance fee is mandatory anywhere.
  • Reputable beach clubs: Praia dos Amores, Sunset Lounge, Bardot Beach Club at R$80–R$200.
  • Refuse "VIP bed," "private cabana," or "exclusive zone" upsells above R$200.
  • Book massages at a hotel spa or Spa Coca-Búzios at R$250–R$400 for 60 minutes.
  • Jet-ski rental: comparison-shop three operators on the beach; fair price is R$200–R$350 for 30 minutes.
Scam #5
The Búzios Pousada Bait-and-Switch
⚠️ High
📍 Búzios pousada listings (Booking.com, Airbnb, Hotels.com), Carnaval (Feb–Mar) booking window, Réveillon (NYE) accommodations, WhatsApp 'private apartment' Búzios offers

Carnaval and Réveillon (NYE) Búzios accommodation sees phantom Booking.com listings with stolen photos, confirmed-booking cancellations 30–60 days before peak dates with the same unit relisted at 3–5× the price, and WhatsApp "private villa" sellers demanding 100% PIX upfront for properties that aren't theirs. Avoid Airbnb peak-season; book hotel chains.

Búzios accommodations spike 4–8× during Carnaval (late February or early March) and Réveillon (NYE, Dec 28–Jan 2), and a 2025 cancel-and-relist fraud ecosystem mirrors the Rio pattern. You book a beachfront pousada in October at R$450/night, get a confirmation email, and forty-five days before Carnaval the host cancels with a vague "pousada emergency." You scramble — the same unit is back on Booking.com under a slightly different name at R$1,800.

The variants share the same logic. Booking.com and Airbnb "phantom listings" use stolen photos of luxury Búzios pousadas — you pay in advance, arrive, and find no property. Confirmed-booking cancellations land 30–60 days before Carnaval or NYE with the same unit relisted at 3–5× the original price (the platform refunds the original payment, but you're stuck a month from travel with prices doubled). WhatsApp "private Búzios villa for Carnaval R$4,000/night" demands 100% PIX upfront — Brazilian PIX is irreversible the moment it clears, and the listing disappears the second payment confirms. A "beachfront pousada" booking turns out to be 300–500 m inland with no beach view (always verify on Google Maps before paying). On-arrival bait-and-switch downgrades a confirmed beachfront unit to an inferior room. "Cleaning deposit R$800 cash on arrival" never returns at checkout. And "mandatory pousada Carnaval package R$2,500 per person" covers things ("cocktail welcome + breakfast") that are already included at most properties.

Book Carnaval and Réveillon Búzios accommodation 6–9 months ahead via Booking.com or established hotel chains with platform-protected payment — reputable pousadas include Casas Brancas, Pedra da Laguna, Vila Cidade Búzios, and Solar do Peixe Vivo. Avoid Airbnb in peak season (cancel-and-relist is well-documented across Brazil) and never PIX-transfer to WhatsApp "private villa" sellers — PIX is irreversible. Cross-reference any pousada name on Google Maps and Tripadvisor before booking, verify "beachfront" via satellite view, and refuse "cleaning deposit cash on arrival" demands. For budget Carnaval or NYE, base in Cabo Frio (10 km away) at half the rate. Book direct from pousada websites (most have own domains) for 5–10% off Booking.com — saving R$500+ per stay.

Red Flags

  • A Booking.com Carnaval or NYE listing priced 4–8× below the surrounding peak market.
  • A WhatsApp "private villa R$4,000/night" demanding 100% PIX upfront.
  • A "beachfront pousada" that's 300–500 m inland on Google Maps satellite view.
  • A "cleaning deposit R$800 cash on arrival" demand that wasn't on the booking.
  • A "mandatory Carnaval package R$2,500 per person" for things already included (breakfast, cocktail).

How to Avoid

  • Book 6–9 months ahead via Booking.com or hotel chains with platform-protected payment.
  • Reputable pousadas: Casas Brancas, Pedra da Laguna, Vila Cidade Búzios, Solar do Peixe Vivo.
  • Avoid Airbnb peak-season; never PIX-transfer to WhatsApp "private villa" sellers.
  • Cross-reference any pousada name on Google Maps and Tripadvisor before paying.
  • For budget Carnaval or NYE, base in Cabo Frio (10 km away) at roughly half the Búzios rate.
Scam #6
The Arraial do Cabo Combo
🔶 Medium
📍 Búzios Rua das Pedras tour-booking touts, hotel concierge tour desks, combined Arraial do Cabo / Cabo Frio day-trip operators, schooner-tour upsells

Rua das Pedras tour-touts and hotel concierges bundle the Arraial do Cabo + Cabo Frio day-trip at R$600 per person, plus a R$1,500 "private speedboat to Arraial" upsell. The DIY total — 1001 bus to Arraial plus a schooner booked at the marina — is R$130–R$230, and the schooner runs the same Lagoa Azul route everyone else stops at.

Búzios is often combined with day-trips to neighboring Arraial do Cabo (45 minutes south, world-class snorkeling) and Cabo Frio (15 minutes south, beaches), and 2025 tour operators host a commission-kickback ecosystem around the route. A Rua das Pedras tout shows you a brochure: "Arraial do Cabo + Cabo Frio combined day-trip, R$600 per person, all-inclusive." Your hotel concierge will match it.

The DIY anchor sits at a quarter of that. The 1001 Auto Viação bus from Búzios Rodoviária to Arraial is R$25–R$40 each way (45 minutes) — book at the terminal or 1001.com.br. At Arraial, walk 5–10 minutes from the rodoviária to the marina pier and book a schooner directly with operators like Lancha Albatroz, Os Moleques, or Xeretas Tour at R$80–R$150 per person for 5–6 hours including lunch and snorkel gear. Total round-trip: R$130–R$230. The trap menu sits on top of that. "Combined day-trip R$600 per person" against the real DIY at a third the price. "Recommended driver + tour combo R$800" hotel-concierge kickback markup. "Private speedboat to Arraial R$1,500 per person" — the regular schooner stops at the same Lagoa Azul. "Lagoa Azul exclusive access R$300" — Lagoa Azul is on every standard schooner route. "Commission-restaurant lunch stop R$250 per person" added to the day-trip route (real Arraial seafood is R$60–R$100). And "Cabo Frio Praia do Forte fortress entry R$80" against the real R$8 IPHAN entry to Forte São Mateus.

For Arraial do Cabo, take the 1001 Auto Viação bus from Búzios Rodoviária at R$25–R$40 each way (45 minutes, book at 1001.com.br) and walk 5–10 minutes to the marina pier — book schooners direct with Lancha Albatroz, Os Moleques, or Xeretas Tour at R$80–R$150 per person. For Cabo Frio, the 1001 bus is R$8–R$15 (15 minutes) and Forte São Mateus entry is R$8 IPHAN. Refuse every Búzios-side "combined day-trip R$600+" bundle (real DIY total R$130–R$230) and every "Lagoa Azul exclusive access" upsell — Lagoa Azul is on the standard schooner route. Eat at Restaurante Caçula (Praia dos Anjos) or Saint Tropez (waterfront) in Arraial at R$60–R$100, and verify your schooner has a boarding ladder before paying — many require sea-jump boarding.

Red Flags

  • An "Arraial + Cabo Frio combined day-trip R$600 per person" bundle (DIY total R$130–R$230).
  • A hotel concierge "recommended driver + tour combo R$800" — that's the kickback markup.
  • A "private speedboat to Arraial R$1,500 per person" — the regular schooner is R$80–R$150.
  • A "Lagoa Azul exclusive access R$300" charge — Lagoa Azul is on every standard schooner route.
  • A "commission-restaurant lunch stop R$250 per person" added silently to the day-trip.

How to Avoid

  • Take the 1001 Auto Viação bus Búzios–Arraial at R$25–R$40 each way (45 minutes).
  • Book schooners direct in Arraial: Lancha Albatroz, Os Moleques, or Xeretas Tour at R$80–R$150.
  • Refuse every Búzios-side "combined R$600+" bundle.
  • For Cabo Frio, the 1001 bus is R$8–R$15; Forte São Mateus entry is R$8 IPHAN.
  • Eat in Arraial at Restaurante Caçula or Saint Tropez at R$60–R$100 per meal.

🆘 What to Do If You Get Scammed

📋 File a Police Report

Go to the nearest Civil Police (Polícia Civil) station. Call 190 (emergency) or 197 (civil police). Get an official crime report — you'll need this for insurance claims. You can also report online at delegaciaonline.rj.gov.br.

💳 Cancel Your Cards

Call your bank immediately. Most have 24/7 numbers on the back of the card (keep a photo saved separately). Block any suspicious transactions before the thieves use your details.

🛂 Lost Passport?

Contact your nearest embassy or consulate. The US Consulate General is at Av. Presidente Wilson, 147, Centro, Rio de Janeiro. For emergencies: +55 21 3823-2000.

📱 Track Your Device

If your phone was stolen, use Find My (iPhone) or Find My Device (Android) from another device. Don't confront thieves yourself — share the location with police instead.

For travelers who want CALM SWIMMING: Praia da Tartaruga (sheltered, family-friendly), Praia da Azeda (small bay, no waves), Praia João Fernandes (clear water, beach-club access). For SCENIC SUNSET: Praia da Ferradura (horseshoe bay), Praia Brava. For SURFING: Praia Geribá (waves, surf schools). For SECLUDED: Praia da Foca, Praia das Caravelas. Reputable beach clubs at João Fernandes: Praia dos Amores, Sunset Lounge, Bardot Beach Club at R$80–R$200 chair + umbrella + service. All beaches are PUBLIC federal property — no entrance fee mandatory. The 5–6 hour schooner tour (R$80–R$130 group via Buzios Trolley / Tour Shop) is the best way to see all 11 beaches in one day.
Eat 1–2 blocks OFF the Rua das Pedras tourist strip at: Cigalon (Orla Bardot waterfront, French-Brazilian, Google 4.7+, R$80–R$180), Sawasdee (Manguinhos Thai, R$70–R$150), Bar do David (Centro working-class, R$50–R$100), Fishbone (Praia da Armação seafood, R$80–R$200). BEFORE sitting, ask for menu and CONFIRM (a) couvert artístico R$ amount per person, (b) 'serviço 10%' policy (Brazilian Lei 13.419/2017 makes service charge OPTIONAL — you can refuse), (c) credit-card surcharge — photograph the menu. For seafood, agree weight in writing BEFORE cooking — fair lobster R$400–R$700 per kg, white-fish R$80–R$150 per kg. Skip USD-priced menus (they pad 10–15% exchange) — request real R$ amounts.
Take 1001 Auto Viação bus from Búzios Rodoviária to Arraial do Cabo at R$25–R$40 each way (45 min). At Arraial, walk to the marina pier (5–10 min from rodoviária) and book a schooner DIRECT with Lancha Albatroz, Os Moleques, or Xeretas Tour at R$80–R$150 per person (5–6 hours, includes Lagoa Azul Blue Lagoon, Praia do Forno, Praia dos Anjos with snorkel + buffet). Refuse every Búzios-side 'combined day-trip R$600+' bundle — DIY total is R$130–R$230. Lagoa Azul is a STANDARD schooner stop — refuse 'exclusive access' upsells. Eat at Restaurante Caçula (Praia dos Anjos, R$60–R$100) or Saint Tropez waterfront.
