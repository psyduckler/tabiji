The 6 Scams

Scam #1
Rio-to-Búzios Private Transfer Overcharge & 1001 Bus Alternative
🔶 Medium
📍 GIG airport tour-desk transfer counters, Rio Zona Sul hotel concierge, Búzios Rodoviária (bus station), 1001 Auto Viação routes, private-driver 'door-to-door' WhatsApp dealers

Búzios is Rio state's premier weekend beach getaway.

175 km east, 2.5–3 hr drive — and the transfer ecosystem hosts a 2025 overcharge pattern targeting foreigners unfamiliar with the budget alternatives.

Legitimate fares (2025): 1001 Auto Viação direct bus from Rio Rodoviária Novo Rio terminal to Búzios is R$95–R$130 (2.5–3 hr, 4–6 daily departures). Private transfer via reputable operators (BR Transfers, Carioca Transfers, Rio Transfer Service): R$350–R$500 per car (1–4 passengers). Uber Rio-to-Búzios is theoretically possible but often R$600–R$900 due to the long inter-city ride. The 2025 scams: (a) GIG airport tour-desk 'private transfer to Búzios R$1,200–R$2,000' (3–4× real reputable rate); (b) Rio Zona Sul hotel concierge 'recommended driver R$1,500' kickback markup; (c) 'shared shuttle van R$250 per person' that's identical to 1001 bus at 2× the price; (d) WhatsApp 'private apartment + transfer combo US$400 per person' bundled with kickback markup; (e) Búzios Rodoviária arrival 'taxi to your pousada R$150' for trips under 2 km (real R$30–R$50 via local taxis or 5-min walk); (f) return-Rio quote inflated as urgency at end-of-trip ('only R$1,800 available today, last bus sold out'); (g) 'door-to-door' driver claiming insurance includes Costa do Sol toll fees — toll is R$26 each way and should be in quoted price.

For travelers: (1) take 1001 Auto Viação bus from Novo Rio terminal R$95–R$130 — comfortable air-conditioned coach with reclining seats, restroom, WiFi; book at 1001.com.br 1–2 weeks ahead in high season; (2) for door-to-door private, book BR Transfers or Carioca Transfers via direct website at R$350–R$500 per car; (3) IGNORE every GIG / hotel-concierge 'recommended transfer' above R$800; (4) at Búzios Rodoviária, walk to Rua das Pedras (center) in 5–10 min, or local taxi R$30–R$50 to most pousadas (Búzios is small, 9 km long); (5) for return to Rio, book 1001 ticket at the Búzios Rodoviária box office on arrival OR online; refuse 'last bus' urgency upsells; (6) Costa do Sol highway tolls (R$26 each way) should be included in private-transfer quotes — NOT a surprise add-on; (7) for a budget-conscious Rio-Búzios round-trip, total bus cost is R$190–R$260 vs private R$700–R$1,000.

Red Flags

  • GIG tour-desk 'private transfer to Búzios R$1,200–R$2,000'
  • Hotel concierge 'recommended driver R$1,500' (real R$350–R$500)
  • 'Shared shuttle van R$250 per person' (1001 bus is R$95–R$130)
  • Búzios Rodoviária 'taxi to pousada R$150' for under-2-km trip
  • 'Last bus sold out, R$1,800 only option' return-trip urgency

How to Avoid

  • 1001 Auto Viação bus Novo Rio-Búzios R$95–R$130 (book 1001.com.br).
  • Private door-to-door: BR Transfers or Carioca Transfers R$350–R$500 per car.
  • IGNORE 'recommended transfer' offers above R$800.
  • At Búzios Rodoviária, walk 5–10 min to center OR local taxi R$30–R$50.
  • Costa do Sol toll R$26 each way should be included in private quote.
Scam #2
Búzios Beach-Hopping Schooner Tour Overcharge & Unlicensed Operators
🔶 Medium
📍 Praia da Armação main pier, Praia dos Ossos boat launches, Rua das Pedras tour-booking touts, Praia João Fernandes / Geribá / Ferradura schooner stops

The Búzios beach-hopping schooner tour (Passeio de Escuna).

A 5–6 hour boat trip stopping at 8–12 of the peninsula's beaches with snorkelling and a buffet — is the destination's flagship excursion and hosts a 2025 overcharge + unlicensed-operator ecosystem.

Legitimate cost: a reputable group schooner tour (Buzios Trolley, Tour Shop, Maravilhas do Mar) is R$80–R$130 per person for 5–6 hours including buffet lunch + snorkel gear + soft drinks; with cocktails / open bar add R$50. Private speedboat or yacht charter is R$800–R$2,500 per group (4–8 people). The 2025 scam patterns: (a) Rua das Pedras pier-front touts selling 'private schooner R$600 per person' (real group tour R$80–R$130); (b) 'VIP exclusive route to Praia das Caravelas R$1,200' — Caravelas is a public beach included in the standard route; (c) unlicensed boats without life jackets, VHF radio, insurance, or Marinha (Brazilian Navy) registration — documented serious safety issues during summer storms; (d) 'all-inclusive open bar premium R$350 per person' that's the standard tour with 2 extra beers; (e) 'glass-bottom boat upgrade R$200' — glass-bottom boats exist but the regular schooner stops at all the same snorkel sites; (f) 'private chef on-board R$800' — included buffet is a generic Brazilian beach lunch served on every reputable tour; (g) 'photo + drone video package R$400' upsells that rarely deliver.

For travelers: (1) book schooner tour ONLY through Google 4.5+ operators with at least 50 reviews: Buzios Trolley (Pier Marina), Tour Shop (Rua das Pedras), Maravilhas do Mar (Praia da Armação) — R$80–R$130 per person 5–6 hours; (2) VERIFY life jackets for every passenger BEFORE boarding; check operator's Marinha registration sticker on the boat (mandatory for licensed Brazilian boats); (3) REFUSE every 'private schooner' / 'VIP exclusive' / 'all-inclusive premium' upsell; the group tour is the same experience; (4) pre-book online at the operator website 2–3 days ahead in high season (Dec–Feb, July) — the popular boats sell out; (5) bring biodegradable sunscreen ONLY — Búzios beaches have coral; (6) for travelers with motion-sickness, take Dramamine 30 min before departure; sit in the center of the boat (less rocking); (7) the standard 12-beach stops include João Fernandes, Azeda, Azedinha, Tartaruga, Ferradura, Forno, Brava — verify your operator covers these; (8) skip the 'cocktail open bar add-on' — overpriced; bring your own can drinks for the boat ride.

Red Flags

  • Pier-front tout selling 'private schooner R$600 per person' (group R$80–R$130)
  • 'VIP exclusive route to Praia das Caravelas R$1,200' (it's a public beach)
  • Boat without visible life jackets / VHF radio / Marinha registration sticker
  • 'All-inclusive open bar premium R$350' (just standard tour + 2 beers)
  • 'Private chef on-board R$800' (standard buffet is included on all tours)

How to Avoid

  • Book Google 4.5+: Buzios Trolley, Tour Shop, Maravilhas do Mar R$80–R$130.
  • Verify life jackets + Marinha registration sticker BEFORE boarding.
  • REFUSE every 'private' / 'VIP' / 'premium' upsell.
  • Pre-book 2–3 days ahead in high season (Dec–Feb, July).
  • Standard route: João Fernandes, Azeda, Azedinha, Tartaruga, Ferradura.
Scam #3
Búzios Rua das Pedras Restaurant Tourist-Menu & 'Couvert Artístico' Bill-Padding
🔶 Medium
📍 Rua das Pedras restaurant strip (cobblestone main street), Praia da Armação waterfront dining, Orla Bardot promenade restaurants, Centro Histórico tourist-menu spots

Búzios Rua das Pedras (the cobblestone main street with most tourist restaurants) hosts a 2025.

Bill-padding ecosystem common to Brazilian beach destinations, plus a unique 'couvert artístico' (artistic cover charge for live music) inflation pattern.

The 2025 scam patterns: (a) Rua das Pedras waterfront restaurants with USD-only menus at 2–3× local prices (Brazilian moqueca dish US$35+ vs R$80–R$120 at non-tourist spots); (b) 'couvert artístico' (artistic cover) added at R$80–R$200 per person for 'live music' that's a single guitarist playing 30 min; legitimate couvert artístico is R$15–R$40 per person and disclosed before seating; (c) 'serviço 10%' (service charge) added — Brazilian law requires this be OPTIONAL but waiters often present as mandatory; you can refuse; (d) 'agua mineral R$25' (real R$6–R$12) — bottled water padding; (e) 'lagosta peso variável' (lobster, market price) verbal quotes at R$800–R$1,500 per kg without weight disclosed; (f) credit-card surcharge 5–10% added unannounced; (g) bill-switcheroo where R$ amount on receipt differs from what's charged to card by R$100–R$500.

For travelers: (1) eat 1–2 blocks OFF Rua das Pedras at authentic Búzios spots: Cigalon (Orla Bardot waterfront, Google 4.7+, French-Brazilian), Sawasdee (Thai, Manguinhos), Bar do David (working-class spot Centro), Fishbone (Praia da Armação seafood) — transparent pricing R$70–R$180 per meal; (2) BEFORE sitting, ask for the menu and CONFIRM (a) couvert artístico amount per person, (b) 'serviço 10%' policy (you can refuse), (c) credit-card surcharge — photograph the menu; (3) for water, order 'água sem gás copo' (tap water glass, free) at most spots OR bring your own bottled water (can-drinks usually allowed at sit-down tables); (4) for seafood, agree weight in writing BEFORE cooking — fair lobster R$400–R$700 per kg (1 kg feeds 2); fair red snapper R$80–R$150 per kg; (5) 'serviço' is OPTIONAL by Brazilian law (Lei 13.419/2017 makes it explicit) — tip 10% if happy with service, refuse if not; (6) USD-priced menus pad 10–15% exchange — request real R$ amount; (7) for sunset cocktails, the Orla Bardot promenade boardwalk (free public space) with view of bay is more atmospheric than overpriced 'sunset bar' tables.

Red Flags

  • Rua das Pedras USD-only menu at 2–3× local prices
  • 'Couvert artístico R$80–R$200 per person' for one guitarist (real R$15–R$40)
  • 'Serviço 10%' presented as mandatory (Brazilian law: optional)
  • 'Agua mineral R$25' (real R$6–R$12)
  • Lobster 'peso variável' verbal quote without weight disclosed

How to Avoid

  • Eat off Rua das Pedras: Cigalon, Sawasdee, Bar do David, Fishbone.
  • BEFORE sitting confirm couvert / serviço / card surcharge in writing.
  • 'Serviço 10%' is OPTIONAL (Lei 13.419/2017); refuse if poor service.
  • Lobster: agree weight × R$400–R$700/kg in writing BEFORE cooking.
  • Sunset: free Orla Bardot boardwalk beats overpriced sunset-bar tables.

Scam #4
Búzios Beach Vendor & 'VIP Beach Bed' Upcharge at João Fernandes / Geribá
🟢 Low
📍 Praia João Fernandes (high-end beach), Praia Geribá (surf beach), Ferradura, Praia da Tartaruga, beach-club VIP zones, walking beach-vendor strips

Búzios's premier beaches.

João Fernandes, Geribá, Ferradura — host a 2025 beach-club + walking-vendor ecosystem with VIP upcharge patterns common to upscale Brazilian beach destinations.

The 2025 patterns: (a) João Fernandes beach-club 'VIP couples bed R$800 per day' (standard chair + umbrella R$80–R$150 with R$100–R$200 food/drink minimum at reputable spots — Praia dos Amores, Sunset Lounge, Bardot Beach Club); (b) walking vendors with bracelets, sarongs, jewelry, henna tattoos at aggressive 'special price my friend' pitches — opening offer often 3–4× fair market; (c) 'fresh coconut water R$25' (real R$8–R$15 from licensed beach kiosks); (d) 'beach massage R$200/30 min' becoming R$600 'extended deluxe' (real reputable spa massage at hotel R$250–R$400 for 60 min); (e) 'jet-ski rental R$600/30 min' at Geribá (comparison-shop 3 operators — fair R$200–R$350); (f) 'photo with parrot R$80' (wildlife welfare concern + scam); (g) 'private cabana R$1,500/day' at João Fernandes (standard chair + umbrella R$100–R$200 covers same access).

For travelers: (1) all Brazilian beaches are PUBLIC federal property (Lei 7.661/1988) — no entrance fee, no chair-rental obligation; legitimate beach clubs offer chair + umbrella + service at R$80–R$200 with food/drink minimum; (2) reputable beach clubs at João Fernandes: Praia dos Amores, Sunset Lounge, Bardot Beach Club — Google 4.5+, transparent pricing; at Geribá: Casa Geribá, Mestre Albino, Banca do Surf; at Ferradura: Pier 220; (3) REFUSE every 'VIP couples bed', 'private cabana', 'exclusive zone' upsell above R$200 — standard chair-and-umbrella is the same; (4) firm 'não obrigado' to walking vendors; do not engage; (5) coconut water from licensed kiosks (with posted prices) at R$8–R$15; refuse R$25 walking-vendor pricing; (6) for massage, book at hotel spa or Spa Coca-Búzios at R$250–R$400; (7) jet-ski / banana-boat: comparison-shop 3 operators on the beach — fair jet-ski R$200–R$350 for 30 min; (8) refuse 'photo with parrot / monkey / iguana' — wildlife welfare and 100% scam; (9) for travelers, Praia da Tartaruga (calm, sheltered) is best for swimming; Geribá is for surfers (waves).

Red Flags

  • João Fernandes 'VIP couples bed R$800 per day' (standard R$80–R$150)
  • Walking vendor 'special price my friend' opening 3–4× fair market
  • 'Fresh coconut water R$25' from walking vendor (kiosk R$8–R$15)
  • 'Beach massage R$200' becoming R$600 'extended deluxe'
  • 'Photo with parrot R$80' (wildlife welfare + scam)

How to Avoid

  • All Brazilian beaches are PUBLIC — no entrance fee mandatory.
  • Reputable: Praia dos Amores, Sunset Lounge, Bardot Beach Club R$80–R$200.
  • REFUSE 'VIP bed' / 'private cabana' / 'exclusive zone' upsells.
  • Massage at hotel spa or Spa Coca-Búzios R$250–R$400 (60 min).
  • Jet-ski: comparison-shop 3 operators; fair R$200–R$350 for 30 min.
Scam #5
Búzios Pousada / Airbnb Cancel-and-Relist Fraud at Carnaval & NYE
⚠️ High
📍 Búzios pousada listings (Booking.com, Airbnb, Hotels.com), Carnaval (Feb–Mar) booking window, Réveillon (NYE) accommodations, WhatsApp 'private apartment' Búzios offers

Búzios accommodations spike 4–8× during Carnaval (late Feb / early Mar) and Réveillon / NYE (Dec 28 – Jan 2).

And a 2025 cancel-and-relist fraud ecosystem mirrors the Rio pattern.

The 2025 scam patterns: (a) Booking.com / Airbnb 'phantom listings' using stolen photos of luxury Búzios pousadas — tourist pays in advance, arrives, finds no property; (b) confirmed-booking cancellation 30–60 days before Carnaval / NYE with re-listing at 3–5× original price; (c) WhatsApp 'private Búzios villa for Carnaval R$4,000/night' demanding 100% PIX upfront — irreversible Brazilian payment; (d) Praia da Armação / Geribá-area 'beachfront pousada' that's actually 300–500 m inland with no beach view; (e) on-arrival bait-and-switch from confirmed beachfront to inferior unit; (f) 'cleaning deposit R$800 cash on arrival' that never returns; (g) 'mandatory pousada Carnaval package R$2,500 per person' covering 'cocktail welcome + breakfast' — a normal pousada inclusion at most properties; (h) 'minimum 5-night booking R$12,000+' for Carnaval that's 4× normal nightly rate × 5.

For travelers: (1) for Carnaval and Réveillon / NYE in Búzios, BOOK 6–9 MONTHS AHEAD via Booking.com or established hotel chains with platform-protected payment; reputable Búzios pousadas: Casas Brancas (boutique luxury), Pousada Pedra da Laguna, Hotel Pousada Vila Cidade Búzios, Pousada Solar do Peixe Vivo; AVOID Airbnb peak-season Búzios (cancel-and-relist pattern documented across Brazil); (2) NEVER PIX-transfer to private 'apartment' or 'villa' WhatsApp sellers — irreversible; (3) cross-reference any pousada name on Google Maps + Tripadvisor BEFORE booking — fake listings have no independent online footprint; (4) confirm 'beachfront' vs 'beach access' in writing — Búzios is small (9 km long, 11 beaches), but '5-min walk to beach' can be a stretch; verify via Google Maps satellite view before booking; (5) 'cleaning deposit cash on arrival' is illegitimate — request via platform only; (6) for budget Carnaval / NYE, consider BUSING in for the day from Rio (3 hr) and booking accommodation in less-stressed Cabo Frio (10 km from Búzios) at half the rate; (7) 'pousada Carnaval package' should ALREADY include breakfast — refuse 'cocktail welcome' surcharges; (8) book direct from pousada websites (most have own domains) for 5–10% discount over Booking.com — saving R$500+ per stay.

Red Flags

  • Booking.com Carnaval/NYE listing too good to be true (4–8× discount)
  • WhatsApp 'private villa R$4,000/night' demanding 100% PIX upfront
  • 'Beachfront pousada' that's 300–500 m inland on Google Maps
  • 'Cleaning deposit R$800 cash on arrival' demand
  • 'Mandatory Carnaval package R$2,500/person' for breakfast + cocktail

How to Avoid

  • Book 6–9 months ahead via Booking.com / hotel chains with platform protection.
  • Reputable pousadas: Casas Brancas, Pedra da Laguna, Vila Cidade, Solar do Peixe Vivo.
  • AVOID Airbnb peak-season; NEVER PIX-transfer to WhatsApp sellers.
  • Cross-reference name on Google Maps + Tripadvisor BEFORE booking.
  • For budget Carnaval/NYE, base in Cabo Frio at half Búzios rate.
Scam #6
Búzios Day-Trip 'Arraial do Cabo' & 'Cabo Frio' Combined Tour Commission Scam
🔶 Medium
📍 Búzios Rua das Pedras tour-booking touts, hotel concierge tour desks, combined Arraial do Cabo / Cabo Frio day-trip operators, schooner-tour upsells

Búzios is often combined with day-trips to neighboring Arraial do Cabo (45 min south, world-class snorkelling) and Cabo Frio (15 min south, beaches).

And 2025 tour operators host a commission-kickback ecosystem.

Legitimate cost: Arraial do Cabo schooner snorkelling tour booked DIRECT in Arraial: R$80–R$150 per person (5–6 hours, includes lunch + snorkel gear). Búzios-to-Arraial transport: 1001 bus R$25–R$40 each way (45 min); Uber R$80–R$120; private driver R$200–R$350. The 2025 scam patterns: (a) Búzios Rua das Pedras tour-booking touts selling 'Arraial do Cabo + Cabo Frio combined day-trip R$600 per person' (real DIY: R$50–R$80 transport + R$80–R$150 schooner = R$130–R$230 total); (b) hotel-concierge 'recommended driver + tour combo R$800' kickback markup; (c) 'private speedboat to Arraial R$1,500 per person' (real schooner R$80–R$150); (d) 'lagoa azul (Blue Lagoon) exclusive access R$300' — Lagoa Azul is part of the standard Arraial do Cabo schooner route; (e) 'commission-restaurant lunch stop R$250 per person' added to the day-trip route (real Arraial seafood lunch R$60–R$100); (f) 'Cabo Frio Praia do Forte fortress entry R$80' (real R$8 IPHAN entry); (g) 'photo + drone video R$300' upsells.

For travelers: (1) for Arraial do Cabo day-trip, take the 1001 Auto Viação bus from Búzios Rodoviária R$25–R$40 each way (45 min) — book at terminal or 1001.com.br; (2) at Arraial, walk to the marina pier (5–10 min from rodoviária) and book a schooner DIRECT with operators like Lancha Albatroz, Os Moleques, Xeretas Tour at R$80–R$150 per person; (3) REFUSE every Búzios-side 'combined day-trip R$600+' bundle — DIY is R$130–R$230; (4) Lagoa Azul (Blue Lagoon) is a standard schooner stop — refuse 'exclusive access' upsells; (5) for Cabo Frio, take 1001 bus R$8–R$15 each way (15 min); Praia do Forte entry to São Mateus fortress is R$8 IPHAN; (6) eat at authentic Arraial seafood spots: Restaurante Caçula (Praia dos Anjos), Saint Tropez (waterfront) at R$60–R$100 per meal; (7) for travelers, Arraial schooner tours involve hopping in/out of the boat for snorkel — request operator with boarding ladder, not just sea-jump; (8) bring biodegradable sunscreen ONLY for snorkel sites.

Red Flags

  • 'Arraial + Cabo Frio combined day-trip R$600 per person' (DIY R$130–R$230)
  • Hotel-concierge 'recommended driver + tour combo R$800' kickback
  • 'Private speedboat to Arraial R$1,500 per person' (schooner R$80–R$150)
  • 'Lagoa Azul exclusive access R$300' (standard schooner stop)
  • 'Commission-restaurant lunch R$250 per person' added to day-trip

How to Avoid

  • 1001 Auto Viação bus Búzios-Arraial R$25–R$40 each way (45 min).
  • Book schooner DIRECT in Arraial: Lancha Albatroz, Os Moleques, Xeretas R$80–R$150.
  • REFUSE every 'combined R$600+' Búzios-side bundle.
  • Cabo Frio: 1001 bus R$8–R$15; Praia do Forte fortress entry R$8 IPHAN.
  • Eat: Restaurante Caçula or Saint Tropez R$60–R$100.

🆘 What to Do If You Get Scammed

📋 File a Police Report

Go to the nearest Civil Police (Polícia Civil) station. Call 190 (emergency) or 197 (civil police). Get an official crime report — you'll need this for insurance claims. You can also report online at delegaciaonline.rj.gov.br.

💳 Cancel Your Cards

Call your bank immediately. Most have 24/7 numbers on the back of the card (keep a photo saved separately). Block any suspicious transactions before the thieves use your details.

🛂 Lost Passport?

Contact your nearest embassy or consulate. The US Consulate General is at Av. Presidente Wilson, 147, Centro, Rio de Janeiro. For emergencies: +55 21 3823-2000.

📱 Track Your Device

If your phone was stolen, use Find My (iPhone) or Find My Device (Android) from another device. Don't confront thieves yourself — share the location with police instead.

For seafood, agree weight in writing BEFORE cooking — fair lobster R$400–R$700 per kg, white-fish R$80–R$150 per kg. Skip USD-priced menus (they pad 10–15% exchange) — request real R$ amounts.
Take 1001 Auto Viação bus from Búzios Rodoviária to Arraial do Cabo at R$25–R$40 each way (45 min). At Arraial, walk to the marina pier (5–10 min from rodoviária) and book a schooner DIRECT with Lancha Albatroz, Os Moleques, or Xeretas Tour at R$80–R$150 per person (5–6 hours, includes Lagoa Azul Blue Lagoon, Praia do Forno, Praia dos Anjos with snorkel + buffet). REFUSE every Búzios-side 'combined day-trip R$600+' bundle — DIY total is R$130–R$230. Lagoa Azul is a STANDARD schooner stop — refuse 'exclusive access' upsells. Eat at Restaurante Caçula (Praia dos Anjos, R$60–R$100) or Saint Tropez waterfront.
