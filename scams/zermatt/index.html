The 4 Scams

Scam #1
Premium Ski Chalets Wire-Transfer Booking Scam
⚠️ High
📍 premiumskichalets.com listings, Zermatt chalet booking sites without .ch domains, off-Booking-platform email-redirect rentals

A 2025 Reddit thread with 27 upvotes documents a coordinated chalet-booking scam ring operating through premiumskichalets.com and similar look-alike domains.

Multiple groups of experienced traveler-skiers booked Zermatt houses via the site, wired payment after professional-seeming communication, and arrived to find the property either nonexistent or already occupied — with the scammer ring banking the deposits and disappearing. The Reddit poster's coworker had been hit by the same operator two weeks prior.

The trap is the wire transfer. Premium Ski Chalets and similar phantom operators copy real Zermatt chalet hero photos, post under-market rates that beat Booking.com listings by 20 to 30 percent, and respond promptly during the booking flow with polished English. The single red flag — wire-transfer-only payment with no card-hold option — is dismissed as normal European business practice. Two weeks before arrival, the rental contract is silent; the morning of check-in, the address is wrong or the keybox empty.

The defense is platform discipline. Zermatt Tourism's official site at zermatt.ch publishes a verified-operator list for chalets and apartments; legitimate Zermatt chalet operators use.ch domains, list a Swiss VAT number, accept refundable card holds rather than wire transfers, and maintain Trustpilot or Google review histories. The 2025 Reddit thread's commenters echoed the same checklist:ch domain, Swiss VAT (CHE-XXX.XXX.XXX), refundable card-hold, verifiable Google Maps property pin. Refuse any chalet booking that demands wire transfer and book only through zermatt.ch's verified-operator list or directly via the property's.ch website.

Red Flags

  • Operator domain ends in.com.eu rather than.ch
  • No Swiss VAT number (CHE-XXX.XXX.XXX format) listed on the website
  • Wire-transfer-only payment with no refundable card-hold option
  • Rate is 20-30 percent below comparable Booking.com listings
  • Property cannot be verified on Google Maps Street View

How to Avoid

  • Book chalets only through zermatt.ch's verified-operator list.ch property sites.
  • Refuse any booking that demands wire-transfer payment with no card alternative.
  • Verify the operator's Swiss VAT number on the official ESTV register.
  • Cross-check the property on Google Maps Street View before paying.
  • Use Trustpilot to verify operator reputation — refuse properties with no review history.
Scam #2
Visp-Zermatt Train Ticket Repurchase Forced
🔶 Medium
📍 Matterhorn Gotthard Bahn (MGB) Visp-to-Zermatt segment, Visp Station mobile-ticket inspection, Zermatt Bahnhof arrival platform

Matterhorn Gotthard Bahn conductors on the Visp-Zermatt segment force cash repurchase by refusing valid mobile tickets.

A 2024 Reddit thread documents the pattern: scanner declares the QR invalid, conductor demands cash on the spot under threat of trouble. The Reddit poster's wife had a typo in the email field of her SBB account but kept a screen-shot of the valid ticket number; the conductor on the previous Martigny-to-Visp leg accepted the ticket without issue, but the Visp-to-Zermatt conductor forced repurchase.

The pattern exploits the segmented MGB-versus-SBB ticketing on the Visp-Zermatt route. SBB tickets cover Visp; from Visp the route shifts to Matterhorn Gotthard Bahn (MGB) ticketing, and conductors on the MGB segment have been known to use scanner-failure as a pretext to force repurchase from tourists who cannot easily contest in real time. The Reddit poster eventually escalated at Zermatt Bahnhof and was offered a partial refund — but only after a confrontation with multiple agents.

The defense is documentation. Carry both a screen-shot of the SBB or MGB mobile ticket AND the booking confirmation email; if a conductor's scanner refuses the QR, request the conductor's badge number, photograph the inspection, and file a complaint at Zermatt Bahnhof's customer service desk on arrival. The Swiss Travel Pass and Half Fare Card both cover the Visp-Zermatt MGB segment, and pass holders should also carry the physical pass card. Carry both the QR ticket screen-shot and the booking confirmation email, photograph any inspection dispute, and escalate at Zermatt Bahnhof customer service rather than paying the repurchase on the train.

Red Flags

  • Conductor scanner refuses to read a properly-purchased mobile ticket
  • Conductor demands cash repurchase rather than offering a written dispute slip
  • Threat of trouble or police involvement if repurchase is refused
  • Inspection happens only on the MGB segment, not the prior SBB leg
  • Conductor refuses to provide a badge number or written documentation

How to Avoid

  • Carry both the QR ticket screen-shot AND the booking confirmation email.
  • Photograph any conductor inspection dispute including the badge or scanner.
  • Refuse cash repurchase on the train — request a written dispute slip instead.
  • Escalate at Zermatt Bahnhof customer service desk on arrival.
  • Use a Swiss Travel Pass or Half Fare Card for the Visp-Zermatt MGB segment.
Scam #3
Hotel Half-Pension Captive Restaurant Markup
🔶 Medium
📍 Zermatt 4-star and 5-star hotel restaurants, half-pension dinner package upsells, hotel-restaurant captive booking

Zermatt's premium hotels heavily push half-pension (HP) booking upgrades that lock guests into hotel-restaurant dinners at marked-up prices.

The 2026 Reddit 'My honest Zermatt review' thread with 63 upvotes flagged the hotel-restaurant captive pattern: HP rates appear cheaper than booking room-only and dining out, but the kaiseki-style fixed menus run 80 to 140 CHF per person for what an independent Zermatt restaurant charges 50 to 75 CHF for a comparable 3-course dinner.

The trap is the upfront framing. Booking sites display HP as a 30-50 CHF per-person upgrade above the room-only rate, which sounds cheaper than dining out at 80-100 CHF expected per night. What gets buried in the fine print: the HP dinner is a fixed menu with no à la carte option, no opt-out for skipping nights, and no refund for missed dinners. By night three of a five-night stay, the fixed-menu rotation feels punitive, but you are locked in.

The defense is to book room-only. Zermatt has dozens of independent restaurants — Brown Cow, Café DuSud, Whymper-Stube, Restaurant Walliserkanne — that price 3-course Swiss-Alpine dinners at 50-75 CHF without the captive markup. The 2021 Reddit thread on Zermatt budgeting (95↑) explicitly recommends staying at hostels or boutique hotels (La Petit, Backstage) without HP and packing or eating independently. Book room-only and eat at independent restaurants like Whymper-Stube or Brown Cow — half-pension dinners are 60-80 percent more expensive than equivalent independent meals.

Red Flags

  • Hotel pushes HP upgrade as a 30-50 CHF cheaper-than-dining-out option
  • HP fine print specifies no à la carte option and no missed-night refund
  • Fixed dinner menu is the same rotation every 3-4 nights
  • Hotel discourages mention of independent Zermatt restaurants
  • HP package excludes drinks, side orders, or post-dinner desserts

How to Avoid

  • Book room-only and eat at independent Zermatt restaurants.
  • Try Whymper-Stube, Brown Cow, Café DuSud, or Restaurant Walliserkanne for 50-75 CHF mains.
  • Read the HP fine print before adding.
  • Stay at boutique hotels (La Petit, Backstage) where HP is not pushed.
  • Pack lunches from the Coop on Bahnhofstrasse to offset on-mountain restaurant prices.
Scam #4
Gornergrat Bahn Pass Discount Confusion
🟢 Low
📍 Gornergrat Bahn ticket counter at Zermatt Bahnhof, Klein Matterhorn cable-car pass office, Sunnegga funicular base

Gornergrat Bahn counter staff sell the 132 CHF rack rate without surfacing the 50 percent Swiss Travel Pass discount.

Pass holders pay full fare unless they explicitly produce the pass and request the discounted rate. A 2025 Reddit thread on Swiss lift passes documents the pattern: pass holders queuing at the ticket window receive no automatic discount unless they explicitly produce the pass card and ask for the discounted fare.

The mechanic is staff-discretion. Both the Swiss Travel Pass (consumer travel pass for tourists) and the Half Fare Card (locals' annual discount card) are widely valid on the Gornergrat Bahn — but the Zermatt Bahnhof MGB counter does not surface the discount as a default. The 132 CHF round-trip drops to 66 CHF with either pass; the savings on a family of four is 264 CHF in a single morning. Tourists who don't know to ask leave the discount on the table.

The defense is to buy on the SBB or Matterhorn Gotthard Bahn app, which automatically applies the Swiss Travel Pass or Half Fare Card discount linked to your account. Alternatively, present the physical pass at the counter and explicitly request the discounted fare in English: 'I have a Swiss Travel Pass — half fare please.' The 2026 Reddit thread on European resort-cost breakdowns lists the Gornergrat 50-percent discount as the single largest savings line for Zermatt visitors. Buy Gornergrat Bahn tickets on the SBB or Matterhorn Gotthard Bahn app linked to your Swiss Travel Pass or Half Fare Card — the 50-percent discount is automatic and saves 66 CHF per adult round-trip.

Red Flags

  • Counter staff quote the 132 CHF rack-rate without asking about passes
  • Pass discount is not displayed on the counter ticket-machine screen
  • Staff respond 'no discount available' without checking the pass card
  • Same-day on-counter ticket prices match the rack rate even for pass holders
  • App ticket option is not mentioned by counter staff

How to Avoid

  • Buy Gornergrat Bahn tickets on the SBB or Matterhorn Gotthard Bahn app.
  • Link your Swiss Travel Pass or Half Fare Card to the SBB app for auto-discount.
  • At the counter, explicitly state: 'I have a Swiss Travel Pass — half fare please.'
  • Use the Klein Matterhorn cable-car combo if you have the Peak Pass for additional savings.
  • Refuse rack-rate tickets if you have a valid pass and request the discounted fare.

🆘 What to Do If You Get Scammed

📋 File a Police Report

Go to the nearest Swiss Cantonal Police (Kantonspolizei) station. Call 117 (Police) or 112 (Emergency). Get an official crime report — you'll need this for insurance claims. You can also report online at police.ch.

💳 Cancel Your Cards

Call your bank immediately. Most have 24/7 numbers on the back of the card (keep a photo saved separately). Block any suspicious transactions before the thieves use your details.

🛂 Lost Passport?

Contact the US Embassy in Bern at Sulgeneckstrasse 19. For emergencies: +41 31-357-7011.

📱 Track Your Device

If your phone was stolen, use Find My (iPhone) or Find My Device (Android) from another device. Don't confront thieves yourself — share the location with police instead.
