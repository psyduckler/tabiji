Scam #1
Puerto Escondido Airport (PXM) & Oaxaca-Transfer Taxi Overcharge
🔶 Medium
📍 Puerto Escondido Airport (PXM) arrivals, Oaxaca Airport (OAX) transfer pickup, Zicatela Beach drop-offs, La Punta taxi queue, hotel-lobby transfer desks

PXM arrivals kiosks and Oaxaca-transfer drivers quote MX$ 600–MX$ 1,000 for a Zicatela ride that should cost MX$ 250–MX$ 350 on the official Transporte Terrestre prepaid booth.

Puerto Escondido is reached via the small PXM airport or a 7-hour drive or bus from Oaxaca City, and both entry points run a 2025 overcharge ecosystem. Community threads on community forums including 'Car Hire Necessary? Oaxaca / Puerto Escondido', document the current fare landscape.

Legitimate fares: PXM airport to Zicatela (7 km, 15 min) via official Transporte Terrestre prepaid MX$ 250–MX$ 350; Uber has limited availability in Puerto Escondido but does work — MX$ 150–MX$ 250 when available. Oaxaca-Puerto Escondido via ADO bus MX$ 380–MX$ 480 (7-hour night bus, safer than driving); via flight MX$ 1,800–MX$ 2,500 (45 min). The 2025 scam patterns: (a) PXM arrivals-curb kiosks quoting MX$ 600–MX$ 1,000 for Zicatela transfer (2-3x real); (b) 'VIP private driver' at MX$ 1,500 for standard sedan; (c) 'combined PXM + Mazunte tour US$ 80' bundled; (d) Oaxaca rental-car agencies offering 'one-way to Puerto Escondido' with MX$ 3,000–MX$ 5,000 'one-way drop fee' (real fair rate MX$ 1,200–MX$ 2,000); (e) hotel concierge 'recommended transfer' at 2x Uber rate; (f) colectivos at ADO terminal overcharging foreigners ('foreign fare MX$ 150 per person' vs real MX$ 40); (g) 'surf camp transfer' bundle at 3x independent cost; (h) Mazunte day-trip 'private driver' at US$ 80 one-way (real public transport MX$ 40–MX$ 60).

For travelers: (1) at PXM airport, use the official Transporte Terrestre prepaid booth inside arrivals at MX$ 250–MX$ 350 to Zicatela; Uber works but supply is thin, so allow 5-10 min of patience; (2) from Oaxaca City, take the ADO primera-clase night bus at MX$ 380–MX$ 480 (7-hour comfortable ride) rather than driving the winding mountain road; (3) IGNORE every 'VIP driver' upsell above MX$ 500; (4) for a Mazunte day-trip (70 km east), take a public colectivo MX$ 40–MX$ 60 or Uber MX$ 350–MX$ 500 one-way; (5) for long-term accommodation, book via Booking.com or Hotels.com and AVOID WhatsApp and Instagram private sellers; (6) save Puerto Escondido Tourist Police +52 954 582 0175.

Red Flags

  • PXM arrivals kiosk quoting MX$ 600–MX$ 1,000 to Zicatela (real MX$ 250–MX$ 350)
  • 'VIP private driver MX$ 1,500' for standard sedan
  • 'Combined PXM + Mazunte tour US$ 80' bundle
  • Oaxaca rental-car 'one-way drop fee MX$ 3,000–MX$ 5,000'
  • Colectivo driver demanding 'foreign fare MX$ 150' (real MX$ 40)

How to Avoid

  • Transporte Terrestre prepaid booth PXM-Zicatela: MX$ 250–MX$ 350
  • Oaxaca-PE via ADO primera-clase night bus MX$ 380–MX$ 480 (7-hour safe)
  • Mazunte day-trip: colectivo MX$ 40–MX$ 60 or Uber MX$ 350–MX$ 500
  • Book accommodation via Booking.com/Hotels.com (not WhatsApp)

Scam #2
Puerto Escondido Surf-School & Board-Rental Overcharge & Damage Scam
🔶 Medium
📍 Zicatela Beach surf rental shops, La Punta surf schools, Playa Carrizalillo board-rental kiosks, hotel-adjacent surf operators, 'instructor' beach approaches

Beach-approach 'instructors' and unverified rental shops at Zicatela, La Punta, and Carrizalillo push US$ 80 private lessons, 'pre-damaged' board fees, and bait-and-switch surf camps on first-time visitors.

Puerto Escondido's 'Mexican Pipeline' at Zicatela Beach is a world-class surf destination and supports a 2025 surf-school and board-rental overcharge ecosystem. Community threads on community forums including 'Surf spots in Mexico for an intermediate surfer?', cover the current pricing landscape.

Legitimate costs: Board rental at reputable shops: shortboard MX$ 250–MX$ 400 per 4 hours; longboard MX$ 350–MX$ 500. Surf lesson MX$ 500–MX$ 800 per 2 hours (one-on-one) or MX$ 350–MX$ 500 (group). Zicatela Beach is for EXPERIENCED surfers only (8-25 ft waves); La Punta (Puerto Escondido's west end) and Playa Carrizalillo are beginner/intermediate friendly. The 2025 scam patterns: (a) Zicatela beach-approach 'instructor' offering 'private lesson US$ 80' for non-qualified individuals (real school instructors have SERF or SERM certification); (b) board-rental 'pre-damage' fraud — unphotographed dings claimed as 'tourist damage' at return with US$ 80–US$ 200 demands; (c) 'surf camp all-inclusive US$ 600/week' with bait-and-switch accommodations and instruction quality; (d) 'Zicatela beginner lesson US$ 120' (Zicatela is too dangerous for beginners — this IS a scam by definition); (e) 'broken fin repair fee MX$ 800' for minor surface dings; (f) 'premium longboard US$ 60/day' for same board rentable at US$ 25 elsewhere; (g) hotel-concierge kickback where 'recommended surf school' is 1.5-2x Google-verified shop; (h) 'wetsuit rental MX$ 200/day' where wetsuits are often included in package.

For travelers — and especially non-strong swimmers: (1) DO NOT attempt surf at Zicatela 'Mexican Pipeline' — waves are 8-25 ft and have caused documented drownings; (2) for beginner/intermediate surf, choose La Punta or Playa Carrizalillo (gentler waves, beginner-friendly); (3) book surf lessons ONLY at Google 4.7+ schools: Puerto Surf School, Oasis Surf, Mexpipe Surf Academy — MX$ 500–MX$ 800 per 2-hour one-on-one; (4) verify instructor has SERF/SERM certification; (5) photograph board condition BEFORE leaving rental shop; Community reports document every scratch/ding with timestamp; (6) REFUSE beach-approach 'instructor' offers without verifiable certification; (7) for surf camps, book established operators via Booking.com (Selina Hostels has surf-lesson add-ons); (8) observe Zicatela water-safety: never surf alone, always wear a leash, and respect lifeguard warnings.

Red Flags

  • Beach-approach 'instructor' offering 'private lesson US$ 80' unlicensed
  • Board-rental shop with unphotographed dings at pickup
  • 'Surf camp all-inclusive US$ 600/week' bait-and-switch
  • 'Zicatela beginner lesson US$ 120' (Zicatela too dangerous for beginners)
  • 'Broken fin repair fee MX$ 800' for minor ding

How to Avoid

  • Book Google 4.7+ schools: Puerto Surf School, Oasis Surf, Mexpipe
  • Beginners: La Punta or Playa Carrizalillo (not Zicatela)
  • Verify instructor SERF/SERM certification
  • PHOTOGRAPH board condition BEFORE leaving rental shop
  • Wetsuit typically included in package; refuse MX$ 200/day add-on
Scam #3
Puerto Escondido Beach-Tour Unlicensed Boat & Turtle/Dolphin Operator
⚠️ High
📍 Playa Principal boat launches, Manialtepec Lagoon (bioluminescence + birds), Playa Bacocho turtle-release center, La Punta tour-booking booths

Unlicensed boat operators at Playa Principal and Manialtepec Lagoon push 'private' dolphin, turtle, and bioluminescence tours at 3-5x group rates, often without life jackets, VHF radio, or a captain's license.

Puerto Escondido's iconic boat experiences — morning dolphin and turtle sightings, Manialtepec Lagoon bioluminescence (Aug-Dec), and turtle releases at Playa Bacocho (year-round) — support a 2025 unlicensed-operator ecosystem. Community threads on community forums including 'First time, couple, March 2026' document the pattern.

Legitimate costs: Dolphin + sea-turtle morning boat tour MX$ 400–MX$ 700 per person (2-3 hours from Playa Principal). Manialtepec Lagoon bioluminescence + bird tour MX$ 500–MX$ 800 per person (3-4 hour evening, Aug-Dec only). Turtle release at Playa Bacocho / Playa Escobilla: MX$ 100–MX$ 200 entry fee (legitimate conservation program). The 2025 scam patterns: (a) Playa Principal touts selling 'private dolphin boat MX$ 2,500' when group tour is MX$ 500; (b) 'VIP bioluminescence MX$ 1,800 per person' when group tour is MX$ 700; (c) 'guaranteed dolphin/turtle sighting MX$ 300 extra' (wildlife can't be guaranteed; this is fraud); (d) unlicensed boats without life jackets, VHF radio, or insurance; (e) 'mandatory marine-park fee MX$ 200' at the boat launch (no such mandatory fee exists for Puerto Escondido tours); (f) off-season (Jan-July) bioluminescence offers — plankton is minimal; (g) 'private turtle-release ceremony US$ 80 per person' when conservation programs accept donations of MX$ 100–MX$ 200; (h) 'swim with dolphins US$ 120 per person' — dolphins in wild are not to be swum with (ethical concern + illegal); tourists who 'swim' get charged extra for cordoned-off setup.

For travelers: (1) book boat tours ONLY via Google 4.7+ operators: Lalo Ecotours (Manialtepec specialist), Playa Principal Tours, Hidden Voyages — MX$ 400–MX$ 800 per person group tour; (2) VERIFY life jackets, VHF radio, and captain license BEFORE boarding; (3) REFUSE 'private' upsells — group tours are same experience at 1/5th cost; (4) for Manialtepec bioluminescence, only book Aug-Dec on dark-moon nights; (5) REFUSE 'guaranteed sighting' upcharges — wildlife unpredictable; (6) for turtle release, go to Playa Bacocho at 5-6 PM, pay conservation fee MX$ 100–MX$ 200, observe ethical release; AVOID 'private ceremony' upsells; (7) do NOT participate in 'swim with wild dolphins' — unethical and illegal; (8) motion-sickness meds helpful — open Pacific can be rough; (9) for travelers with mobility concerns, boat-dock boarding can be slippery — request help or pass on tour; (10) bring biodegradable sunscreen for marine-safety.

Red Flags

  • Beach tout selling 'private dolphin boat MX$ 2,500' (group MX$ 500)
  • 'Guaranteed dolphin/turtle sighting MX$ 300 extra' (wildlife can't be guaranteed)
  • 'Mandatory marine-park fee MX$ 200' at boat launch (fake)
  • Off-season (Jan-July) bioluminescence tour offerings
  • 'Swim with wild dolphins US$ 120' (unethical + illegal)

How to Avoid

  • Book Google 4.7+ operators: Lalo Ecotours, Playa Principal Tours
  • Verify life jackets, VHF radio, captain license BEFORE boarding
  • Manialtepec bioluminescence ONLY Aug-Dec dark-moon nights
  • Turtle release at Playa Bacocho 5-6 PM with MX$ 100–MX$ 200 conservation fee
  • Refuse 'swim with wild dolphins' (illegal + unethical)

Scam #4
Puerto Escondido Zicatela & La Punta Restaurant Tourist-Menu Padding
🔶 Medium
📍 Zicatela main beachfront restaurants, La Punta surf-bar strip, Rinconada tourist dining, Centro Mercado Zicatela tourist-menu venues

English-only tourist menus along the Zicatela beachfront and La Punta surf-strip run 2-3x local prices, pre-add 18% gratuity on top of a tip line, and pitch 'complimentary' guacamole that arrives as a MX$ 180 line item.

Puerto Escondido's Zicatela beachfront and La Punta surf-strip host a 2025 tourist-menu bill-padding ecosystem common to Mexican beach destinations. Community threads on community forums including 'Last minute accommodation in Puerto Escondido' cover the landscape.

The 2025 scam patterns: (a) Zicatela beachfront restaurants with English-only menus at 2-3x local prices (fish taco MX$ 120 tourist vs MX$ 40 at Mercado Benito Juárez); (b) 'gratuity 18%' pre-added + tip line (double-tipping); (c) 'credit-card processing 10-15%' announced at bill; (d) 'seafood market price' at MX$ 1,500 lobster without weight disclosed; (e) 'complimentary guacamole' arriving as MX$ 180 line item; (f) USD-priced menus padding 10-15% exchange; (g) 'live music service MX$ 100/person' added unannounced; (h) La Punta surf-bar 'tequila shots buy 3 get 1 free' where 'free' is the overpriced one; (i) 'premium mezcal flight US$ 45' at standard mezcal.

For travelers: (1) eat at AUTHENTIC Puerto Escondido spots 2-3 blocks off Zicatela: Mi Pequeño Mundo (Mercado Benito Juárez), La Hosteria (authentic Oaxacan, Google 4.7+), Espadín Mezcal Bar (transparent mezcal pricing), Restaurante Los Tios (seafood); (2) Zicatela beachfront reputable: Almoraduz (Google 4.7+ with printed menu), Espresso Bar — budget MX$ 250–MX$ 400 per meal; (3) La Punta has shifted to more 'digital nomad' prices — expect US$ 15–US$ 25 per meal at reputable Nautilo, Lychee; (4) INSPECT bill — refuse 'gratuity + tip' double; Community reports confirm card surcharge 0-3%; (5) request MX$ bill (not USD); (6) seafood: agree weight × price/kg IN WRITING; (7) refuse uninvited 'complimentary' appetizers; (8) for budget eats, Mercado Benito Juárez (Rinconada) has authentic Oaxacan at MX$ 60–MX$ 120 per meal; (9) buy tequila or mezcal at Chedraui (Rinconada) rather than at bars — a bottle runs MX$ 400–MX$ 1,000 vs MX$ 200 per shot.

Red Flags

  • Zicatela beachfront English-only menu at 2-3x local prices
  • 'Gratuity 18%' pre-added + tip line (double-tipping)
  • Seafood 'market price MX$ 1,500 lobster' without weight
  • 'Complimentary guacamole' as MX$ 180 line item
  • 'Live music service MX$ 100/person' unannounced

How to Avoid

  • Eat off Zicatela: Mi Pequeño Mundo, La Hosteria, Los Tios at MX$ 250–MX$ 400
  • Zicatela reputable: Almoraduz, Espresso Bar with printed menu
  • Budget: Mercado Benito Juárez authentic MX$ 60–MX$ 120 per meal
  • Inspect bill; refuse double-tipping; card 0-3%
  • Seafood: weight × price/kg IN WRITING; request MX$ bill

Puerto Escondido Airbnb Surf-House Fake-Listing & Long-Term Rental Fraud
⚠️ High
📍 Airbnb/Booking.com Puerto Escondido listings, Instagram 'PE Surf House' DMs, Facebook 'Puerto Escondido Rentals' groups, WhatsApp-only digital-nomad housing offers

Instagram DMs, WhatsApp-only 'surf house' listings, and Facebook rental groups use stolen photos to demand full-upfront bank transfers for properties that don't exist or sit 300m inland from the beach they advertise.

Puerto Escondido's 2020-2025 digital-nomad and surf-tourism boom has outpaced legitimate accommodation supply, creating a 2025 fake-listing ecosystem. Community threads on community forums including 'Last minute accommodation in Puerto Escondido needed', cover the current landscape.

The 2025 scam patterns: (a) Instagram DM/Facebook 'exclusive surf house' listings using stolen photos — properties don't exist or are 200-500m from surf; (b) WhatsApp-only 'digital-nomad monthly rental' demanding full-month payment upfront via bank transfer; (c) confirmed-booking cancellations during peak season (Dec-Feb, Jun-Aug Mexican Pipeline) with re-listing at 3-5x rate; (d) fake 'beachfront surf camp' where the 'beachfront' is actually 300m+ inland; (e) 'all-inclusive surf + accommodation + lessons US$ 1,200/week' bait-and-switch quality; (f) 'deposit US$ 500 to secure' non-refundable bank transfer; (g) on-arrival bait-and-switch to inferior property; (h) 'cleaning deposit US$ 200 cash' that never returns; (i) 'mandatory weekly cleaning fee US$ 80' added unexpectedly mid-stay.

For travelers: (1) book ONLY via Booking.com, Hotels.com, Airbnb (platform-protected); or direct from verified hotel websites (Selina Hostels, Hotel Santa Fe, Bungalows Pacífico) — all with Booking.com 4.6+ ratings; (2) NEVER bank-transfer to Instagram DM or WhatsApp private sellers; platform secure payment only; (3) cross-reference property name + photos on Google Maps + Tripadvisor BEFORE booking — fake if zero independent results; (4) for peak season (Dec-Feb, Jun-Aug), book 3-6 months ahead on Booking.com with free cancellation; (5) AVOID Airbnb for peak season on Puerto Escondido — Mexican Airbnb cancellation pattern is documented; (6) for monthly digital-nomad stays, prefer Selina Hostels or verified co-living spaces (Casa Wabi Surf, Boulder Guesthouse); (7) photograph property condition on arrival; (8) confirm 'beachfront' vs 'beach access' in writing — a 200m 'walk to beach' is a common misleading claim.

Red Flags

  • Instagram DM 'exclusive surf house' with stolen photos
  • WhatsApp-only monthly rental requiring full-upfront bank transfer
  • 'Beachfront' advertised that's 200-500m inland
  • 'Surf camp all-inclusive US$ 1,200/week' bait-and-switch
  • 'Mandatory weekly cleaning fee US$ 80' added mid-stay

How to Avoid

  • Book ONLY via Booking.com, Hotels.com, Airbnb (platform-protected)
  • Reputable: Selina Hostels, Hotel Santa Fe, Bungalows Pacífico (4.6+)
  • NEVER bank-transfer to Instagram/WhatsApp private sellers
  • Cross-reference name + photos on Google Maps + Tripadvisor

Scam #6
Puerto Escondido Beach Vendor & 'Massage on the Beach' Pressure Scam
🔶 Medium
📍 Zicatela beachfront, Playa Carrizalillo, Playa Principal, La Punta sunset zone, beach-vendor commercial stretches

Beach vendors and 'massage on the beach' pitches across Zicatela, Playa Carrizalillo, and La Punta open with 'whatever you want to pay' or 'free henna,' then escalate into MX$ 500–MX$ 1,200 demands before you can leave.

Puerto Escondido's beaches support an active 2025 vendor culture similar to Puerto Vallarta's Playa Los Muertos. Community threads on community forums including 'First time, couple, March 2026' document the pattern.

The 2025 scam patterns: (a) beach vendors walking up with sarongs, jewelry, henna, coconuts — 'whatever you want to pay' pitch that becomes MX$ 500–MX$ 1,200 demand; (b) 'free henna tattoo' that becomes MX$ 400 when trying to leave; (c) 'massage on the beach MX$ 400 for 60 min' that ends with MX$ 1,200 'extended service' bill; (d) 'fruit vendor buy 2 get 1 free' where 'free' is the overpriced one; (e) 'friendly local photographer' offering to take family photo with YOUR phone, demanding MX$ 200–MX$ 500 'tip'; (f) 'beach-chair rental US$ 25' at free public sand; (g) 'surfboard leash rental MX$ 100' at beach shop with included leash in board rental; (h) 'snorkel mask rental MX$ 200' at Playa Carrizalillo (included in beach-club access MX$ 50).

For travelers: (1) firm 'no gracias' and keep walking — don't stop or engage vendors; (2) all Puerto Escondido beaches are PUBLIC federal property — no entrance fee or chair 'rental' on sand is mandatory; legitimate beach clubs offer chair + umbrella MX$ 100–MX$ 200 with drink/food minimum; (3) REFUSE every 'free' offer — henna, photos, fruit — if accepted, negotiate and agree price BEFORE service; (4) for beach massage, book at inland spa (Spa Ecológico Pochote, Temazcal Zicatela) at MX$ 800–MX$ 1,500 for 60-90 min authentic; (5) take photos yourself with phone timer OR ask a restaurant server; (6) refuse 'beach-chair rental US$ 25' — walk to sand and sit on towel; (7) for snorkel at Playa Carrizalillo, buy own mask/snorkel at OXXO MX$ 150 (total purchase) vs MX$ 200/day rental; (8) for coconut vendor, ask price BEFORE and have small bills ready; fair MX$ 50–MX$ 80 per opened coconut; (9) Playa Carrizalillo is the most vendor-dense beach; Playa Principal is calmer; La Punta at sunset is party-dense (louder vendor culture).

Red Flags

  • Beach vendor 'whatever you want to pay' then demanding MX$ 500+
  • 'Free henna' becoming MX$ 400 at the end
  • 'Massage on the beach MX$ 400 for 60 min' becoming MX$ 1,200
  • 'Friendly photographer' using your phone, demanding MX$ 200 tip
  • 'Beach-chair rental US$ 25' at free public sand

How to Avoid

  • Firm 'no gracias' and keep walking; no engagement
  • Beaches are PUBLIC — no mandatory chair rental on sand
  • REFUSE 'free' offers; negotiate price BEFORE any service
  • Beach massage: book inland at Pochote or Temazcal Zicatela
  • Buy own snorkel mask at OXXO MX$ 150 total (not MX$ 200/day rent)

🆘 What to Do If You Get Scammed

📋 File a Police Report

Go to the nearest Mexican Police (Policía) station. Call 911. Get an official crime report — you'll need this for insurance claims. You can also report online at gob.mx.

💳 Cancel Your Cards

Call your bank immediately. Most have 24/7 numbers on the back of the card (keep a photo saved separately). Block any suspicious transactions before the thieves use your details.

🛂 Lost Passport?

Contact your nearest embassy or consulate. The US Embassy is at Paseo de la Reforma 305, Cuauhtémoc, 06500 Mexico City. For emergencies: +52 55-5080-2000.

📱 Track Your Device

If your phone was stolen, use Find My (iPhone) or Find My Device (Android) from another device. Don't confront thieves yourself — share the location with police instead.
