The 3 Scams

Scam #1
The Positano Parking Hustle
🔶 Medium
📍 Positano roadside, unofficial parking areas

You drive to Positano and a guy in a reflective vest waves you into a 'parking spot' on the side of the road.

He charges €40 for 4 hours. Official street parking is €3/hour. His 'spot' may not even be legal — and you could return to find a real parking ticket on your windshield on top of what you already paid.

Red Flags

  • Person waving you into a spot isn't at an official parking facility
  • Reflective vest but no uniform or ID
  • Cash only, no receipt or ticket machine
  • Price much higher than posted street parking rates

How to Avoid

  • Use official parking garages in Positano (book in advance in summer)
  • Street parking machines issue printed tickets — only pay at those
  • Take the SITA bus from Sorrento instead of driving
  • Better yet, take the ferry from Salerno or Amalfi to avoid driving entirely
Scam #2
The Restaurant Sea View Surcharge
🟢 Low
📍 Waterfront restaurants in Amalfi, Positano, Ravello

You sit down at a stunning cliffside restaurant in Ravello.

The menu prices look steep but acceptable. After eating, the bill includes a €5 per person 'coperto' (cover charge), a €3 per person 'servizio' (service charge), a €15 'view supplement,' and the '€2 bread and water' you didn't order. A €30 lunch becomes €65.

Red Flags

  • No prices for bread, water, or cover charges listed on the menu
  • Waiter brings bread and water without asking
  • Bill includes line items you didn't see on the menu

How to Avoid

  • Always ask about coperto, servizio, and any supplements before ordering
  • Say 'acqua del rubinetto' for free tap water
  • Decline bread if you don't want to pay for it
  • Check Google reviews for complaints about hidden charges before choosing a restaurant
Scam #3
The Fake Tour Boat Operator
🔶 Medium
📍 Amalfi harbor, Positano beach

A man on Amalfi harbor offers private boat tours along the coast for €150 — half the going rate.

You pay cash, board a small boat, and spend 2 hours instead of the promised 4. The 'Blue Grotto stop' is actually just circling the entrance because he doesn't have the permit to enter. He drops you at Positano instead of returning to Amalfi, saying 'schedule changed.'

Red Flags

  • Price significantly below the standard €250-400 for private coast tours
  • Cash only, no invoice or company name
  • Boat has no visible registration or company branding
  • Tour details are vague and verbal only

How to Avoid

  • Book through established operators listed on the official Amalfi Coast tourism site
  • Expect to pay €250-400 for a legitimate 4-6 hour private boat tour
  • Confirm in writing: duration, stops, return point, and what's included
  • Viator and GetYourGuide offer verified operators with cancellation protection

🆘 What to Do If You Get Scammed

📋 File a Police Report

Go to the nearest Carabinieri / Polizia di Stato station. Call 112 (Carabinieri) or 113 (Polizia). Get an official crime report — you'll need this for insurance claims. You can also report online at poliziadistato.it.

💳 Cancel Your Cards

Call your bank immediately. Most have 24/7 numbers on the back of the card (keep a photo saved separately). Block any suspicious transactions before the thieves use your details.

🛂 Lost Passport?

Contact your nearest embassy or consulate. The US Embassy in Rome is at Via Vittorio Veneto 121, 00187 Rome. For emergencies: +39 06-4674-1.

📱 Track Your Device

If your phone was stolen, use Find My (iPhone) or Find My Device (Android) from another device. Don't confront thieves yourself — share the location with police instead.
