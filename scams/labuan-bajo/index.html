Scam #1
Labuan Bajo Komodo Airport (LBJ) & Hotel Transfer Overcharge
🔶 Medium
📍 Komodo International Airport (LBJ) arrivals, Labuan Bajo harbour shuttle pickups, hotel-transfer pickups at airport exit, marine-terminal drop-off

flights up 40% year-over-year post-2023 infrastructure upgrade — and the arrival transfer ecosystem has scaled with the traffic.

flights up 40% year-over-year post-2023 infrastructure upgrade — and the arrival transfer ecosystem has scaled with the traffic. The legitimate taxi fare LBJ to Labuan Bajo town center (5 km) is Rp 75K–100K; to specific marine terminals (Boat Basin) Rp 125K–175K.

The 2025 scams: (a) arrivals-curb taxi kiosks quoting Rp 250K–500K for the 5-km transfer (real Rp 75K–100K); (b) 'hotel transfer included' bait where the 'driver' charges for a fabricated 'toll road fee' or 'harbour entry fee' at drop-off; (c) Grab/Gojek weak presence in Labuan Bajo (smaller city, thinner driver supply) — touts exploit this with 'Grab doesn't work here' claim (partially true but not a license to overcharge); (d) return-airport overcharge where drivers who bring you from hotel to marine terminal demand Rp 400K for the reverse trip at 5 AM pre-cruise departure.

For older travelers: (1) most hotels in Labuan Bajo include free airport transfer with advance booking (email or WhatsApp 24 hours ahead) — use this; (2) if paying for transfer, book through your hotel at Rp 75K–150K (hotel-arranged) not airport-curb taxi; (3) Grab IS available in Labuan Bajo but with thinner supply (wait 5–15 min) — typical fare LBJ-to-town Rp 50K–80K; (4) for pre-cruise early-morning marine-terminal transfers, arrange with your hotel the night before at Rp 100K–150K; (5) IGNORE every arrivals-curb kiosk quoting Rp 250K+; (6) for late-arriving flights (most LBJ flights land 8 AM–6 PM; some 10 PM arrivals), airport taxi is the only option — negotiate Rp 150K firm before loading; (7) always ask for a printed rate card before agreeing to any transfer.

Red Flags

  • Arrivals-curb kiosk quoting Rp 250K–500K for 5-km transfer to town
  • 'Grab doesn't work here' claim used to justify 3x overcharge
  • 'Toll road fee' or 'harbour entry fee' added at drop-off
  • Return-trip quote Rp 400K for pre-dawn marine-terminal transfer
  • No printed rate card or written quote before loading

How to Avoid

  • Use hotel's free airport transfer (most include with advance booking).
  • If paying, book via hotel at Rp 75K–150K, not airport-curb taxi.
  • Grab IS available in Labuan Bajo but slower — wait 5–15 min.
  • Pre-book pre-dawn marine-terminal transfer night before (Rp 100K–150K).
  • Ask for printed rate card; negotiate Rp 150K firm before loading.
Scam #2
Komodo National Park Entry Fee Confusion ($500 Pass vs Real Rp 500K)
⚠️ High
📍 Komodo National Park entry at Loh Liang (Komodo Island), Loh Buaya (Rinca Island), Pink Beach, Padar viewpoint, Labuan Bajo park-entry booking offices

Komodo National Park has gone through a turbulent 2023–2025 pricing policy change that scammers now exploit aggressively. In Aug 2022 the Indonesian government announced a USD250 per person 'premium pass' for Komodo Island — massive backlash caused the policy to be canceled. The 2025 reality: the entry fee is Rp 500,000 (~US$30) per foreign visitor per day, paid at the park booth on arrival; Rinca Island (alternative dragon viewing, same species) has the same fee.

The 2025 scams exploiting this confusion: (a) Labuan Bajo tour operators still quoting 'Komodo Premium Pass $250–$500 per person' as if it were legitimate — this policy was CANCELLED in 2022; (b) fake-ticket resellers online selling 'skip-the-line Komodo pass Rp 1.5M–2M' — there is no skip-the-line and real fee is Rp 500K at the gate; (c) in-park 'Ranger service fee Rp 500K' demanded at Loh Liang — rangers ARE required for dragon-proximity safety but the fee is included in the Rp 500K entry; (d) 'Komodo Conservation Foundation donation Rp 200K' demanded after entry — no such mandatory donation; (e) Pink Beach 'separate entry fee Rp 150K' — Pink Beach is INSIDE the National Park and covered by the Rp 500K day-pass.

For older travelers: (1) the REAL 2025 fee is Rp 500,000 (~US$30) per foreign visitor per day — paid at the park gate on arrival; includes ranger accompaniment (mandatory for dragon proximity); (2) REFUSE any tour operator quoting 'Komodo Premium Pass $250+' — that policy is CANCELLED; (3) Pink Beach, Padar viewpoint, and Rinca are all covered by the same day-pass; (4) no 'skip-the-line' exists — the queue is 15–30 min even in peak season; (5) no 'mandatory donation' — refuse any post-entry donation demand; (6) Rinca Island has the same dragons as Komodo (same species) and is 30 min closer (less boat time) — consider Rinca for older travelers who don't need the 'Komodo Island' bucket-list checkbox; (7) confirm your boat tour includes the Rp 500K entry fee in writing — some operators quote boat price only and add entry at the gate; (8) bring Rp 500K per day per person in small bills (Rp 50K, 100K) — park gate does not take cards; (9) do NOT approach dragons without a ranger; fatalities occur annually.

Red Flags

  • Tour operator quoting 'Komodo Premium Pass $250–$500' (policy CANCELLED 2022)
  • 'Skip-the-line pass Rp 1.5M–2M' sold online (no skip-the-line exists)
  • 'Ranger service fee Rp 500K' demanded separately (included in entry)
  • 'Komodo Conservation Foundation donation Rp 200K' post-entry
  • 'Pink Beach separate fee Rp 150K' (Pink Beach is inside the park)

How to Avoid

  • Pay ONLY Rp 500,000 per person per day at the park gate — real 2025 fee.
  • REFUSE every 'Premium Pass' quote — that policy was canceled 2022.
  • Pink Beach, Padar, Rinca all covered by same day-pass.
  • Rinca Island has the same dragons — often better for older travelers.
  • Bring Rp 500K per person in small bills (park gate doesn't take cards).

typically covering Padar viewpoint, Pink Beach, Komodo or Rinca Island, and Manta Point snorkel — is the region's flagship excursion and hosts a 2025 safety-compromise ecosystem.

typically covering Padar viewpoint, Pink Beach, Komodo or Rinca Island, and Manta Point snorkel — is the region's flagship excursion and hosts a 2025 safety-compromise ecosystem.

The 2025 scams: (a) boat-type misrepresentation — 'fast speedboat 1-hour to Padar' becomes an old wooden phinisi at 3-hour speed; (b) overcrowding — 30+ passengers on boats with legal capacity 20; (c) missing safety equipment — no life jackets, no VHF radio, no first-aid, crew without proper training; (d) 'private tour Rp 8M per boat' when the same boat is sold simultaneously to 3 other groups; (e) no-show breakfast/lunch or food quality far below advertised 'fresh seafood' (rice + egg reality); (f) weather-cancellation scam — 'trip canceled due to weather, Rp 200K refund fee' when actual weather was fine.

  • Boat type promised 'fast speedboat' turns out to be slow wooden phinisi
  • 30+ passengers on a boat with 20-passenger legal capacity
  • No visible life jackets, VHF radio, or first-aid kit
  • 'Private tour' sold simultaneously to 3 other groups on same boat
  • Last-minute 'weather cancellation Rp 200K refund fee' with clear skies

How to Avoid

  • Book via Klook, Viator, or certified licensed operators only.
  • Reputable 2025: Wanua Adventure, Komodo Tour Flores, Sunshine Bali.
  • CONFIRM boat type, passenger count, food menu, safety gear in writing.
  • For older travelers, one-day speedboat tour is safer than overnight phinisi.
  • Verify life jackets and VHF radio BEFORE departure.

Scam #4
Komodo Liveaboard Overpromising & Quality-Mismatch Scam
⚠️ High
📍 Labuan Bajo harbour liveaboard terminals, online liveaboard booking sites, marine-terminal boat-boarding, remote-anchorage overnight stops

The 'Komodo liveaboard' (3D2N, 4D3N, 6D5N phinisi schooner cruise covering Komodo, Rinca, Padar, Pink Beach, Manta Point + overnight anchorages) is one of Southeast Asia's iconic experiences — and hosts a 2025 quality-mismatch scam ecosystem at every price tier.

The 2025 scams: (a) price tier misrepresentation — 'deluxe cabin with private bathroom' photographed from a different boat; real cabin is a 4-person bunkroom with shared toilet; (b) itinerary cuts — promised 'Manta Point + Padar sunrise + Pink Beach + Kelor Island' becomes 2 of 4 sites due to 'weather'; (c) dive-operator bundling scam where liveaboard claims 'PADI-certified dives included' but operator is not licensed; (d) food-quality gap — 'fresh seafood daily' becomes rice + egg + instant noodles from day 2; (e) drinking-water scarcity (large-bottle water 'not included' with Rp 50K per bottle at sea); (f) dangerous boats without VHF, radar, life rafts, or safety certification. for experienced-diver liveaboard selection.

For older travelers — or anyone without strong boat experience — the practical defense: (1) book ONLY through established operators with 50+ Google or Tripadvisor 4.8+ reviews: Le Voyage, Calico Jack, Luxury Cruise Komodo, Samata, Sea Safari; verify via direct booking (not resale sites); (2) for older travelers (65+), choose mid-tier or luxury operators (Le Voyage 4D3N $1,500+, Samata $2,500+) — budget operators cut corners dangerously; (3) CONFIRM in writing: specific boat name (not operator brand), exact cabin type (photo from your actual cabin), exact itinerary, food menu, dive-operator certification, safety equipment list (VHF, radar, life rafts, AED); (4) avoid 3D2N overnight on-boat unless the cabin photos are verifiable; consider DAY-TRIP speedboat from Labuan Bajo (Rp 1.8M–2.5M) hitting the same sites with return to hotel nightly; (5) purchase travel insurance with marine/evacuation coverage (US$100–$150 extra for comprehensive); (6) verify the boat has radar and VHF — Flores Sea can be rough and remote; (7) if boat quality is grossly misrepresented upon boarding, you have legal standing to refuse and demand refund — don't board a clearly unsafe boat.

Red Flags

  • Liveaboard photos showing 'deluxe cabin' that don't match actual boarding
  • Operator cutting itinerary sites 'due to weather' in clear conditions
  • 'PADI dives included' but operator has no visible dive-shop license
  • Food quality drops to rice+egg+instant noodles from day 2
  • Boat lacks VHF radio, radar, life rafts, or visible safety certification

How to Avoid

  • Book operators with 50+ Google/Tripadvisor 4.8+ reviews (Le Voyage, Samata).
  • Older travelers: mid-tier/luxury tier ($1,500+); budget tier cuts corners.
  • CONFIRM boat name, cabin photos, itinerary, food, safety gear in writing.
  • Consider day-trip speedboat (Rp 1.8M–2.5M) instead of overnight.
  • Purchase marine/evacuation travel insurance; verify VHF + radar.
Scam #5
Pink Beach / Padar / Rinca 'Mandatory Ranger' & Photo-Spot Fee Padding
🔶 Medium
📍 Pink Beach (Komodo National Park), Padar Island viewpoint summit, Rinca Island (Loh Buaya entry), Kelor Island snorkel stop, Kanawa Island approach

Beyond the headline Komodo dragon experience, the secondary Komodo National Park sites (Pink Beach, Padar viewpoint, Rinca Island, Kelor, Kanawa) host a 2025 add-on fee scam layered on the core Rp 500K entry.

The 2025 scams: (a) 'mandatory Pink Beach ranger Rp 300K' — no ranger is required at Pink Beach (it's a public beach inside the park, day-pass covers access); (b) Padar viewpoint 'summit fee Rp 150K' — the 1-hour uphill hike has no separate fee, day-pass covers it; (c) 'best photo spot access Rp 100K' at Padar's Instagram-famous viewpoint — no such fee exists; (d) Rinca Island 'separate entry Rp 250K' — Rinca is in the same park, same Rp 500K day-pass; (e) Kelor Island 'snorkel gear hire Rp 150K' when your boat-tour operator should include it; (f) 'conservation fundraiser' requested by park rangers post-photo for 'dragon medication'.

For older travelers: (1) the ONE Rp 500,000 day-pass covers Komodo Island, Rinca Island, Padar Island, Pink Beach, Kelor, Kanawa, and Manta Point — NO additional per-site fees exist for foreign tourists; (2) REFUSE every 'Pink Beach ranger', 'Padar summit', 'best photo spot' fee claim — none is legitimate; (3) Padar summit requires 1-hour uphill hike (sometimes wet stairs in rainy season) — for older travelers with knee issues, view from the mid-rim or skip; (4) Komodo/Rinca dragons — rangers ARE mandatory for proximity safety (included in entry); walk in a single line behind ranger, keep 5m distance from dragons, no flash photography; (5) Kelor Island snorkel gear — if your boat didn't include it, ask about operator refund; don't pay Rp 150K onsite for low-quality rental; (6) refuse any post-photo 'conservation fundraiser' request — donate directly to Komodo Survival Program online if wanting to help; (7) carry Rp 500K for entry in small bills (50K, 100K); (8) respect wildlife — dragons have attacked tourists getting too close; photograph with zoom lens from distance.

Red Flags

  • 'Mandatory Pink Beach ranger Rp 300K' claim (no ranger required there)
  • 'Padar viewpoint summit fee Rp 150K' (included in day-pass)
  • 'Best photo spot access Rp 100K' at Padar (no such fee)
  • 'Rinca Island separate entry Rp 250K' (covered by same day-pass)
  • 'Conservation fundraiser Rp 200K for dragon medication' post-photo

How to Avoid

  • ONE Rp 500,000 day-pass covers ALL park sites — Komodo/Rinca/Padar/Pink Beach.
  • REFUSE every 'ranger fee', 'summit fee', 'photo-spot fee' claim.
  • Rangers for dragon-proximity ARE included in entry — don't pay extra.
  • Carry Rp 500K per person in small bills (50K, 100K) for park gate.
  • Donate to Komodo Survival Program online if wanting to support conservation.

🆘 What to Do If You Get Scammed

📋 File a Police Report

Go to the nearest Indonesian National Police (Polri) station. Call 110 (Police) or 112 (Emergency). Get an official crime report — you'll need this for insurance claims. You can also report online at polri.go.id.

💳 Cancel Your Cards

Call your bank immediately. Most have 24/7 numbers on the back of the card (keep a photo saved separately). Block any suspicious transactions before the thieves use your details.

🛂 Lost Passport?

Contact your nearest embassy or consulate. The US Embassy in Jakarta is at Jl. Merdeka Selatan No. 3-5, Jakarta 10110. For emergencies: +62 21-5083-1000.

📱 Track Your Device

If your phone was stolen, use Find My (iPhone) or Find My Device (Android) from another device. Don't confront thieves yourself — share the location with police instead.
