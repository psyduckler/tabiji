The 6 Scams

Scam #1
Bariloche Rental Car Smash-and-Grab at Scenic Viewpoints
⚠️ High
📍 Circuito Chico scenic viewpoints (Cerro Campanario, Llao Llao, Punto Panorámico), Ruta 40 shoulder pullouts, Cerro Catedral ski parking, Cerro Otto overlook

is the 2025 Bariloche-specific anchor documenting a pervasive smash-and-grab pattern against rental cars at scenic viewpoints.

+ + + Community reports document the Argentine Patagonia pattern. The setup: tourists park a rental car at Cerro Campanario, Punto Panorámico on Circuito Chico, or a Ruta 40 shoulder pullout to take photos; a team of 2–3 smashes the passenger-side window and grabs visible valuables (cameras, laptops, backpacks, wallets) in 15–30 seconds. Peak-activity zones: Cerro Campanario lower parking (1,050m), Circuito Chico Llao Llao viewpoint, Ruta 40 Nahuel Huapi shore pullouts, Cerro Otto base station, Puerto Pañuelo ferry parking. The thieves target rental-agency-branded cars (Avis, Hertz, Europcar stickers visible) and out-of-province plates (not P-series Rio Negro).

For older travelers driving in Bariloche, the defensive playbook: (1) never leave visible valuables in the rental car — all bags, laptops, cameras, jackets, shopping bags go in the trunk before you arrive at the viewpoint (thieves watch the parking lot and target cars where items are transferred trunk-side at the viewpoint itself); (2) better: take valuables with you to viewpoints rather than leaving them in the car (most Circuito Chico viewpoints are 2–15 min walks from parking); (3) rental car: choose models without rental-branding stickers, and with local P-series plates if possible (ask the agency); (4) for Cerro Catedral ski-season parking, use the paid structured lots (Valet Cerro Catedral, $3,000–$5,000 ARS per day) with attendant rather than free street parking; (5) if you witness a break-in or return to a broken window, do not chase the thieves — call 911 and Turismo Policía Bariloche (0294-442-2515); file a denuncia (police report) at the local Comisaría within 24h — Required for both rental-car insurance and travel insurance; (6) leaving a car unlocked with an obvious 'nothing to steal' note sometimes reduces break-ins vs locked car with trunk assumed-valuable (rental-car advice from community forums is split on this); (7) consider skipping car rental and using organized Circuito Chico bus tour ($30–$45 USD per person via Turisur or Bariloche Adventure) if you only plan 1–2 scenic-viewpoint days.

Red Flags

  • Rental car with agency-branded sticker or out-of-province plate at viewpoint
  • Visible valuables (camera, laptop, backpack) in car interior
  • Parking in unmarked free pullout without attendant
  • Cars parked at the same viewpoint with smashed passenger windows already visible
  • Leaving car while all 4 passengers go to viewpoint (no one watching)

How to Avoid

  • All valuables in trunk before arriving at viewpoint (not at viewpoint itself).
  • Take valuables with you to viewpoints where feasible (2–15 min walks).
  • Paid structured lots at Cerro Catedral (Valet, $3,000–$5,000/day).
  • Rent cars without agency-branding stickers; request local P-series plate.
  • Skip rental car entirely: Turisur or Bariloche Adventure Circuito Chico bus $30–$45.
Scam #2
Bariloche Chocolate Shop Tourist Markup (Havanna, Rapa Nui, Mamuschka)
🟢 Low
📍 Centro Civico / Calle Mitre chocolate strip (Havanna, Rapa Nui, Mamuschka, Del Turista, Abuela Goye), Bariloche Centro tourist shops, hotel-concierge chocolate packages

Bariloche's 'Swiss Alps of Argentina' branding (German-Swiss settlers arrived 1890s–1940s) includes an iconic chocolate tradition.

Legitimate chocolate shops on Calle Mitre: Rapa Nui (Argentine-owned, since 1937, flagship store Mitre 202), Mamuschka (since 1988, Mitre 216), Del Turista (since 1949, Mitre 239), Abuela Goye (since 1961, Mitre 258), Havanna (national chain, Mitre 220). All are genuinely historic and produce quality chocolate — the 'scam' is not in their authenticity but in aggressive markup on Calle Mitre tourist strip vs factory stores and supermarkets. 2025 pricing: 250g chocolate bar at Calle Mitre tourist shop $18–$28 USD; same item at Rapa Nui factory store (Av. Bustillo 1550, 5 km from center) $10–$15; supermarket Coto or La Anónima in Bariloche $8–$12 for mass-market brands (not the artisan chocolatiers but often same Argentine chocolate). Scam variants: (1) hotel-concierge 'Bariloche Chocolate Package' at $80–$150 bundling what's $30–$50 at factory stores; (2) Calle Mitre 'chocolate factory tour' at $25+ USD that's actually just a shopping visit to the tourist store; (3) 'Edición Limitada Bariloche' packaging at 3–5x mass-market price for near-identical recipe; (4) 'bulk tasting fees' $10–$15 per person that supermarket sampling plates cover free.

For older travelers shopping for Bariloche chocolate, the fair-price playbook: (1) for authentic chocolates, visit factory stores outside Calle Mitre: Rapa Nui factory (Av. Bustillo 1550, 5 km via Bus 10 or short Uber — $5–$8 USD), Mamuschka factory (outside town), Abuela Goye factory; prices are 30–50% lower than Calle Mitre for identical products; (2) if you prefer Calle Mitre walking experience, compare prices across 3+ shops before buying — Del Turista often has promotional prices; (3) avoid hotel-concierge 'Chocolate Package' at $80–$150 — identical to $30–$50 at factory stores; (4) for budget-conscious gifts, Coto or La Anónima supermarket stocks mass-market Argentine chocolates (Cabsha, Cachafaz, Bon o Bon, Pixar) at $8–$12 USD for 500g assortment; (5) skip the 'Chocolate Factory Tour' paid visits — they're just shopping stops; the free Museo del Chocolate at Havanna (Mitre 220); (6) transport chocolate home: winter months avoid day-shipping; summer months pack chocolates in insulated bag + check as luggage; carry-on risks melting during Patagonia-Buenos Aires connection; (7) for authentic Bariloche craft/souvenir beyond chocolate, visit the Feria Artesanal (Mitre + Rolando) for local leather + wood + textiles at fair artisan prices.

Red Flags

  • Calle Mitre chocolate bar at $18–$28 USD (same item factory store $10–$15)
  • Hotel-concierge 'Chocolate Package' at $80–$150
  • 'Chocolate Factory Tour' at $25+ USD that's just a shopping visit
  • 'Edición Limitada Bariloche' packaging at 3–5x mass-market price
  • 'Bulk tasting fee' $10–$15 per person

How to Avoid

  • Visit factory stores: Rapa Nui Av. Bustillo 1550 (30–50% lower than Calle Mitre).
  • Compare 3+ Calle Mitre shops before buying.
  • Supermarket mass-market: Coto / La Anónima Argentine chocolate $8–$12/500g.
  • Skip paid 'Chocolate Factory Tours' — just shopping stops.
  • For non-chocolate souvenirs: Feria Artesanal Mitre + Rolando.
Scam #3
Bariloche Circuito Chico Tour Package & Centro Cívico Pickpocket
🟢 Low
📍 Centro Cívico (Bariloche main plaza), Circuito Chico 60 km scenic loop, Llao Llao Hotel area, Avenida Bustillo tour-operator strip, hotel-concierge Circuito Chico desks

Bariloche's Circuito Chico is Patagonia's most iconic scenic drive.

60 km loop west of town through Cerro Campanario (1,050m viewpoint via chairlift), Llao Llao Hotel (luxury landmark), Punto Panorámico, Playa Bonita, and Colonia Suiza artisan market. Legitimate 2025 tour options: (1) self-drive rental car $40–$60 USD/day (with smash-and-grab caveat — See separate scam entry); (2) Turisur bus tour ($30–$40 USD per person, half-day, Spanish narration); (3) Bariloche Adventure bus tour ($35–$45 USD, English option); (4) Uber/Cabify private ($80–$120 USD for a 4-hour round trip with photo stops); (5) public Bus 20 (ARS2,000, 90 min one-way, infrequent). Scam variants: (1) hotel-concierge 'Circuito Chico VIP Tour' at $150–$250 USD per person for what's $30–$40 via direct Turisur; (2) unlicensed 'Bariloche Adventure' imitators at $40–$60 without insurance or proper permitting; (3) 'Private Photography Tour Circuito Chico' at $400+ USD for a standard bus tour with extra camera stops; (4) Cerro Campanario chairlift combo 'VIP' tickets at $50+ USD when direct is $25 at the base station.

For older travelers in Bariloche, Circuito Chico clean playbook: (1) most accessible: Turisur half-day bus tour ($30–$40 USD, book at Turisur office Mitre 219 or online at turisur.com.ar) — Includes all major viewpoints, pickup from hotels; (2) for English narration, Bariloche Adventure ($35–$45, book direct at baricheadv.com.ar); (3) Cerro Campanario chairlift ($25 USD) operates 9am–6pm Dec–Apr, 10am–5pm rest of year — book day-of at base station (no reservation needed, rarely sells out); (4) Llao Llao Hotel lobby + grounds are free to visit — Don't pay 'entrance fee' to anyone claiming to represent the hotel; (5) Colonia Suiza artisan market (Sunday morning) is free to enter and offers legitimate local crafts at fair prices; (6) for pickpocket awareness in Centro Cívico (Bariloche's main plaza), keep wallet in front pocket or money belt; occasional petty theft during peak ski season (June–September) and summer (December–February) holidays; (7) avoid hotel-concierge 'Circuito Chico VIP' upgrades — identical content to $30 direct bus tour.

Red Flags

  • Hotel-concierge 'Circuito Chico VIP Tour' at $150–$250 per person
  • Unlicensed 'Bariloche Adventure' imitator without permit or insurance
  • 'Private Photography Tour Circuito Chico' at $400+ USD
  • 'Cerro Campanario VIP combo' at $50+ (direct chairlift is $25)
  • 'Llao Llao entrance fee' tout at the hotel gates (hotel is free to visit)

How to Avoid

  • Turisur bus tour $30–$40 USD — book at Mitre 219 or turisur.com.ar.
  • English option: Bariloche Adventure $35–$45 at baricheadv.com.ar.
  • Cerro Campanario chairlift $25 direct at base station (no reservation needed).
  • Llao Llao Hotel lobby + grounds are free to visit — refuse 'entrance fee' touts.
  • Colonia Suiza Sunday artisan market free and fair-priced.

Scam #4
Bariloche BRC Airport & Long-Distance Bus Terminal Overcharge
🔶 Medium
📍 Bariloche International Airport (BRC), Bariloche Omnibus Terminal (Estación Terminal de Ómnibus), Av. 12 de Octubre bus operator ticket booths, 'Private Transfer Patagonia' online sites

Bariloche International Airport (BRC) is 14 km east of downtown (20 min drive).

Legitimate 2025 transfers: (1) Uber/Cabify/DiDi via app $8,000–$18,000 ARS ($8–$18 USD) depending on surge; (2) licensed metered taxi at official queue $15,000–$25,000 ARS; (3) shared airport shuttle (Via Bariloche Shuttle, Bariloche Transfer) $4,000–$6,000 ARS per person; (4) public Bus 72 or 74 ARS1,500 (slow, infrequent). Long-distance bus terminal (ETO) at Av. 12 de Octubre 1884: legitimate operators include Via Bariloche (region leader), Andesmar, Flecha Bus, Mercedes-Benz, and Chevallier for routes to Buenos Aires (22-hour overnight, $80–$150 USD first-class), Mendoza (17-hour, $60–$120), and El Bolsón (2-hour, $10–$15). Scam variants: (1) BRC arrivals-hall 'Private Transfer Patagonia' touts at $80–$150 USD for an $8–$18 USD Uber ride; (2) 'Private driver Bariloche-El Calafate' online-booked services at $800–$1,500 USD when Via Bariloche bus is $60–$100 USD (24-hour journey); (3) 'Flybondi' budget airline cancellations per community reports (2025) — Flybondi has documented 2025 pattern of cancelling flights day-of with minimal compensation; (4) 'Taqsa bus company' reliability issues per community reports (2025) — Taqsa has mixed reviews for Patagonia routes; (5) bus terminal 'luggage worker' demanding $2,000–$5,000 ARS 'baggage handling fee' per community reportst receive wages' (2025).

For older travelers at BRC or Bariloche bus terminal, the clean playbook: (1) from BRC airport, use Uber/Cabify/DiDi at the designated rideshare zone ($8,000–$18,000 ARS) or licensed metered taxi ($15,000–$25,000); avoid arrivals-hall 'Private Transfer' touts at $80+ USD; (2) for long-distance bus from Bariloche, book only via Via Bariloche (viabariloche.com.ar — Region-leading reliability), Andesmar (andesmar.com), or Flecha Bus (flechabus.com.ar) — buy tickets directly via each company's website or at the ETO terminal counter; (3) avoid Taqsa for Bariloche-area routes per 2025 Reddit warnings — Consider Via Bariloche or Andesmar as alternatives; (4) for Flybondi flights (domestic budget airline), understand the 2025 cancellation-risk profile: if ticket is for key itinerary (e.g. single-day connection to El Calafate), book Aerolíneas Argentinas instead; (5) for Bariloche-El Calafate route (24-hour bus), Via Bariloche cama-suite class ($80–$120 USD) is the most reliable — OR fly Aerolíneas Argentinas (1.5 hour, $150–$250 USD); (6) at bus terminal, 'baggage handling fee' demands from uniformed 'workers' are illegitimate — your bus ticket includes baggage — refuse and report to terminal management; (7) confirm any Booking.com BRC-area hotel reservation by phone 1 week before arrival.

Red Flags

  • BRC arrivals-hall 'Private Transfer Patagonia' at $80–$150 USD
  • 'Private driver Bariloche-El Calafate' at $800–$1,500 USD (bus is $60–$100)
  • Flybondi ticket day-of cancellation with minimal compensation
  • Taqsa bus reliability issues per 2025 Patagonia reports
  • Bus terminal 'luggage handling fee' $2,000–$5,000 ARS demanded

How to Avoid

  • BRC: Uber/Cabify/DiDi ($8k–$18k ARS) or metered Radio Taxi ($15k–$25k).
  • Long-distance bus: Via Bariloche / Andesmar / Flecha Bus direct booking.
  • Avoid Taqsa for Bariloche-area; avoid Flybondi for critical connections.
  • Bariloche-El Calafate: Via Bariloche cama-suite ($80–$120) or Aerolíneas flight.
  • Refuse bus terminal 'baggage handling fee' demands.
Scam #5
Bariloche Cerro Catedral Ski Resort & Lift-Pass Reseller Scam
🟢 Low
📍 Cerro Catedral ski resort (South America's largest by skiable terrain), Catedral Alta Patagonia base area, hotel-concierge ski-pass desks, Villa Catedral parking, third-party ski-pass reseller sites

Cerro Catedral is Patagonia's flagship ski resort (1,200 ha skiable terrain, 39 lifts, 120 km pistes) operating June–October.

Legitimate 2025 pricing at Catedral Alta Patagonia (catedralaltapatagonia.com): day-pass adult low-season (June, late October) $45–$60 USD; day-pass high-season (July 15–August 31, 'vacaciones de invierno') $70–$95 USD; week-pass low $280–$380 USD; week-pass high $450–$600 USD. Rental equipment: skis + boots $25–$40 USD/day, snowboard $30–$45, helmet $8–$12. Scam variants: (1) hotel-concierge 'Cerro Catedral VIP Ski Package' at $200–$400 per day bundling $70 day-pass + $35 rental + lunch at 3x markup; (2) third-party ski-pass reseller sites charging 50–100% markup over Catedral Alta Patagonia direct pricing; (3) 'fast-track lift-pass' add-ons at $50+ per day (doesn't exist — Catedral does not have a skip-the-line program); (4) Villa Catedral parking 'attendants' demanding $3,000–$5,000 ARS cash for free street parking; (5) ski-gear rental shops outside Villa Catedral at 2x the on-mountain prices with lower-quality equipment; (6) off-platform Airbnb 'ski-in/ski-out condo' at Villa Catedral demanding USD cash — legitimate ski accommodation is booked via Booking.com or the Cerro Catedral official lodging partners.

For older travelers skiing Cerro Catedral, the clean playbook: (1) buy lift passes directly at catedralaltapatagonia.com or at the Villa Catedral ticket windows — $45–$95 per day depending on season; (2) rent equipment ON THE mountain at Catedral Alta Patagonia gear shop or affiliated Scandinavian or Casa del Deportista (Villa Catedral base) — $25–$45 per day full kit; avoid gear-rental shops in Bariloche town (2x markup); (3) avoid hotel-concierge 'VIP Ski Package' at $200–$400/day — identical experience for $105–$140 direct; (4) 'Fast-track lift pass' does not exist — refuse this upsell; Catedral lift lines peak 10am–2pm and are reasonable otherwise; (5) Villa Catedral parking: use the paid structured lot at Catedral base ($3,000–$5,000 ARS/day attended) — refuse 'attendants' at free street parking; (6) for on-mountain lunch, the Refugio Lynch (at top of Punta Princesa lift) offers best views at legitimate $25–$40 USD; on-mountain Villa Catedral base restaurants $15–$30; avoid 'VIP mountain lunch' packages at $60+ USD; (7) for accommodation at Villa Catedral (ski-in/ski-out), book via Booking.com platform: Hotel Catedral Inn, Pire-Hue (boutique), Apartments Villa Catedral — never off-platform Airbnb with cash USD demand; (8) for older travelers new to skiing or refreshing, the Escuela Argentina de Esquí at Cerro Catedral offers lessons at $60–$100 USD per 2-hour group session with English-speaking instructors — book direct.

Red Flags

  • Hotel-concierge 'Cerro Catedral VIP Ski Package' at $200–$400/day
  • Third-party ski-pass reseller at 50–100% markup over direct
  • 'Fast-track lift-pass' $50+ upsell (doesn't exist at Catedral)
  • Villa Catedral 'parking attendant' demanding $3,000–$5,000 ARS for free street parking
  • Bariloche-town gear-rental at 2x on-mountain prices

How to Avoid

  • Lift passes direct at catedralaltapatagonia.com or Villa Catedral windows ($45–$95/day).
  • Rent gear on-mountain at Catedral or Scandinavian / Casa del Deportista ($25–$45/day).
  • Skip 'VIP Ski Package' — identical for $105–$140 direct.
  • Parking: paid structured lot at Catedral base ($3,000–$5,000 attended).
  • Accommodation: Booking.com platform only (Hotel Catedral Inn, Pire-Hue, Apartments Villa Catedral).

Bariloche's short-term accommodation market experiences extreme peak-season compression: ski season (mid-June to August) and summer (December–February) see 3–5x normal pricing with Villa Catedral and Av. Bustillo properties often sold out 6+ months ahead. Legitimate 2025 pricing: Bariloche downtown 3-star $60–$120 USD/night shoulder (Mar–May, Sep–Nov), $150–$280 USD peak; boutique Llao Llao area $200–$400 shoulder, $500–$900 peak; full cabaña rentals (Panamericana Bariloche, Villa Sofia, Cumbres de Bariloche) $150–$400 shoulder, $400–$800 peak. Scam variants: (1) Facebook Marketplace / WhatsApp 'Bariloche cabaña ski-season' listings demanding USD cash-on-arrival at inflated rates; (2) 'Villa La Angostura cabin Panamericana' listings at 30% below market demanding Zelle/PayPal with cloned Booking.com photos; (3) Airbnb 'partly platform, partly USD cash' TOS-violation requests (Argentina-wide pattern); (4) Booking.com confirmed-reservation cancellations during peak season with re-listing at 2–3x original rate; (5) 'ski-in/ski-out Villa Catedral apartment' listings at 50% below comparable verified listings — Too-good-to-be-true; (6) cross-references general Bariloche-area fraud patterns.

For older travelers booking Bariloche accommodation, the protective playbook: (1) book short-term stays only via Airbnb/VRBO/Booking.com platform payment in full — never Zelle/Venmo/PayPal friends-and-family or USD cash-on-arrival, regardless of what the host says; (2) for peak ski season (June–August) and summer (December–February), book 6–9 months ahead via Booking.com — Last-minute 'Bariloche ski cabaña' Facebook listings are 90%+ fraud; (3) legitimate Bariloche hotels: Llao Llao Hotel Resort (iconic Argentine landmark), Alma del Lago Suites & Spa, Cacique Inacayal Lake Hotel, Nido del Cóndor, Hotel Panamericano Bariloche; Villa Catedral: Hotel Catedral Inn, Pire-Hue; boutique: Rochester Hotel Bariloche; (4) for long-term winter rentals (1+ month), use legitimate Argentine real-estate portals ZonaProp / ArgenProp / Bariloche-specific Inmobiliaria Alonso or Nuevo Tiempo — never WhatsApp or Facebook Marketplace listings; (5) Airbnb: verify host has 10+ reviews with 4.7+ rating + superhost status; refuse 'partly cash' requests; (6) confirm Booking.com reservation with property BY phone 1 week before arrival (Argentina-wide 2025 cancellation pattern); (7) for ski-accommodation specifically, cross-reference 'ski-in/ski-out' claim on Google Maps — Villa Catedral proper is the only true ski-in/ski-out; 'near Villa Catedral' listings 5+ km away require 30-min Uber to the lifts.

  • Airbnb 'partly platform, partly cash' TOS-violation request
  • Booking.com peak-season reservation cancelled + re-listed at 2–3x

🆘 What to Do If You Get Scammed

📋 File a Police Report

Go to the nearest Policía Federal Argentina station. Call 911 (Police) or 107 (Medical Emergency). Get an official crime report — you'll need this for insurance claims. You can also report online at fiscales.gob.ar.

💳 Cancel Your Cards

Call your bank immediately. Most have 24/7 numbers on the back of the card (keep a photo saved separately). Block any suspicious transactions before the thieves use your details.

🛂 Lost Passport?

Contact your nearest embassy or consulate. The US Embassy in Buenos Aires is at Avenida Colombia 4300, C1425GMN Buenos Aires. For emergencies: +54 11-5777-4533.

📱 Track Your Device

If your phone was stolen, use Find My (iPhone) or Find My Device (Android) from another device. Don't confront thieves yourself — share the location with police instead.

Always buy at the factory stores on Av. Bustillo, not the Calle Mitre tourist strip. Bariloche's chocolate scene is a genuine regional specialty (inherited from Swiss/German/Austrian settlers) but Calle Mitre's concentration of Havanna, Rapa Nui, Mamuschka, Del Turista, Abuela Goye stores charges tourist markup of 30–50% on identical product sold cheaper at the factories. The 2025 playbook: (1) Rapa NUI (Av. Bustillo Km 4.5) — full factory + tasting room, 30% cheaper than the Mitre location, same product; (2) Mamuschka (Av. Bustillo Km 7 factory) — full selection at tasting-room prices, ~35% off Mitre; (3) Abuela Goye (Av. Bustillo factory store) — 35–40% off Mitre for premium bonbons and chocolate bars; (4) Del Turista (Av. Bustillo outlet) — Regional chocolates at factory prices; (5) Havanna alfajores — buy at any supermarket (La Anónima, Chango Más, Carrefour Bariloche) for ~40% less than Havanna tourist-street stores — they're identical factory product. Línea 20 bus runs Av. Bustillo Km 0 (Centro) to Km 25 (Llao Llao) every 20 min, stops directly at each factory. The Calle Mitre stores are fine for a quick sample but never for bulk-buy gifts.
Circuito Chico is a 60 km scenic loop west of Bariloche along Av. Bustillo through Lago Nahuel Huapi forest to Llao Llao Hotel and back. Stunning scenery but the signature risk is rental-CAR smash-AND-grab at scenic pullouts. Per, window breaks are documented at: Cerro Campanario parking (chairlift area), Llao Llao Hotel public parking, Punto Panorámico pullout, Bahía López pullout. The clean playbook: (1) never leave bags, cameras, laptops, passports, or anything visible in the car at any stop — take all valuables with you on every pullout, or use the hotel safe before departing; (2) park in full public view, not secluded forest pullouts; (3) photograph rental car condition at pickup (all sides + dashboard + odometer) and immediately report any damage to police (911 + Comisaría Turística Bariloche +54 294 442-3022); (4) rent from Hertz / Avis / Localiza at BRC airport — Not unofficial street booths; (5) full insurance with zero-deductible recommended ($50–$90 USD/day); (6) if windows broken, file denuncia (police report) within 24 hours — Required for rental-company insurance claims; (7) alternative if worried: take the guided Circuito Chico tour (Via Bariloche, Bariloche Turismo, $80–$120 USD/person) or Línea 20 municipal bus (ARS2,500) + stop-and-go.
BRC (Bariloche International Airport) is 14 km east of Bariloche center (20–30 min). Legitimate 2025 options: (1) Uber / Cabify / DiDi from the designated rideshare zone outside arrivals — typically $12,000–$20,000 ARS ($11–$18 USD), best default; (2) Línea 72 municipal bus from airport to Terminal de Ómnibus — ARS2,000 ($2 USD), 45 min, with luggage racks (cheapest option, stops at Centro Cívico en route); (3) Airport-taxi official counter inside arrivals — posted flat rates $20,000–$35,000 ARS ($18–$32 USD); (4) hotel shuttle if staying at Llao Llao / Charming Luxury / Alma del Lago — verify booking confirmation includes airport pickup. Avoid all arrivals-ranks 'taxi flat-rate' quotes of $40–$80 USD (2–4x legitimate) and 'Private Transfer' touts quoting $80–$150 USD. Confirm Uber/Cabify driver's license plate matches app screenshot before entering; photograph plate. Pay via app. For long-distance buses out of Bariloche (to El Bolsón, El Chaltén, El Calafate, Buenos Aires), book Via Bariloche / Andesmar / Flecha only — verify routes + schedules at plataforma10.com.ar — avoid small operators Taqsa / TAC (frequent breakdowns + cancellations per community reports traveler reports).
Book only via Airbnb / VRBO / Booking.com / Hotels.com platform payment in full — never off-platform. Bariloche's signature accommodation scams target premium-property seekers: (1) 'Llao Llao cabin direct rental' offers via WhatsApp / Instagram / Facebook Marketplace demanding 30–50% USD-cash deposit wire — property often doesn't exist or is rented by someone other than the poster; (2) 'Villa La Angostura private chalet' (30 km north of Bariloche) with photos stolen from legitimate listings, 'owner' requesting Western Union or crypto deposit; (3) 'corporate rate' emails claiming to be from Llao Llao Hotel / Charming Luxury Lodge / Correntoso Lake & River Hotel offering 40% off via intermediary booking agent — all premium Bariloche properties book only via their official websites, never intermediaries. The clean playbook: (1) for mid-range stays use Booking.com / Airbnb platform payment with full protection + dispute mechanism; (2) for premium properties (Llao Llao Hotel, Charming Luxury Lodge, Correntoso, El Casco Art Hotel, Alma del Lago) book direct via the property's official website — verify URL matches TripAdvisor/Booking.com listing link; (3) for cabañas verify Google Street View of the address, review count 50+ on Booking/Airbnb, and look for 'verified host' badges; (4) never pay via wire transfer, Western Union, USDT, or crypto — all reputable Bariloche accommodations accept Visa/Mastercard and Booking/Airbnb platform payment.
