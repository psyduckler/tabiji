The 6 Scams

Scam #1
Dalat Lien Khuong Airport (DLI) Taxi Overcharge & 'Officials' Scam
⚠️ High
📍 Lien Khuong Airport (DLI, 30 km from Dalat center), airport taxi queue, arrivals hall unofficial greeter zone

Dalat has a 2025-documented airport scam with an unusual twist.

One traveler wrote: 'There's video circulating showing ahem (officials) at the Dalat airport making an arriving' tourist pay additional fees that aren't legitimate — the scammers wear uniform-like clothing to appear authoritative. The legitimate fare from Lien Khuong Airport to central Dalat is 250,000–350,000 VND by Grab or licensed taxi on meter, or 60,000 VND by public bus. Unofficial operators quote 'fixed prices' of 500K–700K VND.

Community reports document the 2025 Grab rate: '250-300k VND from city center' — a clean baseline for older travelers...

For older travelers, the practical playbook: (1) book Grab or Xanh SM yourself on airport Wi-Fi AFTER luggage — expected fare 250K–350K VND; (2) licensed Mai Linh (green, 1055) on meter — expect 300K–400K VND; (3) the green public bus at 60K VND is the budget alternative but runs hourly; (4) REFUSE any 'official' or 'airport staff' demanding additional fees — Lien Khuong has no legitimate arrival fees beyond immigration; (5) for night arrivals after 10 PM, pre-arrange hotel transfer — taxi supply thins out; (6) photograph the taxi plate number.

Red Flags

  • 'Airport official' in uniform-like clothing demanding cash fees in arrivals
  • Driver quotes 'fixed price' 500K+ VND for DLI-Dalat center
  • Meter 'broken' at Lien Khuong airport
  • Unofficial greeter inside arrivals hall offering 'taxi' without visible license
  • Taxi livery almost matching Mai Linh but phone number slightly off

How to Avoid

  • Book Grab or Xanh SM on airport Wi-Fi AFTER luggage — expected 250K–350K VND.
  • Licensed Mai Linh (green, 1055) on meter — expect 300K–400K VND.
  • Public bus 60K VND (hourly) as budget alternative.
  • REFUSE 'officials' demanding fees — Lien Khuong has no legitimate arrival fees.
  • For night arrivals after 10 PM, pre-arrange hotel transfer.
Scam #2
Dalat Canyoning Unlicensed Operator & Safety Scam
⚠️ High
📍 Datanla Waterfall canyoning starting point, Dalat-center canyoning tour booking offices, unlicensed operators in Elephant Falls area

Dalat is Vietnam's canyoning capital — abseiling down waterfalls is the signature adventure activity.

But 2015–2025 community reports document serious safety and pricing scams.

The critical 2015 baseline: three British tourists drowned at Datanla Waterfall on a canyoning tour run by an unlicensed operator — the incident led to new Dalat canyoning regulations but unlicensed operators continue to run 'budget' tours at $25–$35 per person (legitimate rate is $55–$80 with proper safety gear, international-certified guide, insurance).' is the 2025 community guide warning to vet operators.

For older travelers (NOTE: canyoning is physically demanding — consider whether it's appropriate), the practical playbook: (1) use ONLY licensed operators: Viet Challenge (vietchallenge.com), Phat Tire Ventures (phattireventures.com), Highland Sport Travel — each with international-certified guides, proper equipment, and insurance; (2) expected rate: $55-80 per person for full-day canyoning including transfer, gear, lunch, insurance; (3) REFUSE budget operators at $25-35 — legal minimum for proper gear and insurance is $50+; (4) verify the operator carries international-standard canyoning certification; (5) for older travelers, consider alternative Dalat activities (cable car, flower gardens, coffee plantations, Crazy House) rather than canyoning.

Red Flags

  • Tour operator quotes $25-35 per person for full-day canyoning
  • No international canyoning certification visible or claimed
  • No insurance coverage mentioned in package terms
  • 'Budget tour' booked via hotel concierge or street tout
  • Operator has fewer than 100 Google reviews or mixed review patterns

How to Avoid

  • Use ONLY licensed operators: Viet Challenge, Phat Tire Ventures, Highland Sport Travel.
  • Expected rate: $55-80 per person full-day with certified guide, gear, insurance.
  • REFUSE budget operators at $25-35 — legal minimum for proper safety is $50+.
  • Verify international canyoning certification.
  • For older travelers, consider alternatives (cable car, Crazy House, flower gardens).
Scam #3
Dalat Flower Gardens & 'Valley of Love' Ticket & Photo Scam
🔶 Medium
📍 Valley of Love (Thung Lũng Tình Yêu) garden, Dalat Flower Gardens (Vườn Hoa Đà Lạt), photo-op photographer stations, Love Valley lake rental

Dalat's Flower Gardens and Valley of Love are major tourist attractions, but the photo-ticket-costume ecosystem around them has a documented pressure pattern.

The scam variants: (1) 'free photo session' pivots to 300K–500K VND per printed photo at the on-site photo stand; (2) 'traditional costume rental' at 150K–200K VND per hour with 'cleaning deposit' of 500K+ VND that becomes 'cleaning fee' at return; (3) swan boat rental quoted at 100K VND for 30 min but extends to 'additional 100K' if you're 5 minutes over; (4) 'romantic bridge photo station' fee at 100K VND on top of garden entrance; (5) 'horse-drawn carriage' quoted at 200K VND for '5 minutes' that turns out to be 2 minutes.

For older travelers, the practical playbook: (1) pay the Valley of Love entrance (100,000 VND adult, 2025) ONLY at the official ticket booth — decline any 'photographer included' upsell; (2) REFUSE 'free photo session' offers — scammers charge 200K+ per print after the fact; (3) for costume rental, confirm the RETURN condition in writing before paying and photograph the costume state at pickup; (4) for swan boats and horse carriages, confirm total price and time in writing; (5) bring your own camera — the photo stations are overpriced; (6) community-recommended alternatives: Dalat Flower Park (60K VND — smaller but less scam-pressured), Truc Lam Zen Monastery gardens (free), Love Hill (free with coffee purchase at Panorama Café).

Red Flags

  • 'Free photographer' approaches at garden entrance or photo-op zone
  • Costume rental with 500K+ VND 'cleaning deposit'
  • Swan boat 'additional time' charged at 5 minutes over
  • 'Romantic bridge photo station' fee on top of garden entrance
  • Horse carriage '5 minutes' that turns out to be 2 minutes

How to Avoid

  • Pay Valley of Love entrance (100K VND) ONLY at official booth.
  • REFUSE 'free photo session' offers — 200K+ per print charged after.
  • For costume rental, confirm return condition in writing; photograph state at pickup.
  • For swan boats/carriages, confirm total price + time in writing.
  • Alternatives: Dalat Flower Park (60K), Truc Lam Monastery gardens (free).

Scam #4
Dalat Fake Resort Booking & Hotel Page Fraud
⚠️ High
📍 Online — fake resort booking pages mimicking real Dalat properties, Booking.com/Agoda listings with fake photo reverse-image-search matches, private WhatsApp referrals

Dalat has a specific 2025-documented fake-resort-page scam.

One traveler wrote: 'The resort page was a fake/scam page created to look exactly like the actual page. It has' — scammers clone real Dalat resort website pages, redirect Google search results, and take payment via wire transfer or Bizum for bookings that don't exist at the real resort.

The 2025 scam patterns: (1) Google search for 'Dalat resort' returns ads that lead to clone pages; (2) clone pages have identical branding, photos, and booking forms as the legitimate resort; (3) payment request via wire transfer, Bizum, or unfamiliar gateway (legitimate resorts use Booking.com/Agoda or Stripe); (4) confirmation email arrives but booking doesn't exist at the real property; (5) at check-in arrival, the real resort has no booking record.

For older travelers booking Dalat accommodation, the protective playbook: (1) book ONLY via Booking.com, Agoda, Airbnb, or Vrbo with platform-verified payment; (2) if booking directly with a resort, navigate to the website by TYPING the URL, not clicking Google ads; (3) pay by credit card only — refuse wire transfer, Bizum, or cryptocurrency; (4) verify the resort's actual website URL by cross-referencing 2-3 sources (TripAdvisor, Booking.com listing, Google Maps business page); (5) community-recommended Dalat hotels with verified long review histories: Ana Mandara Villas Dalat, Dalat Palace Heritage Hotel, Dalat Edensee Lake Resort, Saigon-Da Lat Hotel; (6) if defrauded, file credit card chargeback within 48 hours and report to Vietnam consumer protection.

Red Flags

  • Resort page arrived via Google ad rather than direct typed URL
  • Payment requested via wire transfer, Bizum, or unfamiliar gateway
  • Booking confirmation email but no verification at Booking.com/Agoda cross-check
  • Resort name almost matches real property but URL has slight spelling difference
  • Prices 30-50% below comparable platform-verified rates

How to Avoid

  • Book ONLY via Booking.com, Agoda, Airbnb, or Vrbo with platform-verified payment.
  • If booking direct, TYPE the URL manually — never click Google ads.
  • Pay by credit card only — refuse wire transfer, Bizum, cryptocurrency.
  • Cross-reference real resort URL via 2-3 sources (TripAdvisor, Booking.com, Google Maps).
  • Community-recommended: Ana Mandara Villas Dalat, Dalat Palace Heritage, Dalat Edensee.
Scam #5
Dalat Night Market Food Vendor Overcharge & Shrinking Portions
🟢 Low
📍 Dalat Night Market (Cho Dem Dalat) on Phan Dinh Phung Street, street food vendor stalls around the night market perimeter, tourist-facing BBQ stands

Dalat Night Market is a highlight for many travelers, but has a documented pattern of tourist-vs-local pricing and portion manipulation.

The scam variants: (1) 'bánh tráng nướng' (Vietnamese pizza) quoted at 50,000 VND to tourists but 20,000 VND to locals for identical dish; (2) 'strawberry' bag quoted at 100K VND for what should be 30K (Dalat's strawberries are famous and legit-quality but tourist-area vendors mark up 3x); (3) skewered meats priced 40K+ per stick vs 15-20K local rate; (4) 'hot soy milk' (sữa đậu nành nóng) 25K VND vs 10K; (5) vendor 'accidentally' charges for an extra item you didn't order.

For older travelers, the practical playbook: (1) observe what locals pay before ordering — watch which stalls have Vietnamese customers vs tourist-only; (2) ask the price BEFORE ordering, and ideally point at posted prices if available; (3) carry small-denomination notes (10K, 20K, 50K) — paying with a large note increases 'no change' scam risk; (4) expected local prices: bánh tráng nướng 20K-30K, grilled sweet potato 10K-15K, soy milk 10K, skewer 15-20K, strawberry 50K/500g; (5) try community-recommended authentic venues for sit-down meals: Long Hoa Restaurant (Nguyen Du, posted Vietnamese menu), Trong Dong Restaurant (Bac Lieu), Nhat Ly (Khu Hoa Binh); (6) the night market is a photo-stop experience more than a food destination — plan for evening dinner elsewhere.

Red Flags

  • Vendor quotes price only after you've ordered
  • 'Bánh tráng nướng' at 50K+ VND when local rate is 20K
  • Strawberries 100K per 500g (local rate 50K)
  • Extra item added to your order that you didn't request
  • 'No small change' excuse when you hand over a large note

How to Avoid

  • Observe what locals pay before ordering at any stall.
  • Ask price BEFORE ordering; point at posted prices if available.
  • Carry small-denomination notes (10K, 20K, 50K) — avoids 'no change' scam.
  • Expected local prices: bánh tráng nướng 20-30K, soy milk 10K, skewer 15-20K.
  • For sit-down meals: Long Hoa, Trong Dong, Nhat Ly (posted Vietnamese menus).

Dalat Motorbike Tour & 'Easy Rider' Overcharge
🔶 Medium
📍 Dalat town center 'Easy Rider' solicitation spots, Hoang Van Thu Street tour offices, hotel-concierge motorbike tour bookings, Dalat-to-Hoi An multi-day Easy Rider trips

a pillion-passenger multi-day Central Highlands experience.

a pillion-passenger multi-day Central Highlands experience. Legitimate operators provide an experienced local rider, insurance, luggage transport, and honest pricing. But a 2024-2025 copycat ecosystem has emerged with inflated pricing and unlicensed operators. It's a good pace if yo' u're comfortable on the back of a motorbike.

The scam variants: (1) 'Easy Rider' solicitor at Dalat Market approaches tourists offering 'special rate' tours at $50-70/day; legitimate operators charge $60-80/day for proper gear, insurance, and experienced guides; (2) 'budget tour' at $35/day with no insurance and minimally-experienced rider (safety risk); (3) hotel concierge 'partner' tour at $90-120/day (kickback arrangement); (4) 'multi-day to Hoi An' priced at $500+ for 5-day trip when legitimate rate is $350-450; (5) rider refuses to provide helmets or insurance documentation.

For older travelers considering an Easy Rider experience, the protective playbook: (1) use ONLY community-vetted operators: Dalat Easy Riders (dalat-easyrider.com), Vietnam Easy Riders, OneTrip Vietnam — each has long-standing reputations and written insurance documentation; (2) expected rate: $60-80 per day for full-service tour including insurance, gear, luggage transport; (3) multi-day Dalat-Hoi An trip: $350-450 total for 5-6 days; (4) REFUSE street solicitor 'special rate' offers without verifiable operator credentials; (5) verify the rider has proper helmet for you AND valid insurance documentation; (6) for older travelers unfamiliar with Vietnamese traffic or motorbike-travel, a private car-with-driver via Klook is safer — $60-100/day for Dalat-area day trips, $500-700 for Dalat-Hoi An multi-day.

Red Flags

  • Street solicitor at Dalat Market offering 'special rate' Easy Rider tour
  • 'Budget tour' at $35/day with no insurance or helmet documentation
  • Hotel concierge 'partner tour' at $90-120/day
  • Rider doesn't provide passenger helmet or refuses insurance questions
  • 'Discount' multi-day Hoi An trip at $250 (legit rate $350-450)

How to Avoid

  • Use community-vetted operators: Dalat Easy Riders, Vietnam Easy Riders, OneTrip Vietnam.
  • Expected rate: $60-80 per day full-service with insurance, gear, luggage transport.
  • Multi-day Dalat-Hoi An: $350-450 total for 5-6 days.
  • REFUSE street solicitor 'special rate' without verifiable operator credentials.
  • Verify helmet + insurance documentation; for older travelers consider private car-with-driver ($60-100/day).

🆘 What to Do If You Get Scammed

📋 File a Police Report

Go to the nearest Vietnamese Police (Công An) station. Call 113. Get an official crime report — you'll need this for insurance claims. You can also report online at hanoi.gov.vn.

💳 Cancel Your Cards

Call your bank immediately. Most have 24/7 numbers on the back of the card (keep a photo saved separately). Block any suspicious transactions before the thieves use your details.

🛂 Lost Passport?

Contact your nearest embassy or consulate. The US Embassy is at 7 Lang Ha Street, Ba Dinh District, Hanoi. For emergencies: +84 24 3850-5000.

📱 Track Your Device

If your phone was stolen, use Find My (iPhone) or Find My Device (Android) from another device. Don't confront thieves yourself — share the location with police instead.
