Scam #1
Belo Horizonte (CNF) / Confins Airport-to-Ouro Preto Transfer Overcharge
🔶 Medium
📍 Aeroporto de Confins (CNF) arrivals, BH-Ouro Preto BR-040 route, rodoviária zones

Ouro Preto (UNESCO baroque colonial town 100 km SE of Belo Horizonte in Minas Gerais) has no commercial airport.

Travelers fly to Belo Horizonte Confins (CNF, 130 km NW of Ouro Preto, 2-hour drive) and transfer via bus, private car, or Uber. 2025 hotel-concierge + airport-tout commission markups on this transfer are systematic.

Legitimate 2026 cost: Uber / 99 from CNF to Ouro Preto R$350–R$500 (2 hours via BR-040); rental car from CNF R$180–R$280 per day (2-hour drive, BR-040 is well-maintained + signposted). Budget public option: Pássaro Verde ônibus 'Confins-Belo Horizonte Rodoviária' R$15 (1 hour) + change to Empresa União 'BH-Ouro Preto' R$45–R$60 (2 hours) = R$60–R$75 total, 4 hours door-to-door. Direct Confins-Ouro Preto shuttle (via Ouropreto Turismo / Ferrari Transportes) R$180–R$280 per person (pre-book 1 week ahead, 2-hour direct ride). The 2025 scam patterns: (a) airport tout at CNF offering 'private transfer Ouro Preto R$900' (real Uber R$350–R$500); (b) hotel-concierge 'VIP transfer with lunch stop in Tiradentes R$1,500' (real DIY); (c) fake 'Confins express bus to Ouro Preto direct R$250' sold at CNF — no such direct bus exists, passengers are dropped at BH Rodoviária and told to transfer (eating a 2-hour delay); (d) unlicensed remis drivers at CNF offering 'R$400 cash no receipt' — no insurance, no traceability; (e) 'BR-040 is dangerous at night' scare — ask for R$600 daytime-only surcharge (BR-040 is a well-maintained federal highway, safe day + night); (f) return-leg Ouro Preto hotel concierge selling 'airport drop-off R$800' at 2x markup vs pre-booked shuttle R$180–R$280; (g) 'mandatory BR-040 tolls R$100' padding (real: 2 tolls totalling R$8–R$15 on BR-040 Confins-Ouro Preto); (h) 'Tiradentes combined day-trip R$1,800' airport offer — Tiradentes is 200 km in a DIFFERENT direction; this is commission-stop pattern.

For travelers: (1) for door-to-door comfort, pre-book the Confins-Ouro Preto direct shuttle via Ouropreto Turismo or Ferrari Transportes at R$180–R$280 per person (2-hour direct ride, luggage-friendly, reputable) — reserve 1 week ahead at ouropretoturismo.com.br or via your Ouro Preto pousada; (2) OR book Uber at CNF at R$350–R$500 for 2-hour drive (confirm driver willingness for 130-km trip BEFORE booking); (3) Uber at CNF may have 10–20 min wait time — meet driver at the designated 'Aplicativos' pickup zone on CNF's arrivals level (signposted); (4) REFUSE every 'private transfer Ouro Preto R$900+' airport tout + hotel-concierge offer — commission markup 2–3x; (5) the cheapest option is Pássaro Verde CNF-BH + Empresa União BH-Ouro Preto (R$60–R$75 total, 4 hours) — works for budget travelers or solo backpackers but not ideal for travelers with luggage (two transfers, 4-hour trip); (6) for return to CNF, PRE-BOOK the Ouropreto Turismo shuttle at check-in to your pousada — don't wait until departure day; (7) REFUSE every 'BR-040 dangerous / combined Tiradentes R$1,800' upsell — direct transfer is the correct answer; (8) real BR-040 tolls are R$8–R$15 round-trip; REFUSE 'mandatory R$100 toll' surcharges; (9) DEATUR Minas Gerais (Belo Horizonte) +55 31 2108 6300; PROCON Minas Gerais 151.

Red Flags

  • Airport tout offering 'private transfer Ouro Preto R$900' (real Uber R$350–R$500)
  • Hotel-concierge 'VIP transfer + Tiradentes lunch R$1,500' (commission upsell)
  • Fake 'Confins-Ouro Preto direct bus R$250' (no such route exists)

Ouro Preto Centro Histórico 'Mandatory Guide' & Church-Entry 'Skip-the-Line' Scam
🟢 Low
📍 Praça Tiradentes, Matriz Pilar, Matriz Antônio Dias, Igreja São Francisco, Museu da Inconfidência

Ouro Preto's Centro Histórico (Brazil's first UNESCO heritage site, declared 1980.

An 8×6 block baroque colonial town famous for its 23 churches, the Inconfidência Mineira history, and Aleijadinho sculpture) attracts heavy tourist flow and hosts a 2025 unlicensed-guide + fake skip-the-line pattern.

Legitimate 2026 cost: Igreja Matriz de Nossa Senhora do Pilar R$20 entry (UNESCO's gold-leaf interior, 434 kg of gold on the altar); Matriz Antônio Dias R$20 (Aleijadinho sculpture + tomb); Igreja São Francisco de Assis R$20 (Aleijadinho's finest work — the façade + altarpiece); Museu da Inconfidência R$15 (Praça Tiradentes, Sociedade history); Casa dos Contos R$10 (colonial currency house); Mina da Passagem R$50 (historic gold mine tour, 2 km outside Centro). Licensed Cadastur guides (check the official badge issued by Ministério do Turismo) R$150–R$250 for a 3–4 hour guided circuit of main sites. The 2025 scam patterns: (a) 'mandatory guide R$200' offered on Praça Tiradentes by someone without visible Cadastur ID — REFUSE; licensed guides wear a visible badge; (b) fake 'skip-the-line church entry' tickets sold at R$60 each (real R$20 at the church door — there's rarely a line long enough to 'skip'); (c) 'combined ticket R$200 all 5 churches' sold on Praça Tiradentes — no such combined ticket officially exists; churches are individually ticketed (total 5 × R$20 = R$100 DIY); (d) 'IPHAN preservation tax R$80 per tourist' demanded at the Matriz Pilar — there is NO such tax; IPHAN (federal heritage institute) does not collect from visitors; (e) 'exclusive after-hours altar photography R$500' — Ouro Preto churches close at 4:45 PM; no legitimate after-hours exists; photography is PROHIBITED inside most churches (historical flash-damage concerns); (f) Mina da Passagem tout at Centro offering 'combined mine + 3 churches R$300' (real DIY: R$50 mine + R$60 for 3 churches = R$110 + Uber); (g) Praça Tiradentes 'local artesão' forcing a carved soapstone trinket into your hand for 'gift' — then demanding R$50 after you've touched it; (h) 'official Ouro Preto passport stamp R$30' sold near the Museu da Inconfidência — no such thing exists; Brazilian domestic-tourist passports aren't stamped for UNESCO sites.

For travelers: (1) for guided walking tours, book via the Ouro Preto tourism office at Praça Tiradentes 41 (official Centro de Informações Turísticas, open 9 AM–5 PM daily) at R$150–R$250 for 3–4 hours — REFUSE every unlicensed 'guide' approach on the square; (2) the 5 main churches have INDIVIDUAL R$20 tickets paid at each church door — no 'combined ticket' exists; budget R$100 for 5 churches over 1–2 days; (3) REFUSE every 'skip-the-line' / 'IPHAN tax' / 'after-hours photography' / 'passport stamp' offer — these are all fraudulent; (4) the Museu da Inconfidência (Praça Tiradentes, R$15) is a mandatory visit for understanding the 1789 Inconfidência Mineira rebellion — it includes Tiradentes's remains + period artefacts; buy ticket at the museum, not from street touts; (5) for Mina da Passagem (the historic gold mine 2 km from Centro), take a short Uber R$15–R$25 each way + pay R$50 entry at the mine — REFUSE every Centro-street 'combined package R$300' offer; (6) for Aleijadinho sculpture (Antônio Francisco Lisboa, Brazil's greatest baroque artist), São Francisco de Assis church is the masterpiece — spend a full 45 min inside studying the façade + altarpiece + Stations of the Cross; (7) REFUSE every 'local artesão' trinket-in-hand approach on Praça Tiradentes — walk away without paying; (8) Ouro Preto Centro Histórico is cobblestone + STEEP HILLS (gradient 15–25% on some streets) — for travelers with mobility concerns, use the 'Ouro Preto Trenzinho' tourist mini-tram R$45 per person (30-min hop-on circuit) or Uber between sites rather than walking all day; (9) Prefeitura de Ouro Preto Secretaria de Turismo (www.ouropreto.mg.gov.br/turismo) for verified guide + operator info; DEATUR Minas Gerais +55 31 2108 6300.

Scam #3
Soapstone & Gemstone ('Pedra-Sabão' & Emerald) Tourist-Shop Overcharge + Counterfeit Risk
🔶 Medium
📍 Centro Histórico souvenir shops, Feira de Artesanato Largo do Rosário, Mina da Passagem gift shop

Ouro Preto's signature souvenir is carved pedra-sabão (soapstone) crafted by local artisans in the Minas Gerais tradition.

And Minas Gerais is a significant source of Brazilian emerald + topaz + aquamarine. Both categories host a 2025 tourist-shop overcharge + counterfeit pattern.

The 2025 scam patterns: (a) Centro Histórico souvenir shops pricing pedra-sabão figurines at R$80–R$250 for items that sell at the Feira de Artesanato Largo do Rosário for R$15–R$60 direct from artisans; (b) 'IGS certificate Brazilian emerald R$2,500' — tourist-shop loose stones with fake certificates; legitimate IBGM (Instituto Brasileiro de Gemas e Metais Nobres) certificates are issued only by laboratory-tested stones, NOT by shop-issued paperwork; (c) 'topaz-aquamarine set R$3,500' — synthetic stones dyed blue; real Brazilian topaz is less vivid than the Swiss-blue heat-treated version; (d) 'artisanal jewelry R$1,200' with silver-plated (not sterling) settings — legitimate 925 sterling silver stamped '925' on the back; (e) Mina da Passagem gift-shop 'mine-recovered gold pendant R$900' — historic mine production ceased in 1954; no actual 'mine-recovered' gold is for sale; all gold sold is modern; (f) 'exclusive artisan studio tour R$300' — most Centro studios are open free to visitors during work hours (10 AM–4 PM); (g) shipping scam — 'we'll ship to your country for R$200' + item never arrives; use ONLY Correios with tracking + insurance declared value; (h) 'custom-order Aleijadinho replica R$2,000' — deposit demanded via PIX (IRREVERSIBLE), replica never arrives.

For travelers: (1) BUY pedra-sabão DIRECT at the Feira de Artesanato Largo do Rosário (Sat-Sun 9 AM–5 PM, also some weekday stalls) at R$15–R$60 for typical figurines — artisans carve on-site, genuine Minas-tradition work; REFUSE Centro Histórico souvenir shops charging R$80–R$250 for identical pieces; (2) for emerald / topaz / aquamarine jewelry, BUY ONLY at IBGM-certified jewellers with a visible IBGM badge (Brazilian Gem and Precious Metals Institute) — reputable Ouro Preto: Instituto Federal Museu de Mineralogia (educational gem info + small sales) at Escola de Minas, Matriz Alves; REFUSE every loose-stone sale without laboratory IGI or GIA certificate (not 'shop-issued'); (3) REFUSE every 'Mina da Passagem mine-recovered gold' sales pitch — production ceased 1954; (4) for sterling silver, check the '925' stamp on the back of any piece BEFORE paying; (5) AVOID every 'custom-order Aleijadinho replica' PIX-deposit request — never PIX-transfer for an undelivered item (IRREVERSIBLE); (6) for shipping, use Correios (Brazilian postal) with tracking + insurance — declare true value + keep receipts; (7) for travelers, the authentic Ouro Preto souvenir experience is the Sat/Sun Feira de Artesanato at Largo do Rosário — buy a R$30–R$60 soapstone figurine from an artisan you watched carve; the provenance is clear, the price is fair, the piece is a genuine heritage craft; (8) REFUSE every 'exclusive artisan studio tour R$300' — most studios welcome visitors free during work hours (ask in Portuguese 'posso ver o senhor trabalhar?'); (9) PROCON Minas Gerais 151 for purchase disputes; DEATUR Minas Gerais +55 31 2108 6300.

Red Flags

  • Centro Histórico shop R$80–R$250 for pedra-sabão (feira artisans R$15–R$60 same piece)

  • 'Mina da Passagem mine-recovered gold' sales pitch (mine ceased 1954, no such gold)
  • Custom-order Aleijadinho replica demanding PIX-transfer deposit (IRREVERSIBLE)
  • Silver jewelry without '925' stamp on back (legitimate sterling is stamped)

How to Avoid

  • Pedra-sabão DIRECT at Feira de Artesanato Largo do Rosário Sat-Sun R$15–R$60.
  • Jewelry ONLY at IBGM-certified jewellers; demand IBGM / GIA / IGI laboratory certificate.
  • NEVER PIX-transfer for custom / shipped items (IRREVERSIBLE); use Correios tracking + insurance.

  • PROCON Minas Gerais 151; DEATUR Minas Gerais +55 31 2108 6300.

Ouro Preto Carnaval Repúblicas Booking Fraud & Peak-Date Accommodation Scams
⚠️ High
📍 Ouro Preto Repúblicas estudantis, Airbnb / Instagram / WhatsApp 'aluguel temporada' sellers

Ouro Preto hosts one of Brazil's most famous traditional Carnavals.

Organized around the UFOP (Universidade Federal de Ouro Preto) 'repúblicas estudantis' (student houses, each with a name + history, equivalent to university residential societies) hosting massive 5-night block parties. The event draws 80,000+ visitors to a UNESCO town of 75,000 residents and creates extreme accommodation pressure + predictable booking fraud.

The 2025–2026 scam patterns: (a) 'República open to guests R$500 per night' — some repúblicas do host paying guests during Carnaval, but the legitimate ones require introduction via UFOP student contacts; random Instagram / WhatsApp 'república sublet R$500' offers are frequently fraudulent PIX-deposit scams; (b) Airbnb cancel-and-relist — host accepts your Ouro Preto Carnaval booking 9 months out at R$450/night, cancels 30 days before dates citing 'pousada emergency' — re-lists same unit at R$1,400/night; Airbnb refunds the original payment but you're stuck 30 days out; (c) Instagram / WhatsApp 'aluguel temporada Ouro Preto Carnaval' sellers demanding PIX-transfer deposit — Brazilian PIX is IRREVERSIBLE, listing disappears; (d) fake 'reptúblicas estudantis official booking agency' websites (URL spoofing real UFOP sites) — PIX deposit, no room; (e) hotel overbooking for Carnaval — Ouro Preto's limited hotel stock (Pousada Solar das Lajes, Pousada do Mondego, Grande Hotel de Ouro Preto) oversells the peak week then 'relocates' paying guests to Mariana (15 km away) on arrival night; (f) counterfeit 'bloco' / 'república' party wristbands sold on Facebook Marketplace at 30–60% discount off face value — wristbands scan 'invalid' at the republica door; legitimate pricing R$80–R$200 per night per republica depending on bloco; (g) 'VIP camarote private view' offers for the Praça Tiradentes parade (Tue-Thu nights) — the parade is 100% FREE public viewing; no VIP camarote legitimately exists; (h) 'Carnaval transfer shuttle BH-Ouro Preto R$400 per person each way' WhatsApp offers — legitimate shuttle R$180–R$280 pre-booked.

For travelers: (1) AVOID Ouro Preto during Carnaval (typically Feb–Mar, 5 nights) UNLESS you specifically want the university-town bloco experience — the town is overwhelmed, accommodations 3–5x normal, noise until 6 AM; for travelers wanting Minas Carnaval, BELO HORIZONTE (street blocos + large-venue events) or São João del-Rei (smaller, quainter) are better choices; (2) if you DO go, BOOK accommodation 9–12 months ahead via RECOGNISED hotels ONLY — reputable Ouro Preto: Pousada Solar das Lajes, Pousada do Mondego, Grande Hotel de Ouro Preto, Pousada Jaime Alcover, Solar do Rosário; AVOID Airbnb for Carnaval dates entirely; (3) AVOID every Instagram / WhatsApp 'república sublet' offer — legitimate repúblicas require UFOP student introductions, NOT random online bookings; (4) NEVER PIX-transfer to Instagram / WhatsApp 'aluguel' sellers — IRREVERSIBLE; only pay via credit card on Booking.com / Expedia / hotels.com (chargeback protection); (5) for blocos / parade tickets, buy ONLY at the official UFOP-sanctioned outlets on Praça Tiradentes or via Sympla (sympla.com.br) — REFUSE Facebook / WhatsApp discount resellers; (6) the Praça Tiradentes parade (Tue-Thu nights during Carnaval) is 100% FREE public viewing — REFUSE every 'VIP camarote R$500' offer; (7) for BH-Ouro Preto Carnaval transfer, pre-book via Ouropreto Turismo at R$180–R$280 per person (1 month ahead, limited seats during Carnaval) — REFUSE WhatsApp shuttle offers above R$300; (8) confirm your hotel booking 72 hours before arrival via direct phone call (NOT email); demand written overbooking-relocation commitment; (9) PROCON Minas Gerais 151 for booking fraud; DEATUR Minas Gerais +55 31 2108 6300.

🟢 Low
📍 Centro Histórico restaurants, Praça Tiradentes, Largo do Rosário dining

Ouro Preto's dining scene centers on the 'comida mineira' tradition (feijão tropeiro, frango com.

Quiabo, tutu à mineira, pão de queijo) served either à la carte or via the common 'comida por kg' (pay-by-weight) lunch buffet. 2025 couvert + serviço + weighing padding is systematic at Centro Histórico tourist-zone restaurants.

Brazilian restaurant law (Lei 13.419/2017) makes the 10% serviço charge OPTIONAL. INMETRO regulates commercial scales — 'comida por kg' buffets MUST display an INMETRO-certified scale with certification sticker visible. The 2025 scam patterns: (a) Centro Histórico tourist-zone 'comida mineira kg buffet' R$110–R$160 per kg (regional real R$70–R$100 per kg); (b) scale uncertified by INMETRO padding weight by 10–25% — ALWAYS check the Inmetro Calibração 2025/2026 sticker BEFORE plating; (c) 'bebida não incluso' (drinks not included) with R$15 bottled water or R$25 guaraná added separately to the plate-weight bill; (d) couvert artístico R$30–R$80 per person at upscale Centro Histórico restaurants (legitimate R$15–R$30 for a genuine Minas MPB / sertanejo musician); (e) 'serviço 10%' added silently + menu shows 'serviço opcional' fine print; (f) credit-card surcharge 5–10% at bill-time ('we prefer PIX'); (g) 'pão de queijo de cortesia' — then billed R$8 each at R$40 for a basket ('cortesia' is deceptive wording); (h) 'cachaça mineira premium R$35 shot' at rodízios (legitimate local cachaça R$10–R$20); (i) 'churrasquinho na calçada' street-food stands at Praça Tiradentes charging R$25 per skewer (legitimate R$10–R$15 at the locals' Feira de Alimentos off Rua São José).

For travelers: (1) at 'comida mineira kg buffet', VERIFY the scale has a visible INMETRO certification sticker (Inmetro Calibração 2025/2026) — if missing, walk out; (2) BEFORE plating, check posted R$ per kg (legitimate 2026 Ouro Preto lunch R$70–R$100 per kg); reputable buffets: Chafariz (Largo do Rosário, Google 4.5+, R$85/kg), O Passo Pizza Jazz (Rua São José, R$95/kg), Bené da Flauta (Centro, R$90/kg); (3) drinks are SEPARATE from the plate-weight — ask 'bebida está separada?' BEFORE you fill water / guaraná; (4) BEFORE sitting at à la carte restaurants, confirm couvert + serviço + card surcharge IN WRITING; photograph menu; (5) REFUSE 'pão de queijo cortesia' that arrives billed — demand the 'cortesia' wording be honored or decline the basket; (6) reputable à la carte comida mineira: Chafariz (regional fine dining, R$100–R$200 per meal), Restaurante Alpina (mountain-view, R$90–R$160), Restaurante Beco (Centro, R$70–R$130), Restaurante Le Coq d'Or (French-Mineiro fusion, R$120–R$220); (7) for cachaça, reputable local artisanal producers (Alambique da Cachaça da Serra, Maria Izabel, Anísio Santiago) — buy at the distillery R$30–R$80 per 500ml, NOT at Centro tourist-shop R$150+; (8) if serviço is added against your wishes for poor service, politely REFUSE and cite Lei 13.419/2017; (9) PROCON Minas Gerais 151 for billing disputes; DEATUR Minas Gerais +55 31 2108 6300.

🟢 Low
📍 Ouro Preto hotel lobbies, Centro tour-desks, Mariana / Congonhas / Tiradentes day-trip routes

Ouro Preto is the anchor of Minas Gerais's 'Estrada Real' colonial-gold-circuit.

A cluster of UNESCO + historic baroque towns that 2025 hotel concierges + Centro tour-desks package into commission-heavy day-trip bundles. 🆘 What to Do If You Get Scammed

📋 File a Police Report

Go to the nearest Civil Police (Polícia Civil) station. Call 190 (emergency) or 197 (civil police). Get an official crime report — you'll need this for insurance claims. You can also report online at delegaciaonline.rj.gov.br.

💳 Cancel Your Cards

Call your bank immediately. Most have 24/7 numbers on the back of the card (keep a photo saved separately). Block any suspicious transactions before the thieves use your details.

🛂 Lost Passport?

Contact your nearest embassy or consulate. The US Consulate General is at Av. Presidente Wilson, 147, Centro, Rio de Janeiro. For emergencies: +55 21 3823-2000.

📱 Track Your Device

If your phone was stolen, use Find My (iPhone) or Find My Device (Android) from another device. Don't confront thieves yourself — share the location with police instead.
