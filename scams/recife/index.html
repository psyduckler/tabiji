Scam #1
Recife (REC) Airport Taxi & Boa Viagem Transfer Overcharge
🔶 Medium
📍 Recife Guararapes International Airport (REC) arrivals halls, 'Special Taxi' / Coopataxi kiosks, Boa Viagem / Recife Antigo / Olinda drop-offs

Recife Guararapes Airport (REC, 11 km from Boa Viagem) is North-East Brazil's main coastal arrival.

Point and hosts a 2025 taxi-overcharge ecosystem common to Brazilian airports.

The 2025 scams: (a) 'Special Taxi' / Coopataxi kiosks inside REC arrivals quote R$100–R$200 to Boa Viagem (real Uber R$30–R$60); (b) sign-holders posing as 'pousada transfer' agents intercepting tourists; (c) Uber driver cancel-and-recontract scam — same template as Salvador / Rio; (d) 'meter broken' yellow taxi demands at hotel arrival with R$150+ flat-rate cash demand; (e) REC-to-Olinda 'connecting transfer R$250' sold by touts (real Uber R$60–R$90 for the 12-km cross-city ride); (f) 'overnight surcharge R$50' added unannounced after late-night arrivals (no such legitimate surcharge); (g) hotel-concierge 'recommended driver R$200' kickback markup; (h) credit-card cloning at unofficial taxi-cooperative counters via 'manual entry' card-machines.

For travelers: (1) book Uber, 99, or InDriver on airport Wi-Fi AFTER luggage — typical REC to Boa Viagem R$30–R$60 (15 min); to Recife Antigo (Centro) R$40–R$70 (20 min); to Olinda R$60–R$90 (25 min); meet driver at the designated 'Aplicativos' (apps) pickup zone signposted at terminal exit; (2) IGNORE every 'Special Taxi' kiosk inside terminal quoting above R$100 to Boa Viagem; (3) the Recife Metro Centro line connects REC to Centro for R$4.30 (35 min, runs 5 AM–11 PM) — budget alternative; (4) NEVER hand a credit card to any taxi driver — pay in-app or cash only; (5) for late-night arrivals (post-22:00), Uber Comfort or 99 Top is worth the R$10–R$20 surcharge; (6) for Olinda, use Uber direct (not REC airport-arranged transfer); (7) save DEATUR Recife +55 81 3322 4830 (English available, Praça do Marco Zero, Recife Antigo); US Consulate Recife +55 81 3416 3050 (covers all of NE Brazil).

Red Flags

  • 'Special Taxi' / Coopataxi kiosk inside REC quoting R$100–R$200 to Boa Viagem
  • Sign-holder posing as 'pousada transfer' for hotel without shuttle
  • Uber driver canceling at curb and re-quoting R$150+ cash
  • Yellow taxi 'meter broken' with R$150+ flat-rate demand at hotel
  • Hotel-concierge 'recommended driver R$200' kickback (real Uber R$30–R$60)

How to Avoid

  • Book Uber/99/InDriver on airport Wi-Fi: REC-Boa Viagem R$30–R$60.
  • Olinda via Uber R$60–R$90 (NOT REC airport arrangement).
  • OR Recife Metro Centro line R$4.30 (35 min, 5 AM–11 PM).
  • NEVER hand credit card to any taxi driver.
  • DEATUR Recife +55 81 3322 4830; US Consulate Recife +55 81 3416 3050.
Scam #2
Boa Viagem Beach Shark-Attack Risk & 'Safe Swimming Zone' Misinformation Scam
⚠️ High
📍 Praia de Boa Viagem (full 7-km strip, Recife south zone), Praia do Pina, Praia da Piedade, hotel-zone beachfront, beach kiosk (quiosque) zones

Praia de Boa Viagem.

Recife's main urban beach — sits on one of the world's most documented shark-attack coastlines, and the broader Pernambuco metro region (Boa Viagem, Piedade, Paulista / Olinda) has logged repeated fatal incidents through 2026. This is NOT a financial scam but a critical safety issue tourists are often misinformed about by hotel staff and beach vendors. — 13-year-old Deivson Rocha Dantas was killed at Praia Del Chifre (Olinda / Paulista side, ~20 km north of Boa Viagem) on 29 Jan 2026. Community reports document the swim-safety landscape.

The 2025–2026 reality: (a) Boa Viagem has recorded 60+ documented shark attacks since 1992 with 25+ fatalities — one of the world's highest attack rates per beach, per CEMIT (Comitê Estadual de Monitoramento de Incidentes com Tubarões) data published by the Pernambuco state government; (b) the cause is a combination of environmental factors (Suape Port construction in the 1990s disrupted bull-shark migratory routes along the reef channel) and an active bull-shark / tiger-shark population in the offshore reef system; (c) the 2026 Praia Del Chifre fatality shows that the RISK ZONE extends north of Boa Viagem into Olinda and Paulista — not just the urban-Recife strip; (d) red-and-yellow warning signs are POSTED at all Boa Viagem beach access points (in Portuguese AND English) — they are not decoration, they identify the actual danger zone past the reef line; (e) some hotel concierges and beach kiosk vendors DOWNPLAY the risk to retain tourists ('the signs are old', 'attacks haven't happened recently') — this is misinformation; (f) 'safe swimming zones' marked on Boa Viagem are the natural tide pools INSIDE the reef line at low tide ONLY — NOT past the reef; (g) 'beach lifeguard says it's safe today' is unreliable — risk varies by time of day, tide, and water conditions, and lifeguards may not have current shark-spotting data.

For travelers — and any traveler considering swimming in metropolitan Recife: (1) DO NOT swim at Boa Viagem past the reef line (0–50m from shore is the natural tide-pool zone; past the reef is shark territory); (2) for actual ocean swimming, travel south to PRAIA DE PIEDADE (immediately south of Boa Viagem — still within the CEMIT risk zone, swim only 0–50m from shore with lifeguards present), PRAIA DO PAIVA (30 min south, gated-community beach, outside the shark-attack hot-zone per 2025 CEMIT data), or best of all PORTO DE GALINHAS (1-hour bus / Uber south, natural reef tide pools, zero shark attacks on record — Brazil's gold-standard safe beach); (3) AVOID Paulista / Praia Del Chifre / Olinda-side beaches for swimming after the Jan 2026 Del Chifre fatality — sightseeing only; (4) ALWAYS swim between the red-and-yellow lifeguard flags; NEVER outside them regardless of what a vendor or concierge tells you; (5) lifeguard hours on Boa Viagem are 8 AM–5 PM — Corpo de Bombeiros 193 for any emergency; (6) DISREGARD 'the signs are old' or 'no attacks since…' statements from hotel staff, kiosk vendors, or tour operators — the 2026 Del Chifre fatality documents the risk is ACTIVE; (7) for travelers, the best day-trip is Porto de Galinhas — book an Uber round-trip (R$360–R$450) or public Ônibus R$22 from TIP terminal; reputable beach operators: Jangada Tours Porto de Galinhas, Maragogi Tours; (8) if you DO swim at Boa Viagem, go ONLY between 10 AM–4 PM when lifeguards are fully staffed and the tide pools are most visible; (9) DEATUR Recife +55 81 3184 3450; SAMU 192; Polícia Militar 190.

Red Flags

  • Hotel concierge / beach vendor saying 'shark warning signs are old, you're safe'
  • 'Lifeguard said it's safe today' — lifeguards don't have real-time shark-spotting data
  • Tourists swimming past the reef line (the actual shark-attack zone — inside reef = tide pools, outside = danger)
  • Red flag posted at beach access being ignored by other swimmers (still means NO ENTRY for you)
  • Beach-vendor promoting 'Boa Viagem swim tour / lesson' that ventures beyond the reef

How to Avoid

  • DO NOT swim at Boa Viagem past the reef line (60+ attacks, 25+ fatalities since 1992 per CEMIT data).
  • Boa Viagem safe for sunbathing, walking, low-tide natural-pool wading inside the reef ONLY.
  • For real swimming: Uber / Ônibus to Porto de Galinhas (1h south, zero shark history, natural reef pools).
  • Red-and-yellow lifeguard flag = swim zone; RED FLAG = NO ENTRY; flags current-day and authoritative.
  • Emergency: Corpo de Bombeiros 193; SAMU 192; DEATUR Recife +55 81 3184 3450.
Scam #3
Recife Antigo / Marco Zero Pickpocket & Phone-Snatch Ring
⚠️ High
📍 Recife Antigo (old town colonial center), Marco Zero plaza, Rua do Bom Jesus (synagogue street), Cais do Sertão / Paço Alfândega, Bairro do Recife waterfront, Casa da Cultura Centro

Recife's Centro Histórico (Recife Antigo + Marco Zero + Casa da Cultura) hosts the city's highest-density tourist-pickpocket activity.

cover the broader 2025 safety landscape.

The 2025 scams: (a) Marco Zero plaza organized pickpocket teams targeting visitors photographing the iconic compass mosaic + Kahal Zur Israel synagogue (oldest in Americas) — distraction-theft templates similar to São Paulo 'clowns'; (b) Casa da Cultura (former 19th-century prison turned artisan market) interior — distraction-team brushing past in narrow stall aisles; (c) Cais do Sertão museum approach — phone-snatch from outdoor café tables; (d) Rua do Bom Jesus narrow cobblestone funnel — pickpocket-team setups at Sunday-market crowds; (e) 'helpful local' offering directions while watching your bag; (f) variant: 'your shoelace is untied' distraction at Marco Zero crowds; (g) variant: 'dropped wallet' team where one 'finds' a wallet, asks tourist 'is it yours?' as cover for pocket-pick by partner; (h) night-time Recife Antigo is significantly less safe — most documented theft happens post-19:00 in the colonial-zone narrow streets.

For travelers: (1) Recife Antigo / Marco Zero is WORTH visiting for the colonial architecture (Marco Zero compass mosaic, Kahal Zur Israel synagogue, Cais do Sertão museum, Casa da Cultura, Paço Alfândega shopping center) — but go ONLY DURING DAYTIME (9 AM–5 PM) on weekdays; weekends are louder + more pickpocket cover; (2) bring NOTHING you can't afford to lose — leave passport, jewelry, valuable watch in hotel safe; (3) wear a money belt OR crossbody bag with zipper; no back-pocket wallets; (4) keep phone in zipped front pocket between uses; NEVER on outdoor café table; (5) for photographs at Marco Zero / Kahal Zur Israel, use phone-timer or ask SECURITY GUARDS / hotel staff; (6) take Uber/99 BETWEEN sights and your hotel — do NOT walk to / from Recife Antigo at night; (7) AVOID Recife Antigo after dark (post-19:00) unless going directly to a specific reserved-table restaurant via Uber drop-off; (8) for Sunday markets at Marco Zero (10 AM–5 PM), go in the morning when crowds are sparser; (9) DEATUR Recife +55 81 3322 4830 (Praça do Marco Zero, in Recife Antigo); file Boletim de Ocorrência (BO) within 24 hours for insurance + chargebacks.

Red Flags

  • Team approaching while you photograph the Marco Zero compass mosaic
  • 'Your shoelace is untied' distraction at Marco Zero crowds
  • 'Dropped wallet, is this yours?' approach (cover for pocket-pick)
  • Phone on outdoor café table at Cais do Sertão
  • Walking Recife Antigo after dark (post-19:00)

How to Avoid

  • Recife Antigo daytime only (9 AM–5 PM weekdays preferred).
  • Leave valuables in hotel safe; money belt + crossbody bag with zipper.
  • Phone in zipped front pocket; never on outdoor café table.
  • Uber/99 BETWEEN sights and hotel; avoid post-19:00 walking.
  • Sunday market: go 10–12 AM when crowds sparser; DEATUR +55 81 3322 4830.

Olinda.

UNESCO-listed colonial old town on a hill 6 km north of Recife — is Recife's flagship day-trip and hosts a 2025 'mandatory local guide' commission scam plus tour-operator commission stops.

The 2025 scam patterns: (a) Alto da Sé (Olinda's iconic hilltop viewpoint with 360° Recife panorama) — 'mandatory local guide R$100' demanded by self-appointed Pelourinho-style touts at the viewpoint plaza; no guide is required — Olinda is a free public UNESCO town walkable in 2–3 hours; (b) hotel-concierge 'Olinda + Recife Antigo combined day-trip R$350 per person' kickback markup (real DIY: R$60–R$90 Uber round-trip + free walking = R$100 per couple); (c) 'private heritage tour with English-speaking guide R$250 per person' (Free Walker Tour Olinda offers donation-based tours at R$30–R$50 voluntary per person); (d) commission-restaurant lunch stops at Recife-side tour-operator routes — 'Mariscada Pernambucana lunch R$200 per person' at commission-kickback restaurants vs R$80–R$130 at authentic Olinda spots; (e) 'commission-shop atelier visit R$100 mandatory' at Olinda artisan workshops on tour routes; (f) 'photo with frevo dancer R$80' at Alto da Sé (legitimate frevo street performances are tip-based at R$5–R$20 voluntary); (g) Olinda Carnaval (one of Brazil's most authentic) attracts fake-ticket resellers — there are NO tickets for Olinda street Carnaval; it's free public access to all blocos.

Scam #5
Recife Boa Viagem Restaurant Couvert + Serviço Padding & 'Frutos do Mar Market Price' Trap
🔶 Medium
📍 Boa Viagem beachfront restaurants (Avenida Boa Viagem, Avenida Conselheiro Aguiar), Recife Antigo Marco Zero restaurants, Casa da Cultura Centro tourist-menu spots, Pina waterfront dining strip

Recife's Boa Viagem beachfront and Recife Antigo dining hubs host a 2025 couvert artístico.

Serviço padding ecosystem common to Brazilian coastal cities, plus a specific 'frutos do mar peso variável' (variable-weight seafood platter) overcharge pattern.

The 2025 scam patterns: (a) Boa Viagem beachfront restaurants with 'couvert artístico R$25–R$60 per person' for live music that's a single guitarist for 30 min — legitimate is R$10–R$25 disclosed BEFORE seating; (b) 'serviço 13–15%' instead of standard 10% (Brazilian Lei 13.419/2017 makes serviço OPTIONAL — you can refuse); (c) 'taxa de couvert + serviço' double-charging (illegal per PROCON PE); (d) 'frutos do mar para 2' (seafood platter for 2) market-price quoted at R$350–R$600 without weight or species disclosed (real R$150–R$280 at authentic spots with same items); (e) 'lagosta peso variável' market-price lobster at R$800–R$1,400 per kg without weight disclosed; (f) 'agua mineral R$18' (real R$5–R$10); (g) 'caipirinha de cachaça artesanal R$45' from house cachaça (real R$18–R$30); (h) Recife Antigo USD-only menus at 2–3× local prices for Pernambucano dishes; (i) bill-switcheroo where R$ amount on receipt differs from card by R$50–R$200; (j) Marco Zero Sunday-market food stalls overcharging tourists 1.5–2× weekday rates.

For travelers: (1) eat at AUTHENTIC Pernambucano spots: Restaurante Oficina do Sabor (Olinda, Pernambuco classic, Google 4.5+, R$70–R$150 per meal), Beijupirá (Boa Viagem, contemporary Northeast cuisine, R$100–R$250), Veggie Lab (Boa Viagem, vegetarian-friendly Pernambucano, R$60–R$130), Restaurante Leite (Recife Antigo, traditional Brazilian since 1882, R$80–R$180), Pernambucanas Casa da Tia Anastácia (Boa Viagem, working-class authentic, R$50–R$100); (2) BEFORE sitting, ASK for menu and CONFIRM (a) couvert artístico R$ amount per person (legitimate R$10–R$25), (b) serviço % policy (10% standard, OPTIONAL by Brazilian law), (c) credit-card surcharge — photograph the menu; (3) for FRUTOS DO MAR PARA 2 (seafood platter for 2 — Pernambuco's signature dish), agree the EXACT items + weight + price IN WRITING BEFORE cooking — fair platter R$150–R$280 with shrimp + mussels + clams + fish; refuse 'market price R$350–R$600' without itemisation; (4) for lobster, agree weight × R$400–R$700 per kg in writing; (5) 'serviço' is OPTIONAL by Brazilian Lei 13.419/2017 — tip 10% if happy, refuse if poor service or padded; (6) for water, order 'água sem gás copo' (free tap water) at most spots; (7) for caipirinha, expect R$18–R$30 at reputable spots — anything above R$50 is tourist-trap; (8) PROCON PE can be contacted via 151 or procon.pe.gov.br for any abusive bill.

Red Flags

  • Boa Viagem 'couvert artístico R$25–R$60/person' for one guitarist
  • 'Serviço 13–15%' instead of standard 10% (often padded)
  • 'Frutos do mar para 2' market-price R$350–R$600 without itemisation
  • Lobster 'peso variável' verbal quote without weight × price/kg
  • 'Agua mineral R$18' (real R$5–R$10)

How to Avoid

  • Eat: Oficina do Sabor (Olinda), Beijupirá, Veggie Lab, Restaurante Leite, Tia Anastácia.
  • BEFORE sitting confirm couvert + serviço + card surcharge in writing.
  • Frutos do Mar para 2: agree items + weight + price IN WRITING (R$150–R$280 fair).
  • Lobster: agree weight × R$400–R$700/kg in writing.
  • PROCON PE: 151 or procon.pe.gov.br for abusive bills.

🆘 What to Do If You Get Scammed

📋 File a Police Report

Go to the nearest Civil Police (Polícia Civil) station. Call 190 (emergency) or 197 (civil police). Get an official crime report — you'll need this for insurance claims. You can also report online at delegaciaonline.rj.gov.br.

💳 Cancel Your Cards

Call your bank immediately. Most have 24/7 numbers on the back of the card (keep a photo saved separately). Block any suspicious transactions before the thieves use your details.

🛂 Lost Passport?

Contact your nearest embassy or consulate. The US Consulate General is at Av. Presidente Wilson, 147, Centro, Rio de Janeiro. For emergencies: +55 21 3823-2000.

📱 Track Your Device

If your phone was stolen, use Find My (iPhone) or Find My Device (Android) from another device. Don't confront thieves yourself — share the location with police instead.
