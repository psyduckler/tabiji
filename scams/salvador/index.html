Scam #1
Salvador (SSA) Airport Taxi Mafia & Late-Night Uber Confusion
⚠️ High
📍 Salvador Bahia Airport (SSA) arrivals halls (Terminals 1 & 2), 'Special Taxi' kiosks, Cocobambu / Coopataxi counters, Barra / Pelourinho / Itapuã drop-offs

Salvador's Bahia Airport (SSA, 28 km from Pelourinho center) has South-East Brazil's most-documented 2025 late-night taxi-overcharge ecosystem.

Community reports document the broader 2025 pattern.

The 2025 scams: (a) 'Special Taxi' / Coopataxi kiosks inside SSA arrivals quote R$ 200–R$ 400 to Barra / Pelourinho / Rio Vermelho (real Uber R$ 70–R$ 130); (b) sign-holders posing as 'pousada transfer' agents intercepting tourists; (c) Uber driver cancel-and-recontract scam — SSA has documented thinner Uber driver supply post-22:00, and unauthorized drivers exploit by approaching canceled-ride passengers with cash quotes; (d) 'meter broken' yellow taxi demands at hotel arrival with R$ 250+ flat-rate cash demand; (e) 'overnight surcharge R$ 80' added unannounced after late-night arrivals (no such legitimate surcharge); (f) credit-card cloning at unofficial taxi-cooperative counters via 'manual entry' card-machines; (g) Carnaval-period (Feb–Mar) prices spike 3–5×.

For travelers: (1) book Uber, 99, or InDriver on airport Wi-Fi AFTER luggage — typical SSA to Pelourinho R$ 80–R$ 130, to Barra R$ 70–R$ 110, to Rio Vermelho R$ 70–R$ 120, 35–60 min depending on traffic; meet driver at the designated 'Aplicativos' (apps) pickup zone signposted at terminal exit; (2) for late-night arrivals (post-22:00), Uber Comfort or 99 Top is worth the R$ 15–R$ 30 surcharge — driver supply is thinner; allow 10–20 min wait time; (3) IGNORE every 'Special Taxi' kiosk inside terminal quoting above R$ 180; (4) pre-book your hotel airport pickup if the pousada offers it (many Salvador boutique pousadas include free transfer); (5) NEVER hand a credit card to any taxi driver — pay in-app or cash only; (6) for Carnaval period (Feb–Mar), budget 3–5× normal taxi rates — book Uber Black or pre-arrange hotel transfer; (7) get a Brazilian eSim (Vivo, Claro, TIM) for guaranteed connectivity to book Uber; (8) save DEATUR Salvador +55 71 3116 6817 (English available, Praça da Sé) and US Consulate-Recife (covers Bahia) +55 81 3416 3050.

Red Flags

  • 'Special Taxi' / Coopataxi kiosk inside SSA quoting R$ 200–R$ 400 to Pelourinho
  • Sign-holder posing as 'pousada transfer' for hotel without shuttle
  • Uber driver canceling at curb post-22:00 and re-quoting R$ 200+ cash
  • Yellow taxi 'meter broken' with R$ 250+ flat-rate demand at hotel
  • 'Overnight surcharge R$ 80' added after late-night arrival (fake)

How to Avoid

  • Book Uber/99/InDriver on airport Wi-Fi: SSA-Pelourinho R$ 80–R$ 130
  • Late-night (post-22:00): Uber Comfort/99 Top R$ 15–R$ 30 surcharge
  • IGNORE 'Special Taxi' kiosks quoting above R$ 180
  • NEVER hand credit card to any taxi driver
  • DEATUR Salvador +55 71 3116 6817 (English available)

Pelourinho 'Fita do Senhor do Bonfim' Ribbon-Tying Forced-Tip Scam
🔶 Medium
📍 Pelourinho cobblestone streets (Largo do Pelourinho, Terreiro de Jesus), Igreja do Senhor do Bonfim church area (Itapagipe peninsula), Mercado Modelo, walking-tour pickup zones

Salvador's iconic 'Fita do Senhor do Bonfim' (Bonfim ribbon, traditional Bahian Catholic-Candomblé.

Blessing ribbon) is sold for ~R$ 0.50–R$ 2 at official church locations and at every Pelourinho gift shop — but a documented 2025 street-vendor variant runs an aggressive forced-tip scam. cover broader Salvador safety...

The 2025 scam pattern: (a) friendly local approaches tourist on Pelourinho cobblestones offering 'free fita do Bonfim' — a colorful printed ribbon symbolising 'three wishes'; (b) ribbon is tied around the tourist's wrist with three knots (each representing a wish); (c) once tied, demand for R$ 20–R$ 100 'donation' (real fita is R$ 0.50–R$ 2 at the actual Bonfim church gift shop); (d) refusal escalates to verbal pressure / accusations / 'bad luck if you remove the blessing'; (e) variant: 'photo for charity / Pelourinho cultural fund R$ 50' demand after unsolicited photo with costumed Bahiana woman; (f) variant: 'capoeira roda donation R$ 50' demand after watching a 5-min capoeira street performance (real performances are tip-based at R$ 5–R$ 20 voluntary); (g) variant: 'street artist quick caricature R$ 30' that becomes R$ 150 'frame + delivery'; (h) targeted at obvious tourists (cameras, English-speaking, hotel-zone clothes).

Red Flags

  • Friendly local offering 'free fita do Senhor do Bonfim' on Pelourinho
  • Ribbon tied around your wrist with three knots before you agree on price
  • 'Donation R$ 20–R$ 100' demand after the ribbon is tied
  • 'Photo with costumed Bahiana R$ 50' demanded after unsolicited photo
  • 'Capoeira roda donation R$ 50' demand after watching street performance

How to Avoid

  • Buy fita at OFFICIAL Igreja do Senhor do Bonfim gift shop R$ 0.50–R$ 2
  • Firm 'não obrigado' to every Pelourinho 'free' offer

  • Bahiana photos: agree R$ 5–R$ 10 BEFORE photo

🆘 What to Do If You Get Scammed

📋 File a Police Report

Go to the nearest Civil Police (Polícia Civil) station. Call 190 (emergency) or 197 (civil police). Get an official crime report — you'll need this for insurance claims. You can also report online at delegaciaonline.rj.gov.br.

💳 Cancel Your Cards

Call your bank immediately. Most have 24/7 numbers on the back of the card (keep a photo saved separately). Block any suspicious transactions before the thieves use your details.

🛂 Lost Passport?

Contact your nearest embassy or consulate. The US Consulate General is at Av. Presidente Wilson, 147, Centro, Rio de Janeiro. For emergencies: +55 21 3823-2000.

📱 Track Your Device

If your phone was stolen, use Find My (iPhone) or Find My Device (Android) from another device. Don't confront thieves yourself — share the location with police instead.
