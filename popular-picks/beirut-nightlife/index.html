12 Best Nightlife Spots in Beirut

Beirut's nightlife is an exhilarating blend of high-energy clubs, chic rooftop lounges, underground bunkers, and live music venues — renowned across the Middle East for its vibrant and diverse after-dark scene. Interactive map included.

Quick answer

Beirut is the undisputed nightlife capital of the Middle East — a city where the party does not stop until the sun comes up, and sometimes not even then.

From converted warehouses in Karantina pumping techno until dawn, to elegant rooftop lounges overlooking the Mediterranean, to intimate speakeasies hidden behind unmarked doors in Mar Mikhael — Beirut delivers a nightlife scene that rivals Berlin, Barcelona, and Bangkok. CNN has ranked it among the top three cities in the world for nightlife, and anyone who has experienced a Saturday night here understands why.

Frequently asked questions

What are the best neighborhoods for nightlife in Beirut?

The most vibrant nightlife districts in Beirut are Mar Mikhael and Gemmayze, known for their dense concentration of bars, pubs, and restaurants. Karantina is the hub for large-scale clubs like The Grand Factory and B018, while Downtown offers upscale rooftop lounges and sophisticated venues like Capitole and Iris.

What is the typical dress code for Beirut nightlife?

Beirut's nightlife ranges from smart casual to dressy. For upscale rooftop bars and exclusive clubs, smart attire is recommended -- collared shirts for men, stylish dresses for women. For casual pubs and most bars in Mar Mikhael, a relaxed but stylish outfit is usually fine. Avoid beachwear or overly casual clothing at clubs.

Is Beirut nightlife expensive for tourists?

Compared to major European or North American cities, Beirut's nightlife can be relatively affordable, especially for drinks at local pubs and bars in Mar Mikhael. However, upscale cocktail bars and major clubs with international DJs can have prices comparable to Western capitals. The fluctuating Lebanese Lira exchange rate can make prices variable, so check current rates.

What are the typical operating hours for clubs and bars in Beirut?

Bars typically open around 6 PM or 7 PM and get busy after 9 PM, often closing around 2 AM on weekdays and later on weekends. Clubs generally open around 10 PM or 11 PM, with peak hours from 1 AM to 4 AM, often staying open until well after sunrise on Fridays and Saturdays. There is no official closing time, and Beirut is famous for partying until dawn.

Is it safe to go out at night in Beirut?

Beirut is generally considered safe for nightlife. Common sense precautions apply: keep an eye on your belongings, avoid walking alone in unlit areas, and arrange reliable transportation via taxis or ride-sharing apps after a night out. Locals are usually very friendly and welcoming to tourists. The main nightlife districts -- Mar Mikhael, Gemmayze, and Downtown -- are busy and well-lit.

Do I need reservations for nightlife venues in Beirut?

For popular rooftop bars like Iris, dinner-show venues like MusicHall, and upscale lounges like Capitole, reservations are highly recommended -- especially on weekends. For most casual bars and pubs in Mar Mikhael, reservations are not necessary, but arriving earlier helps secure a good spot.

What is the best night of the week to go out in Beirut?

Thursday, Friday, and Saturday are the peak nights. The Grand Factory hosts its famous CU NXT SAT parties on Saturdays, while Fridays are big at AHM and B018. Thursday is popular for bar-hopping in Mar Mikhael. Some venues run midweek events too -- Wednesday at AHM features funk and soul nights that draw a solid crowd.

How do I get around Beirut at night?

Uber and Bolt work in Beirut and are the most convenient option for getting around at night. Regular taxis are also available, but agree on the fare before getting in. Driving yourself is common among locals, but finding parking near Mar Mikhael or Downtown on weekends can be challenging. Many bars in Mar Mikhael and Gemmayze are within walking distance of each other.

Planning your Beirut nightlife tour

The ideal Beirut night out unfolds across three distinct acts, each in a different neighborhood.

Start early evening (7–9 PM) with sunset cocktails at Iris on the Waterfront — the views and vibe set the tone perfectly. Then head to Mar Mikhael (9–11 PM) for a bar crawl: start at Internazionale for cheap drinks, hit Central Station for world-class cocktails, and stop at Vyvyan's for pizza and one last round. For the late-night crowd, move to Karantina (midnight+): The Grand Factory on Saturdays for electronic music, B018 for the iconic bunker experience, or AHM for the multi-room waterfront club.

For a cultural alternative, book MusicHall on a Friday or Saturday for the dinner-show experience, or catch a live performance at Metro Al Madina in Hamra. If you prefer a relaxed night, stick to Mar Mikhael — you can bar-hop the entire strip on foot.

Budget roughly $30–$60 for a full night out including drinks and transport. Mar Mikhael bars are the most affordable; clubs and rooftop lounges will run higher. Uber/Bolt is the safest way to get between neighborhoods at night.
