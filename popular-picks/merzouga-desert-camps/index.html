Quick answer

Desert camps in Merzouga, Morocco, range from budget-friendly guesthouses ($20/night) to luxury glamping experiences ($300/night), making it accessible for various budgets. Kam Kam Dunes is a top recommendation for travelers seeking a top-tier glamping experience in the deep Erg Chebbi dunes. For the best experience, plan your visit during the shoulder seasons (spring or fall) for pleasant temperatures.

Merzouga sits at the edge of Erg Chebbi — Morocco's most photogenic sand sea, where golden dunes rise 150 meters and the Milky Way blazes overhead with zero light pollution. Every traveler passing through wants the same thing: to sleep in the desert, ride a camel at sunset, and wake up to a Saharan sunrise.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best desert camp in Merzouga?

Based on local consensus Merzouga Luxury Desert Camp and Desert Luxury Camp are the most recommended options. Both offer well-maintained tents deep in the Erg Chebbi dunes with camel treks, Gnawa music, and clear night skies for stargazing.

How much does a desert camp in Merzouga cost?

Budget bivouac camps start around $20–$50 per night for basic Berber tent stays. Mid-range luxury camps cost $60–$150 per night, typically including camel trek, dinner, and breakfast. High-end glamping experiences run $150–$300+ per night with private tents, en-suite bathrooms, and premium amenities.

Should I book a desert tour from Marrakech or go directly to Merzouga?

Reddit overwhelmingly recommends going directly to Merzouga rather than booking a 3-day tour from Marrakech. The tours involve 8–10 hours of driving each way and you spend very little time in the actual desert. If you go directly (bus or private transfer), you can spend 2–3 nights and truly experience the Sahara.

When is the best time to visit Merzouga desert camps?

October to April is ideal — temperatures are comfortable (15–25°C during the day). Avoid June through August when daytime temperatures can exceed 45°C. March and October offer the sweet spot of warm days, cool nights, and clear skies for stargazing.

Are Merzouga desert camps safe?

Reputable camps with strong reviews on Google, TripAdvisor, and local consensus are generally very safe. Stick to well-reviewed camps and book directly when possible. Avoid random touts and always check recent reviews. Reddit travelers consistently report feeling safe at established camps.
