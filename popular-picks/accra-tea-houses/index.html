Top verdicts

  • Kwae Terrace: Stunning rooftop views with a bookstore-cafe hybrid concept.
  • Tea Baa: Accra's most dedicated tea-focused establishment with diverse blends.
  • Cuppa Cappuccino: 25+ years serving tea in a beautiful garden setting.

Accra's cafe and tea scene has evolved dramatically, offering everything from dedicated tea houses to stylish cafes with excellent tea programs. While traditional tea houses in the East Asian sense are rare, you'll find hibiscus-based Sobolo, ginger infusions, and international tea varieties served in beautiful settings.

The city's tea culture centers around upscale neighborhoods like Airport Residential Area, East Legon, Osu, and Labone. Many spots double as co-working spaces, making them popular with digital nomads and remote workers. Whether you're seeking a quiet afternoon escape, a productive work session, or a stylish brunch spot, Accra delivers.

We analyzed recommendations from local food blogs, Tripadvisor reviews, Time Out Accra, and the Accra food community to compile this guide. Each venue was verified for current hours, ratings, and tea selection as of April 2026.
