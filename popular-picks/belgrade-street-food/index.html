Belgrade sits at the crossroads of Ottoman, Austro-Hungarian, and Slavic culinary traditions — and nowhere is this more evident than in its street food. The Serbian capital's grill culture ( roštilj ) is a religion unto itself: smoky pljeskavica patties, perfectly charred ćevapi, and impossibly creamy kajmak. Add 24-hour bakeries, bustling farmers' markets, and artisan ice cream, and you have one of Europe's most underrated food cities. A full feast rarely costs more than $7.

Frequently asked questions

What is the most popular street food in Belgrade?

Pljeskavica is Belgrade's most iconic street food — a grilled meat patty made from pork, beef, and lamb, served in a lepinja with kajmak, ajvar, or urnebes. The Leskovac-style pljeskavica is legally protected and considered the gold standard.

Where can I find the best ćevapi in Belgrade?

Ćevap kod Dekija in Dorćol is the consensus top pick. Walter on Strahinjića Bana is the most popular chain option with Bosnian-style ćevapi. Drama Ćevapi near Kalemegdan is a great casual spot.

What is burek and where should I try it?

Burek is a flaky phyllo pastry filled with meat, cheese, spinach, or potato. Pekara Trpković is the Reddit favorite. Burek Non-Stop Čubura is open 24/7. Lili M-Burek near Zeleni Venac is beloved by locals.

How much does street food cost in Belgrade?

Belgrade street food is exceptionally affordable. Pljeskavica costs 350-600 RSD ($3-5 USD), ćevapi runs 500-800 RSD ($4.50-7), and burek is 120-250 RSD ($1-2.20). A full meal rarely exceeds $7.

Is Belgrade street food good for vegetarians?

Serbian street food is meat-focused, but vegetarians have options: cheese/spinach/potato burek, palačinke (crepes), gibanica (cheese pastry), proja (cornbread), and fresh produce at Zeleni Venac market.

What time do Belgrade street food spots open?

Bakeries open at 6-7 AM. Grill spots open at 9-10 AM and run until midnight. Burek Non-Stop Čubura is open 24 hours. Late-night pljeskavica stands come alive after midnight.

What should I order at a Belgrade grill restaurant?

Start with gurmanska pljeskavica with ajvar. Add 10 ćevapi with kajmak and onions. Get urnebes and šopska salata as sides. Pair with Jelen or Zaječarsko beer. Total cost: under €10.
