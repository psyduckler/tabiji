Quick answer

For the most authentic Ghanaian street food experience, head to Osu Night Market after 6pm. For air-conditioned comfort with variety, Accra Mall Food Court is your best bet. Budget: GHS 15-400+ depending on venue type.

Top verdicts

  • Osu Night Market: The ultimate Accra street food experience with Ga Kenkey, grilled pork, and kelewele.
  • Accra Mall Food Court: Most diverse mall food court with KFC, local options, and upscale dining.
  • The Mix Design Hub: Rooftop dining with ocean views and African-Western fusion cuisine.

Accra's food scene spans everything from bustling night markets to gleaming mall food courts. Whether you want authentic jollof rice at local prices or upscale pan-Asian fusion, this guide covers the full spectrum of multi-vendor dining experiences in Ghana's capital.

The city's food halls fall into three categories: traditional markets like Osu Night Market and Makola where vendors have served generations of locals; modern mall food courts offering international chains alongside Ghanaian favorites; and contemporary food hubs like The Mix and Kozo that bring multiple cuisines under one stylish roof.

We researched local food blogs, TripAdvisor reviews, and recommendations from Accra residents to build this guide. Each venue was verified for current operations as of April 2026.
