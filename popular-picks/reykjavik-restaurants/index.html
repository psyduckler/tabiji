Quick Picks by Budget

💰 Budget
Bæjarins Beztu Pylsur — Iconic hot dogs, a must-try for any budget traveler looking for a quick, delicious bite.
Brauð & Co — Perfect for an affordable breakfast, artisanal pastries, or a coffee break.
Sægreifinn (The Sea Baron) — Famous for its legendary lobster soup at a surprisingly reasonable price, a true local gem.

Messinn — Casual and cozy, offering generous portions of expertly pan-fried fish, a seafood lover's delight.

Rok Restaurant — Trendy small plates ideal for sharing, featuring modern Icelandic and European flavors with a cool vibe.

Dill Restaurant — Iceland's first Michelin-starred restaurant, pioneering New Nordic cuisine with an innovative tasting menu.
ÓX Restaurant — An incredibly intimate and exclusive fine dining experience around a chef's table, offering a personalized tasting menu.
