Quick answer

Orlando's nightlife has a secret, and it's whispered behind unmarked doors and through forgotten alleyways: the speakeasy. The city known for its magical theme parks also boasts a thriving, clandestine cocktail scene where mixology is an art form and the atmosphere is pure, unadulterated cool. From historic downtown hideaways to suburban surprises, these bars offer an escape from the ordinary, inviting you into a world of expertly crafted drinks and intimate vibes.

  • Hanson's Shoe Repair: Those seeking an exclusive, old-school speakeasy experience with top-tier mixology and a stunning city view.

The modern speakeasy trend in Orlando draws inspiration from the Prohibition era, recreating the allure of illicit gatherings and the sophisticated charm of a bygone age. This movement isn't just about hiding a bar; it's about curating an experience, often featuring unique entrances, password requirements, and interiors designed to transport patrons to another time. It’s a nod to history, blended seamlessly with contemporary mixology.

You'll find a wide spectrum of speakeasy styles across Orlando. Some lean into true 1920s Prohibition aesthetics, complete with vintage decor and classic cocktails. Others offer a more contemporary take, blending modern design with a hidden entrance and innovative drink menus. There are themed speakeasies, quiet lounges perfect for a romantic evening, and vibrant spots where the secret adds an extra layer of fun to a social gathering. Whatever your preference, Orlando’s hidden bar scene has a clandestine corner just for you.

Frequently asked questions

What is a speakeasy bar?

A speakeasy bar is a hidden establishment that serves alcoholic beverages, often with a secretive entrance, unique theme, and focus on craft cocktails. They harken back to the Prohibition era, creating an exclusive and intimate atmosphere that contrasts with conventional bars.

Do I need a password to enter Orlando's speakeasies?

Some of Orlando's speakeasies, like Hanson's Shoe Repair, do require a password for entry, which usually changes daily or weekly and can often be found on their social media or website. Others may have discreet entrances but no explicit password requirement. It's always a good idea to check ahead.

Are Orlando's speakeasies expensive?

Prices at Orlando's speakeasies vary, generally ranging from $10 to $25 per cocktail. While some spots, particularly upscale lounges, lean towards the higher end due to premium ingredients and expert craftsmanship, others offer excellent value without compromising the unique experience.

Are there any speakeasies outside of Downtown Orlando?

Yes, Orlando's speakeasy scene extends beyond the downtown core. Winter Park, for example, is home to several exclusive hidden bars like The Gibson and SAKAITEN (The Library). You can also find unique options in Audubon Park (The Neighbors) and even International Drive (Vault 5421).

Can I eat at Orlando speakeasies?

While the primary focus of most speakeasies is on cocktails, many offer a limited menu of small bites or appetizers perfect for pairing with your drinks. Some, like The Neighbors (within East End Market) or SAKAITEN (with its sushi restaurant), have more substantial food options available nearby or as part of the experience.

Are Orlando speakeasies good for groups?

It depends on the venue. While some speakeasies, like Mathers Social Gathering, have more spacious layouts accommodating groups, many are designed for intimacy and are better suited for couples or small parties of 2-4 people. Always consider calling ahead or making a reservation for larger groups.

What is the dress code for Orlando speakeasies?

Most Orlando speakeasies encourage smart casual attire, especially for upscale venues like The Gibson or Hanson's Shoe Repair. Avoid flip-flops, athletic wear, or excessively casual clothing. While a jacket isn't always required, dressing up a bit will enhance your experience and fit the sophisticated ambiance.

Are Orlando's speakeasies accessible?

Accessibility varies greatly due to the nature of hidden entrances and older buildings. Some venues might involve stairs or discreet elevators. It's best to check with the individual establishment directly regarding specific accessibility features and accommodations for wheelchairs or mobility aids before your visit.
