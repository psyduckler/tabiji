Quick answer

Confiserie Sprüngli serves Zürich's most iconic brunch — the Sunday buffet at Paradeplatz with bircher muesli, Swiss cheeses, and legendary Luxemburgerli. For the best value, Café du Bonheur in Kreis 4 delivers cozy French-inspired weekend brunch without breaking the bank.

How We Picked These

Zürich brunch typically costs CHF 15–55 per person, ranging from affordable cafés like Café du Bonheur under CHF 25 to Sprüngli's legendary Sunday buffet at CHF 55. Haus Hiltl, the world's oldest vegetarian restaurant, consistently ranks among the best. Reservations are strongly recommended on weekends for popular spots.

We cross-referenced Reddit threads (r/zurich, r/askswitzerland), local food blogs, and Google reviews to find brunch spots that consistently earn praise from both locals and visitors. Every restaurant on this list has genuine community endorsements — no pay-to-play, no sponsored picks.
