Quick answer

San Diego's rooftop bars, ranging from $7-$22 for drinks, offer diverse experiences, but Mister A's is a top recommendation for fine dining. The best time to visit is September through November for warm days and spectacular sunsets. This guide, curated from local local consensus, highlights the best spots, from Gaslamp to La Jolla.

Top verdicts

  • Mister A's: The undisputed king of San Diego rooftop bars.
  • The Nolen Rooftop: The best pure cocktail bar on a rooftop in San Diego.
  • Top of the Hyatt: The highest rooftop bar in San Diego.

What we found: the city's rooftop scene spans everything from white-tablecloth fine dining 12 stories up (Mister A's) to a 10,000 sq ft dog-friendly brewery patio (Kairoa). The Gaslamp and Little Italy dominate, but La Jolla and Mission Beach have some of the best ocean-view options. Cocktails typically run $14–$22, with happy hours offering the best value.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best rooftop bar in San Diego?

Mister A s on the 12th floor in Bankers Hill is widely considered the best, with 180-degree views of the skyline, Balboa Park, and the harbor.

Are there rooftop bars with ocean views?

Yes. Cannonball at Belmont Park, Georges at the Cove in La Jolla, and Top of the Hyatt all offer ocean or harbor views.

How much do drinks cost at San Diego rooftop bars?

Cocktails typically range from 14-22 dollars. Budget options like Kairoa Brewing offer craft beers from 7-10 dollars.

Do I need reservations?

Reservations are strongly recommended for Mister As, Born and Raised, Seneca Trattoria, and Lumi. The Nolen, Altitude, and Top of the Hyatt are walk-in friendly.

Best time to visit rooftop bars in San Diego?

September through November is the sweet spot. Arrive 60-90 minutes before sunset for golden hour.

What should I wear?

Mister As and Born and Raised expect smart-casual. Kairoa, Cannonball, and Coco Maya are casual-friendly.
