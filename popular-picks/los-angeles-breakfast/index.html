Quick answer

Start your day the L.A. way with a breakfast that's as diverse and dynamic as the city itself. From sun-drenched patios serving health-conscious bowls to bustling diners dishing out stacks of pancakes, Los Angeles offers an unparalleled array of morning meal experiences. This guide cuts through the noise to bring you the top spots where locals and visitors alike fuel up for a day in the City of Angels.

Top verdicts

  • République: Those seeking a refined brunch experience with exquisite pastries and a lively, chic atmosphere, perfect for a special occasion.
  • Sqirl: Foodies looking for a unique, trendsetting breakfast with a focus on fresh, healthy ingredients and Instagram-worthy dishes.
  • The Griddle Cafe: Anyone craving huge, over-the-top pancakes and a classic American diner experience with a touch of Hollywood flair.

Los Angeles's breakfast culture has evolved significantly from its early diner roots, influenced by waves of immigration and a strong embrace of health and wellness trends. The city's penchant for innovation means traditional breakfast items are often reimagined with a Californian twist, incorporating fresh, local produce and international flavors. This blend of old and new creates a unique breakfast identity that is distinctly Angeleno.

Whether you're craving a classic American breakfast, an avant-garde pastry creation, a hearty Mexican-inspired morning feast, or a plant-based sensation, L.A. has a breakfast spot tailored to your desires. The city caters to all palates and preferences, from quick grab-and-go options to leisurely brunch experiences complete with mimosas and artisanal coffee.

Our methodology included extensive research across popular Reddit subreddits like r/LosAngeles, cross-referencing hundreds of user recommendations with insights from reputable food critics and local publications. This community-driven approach ensures our picks reflect authentic local favorites and hidden gems.

Frequently asked questions

What are the typical breakfast hours in Los Angeles?

Most dedicated breakfast and brunch spots in Los Angeles open between 7:00 AM and 8:00 AM on weekdays, and often a bit later, around 8:00 AM to 9:00 AM, on weekends. Brunch menus typically run until 2:00 PM or 3:00 PM. Some diners, like The Original Pantry Cafe, are open 24/7, offering breakfast around the clock.

Are there good options for healthy or vegan breakfast in Los Angeles?

Absolutely! Los Angeles is a hub for health-conscious and plant-based dining. Sqirl offers many fresh, seasonal options, and Pura Vita Pizzeria & Wine Bar provides a fantastic entirely vegan Italian-inspired brunch. Many cafes also feature acai bowls, avocado toast, and fresh juices.

How much should I expect to pay for breakfast in L.A.?

Breakfast prices in Los Angeles vary widely. A casual meal at a diner or counter-service spot like Eggslut or The Original Pantry Cafe might cost $15-25 per person. Mid-range cafes and bistros like Joan's on Third or Little Dom's typically run $20-35 per person. Upscale brunch experiences at places like République or Bottega Louie can range from $30-50+ per person, especially with drinks.

Is it easy to find parking at breakfast spots in Los Angeles?

Parking in Los Angeles can be notoriously challenging, particularly in popular neighborhoods like Silver Lake, West Hollywood, and parts of Downtown. Many venues offer valet parking (often paid), or you might find metered street parking. For some spots, using a ride-sharing service might be more convenient, especially on weekends.

Do I need reservations for breakfast or brunch in L.A.?

For highly popular brunch spots, especially those with a sit-down service like République or Little Dom's, reservations are strongly recommended, particularly for weekend visits. For more casual cafes or counter-service places like Sqirl or Eggslut, reservations are not usually taken, and you'll typically join a queue upon arrival. Always check the venue's website for their specific policy.

Are there any iconic or historic breakfast diners in Los Angeles?

Yes, Los Angeles boasts several iconic and historic diners. The Original Pantry Cafe in Downtown LA, open since 1924 and famously cash-only, offers a true step back in time. Roscoe's House of Chicken and Waffles, while more modern in its iconic status, has a rich history of serving soulful breakfast for decades.

What's the best breakfast in Los Angeles for families with kids?

For families, The Griddle Cafe (with its massive, fun pancakes) and Roscoe's House of Chicken and Waffles (for a unique, lively experience) are popular choices. Casual spots like Joan's on Third or Grand Central Market (where Eggslut is located) also offer diverse options that cater to various tastes and usually have a more relaxed atmosphere.

Can I find good coffee at these breakfast places?

Absolutely! Los Angeles has a thriving coffee culture, and all the venues on our list prioritize serving high-quality coffee. Many, like République and Sqirl, have dedicated coffee programs with specialty brews. You'll find everything from classic American drip coffee to artisanal espresso drinks and cold brews to perfectly complement your breakfast.
