Quick answer

Tulum's beach clubs range from budget-friendly to ultra-luxurious, but the top recommendation for a classic, relaxed experience is Ziggy's Beach Club. Keep in mind that seaweed season (typically May-September) can impact the beach quality, so check conditions before you go.

Tulum's beach club scene is a love-it-or-hate-it affair. On one hand: powder-white sand, impossibly blue Caribbean water, and cocktails served on daybeds under palm trees. On the other: minimum spends, influencer swarms, and seaweed that can ruin even the prettiest beach.

Frequently asked questions

How much do Tulum beach clubs cost?

Most beach clubs have a minimum spend of $30–$80 USD per person, which goes toward food and drinks. Premium clubs like Taboo can be $100+. Some smaller spots have no minimum or a low $20 minimum. Drinks are typically $10–$18 USD each.

Is the seaweed bad in Tulum?

Sargassum seaweed is a real issue in Tulum, especially from May through September. Beach clubs clean their areas daily, so they're usually better than public beaches. January through April tends to have the least seaweed. Check recent Reddit posts or iSeahorse before your trip for current conditions.

What is the best beach club in Tulum for a chill day?

Ziggy's, Coco Tulum, and Hip Hotel are the most recommended for a relaxed, bohemian vibe without the party scene. For families or couples wanting peace, these are consistently the top Reddit picks.

Are Tulum beach clubs overpriced?

Many Redditors say yes — Tulum has gotten expensive compared to a few years ago. However, compared to beach clubs in Mykonos or Ibiza, it's still more affordable. The key is managing expectations: know the minimum spend before you go, and choose clubs that match your budget and vibe.

What's the difference between 'minimum spend' and 'entry fee' at Tulum beach clubs?

A 'minimum spend' means you pay nothing to enter, but you must spend a set amount on food and drinks. Any spending contributes to this total. An 'entry fee,' common at places like <a href="#papaya-playa-project"><strong>Papaya Playa Project</strong></a> for night events, is a separate charge just to get in, and often does not include any credit towards food or beverages.

Are Tulum beach clubs kid-friendly?

While many beach clubs welcome children, the 'kid-friendly' level varies. Places like <a href="#ziggys-beach-club"><strong>Ziggy's Beach Club</strong></a> and <a href="#la-zebra"><strong>La Zebra</strong></a> are generally more accommodating with families, offering a relaxed atmosphere and good food. High-energy party spots like <a href="#taboo-tulum"><strong>Taboo Tulum</strong></a> or late-night events at <a href="#papaya-playa-project"><strong>Papaya Playa Project</strong></a> are typically geared towards adults.

Which Tulum beach clubs are best for a quieter, more relaxed experience?

For a truly tranquil day, consider starting your visit early in the morning at almost any club, as the crowds typically arrive later. Specifically, <a href="#ziggys-beach-club"><strong>Ziggy's Beach Club</strong></a> consistently receives high marks for its laid-back atmosphere and excellent service, making it perfect for relaxation. <a href="#coco-tulum"><strong>Coco Tulum</strong></a> also offers a charmingly rustic and serene setting, ideal for unwinding.

How much does a day at a Tulum beach club typically cost?

The cost can vary significantly based on the club and your spending habits. Budget-friendly options like <a href="#hip-hotel-tulum"><strong>Hip Hotel Tulum</strong></a> might have a minimum spend of $20-$30. Mid-range clubs like <a href="#ziggys-beach-club"><strong>Ziggy's Beach Club</strong></a> or <a href="#la-zebra"><strong>La Zebra</strong></a> often range from $40-$60. Luxury clubs such as <a href="#be-tulum"><strong>Be Tulum</strong></a> or <a href="#taboo-tulum"><strong>Taboo Tulum</strong></a> can have minimums from $60 up to $150+, plus any extra purchases and tips.

Are there any Tulum beach clubs known for live music or entertainment?

Absolutely! <a href="#la-zebra"><strong>La Zebra</strong></a> is renowned for its vibrant Sunday salsa nights, drawing enthusiastic dancers and spectators. <a href="#vagalume"><strong>Vagalume</strong></a> frequently hosts acclaimed DJs, especially transitioning from day to night. And of course, <a href="#papaya-playa-project"><strong>Papaya Playa Project</strong></a> is the go-to for major full moon parties and weekend electronic music events, establishing itself as a premier nightlife destination.
