Quick answer

Spokane's thrifting landscape is a treasure hunter's dream, brimming with diverse options that cater to every style and budget. Whether you're on the hunt for a unique vintage outfit, budget-friendly home decor, or an antique conversation piece, the Lilac City's second-hand stores deliver an unparalleled shopping experience.

  • Boulevard Mercantile: Serious collectors and those looking for unique statement pieces for their home, from mid-century modern to rustic farm-chic.
  • The Bohemian: Fashion-forward individuals seeking standout vintage pieces, from 70s dresses to quirky accessories.
  • Consign Local: Shoppers looking for upscale home furnishings or designer clothing without the brand new price tag.

The culture of thrifting in Spokane runs deep, mirroring the city's community-focused spirit and appreciation for sustainability. Historically, many of these stores began as community initiatives, evolving into beloved local institutions that not only offer affordable goods but also support various charitable causes across the region, fostering a cycle of giving and repurposing.

From sprawling warehouses filled with general household goods to meticulously curated boutiques showcasing designer consignment and retro apparel, Spokane's thrift stores offer a remarkable array of styles. Shoppers can delve into racks of pre-loved fashion, browse shelves of forgotten books, unearth one-of-a-kind furniture, or discover rare collectibles.

Frequently asked questions

What kind of items can I expect to find in Spokane thrift stores?

Spokane's thrift stores offer an incredibly diverse range of items. You can find everything from clothing, shoes, and accessories (vintage, contemporary, and designer) to furniture, home decor, kitchenware, books, electronics, and even architectural salvage or building materials. The inventory varies greatly from store to store, ensuring unique discoveries on every visit.

Are thrift store prices in Spokane generally affordable?

Yes, Spokane is known for its relatively affordable thrifting scene. General thrift stores like Goodwill and Value Village offer very low prices on most items. Specialty vintage and consignment shops will have higher price points reflecting the curation, brand, or rarity of items, but still significantly less than buying new.

Do Spokane thrift stores accept donations?

Most large thrift organizations like Goodwill, Value Village, Habitat for Humanity ReStore, and STCU Good Samaritan Thrift Store happily accept donations of gently used goods. It's always best to check their specific websites or call ahead for a list of accepted items and donation hours, as policies can vary.

What's the best way to find unique vintage items in Spokane?

For unique vintage items, focus on stores known for their curation, such as The Bohemian, Lucky Vintage & Thrift, and Boulevard Mercantile. Visiting smaller, independent shops often yields more distinctive finds. Regular visits are key, as inventory constantly changes, and striking up conversations with shop owners can sometimes lead to insider tips on new arrivals.

Are there any specific days to get the best deals at Spokane thrift stores?

While new inventory arrives daily at many stores, some places like Value Village have specific discount days or weekly sales based on colored tags. Signing up for email lists or loyalty programs can inform you about these deals. Weekday mornings are generally recommended for the best selection before crowds pick up.

Can I sell my clothes or furniture at Spokane's thrift stores?

You generally 'donate' items to larger thrift chains like Goodwill, receiving a tax-deductible receipt. For selling items, you'll want to visit 'consignment' stores like Consign Local or Refinery. These stores accept items based on quality, brand, and current trends, and you receive a percentage of the sale price once the item sells. Always contact them directly for their specific consignment policies.

Is parking usually available at Spokane thrift stores?

For most larger thrift stores like Goodwill, Value Village, and ReStore, dedicated parking lots are typically available, often with ample space. Smaller vintage boutiques, especially those on busier streets like North Monroe, might have limited parking lots or rely more on street parking, which can sometimes require a short walk.

Are there changing rooms available at vintage clothing stores in Spokane?

Yes, most dedicated vintage clothing stores like The Bohemian and Lucky Vintage & Thrift provide changing rooms. However, larger general thrift stores (Goodwill, Value Village) may have limited or no changing rooms available due to the high volume and nature of their inventory. It's a good idea to wear clothes that are easy to try things on over, or ask a staff member upon arrival.
