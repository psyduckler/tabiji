Accra's wine scene has evolved dramatically over the past decade. While dedicated natural wine bars are still emerging in Ghana, the city now boasts sophisticated venues where wine takes center stage — from cozy wine cafes in Labone to glamorous rooftop lounges with panoramic city views.

The wine market in Accra caters primarily to the city's cosmopolitan mix of expats, business travelers, and Ghana's growing affluent class. South African wines dominate due to proximity and favorable trade relationships, but you'll find impressive selections from France, Italy, Chile, and beyond at the city's best establishments.
