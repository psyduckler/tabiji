Frequently asked questions

When do cocktail bars open in Buenos Aires?

Buenos Aires runs on a dramatically later schedule than most cities. Cocktail bars typically open around 8–9pm but don't get lively until 11pm–midnight. Peak hours are midnight to 3am on weekends. Clubs and late-night bars often operate until 6–8am. If you arrive at 9pm, you may be the only person there. Dinner in BA starts at 9–10pm, so plan your cocktail bar visits accordingly — go after a late dinner, not before.

What cocktails are Buenos Aires known for?

Buenos Aires has a strong cocktail culture centered on fernet-cola (the national drink — Fernet Branca mixed with Coca-Cola, consumed in enormous quantities), Aperol Spritz, malbec wine, and craft gin & tonics. The city's top cocktail bars have moved well beyond these classics into sophisticated house cocktails using Argentine botanicals, South American spirits like pisco and singani, and vermouth (which has had a massive revival). Craft cocktails typically run 1,500–2,500 ARS ($1.50–$2.50 USD at the official rate, though rates fluctuate significantly).

How much do cocktails cost in Buenos Aires?

Buenos Aires cocktail prices are extremely favorable for international visitors due to the peso's exchange rate. At cocktail bars, expect to pay 2,000–4,000 ARS per cocktail (roughly $2–$4 USD at official rates, potentially less at blue-dollar rate). A full night of cocktails at a world-class bar like Florería Atlántico might cost what a single cocktail costs in London or New York. Always check the current ARS/USD rate before your trip as it fluctuates significantly.

What is the best neighborhood for cocktail bars in Buenos Aires?

Palermo (particularly Palermo Soho and Palermo Hollywood) has the highest concentration of cocktail bars and is the most accessible for visitors. San Telmo has a grittier, more bohemian vibe with Doppelgänger as the standout. Villa Crespo has the beloved 878 Bar. Retiro has Florería Atlántico and Gran Bar Danzón. For a bar crawl, Palermo Soho is the easiest starting point — multiple good bars within walking distance.
