Quick answer

Atlanta's Vietnamese food scene is more dynamic than ever, and at its heart lies a thriving culture of pho. From steamy bowls packed with rare steak and tender brisket to fragrant chicken variations, the city’s pho offerings range from traditional, no-frills establishments to modern, elevated dining experiences.

Top verdicts

  • Nam Phuong: Those seeking a classic, high-quality pho experience in a vibrant, family-friendly setting with a wide range of Vietnamese dishes.
  • Lee's Bakery: Pho lovers on a budget who also appreciate top-tier banh mi, or anyone looking for a quick and reliable, comforting bowl.
  • Anh's Kitchen: Diners looking for an elevated Vietnamese experience, perfect for a date night or when craving high-quality pho in a stylish setting with craft cocktails.

Pho, a Vietnamese noodle soup, has a rich history originating in early 20th-century northern Vietnam. Traditionally, it's a dish of rice noodles (bánh phở) in a clear broth, with thinly sliced beef (phở bò) or chicken (phở gà), and served with fresh herbs, bean sprouts, lime, and chilies. Each region, and indeed each family, often has its own subtle twist on the classic.

In Atlanta, you'll find a spectrum of pho styles. Many restaurants on Buford Highway specialize in robust, savory broths typical of Southern Vietnamese pho, often served with a generous array of fresh accompaniments. Meanwhile, newer establishments in neighborhoods like Poncey-Highland might offer lighter, more refined broths or creative interpretations with unique proteins and presentation, catering to diverse palates.

Frequently asked questions

What is the typical price range for pho in Atlanta?

The price range for a bowl of pho in Atlanta typically falls between $10 and $20 at most traditional and casual establishments. Upscale or modern Vietnamese restaurants might offer pho bowls for $18 to $30, depending on ingredients and portion size. Expect slightly higher prices for specialty broths or premium cuts of meat.

Are there vegetarian or vegan pho options available in Atlanta?

Yes, many Vietnamese restaurants in Atlanta now offer vegetarian or vegan pho options. These typically feature a vegetable-based broth, often with tofu and a generous assortment of fresh vegetables like mushrooms, bok choy, and carrots. It's always best to ask the staff to confirm ingredients and ensure no fish sauce or animal products are used if you have strict dietary requirements.

What are the common types of pho found in Atlanta?

In Atlanta, you'll commonly find Phở Bò (beef pho) and Phở Gà (chicken pho). Beef pho variations include Phở Tái (rare steak), Phở Chín (well-done brisket), Phở Nạm (flank), Phở Gân (tendon), Phở Sách (tripe), and Phở Đặc Biệt (combination beef pho). Some modern spots might offer unique proteins like oxtail or seafood.

What condiments and garnishes are typically served with pho?

Pho is almost always served with a plate of fresh garnishes, including bean sprouts, cilantro, Thai basil, and lime wedges. Common condiments found at the table include Sriracha hot sauce, hoisin sauce, sliced jalapeños, and sometimes pickled garlic or chili oil. Don't be shy to customize your bowl to your liking!

Is Buford Highway the best area for pho in Atlanta?

Buford Highway is widely considered the epicenter of authentic Vietnamese and other international cuisine in Atlanta, and it houses a high concentration of excellent pho restaurants like Nam Phuong and Pho 24. While it's a prime destination, you can also find fantastic pho in other neighborhoods like Poncey-Highland (Anh's Kitchen) and Avondale Estates (Lotus Leaf).

What's the difference between Northern and Southern Vietnamese pho?

Northern Vietnamese pho (Phở Bắc) tends to have a clearer, simpler broth with less emphasis on garnishes, often served with wider noodles and more green onions. Southern Vietnamese pho (Phở Nam) usually has a richer, sweeter, and more complex broth, often seasoned with star anise and served with a generous plate of fresh herbs, bean sprouts, and various sauces like hoisin and Sriracha. Most pho in Atlanta is Southern style.

Can I get pho for takeout or delivery in Atlanta?

Absolutely! Most pho restaurants in Atlanta offer takeout, and many are available through popular delivery apps like DoorDash, Uber Eats, and Grubhub. When ordering for delivery, the broth, noodles, and meats/vegetables are usually packaged separately to prevent the noodles from getting soggy and to maintain optimal freshness.

Is pho a healthy meal option?

Pho can be a relatively healthy and wholesome meal. It's often rich in protein from the meat and nutrients from the herbs and vegetables. The broth itself can be low in calories, but it can be high in sodium. To make it healthier, opt for leaner cuts of meat, load up on fresh vegetables, and use condiments sparingly to control sodium intake.
