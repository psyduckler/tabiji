Guangzhou is the undisputed capital of dim sum. The city's yum cha tradition dates back centuries, and locals still start their mornings with a pot of tea and two items. From Michelin-starred hotel restaurants to century-old bakeries, prices range from ¥50 to ¥500+ per person.

Three tiers dominate: Michelin-starred hotel dim sum (Hongtu Hall, Jade River, Lei Garden), heritage institutions (Tao Tao Ju since 1880, Guangzhou Restaurant, Lian Xiang Lou since 1889), and modern everyday chains (Dian Dou De). Each offers a different slice of Guangzhou's dim sum culture.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best dim sum in Guangzhou?

Hongtu Hall at the White Swan Hotel holds a Michelin star and is the most acclaimed. For accessible everyday yum cha, Dian Dou De and Tao Tao Ju are top picks.

What is yum cha?

Yum cha means drinking tea - the social ritual of eating dim sum with tea. In Guangzhou, locals say they go for yum cha.

What time for dim sum?

7-8 AM opening. For Hongtu Hall arrive by 7:30 AM. Traditional window is 7-11 AM.

How much does dim sum cost?

Budget: 60-100 yuan (US$8-14). Mid-range: 100-200 yuan. Premium: 200-500+ yuan.

Must-order dim sum dishes?

Har gow, siu mai, char siu bao, dan tat. Also try cheong fun, phoenix talons, liu sha bao.

English menus available?

Yes at tourist-friendly spots like Hongtu Hall, Lei Garden, Dian Dou De. Many use QR ordering.

What is yi zhong liang jian?

One pot, two items - ordering just two dim sum items at a time with tea to keep food fresh.
