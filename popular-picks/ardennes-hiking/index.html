10 Best Hiking Trails in the Belgian Ardennes

Trails Map

1Ninglinspo Valley

Difficulty: Moderate Distance: 6 km Time: 2-3 hours
“The Ninglinspo is probably the most known one, features a nice path following a little stream up a river past a ton of natural pools and very small waterfalls.”
tabiji verdict: The most popular and enchanting walk in the Ardennes. It's a magical trail that follows the Ninglinspo mountain river, requiring you to cross charming wooden bridges and scramble over rocks. It's famous for a reason, so it can get busy, especially on weekends. Go early to enjoy the peace.

2Hautes Fagnes (High Fens)

Difficulty: Easy to Moderate Distance: Varies (multiple trails) Time: 1-5 hours
“High fens natural reserve is pretty nice to visit but beware there might be a lot of people.”
tabiji verdict: A unique, otherworldly landscape of high moorlands, peat bogs, and misty forests. The wooden boardwalks make it accessible and protect the fragile ecosystem. The area around Signal de Botrange, Belgium's highest point, offers stunning panoramic views. A must-do for its distinctive atmosphere.

3Vallée de la Hoëgne

Difficulty: Moderate Distance: 8 km Time: 3-4 hours
“Hoëgne has another quite known walk, also following a stream with a bunch of bridges and stuff, quite nice!”
tabiji verdict: Similar to Ninglinspo but often considered its wilder, less-crowded cousin. The trail follows the Hoëgne river, a torrent that has carved a deep valley into the rock. Expect beautiful waterfalls, cascades, and a series of picturesque bridges. A truly classic Ardennes hike.

4Rochehaut and the Semois Valley

Difficulty: Moderate to Difficult Distance: 4 km (Les Echelles hike) Time: 2-3 hours
“This one is not too challenging and takes you passed the best panorama in Belgium (Panorama de Frahan) The hike itself is just one of many hills in the Ardennes but Rochehaut offers such a stunning, green view.”
tabiji verdict: Home to what's often called the most beautiful viewpoint in Belgium, looking down on the village of Frahan nestled in a meander of the Semois river. For the adventurous, the "Ladder Walk" (Promenade des Echelles) is a thrilling trail involving actual ladders to navigate the steep cliffs.

5Bouillon Castle Trails

Difficulty: Varies Distance: Varies Time: 1-4 hours
“There's also Bouillon and La-Roche-en-Ardenne which both have great hikes around there in very nice Ardennes scenery.”
tabiji verdict: The area around the magnificent medieval castle of Bouillon is crisscrossed with hiking trails. You can hike up to the "Tombeau du Géant" (Giant's Tomb), another stunning viewpoint over the Semois, or explore the dense forests surrounding the historic town.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the best hiking areas to start with in the Belgian Ardennes?

Start with the best-known Ardennes hiking zones on this page: the Ninglinspo valley, Hautes Fagnes boardwalk areas, and the Semois-side trails near Bouillon and Rochehaut. They give you the strongest mix of scenery, signage, and traveler feedback.

Are Ardennes hikes beginner-friendly?

Some are, but not all. The easier walks tend to be shorter forest loops, river promenades, and boardwalk-style nature routes. Trails with stream crossings, steeper elevation, or rocky footing are better for hikers with stronger shoes and a bit more experience.

Do I need a car for hiking in the Belgian Ardennes?

A car makes the Ardennes much easier because many trailheads are spread across rural areas, but you can still do some hikes by combining trains or buses with taxi transfers. If flexibility matters more than strict budget, driving is the smoother option.
