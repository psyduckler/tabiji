Quick answer

Auckland is known as the "City of Sails" — and where there are boats, there's exceptional seafood. The Waitemata Harbour and surrounding waters deliver some of New Zealand's finest catches to tables across the city, from premium crayfish (NZ rock lobster) to seasonal Bluff oysters and local snapper.

The city's seafood scene spans from white-tablecloth fine dining at Westhaven Marina and the Viaduct to bustling fish markets where you can have your catch cooked to order. Whether you're seeking roasted crayfish with harbour views, freshly shucked oysters at a walk-in eatery, or the city's best fish and chips by the beach, Auckland delivers.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best seafood restaurant in Auckland?

Sails Restaurant overlooking Westhaven Marina is widely considered Auckland's best fine-dining seafood destination, famous for its roasted crayfish and harbour views. For a more casual oyster experience, Depot Eatery & Oyster Bar on Federal Street is consistently ranked among the city's best.

Where can I get fresh oysters in Auckland?

Depot Eatery & Oyster Bar on Federal Street has one of Auckland's best oyster bars, shucked to order. Oyster & Chop at Viaduct Harbour also specialises in fresh New Zealand oysters with daily happy hour specials between 3pm and 6pm. Both serve Bluff oysters when in season (March–August).

Is the Auckland Fish Market worth visiting?

Yes — the Auckland Fish Market on Jellicoe Street in Wynyard Quarter is a vibrant indoor-outdoor hub with fresh fish stalls and casual seafood counters. Locals recommend buying fresh kina (sea urchin) or having fish cooked to order. It's more casual and affordable than waterfront restaurants.

What is the best waterfront seafood restaurant in Auckland?

FISH Restaurant at the Hilton on Princes Wharf sits 300 metres out to sea with unparalleled Waitemata Harbour views. Harbourside Ocean Bar Grill in the Ferry Building and Sails Restaurant at Westhaven Marina are also excellent waterfront options with premium seafood menus.

Where do Auckland locals eat seafood?

Locals consistently recommend Swashbucklers at Westhaven Marina for its relaxed maritime vibe and great-value seafood platters, Depot for oysters and shared plates, and the Auckland Fish Market for the freshest catch at reasonable prices. For fish and chips, Oceanz Seafood at Mission Bay is a local favourite.

What seafood is unique to Auckland and New Zealand?

Look for crayfish (NZ rock lobster), pāua (abalone), kina (sea urchin), Bluff oysters (seasonal, March–August), green-lipped mussels, whitebait, and snapper — all uniquely New Zealand. Many of Auckland's top restaurants feature these on their menus, with Sails and Harbourside being particularly renowned for crayfish.
