Quick answer

Ghent's craft beer scene ranges from medieval bars to modern breweries, with prices varying across establishments; Dulle Griet is our top recommendation for its iconic shoe-deposit Kwak beer experience. This list focuses on bars highly recommended by locals and discerning beer lovers, offering authentic experiences for every taste.

Ghent doesn't shout about its beer culture — it doesn't need to. While Bruges lures tourists with chocolate and bruges, Ghent quietly runs one of Europe's most authentic beer scenes: medieval bars with 450+ taps, a city brewery making hop-free gruit beers using medieval recipes, a hipster craft taproom in a repurposed industrial dock, and the smallest bar in the city where you might not even get a stool.

Frequently asked questions

What is the most famous bar in Ghent for Belgian beer?

Dulle Griet on Vrijdagmarkt is Ghent’s most iconic beer bar, famous for its 450+ beers and the ritual of leaving one of your shoes as a deposit before ordering a Kwak beer in its special glass with a wooden stand. A must-do experience in Ghent.

What is gruit beer and where can I try it in Ghent?

Gruit is an ancient style of beer brewed with herbs and spices instead of hops — a recipe that predates the widespread use of hops in brewing. Gruut City Brewery in Ghent specializes in gruit beers, making it one of the only places in the world where you can taste this medieval style fresh from the brewery.

How many beers can I find in Ghent's best beer bars?

Ghent’s top beer bars have staggering selections. Bierhuis Cornelius offers 600+ Belgian beers. Dulle Griet has 450+. Trollekelder has 200+. Waterhuis aan de Bierkant has 150+. Belgium produces over 1,500 distinct beer styles, so even a long visit won’t exhaust the options.

Is Ghent better than Bruges for craft beer?

Most beer enthusiasts say yes. Ghent has a more authentic, less tourist-trap atmosphere than Bruges. The locals actually drink in the beer bars, the selections are broader, and spots like Dok Brewing Company and Gruut Brewery offer genuinely innovative craft beer experiences. Bruges is more famous for chocolate; Ghent is Belgium’s real beer city.

What Belgian beers should I try when visiting Ghent?

Must-tries: Gruut Amber (the city’s own hop-free gruit beer), Dulle Griet’s Kwak (in the famous hourglass glass), a local Trappist like Westvleteren or Rochefort 10, any sour/lambic from Cantillon, and a Ghent Strop (the local lager). At Trollekelder, ask the bartender to guide you — they’re renowned for their expertise.
