Quick answer

Nashville's vegan food scene has exploded, transforming Music City into an unexpected haven for plant-based eaters. Gone are the days of limited options; today, the city boasts an impressive array of dedicated vegan establishments and incredibly vegan-friendly spots that cater to every craving, from comforting Southern classics to innovative global cuisine.

Top pick

Top verdicts

  • Graze Nashville: Foodies seeking inventive vegan dishes in a chic, vibrant atmosphere perfect for a date night or a fun meal with friends.
  • The Southern V: Anyone craving nostalgic Southern flavors, vegan-style, in a cozy and welcoming environment, ideal for a comforting meal.
  • The Wild Cow: Those seeking a diverse menu of fresh, healthy, and customizable vegetarian/vegan fare in a laid-back, community-focused setting.

The surge in vegan popularity in Nashville reflects a broader national trend, but with a distinct Southern twist. Rooted in a culture known for its hospitality and hearty meals, Nashville's vegan chefs have risen to the challenge, reinventing beloved dishes without compromising on flavor or authenticity. This culinary evolution has made vegan dining not just accessible, but an exciting adventure.

Visitors and locals alike can now explore a spectrum of vegan styles. Whether you're craving a soulful plate of hot chik'n and waffles, a gourmet plant-based burger, fresh and vibrant Asian bowls, or indulgent vegan ice cream, Nashville has a spot for you. The variety ensures that every meal can be a new discovery.

Frequently asked questions

Are there any fully vegan restaurants in Nashville, or just places with vegan options?

Nashville has a fantastic mix! While many restaurants offer excellent vegan options (like The Wild Cow), the city also boasts several dedicated, 100% vegan establishments such as Graze Nashville, The Southern V, Vege-licious Cafe, Sunflower Bakehouse, Vegan Bowl Nashville, KOKOS Ice Cream, Buns & Buns, and The Loving Hut. You'll have no shortage of fully plant-based choices.

Which Nashville vegan restaurants are best for a quick, casual meal?

For quick and casual, Vegan Bowl Nashville is excellent for customizable Asian-inspired bowls. Buns & Buns offers fast and delicious gourmet vegan burgers. Sunflower Bakehouse is perfect for a quick breakfast or light lunch with great pastries and coffee. Woodbine Cafe also provides quick, hearty diner fare.

Are there any upscale or fine-dining vegan options in Nashville?

While Nashville's vegan scene leans more towards casual and comfort dining, Graze Nashville offers a more elevated and trendy experience with its innovative menu and chic ambiance, making it suitable for a nicer dinner or date night. The Southern V, while comfortable, also presents beautifully crafted, high-quality dishes that feel special.

Do any vegan restaurants in Nashville offer traditional Southern dishes?

Absolutely! The Southern V is renowned for its incredible vegan renditions of Southern comfort food, including hot chik'n, biscuits, and mac & cheese. Vege-licious Cafe also specializes in authentic vegan soul food that tastes truly traditional and comforting.

Are there good vegan breakfast or brunch spots in Nashville?

Yes, several! Graze Nashville has a highly-rated weekend brunch with creative vegan dishes. The Southern V offers fantastic breakfast options like hot chik'n biscuits. Sunflower Bakehouse is a delightful spot for vegan pastries and breakfast sandwiches, and Woodbine Cafe is known for its classic diner-style vegan breakfast plates.

Can I find vegan desserts or ice cream in Nashville?

Definitely! KOKOS Ice Cream is a must-visit for artisanal, creamy vegan ice cream with unique flavors. Sunflower Bakehouse is a dedicated vegan bakery offering a wide array of cakes, cookies, and pastries. Many of the full-service vegan restaurants also offer tempting dessert options on their menus.

Are Nashville's vegan restaurants generally family-friendly?

Most vegan restaurants in Nashville are very welcoming to families. Places like The Southern V and The Wild Cow have a relaxed atmosphere and menu items that appeal to a wide range of tastes. Vegan Bowl Nashville and Buns & Buns are also great for families looking for quick, customizable meals.

What's the best area in Nashville to find a concentration of vegan restaurants?

East Nashville and North Nashville are excellent areas for vegan dining. East Nashville is home to gems like Graze Nashville and The Wild Cow, while North Nashville boasts The Southern V and Vege-licious Cafe, offering a vibrant cluster of plant-based culinary experiences.
