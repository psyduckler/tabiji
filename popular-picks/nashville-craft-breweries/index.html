Quick answer

Nashville's craft breweries offer pints averaging $6-$9, and Bearded Iris is the most consistently recommended brewery on Reddit. This guide highlights the best Nashville breweries, considering local consensus from 2021-2025, to help you navigate Music City's diverse beer scene.

Top verdicts

  • Bearded Iris Brewing: The undisputed king of Nashville hazy IPAs.
  • Southern Grist Brewing Company: Nashville's mad scientist brewery.
  • TailGate Brewery: Nashville's most-reviewed brewery.

Four neighborhoods dominate the scene: Germantown (Bearded Iris, Monday Night), East Nashville (Southern Grist, East Nashville Beer Works), The Gulch/SoBro (Tennessee Brew Works, New Heights), and West Nashville/Bellevue (TailGate). You can hit 3–4 in a single afternoon walk through Germantown alone.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best craft brewery in Nashville?

Bearded Iris Brewing is the most consistently recommended Nashville brewery across Reddit, known for its hop-forward IPAs and hazies. Southern Grist is a close second with its wildly creative flavor combinations.

What are Nashville's best IPAs?

Bearded Iris Homestyle is the signature Nashville IPA. Southern Grist makes creative fruited IPAs. Smith & Lentz offers technically precise West Coast styles.

Are Nashville breweries kid and dog friendly?

Many are. East Nashville Beer Works has a playground rivaling local parks. TailGate Brewery is very family-friendly with a full food menu. Most taprooms welcome dogs on their patios.

What does a pint cost at Nashville breweries?

Pints typically run $6-$9 depending on style and ABV. Flights (4-5 tasters) cost $10-$15. Budget $20-$30 per person for a few drinks.

Where is the best brewery neighborhood in Nashville?

Germantown has the highest concentration with Bearded Iris and Monday Night Preservation Co. within walking distance. East Nashville around Trinity Lane is another hotspot.

Do Nashville breweries serve food?

Many do. Tennessee Brew Works and TailGate have full menus. Others like Bearded Iris and Barrique host rotating food trucks. Smith & Lentz has a full pizza program.

What is the oldest brewery in Nashville?

Blackstone Brewing opened in 1994, making it Nashville's oldest surviving craft brewery. Yazoo Brewing, founded in 2003, is the city's most established mid-size brewery.
