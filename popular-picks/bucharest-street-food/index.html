#1

Caru' cu Bere

Traditional Romanian Historic Beer Hall
📍 NeighborhoodOld Town
💰 Price Range70-120 RON ($15-26) per person

An iconic Bucharest institution, Caru' cu Bere is famed for its stunning Gothic Revival architecture, lively atmosphere, and classic Romanian dishes. It's a must-visit for anyone wanting a comprehensive traditional dining experience, complete with live music and folk dancing in the evenings. The history here is almost as rich as the food.
