Quick answer

Accra's coffee scene is thriving, with specialty roasters, coworking cafes, and unique experiences like Ethiopian coffee ceremonies. From the stylish Cafe Kwae to the creative hub Kukun, there's a spot for every coffee lover and remote worker.

Top verdicts

  • Cafe Kwae: The stylish choice in Airport City's One Airport Square.
  • Jamestown Coffee: Local roaster championing Ghanaian coffee beans.
  • Kukun: Creative hub combining cafe, art space, and coworking.

Accra's coffee culture has evolved dramatically in recent years, moving beyond instant Nescafe to embrace specialty roasters, artisanal brewing methods, and unique cafe experiences.

Ghana may be famous for cocoa, but its coffee scene is catching up fast. You'll find everything from Portuguese-inspired espresso bars like Vida e Caffe to local champions like Jamestown Coffee Company roasting Ghanaian beans on-site. For remote workers, spots like Kukun and Cafe Kwae offer reliable Wi-Fi and comfortable workspaces.

The most unique experience? Simret Ethiopian Restaurant's traditional coffee ceremony, where beans are roasted, ground, and brewed tableside in an elaborate ritual that offers cultural immersion alongside exceptional coffee.

We analyzed reviews across TripAdvisor, Google, local food blogs, and expat forums to build this guide, cross-referencing with recommendations from Time Out Accra and Lonely Planet.
