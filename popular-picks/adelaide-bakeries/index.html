Top verdicts

  • Abbots & Kinney: Creative pastry lovers and Instagram-worthy baked goods
  • Prove Patisserie: Serious sourdough enthusiasts and pastry connoisseurs
  • The Lost Loaf: Serious bread lovers seeking premium French-style bakes

The city's bakeries span the spectrum from Abbots & Kinney's Instagram-famous pastries to Perryman's 160-year-old heritage bakery. You'll find 48-hour fermented sourdough at Prove Patisserie, authentic German strudel at Hahndorf's German Cake Shop, and a 24-hour institution at Bakery on O'Connell for those late-night pie cravings.

What sets Adelaide apart is the quality-to-price ratio — expect to pay $5-7 AUD for croissants that would cost double in Sydney, and $8-12 AUD for sourdough loaves that rival any European bakery.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best bakery in Adelaide?

Based on local consensus, Abbots & Kinney is considered one of the best overall for creative pastries and croissants, with Prove Patisserie and The Lost Loaf close behind for artisan sourdough. For traditional pies and pastries, Bakery on O'Connell and Perryman's Bakery are local institutions.

Where can I get the best croissants in Adelaide?

The best croissants in Adelaide can be found at Abbots & Kinney (creative flavors like cinnabon), Prove Patisserie (classic butter croissants), The Lost Loaf (French Lescure butter croissants), and Boulangerie 113 (authentic French-style almond croissants). Jenny's Bakery is famous for their viral pistachio croissant.

Which Adelaide bakery is open 24 hours?

Bakery on O'Connell in North Adelaide is Adelaide's only 24-hour bakery, open 7 days a week. They bake over 200 products on premises including pies, pasties, donuts, cakes, and bread — perfect for late-night cravings or early morning breakfast runs.

Where can I find the best sourdough bread in Adelaide?

The best sourdough in Adelaide comes from Prove Patisserie (48-hour fermented loaves), The Lost Loaf (36-hour labor of love in rye, wholewheat, and seeded varieties), Dough at Adelaide Central Market, and Boulangerie 113 with their award-winning artisan sourdough.

Is Hahndorf worth visiting for bakeries?

Absolutely. The German Cake Shop in Hahndorf (30 minutes from Adelaide CBD) is a must-visit for authentic German baked goods including apple strudel, beesting cake (bienenstich), giant pretzels, and German rye bread. It's a perfect day trip combined with exploring Australia's oldest surviving German settlement.

What's the best bakery near Adelaide Central Market?

Dough at Stall 45 inside Adelaide Central Market is the top choice. They specialize in artisan sourdough, French patisserie-style croissants, baguettes, and tarts. The market is open Tuesday to Saturday.

How much do bakery items cost in Adelaide?

Adelaide bakeries offer excellent value. A butter croissant costs $4.50-7 AUD, sourdough loaves run $8-14 AUD, meat pies are $6-9 AUD, and specialty pastries range from $6-12 AUD. Most bakeries in this guide are $ to $$ range.

Which Adelaide bakery has the best pies?

For traditional Australian pies, Bakery on O'Connell has the widest selection with over 200 products. Perryman's Bakery in North Adelaide is beloved for their meat pies made with local ingredients. For artisan pies, check out Jenny's Bakery.
