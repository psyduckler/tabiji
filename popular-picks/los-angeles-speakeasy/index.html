Quick answer

Los Angeles's speakeasy scene is a captivating journey into the city's clandestine past, where hidden doors lead to dimly lit havens of exquisite cocktails and timeless ambiance. Beyond the glitz and glamour, these secretive spots offer an intimate escape from the bustling city, promising an evening of discovery and expertly crafted libations.

  • The Varnish: Those seeking a true, unpretentious speakeasy vibe with perfectly executed classic drinks.

Rooted deeply in the Prohibition era, the speakeasy tradition found a fertile ground in a city known for its rebellious spirit and love for a good story. What started as illicit drinking dens during the 1920s has evolved into a sophisticated subculture, blending historical charm with modern mixology. Each bar tells its own tale, often through intricate decor, secret entrances, and a palpable sense of exclusivity.

From classic Art Deco lounges serving timeless concoctions to modern, experimental bars pushing the boundaries of mixology, LA's speakeasy landscape is incredibly diverse. You’ll find everything from barbershop fronts hiding bustling bars to grand, multi-room theatrical experiences, each offering a unique twist on the hidden bar concept. Whether you crave a quiet corner for conversation or a lively atmosphere with live music, there's a clandestine spot waiting to be uncovered.

Frequently asked questions

What is the average price range for a cocktail at an LA speakeasy?

You can generally expect to pay between $15 and $25 per cocktail at most Los Angeles speakeasies. Some establishments with highly experimental or premium spirits might go up to $30-40 per drink, while a few more casual spots might offer options closer to $12-15. This price reflects the quality of ingredients, expert craftsmanship, and the unique ambiance.

Are there any dress codes for Los Angeles speakeasies?

Many LA speakeasies, especially those with an upscale or theatrical theme like The Edison or No Vacancy, have dress codes. These typically range from 'upscale casual' to 'cocktail attire,' meaning no sportswear, shorts, flip-flops, or excessively casual clothing. It's always best to check the specific venue's website or social media for their current policy to avoid being turned away.

How do I find the secret entrance to an LA speakeasy?

Finding the secret entrance is part of the fun! Many speakeasies are hidden behind unassuming fronts like barbershops (Blind Barber), old delis (The Varnish), or even refrigerator doors (Good Times at Davey Wayne's). Look for subtle clues on their websites or social media, or listen to insider tips. Sometimes, a discreet sign or an unlabelled door is your only hint.

Do I need reservations for LA speakeasies?

It varies greatly by venue. For smaller, more intimate spots or those on weekdays, you might be able to walk in. However, for popular weekend nights, larger groups, or venues that offer dining or specific entertainment (like Apotheke or Employees Only), reservations are highly recommended and sometimes mandatory to ensure entry without a long wait.

Are LA speakeasies just for drinks, or do they serve food?

While the primary focus of most speakeasies is on exceptional cocktails, many also offer a curated food menu. This can range from small bites and appetizers (e.g., The Varnish's connection to Cole's) to full late-night dining options at places like Employees Only. Check the individual venue's website for their food offerings if you plan to eat.

What's the best speakeasy for a first-timer in Los Angeles?

For a memorable first-time experience, The Varnish is an excellent choice for its authentic classic vibe and exceptional drinks. If you're looking for something more theatrical and visually stunning, No Vacancy offers an unforgettable immersion into the speakeasy world with entertainment and unique entries.

Can I go to an LA speakeasy if I'm under 21?

No, all speakeasies in Los Angeles are bars or bar-focused establishments, and as such, they strictly enforce a 21+ age policy. You will need a valid government-issued ID to enter, regardless of whether you plan to drink alcohol.

Are LA speakeasies accessible for people with disabilities?

Accessibility varies significantly due to the nature of hidden entrances and historic buildings. Some venues might have stairs or less accommodating entryways. It's always best to call the specific speakeasy in advance to inquire about their accessibility features, such as ramps, elevators, or accessible restrooms, to ensure a comfortable visit.
