Frequently asked questions

Is craft beer popular in Zurich?

Zurich has a growing craft beer scene that's matured significantly since 2015. While Switzerland is traditionally known for wine and its mainstream lagers (Feldschlösschen, Quöllfrisch), the city now has 10+ dedicated craft beer bars and several microbreweries with taprooms. Kreis 4 and Kreis 5 are the main neighborhoods for craft beer, with Bierwerk Züri near the Hauptbahnhof as the most accessible starting point.

How much does craft beer cost in Zurich?

Expect to pay CHF 7–12 ($8–$14 USD) per pint at Zurich craft beer bars. Zurich is one of the world's most expensive cities, and beer prices reflect this. Bierwerk Züri is considered reasonably priced by local standards. Happy hour deals are rare but some bars offer reduced prices on slower weeknights. A 3dl (300ml) pour typically runs CHF 5–7.

What are the best Swiss craft beer brands to try?

Top Swiss craft breweries to seek out: BIERlab — Zurich's own microbrewery known for rotating NEIPAs and sours. Dr. Brauwolf — small-batch Zurich brewery with cult following. Brasserie de la Pièce — Geneva-based with excellent saisons. Brausyndikat — just outside Zurich, innovative and wild. Chopfab — from Winterthur, one of Switzerland's largest craft operations. Luzerner Bier — from Lucerne with a strong traditional range. St. Laurentius in Bülach — a Reddit favorite for beer quality.

What is the best area in Zurich for craft beer?

Kreis 4 and Kreis 5 (Zurich's Langstrasse area and Industriequartier) have the highest concentration of craft beer spots — Hafenkneipe, NORDEN Bar, BIERlab, and Eldorado are all within walking distance. The area around Hauptbahnhof (main station) has Bierwerk Züri and The International Beer Bar within minutes. For a brewery experience, head to Oerlikon in the north for Brauerei Oerlikon's taproom.

Can you do a craft beer bar crawl in Zurich?

Absolutely. The best route through Kreis 4/5: Start at Bierwerk Züri (near HB, opens 2pm) → walk to The International Beer Bar (10 min) → Eldorado (5 min on Limmatstrasse) → Hafenkneipe (8 min) → NORDEN Bar (5 min) → BIERlab (10 min). That's 6 world-class beer spots in roughly 2 hours of walking. All are within Zurich's compact city center.

Are Zurich craft beer bars English-friendly?

Very much so. Zurich is highly international and English is widely spoken. All the bars on this list have English-speaking staff and most have English menus or beer descriptions. The International Beer Bar is explicitly international in its focus. The Alehouse has strong Anglo-pub roots. Craft beer culture in Zurich attracts a diverse crowd of expats, locals, and tourists.

When is the best time to visit Zurich for craft beer?

The annual Zurich Craft Beer Festival (usually held in autumn) is the city's biggest beer event with dozens of Swiss and international breweries pouring. Summer is great for outdoor beer garden sessions — Fork & Bottle, The Alehouse, and Hafenkneipe all have outdoor seating. Winter is perfect for cozy taproom sessions at BIERlab or Dr. Brauwolf. Most bars open from 4–5pm on weekdays and earlier on weekends.
