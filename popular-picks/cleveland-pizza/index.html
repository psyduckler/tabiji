Quick answer

Cleveland's pizza landscape is far more diverse and delicious than many outsiders might expect, boasting a rich blend of traditional pizzerias and innovative new establishments that rival any major food city. From generations-old family recipes to avant-garde creations, the Forest City serves up slices and whole pies that cater to every palate and preference.

Top verdicts

  • Il Rione: Those seeking an authentic Neapolitan pizza experience in a lively, upscale casual setting, perfect for a date night or group dinner.
  • Citizen Pie: Foodies looking for creative pizza combinations and a vibrant atmosphere, ideal for a casual lunch or dinner with friends.
  • Angelo's Pizza: Families and groups looking for consistently delicious, generously topped classic American pizza in a welcoming, casual setting.

The city's pizza heritage is deeply rooted in its strong Italian-American immigrant history, particularly in neighborhoods like Little Italy, where classic red-sauce joints first brought Neapolitan and then Americanized versions of pizza to the forefront. As the city evolved, so did its pizza, absorbing influences from various culinary trends and a growing appreciation for artisanal ingredients and diverse crust styles.

Today, Cleveland offers an incredible array of pizza variations. You'll find delicate, wood-fired Neapolitan pizzas with blistered crusts, hearty New York-style slices perfect for folding, deep-dish Detroit-style squares with crispy cheese edges, and unique Cleveland-style pies that often feature generous toppings and a slightly sweeter sauce. There's also a burgeoning scene of gourmet and specialty pizzas at local breweries and upscale eateries.

Frequently asked questions

What style of pizza is most popular in Cleveland?

Cleveland's pizza scene is quite diverse, but classic American-style pizzas with a medium-thick crust and generous toppings remain extremely popular. There's also a strong and growing appreciation for authentic Neapolitan wood-fired pizzas, which you can find at several top-rated establishments like Il Rione and Citizen Pie.

Are there any good Detroit-style pizza options in Cleveland?

Yes, Detroit-style pizza has gained significant popularity in Cleveland. Pizzeria DiLauro in Lakewood is highly recommended for its authentic square pies with crispy, cheesy edges and fluffy crust. Several other pizzerias also offer Detroit-style options on their menus.

Can I find gluten-free or vegan pizza options in Cleveland?

Many pizzerias in Cleveland are accommodating to dietary restrictions. Most modern American and Neapolitan pizzerias offer gluten-free crusts as an alternative, though cross-contamination can occur. Vegan options, typically involving dairy-free cheese and vegetable toppings, are also increasingly available, especially at places like Citizen Pie and Saucy Brew Works.

What's the typical price range for a pizza in Cleveland?

The price for a pizza in Cleveland can vary depending on the style and establishment. A large classic American or New York-style pizza might range from $18-$25, while a gourmet Neapolitan pizza, often smaller in size, could be $16-$22. Expect to pay around $15-$30 per person for a full meal with drinks.

Do Cleveland pizzerias offer delivery or takeout?

Yes, almost all pizzerias in Cleveland offer takeout, and many also provide delivery services, either directly or through third-party apps like DoorDash, Uber Eats, and Grubhub. Checking the specific restaurant's website or preferred delivery platform is the best way to confirm options and availability for your location.

What are some must-try pizza toppings unique to Cleveland?

While not strictly unique, Clevelanders have a strong love for classic pepperoni, often served in 'cup and char' style. You'll also find local twists like Chicken Paprikash pizza at Angelo's, blending traditional Hungarian-Cleveland flavors with pizza. Many gourmet spots also feature local cheeses and seasonal produce.

Is it easy to find parking near Cleveland's best pizza places?

Parking accessibility varies by neighborhood. In areas like Ohio City or Downtown, street parking can be tight and metered, or you may need to use a parking garage. In suburban locations like Lakewood or University Heights (e.g., Angelo's, Geraci's), there's often more dedicated lot parking or easier street parking available. Always check ahead or plan for a short walk.

Are there any pizza festivals or events in Cleveland?

Cleveland occasionally hosts food festivals that feature pizza vendors, such as the Taste of Tremont or larger city-wide food events. While there isn't a dedicated 'Cleveland Pizza Fest' annually, keep an eye on local event listings from Cleveland Scene or Destination Cleveland for pop-ups and culinary celebrations that might include your favorite pie.
