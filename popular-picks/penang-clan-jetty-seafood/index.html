#1

Hai Boey Seafood (海尾海鲜)

Malaysian Seafood Chinese Seafood Al Fresco
📍 NeighborhoodWeld Quay
💰 Price RangeMYR 50-120 per person (~$11-26)

Hai Boey Seafood is a bustling open-air eatery known for its incredibly fresh seafood, often displayed live for you to pick. Located right on the water's edge at Weld Quay, it offers fantastic views and a lively atmosphere. Their cooking style focuses on bringing out the natural sweetness of the seafood with traditional Malaysian and Chinese preparations.
