Quick answer

Penang's legendary cendol, ranging from approximately RM3 to RM8 ($0.65-$1.75 USD), is best experienced at the heritage stalls of Penang Road, known for their rich santan and bold gula melaka. Since 1936, this simple dessert of shaved ice, pandan jelly, coconut milk, and palm sugar has been a pillar of Malaysian food culture, with the best versions extending beyond the famous George Town stalls.

Penang's cendol is legendary for a reason. The combination of shaved ice, silky pandan jelly noodles, fragrant gula melaka, and rich coconut milk has made this simple dessert a pillar of Malaysian food culture. George Town's Penang Road has been the epicenter since 1936, but the best cendol in Penang extends far beyond one famous stall.

Frequently asked questions

What is cendol?

Cendol is a Southeast Asian dessert of shaved ice, pandan jelly, coconut milk, and palm sugar.

Where is the original Penang Road cendol?

At 27-29 Lebuh Keng Kwee, George Town. Since 1936.

How much does cendol cost?

RM3-RM5 ($0.65-$1.10 USD).

Penang or Melaka cendol?

Both excellent but different.

Best time for cendol?

10-11 AM to 5-6 PM.

Cendol vs ice kacang?

Cendol has pandan jelly and gula melaka; ice kacang has more toppings.

Cendol durian?

Available June-August.
