Bangkok is now one of the most exciting natural wine cities in Asia — and the scene is still accelerating.

Backed by tax reforms, a new generation of Thai sommeliers, and a clientele that embraces experimentation, the city has gone from zero dedicated natural wine bars to over a dozen in just a few years. The movement started with About Eatery pioneering the concept near Asoke, then exploded as Mod Kaew, No Bar, and Unscene brought low-intervention wines to neighborhoods across the city.

What makes Bangkok special is how naturally (pun intended) these wines pair with the local cuisine. The acidity of a pet-nat cuts through rich curries, orange wines stand up to lemongrass and galangal, and a chilled skin-contact white is revelatory with som tum. Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is natural wine?

Natural wine is made with minimal intervention in both the vineyard and the cellar. This typically means grapes are organically or biodynamically farmed, fermentation occurs with native yeasts, and there are no or very few additives (like sulfur dioxide) used during winemaking. The result is often a more vibrant, unfiltered, and expressive wine.

Is natural wine more expensive than regular wine in Bangkok?

Natural wines can be slightly more expensive due to artisanal production methods and smaller batch sizes. In Bangkok, expect to pay 300–800 baht per glass and 1,200–4,000 baht per bottle at most natural wine bars. However, spots like Mod Kaew keep markups low, and No Bar Wine Bar offers approachable options. The price gap is shrinking as natural wine gains popularity in Thailand.

Can I find natural wine in Bangkok outside of dedicated bars?

Yes. Beyond dedicated natural wine bars, many high-end restaurants, gourmet delis like VIVIN Grocery, and specialty bottle shops now stock a variety of natural wines. Earth House restaurant has an excellent natural wine list alongside its farm-to-table menu. Look for 'organic', 'biodynamic', or 'low-intervention' labels, or ask a knowledgeable staff member for recommendations.

What food pairs best with natural wine in Bangkok?

Natural wines pair beautifully with Thai and Southeast Asian cuisine. Lighter, fruitier natural reds and orange wines complement spicy Thai dishes wonderfully, while crisp natural whites and pet-nats are fantastic with seafood. Many Bangkok wine bars like About Eatery and Earth House design menus specifically around natural wine pairings. Do not be afraid to experiment.

Are natural wines always cloudy or fizzy?

Not necessarily. While some natural wines are intentionally left unfiltered (which can give them a cloudy appearance) or are pet-nats (naturally sparkling), many are clear and still. The diverse styles of natural winemaking mean you can find everything from crisp, clean whites to robust, age-worthy reds. Places like Must Wine Bar have over 600 labels spanning all styles.

What is the best neighborhood for natural wine bars in Bangkok?

The Sukhumvit corridor from Asoke to Thonglor is the epicenter, with About Eatery (Asoke), Unscene (Soi 49), Must Wine Bar and Swirl (both Thonglor 13), and Salon Kiku (Soi 51) all within reach. The Ari neighborhood has No Bar Wine Bar for a more local feel, while Sathorn's Mod Kaew and Smalls offer excellent options south of the river.

Do Bangkok natural wine bars accept walk-ins or do I need reservations?

Most Bangkok natural wine bars are casual and accept walk-ins, especially early in the evening. However, smaller spots like Salon Kiku (seats around 30) and Earth House can fill up quickly on weekends. Mod Kaew's new Sathorn location has more space but still gets busy on Friday and Saturday nights. For weekend visits, messaging ahead on Instagram or Facebook is recommended.

What is orange wine and where can I try it in Bangkok?

Orange wine is made from white grapes that are fermented with their skins, giving the wine an amber or orange hue and more tannic structure. It is a natural wine staple and pairs exceptionally well with Thai food. Salon Kiku is known for its edgy orange wine selection (they hosted Bangkok's 'Call It Amber' week), while No Bar Wine Bar, Mod Kaew, and Unscene all regularly feature orange wines by the glass.

Planning your Bangkok natural wine crawl

The ideal Bangkok wine bar crawl covers three distinct neighborhoods in one evening, using BTS and a short taxi ride.

Start early at Swirl in Thonglor 13 (opens 2:30 PM) to browse the 1,000-bottle cellar and taste by the glass. Walk next door to Must Wine Bar when it opens at 5 PM for a flight of their award-winning natural wine selection. Take the BTS north to Ari for No Bar Wine Bar's intimate setting and Thai fusion food pairing.

For a different route, begin at About Eatery near Asoke for the pioneer experience with Italian food, then cab south to Mod Kaew on Sathorn Soi 12 for the award-winning main event. End the night at Smalls on Suan Phlu for jazz and a final glass.

Weekend warriors should reserve Earth House (Wed–Sun) for a farm-to-table dinner with biodynamic pairings, or seek out Salon Kiku on a Friday for orange wine and DJs. Budget roughly ฿2,000–5,000 ($55–$140 USD) per person for a full evening across 2–3 bars including food.
