Perched above Jabal Amman's historic First Circle, Canteloupe offers one of the city's most iconic rooftop experiences. Its multi-level terraces provide spectacular panoramic views, especially at sunset, overlooking the Citadel and downtown. The ambiance is chic yet relaxed, making it perfect for both sophisticated dinners and lively evening drinks.
