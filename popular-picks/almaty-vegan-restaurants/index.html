Almaty might seem like an unlikely destination for vegan travelers — after all, Kazakhstan is famous for its nomadic meat-heavy cuisine. But the country's largest city has quietly developed a thriving plant-based food scene, driven by health-conscious locals and the city's diverse international community.

The options range from dedicated vegan restaurants like Plants Kitchen & Bar to long-standing vegetarian institutions like Govinda's. Indian restaurants offer some of the most reliable vegan choices, with Tandoor, Spice Mantra, and Magic Masala all serving extensive meat-free menus. Middle Eastern spots like Harissa provide excellent hummus, falafel, and mezze platters.

Georgian restaurants are a pleasant surprise — dishes like lobio (bean stew), ajapsandali (vegetable stew), and cheese-free khachapuri variations make them unexpectedly vegan-friendly. We analyzed recommendations from HappyCow, TripAdvisor, The Steppe magazine, and various travel blogs to compile this guide.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it easy to find vegan food in Almaty?

While Kazakhstan is traditionally a meat-heavy cuisine, Almaty has seen a growing number of vegan-friendly restaurants in recent years. You'll find dedicated vegetarian cafes like Govinda's and Plants Kitchen & Bar, plus excellent vegan options at Indian restaurants like Tandoor and Spice Mantra. Middle Eastern spots like Harissa also offer abundant plant-based mezze dishes.

What's the best fully vegan restaurant in Almaty?

Plants Kitchen & Bar is Almaty's premier 100% vegan restaurant, offering everything from vegan burgers and pizzas to ramen and desserts. Located inside Forum mall, it's popular with both vegans and curious locals. For a more spiritual dining experience, Govinda's Vegetarian Cafe serves sattvic Indian food without onions or garlic.

How much does vegan food cost in Almaty?

Vegan dining in Almaty is very affordable. Budget options like Govinda's offer full meals for ₸1,500-2,500 (about $3-5 USD). Mid-range restaurants like Harissa and Spice Mantra run ₸3,000-6,000 per person. Upscale health food spots like The GreenHouse charge ₸5,000-10,000 for specialty dishes.

Are Indian restaurants in Almaty good for vegans?

Yes! Indian restaurants are among the best options for vegans in Almaty. Tandoor, Spice Mantra, and Magic Masala all offer extensive vegetarian menus with clearly marked vegan options. Many dishes can be made vegan upon request. Govinda's is entirely vegetarian and offers many vegan-friendly choices.

What traditional Kazakh food is vegan?

Traditional Kazakh cuisine is heavily meat-based, making it challenging for vegans. However, you can find baursak (fried dough), some flatbreads, and vegetable salads. Georgian restaurants like Daredzhani offer lobio (bean stew) and vegetable dishes. For authentic local flavors without meat, try the vegetable plov at Navat or ajapsandali (vegetable stew) at Georgian spots.

Where can I find hummus and falafel in Almaty?

Harissa is the go-to spot for Middle Eastern vegan food in Almaty, offering excellent hummus, falafel, and a wide variety of mezze. The restaurant is known for being extremely vegan-friendly with clearly labeled options. Lebanese restaurants in the city also serve good mezze platters with plenty of plant-based choices.

What neighborhoods in Almaty have the most vegan options?

The central area around Abylai Khan Avenue and Tole Bi Street has the highest concentration of vegan-friendly restaurants, including Govinda's, The GreenHouse, Tandoor, and Spice Mantra. The Dostyk area also has good options. Most restaurants on this list are within walking distance of each other in central Almaty.

Can I order vegan food delivery in Almaty?

Yes! Most restaurants on this list offer delivery through Wolt, Glovo, or Yandex Eda. Plants Kitchen & Bar, Harissa, and Spice Mantra are all available on delivery apps. Some restaurants like Govinda's may have more limited delivery options, so calling ahead is recommended.
