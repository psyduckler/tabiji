Quick answer

Las Vegas isn't just a city of bright lights and high stakes; it's a world-class destination for unparalleled fine dining experiences. From Michelin-starred temples of gastronomy to innovative culinary adventures, the city's food scene challenges preconceived notions and delights the most discerning palates.

Best overall
Joël Robuchon
Top pick
Joël Robuchon — Unmatched French haute cuisine and service.

Top verdicts

  • Joël Robuchon: Celebrations, romantic evenings, or anyone seeking the absolute pinnacle of French haute cuisine and a truly unforgettable dining experience.
  • Guy Savoy: Discerning diners who appreciate classic French techniques with contemporary flair, ideal for romantic dinners or significant celebrations.
  • Picasso: Romantic evenings, art lovers, or anyone looking for a visually spectacular dining experience with world-class cuisine.

The city's evolution into a culinary powerhouse began decades ago, with pioneering chefs bringing European sophistication to the desert. Over time, this grew into a vibrant culture of culinary innovation, attracting top talent and pushing the boundaries of what's possible in resort dining, making it a competitive global culinary hub.

Today, Vegas boasts an incredible array of fine dining styles. Whether you crave classic French haute cuisine, avant-garde Spanish molecular gastronomy, authentic Japanese omakase, or perfectly seared steaks with a view, you'll find an establishment catering to every exquisite taste and preference.

Frequently asked questions

What is the typical dress code for fine dining in Las Vegas?

Most fine dining establishments in Las Vegas enforce a business casual or smart casual dress code. This usually means collared shirts and slacks for men, and dresses, skirts, or dressy slacks for women. Jackets are often recommended for men at the most exclusive places like Joël Robuchon or Guy Savoy. Avoid shorts, t-shirts, flip-flops, and athletic wear.

Are children allowed in these fine dining restaurants?

While policies vary, many ultra-fine dining restaurants maintain an adult-oriented atmosphere and may not be suitable for young children, or even restrict diners to ages 5 or older. It's always best to check with the specific restaurant when making a reservation if you plan to bring children.

How far in advance should I make reservations for fine dining in Las Vegas?

For top-tier fine dining, especially Michelin-starred venues or those with limited seating like é by José Andrés, reservations should be made as far in advance as possible, often 2-3 months out, particularly for weekend evenings or holidays. Other popular spots can often be booked 2-4 weeks in advance.

Do these restaurants cater to dietary restrictions or allergies?

Yes, high-end fine dining restaurants are typically well-equipped to handle most dietary restrictions and allergies. It is crucial to inform the restaurant of any dietary needs when making your reservation and again upon arrival. Chefs are usually happy to adapt dishes or suggest suitable alternatives.

Is tipping customary for fine dining in Las Vegas?

Yes, tipping is customary and expected in Las Vegas fine dining. A gratuity of 18-20% of the total bill (before tax) is standard for excellent service. Some restaurants may automatically add a service charge for larger parties, so always check your bill.

Are there any excellent fine dining options off the Las Vegas Strip?

Absolutely! While many top establishments are on the Strip, places like Raku in Chinatown offer an exceptional fine dining experience with a distinct local vibe, specializing in Japanese robatayaki. Vetri Cucina at the Palms also provides incredible Italian cuisine with stunning panoramic views away from the Strip's immediate bustle.

Can I expect vegetarian or vegan options at these upscale venues?

Most fine dining restaurants can accommodate vegetarian and often vegan diets, though the extent of options will vary. Many chefs are skilled in creating bespoke plant-based dishes with advance notice. Always communicate your dietary preferences when booking and with your server upon arrival to ensure a tailored and delightful experience.

What's the best way to get to these restaurants if I'm staying far away?

If you're staying far from your chosen restaurant, ride-sharing services like Uber or Lyft, or traditional taxis, are the most convenient options. On the Strip, some resorts are connected by free trams, and the Deuce bus runs frequently. Always factor in potential traffic and walking distances within large resorts when planning your travel time.
