  • Skybar 25: Accra's most iconic elevated bar with the highest vantage point in the city.
  • The Lexington: The best rooftop pool-and-bar combo for daytime through sunset.
  • The Republic: Where Accra's creative crowd gathers for craft cocktails and live DJ sets.

Accra's rooftop bar scene has exploded over the past five years. What was once limited to hotel terraces now includes purpose-built sky lounges, poolside cocktail bars, and open-air restaurants perched above the city's most vibrant neighborhoods.

The city's warm tropical climate means rooftop drinking is a year-round activity, though the dry season (November through March) offers the clearest skies and most comfortable evenings. Most rooftop bars cluster around three neighborhoods: Airport Residential Area (the upscale hub), Osu (the nightlife epicenter), and Ridge (the hotel district).

Prices in Accra's rooftop bars reflect the city's growing luxury market. Premium venues like Skybar 25 and The View Bar command cocktail prices of GHS 100-180, while mid-range spots in Osu offer solid drinks in the GHS 50-100 range. Local beers (Club, Star, Guinness Foreign Extra) are available everywhere and remain affordable at GHS 25-50.

We compiled this guide from travel blogs, TripAdvisor rankings, local nightlife guides, and expat community recommendations to identify the 10 rooftop bars that consistently deliver on views, drinks, and atmosphere.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best rooftop bar in Accra?

Skybar 25 is the consensus best rooftop bar in Accra. Located on the 25th floor of the Villaggio Vista tower in Airport Residential Area, it offers 360-degree panoramic views, expert cocktails, and an atmosphere that is hard to match anywhere else in West Africa.

What is the best time to visit rooftop bars in Accra?

The dry season from November through March offers the most comfortable rooftop drinking weather, with clear skies and lower humidity. On any given night, arriving around 5:30 PM gives you the best chance to catch a stunning Accra sunset from your elevated perch.

What is the dress code for rooftop bars in Accra?

Premium venues like Skybar 25 and The View Bar enforce smart-casual dress codes (no shorts, flip-flops, or athletic wear). Mid-range spots like Plot Seven, The Republic, and Bella Roma are more relaxed but still expect presentable attire. When in doubt, smart casual is the safe choice.

How much do drinks cost at Accra's rooftop bars?

Cocktails at premium venues like Skybar 25 run GHS 120-250 (about $8-17 USD). Mid-range spots charge GHS 50-130 per cocktail. Local beers (Club, Star, Guinness Foreign Extra) cost GHS 25-50 everywhere. Budget about GHS 200-400 per person for a night out including a couple of drinks and a snack at a mid-range bar.

Do I need reservations at Accra rooftop bars?

Reservations are strongly recommended for Skybar 25 on Friday and Saturday evenings, and for The View Bar at Kempinski on weekends. Most other bars operate on a first-come, first-served basis. For groups of six or more, calling ahead is always a smart move regardless of the venue.

Are Accra's rooftop bars safe for tourists?

Yes. All bars in this guide are located in well-established, secure neighborhoods with good security. Use ride-hailing apps like Bolt or Uber for transport rather than flagging taxis on the street, keep your valuables secure, and avoid walking alone late at night in unfamiliar areas.

Which Accra rooftop bars have pool access?

The Lexington Rooftop Pool & Bar is the best option for combining rooftop drinks with a pool dip. The Altitude at Tang Palace Hotel also offers poolside service. Both may charge a day-pass fee for non-hotel guests, which can often be credited toward food and drink purchases.

What is the best budget rooftop bar in Accra?

Gold Coast Restaurant & Cocktail Bar on Oxford Street in Osu offers a genuine rooftop experience with cocktails starting around GHS 50-70 and local beers from GHS 25. The Republic Bar & Grill also offers great value, especially during their happy hour deals on weekday evenings.
