Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best rooftop bar in Accra?

Skybar 25 is widely considered Accra's premier rooftop bar. Located atop the Alto Tower in Villaggio Vista — one of West Africa's tallest buildings — it offers unparalleled 360-degree panoramic views of the city. The venue is members-only but accepts tourist reservations.

How much do drinks cost at Accra rooftop bars?

Cocktails at Accra rooftop bars typically range from GHS 60 to GHS 150 ($4-10 USD). Premium venues like Skybar 25 and Enigma Sky Lounge are at the higher end, while more casual spots like The Hayvin and Esuro offer drinks starting around GHS 50-70.

Do I need reservations for rooftop bars in Accra?

Reservations are essential for Skybar 25 and highly recommended for Enigma Sky Lounge and Luna Rooftop Bar, especially on weekends. Most venues get busy after 9pm on Friday and Saturday nights.

What is the dress code for rooftop bars in Accra?

Upscale venues like Skybar 25, Enigma Sky Lounge, and Carbon require smart-casual attire — no slippers, flip-flops, or beachwear. More relaxed spots like The Hayvin and Clubsmooth are less strict but still favor fashionable casualwear.

Which rooftop bars have the best sunset views?

Helicopter Bar at Suncity Hotel has the best unobstructed westward views for sunset. Skybar 25 offers the highest vantage point with 360-degree views. Plan to arrive by 5:30pm to secure a good spot.
