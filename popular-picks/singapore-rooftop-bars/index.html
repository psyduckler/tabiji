Singapore's sky-high bar scene is world-class and brutally competitive. We dug through r/askSingapore, r/singapore, and r/SGNightLife to find which rooftops locals actually recommend — and which are overpriced tourist traps.

Quick answer

Singapore's rooftop bars range from SGD 15-40+ per cocktail, with LeVeL33 offering the best value and CE LA VI providing the most iconic experience. Dress codes are strictly enforced, so smart casual attire is recommended, especially during sunset hours on weekdays or the cooler months from November to January.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best rooftop bar in Singapore for views of Marina Bay?

CE LA VI at Marina Bay Sands Level 57 is the iconic choice. HighHouse at One Raffles Place (Level 61-63) is Singapore's highest rooftop with 360-degree views. LeVeL33 pairs house-brewed craft beer with spectacular Marina Bay views. For a quieter option, Altro Zafferano on Level 43 of Ocean Financial Centre is exceptional.

How much do drinks cost at Singapore rooftop bars?

Expect SGD 18-35 per cocktail (USD 13-26). Marina Bay Sands venues like CE LA VI and LAVO are SGD 25-40+. Southbridge and LeVeL33 start around SGD 15-18. Happy hour brings prices down by SGD 5-8. Budget around SGD 80-120 per person for a mid-tier rooftop bar evening.

Do Singapore rooftop bars have dress codes?

Yes. LeVeL33 is strict (no open-toed sandals, shorts, or t-shirts for men). CE LA VI and LAVO require smart casual. The safest approach: closed-toe shoes, collared shirt or smart blouse, no shorts.

When is the best time to visit Singapore rooftop bars?

Sunset (6:30-7:30pm) is prime time. Weekday evenings are far less crowded. For CE LA VI or LAVO on Saturday nights, book well in advance. November to January offers the most comfortable outdoor weather.

Are there rooftop bars in Singapore outside Marina Bay and the CBD?

Yes. Mr. Stork is at Andaz Singapore in Bugis. Las Palmas Rooftop Bar is in Novena. Moon Rooftop Bar is in Tanjong Pagar. Lady Wu is in Boat Quay. Kinki at Customs House offers open-air waterfront dining with Marina Bay views.

Which Singapore rooftop bar has the best happy hour?

LeVeL33 consistently gets praised on Reddit for happy hour on house-brewed craft beers. For the best value-to-view ratio, LeVeL33 happy hour is hard to beat — world's highest urban craft brewery, Marina Bay views, and prices far lower than MBS venues.
