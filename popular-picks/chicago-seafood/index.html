Frequently asked questions

Is Chicago good for seafood despite being inland?

Absolutely! Despite being landlocked, Chicago has a long-standing reputation as a major transportation hub, allowing it to receive fresh seafood daily from both coasts and international waters. The city's diverse culinary scene ensures that chefs put these high-quality ingredients to excellent use, creating a surprising and robust seafood offerings.

What type of seafood is Chicago famous for?

While Chicago isn't known for a specific local catch, its seafood scene is renowned for variety and quality. Stone crab (seasonal), Alaskan king crab, and fresh oysters from both East and West Coasts are particularly popular. You'll also find exceptional preparations of classics like Chilean sea bass, salmon, and unique regional specialties from various global cuisines.

Are there any budget-friendly seafood options in Chicago?

Yes, delicious seafood in Chicago doesn't always have to break the bank. Places like Calumet Fisheries offer iconic smoked and fried fish at very affordable prices, perfect for a casual, cash-only experience. Fish Bar also provides excellent Southern-inspired fried seafood at a mid-budget price point, offering great value without sacrificing flavor.

What are the best neighborhoods for seafood in Chicago?

River North is arguably the hub for upscale seafood dining, boasting establishments like RPM Seafood, Shaw's Crab House, and Joe's Seafood. Lincoln Park offers a mix of mid-range and casual options, including Oyster Bah and Fish Bar. The Gold Coast is home to elegant choices like Hugo's Frog Bar and Nico Osteria, while the Loop caters to business diners with spots like Catch 35.

Do Chicago seafood restaurants accommodate dietary restrictions?

Most reputable seafood restaurants in Chicago are accustomed to handling various dietary restrictions and allergies. It's always best to inform your server upon arrival, or even mention it when making a reservation. Many establishments can offer gluten-free, dairy-free, or vegetarian options, especially given the natural versatility of seafood and fresh produce.

Can I find fresh oysters in Chicago?

Yes, absolutely! Chicago has a thriving oyster scene. Many upscale seafood restaurants and dedicated oyster bars, such as Shaw's Crab House and Oyster Bah, offer extensive raw bar selections with daily rotating varieties from different regions. Happy hour deals on oysters are also a popular feature at many spots.

Is it better to make reservations for seafood restaurants in Chicago?

For most mid-to-upscale seafood restaurants, especially for dinner on weekends or during peak seasons, reservations are highly recommended and often essential. Popular spots like RPM Seafood and Joe's Seafood can book up weeks in advance. More casual eateries might operate on a walk-in basis, but you might encounter wait times.

Are there seafood places good for families with kids?

While many upscale seafood restaurants are more geared towards adults, places like Shaw's Crab House often have a more accommodating atmosphere and menu for families. Casual spots like Fish Bar can also be excellent choices. Calumet Fisheries, being takeout-only, is perfect for a unique family picnic experience, weather permitting.
