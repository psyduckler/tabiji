Eggless Dessert Cafe

Modern Australian Vegetarian Dessert Bar
📍 NeighborhoodGoodwood
💰 Price RangeAUD $20-35 per person / USD $13-23

An Adelaide institution, Eggless Dessert Cafe is renowned for its innovative, vegetarian-friendly desserts that change seasonally. Expect a cozy, intimate atmosphere and dishes that are as beautiful as they are delicious. It's a walk-in-only spot with a loyal following, so be prepared for a potential wait – it's always worth it.
