# Best Tannery Viewpoints in Fez

Fez, Morocco is home to the world's oldest and most famous tanneries. The Chouara Tannery is a must-see for any visitor to Fez, but finding a good viewpoint can be a challenge. Here are the best ways to get a great view of the tanneries.

## The Leather Shops

The best way to get a great view of the tanneries is from the balconies of the surrounding leather shops. These shops are built around the tanneries and offer a panoramic view of the pits.

**How to get there:**

As you approach the tanneries, you will be approached by touts who will offer to take you to a shop. You can either go with one of them or simply walk into any of the shops yourself.

**What to expect:**

The shopkeepers will offer you a sprig of mint to help with the smell, which can be quite strong. They will then take you to their balcony for a view of the tanneries. After you have taken your pictures, they will take you back into the shop and begin their sales pitch. Be prepared for a hard sell, but don't feel pressured to buy anything. A small tip is appreciated if you don't purchase anything.

## Other Viewpoints

While the leather shops offer the best views, there are a few other places where you can get a glimpse of the tanneries.

*   **From the street:** You can see parts of the tanneries from the street, but the view is not as good as from the shops.
*   **From a rooftop cafe:** There are a few rooftop cafes in the area that offer a view of the tanneries. This is a good option if you want to relax and have a drink while you take in the view.

## Tips for Visiting the Tanneries

*   **Wear comfortable shoes:** You will be doing a lot of walking.
*   **Bring a scarf or handkerchief:** You can use it to cover your nose if the smell is too strong.
*   **Be prepared to haggle:** If you decide to buy something, be prepared to haggle for a good price.
*   **Enjoy the experience:** The tanneries are a unique and fascinating place. Take your time and enjoy the experience.
