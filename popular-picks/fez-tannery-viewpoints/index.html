Best Tannery Viewpoints in Fez

A Reddit-backed guide to getting the best view of the Chouara Tannery, one of the world's oldest tanneries.

Sources: r/travel, r/Morocco
Updated: March 2026

The Chouara Tannery in Fez is a remarkable sight, but finding a good viewpoint can be tricky. Here's how to get the best views, based on the experiences of other travelers.

1The Leather Shops

The best and most common way to see the tanneries is from the balconies of the leather shops that surround them. These shops offer panoramic views of the entire tannery complex.

How it works: As you get close to the tanneries, you'll likely be approached by someone offering to guide you to a shop. You can either follow them or simply walk into one of the many shops lining the alleys around the tannery. The shopkeepers will usually give you a sprig of mint to help with the strong smell and then lead you to their balcony.

The catch: After you've taken in the view, you'll be guided back into the shop for a sales pitch. Be prepared for a hard sell, but you are not obligated to buy anything. If you don't make a purchase, a small tip (20-50 MAD) is a nice gesture.

"You can get a great view from the leather shops. They all have balconies overlooking the pits. Just be prepared for the hard sell." — r/travel

2Rooftop Cafes

For a more relaxed experience, consider a rooftop cafe. Several cafes in the area have terraces that overlook the tanneries. This is a great option if you want to enjoy the view without the pressure of a sales pitch.

You'll have to buy a drink or a snack, but you can stay as long as you like. This is a good way to escape the hustle and bustle of the medina for a while.
