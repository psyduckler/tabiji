Frequently asked questions

What are the best cocktail bars in Roppongi?

The top cocktail bars in Roppongi include Bar Starman (4.8★), THESE library lounge, Musium record bar, The Bar at The Ritz-Carlton (45th floor views), BAUHAUS rock bar, Roku Nana rooftop speakeasy, and AGAVE tequila bar. Roppongi's cocktail scene ranges from intimate speakeasies to world-class hotel bars.

Are Roppongi bars safe for tourists?

The bars on this list are all legitimate, well-reviewed establishments. Roppongi has a reputation for touts and overcharging at certain clubs, but the cocktail bars here are a different world entirely. Stick to named establishments, avoid street touts, and you'll have zero issues. The Nishiazabu side (THESE, Boctok) is particularly safe and upscale.

How much do cocktails cost in Roppongi?

Cocktails in Roppongi range from ¥1,200–¥3,500 (~$8–$24 USD). Standing bars like Awabar and Bar Propaganda are on the lower end (¥1,200–¥1,800). Mid-range bars like K-Julep and Musium run ¥1,500–¥2,200. Hotel bars like The Ritz-Carlton are ¥2,500–¥3,500+. Many bars have a cover/table charge (席料) of ¥500–¥1,500.

What time do cocktail bars open in Roppongi?

Most Roppongi cocktail bars open between 6–8 PM and stay open until 2–5 AM. Bar Starman and Musium open at 8 PM and run until 4–5 AM. Bar Propaganda opens at 5 PM and goes until 5–7 AM on weekends. The Ritz-Carlton bar opens earlier at 3 PM. Many bars are closed on Sundays.

What is the dress code for Roppongi cocktail bars?

Most Roppongi cocktail bars have no strict dress code, but smart casual is the norm. The Ritz-Carlton bar expects business casual or above (no shorts, sandals, or sportswear). Bars like BAUHAUS, Bar Propaganda, and Awabar are fully casual. The general rule: if you're coming from dinner at a nice restaurant, you're dressed right for any bar on this list.

Is Roppongi or Ginza better for cocktail bars?

Ginza is Tokyo's classic cocktail district — quiet, formal, old-school bartending perfection. Roppongi is more eclectic, international, and late-night. If you want a perfectly stirred highball in reverent silence, go to Ginza. If you want tequila flights at AGAVE, vinyl at Musium, rooftop vibes at Roku Nana, and bars open until 5 AM, Roppongi wins. Many serious drinkers do both in one night — Ginza for the early evening, Roppongi for the late session.
