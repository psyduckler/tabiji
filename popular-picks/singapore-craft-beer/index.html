Quick answer

Singapore's craft beer scene has quietly become one of the best in Asia. From a hawker center stall pouring rotating international taps alongside chicken rice, to the world's highest urban microbrewery 33 floors above Marina Bay — the city offers something no other beer destination can match.

Best overall

Top verdicts

  • LeVeL33: Special occasions, sunset drinks, impressing visitors
  • Smith Street Taps: Unique experience, pairing craft beer with hawker food, budget-conscious beer lovers
  • Brewerkz: Beer history buffs, riverside drinking, groups wanting full food menu

Prices are higher than Southeast Asian neighbors (S$10–22 vs $2–5 in Bangkok), but the quality and diversity justify it. The tropical climate makes cold craft beer a welcome treat year-round.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best craft beer bar in Singapore?

LeVeL33 is the most iconic choice - it's the world's highest urban microbrewery with stunning Marina Bay views. For the most uniquely Singaporean experience, Smith Street Taps in Chinatown hawker center is unbeatable. SG Taps on Duxton Hill is best for discovering local Singapore breweries.

How much does craft beer cost in Singapore?

Craft beer in Singapore ranges from S$10-22 per pint. Hawker center spots like Smith Street Taps offer S$10-16 pints, while premium venues like LeVeL33 charge S$14-22. This is higher than Southeast Asian neighbors (S$2-5 in Bangkok) but reflects Singapore's quality and import taxes.

Where can I find local Singapore craft beer?

SG Taps on Duxton Hill dedicates 50% of their taps to local Singapore breweries. Lion Brewery Co on Club Street is a homegrown microbrewery. Little Island Brewing Co in Changi Village brews their own award-winning beers on-site.

What's the most unique craft beer experience in Singapore?

Smith Street Taps - a craft beer stall inside a traditional Chinatown hawker center. Pair rotating international taps with chicken rice and char kway teow. Orh Gao Taproom is another unique spot - named after the Hokkien term for Guinness, it transforms from a coffee shop by day to a craft beer bar at night.

Is craft beer in Singapore worth the price?

Yes - Singapore's craft beer scene offers exceptional quality and diversity, making it one of the best in Asia. The higher prices (S$10-22 vs S$2-5 in Thailand) reflect import taxes and venue quality. The tropical climate makes cold craft beer a welcome treat year-round.

What is Singapore's oldest craft brewery?

Brewerkz, founded in 1997, is Singapore's longest-running craft brewery. Located at Riverside Point by Clarke Quay, they helped kickstart Singapore's craft beer story and have since won Asia Beer Championship awards.
