Quick answer

For the quintessential Accra street food experience, head to Auntie Muni Waakye in Labone for the city's most legendary waakye — but only on weekends. For everyday options, Osu Night Market offers the full spectrum of Ghanaian street food in one bustling location.

Top verdicts

  • Auntie Muni Waakye: The most famous waakye in Accra — weekend queues are part of the experience.
  • Kenkey Plaza: 50+ years serving authentic Ga kenkey with fried fish in Accra Central.
  • Osu Night Market: The ultimate evening street food destination with kelewele, chichinga, and more.

Accra's street food scene is a vibrant, flavor-packed journey into Ghanaian culinary culture. From the legendary waakye joints of Labone to the smoky chichinga vendors of Oxford Street, eating on the streets is how Accra lives and breathes.

The city's signature dishes tell stories of cultural heritage: waakye, the beloved rice-and-beans dish colored with millet leaves, originated in the north but conquered Accra's mornings. Kenkey, the fermented corn staple of the Ga people, pairs perfectly with fried tilapia and the fiery black pepper sauce called shito. And kelewele — those addictive cubes of spicy fried plantain — are the ultimate evening snack.

We researched local food blogs, travel guides, TikTok food tours, and the Accra food community to compile this guide, prioritizing spots with consistent quality, cultural authenticity, and safe food handling practices.
