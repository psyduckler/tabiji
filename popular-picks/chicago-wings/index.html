Quick answer

From the classic Buffalo heat to unique global flavors, Chicago's wing scene is a delicious journey waiting to be explored. Whether you're a purist seeking perfectly sauced drums and flats or an adventurer craving crispy Korean fried chicken, this guide is your definitive passport to the city's top wing destinations.

Top pick

Top verdicts

  • Jake Melnick's Corner Tap: Those craving classic American bar wings with a wide variety of heat levels and a lively, sports-bar atmosphere.
  • Crisp: Anyone seeking authentic, perfectly crispy Korean fried chicken with bold, sweet, and spicy flavors.
  • Harold's Chicken Shack: Experiencing an iconic piece of Chicago food culture with classic fried wings and their famous mild sauce.

The humble chicken wing has evolved into a culinary canvas in Chicago, mirroring the city's diverse population and rich food history. While classic American sports bars have always offered their take, waves of immigration have introduced techniques and flavors from Asia, Latin America, and beyond, transforming the wing into an exciting gastronomic experience. What started as a bar snack has blossomed into a standalone craving for many.

In Chicago, you'll encounter a thrilling array of wing styles: the iconic Buffalo wing with its tangy, spicy sauce; the twice-fried, ultra-crispy Korean fried chicken (KFC) often glazed in sweet-spicy or soy-garlic concoctions; the unique Chicago-style fried chicken wings drenched in mild sauce; and fiery Szechuan variations that bring a numbing, complex heat. There's a wing for every palate and every occasion.

Frequently asked questions

What is Chicago-style fried chicken and how does it relate to wings?

Chicago-style fried chicken, famously pioneered by Harold's Chicken Shack, refers to whole or cut-up chicken pieces, including wings, that are deep-fried until extra crispy and then often served with a generous dousing of a sweet and tangy 'mild sauce'. It's a distinct local preparation that sets it apart from typical fried chicken.

Are there vegetarian or vegan wing options available in Chicago?

While traditional chicken wings are the focus, many Chicago restaurants, especially those with diverse menus, now offer plant-based alternatives. Look for cauliflower wings or seitan-based 'wings' at some gastropubs or dedicated vegan eateries. It's best to check individual restaurant menus or call ahead for specific options.

What are some popular wing sauce flavors unique to Chicago?

Beyond the standard Buffalo, Chicago is known for Harold's signature mild sauce which is unique to the city's fried chicken scene. Additionally, the Korean fried chicken spots like Crisp offer distinct sweet-spicy and soy-garlic glazes, while Szechuan spots like Lao Sze Chuan feature dry chili wings with numbing mala spice that you won't find just anywhere.

Can I find spicy wings in Chicago that are genuinely hot?

Absolutely! Chicago's wing scene caters to all heat levels. Spots like Jake Melnick's Corner Tap are famous for their XXX Hot sauces, while Big Boss Spicy Fried Chicken and Lao Sze Chuan deliver authentic Szechuan heat and numbing spice. Always ask your server about the spice level if you're unsure, but prepare for some serious heat at these venues.

Is it better to order wings bone-in or boneless in Chicago?

This is a matter of personal preference! Bone-in wings offer a more traditional experience and are often considered more flavorful due to the bone. Boneless wings, which are typically breaded chicken breast pieces, are convenient and less messy. Many places, especially those specializing in American or Korean styles, offer both options, allowing you to choose based on your preference.

What's the typical price range for a dozen wings in Chicago?

The price for a dozen wings in Chicago can vary significantly based on the establishment and style. At budget-friendly spots like Harold's or Crisp, a dozen might cost $15-$25. At mid-range pubs or sit-down restaurants such as Jake Melnick's or Piece, expect to pay $20-$35 for a dozen. Prices can fluctuate with market conditions and promotions.

Are there any wing festivals or events in Chicago?

Chicago occasionally hosts wing-themed festivals and competitions, especially during warmer months. Events like 'WingOut Chicago' or smaller neighborhood food festivals often feature local restaurants showcasing their best wings. Keep an eye on local event listings (e.g., Choose Chicago, Block Club Chicago) for upcoming dates and venues, as these are great opportunities to sample many styles.

What's the best way to travel between different wing spots across Chicago?

Chicago's public transportation, the CTA 'L' train and bus system, is efficient for navigating the city. Many top wing spots are located near 'L' stations or bus routes. Ride-sharing apps like Uber and Lyft are also readily available for direct travel. For shorter distances, walking or using Divvy bikes can be enjoyable ways to explore neighborhoods between your wing stops.
