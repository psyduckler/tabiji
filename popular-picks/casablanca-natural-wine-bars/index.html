Frequently Asked Questions

What is 'natural wine' and why is it special?

Natural wine refers to wine made with minimal intervention in both the vineyard and the cellar. This typically means organic or biodynamic farming, no artificial additives, and often relies on wild yeasts for fermentation. The result is a wine that often expresses a unique terroir and can have a vibrant, "alive" quality, offering diverse and sometimes surprising flavors not found in conventional wines.

Is it easy to find natural wine in Casablanca?

While dedicated natural wine bars are not as prevalent as in some Western cities, Casablanca's high-end restaurants and chic bistros are increasingly expanding their wine lists to include organic, biodynamic, and low-intervention options. You might need to ask your sommelier or waiter for specific recommendations, but a curated selection is often available in sophisticated venues.

What are the typical price ranges for natural wine in Casablanca?

Prices for natural wines in Casablanca vary widely based on the venue and the origin of the wine. A glass might range from 80–150 MAD (8–15 USD), while a bottle could start from 350 MAD (35 USD) for local selections and go upwards of 700–1000+ MAD (70–100+ USD) for premium international labels in fine dining establishments.

Can I find Moroccan natural wines in Casablanca?

Yes, Morocco has a burgeoning wine scene, with several vineyards now embracing organic and biodynamic practices. Many of Casablanca's better restaurants and wine-focused venues will feature a selection of high-quality Moroccan wines, some of which adhere to natural winemaking principles. Do not hesitate to ask for local, artisanal options.

Are there any natural wine shops or places to buy bottles in Casablanca?

While dedicated natural wine shops are rare, you can often find a good selection of organic and some natural-leaning wines at gourmet supermarkets like Carrefour Gourmet or at larger specialized wine retailers. Some high-end delis or boutique food stores may also carry a limited, curated selection. It is best to inquire directly with staff about their natural or organic offerings.

What kind of food pairs well with natural wines in Casablanca?

Natural wines are incredibly versatile. Lighter, zesty natural whites and rosés pair beautifully with Casablanca's fresh seafood and Mediterranean dishes. Earthier or fruitier natural reds complement Moroccan tagines, grilled meats, and spiced dishes. Do not be afraid to experiment, as the unique characteristics of natural wines often create delightful pairings with diverse flavors.

Planning your Casablanca wine tour

The ideal natural wine crawl in Casablanca takes you from oceanfront fine dining to historic medina gems in one memorable evening.

Start with a late-afternoon glass at Sky 28 on the 28th floor of the Kenzi Tower for panoramic sunset views and a small-batch natural wine by the glass. Head to Le Cabestan on the Corniche for dinner — a biodynamic white paired with grilled sea bass as the Atlantic crashes below. For a nightcap, Rick's Café in the old medina offers Moroccan reds with live piano in an atmosphere straight out of the Golden Age of cinema.

For a more budget-friendly daytime experience, start with breakfast at La Sqala's fortress garden, then explore Brasserie La Bavaroise's deep wine cellar over a leisurely lunch. End with a seafood dinner and organic Chablis at Le Rouget de l'Isle.

Budget roughly 500–1,500 MAD ($50–$150 USD) for a multi-stop wine evening, or 300–600 MAD ($30–$60) for a budget-conscious daytime tour focusing on La Sqala and La Bavaroise.
