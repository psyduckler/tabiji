Quick answer

Detroit's wing game isn't just strong, it's legendary. From sticky-sweet to fiery-hot, crispy-fried to perfectly smoked, the Motor City offers a diverse and deeply satisfying journey for any wing aficionado. This isn't just about a quick bite; it's about experiencing a piece of Detroit's soul, one flavorful drumstick or flat at a time.

Best overall
Sweetwater Tavern
Top pick

Top verdicts

  • Sweetwater Tavern: Anyone seeking an authentic Detroit dining experience and a taste of the city's most famous wings in a bustling, no-frills setting.
  • Detroit Wing Company: Wing connoisseurs who love exploring a vast menu of flavors, from classic Buffalo to unique, gourmet options, in a casual setting.
  • Penny Red's: Those looking for expertly fried wings in a stylish, bustling environment, perfect for a lively meal or pre-event bite.

The humble chicken wing has carved out a significant niche in Detroit's food culture, deeply rooted in the city's robust bar scene, sports traditions, and a love for communal dining. Historically, wings have been a staple in neighborhood taverns, evolving from simple bar snacks to culinary stars, each establishment adding its unique twist to the classic.

You'll find an incredible spectrum of wing styles here, ranging from the iconic 'Sweetwater' sticky-sweet barbecue wings that put Detroit on the wing map, to crispy Korean-fried, classic Buffalo, smoky dry rubs, and innovative gastropub creations. Whether you prefer bone-in or boneless, a mild tang or a scorching inferno, Detroit has a wing for every palate.

Frequently asked questions

What makes Detroit-style wings unique?

Detroit-style wings, famously exemplified by Sweetwater Tavern, are characterized by their signature sticky-sweet and savory sauce that carmelizes perfectly on the crispy skin. It's often a secret recipe, but usually involves a blend of BBQ sauce, spices, and a touch of sweetness, making them distinct from classic Buffalo or other regional styles.

Are there vegetarian or vegan wing options available in Detroit?

While traditional chicken wings dominate the scene, several gastropubs and trendy eateries in Detroit are increasingly offering plant-based alternatives. Look for cauliflower wings or mushroom-based 'wings' at places like The Commons, or ask at spots like Bobcat Bonnie's, which often feature diverse menus. It's best to call ahead to confirm availability.

What's the typical price for a plate of wings in Detroit?

The price for a full order of wings (typically 6-10 pieces) in Detroit generally ranges from $12 to $25. Budget-friendly spots like Detroit Wing Company or Bronx Bar might be on the lower end, while gastropubs or more upscale establishments like Penny Red's will be at the higher end, reflecting quality and ambiance.

Can I find spicy wings in Detroit?

Absolutely! Detroit has a fantastic selection of spicy wings for heat seekers. Many places, including Detroit Wing Company and Bookies Bar & Grille, offer various heat levels for their Buffalo sauces. Some spots, like Batch Brewing Company, also feature specialty sauces with a significant kick, often with Asian or Caribbean influences.

Are any of these wing spots good for families with kids?

Many of the mid-range and casual spots are suitable for families. Detroit Wing Company, Penny Red's (earlier in the day), and Motor City Brewing Works generally have kid-friendly menus and atmospheres. Sweetwater Tavern can be lively but is also a family favorite. For a quieter experience, consider visiting during lunch or early dinner hours.

What's the best neighborhood for a wing crawl?

Downtown Detroit offers a high concentration of top-tier wing spots like Sweetwater Tavern, Checker Bar & Grill, Bookies, and Penny Red's, making it ideal for a walkable wing crawl. Midtown and Corktown also boast excellent clusters, with options like Union Street, Batch Brewing, and Bobcat Bonnie's all within a short distance of each other.

Do any of these places offer unique, non-traditional wing flavors?

Yes, several spots in Detroit are known for innovative wing flavors beyond the usual Buffalo. Batch Brewing Company and Bobcat Bonnie's frequently feature creative, rotating sauces like spicy peanut butter, Korean BBQ, or unique dry rubs. Union Street's Sticky Asian Wings are also a beloved non-traditional option.

Is outdoor seating available at any of these wing restaurants?

Yes, many Detroit establishments offer outdoor seating, especially during warmer months. Batch Brewing Company, Motor City Brewing Works, and Bobcat Bonnie's often have patios or outdoor dining areas that are very popular. It's always a good idea to call ahead or check their website/social media for current outdoor seating availability.
