Miami's skyline was made for rooftop bars. From Brickell's glass towers to South Beach's ocean-view terraces, the city offers some of the best elevated drinking in the U.S. We scoured Reddit threads on r/Miami, Eater, TimeOut, and The Rooftop Guide to find where locals actually go — not just where tourists end up.

The result: 12 rooftop bars spanning Brickell, South Beach, Coconut Grove, Wynwood, and Coral Gables. Cocktails range from $14 to $30, and most offer happy hour deals that make the skyline views surprisingly accessible.

How we built this list

We analyzed 200+ Reddit posts and comments across r/Miami, r/VisitingMiami, and r/MiamiBeach — spanning 2022 to 2025. Bars were cross-referenced with Eater Miami, The Infatuation, TimeOut, and The Rooftop Guide. We weighted responses from Miami residents and repeat visitors more heavily than tourist one-offs.
