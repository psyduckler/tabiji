Warung Babi Guling Pak Malen

Balinese Pork Spicy
★ 4.4 · 9,438 ✍ Google Maps →
📍 NeighborhoodSeminyak / Kerobokan
💰 Price RangeIDR 40,000-60,000 (approx. $2.60-3.90 USD)

A truly iconic spot, Warung Babi Guling Pak Malen serves one of Bali's most famous dishes: suckling pig. Each plate comes piled high with crispy skin, tender shredded pork, sausage, blood sausage, and a fiery sambal matah. Expect a bustling, no-frills atmosphere, but the flavor makes it an essential Balinese experience.
