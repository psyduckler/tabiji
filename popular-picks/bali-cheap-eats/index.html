Quick answer

Budget-conscious travelers can find delicious meals in Bali ranging from Rp 15,000–80,000 ($1–$5), with family-run warungs offering the most authentic and affordable Balinese cuisine. For the best value and experience, venture beyond the tourist hotspots of Seminyak and Canggu to discover these hidden culinary gems. We analyzed hundreds of Reddit posts to find the spots that actual expats, repeat visitors, and long-term residents recommend.

Why it made the list
The single most-recommended babi guling on Reddit — and for good reason. Pak Malen has been serving Bali's iconic suckling pig since the 1980s, and the Sunset Road location draws a daily queue of locals and tourists alike. The crispy skin is shatteringly good, the spice paste is complex without being overwhelming, and at Rp 60,000 a plate, it's a fraction of what you'd pay at a tourist-oriented restaurant. Go at 11:30am to beat the lunch rush. This is the #1 "must eat" in Bali according to Reddit.
What to order
Babi Guling Pak Malen, located on Sunset Road in Seminyak, offers Balinese Babi Guling for Rp 55,000–80,000 ($3.50–$5). Order the nasi babi guling — a plate of rice topped with sliced suckling pig, crispy skin, blood sausage (urutan), lawar (spiced coconut vegetable mix), and a rich bone broth soup on the side. Redditors consistently warn that one portion isn't enough, so consider ordering two. For the best crackling, arrive before noon.

Why it made the list
Padang food is Indonesia's greatest culinary export, and Sari Ratu is a reliable chain that does it well. The format is uniquely Indonesian: plates of pre-cooked dishes are brought to your table, you eat what you want, and you only pay for what you've touched. Rendang here is genuinely good — rich, deeply spiced, and falling-apart tender. For travellers who want to try something beyond Balinese cuisine, this is the move. Located on Sunset Road near Pak Malen, so you can do a babi guling lunch and a Padang dinner in the same area.
What to order
Sari Ratu, a Padang/Indonesian restaurant on Sunset Road in Kuta, offers meals for Rp 25,000–45,000 ($1.60–$2.90) and boasts a 4.8★ rating with 36 reviews. Padang food is served buffet-style — point at what you want from the display. Must-tries: rendang (slow-braised beef in coconut and spice), ayam pop (Padang-style fried chicken), gulai otak (brain curry — trust us), and daun singkong (cassava leaf). You only pay for what you eat. Rice is the base, load up on sides.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a meal cost at a warung in Bali?

A typical meal at a local warung in Bali costs between Rp 15,000–50,000 (about $1–$3.20 USD). Nasi campur (mixed rice) runs Rp 20,000–35,000, nasi goreng or mie goreng is Rp 15,000–30,000, and babi guling (suckling pig) costs Rp 30,000–60,000 per portion. Even tourist-area warungs like Warung Murah in Seminyak keep prices under Rp 40,000.

What is babi guling and where should I try it in Bali?

Babi guling is Bali's signature dish — a whole suckling pig stuffed with a spice paste of turmeric, lemongrass, chilli, garlic, and shallots, then spit-roasted over coconut husk fire for hours. Reddit's top picks are Babi Guling Pak Malen on Sunset Road (the most famous), Pande Egi in Gianyar (locals-only favourite), and Warung Men Agus in Canggu.

What is the difference between a warung and a restaurant in Bali?

A warung is a small, family-run eatery — Bali's equivalent of a street food stall or neighbourhood diner. Warungs typically have plastic chairs, simple menus, and prices 3-5x cheaper than tourist restaurants. The best eating in Bali — according to Reddit — happens almost exclusively at warungs.

Is Bali street food safe to eat?

Bali street food and warung food is generally safe, and millions of locals eat at warungs daily. Choose busy places with high turnover, eat where food is cooked fresh, drink bottled water, and ease into spicy food gradually. 'Bali belly' is more often caused by sudden diet changes or contaminated ice in cocktails than by warung food.

Where are the cheapest areas to eat in Bali?

The cheapest areas to eat in Bali are Denpasar and Gianyar — full meals for Rp 15,000–25,000. Sanur is a great middle ground with low prices and a mellow tourist scene. In Ubud, head away from the Monkey Forest area for Rp 20,000–30,000 nasi campur. The general rule: walk 5 minutes off any main tourist road and prices drop by 50%.
