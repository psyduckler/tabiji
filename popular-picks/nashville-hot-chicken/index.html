Quick answer

Nashville's iconic hot chicken, a fiery dish served with white bread and pickles, can be found for $10-$18 at the spots recommended by locals on Reddit. Prince's Hot Chicken Shack is the most respected original spot, while Hattie B's is the most popular overall.

Frequently asked questions

What is Nashville hot chicken?

Nashville hot chicken is bone-in fried chicken coated in a spicy oil-based paste made from cayenne pepper and other spices. Unlike buffalo wings, the heat comes from an oil or lard-based sauce applied after frying, creating a distinctive red-orange coating.

Where was Nashville hot chicken invented?

Prince's Hot Chicken Shack invented Nashville hot chicken in the 1930s. The legend says Thornton Prince's girlfriend made him extra-spicy chicken as revenge, but he loved it and started selling it.

What is the best hot chicken in Nashville?

Prince's is the original and most respected among locals. Hattie B's is the most popular overall with the best atmosphere. Bolton's is the local sleeper pick for the hottest bird. Red's 615 has the highest Google rating at 4.9 stars.

How hot is Nashville hot chicken?

Most spots offer heat levels from mild to 'shut the cluck up.' Medium at Prince's is already very spicy. Start at mild or medium your first time — you can always order hotter next round.

What should I order with hot chicken?

Traditional sides are white bread (to soak up the spice oil), pickles, coleslaw, and crinkle-cut fries. The bread is essential — it absorbs the cayenne oil pooling at the bottom.

Is Hattie B's worth the wait?

The Midtown location can have 60-90 minute waits on weekends. The chicken is excellent but Prince's and Bolton's offer comparable or better hot chicken with shorter waits. Try the Melrose location for fewer crowds.

How much does hot chicken cost in Nashville?

A hot chicken plate (quarter or half bird with sides) costs $10-$18. Sandwiches run $8-$14. Tenders are $10-$16. Budget $12-$20 per person including a drink.

What is the difference between traditional and modern hot chicken?

Traditional Nashville hot chicken, epitomized by <a href="#princes-hot-chicken"><strong>Prince's Hot Chicken</strong></a>, focuses on a dry-rubbed, cayenne-heavy spice blend often applied after frying. Modern interpretations, found at places like <a href="#pepperfire-hot-chicken"><strong>Pepperfire Hot Chicken</strong></a> or <a href="#party-fowl"><strong>Party Fowl</strong></a>, might incorporate different peppers, sauces, or presentation styles, offering creative twists while still honoring the spicy tradition.

Where can I find the spiciest hot chicken in Nashville?

For traditional, extreme heat, <a href="#princes-hot-chicken"><strong>Prince's Hot Chicken</strong></a> is famous for its 'XXX Hot' level that truly tests your limits. Another intense option is the new favorite, <a href="#brave-idiot"><strong>Brave Idiot</strong></a>, which boasts pure pepper heat without capsaicin extract, delivering an incredibly flavorful and potent burn for serious spice enthusiasts.

Are there any vegetarian or non-chicken options at hot chicken restaurants?

While hot chicken is the star, many establishments offer alternatives. Hot fish sandwiches are a popular choice at spots like <a href="#boltons-spicy-chicken-and-fish"><strong>Bolton's Spicy Chicken & Fish</strong></a> and <a href="#helens-hot-chicken"><strong>Helen's Hot Chicken</strong></a>. Some modern venues might offer plant-based 'chicken' options or a variety of delicious, non-spicy sides that cater to different dietary preferences.

Which hot chicken spots are best for tourists versus locals?

For tourists looking for a quintessential experience, <a href="#hattie-bs-hot-chicken"><strong>Hattie B's Hot Chicken</strong></a> offers accessibility and a vibrant atmosphere, or head to <a href="#princes-hot-chicken"><strong>Prince's Hot Chicken</strong></a> for the original. Locals often frequent places like <a href="#boltons-spicy-chicken-and-fish"><strong>Bolton's Spicy Chicken & Fish</strong></a>, <a href="#400-degrees"><strong>400 Degrees</strong></a>, or <a href="#bj-hot-chicken"><strong>BJ Hot Chicken</strong></a> for authentic flavors away from the main tourist hubs.

Do I need reservations for Nashville hot chicken restaurants?

Generally, most traditional hot chicken spots operate on a first-come, first-served basis and do not take reservations, especially for quick-service or food truck formats like <a href="#brave-idiot"><strong>Brave Idiot</strong></a>. However, a sit-down restaurant with a bar, like <a href="#party-fowl"><strong>Party Fowl</strong></a>, might offer reservations or be more suitable for larger groups, so it's always best to check their specific policy.

What are the typical side dishes served with hot chicken?

Accompanying your spicy bird are classic Southern comfort foods designed to complement the heat. Expect staples such as creamy coleslaw, rich mac & cheese, savory collard greens, crispy crinkle-cut fries, and often a refreshing potato salad or baked beans. These sides help balance the spice and complete the authentic Nashville hot chicken meal.
