Quick answer

Colombo's rooftop bar scene has exploded alongside the city's hotel boom. With Indian Ocean sunsets, Lotus Tower views, and cocktails from LKR 950–4,500 (~$3–$15 USD), it's one of South Asia's most affordable skyline drinking cities. ON14 at the Radisson is the most popular overall; The Bar On The Top at Granbell Hotel has the best panoramic views.

Top verdicts

  • ON14 Rooftop Lounge & Bar: Colombo's most popular rooftop with 1,500+ Google reviews — the 14th-floor perch at the Radisson delivers consistent cocktails and reliable ocean views.
  • The Bar On The Top: The newest and highest addition to Colombo's skyline — 27th floor at Granbell Hotel with jaw-dropping 360° views and a Japanese fine dining restaurant next door.
  • Cloud Red: Cinnamon Red's 26th-floor bar combines trendy vibes, strong cocktails, and one of Colombo's best infinity pools for hotel guests.

Colombo's rooftop bars offer cocktails ranging from LKR 950 to LKR 4,500 (approximately $3–$15 USD), with The Bar On The Top at Granbell Hotel recommended for its stunning Indian Ocean sunset views. These bars provide a sophisticated yet affordable alternative to rooftop experiences in cities like Dubai or Singapore. This guide highlights the best spots to enjoy Colombo's transformed skyline, considering factors like view quality and atmosphere.

Colombo has quietly become one of South Asia's most exciting rooftop bar cities. A wave of international hotel openings — Granbell from Japan, NH Collection from Spain, Radisson from Sweden — have stacked the skyline with bars competing for the best Indian Ocean sunset view. And unlike Dubai or Singapore, a full evening of cocktails here won't cost you more than a single round in London.

We combed through Reddit threads on r/srilanka, r/SriLankaTravel, and travel blogs to find where actual visitors and Colombo residents drink above the city. The scene skews hotel-bar heavy — Sri Lanka's licensing laws favor hotels — but the quality is genuinely impressive, and the views are world-class.
