Quick Picks by Budget

💰 Budget
Bar Peached — Asian-fusion small plates in a stylish setting, offering fine dining quality at a more accessible price point.
Geraldine's Counter — Southern comfort food elevated to an art form, providing a cozy yet upscale experience without the exorbitant price tag.
Contigo — Rustic, ranch-inspired American cuisine focusing on farm-to-table ingredients in a lively, upscale setting.
🍽️ Mid-Range
Juniper — Northern Italian done exquisitely, with a vibrant atmosphere and meticulously crafted dishes perfect for a sophisticated meal.
Odd Duck — Inventive, farm-to-table American dishes with a playful twist, offering a truly unique and memorable dining adventure.
Uchi — Renowned for its creative take on Japanese cuisine, Uchi delivers an unforgettable omakase or à la carte experience that's worth every penny.
Emmer & Rye — Pioneering farm-to-table dining with a focus on house-milled grains and a constantly evolving menu of exceptional American dishes.
✨ Splurge
Otoko — An exclusive omakase experience, offering a multi-course Japanese tasting menu that is both artistic and unforgettable.
Hestia — Modern Mediterranean cuisine cooked over a 20-foot custom hearth, delivering bold flavors and a dramatic dining spectacle.
Jeffrey's — Austin's quintessential upscale steakhouse, offering impeccable service, classic cocktails, and prime cuts in an elegant West Austin setting.
Lutie's Garden Restaurant — Set in a stunning garden oasis, Lutie's offers whimsical, seasonally driven dishes inspired by Texas bounty in a truly magical atmosphere.
Comedor — Sleek, modern Mexican cuisine with a focus on fresh, high-quality ingredients, served in a breathtaking brutalist-inspired space.
