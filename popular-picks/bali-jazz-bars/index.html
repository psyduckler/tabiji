Bali's jazz scene is one of Southeast Asia's best-kept secrets — an eclectic mix of intimate clubs, upscale restaurants, and bohemian bars where world-class musicians play alongside talented Indonesian artists.

From the legendary Ryoshi House of Jazz in Seminyak, which has been hosting live performances for over three decades, to the artistic Ubud scene where jazz spills out of garden restaurants and candlelit bars, the island delivers far beyond its beach-club reputation. Whether you prefer sipping craft cocktails while a quartet plays bebop standards, or enjoying a multi-course Italian dinner accompanied by a sultry jazz vocalist, Bali has a venue that matches your mood.

We researched dozens of venues across travel forums, local music blogs, and community recommendations to identify the 12 best spots where you can reliably hear quality live jazz in Bali. Every venue was verified for current operating status, music schedules, and pricing as of April 2026.

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the best area in Bali for jazz bars?

Seminyak is a prime location for jazz bars, offering a mix of dedicated jazz clubs and upscale restaurants with live performances. Ryoshi House of Jazz and Zibiru are both in Seminyak. Ubud also has a strong scene, with Jazz Cafe Ubud, Laughing Buddha Bar, and Night Rooster all within walking distance of each other in central Ubud.

Do I need reservations for jazz bars in Bali?

For popular venues, especially during peak season or for special performances, reservations are highly recommended. Upscale restaurants like Zibiru or MÉTIS definitely require bookings, while more casual bars like The Orchard or Laughing Buddha Bar might just require you to arrive early to get a good spot.

What's the typical price range for drinks at Bali jazz bars?

Prices vary widely depending on the venue. At casual jazz bars, a local beer might cost IDR 35,000–70,000 ($2.50–5 USD), and cocktails IDR 100,000–150,000 ($7–10 USD). At upscale establishments, expect cocktails to range from IDR 150,000–200,000 ($10–13 USD) or more. Some venues might have a cover charge on specific nights.

Are there jazz bars suitable for families in Bali?

While many jazz bars cater to an adult audience, venues that double as restaurants with an earlier start time for their music, like The Corner House or The Orchard, can be suitable for families, especially with older children. Always check the specific venue's policy and ambiance beforehand.

What kind of jazz music can I expect to hear in Bali?

Bali's jazz scene is diverse, featuring everything from traditional swing and bebop to contemporary jazz fusion, blues, and bossa nova. Many local bands incorporate Indonesian influences into their sets, offering a unique cultural twist to familiar jazz sounds. You will find a mix of instrumental and vocal performances.

What's the dress code for Bali's jazz bars?

The dress code varies from casual to smart-casual. For most casual jazz bars and pubs, comfortable resort wear is perfectly fine. However, for upscale restaurants and lounges that feature jazz, like MÉTIS or Zibiru, a more refined attire (smart shorts or trousers and collared shirts for men, elegant dresses for women) is recommended.

When do live jazz performances usually start in Bali?

Most live jazz performances in Bali start between 7:30 PM and 9:00 PM. Ryoshi House of Jazz typically has sets at 9 PM on Monday and Friday. Zibiru features jazz on Wednesday and Saturday evenings. Many Ubud venues like Laughing Buddha Bar start live music at 7:30 PM. Check each venue's schedule as it varies by night.

Is there a jazz festival in Bali?

Yes, the Ubud Village Jazz Festival is an annual event that brings together local and international jazz musicians for performances in Ubud. It has grown into one of Southeast Asia's most respected small-format jazz festivals. Check the festival website at ubudvillagejazzfestival.com for upcoming dates and lineups.

Planning your Bali jazz bar crawl

The ideal jazz crawl in Bali spans two distinct areas — Seminyak and Ubud — each with its own character.

In Seminyak, start with sunset cocktails at Blu-Zea Resort, then head to Ryoshi House of Jazz for the 9 PM set on Monday or Friday. On other evenings, Zibiru's garden jazz (Wednesday or Saturday) pairs perfectly with Italian cuisine, or catch live bands at The Orchard any night of the week. For a splurge, MÉTIS combines fine French dining with an art gallery and elegant live music.

In Ubud, begin your evening at Jazz Cafe for the 7:30 PM set, then walk to Laughing Buddha Bar for cocktails and more live music. End the night at Night Rooster for world-class craft cocktails in an intimate speakeasy setting. For the ultimate sunset experience, book an early dinner at The Sayan House overlooking the Ayung River gorge.

Budget roughly IDR 300,000–500,000 ($20–$33 USD) per person for an evening covering drinks at two venues, or IDR 800,000–1,500,000 ($53–$100 USD) if including dinner at an upscale spot. Transport between Seminyak and Ubud takes about 60–90 minutes, so plan separate evenings for each area.
