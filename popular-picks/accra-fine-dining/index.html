Top verdicts

  • Santoku: Premier Japanese fine dining by former Nobu chefs since 2012.
  • Tunnel Lounge: Highest-rated fine dining in Accra with 4.8★ and exceptional service.
  • Bistro 22: Ghana Tourist Authority's Restaurant of the Year, beloved by locals and expats.

Accra's fine dining scene has transformed dramatically over the past decade, evolving from a handful of hotel restaurants to a diverse culinary landscape that rivals major global cities. From the sleek minimalism of Santoku to the rooftop views at Skybar 25, Ghana's capital offers world-class dining experiences that showcase both international techniques and local ingredients.

The city's upscale restaurants cluster primarily in three areas: the Airport Residential Area (home to La Chaumiere and Tunnel Lounge), the Villaggio Vista complex in Dzorwulu (Santoku and Skybar 25), and the vibrant Osu district (Pomona, Kozo, and Buka). Each neighborhood offers its own character — Airport Residential for business dining, Osu for creative energy, and Dzorwulu for glamorous nights out.

Whether you're celebrating a special occasion, impressing business clients, or simply seeking an exceptional meal, Accra delivers. We analyzed reviews from Tripadvisor, local food blogs, and the Accra food community to compile this definitive guide to the city's finest restaurants.
