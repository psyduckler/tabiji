Quick answer

Yokohama offers a unique blend of robust Iekei ramen and authentic Chinese cuisine, with Heichinro Honten standing out for its classic Cantonese dishes and Yoshimuraya for its foundational ramen.

Quick Picks by Budget

💰 Budget
Edosei — Quick and delicious pork buns, perfect for a budget-friendly snack or street food experience.
Sanmaimon — Savor Yokohama's unique Sanmaimen ramen at an excellent value, a local favorite.
Yoshimuraya Honten — Experience the original Iekei ramen in its birthplace without breaking the bank.
🍽️ Mid-Range
Chikyuu Chukasoba JIKU — Enjoy artisanal shoyu ramen with carefully crafted chicken broth in a modern setting.
Ramen Museum — More than just a meal, it's an immersive retro theme park where you can sample various regional ramens.
Wanfu Shuka — Casual Taiwanese dining offering tasty xiao long bao and other dishes, great for a relaxed meal.
✨ Splurge
Jukeihanten — Indulge in exquisite Cantonese cuisine featuring fresh seafood and seasonal delicacies in a refined atmosphere.
Manchinro Honten — Step into history with a grand Cantonese course meal at this iconic, long-standing institution.
Heichinro Honten — The epitome of upscale Cantonese dining, offering impeccable service and gourmet dim sum for a memorable experience.
