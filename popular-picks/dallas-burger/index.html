Quick answer

Dallas is a city that takes its comfort food seriously, and few dishes embody that spirit quite like the humble (or not-so-humble) burger. From iconic greasy spoons that have stood the test of time to modern culinary marvels pushing the boundaries of what a burger can be, Big D offers a patty for every palate.

Top verdicts

  • Rodeo Goat: Food adventurers and groups looking for unique burger combinations, a lively atmosphere, and excellent craft beer pairings.
  • Maple & Motor: Anyone seeking an authentic, straightforward, and consistently delicious classic American burger experience.
  • Hopdoddy Burger Bar: Families, friends, and burger enthusiasts looking for a high-quality, customizable burger with great drink options and a lively atmosphere.

The burger's journey in Dallas is deeply intertwined with the city's growth. As a hub for ranching and a bustling urban center, demand for hearty, satisfying meals has always been high. Historic diners and drive-ins catered to a blue-collar workforce, while the city's booming economy later paved the way for more upscale and innovative burger experiences, reflecting Dallas's evolution into a diverse metropolitan melting pot.

Today, you'll find an incredible array of burger styles across Dallas. From the classic smashburger with crispy edges and gooey cheese to towering gourmet creations featuring wagyu beef, artisanal buns, and unexpected toppings like fried eggs or foie gras. Don't forget the regional variations, including those with a distinct Texan twist, often involving brisket or jalapeños.

Frequently asked questions

What makes a Dallas burger unique compared to other cities?

Dallas burgers often reflect the city's diverse culinary influences, ranging from classic, no-frills Americana, sometimes with a Texan twist like a chili-cheese topping, to gourmet, chef-driven creations. You'll find a strong emphasis on fresh, high-quality beef and a blend of traditional drive-in experiences with modern gastropub innovations, showcasing both simplicity and culinary ambition.

Are there vegetarian or vegan burger options available at these top spots?

Many of the listed venues, especially modern craft burger bars like Hopdoddy Burger Bar and Rodeo Goat, offer excellent vegetarian and sometimes vegan burger alternatives, often featuring black bean patties, Impossible burgers, or Beyond burgers. It's always best to check their specific menu online or call ahead for the most current options.

What's the typical price range for a good burger in Dallas?

The price for a quality burger in Dallas can vary significantly. Budget-friendly options at classic drive-ins like Keller's might be $8-12, while mid-range craft burgers at places like Maple & Motor or Hopdoddy typically fall between $12-20. Gourmet or chef-driven burgers can range from $18-25 or more, depending on toppings and sides.

Which Dallas burger spot is best for a family with kids?

Hopdoddy Burger Bar is often a great choice for families, offering a lively atmosphere, customizable burgers, and delicious non-alcoholic milkshakes for kids. Blues Burgers is another family-friendly neighborhood spot with a relaxed vibe and plenty of options for everyone. Keller's Drive-In also offers a unique and memorable experience that kids often enjoy.

Do I need to make reservations for any of these burger restaurants?

Generally, classic burger joints like Keller's Drive-In, Maple & Motor, and Adair's Saloon do not take reservations. For craft burger places like Rodeo Goat or Hopdoddy Burger Bar, it's highly recommended to use their online waitlist or make reservations, especially during peak dining hours, to reduce wait times.

Are there any hidden gem burger spots not on the main list?

While our main list focuses on widely acclaimed and Reddit-backed favorites, Dallas is full of smaller, lesser-known gems. Keep an eye out for local pop-ups or small, independent cafes that might surprise you. Places like The Finch or The Royale (though primarily known for other dishes) sometimes offer fantastic, underrated burgers worth seeking out.

What's the best time of day to visit to avoid crowds?

To avoid the busiest times, try visiting for an early lunch (before 11:30 AM) or a late lunch (after 1:30 PM) on weekdays. Similarly, for dinner, an early seating (around 5 PM) or a late dinner (after 8:30 PM) will generally be less crowded than peak dinner hours, especially on weekends.

Can I find gluten-free burger options in Dallas?

Yes, most modern burger establishments in Dallas, including many on our list like Hopdoddy Burger Bar and Rodeo Goat, offer gluten-free buns or lettuce wraps as alternatives. Always inform your server about any dietary restrictions to ensure your meal is prepared safely and to your specifications.
