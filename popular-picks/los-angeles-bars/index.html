Quick answer

Los Angeles boasts a bar scene as diverse and vibrant as the city itself, a true playground for any aficionado of craft cocktails, fine spirits, or simply a great ambiance. From the bustling streets of Downtown to the trendy enclaves of Silver Lake and Hollywood, finding a stellar spot for a drink is never a challenge, but pinpointing the absolute best requires a keen eye and local insight.

Best overall
The Varnish
Top pick

Top verdicts

  • The Varnish: Those seeking an authentic speakeasy experience with impeccably made classic cocktails in a sophisticated, intimate setting.
  • Death & Co Los Angeles: Aficionados of cutting-edge cocktail artistry and those looking for a sophisticated, upscale drinking experience.
  • Everson Royce Bar: Groups looking for a lively atmosphere, excellent cocktails, and delicious bar food, especially on a warm LA evening.

The city's cocktail culture has deep roots, evolving from clandestine speakeasies during Prohibition to mid-century lounges, and now into a globally recognized hub for innovative mixology. LA bartenders are known for pushing boundaries, often incorporating local California produce and unique global inspirations into their creations, a testament to the city's pioneering spirit.

Whether you're craving a meticulously crafted classic at a dimly lit speakeasy, a refreshing beer in a casual neighborhood spot, or an experimental libation with panoramic city views, Los Angeles delivers. The variety spans from high-energy dance floors to intimate, quiet corners perfect for conversation, ensuring there’s a perfect setting for every mood and occasion.

Frequently asked questions

Are reservations required for the best bars in Los Angeles?

For highly popular speakeasies and upscale cocktail lounges like The Varnish or Death & Co Los Angeles, reservations are strongly recommended, particularly for weekend visits. Casual bars and neighborhood spots typically operate on a walk-in basis, but arriving early can help avoid queues.

What is the typical price range for a cocktail in LA's best bars?

You can expect to pay anywhere from $14 to $25 for a craft cocktail at most top-tier bars in Los Angeles, with some high-end or bespoke drinks costing more. Dive bars or more casual spots like Tony's Saloon will offer more budget-friendly options, often under $12.

Do any of these bars have outdoor seating or patios?

Yes, several top bars in Los Angeles offer excellent outdoor seating. Everson Royce Bar is famous for its spacious and lively patio, while Mama Shelter Rooftop Bar provides fantastic open-air views. Bar Flores also has an appealing rooftop patio called La Cha Cha Chá.

Are there good options for non-alcoholic drinks at these bars?

Many of the craft cocktail bars on our list, especially places like Death & Co Los Angeles and The Varnish, are skilled at creating delicious and complex non-alcoholic 'mocktails' upon request. Most bars will also offer standard non-alcoholic options like sodas, juices, and often non-alcoholic beers.

Which neighborhood has the best concentration of great bars in Los Angeles?

The Arts District in Downtown LA is a strong contender, boasting a high concentration of excellent bars within walking distance, including Death & Co Los Angeles, Everson Royce Bar, and Tony's Saloon. Other vibrant areas include Hollywood and Silver Lake, each with their distinct bar scenes.

Are these bars wheelchair accessible?

While many modern establishments are built with accessibility in mind, some older buildings or speakeasies with hidden entrances (like The Varnish) might present challenges. It's always best to call the specific venue directly to inquire about their accessibility features before visiting.

Is there a dress code for the upscale bars in LA?

For upscale cocktail bars and speakeasies, a 'smart casual' dress code is generally appropriate. This means no shorts, flip-flops, or overly casual athletic wear. Places like The Varnish or Employees Only LA lean towards a more polished look. More casual bars like Tony's Saloon or The Friend have no specific dress code.

Can I find live music at these Los Angeles bars?

Yes, live music is a feature at several recommended spots. Sassafras Saloon often hosts live blues and jazz, adding to its unique themed atmosphere. Seven Grand, the whiskey bar, also frequently features live jazz. It's always a good idea to check their respective websites for current schedules.
