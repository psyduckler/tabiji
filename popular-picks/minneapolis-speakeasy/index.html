Quick answer

Minneapolis's speakeasy scene isn't just about hidden doors and password-protected entrances; it's a journey into meticulously crafted experiences, where every detail from the mood lighting to the ice cubes is designed to transport you. Far from a relic of the past, the city has embraced the modern speakeasy, evolving it into a dynamic landscape of sophisticated sips and intimate ambiance.

  • Volstead's Emporium: History buffs and cocktail purists seeking an authentic, immersive speakeasy experience.
  • Prohibition: Couples or groups seeking a sophisticated, high-end cocktail experience with panoramic city vistas.

The spirit of the speakeasy in Minneapolis draws heavily from its Prohibition-era past, where illicit pours flowed freely in secret clubs and backroom bars, often tied to the city's robust brewing history and vibrant immigrant communities. Today, establishments honor that legacy not with illegality, but with an air of exclusivity and a deep respect for the art of mixology.

From classic Art Deco lounges serving timeless cocktails to contemporary hideaways pushing the boundaries of flavor, Minneapolis offers a diverse range of speakeasy styles. You'll find everything from dimly lit, jazz-infused dens perfect for a quiet date night, to bustling, high-energy spots ideal for a pre-dinner aperitif with friends, each with its own unique narrative and signature libations.

Frequently asked questions

What defines a 'speakeasy' in modern Minneapolis?

In modern Minneapolis, a speakeasy typically refers to a bar with a hidden or unmarked entrance, often requiring a bit of searching or local knowledge to find. They usually emphasize craft cocktails, an intimate or themed ambiance (often Prohibition-era), and a focus on personalized service rather than high volume. It's less about legality and more about the experience of discovery and exclusivity.

Are reservations required for most speakeasies in Minneapolis?

For many of the more popular or exclusive speakeasies, especially those known for their bespoke cocktail programs or limited seating, reservations are highly recommended or even required. Places like Cobble Social House operate by reservation only. Others, particularly on weekends, will experience long waits without a prior booking. Always check the individual venue's website or call ahead to confirm their policy.

What is the typical price range for cocktails at Minneapolis speakeasies?

You can expect cocktail prices at Minneapolis speakeasies to range from approximately $12 for simpler drinks at more casual hidden spots to $30 or more for complex, high-end, or bespoke creations at luxury venues. The average price for a quality craft cocktail usually falls between $15 and $22. Remember that these prices do not typically include gratuity.

Are there any dress codes for Minneapolis speakeasies?

While many speakeasies encourage a 'smart casual' to 'cocktail attire' dress code, particularly for upscale spots like Prohibition or The Parlour, very few enforce strict rules. Most venues prioritize comfort and style, so avoid very casual wear like flip-flops or athletic gear. For a true speakeasy experience, dressing up a bit adds to the ambiance and fun.

Do Minneapolis speakeasies serve food, or are they strictly for drinks?

Most Minneapolis speakeasies focus primarily on their cocktail program, but many offer a curated selection of small bites or appetizers that complement their drinks. Some, like Young Joni's Back Bar, are affiliated with restaurants, allowing for easy access to a full menu. It's best to check the individual venue's menu online if you plan to eat more than just a snack.

What's the best way to find the hidden entrances to these bars?

Part of the fun of a speakeasy is the discovery! Many clues are subtle: look for an unmarked door, a specific light fixture, or a discreet sign in an alleyway. Often, these entrances are shared through word-of-mouth or can be found on their websites. Our guide provides insider tips for each venue to help you navigate your way in.

Are Minneapolis speakeasies suitable for larger groups or celebrations?

While the intimate nature of speakeasies generally lends itself better to smaller groups (2-4 people), some venues can accommodate larger parties with advance notice or reservations. However, the true speakeasy experience thrives on quiet conversation and focused mixology, which can be challenging with a big group. Consider a larger, more open cocktail bar for big celebrations.

Do any Minneapolis speakeasies offer non-alcoholic options?

Yes, a growing number of Minneapolis speakeasies are becoming more inclusive with their beverage programs, offering creative and sophisticated non-alcoholic cocktails (often called 'mocktails' or 'spirit-free' drinks). While not every spot will have an extensive menu, most skilled bartenders can craft something delicious and complex to suit a non-drinker's palate. Don't hesitate to ask!
