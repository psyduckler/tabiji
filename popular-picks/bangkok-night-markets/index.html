#1

Jodd Fairs DanNeramit

Street Food Thai International
📍 NeighborhoodLat Phrao
💰 Price RangeTHB 200-500 / $5.50-14 per person

Jodd Fairs DanNeramit is Bangkok's newest and most Instagrammable night market, built on the grounds of a former amusement park. It blends nostalgic charm with trendy food stalls and fashion, featuring a majestic castle backdrop that makes for stunning photos. Expect a lively, youthful crowd and an impressive selection of both classic Thai dishes and modern international snacks.
