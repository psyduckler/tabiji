Antwerp, like all of Belgium, runs on frites. But not all frituurs (fry shops) are created equal. The perfect Belgian frites are a work of art: double-fried for a crispy exterior and a fluffy interior, served in a paper cone with a generous dollop of sauce. Many spots are tourist traps selling mediocre fries. So where do the locals go?

We dug through Reddit threads and local food blogs to find the frituurs that Antwerpenaars and seasoned travelers consistently recommend. Forget the tourist traps — these are the places for an authentic, delicious frites experience.
