Paris Brunch: What to Know

Paris offers a wide range of brunch options, from €10 to €58, with HolyBelly being a top recommendation for its American-style brunch. Keep in mind that the most popular spots, especially in the 10th, 11th, and 3rd arrondissements, can have long weekend lines, so weekdays are best.

Brunch in Paris is a relatively recent phenomenon. Traditionally, Parisians do a quick coffee and croissant. The Anglo-style brunch with eggs, pancakes, and bacon was imported by international chefs and has exploded in popularity over the past decade.

Most brunch spots concentrate in the 10th, 11th, and 3rd arrondissements. Expect no reservations at most spots, weekend lines of 30-60 minutes at peak times (11am-2pm), and prices from EUR 10 to EUR 58.

Pro tip: Go on a weekday if you can. The same spots with hour-long Sunday lines are walk-in friendly on Tuesday mornings.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is brunch popular in Paris?

Yes — brunch has become hugely popular in Paris, especially on weekends. Traditional French breakfast is light (coffee and croissant), so the more substantial Anglo-style brunch is a recent import. Most brunch spots are run by international or younger French chefs. Expect lines between 11am and 2pm on weekends.

How much does brunch cost in Paris?

A la carte dishes typically cost EUR 12-22 per plate. Set menus or buffets range EUR 25-58 per person. HolyBelly's Savory Stack is around EUR 18. Budget spots like Paperboy keep plates under EUR 16.

Do Paris brunch spots take reservations?

Most popular a la carte spots (HolyBelly, Kafkaf, Season) do NOT take reservations — first-come, first-served with 30-60 min weekend waits. Buffet brunches like La Bellevilloise and Le Repaire de Cartouche require reservations. Go on a weekday or arrive before 10am.

What is the best brunch in Paris?

HolyBelly is the most recommended across Reddit, food blogs, and locals. Their Savory Stack (pancakes, fried eggs, bacon, bourbon butter) is iconic. For a French experience, Le Repaire de Cartouche offers oysters, foie gras, and rotisserie chicken.

What arrondissement is best for brunch?

The 10th and 11th arrondissements have the highest concentration — HolyBelly, Kafkaf, The Hood, Le Repaire de Cartouche, Paperboy. The 3rd (Le Marais) is also excellent with Foufou, Season, and Ob-La-Di.

When is the best time for brunch in Paris?

Weekdays 9-11am avoid lines. On weekends, arrive before 10am or after 3pm. Sunday is the busiest day — some spots have 45-60 min waits between 11am and 2:30pm.
