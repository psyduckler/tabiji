Quick answer

Atlanta's nightlife has quietly cultivated a thriving speakeasy scene, offering discerning patrons a thrilling escape from the ordinary. From hidden doors in bustling downtown to secret entrances behind unexpected storefronts, the city is brimming with clandestine cocktail havens that promise an evening of intrigue and exceptional libations.

The modern Atlanta speakeasy draws inspiration from the Prohibition era's illicit drinking establishments, but with a decidedly contemporary twist. While nodding to history with vintage decor and classic cocktail menus, these bars often blend in unique themes, innovative mixology, and a sense of exclusivity that makes discovering each one a unique adventure.

You'll find a wide range of styles across Atlanta's speakeasies. Some transport you back to the Roaring Twenties with strict dress codes and password-entry, focusing on perfectly crafted classics. Others offer playful, immersive themes that change seasonally, or minimalist, art-forward spaces that highlight creative mixology. There are also cozy, intimate spots perfect for a quiet date, and vibrant lounges buzzing with a lively, yet discreet, energy.

Frequently asked questions

What is the average price range for cocktails at Atlanta speakeasies?

Most craft cocktails at Atlanta's speakeasies range from $14 to $20. More exclusive venues or specialty drinks, particularly omakase experiences, can go higher, sometimes $25+ per cocktail. Factoring in a few drinks and perhaps a small bite, expect to spend $20–$60 per person on average.

Do I need a password to enter Atlanta speakeasies?

Some traditional speakeasies, like Red Phone Booth, do require a password for entry, which can often be found on their social media, website, or by asking a local. Other hidden bars might simply require you to find a discreet door or ask a host. It's always best to check the specific venue's requirements beforehand to avoid surprises.

Are there dress codes for Atlanta speakeasies?

Many of the more upscale or traditional speakeasies, such as Red Phone Booth, enforce a strict dress code, typically business casual to cocktail attire (no athletic wear, shorts, flip-flops, or hats). More relaxed hidden bars, like Little Spirit or The James Room, might have a smart casual recommendation but are generally more lenient. When in doubt, it's always better to dress a bit more elegantly.

Can I find non-alcoholic options at these bars?

Yes, most quality speakeasies and cocktail lounges are adept at crafting sophisticated non-alcoholic mocktails. Don't hesitate to ask your bartender for a 'spirit-free' option, and they can often create something delicious and complex using fresh juices, syrups, and bitters. Specify your flavor preferences (e.g., sweet, tart, refreshing).

Are Atlanta speakeasies suitable for groups?

While many speakeasies are intimate and best suited for smaller groups (2-4 people), some larger venues or those with more open layouts can accommodate bigger parties. For groups of 5 or more, it is highly advisable to make a reservation well in advance to ensure space and service. Himitsu, for example, is extremely limited in capacity.

What's the best way to discover new speakeasies in Atlanta?

Beyond guides like this one, follow local food and drink blogs, check out Atlanta-specific Reddit communities (r/Atlanta, r/AtlantaFood), and keep an eye on social media accounts of local bartenders or mixology enthusiasts. Word-of-mouth is also key; ask friendly locals or your favorite bartender for recommendations on hidden spots.

Do speakeasies typically serve food, or just drinks?

Most speakeasies focus primarily on cocktails but often offer a curated selection of small plates or appetizers, sometimes referred to as bar bites or a light snack menu. Places like Eleanor's, being connected to a restaurant, might offer more substantial fare. Always check the menu online if you plan to eat more than just a light snack.

Is it customary to tip at Atlanta speakeasies?

Yes, it is absolutely customary and expected to tip your bartenders and servers at Atlanta speakeasies, just as you would at any bar or restaurant. A standard tip is 18-20% of your total bill, or $2-3 per drink for exceptional service. Good service in a high-skill craft cocktail environment is highly valued.
