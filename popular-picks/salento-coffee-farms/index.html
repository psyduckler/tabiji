Frequently asked questions

What is the best time of year to visit Salento for coffee tours?

The main harvest season in Salento is from April to May, and there is a second, smaller harvest season from October to December. Visiting during these times will give you the chance to see the coffee plants full of ripe cherries.

Do I need to book coffee tours in advance?

It is a good idea to book your tours in advance, especially during the high season (December to February and June to August). Many of the popular farms have websites where you can book online.

How much do coffee tours in Salento typically cost?

Prices for coffee tours in Salento can vary, but you can expect to pay between 30,000 and 60,000 Colombian pesos per person for a standard tour.

How do I get to the coffee farms from Salento?

Many of the coffee farms are located a short Willy (jeep) ride from the main square in Salento. You can also hire a private taxi or even walk to some of the closer farms.
