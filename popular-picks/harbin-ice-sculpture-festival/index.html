#1

Lao Chu Jia (老厨家)

Dongbei Cuisine Harbin Specialties Traditional Chinese
📍 NeighborhoodDaoli District (near Central Street)
💰 Price RangeCNY 80-150 per person / $11-21 USD

Widely regarded as the birthplace of Guo Bao Rou (Sweet and Sour Pork), Lao Chu Jia offers an authentic taste of Harbin's culinary heritage. The atmosphere is bustling and traditional, perfect for experiencing classic Northeast Chinese dishes prepared with generations of expertise. It's a must-visit for any food enthusiast.
