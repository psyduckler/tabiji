Frequently asked questions

What is the best area in Adelaide for cocktail bars?

The central business district (CBD) is undoubtedly the best area for cocktail bars in Adelaide. Specifically, Peel Street, Leigh Street, and their surrounding laneways are densely packed with top-tier establishments, offering everything from hidden speakeasies to vibrant outdoor bars all within easy walking distance.

What is Adelaide's best hidden speakeasy?

Maybe Mae on Peel Street is widely considered Adelaide's best hidden speakeasy. Enter through an unmarked timber door and descend into an Art Deco-inspired underground cocktail den with polished service, inventive drinks, and old-world charm. Baddog on Hyde Street is another excellent option with no signage — if the light is on, they're open.

How much do cocktails cost in Adelaide?

A standard cocktail in Adelaide typically ranges from AUD$18-25 (US$12-17). Premium or more complex signature cocktails, especially at high-end establishments like Maybe Mae or Hains & Co, might cost upwards of AUD$25-30 (US$17-20). Happy hour specials can offer cocktails for around AUD$12-15 (US$8-10).

Are there any rooftop cocktail bars in Adelaide?

Yes, Adelaide features several fantastic rooftop bars. 2KW Bar & Restaurant is the most iconic, perched eight floors above King William Street with panoramic city views and signature cocktails. Proof Bar on Anster Street also has a rooftop terrace with booth seating. These venues are particularly popular during the warmer months.

Do I need reservations for Adelaide cocktail bars?

For most smaller, more intimate cocktail bars, especially on weekend evenings, making a reservation is highly recommended. Venues like Maybe Mae, Bibliotheca, and Pink Moon Saloon fill up quickly. Larger venues or those with extensive outdoor seating might accommodate walk-ins more easily, but checking ahead is always wise.

What's the dress code for Adelaide cocktail bars?

The dress code for Adelaide's cocktail bars is generally smart casual. While few places enforce strict dress codes, most patrons opt for neat attire. Avoid sportswear, flip-flops, or overly casual clothing, especially at more upscale or speakeasy-style venues like Maybe Mae or Hains & Co.

Which Adelaide bar has the best gin selection?

Hains & Co on Gilbert Place is Adelaide's premier destination for gin enthusiasts. This nautical-themed bar specializes in gin, rum, and fine cigars, with wooden ship-style interiors and a constantly rotating menu of expertly crafted cocktails featuring an extensive gin collection from around the world.

Can I visit multiple cocktail bars in one night in Adelaide?

Absolutely! Adelaide's compact CBD makes bar-hopping easy. A great crawl: start at Clever Little Tailor on Peel Street, walk 30 seconds to Maybe Mae, then head to Leigh Street for Pink Moon Saloon, before finishing at Hains & Co on Gilbert Place. That covers 4 distinct styles in one evening, all within a 10-minute walking radius.
