Portland has earned its reputation as a vegan mecca. Travel + Leisure named it the best vegan city in the country, and with good reason — you can eat vegan Sri Lankan curry for lunch, vegan dim sum for an afternoon snack, vegan soul food fried catfish for dinner, and wash it all down at a vegan cocktail bar. We analyzed hundreds of Reddit posts across r/PortlandVegans, r/askportland, and r/Portland to find the 12 restaurants that actual Portlanders keep coming back to.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best vegan restaurant in Portland?

Mis Tacones is the most recommended, with a 4.8-star rating from 873 reviews. Their seitan al pastor tacos are legendary.

Is Portland good for vegan food?

Portland is consistently ranked as one of the best vegan cities in the US with over 50 fully vegan restaurants.

Where do Portland locals eat vegan?

Locals love Mis Tacones for Mexican, Obon Shokudo for Japanese, Dirty Lettuce for soul food, and Feral for fine dining.

What is the most popular vegan dish in Portland?

The Portland Fog donut from Doe Donuts (Earl Grey and vanilla) is the single most mentioned vegan item on Reddit.

How much does vegan food cost in Portland?

Most vegan restaurants are $12-$18 for a main. Face Plant starts around $8. Fine dining at Feral runs $15-$25.

Are there vegan fast food options in Portland?

Yes! Face Plant serves vegan burgers, nuggets, and shakes. Doe Donuts is great for quick sweets.

What neighborhoods have the best vegan food?

NE Killingsworth has Mis Tacones and Feral. SE Belmont/Division has Mirisata, Jade Rabbit, Kati, and Harlow.

What makes Portland such a great city for vegan dining?

Portland is renowned globally for its thriving vegan scene, boasting an incredible concentration of fully plant-based restaurants, food carts, and bakeries. The city's innovative spirit and demand for ethical, sustainable dining have fostered a diverse culinary landscape, from comforting soul food at <a href="#dirty-lettuce"><strong>Dirty Lettuce</strong></a> to sophisticated fine dining at <a href="#feral"><strong>Feral</strong></a>, ensuring something for every palate.

Can I find budget-friendly vegan meals in Portland?

Absolutely! Portland offers fantastic vegan options that won't break the bank. For quick and affordable bites, check out <a href="#face-plant"><strong>Face Plant</strong></a> for fast-food favorites or grab delicious, budget-friendly donuts from <a href="#doe-donuts-ice-cream"><strong>Doe Donuts + Ice Cream</strong></a>. Even many mid-range spots like <a href="#bye-and-bye"><strong>Bye and Bye</strong></a> offer substantial meals under $20, making vegan dining accessible.

What are the best vegan spots for a special occasion in Portland?

For a memorable celebratory meal, <a href="#feral"><strong>Feral</strong></a> stands out with its innovative, artfully presented plant-based dishes in a refined setting, perfect for an intimate evening. Alternatively, <a href="#obon-shokudo"><strong>Obon Shokudo</strong></a> offers a sophisticated Japanese dining experience with exquisite vegan creations that are also ideal for an elegant night out.

Are there vegan restaurants in Portland suitable for families with kids?

Many of Portland's vegan restaurants are family-friendly, offering approachable menus and welcoming atmospheres. <a href="#face-plant"><strong>Face Plant</strong></a> is a great choice for quick, crowd-pleasing vegan fast food, while <a href="#doe-donuts-ice-cream"><strong>Doe Donuts + Ice Cream</strong></a> is always a hit for a fun treat. Even places like <a href="#harlow"><strong>Harlow</strong></a> or <a href="#kati-portland"><strong>Kati Portland</strong></a> can comfortably accommodate families with diverse tastes.

Where can I find diverse international vegan cuisine in Portland?

Portland is a hub for international vegan flavors! Explore vibrant Mexican dishes at <a href="#mis-tacones"><strong>Mis Tacones</strong></a>, authentic Thai at <a href="#kati-portland"><strong>Kati Portland</strong></a>, or unique Sri Lankan curries at <a href="#mirisata"><strong>Mirisata</strong></a>. For Japanese, <a href="#obon-shokudo"><strong>Obon Shokudo</strong></a> offers modern takes, and <a href="#jade-rabbit"><strong>Jade Rabbit</strong></a> provides delectable vegan dim sum, showcasing the city's global culinary reach.

What are some must-try vegan desserts or treats in Portland?

When it comes to vegan sweets, Portland excels! Your first stop should be <a href="#doe-donuts-ice-cream"><strong>Doe Donuts + Ice Cream</strong></a> for their incredible selection of gourmet vegan donuts and creamy ice cream. Beyond that, many vegan-friendly cafes and restaurants, like <a href="#harlow"><strong>Harlow</strong></a>, often feature delicious plant-based dessert options to perfectly complete your meal.
