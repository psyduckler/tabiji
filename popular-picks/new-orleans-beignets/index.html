Quick answer

New Orleans beignets range from budget-friendly ($2) to fine dining ($80), but the iconic Café Du Monde is a must-try for the classic experience. However, travelers suggest exploring alternatives like Loretta's for unique flavors, especially during peak tourist seasons when lines can be long.

Frequently asked questions

What is a beignet?

A beignet (pronounced ben-YAY) is a square piece of fried dough, generously dusted with powdered sugar. French-Creole colonists brought them to New Orleans in the 18th century. Unlike a donut, beignets have no hole and are typically puffier with a chewier interior. They're traditionally served in orders of three alongside café au lait — chicory coffee mixed with hot milk.

Is Café Du Monde really the best for beignets?

Café Du Monde is the most iconic and arguably the benchmark — they've been making beignets since 1862. But locals are split. Many prefer Loretta's Authentic Pralines for their praline-stuffed version, Café Beignet for a more relaxed vibe, or Morning Call for shorter lines. The honest answer: Café Du Monde is a must-try for the experience, but your actual favorite might be somewhere else.

What are the best beignets besides Café Du Monde?

Reddit's top alternatives are Loretta's Authentic Pralines (praline-stuffed beignets in the French Market), Café Beignet (courtyard seating, live jazz, on Royal Street), Morning Call (City Park location, equally historic), and for savory versions, Restaurant R'evolution's crawfish and blue crab beignets.

When is the best time to visit Café Du Monde?

Go early morning (before 9 AM) or late at night (after 10 PM on weekdays) to avoid the worst lines. The original French Quarter location is open until 11 PM–midnight. Weekday mornings are significantly less crowded than weekends. Alternatively, visit the City Park location for the same beignets with shorter waits.

Are there savory beignets in New Orleans?

Yes! Several restaurants serve savory beignets as appetizers. Restaurant R'evolution is famous for their crawfish and blue crab beignets. Café Beignet offers crawfish beignets. Some upscale restaurants like Brennan's and The Vintage incorporate beignets into savory-sweet dessert courses.

How much do beignets cost in New Orleans?

At Café Du Monde, an order of 3 beignets costs about $4.13. Café Beignet charges around $5–6 per order. At sit-down restaurants like Brennan's or R'evolution, beignets are part of a larger ($15–$30+) meal. Popeyes offers 12 beignets for about $7.49.

Can I bring beignet mix home?

Absolutely. Café Du Monde sells their beignet mix in the iconic yellow box at their shop, at the airport, and online. It's a popular souvenir — just add water, roll out, cut into squares, and deep fry at 370°F.

What is the difference between Café Du Monde's locations?

The original <a href="#cafe-du-monde"><strong>Café Du Monde</strong></a> on Decatur Street in the French Quarter is the iconic, bustling, open-air spot known for its vibrant atmosphere and constant crowds. The <a href="#cafe-du-monde-city-park"><strong>Café Du Monde (City Park)</strong></a> location offers the exact same legendary beignets and chicory coffee but in a more serene, park-side setting, generally with shorter lines and ample seating, making it a favorite for locals and families.

Are New Orleans beignets only sweet?

While the traditional New Orleans beignet is a sweet, square piece of fried dough covered in powdered sugar, the city's innovative culinary scene offers savory variations. For a gourmet savory experience, <a href="#restaurant-revolution"><strong>Restaurant R'evolution</strong></a> serves exquisite crawfish and blue crab beignets, transforming the classic into a sophisticated appetizer. Some modern eateries, like <a href="#district-donuts-sliders-brew"><strong>DISTRICT Donuts Sliders Brew</strong></a>, also experiment with various glazes and toppings that can lean less sweet.

Do I need reservations for beignet places in New Orleans?

For most traditional beignet-focused cafes like <a href="#cafe-du-monde"><strong>Café Du Monde</strong></a>, <a href="#cafe-beignet"><strong>Café Beignet</strong></a>, or <a href="#morning-call-coffee-stand"><strong>Morning Call Coffee Stand</strong></a>, reservations are not taken – it's typically first-come, first-served. However, if you're visiting upscale restaurants that serve beignets as part of a larger meal, such as <a href="#brennans"><strong>Brennan's</strong></a>, <a href="#willa-jean"><strong>Willa Jean</strong></a>, or <a href="#restaurant-revolution"><strong>Restaurant R'evolution</strong></a>, reservations are highly recommended, especially for brunch or dinner.

What makes Loretta's Praline-Stuffed Beignets so special?

<a href="#lorettas-authentic-pralines"><strong>Loretta's Authentic Pralines</strong></a> offers a unique twist on the classic beignet by stuffing it with their homemade pecan praline, creating a delightful explosion of flavor and texture. This innovation provides a gooey, sweet surprise inside the airy dough, offering a richer and more decadent experience than the traditional powdered sugar beignet. It's often cited as a must-try alternative to the classic for those seeking something truly unique.

Can I find beignets outside the French Quarter?

Absolutely! While the French Quarter is home to the most famous beignet spots, you can find excellent options throughout New Orleans. For instance, <a href="#morning-call-coffee-stand"><strong>Morning Call Coffee Stand</strong></a> and the <a href="#cafe-du-monde-city-park"><strong>Café Du Monde (City Park)</strong></a> are both located in City Park. On Magazine Street, you'll find creative takes at <a href="#district-donuts-sliders-brew"><strong>DISTRICT Donuts Sliders Brew</strong></a> and <a href="#the-vintage"><strong>The Vintage</strong></a>, while <a href="#willa-jean"><strong>Willa Jean</strong></a> is a popular choice in the Central Business District (CBD).

Is Popeyes a legitimate place to get beignets in New Orleans?

<a href="#popeyes-louisiana-kitchen"><strong>Popeyes Louisiana Kitchen</strong></a> might seem like a controversial pick, but many locals and online communities, particularly on Reddit, swear by their beignets as a surprisingly delicious and convenient option. While they may not offer the traditional cafe experience, Popeyes' beignets are often praised for their consistent quality and accessibility. It's a great 'wild card' choice for a quick, budget-friendly beignet fix that often exceeds expectations.
