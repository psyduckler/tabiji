Just 20 minutes from Brussels, Mechelen feels like a different world. It’s a city with all the charm of Bruges—cobblestone streets, a towering cathedral, cozy squares—but without the crowds. It's a place where you can climb a medieval tower in the morning, taste world-famous beer at the source in the afternoon, and still feel like you’ve discovered a secret.

We dug through Reddit to find what travelers and locals love most about Mechelen. This isn't just a list of sights; it's a guide to experiencing the authentic heart of Flanders.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Mechelen worth a day trip from Brussels?

Absolutely. Mechelen is a 20-minute train ride from Brussels and offers a charming, authentic Belgian experience with far fewer tourists than Bruges or Ghent. It has a beautiful historic center, a world-class brewery, and several excellent museums.

What is Mechelen known for?

Mechelen is known for its carillon school, the Het Anker brewery (home of the Gouden Carolus beer), and its history as the capital of the Burgundian Netherlands. It's a city rich in history but with a relaxed, modern vibe.

How walkable is Mechelen?

The historic center of Mechelen is very compact and easily walkable. Most of the main attractions are within a 15-minute walk of each other. The city has a well-designed walking tour that guides you through the highlights.
