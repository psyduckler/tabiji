Just 20 minutes from Brussels, Mechelen feels like a different world. It’s a city with all the charm of Bruges—cobblestone streets, a towering cathedral, cozy squares—but without the crowds. It's a place where you can climb a medieval tower in the morning, taste world-famous beer at the source in the afternoon, and still feel like you’ve discovered a secret.
