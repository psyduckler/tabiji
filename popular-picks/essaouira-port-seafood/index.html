Essaouira is Morocco&rsquo;s Atlantic fishing town &mdash; a place where the boats come in at dawn and the smoke from charcoal grills drifts through the medina by noon. The port isn&rsquo;t a tourist attraction bolted on to a city; it&rsquo;s the beating heart of the place. Fishermen haul sardines, shrimp, sole, and octopus from the same blue boats their grandfathers used.

Redditors consistently say Essaouira has Morocco&rsquo;s best seafood &mdash; cheaper and fresher than Casablanca, more relaxed than Agadir, and with an atmosphere that makes the meal. This guide covers the 5 experiences worth your time, from a 30-dirham plate of grilled sardines at the port to upscale octopus dishes in a candlelit medina restaurant.
