Quick answer

Portland, Oregon, has firmly cemented its reputation as a pizza lover's paradise, a city where the pursuit of the perfect pie is a serious, delicious endeavor. From traditional Neapolitan char to creative, sourdough-crusted marvels and hearty Detroit-style squares, the options are as varied as the city's unique neighborhoods. This isn't just about grabbing a quick slice; it's about experiencing the passion and craftsmanship that goes into every oven-fired masterpiece.

Top verdicts

  • Apizza Scholls: Pizza purists seeking a perfectly crafted, traditional-leaning pizza in a bustling, convivial setting.
  • Ken's Artisan Pizza: Those looking for a sophisticated, yet relaxed, dining experience with exceptionally crafted Neapolitan-style pizzas and a great wine list.
  • Lovely's Fifty/Fifty: Adventurous eaters and those who appreciate unique flavor combinations and a distinctive sourdough crust, complemented by amazing desserts.

The city's strong farm-to-table ethos and independent spirit have profoundly influenced its pizza culture. Many pizzerias source local, seasonal ingredients, elevating classic combinations and inspiring new, unexpected flavor profiles. This commitment to quality, combined with a willingness to experiment, has fostered a dynamic and competitive pizza scene that consistently earns national recognition.

You'll find everything from classic New York-style slices perfect for a casual grab-and-go to upscale, wood-fired establishments offering sophisticated dining experiences. Portland embraces all forms, be it thin-crust Italian, thick-crust Detroit, chewy sourdough, or even unique cornmeal crusts, ensuring there's a pizza for every palate and occasion.

Frequently asked questions

What is the typical price range for pizza in Portland?

The price range for pizza in Portland varies significantly based on style and ingredients. You can expect to pay around $10-$20 for a couple of slices or a small budget-friendly pie, while a gourmet, whole pie at an artisanal spot can range from $20-$40, typically feeding 1-2 people generously. Expect to spend $15-$40 per person for a sit-down meal including drinks or appetizers.

Are there good vegan or gluten-free pizza options in Portland?

Yes, Portland is incredibly accommodating to dietary restrictions. Many pizzerias offer robust vegan cheese and topping options, with Sizzle Pie being a standout for its extensive vegan menu. For gluten-free, several places offer dedicated gluten-free crusts, though availability and quality can vary. Always call ahead to confirm their current offerings and cross-contamination policies, especially for severe allergies.

What's the best pizza style to try first in Portland?

For a classic Portland experience, start with Neapolitan-style pizza, which is prevalent and expertly executed here. Spots like Apizza Scholls or Ken's Artisan Pizza showcase the city's dedication to quality ingredients and perfectly charred crusts. If you're looking for something uniquely Portland, consider Dove Vivi's cornmeal crust for a distinct flavor and texture.

Do I need reservations for pizza restaurants in Portland?

For the most popular sit-down establishments, especially those known for a more upscale dining experience (like Ken's Artisan Pizza), reservations are highly recommended, particularly on weekends. Most casual slice shops and mid-tier pizzerias operate on a first-come, first-served basis, but be prepared for potential waits during peak dinner hours.

Is it customary to tip at pizza places in Portland?

Yes, it is customary to tip for service in Portland. For full-service, sit-down restaurants, a tip of 18-20% is standard. For counter-service or takeout orders, a smaller tip (e.g., 10-15% or rounding up) is appreciated, especially if you received good service or they went above and beyond.

Which Portland neighborhood has the best concentration of pizza places?

Southeast Portland, particularly the areas around Division, Hawthorne, and Belmont, boasts a high concentration of excellent pizza places, ranging from classic Neapolitan to creative artisan pies. North Portland (especially the Mississippi and Williams corridors) also offers a fantastic selection of diverse pizza styles.

Can I find late-night pizza in Portland?

Absolutely! Portland has several excellent options for late-night pizza. Sizzle Pie is particularly well-known for staying open late, especially on weekends, offering slices and whole pies into the early hours. Other spots like Escape From New York Pizza also cater to late-night cravings, though their hours may vary.

Are there any unique Portland pizza toppings I should try?

Portland's pizza scene loves to innovate. While not universal, you might find pizzas featuring local seasonal ingredients like wild mushrooms, foraged greens, or even unique cheeses from Oregon creameries. Keep an eye out for places that highlight local partnerships or offer creative twists on classic toppings, often listed as daily specials.
