Quick answer

Minneapolis's pizza scene is surprisingly diverse and fiercely beloved, a testament to the city's innovative culinary spirit. Far from a one-note town, the Twin Cities boast an array of styles that rival larger metropolitan areas, from classic Neapolitan to robust Detroit-style, and everything in between. Whether you're craving a crisp, wood-fired crust or a thick, cheesy square, Minneapolis delivers.

  • Young Joni: Foodies and couples looking for an upscale, memorable dining experience with exceptional pizza and drinks.
  • Pizzeria Lola: Families and groups seeking a lively, upscale casual dining experience with imaginative pizza combinations.
  • Wrecktangle Pizza: Those craving a hearty, cheesy, rectangular pizza that delivers on flavor and unique texture.

The city's appreciation for quality ingredients and local sourcing has naturally extended to its pizza culture. Many pizzerias here take pride in using locally milled flour, artisanal cheeses, and fresh produce from Minnesota farms, elevating a simple pizza into a gourmet experience. This dedication reflects a broader trend in Minneapolis's dining landscape, where community and craftsmanship are highly valued.

You'll find a spectrum of pizza experiences here: traditional Neapolitan ovens churning out blistered crusts, inventive chefs pushing boundaries with unique toppings like Korean BBQ, and old-school joints perfecting the classic American slice. There are cozy neighborhood spots perfect for a family dinner, and upscale eateries offering sophisticated pies alongside craft cocktails. The variety ensures there's a perfect pizza for every palate and occasion.

Frequently asked questions

What kind of pizza is Minneapolis famous for?

Minneapolis doesn't have one single 'famous' style but boasts incredible diversity. You'll find top-tier Neapolitan, delicious Detroit-style, unique coal-fired, and excellent classic American pies. The scene is particularly strong in wood-fired and artisanal options, often with creative, locally-inspired toppings.

Are there good vegan or gluten-free pizza options in Minneapolis?

Absolutely! Minneapolis is a very dietary-conscious city. Many pizzerias, including Pizza Luce and Galactic Pizza, offer extensive vegan menus with dairy-free cheeses and plant-based proteins. Most high-quality pizzerias also provide gluten-free crust options, like Black Sheep Pizza, often baked in separate areas to minimize cross-contamination.

What's the typical price range for pizza in Minneapolis?

Prices can vary significantly based on style and restaurant type. A budget-friendly New York-style slice might cost $4-7, while a whole classic American pizza for one can be $15-25. Upscale wood-fired or Detroit-style pizzas often range from $20-40 for a personal or small sharing size, not including drinks. Expect to pay around $15-40 per person for a full meal with drinks.

Do Minneapolis pizzerias offer delivery?

Yes, many pizzerias offer delivery directly through their own services (like Galactic Pizza) or via popular third-party apps such as DoorDash, Uber Eats, and Grubhub. Availability may depend on your location within the city. Check individual restaurant websites or delivery apps for specific options.

Is it customary to tip in Minneapolis restaurants?

Yes, standard tipping practices apply in Minneapolis. For table service, a tip of 18-20% of the pre-tax bill is customary for good service. For takeout or counter service, a smaller tip (e.g., 10% or rounding up) is appreciated but not always expected. Delivery drivers should also be tipped appropriately, usually 15-20% of the order total.

What's the best neighborhood for pizza in Minneapolis?

While great pizza is spread throughout the city, areas like Northeast Minneapolis and the North Loop have a high concentration of highly-rated, diverse pizzerias. Uptown and South Minneapolis also offer excellent options, making it hard to pick just one 'best' neighborhood.

Can I find thin-crust pizza in Minneapolis?

Definitely! While Detroit-style offers thick crusts, many Neapolitan and coal-fired pizzerias specialize in thin, crispy, or chewy crusts with slightly blistered edges. Punch Pizza and Black Sheep Pizza are excellent examples if you prefer a thinner base for your pie.

Are there any unique local pizza toppings I should try?

Minneapolis pizzerias are known for their creativity. You'll find unique toppings like Korean BBQ (Pizzeria Lola's Umami Mama), kimchi, lamb sausage with feta, and locally sourced seasonal vegetables. Don't hesitate to explore beyond traditional pepperoni and sausage for a true taste of Minneapolis innovation.
