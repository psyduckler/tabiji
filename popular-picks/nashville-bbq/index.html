Quick answer

Nashville's BBQ scene offers a diverse range of styles for $14-$22 a plate, with Shotgun Willie's being the most recommended spot according to Reddit. While Nashville lacks a single defining BBQ style, its variety, from Texas brisket to whole-hog, is its strength.

Top verdicts

  • Shotgun Willie's BBQ: Closed Mon–Tue, sells out by mid-afternoon — arrive before noon Wed–Sat.
  • Martin's Bar-B-Que Joint: Patio fills on weekends — go for late lunch (2 PM) for the easiest seating.
  • Edley's Bar-B-Que: 12 South location has the longest wait — try the East or Sylvan Park branches for the same kitchen with no line.

Nashville might play second fiddle to Memphis when it comes to Tennessee BBQ, but Music City's smoked meat scene has exploded in recent years. From Texas-transplant brisket masters to whole-hog traditionalists and creative fusion joints, there's more diversity here than in most Southern BBQ cities.

Unlike Memphis (dry-rub ribs) or Texas (low-and-slow brisket), Nashville doesn't have one defining BBQ style. That's actually its strength — you can get world-class brisket, whole-hog pulled pork, competition ribs, and Thai-spiced hot links all within a 30-minute drive.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best BBQ in Nashville?

Shotgun Willie's is the most recommended BBQ spot in Nashville across Reddit. Their Texas-style brisket and salt-and-pepper ribs are legendary. Martin's Bar-B-Que Joint is a close second for whole-hog pulled pork.

Does Nashville have good BBQ?

Nashville's BBQ scene has grown significantly. While it doesn't have one dominant regional style like Memphis or Texas, you'll find excellent Texas-style brisket, whole-hog tradition, Memphis dry rub, and Nashville's own cayenne-forward style.

What BBQ style is Nashville known for?

Nashville doesn't have one official BBQ style, but the city focuses on smoked pork. Edley's pioneered 'Nashville-style BBQ' with a cayenne and habanero dry rub. You'll also find Texas-style brisket, Memphis dry-rub ribs, and whole-hog pulled pork.

Where should tourists eat BBQ in Nashville?

For downtown visitors, Martin's Bar-B-Que Joint and Peg Leg Porker are the most accessible. Edley's 12 South location is a short ride away and worth it.

How much does BBQ cost in Nashville?

A BBQ plate with two sides runs $14-$22. Sandwiches are $10-$15. A full meal with a drink costs about $18-$25 per person.

Is Shotgun Willie's worth the drive?

Yes. It's technically in Madison (15 minutes from downtown), but Reddit unanimously calls it the best BBQ in the Nashville area. Go early — meats sell out, especially ribs. Closed Monday and Tuesday.

What is the difference between Nashville BBQ and Memphis BBQ?

Memphis BBQ centers on dry-rubbed ribs and pulled pork with tangy tomato-vinegar sauce. Nashville's scene is more eclectic — you'll find Texas brisket, whole-hog Carolina style, Memphis ribs, and Nashville's own cayenne-spiced dry rub.

What defines 'Nashville-style BBQ'?

While not as globally recognized as Texas or Carolina styles, Nashville-style BBQ, popularized by places like <a href="#edleys-bar-b-que"><strong>Edley's Bar-B-Que</strong></a>, is often characterized by a distinctive cayenne-based dry rub applied before smoking. This gives the meat a unique savory-spicy kick, distinct from sweet or vinegar-based sauces.

Where can I find the best whole-hog BBQ in Nashville?

For authentic whole-hog BBQ, <a href="#martins-bar-b-que-joint"><strong>Martin's Bar-B-Que Joint</strong></a> is the reigning champion, upholding the West Tennessee tradition of slow-smoked whole hogs. You can also experience the renowned Carolina whole-hog style from a James Beard Award-winner at <a href="#rodney-scotts-whole-hog-bbq"><strong>Rodney Scott's Whole Hog BBQ</strong></a> on Broadway.

Are there any unique or fusion BBQ spots in Nashville worth trying?

Absolutely! <a href="#curry-boys-bbq"><strong>Curry Boys BBQ</strong></a> stands out with its innovative Thai-Texas fusion, offering uniquely spiced meats and sides that earned it the highest rating in Nashville. Another unique experience is <a href="#bringles-smoking-oasis"><strong>Bringle's Smoking Oasis</strong></a>, which focuses on West Texas BBQ with a standout pastrami that's unlike anything else in the city.

What's the best BBQ for a family with kids in Nashville?

For families, <a href="#martins-bar-b-que-joint"><strong>Martin's Bar-B-Que Joint</strong></a> and <a href="#edleys-bar-b-que"><strong>Edley's Bar-B-Que</strong></a> are both excellent choices, offering spacious, casual environments and menus that appeal to a wide range of tastes. Their multiple locations also make it easy to find a convenient spot with plenty of seating.

Is it worth driving outside of downtown for Nashville BBQ?

Definitely! While downtown has great options, some of Nashville's best BBQ gems are located a short drive away. For instance, <a href="#shotgun-willies-bbq"><strong>Shotgun Willie's BBQ</strong></a>, widely considered the #1 spot, is in Madison, and the Bellevue area boasts <a href="#honeyfire-bbq"><strong>HoneyFire BBQ</strong></a>, a local favorite. These drives often lead to quieter crowds and exceptional food.

Do Nashville BBQ joints typically have outdoor seating?

Many Nashville BBQ establishments embrace the Southern weather with fantastic outdoor seating options. <a href="#martins-bar-b-que-joint"><strong>Martin's Bar-B-Que Joint</strong></a>, <a href="#edleys-bar-b-que"><strong>Edley's Bar-B-Que</strong></a>, and <a href="#peg-leg-porker-bbq"><strong>Peg Leg Porker BBQ</strong></a> are known for their inviting patios, perfect for enjoying your meal al fresco. It's always a good idea to check specific location details if outdoor dining is a priority for your visit.
