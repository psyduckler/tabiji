Quick answer

Los Angeles' pizza game has officially leveled up, transforming from a city once overlooked for its 'za to a burgeoning hotspot rivaling even the most established pizza capitals. Forget what you thought you knew; LA is now a serious contender, boasting a diverse range of styles that will satisfy any craving.

Top verdicts

  • Pizzeria Mozza: Foodies seeking an elevated, gourmet pizza experience with unique, high-quality ingredients and a vibrant atmosphere.
  • Quarter Sheets Pizza Club: Adventurous eaters and those craving a truly unique, gourmet Detroit-style pizza with high-quality, seasonal ingredients.
  • Prime Pizza: Anyone craving a classic, dependable New York slice or a full pie for a casual meal with friends or family.

For decades, Los Angeles was known more for its health-conscious trends and diverse international cuisines than its pizza. However, a new wave of passionate pizzaiolos, often trained in traditional Italian methods or inspired by regional American styles, has cultivated a thriving pizza culture across the sprawling metropolis. From historic red-sauce joints to trendy new establishments, the city's relationship with dough, sauce, and cheese has truly blossomed.

You'll find an incredible spectrum of pizza styles here. Crave a thin, foldable New York slice? Check. Dreaming of a crispy, airy Neapolitan pie blistered in a wood-fired oven? Absolutely. Longing for the thick, caramelized crust of a Detroit-style square or the deep, savory layers of Chicago-style deep dish? LA has it all, plus innovative California-inspired toppings that reflect the city's unique culinary spirit.

Frequently asked questions

What's the best overall pizza style to try in Los Angeles?

Los Angeles excels in a variety of pizza styles, but the city has truly made a name for itself with both innovative California-inspired takes and exceptional Detroit-style pizzas. While Neapolitan and New York are widely available, the creative use of local ingredients and unique crusts in Detroit and Cal-Italian spots often set them apart. Don't limit yourself to just one!

Are there good vegetarian or vegan pizza options in LA?

Absolutely! Many pizzerias in Los Angeles are very accommodating to vegetarian and vegan diets. Most places offer a Margherita or cheese-less vegetable option. Dedicated vegan pizzerias and those with extensive vegan menus are also growing, utilizing plant-based cheeses and meat alternatives that are surprisingly delicious and satisfying. Always check the menu or ask your server.

How expensive is pizza in Los Angeles?

The price of pizza in Los Angeles varies significantly depending on the style and establishment. A single New York-style slice can be as low as $4-6, while a full gourmet wood-fired pizza at an upscale restaurant might range from $20-$35. A full Chicago deep dish can be $30-$45 but easily feeds 2-4 people. Expect to pay anywhere from $15-$50 per person for a sit-down meal, including drinks and appetizers.

Do I need reservations for pizza restaurants in LA?

For highly popular, sit-down pizzerias like Pizzeria Mozza or Jon & Vinny's, reservations are strongly recommended, especially for dinner and weekend brunches. For most New York-style slice shops or casual Detroit-style spots, reservations are not typically needed, and you can usually walk in or order for takeout. Always check the restaurant's website or call ahead to be sure.

What are some lesser-known pizza gems in LA worth exploring?

Beyond the big names, Los Angeles has many fantastic smaller pizza spots. Some local favorites include Blackbird Pizza Shop for unique sourdough pies, Ozzy's Apizza for New Haven-style, and Joe's Pizza for another reliable New York-style fix. Exploring local neighborhoods often reveals charming, independently-owned pizzerias that offer delicious surprises.

Is parking difficult at LA pizza spots?

Parking in Los Angeles can indeed be challenging, especially in popular dining areas like Hollywood, Santa Monica, or Downtown LA. Many establishments offer valet parking (often for a fee), or you might find metered street parking. Residential streets sometimes offer free parking, but pay close attention to signs. For some areas, ride-sharing or public transportation might be the most stress-free option.

Can I find gluten-free pizza options?

Yes, many Los Angeles pizzerias now offer gluten-free crust options. While not every spot will have it, most contemporary or health-conscious pizzerias understand the demand. It's always best to call ahead or check their online menu to confirm availability and to inquire about cross-contamination practices if you have severe allergies.

What's the best neighborhood for a pizza crawl in LA?

For a concentrated pizza crawl, Echo Park and Silver Lake are excellent choices, offering a diverse range within a relatively small area, including Cosa Buona, Masa of Echo Park, and Quarter Sheets Pizza Club. The Fairfax District is also great, with Prime Pizza and Jon & Vinny's located very close to each other, allowing for a delightful double-header.
