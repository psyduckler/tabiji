Quick answer

New Orleans, a city synonymous with culinary excellence, truly delivers when it comes to a perfectly seared steak. While famous for gumbo and jambalaya, the Crescent City boasts a sophisticated steakhouse culture, where prime cuts are revered and dining is an experience in itself.

Best overall

Top verdicts

  • Doris Metropolitan: Foodies seeking an adventurous and high-quality steak experience with unique dry-aging processes and a modern, chic atmosphere.
  • Dickie Brennan's Steakhouse: Those looking for a refined, classic steakhouse experience with a distinct New Orleans touch, perfect for special occasions or business dinners.
  • Mr. John's Steakhouse: Couples or small groups seeking a romantic or intimate steak dinner in a beautiful, historic setting outside the immediate French Quarter.

The city's steakhouse tradition often reflects its broader gastronomic heritage, marrying classic American steakhouse principles with a distinct NOLA sensibility. Many establishments feature local ingredients, unique seasoning blends, and a warm, inviting ambiance that only New Orleans can offer, rooted in a history of elegant dining and hospitality.

From old-school establishments known for their classic preparations and white-tablecloth service to modern, innovative kitchens that infuse global flavors and dry-aging techniques, New Orleans offers a diverse array of steakhouse styles. Whether you seek a prime dry-aged ribeye or a perfectly cooked filet mignon with a Creole cream sauce, there's a steak for every palate.

Frequently asked questions

What defines a 'New Orleans-style' steakhouse?

A New Orleans-style steakhouse typically blends classic American steakhouse elements with Creole and Cajun influences. This might include local seafood appetizers like Oysters Bienville, unique side dishes, or sauces incorporating local flavors, all served with the city's signature hospitality and often in a historic or elegant setting that reflects NOLA's rich culinary traditions.

Are New Orleans steakhouses generally expensive?

Yes, New Orleans' top steakhouses are generally considered high-end dining experiences, with most falling into the $$$$ price tier. Expect to pay between $80 and $150+ per person for a main course, sides, and a drink. There are a few mid-range options like Crescent City Steaks that offer excellent value, but for prime cuts and upscale ambiance, a significant budget is advisable.

Do I need reservations for steakhouses in New Orleans?

For popular steakhouses, especially during peak dining hours or weekends, reservations are strongly recommended. Some of the highest-ranked spots in the French Quarter or Garden District can book up weeks in advance. It's always best to check the restaurant's website or call ahead, particularly if you have a specific time or date in mind.

Are there any casual steakhouse options in New Orleans?

Most true steakhouses in New Orleans lean towards an upscale or refined casual ambiance. Crescent City Steaks offers a more 'old-school' and less formal atmosphere than others, making it a good choice for a delicious steak without the strict dress code. For very casual steak options, you might look at upscale casual restaurants that offer a single, high-quality steak on their menu, rather than a dedicated steakhouse.

What's the typical dress code for New Orleans steakhouses?

The dress code for most upscale New Orleans steakhouses is 'business casual' or 'smart casual.' This typically means collared shirts and slacks for men (jackets often preferred but not required) and elegant dresses, skirts, or dress pants for women. Denim is generally discouraged at the top-tier establishments. It's always a good idea to check the specific restaurant's website for their recommended attire.

Do New Orleans steakhouses offer vegetarian or vegan options?

While steakhouses are primarily meat-focused, many upscale establishments in New Orleans are accustomed to accommodating dietary restrictions. Most will offer a selection of rich side dishes, some of which can be vegetarian or vegan. It's recommended to call ahead and inquire about specific plant-based entrée options if you have strict dietary needs.

Can I find dry-aged steaks in New Orleans?

Yes, several New Orleans steakhouses pride themselves on offering high-quality dry-aged beef. Doris Metropolitan is particularly known for its extensive in-house dry-aging program. Other top-tier steakhouses like Dickie Brennan's and Chophouse New Orleans also feature dry-aged prime cuts, offering enhanced flavor and tenderness for true steak connoisseurs.

Are there steakhouses with live music in New Orleans?

Yes, New Orleans' vibrant music scene extends to its dining establishments. Chophouse New Orleans is a prime example, offering live jazz nightly to complement its prime steaks and classic cocktails. Other restaurants might have live music in their bar areas or during specific events, so it's worth checking their schedules if you're looking for dinner and a show.
