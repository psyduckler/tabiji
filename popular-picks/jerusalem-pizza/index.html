Quick answer

Jerusalem, a city steeped in history and spirituality, has quietly cultivated a thriving and surprisingly diverse pizza landscape. Beyond the ancient stone walls and bustling markets, a new generation of pizzaiolos is crafting everything from classic Neapolitan pies to unique Israeli-fusion creations, drawing crowds and earning fervent praise.

Top verdicts

  • Pizza Flora: Couples and small groups looking for a sophisticated, authentic Neapolitan pizza dinner in a stylish setting.
  • Bardak: Friends seeking a vibrant social atmosphere, craft beers, and delicious, unconventional pizza toppings.
  • Talpiot Pizza (פיצה תלפיות): Families and anyone craving a nostalgic, hearty Israeli-style pizza experience with plenty of toppings.

While pizza might not be the first food that comes to mind when thinking of Jerusalem, the city's rich cultural tapestry and its enthusiastic embrace of international cuisine have paved the way for a remarkable pizza renaissance. Influences from Italy, alongside local Israeli flavors and a strong emphasis on fresh, high-quality ingredients, have created a unique pizza identity.

Visitors and locals alike can now find a wide array of styles: thin-crust Neapolitan pizzas cooked in scorching wood-fired ovens, substantial New York-style slices perfect for a grab-and-go lunch, inventive gourmet pies featuring local cheeses and vegetables, and even classic Israeli-style pizzas found in neighborhood bakeries and takeaways.

Frequently asked questions

What is the typical price range for pizza in Jerusalem?

The price range for a pizza meal in Jerusalem typically falls between ₪60 and ₪120 ($17-$35) per person. Budget-friendly Israeli-style pizzerias and chains can offer options for as low as ₪40-70 ($12-20), while gourmet and authentic Neapolitan spots might lean towards ₪80-140 ($23-40) per person, especially if you include drinks.

Are there vegetarian or vegan pizza options available?

Yes, Jerusalem is very vegetarian-friendly, and most pizza places offer ample vegetarian options, often featuring local vegetables, cheeses, and sauces. Many places, especially the gourmet ones, are also increasingly accommodating to vegans, offering dairy-free cheese or explicitly vegan menu items. Pizzeria Sabich, for example, is a great option for plant-based creativity.

Is it customary to tip in Jerusalem restaurants?

Yes, it is customary to tip in Jerusalem restaurants. The standard practice is to leave a tip of 10-15% of the total bill, provided the service was good. For exceptional service, you might round up to 20%. Tips are usually left in cash or added to the credit card payment, though a cash tip is often preferred by staff.

Can I find gluten-free pizza in Jerusalem?

While not every pizzeria offers gluten-free options, a growing number of establishments, especially modern Italian and gourmet pizza spots, do provide gluten-free crusts. It's always best to call ahead or check their online menu to confirm availability and discuss any severe allergies with the staff upon arrival.

What is 'Israeli-style' pizza?

Israeli-style pizza often features a slightly thicker, chewier crust than Neapolitan, a more robust tomato sauce, and generous toppings which frequently include yellow cheese, olives, corn, mushrooms, and sometimes even a sprinkle of za'atar. It's typically a more substantial and comforting style, popular in local takeaways and bakeries.

Are pizzerias open on Shabbat (Friday evening to Saturday evening)?

Many pizzerias in Jewish areas of Jerusalem, particularly those that are Kosher, will be closed from Friday afternoon until Saturday evening after sundown due to Shabbat. However, some establishments in non-Jewish areas or those that are not Kosher may remain open. It's crucial to check specific opening hours online or call ahead, especially if you plan to dine during Shabbat.

What's the best way to get pizza delivered in Jerusalem?

For pizza delivery in Jerusalem, popular local food delivery apps like Wolt and Ten Bis (which caters primarily to corporate clients) are widely used. Many larger chains like Domino's Pizza also have their own direct delivery services through their websites or dedicated apps. You can also call directly for delivery from many local pizzerias.

Is street parking available near popular pizza spots?

Street parking in central Jerusalem can be challenging and often expensive. Many popular pizza spots are located in pedestrian zones (like Nahalat Shiv'a) or bustling areas (like Mahane Yehuda Market) with limited parking. It's often easier to use public transportation (Light Rail, bus), walk, or park in one of the city's paid parking garages and walk a short distance.
