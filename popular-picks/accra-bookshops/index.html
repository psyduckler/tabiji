Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best bookshop in Accra for African literature?

Vidya Bookstore in Osu is widely considered the best for African literature. They stock an extensive collection of works by Ghanaian and African authors, including classics by Chinua Achebe, Ama Ata Aidoo, and contemporary writers. BookNook.store also specializes in African and Ghanaian literature with excellent online availability.

Where can I find textbooks and academic books in Accra?

The University of Ghana Bookshop at Legon is the top choice for academic texts with competitive prices. EPP Books Services also has an excellent academic section with locations across Accra, including at Legon Mall.

What are typical book prices in Accra?

Book prices in Accra typically range from GHS 50-200 (approximately USD 4-17) for paperbacks. Academic textbooks and imported hardcovers can cost GHS 200-500 or more. Local publications and second-hand options offer more budget-friendly choices.

Can I order books online for delivery in Accra?

Yes, several bookshops offer online ordering with delivery. BookNook.store operates primarily online with delivery across Ghana. City Reads also offers online ordering with pickup locations in Adenta and Osu, plus WhatsApp ordering.
