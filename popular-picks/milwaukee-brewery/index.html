Quick answer

Milwaukee, the 'Brew City,' isn't just living on its legendary past; it's thriving with an unparalleled craft beer scene that rivals any in the nation. From venerable institutions deeply rooted in German brewing traditions to innovative newcomers pushing the boundaries of flavor, the city offers a rich tapestry of brewing experiences.

Top verdicts

  • Third Space Brewing: Craft beer enthusiasts looking for high-quality, award-winning brews in a spacious and energetic setting, perfect for groups.
  • Indeed Brewing Company: Those who appreciate a well-designed space and a wide variety of high-quality craft beers, including some unique small-batch releases.
  • Lakefront Brewery: Tourists and locals seeking a comprehensive brewery experience with history, good food, and a lively communal atmosphere.

The history of beer in Milwaukee is intrinsically linked to its identity, shaped by waves of German immigrants who brought their brewing expertise and established iconic names like Miller, Pabst, and Schlitz. This heritage laid a robust foundation, creating a culture where beer is more than just a drink – it's a way of life, a point of pride, and a communal experience.

Today, Milwaukee's breweries cater to every palate. You’ll find everything from crisp, sessionable lagers and hoppy New England IPAs to adventurous sour ales, barrel-aged stouts, and unique experimental brews. Many venues also offer fantastic food menus, making them perfect destinations for a full meal or just a tasting flight with friends.

Frequently asked questions

What kind of breweries can I expect to find in Milwaukee?

Milwaukee offers a wide range of breweries, from historic institutions like Lakefront Brewery that celebrate the city's brewing heritage, to modern craft breweries like Third Space Brewing known for innovative IPAs, and experimental nano-breweries like Component Brewing Company pushing the boundaries with unique small-batch creations. You'll find everything from traditional lagers to sours, stouts, and more.

Are Milwaukee breweries kid-friendly or dog-friendly?

Many breweries, especially those with spacious taprooms or outdoor patios, are often family-friendly during daytime hours. Lakefront Brewery has a family-friendly tour. For dogs, several breweries like Third Space Brewing and Broken Bat Brewery are explicitly dog-friendly in their taprooms or on their patios, but it's always best to check their specific policies beforehand.

Do Milwaukee breweries serve food, or should I eat beforehand?

Many breweries in Milwaukee are full-service brewpubs offering extensive food menus, such as City Lights Brewing Co. and Good City Brewing. Others, like MobCraft Beer, focus on a specific type of food like pizza. Some taprooms may only offer snacks or rely on rotating food trucks. Check the individual brewery's website to plan your meal accordingly.

What's the best way to do a brewery tour or crawl in Milwaukee?

For a structured experience, consider booking a formal tour at larger breweries like Lakefront. For a self-guided crawl, target a specific neighborhood like Walker's Point (Indeed, MobCraft, Broken Bat, Gathering Place) or Menomonee Valley (Third Space, City Lights) where breweries are within walking distance or a short ride. Ridesharing is ideal for multi-neighborhood crawls.

Are there any special events or seasonal offerings at Milwaukee breweries?

Absolutely! Milwaukee breweries are very active with events. Look for seasonal beer releases, outdoor festivals in summer, Oktoberfest celebrations in fall, live music, trivia nights, yoga events, and charity fundraisers. Following individual breweries on social media or checking their websites is the best way to stay informed.

What are the typical price ranges for beers at Milwaukee craft breweries?

Most craft beers at Milwaukee breweries range from $6 to $9 per pint or pour. Special releases, barrel-aged beers, or higher ABV options might be $10-$15. Flights typically cost $12-$18 for four smaller pours. Overall, a visit for a few drinks usually falls within the $20-$40 per person range.

Is it possible to buy beer to go from Milwaukee breweries?

Yes, nearly all craft breweries in Milwaukee offer beer to go. This typically includes six-packs, four-packs, crowlers (32 oz cans filled on demand), or growlers (64 oz refillable bottles). Many also sell special release bottles or cans directly from their taproom, allowing you to take a taste of Milwaukee home with you.

What's the difference between a brewery and a brewpub in Milwaukee?

A brewery primarily focuses on brewing and selling beer, often with a taproom for on-site consumption and to-go sales. A brewpub, like City Lights Brewing Co. or Good City Brewing, is a restaurant that brews its own beer on the premises and primarily sells it directly to customers alongside a full food menu. Both offer great beer, but brewpubs generally offer a more extensive dining experience.
