Quick answer

Chicago's vegan dining scene has exploded, transforming the Windy City into an unexpected haven for plant-based food lovers seeking everything from classic comfort food to avant-garde culinary experiences. Forget any old notions of limited options; Chicago now boasts a dynamic and diverse array of vegan eateries that cater to every craving and occasion, proving that 'meat-free' certainly doesn't mean 'flavor-free'.

Top verdicts

  • The Chicago Diner: Anyone looking for a dependable, satisfying meal of classic American comfort food, perfect for brunch or a casual dinner with friends.
  • Handlebar: Brunch enthusiasts and those seeking creative, hearty vegan meals in a laid-back, friendly atmosphere with a great beer selection.
  • Bloom Plant Based Kitchen: Foodies seeking a sophisticated, innovative plant-based dining experience for a date night or a celebration.

Historically, Chicago was known for its deep-dish pizza and hearty steakhouses, making the rise of its vegan culture particularly noteworthy. Over the past decade, driven by increasing demand for healthier, more ethical, and sustainable dining, chefs and restaurateurs have embraced plant-based cuisine with open arms, integrating it seamlessly into the city's rich food landscape.

Today, you can find an incredible spectrum of vegan delights, from cozy diners serving up nostalgic American classics and vibrant Pan-Asian fusion spots, to sophisticated New American kitchens pushing the boundaries of plant-based gastronomy. Whether you're after a quick, satisfying bite, a leisurely brunch, or an elegant dinner, Chicago has a vegan restaurant to match your mood and palate.

Frequently asked questions

Are Chicago's vegan restaurants generally affordable?

Chicago offers a wide spectrum of vegan dining prices. You can find very affordable and filling meals at places like Can't Believe It's Not Meat or Vegan Plate, typically in the $15-$25 range. Mid-range options like The Chicago Diner and Handlebar usually run $20-$35, while upscale experiences at Bloom Plant Based Kitchen or Planta Queen can range from $35-$60+ per person, offering something for every budget.

Do I need reservations for vegan restaurants in Chicago?

For more casual or budget-friendly spots, reservations are generally not required. However, for popular brunch destinations such as Handlebar or dinner at upscale venues like Bloom Plant Based Kitchen and Planta Queen, making a reservation is highly recommended, especially on weekends or for larger parties, to ensure you get a table without a long wait.

Are there good vegan options for Chicago's famous deep-dish pizza?

Absolutely! Kitchen 17 is a standout, offering a fantastic vegan deep-dish pizza that rivals its traditional counterparts. They use high-quality plant-based cheeses and toppings, providing an authentic Chicago experience for vegans that is often praised by locals and visitors alike.

Are vegan restaurants in Chicago kid-friendly?

Many vegan restaurants in Chicago are very kid-friendly, especially those with a casual, comfort-food focus like The Chicago Diner or Fancy's, which offer familiar dishes like burgers, fries, and shakes. Handlebar also has a relaxed atmosphere suitable for families. Upscale venues might be better suited for older children or special occasions.

What neighborhoods are best for finding vegan food in Chicago?

Wicker Park, Logan Square, and Lakeview are particularly strong neighborhoods for vegan dining, hosting a high concentration of excellent plant-based eateries, from casual cafes to upscale establishments. River North and Hyde Park also offer notable options, showcasing the city's diverse vegan landscape.

Are there any fully vegan bakeries or dessert spots in Chicago?

While not explicitly listed as full restaurants, many of the venues like The Chicago Diner offer fantastic vegan desserts, especially their famous milkshakes. For dedicated bakeries, check out spots like Liberation Kitchen or Mindful Baking, which specialize in delicious, all-vegan pastries, cakes, and other sweet treats throughout the city.

Can I find gluten-free vegan options at these restaurants?

Most of Chicago's vegan restaurants are very accommodating to dietary restrictions and often provide clearly marked gluten-free options on their menus. Bloom Plant Based Kitchen and Planta Queen, in particular, excel at offering a wide range of delicious gluten-free vegan dishes. Always inform your server about any allergies or dietary needs.

How do Chicago's vegan restaurants compare to other major cities?

Chicago's vegan scene is highly competitive and rapidly growing, offering a breadth of options that can easily stand alongside cities like Los Angeles, New York, or Portland. From innovative fine dining to hearty comfort food and diverse global cuisines, Chicago offers a comprehensive and high-quality plant-based culinary experience that continues to evolve and impress.
