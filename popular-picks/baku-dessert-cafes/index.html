Baku's dessert scene is a delicious fusion of Azerbaijani, Turkish, Persian, and European influences. The city's sweet traditions run deep — from diamond-shaped pakhlava (the local take on baklava, made with cardamom and honey) to crescent-shaped shekerbura pastries filled with ground almonds and sugar.

Tea culture is central to the Azerbaijani dessert experience. Traditional tea houses serve strong black tea from samovars alongside trays of sweets — you'll see locals spending hours over multiple glasses. But Baku also embraces modernity: French-style patisseries like Entrée serve Instagram-worthy croissants, while 24-hour lounges like Malacannes 145 offer European desserts until dawn.
