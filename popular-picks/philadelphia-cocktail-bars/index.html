Frequently asked questions

What are the best cocktail bars in Philadelphia?

The top cocktail bars include The Franklin Mortgage & Investment Company (Prohibition-era speakeasy), Almanac (James Beard Award-winning Japanese-inspired bar), Andra Hem (Scandinavian cocktail lounge), R&D Cocktail Bar (Fishtown's mid-century gem), and The Ranstead Room (hidden speakeasy). Each offers a distinct style from farm-to-glass at Post Haste to vegan-forward at Charlie Was A Sinner.

How much do cocktails cost in Philadelphia?

Craft cocktails in Philadelphia typically range from $12 to $20. Sassafras in Old City is the most affordable with drinks maxing out at $13. Mid-range bars like Next of Kin and Poison Heart charge $13–$17, while upscale spots like Almanac and Caletta run $16–$20. Compared to NYC or LA, Philly offers excellent value.

What neighborhoods have the best cocktail bars in Philly?

Fishtown has the highest concentration with R&D, Next of Kin, Caletta, and Post Haste. Rittenhouse Square offers The Franklin Mortgage, Andra Hem, The Ranstead Room, and La Jefa. Old City features Almanac and Sassafras. For something off-beat, try Poison Heart in Spring Garden or Grace & Proper in Bella Vista.

Are there any speakeasy-style bars in Philadelphia?

Yes — The Ranstead Room hides behind an unmarked door on Ranstead Street, and The Franklin Mortgage & Investment Company is named after an actual Prohibition-era liquor front. Almanac upstairs from Ogawa Sushi also has a hidden, reservation-only feel with just a handful of seats.

What's the best cocktail bar for a date night in Philadelphia?

R&D Cocktail Bar offers dark, candlelit, mid-century modern vibes. Charlie Was A Sinner has trendy, dimly lit atmosphere with velvety booths. Almanac's intimate omakase cocktail experience is ideal for impressing someone special. Caletta's 24-seat Sardinian bar at Hotel Anna & Bel is the most romantic newcomer.

Are there late-night cocktail bars in Philadelphia?

Next of Kin in Fishtown stays open late and is the go-to 1am spot. Sassafras in Old City is open until 2am on weekends. The Franklin Mortgage keeps later hours as well. For post-show drinks, Poison Heart in Spring Garden is ideal.

Which Philadelphia cocktail bars have won awards?

Almanac's Danny Childs is a James Beard Award winner. The Franklin Mortgage has appeared on numerous national best-bars lists since 2010. Post Haste has nationally acclaimed bartending, and Andra Hem has been featured by both Eater and Bon Appétit.
