Quick Picks by Budget

💰 Budget
Donde Fidel Salsa Bar — An iconic, no-frills spot to experience authentic Cartagena salsa in the street.
El Baluarte San Francisco — Perfect for affordable sunset drinks with incredible views over the city walls.
Bazurto Social Club — Dive into the vibrant world of Champeta with energetic live music and budget-friendly drinks.
🍽️ Mid-Range
Cafe Havana — A lively, world-renowned salsa club offering live Cuban music and potent cocktails at reasonable prices for the experience.
Mister Babilla — A classic Cartagena party bar, offering a fun, unpretentious atmosphere with Latin pop and affordable drinks/cover.
El Arsenal: The Rum Box — Explore Colombia's rum heritage with curated tastings and excellent cocktails, a fantastic value for the quality.
✨ Splurge
Alquímico — A multi-level cocktail haven, this trendy rooftop bar delivers exquisite drinks and a chic ambiance for a memorable night out.
El Barón — An intimate speakeasy known for its masterful craft cocktails and sophisticated, low-key atmosphere, ideal for a refined evening.
La Jugada Club House — The quintessential upscale nightclub experience in Cartagena, featuring multiple rooms, top DJs, and a stylish crowd.
