Quick answer

Nashville's pizza scene has truly come into its own, transforming from a supporting act to a headliner in the city's diverse gastronomic portfolio. Forget just hot chicken; Music City now boasts an incredible array of pizzerias, each vying for the title of 'best slice.' From classic New York-style foldable perfection to authentic Neapolitan char and creative, locally-inspired pies, the options are as varied as Nashville's musical genres.

Top verdicts

  • Five Points Pizza: Anyone craving authentic, foldable New York-style pizza in a lively neighborhood setting.
  • City House: Foodies seeking a refined dining experience with gourmet, seasonally-inspired pizzas.
  • Slim & Husky's Pizza Beeria: Casual dining, groups, or anyone looking for creative pizza combinations and a lively atmosphere.

The journey of pizza in Nashville mirrors the city's growth – an influx of new residents and a burgeoning food culture have spurred a delicious evolution. Historically, Italian immigrants brought their culinary traditions to the American South, but it's in recent years that Nashville has truly embraced the global pizza phenomenon, attracting skilled pizzaiolos and innovative chefs eager to put their spin on this beloved dish. This culinary renaissance has created a vibrant competition that only benefits the pizza-loving public.

Today, you can find almost any pizza style imaginable in Nashville. Craving the crisp, airy crust of a traditional Neapolitan pie? We've got it. Longing for the greasy, foldable comfort of a New York slice? It's here. Seeking the thick, caramelized edges of a Detroit-style deep dish or a unique Nashville twist with local ingredients? The city delivers. This guide explores the very best, ensuring you find exactly the pizza experience you're looking for.

Frequently asked questions

What style of pizza is most popular in Nashville?

Nashville boasts a diverse pizza scene, but New York-style and authentic Neapolitan pizzas are arguably the most popular and well-represented. Many pizzerias focus on mastering one of these classic styles, while others offer creative interpretations using local ingredients, making for a rich variety across the city.

Are there good options for gluten-free pizza in Nashville?

Yes, many of Nashville's top pizzerias now offer gluten-free crust options. While cross-contamination can always be a concern in a flour-filled kitchen, venues like Five Points Pizza, Slim & Husky's, and DeSano Pizza Bakery are known for providing tasty gluten-free alternatives, often clearly marked on their menus.

Can I find vegan or vegetarian pizza in Nashville?

Absolutely! Nashville's culinary scene is increasingly catering to dietary preferences. Most pizza places offer vegetarian options, and many, including Slim & Husky's and Folk, can accommodate vegan requests with dairy-free cheese alternatives and a wide selection of vegetable toppings. Always inquire about specific ingredients to ensure your meal aligns with your diet.

Is Nashville pizza expensive compared to other cities?

Pizza prices in Nashville are generally comparable to other major U.S. cities. You can find budget-friendly slices for around $5-8, while a full artisan pie at a premium spot might range from $20-35. The average cost for a mid-range pizza dinner, including a drink, is typically $15-30 per person, offering good value across various price points.

Are there any unique Nashville-specific pizza styles or toppings?

While there isn't one single 'Nashville-style' pizza, many local pizzerias incorporate Southern or Nashville-inspired elements. You might find pizzas featuring hot chicken, pimento cheese, or locally sourced meats and vegetables. Slim & Husky's and Two Boots Nashville are excellent examples of spots that infuse local flavor and creative toppings into their pies.

Which Nashville pizza place is best for a family with kids?

For families, DeSano Pizza Bakery, Mafiaoza's Pizzeria & Pub, and Roma Pizza & Pasta are excellent choices. DeSano offers a lively, open kitchen atmosphere where kids can watch pizzas being made. Mafiaoza's has a classic pub feel with crowd-pleasing pies, and Roma provides generous portions at family-friendly prices. These spots offer casual, welcoming environments for all ages.

What's the best pizza spot for a date night in Nashville?

For a romantic or elevated date night, City House and Folk are top recommendations. Both offer sophisticated atmospheres, exceptional wood-fired pizzas with unique ingredients, and excellent wine or cocktail selections. Roberta's at The Gulch also provides a trendy and vibrant setting suitable for a memorable evening.

Do any Nashville pizzerias offer outdoor seating?

Yes, many Nashville pizzerias provide outdoor seating, especially appealing during pleasant weather. Mafiaoza's in 12 South, Five Points Pizza in East Nashville, and Roberta's at The Gulch all have patio options where you can enjoy your pizza al fresco. It's always a good idea to call ahead to confirm availability, particularly for larger groups or during peak times.
