Quick answer

Knoxville, Tennessee, might be known for its stunning mountain views and vibrant music scene, but its coffee landscape is equally captivating. From cozy nooks perfect for a quiet read to bustling roasteries that hum with conversation, the city offers an unparalleled variety for every coffee lover.

Top verdicts

  • Honeybee Coffee Co. (South Knoxville): Coffee aficionados and remote workers seeking a vibrant yet relaxed environment with top-tier brews.
  • Remedy Coffee: Students and creatives looking for a relaxed, productive environment or a quiet place to meet friends.
  • Wild Love Bakehouse: Anyone with a sweet tooth looking for high-quality baked goods paired with exceptional coffee.

The city's coffee journey has evolved from traditional diners serving simple brews to a sophisticated third-wave movement. This shift reflects Knoxville's broader cultural renaissance, where local businesses prioritize quality, ethical sourcing, and community engagement, creating spaces that are more than just a place for a caffeine fix.

Today, you'll find everything from meticulously prepared single-origin pour-overs and classic Italian espresso drinks to inventive seasonal lattes and delightful coffee-infused concoctions. Many shops also pride themselves on offering a full culinary experience, with artisan pastries, hearty breakfast bites, and light lunch options to complement your drink.

Frequently asked questions

What is the general price range for coffee in Knoxville?

Most specialty coffee drinks in Knoxville range from $3.50 for a drip coffee to $6-7 for a complex latte or pour-over. If you add a pastry or light bite, expect your total to be between $5 and $15 per person, making it an affordable luxury for many.

Are there any coffee shops in Knoxville that roast their own beans?

Yes, several coffee shops in Knoxville pride themselves on roasting their own beans. The Golden Roast is a long-standing example, known for its in-house roasting. Honeybee Coffee Co. and Mahalo Coffee Roasters also roast their beans, ensuring freshness and unique flavor profiles.

Which coffee shops are best for remote work or studying?

Many Knoxville coffee shops offer a conducive environment for remote work or studying. Honeybee Coffee Co. (South Knoxville) and K Brew (North Knoxville) provide ample space and a lively atmosphere. Remedy Coffee and The Golden Roast are also popular choices known for their comfortable seating and reliable Wi-Fi, though they can get busy during peak student hours.

Are Knoxville coffee shops generally dog-friendly?

Many coffee shops in Knoxville welcome dogs, especially on their outdoor patios. K Brew (North Knoxville) is known for being dog-friendly, and other establishments often have water bowls out. It's always best to check with individual shops' policies or look for signs indicating pet-friendliness before bringing your furry friend inside.

Do any Knoxville coffee shops offer drive-thru service?

While many of our top picks focus on the in-store experience, some coffee establishments in Knoxville do offer drive-thru service for convenience, particularly those located on busier thoroughfares. K Brew, for example, has some locations with drive-thru options, perfect for a quick pick-up on your commute.

What kind of food options can I expect at Knoxville coffee shops?

Beyond coffee, you'll find a range of food options. Many specialize in artisan pastries (like Wild Love Bakehouse), while others offer more substantial menus, including breakfast burritos, gourmet toasts, and light lunch fare (such as Frothy Monkey). Always check the specific venue's menu for their culinary offerings.

Are there vegan or gluten-free options available?

Yes, most modern coffee shops in Knoxville are very accommodating to dietary restrictions. You'll typically find a variety of plant-based milk alternatives (oat, almond, soy) for drinks, and many cafes, including Wild Love Bakehouse and K Brew, offer clearly marked vegan and/or gluten-free pastry and food options. It's always wise to ask staff about ingredients.

What's the best way to explore multiple coffee shops in one day?

The best way to explore multiple coffee shops is to focus on walkable areas like Downtown Knoxville and the Old City, where several of our top picks are located close together. Planning a route (like the one suggested in our 'Route' section) that groups venues by neighborhood can maximize your tasting adventure without too much travel.
