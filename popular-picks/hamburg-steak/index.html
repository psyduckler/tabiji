Quick answer

Hamburg is not just the city that gave the world the hamburger — it is one of Germany's premier destinations for serious steak. This port city on the Elbe has been importing premium beef for centuries, and its dining scene reflects a sophisticated appreciation for expertly sourced and prepared meat.

Top verdicts

  • THEO'S Steakhouse: Reserve a window of at least two hours and come hungry — portions are American-large.
  • MASH Steakhouse: The MASH wine cellar tour can be arranged with the booking.
  • Bullerei: The dining room is enormous and noisy; quieter tables are along the brick wall.

The connection runs deep: Hamburg's historic meat market district near Sternschanze, where the old slaughterhouse once stood, is now home to two of the city's best steak restaurants — including TV chef Tim Malzer's Bullerei, housed in the beautifully restored original cattle halls. Nearby, Schlachterborse has been serving steak since 1904, a testament to the neighborhood's carnivorous heritage.

Modern Hamburg adds international flair to this tradition. THEO'S at the Grand Elysee Hotel delivers a New York steakhouse experience with 40-day dry-aged beef cooked in 800-degree ovens. MASH brings Scandinavian precision to Elbe-front dining with cuts from Uruguay, Denmark, and the USA. And Estancia Steaks, run by the Berg family since 1977, serves Argentinian-inspired beef in a stunning Afrika Haus building near the Rathaus.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best steakhouse in Hamburg?

THEO'S at the Grand Elysee Hotel is widely considered the best steakhouse in Hamburg and has been ranked #1 in all of Germany by Falstaff. Their 40-day dry-aged prime beef, cooked in an 800-degree Southbend oven, is exceptional. For a more casual but equally impressive steak experience, Bullerei by TV chef Tim Malzer and MASH on the Elbe riverfront are strong contenders.

How much does a steak dinner cost in Hamburg?

Steak dinners in Hamburg range from around 20-30 EUR at casual spots like Block House and Billy the Butcher, to 45-80 EUR at mid-range restaurants like Estancia Steaks and Schlachterborse, up to 80-150+ EUR at premium steakhouses like THEO'S and MASH for dry-aged cuts and sharing steaks for two.

Do I need reservations for Hamburg steakhouses?

For upscale steakhouses like THEO'S, MASH, [m]eatery, and Butcher's American Steakhouse, reservations are strongly recommended, especially on Friday and Saturday evenings. Block House Jungfernstieg operates on a walk-in basis with no reservations. Bullerei accepts reservations but also has a walk-in bar area.

What makes Hamburg a great city for steak?

Hamburg's position as Germany's largest port city gives it access to premium beef from around the world. The city's historic meat market district (near Sternschanze) and its international trading heritage have created a sophisticated steak culture. From the traditional Schlachterborse, which has served steak since 1904 near the old slaughterhouse, to modern Danish-inspired MASH on the Elbe, Hamburg offers remarkable variety.

Where can I get dry-aged steak in Hamburg?

THEO'S offers 40-day dry-aged prime beef from Ireland, USA, and Germany. MASH serves dry-aged cuts from Uruguay, Denmark, and the USA. Butcher's American Steakhouse has a glass dry-age cabinet visible from the entrance where they age their own beef. [m]eatery in the SIDE Hotel also specializes in premium dry-aged cuts.

Is there a connection between Hamburg and the hamburger steak?

Yes. The Hamburg steak (Frikadelle or Hamburger Steak) originated from Hamburg's port, where minced beef patties were popular among sailors and emigrants in the 18th and 19th centuries. German immigrants brought the concept to America, where it eventually evolved into the modern hamburger. Hamburg proudly claims this culinary heritage.

What is the best area in Hamburg for steakhouses?

The Sternschanze/Schanzenviertel area has the highest concentration of great steak spots, with Bullerei and Schlachterborse both located near the historic meat market. The Altstadt (Old Town) near the Rathaus has Estancia Steaks and Block House Jungfernstieg. For waterfront steak dining, head to Altona where MASH sits on the Grosse Elbstrasse overlooking the Elbe.

Can I visit multiple steakhouses in one day in Hamburg?

Absolutely. Hamburg is very compact and well-connected by public transit. A great steak crawl: start with lunch at Block House Jungfernstieg for a classic Hamburg steak, then take the U-Bahn to Sternschanze for a late afternoon appetizer at Bullerei, and finish with a premium dinner at THEO'S in Rotherbaum or MASH on the Elbe. All within a 20-minute transit radius.
