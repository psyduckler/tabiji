Quick answer

San Diego's brunch culture is built on year-round sunshine, ocean views, and a Mexican-Californian fusion you won't find anywhere else. With most brunch spots priced at $14–$25 per person, this guide covers the 12 restaurants that locals on Reddit consistently recommend.

Top verdicts

  • Morning Glory: Show up before opening on weekends or expect a 90-minute wait by 10 AM.
  • The Cottage La Jolla: Walk-ins only — arrive by 9 AM on weekends, or come Tuesday morning to skip the wait entirely.
  • Cocina 35: Counter service keeps lines moving, but try the original Sixth Avenue location over the satellites.

We analyzed hundreds of Reddit posts across r/sandiego, and r/SanDiegan spanning 2022–2025. Every restaurant was mentioned in at least 3 separate threads by different users.

Five neighborhoods dominate: Little Italy for the Instagram scene, La Jolla for ocean views, Hillcrest for the most options per block, Normal Heights for the new-school crowd, and Pacific Beach for the laid-back surfer vibe.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best brunch spot in San Diego?

Morning Glory in Little Italy is the most recommended on Reddit. Madi (4.8 stars) and Kono's Cafe (4.8 stars) are close contenders.

Where should I go for brunch in La Jolla?

The Cottage La Jolla (4.5 stars, 25+ years), Cody's La Jolla (ocean views), and Caroline's Seaside Cafe (La Jolla Shores).

What are the best Mexican brunch spots in San Diego?

Cocina 35 (downtown) for chilaquiles. Cocina de Barrio (Hillcrest) for modern Mexican with loaded French toast.

Do San Diego brunch spots take reservations?

Most popular spots don't take reservations. Madi and Farmer & The Seahorse accept them. Arrive early on weekends.

How much does brunch cost in San Diego?

$15-25 per person at most spots. Kono's Cafe is $8-15. Farmer & The Seahorse is $25-40.

What is the best brunch with a view in San Diego?

Caroline's Seaside Cafe overlooks La Jolla Shores. Cody's La Jolla has ocean views from its dining room.

What are the best kid-friendly brunch spots?

Great Maple in Hillcrest has pancake toppings for kids. Kono's Cafe is casual and affordable.

What are the best brunch spots with ocean views in San Diego?

For stunning ocean vistas while you dine, look no further than <a href="#codys-la-jolla"><strong>Cody's La Jolla</strong></a>, offering prime views of La Jolla Cove. Another fantastic option with a more casual feel is <a href="#carolines-seaside-cafe"><strong>Caroline's Seaside Cafe</strong></a>, providing lovely beach views right on the sand at Scripps Beach.

Which San Diego brunch spots are family-friendly?

Several spots cater wonderfully to families. <a href="#great-maple"><strong>Great Maple</strong></a> is known for its spacious setting, diverse menu, and delicious comfort food appealing to all ages. <a href="#konos-cafe"><strong>Kono's Cafe</strong></a> in Pacific Beach offers a casual, vibrant atmosphere right by the beach, perfect for kids and a quick, satisfying meal.

Where can I find unique Mexican brunch in San Diego?

San Diego is a hub for incredible Mexican cuisine. For a classic, authentic experience, try <a href="#cocina-35"><strong>Cocina 35</strong></a>, celebrated for its legendary chilaquiles. If you prefer a modern twist with a trendy vibe, <a href="#cocina-de-barrio"><strong>Cocina De Barrio</strong></a> offers creative Mexican-inspired brunch plates and craft cocktails in a stylish setting.

Do I need reservations for San Diego brunch?

For popular weekend brunch spots like <a href="#morning-glory"><strong>Morning Glory</strong></a>, <a href="#madi"><strong>Madi</strong></a>, and <a href="#the-cottage-la-jolla"><strong>The Cottage La Jolla</strong></a>, reservations are highly recommended, sometimes essential, to avoid long waits. Many places use online booking systems like OpenTable. For smaller or more casual spots like <a href="#konos-cafe"><strong>Kono's Cafe</strong></a>, walk-ins are common but expect a wait during peak hours.

What's the most Instagrammable brunch spot in San Diego?

Without a doubt, <a href="#morning-glory"><strong>Morning Glory</strong></a> takes the crown for its highly photogenic pink interior, unique bathroom, and incredibly artistic dishes, making every corner a photo opportunity. <a href="#madi"><strong>Madi</strong></a> also offers a chic, modern aesthetic and beautifully plated food perfect for capturing stylish brunch moments.

Are there budget-friendly brunch options in San Diego?

Absolutely! <a href="#konos-cafe"><strong>Kono's Cafe</strong></a> is a legendary choice for delicious and affordable beachside brunch that offers great value. <a href="#cocina-35"><strong>Cocina 35</strong></a> provides generous portions of authentic Mexican dishes at a great price point, and <a href="#carolines-seaside-cafe"><strong>Caroline's Seaside Cafe</strong></a> offers tasty options with a view that won't break the bank.
