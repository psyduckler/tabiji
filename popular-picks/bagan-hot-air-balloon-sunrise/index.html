Quick answer

Bagan isn't just a hot air balloon destination — it's THE hot air balloon destination. Floating over thousands of ancient brick temples as the sun rises through the morning mist is routinely called a life-changing experience.

Top verdicts

  • Balloons Over Bagan: First-time balloonists who want proven reliability and safety
  • Oriental Ballooning: Travelers seeking a more personal, less touristy experience
  • Golden Eagle Ballooning: Travelers wanting certified European equipment at mid-range prices

The Bagan Archaeological Zone contains over 2,000 Buddhist monuments dating from the 9th to 13th centuries, spread across a vast plain along the Irrawaddy River. From the ground, it's magnificent. From 1,500 feet up in a hot air balloon basket? Absolutely transcendent.

Four operators run sunrise flights during the dry season (October–April): Balloons Over Bagan (the original), Oriental Ballooning, Golden Eagle, and STT Ballooning. Each offers classic and premium tiers, with the main differences being basket size, passenger count, and post-flight amenities.

Frequently asked questions

When is the best time to take a hot air balloon ride in Bagan?

The balloon season runs from mid-October to mid-April. The high season (November-February) offers cool weather, clear skies, and stable winds. December and January provide the best photography conditions but require booking 3-4 weeks in advance due to peak demand.

How much does a hot air balloon ride cost in Bagan?

Prices range from $310-$450 USD per person. Classic flights (12-16 passengers) cost $310-$350, while premium flights (8 passengers with compartments) cost $380-$450. Prices are higher during peak season (mid-December to mid-January).

How long is a Bagan balloon flight?

The actual flight lasts 45 minutes to 1 hour depending on wind conditions. The entire experience, including hotel pickup, pre-flight briefing, flight, post-flight champagne celebration, and return transfer, takes approximately 3-4 hours.

Which balloon company is best in Bagan?

Balloons Over Bagan is the original operator (since 1999) with the longest safety record and largest fleet. Oriental Ballooning offers a more intimate experience with smaller baskets. Golden Eagle and STT provide good value alternatives. All major operators maintain high safety standards.

What is included in a Bagan balloon ride?

Standard inclusions: hotel pickup (around 5:00 AM), light breakfast before flight, safety briefing, 45-60 minute flight, sparkling wine and fruit reception after landing, flight certificate, souvenir cap, and return hotel transfer. Premium flights add full continental breakfast and smaller basket sizes.

How far in advance should I book a Bagan balloon ride?

Book at least 1-2 weeks in advance during shoulder season (October, March-April). For peak season (November-February), book 3-4 weeks ahead. December 20 to January 10 is the busiest period and may require booking months in advance.

Is the Bagan balloon ride worth the price?

Most travelers rate it as a once-in-a-lifetime experience worth the splurge. Floating over 2,000+ ancient temples at sunrise is genuinely unique. However, if budget is tight, you can still enjoy Bagan's temples beautifully from ground level by e-bike or horse cart.

What should I wear for a Bagan balloon ride?

Wear comfortable, layered clothing. Mornings are cool (15-20°C) before sunrise but warm quickly. Closed-toe shoes are essential for landing on uneven terrain. Bring a hat and sunglasses for after sunrise. Avoid loose scarves that could catch on equipment.
