Quick answer

Nashville might be famous for its hot chicken and country music, but beneath the neon glow and twangy tunes, a serious burger scene thrives. From no-frills, perfectly griddled patties to elevated, locally sourced masterpieces, Music City offers a patty for every palate.

Top verdicts

  • The Pharmacy Burger Parlor & Beer Garden: Those seeking a lively atmosphere, an incredible beer selection, and a top-tier gourmet burger experience.
  • Burger Up: Diners looking for a gourmet burger experience with high-quality, ethically sourced ingredients and creative pairings.
  • Hugh-Baby's BBQ & Burger Shop: Fans of classic smash burgers, quick and delicious comfort food, and a straightforward, satisfying meal.

The burger's journey in Nashville mirrors the city's growth – starting with classic American diners and roadside stands, evolving into a sophisticated culinary experience. Historically, places like Rotier's (now closed) set the standard for simple, satisfying burgers, paving the way for today's diverse landscape.

Today, you'll find everything from juicy, hand-smashed creations reminiscent of old-school diners, to gourmet stacks piled high with artisanal ingredients and house-made sauces. Whether you crave a classic cheeseburger, a spicy specialty, or a plant-based alternative, Nashville’s burger joints are pushing the boundaries of what a burger can be.

Frequently asked questions

What style of burger is most common in Nashville?

Nashville's burger scene is quite diverse, but you'll find a strong presence of both 'Classic American' diner-style burgers and 'Smash Burgers.' Many establishments also elevate the experience with 'Gourmet & Gastropub' offerings, focusing on local ingredients and unique toppings.

Are there good vegetarian or vegan burger options in Nashville?

Yes, many of the top burger spots in Nashville, especially those categorized as 'Gourmet & Gastropub' like The Pharmacy and Burger Up, offer excellent vegetarian or vegan patty substitutions. Always check the menu or ask your server for current options and customization possibilities.

What's the typical price range for a good burger in Nashville?

The price range varies significantly depending on the style and ingredients. You can find excellent 'Budget Bites' for $10-15 (like Gabby's), 'Mid-Range Marvels' for $14-22 (like M.L.Rose), and 'Gourmet Grills' or 'Splurge' options for $16-30 (like Burger Up or Husk's bar burger).

Do I need reservations for Nashville burger restaurants?

For most casual or smash burger spots, reservations are not necessary and often not accepted. However, for more upscale establishments or those with a specific dining experience like Burger Up, or if you plan to visit Husk, reservations are highly recommended, especially on weekends or for larger groups.

Which Nashville burger joint is best for a lively atmosphere?

The Pharmacy Burger Parlor & Beer Garden in East Nashville is arguably the best for a lively atmosphere, especially with its expansive outdoor beer garden. M.L.Rose also offers a vibrant, neighborhood pub vibe, perfect for socializing.

Are there any cash-only burger places I should know about?

Yes, Gabby's Burgers & Fries, a beloved Nashville institution known for its classic, no-frills burgers, is strictly cash-only. It's always a good idea to carry some cash when exploring local, independent eateries in Nashville, just in case.

What's the best burger for a late-night craving in Nashville?

For late-night burger cravings, Fat Mo's is a classic choice, offering quick, satisfying, and greasy burgers through its drive-thru. Daddy's Dogs also serves up a great burger and is often open late, catering to the after-hours crowd with its unique charm.

Can I find unique, locally-inspired burger toppings in Nashville?

Absolutely! Nashville's culinary creativity extends to its burgers. You'll find toppings like pimento cheese, fried green tomatoes, local jams, and even hot chicken-inspired sauces. Burger Up and The Pharmacy are particularly known for their inventive and locally-sourced ingredient combinations.
