Quick answer

Nashville's best brunch spots, ranging from Southern comfort to modern American, offer a delightful start to your day. This Editor-curated guide highlights the best morning meals in Music City, with options ranging from communal Southern feasts to hidden gems in East Nashville.

Top verdicts

  • Monell's: First-come, first-served and one of Nashville's longest brunch waits — arrive at 9 AM Sundays.
  • Biscuit Love: Get in line by 8:30 AM weekends; East Nashville and Hillsboro Village locations have shorter waits.
  • Adele's: Reservations are essential — book 2 weeks ahead for a window seat.

Nashville’s brunch scene runs deep — way beyond the tourist-heavy Broadway joints. From Germantown’s communal tables at Monell’s (4.8★, 7,667 reviews) to the upscale weekend buffet at Adele’s in The Gulch, Music City serves some of the South’s most memorable morning meals. Budget $12–$25 per person at casual spots, $30–$50 at upscale destinations.

Four neighborhoods dominate: Germantown for old-school Southern charm, The Gulch for polished all-day dining, East Nashville for creative and eclectic fare, and 12 South for bakery-cafe vibes. Most spots are busiest Saturday and Sunday 10 AM–1 PM. Arrive by 9:30 AM or expect a wait.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best brunch spot in Nashville?

Monell's is the most beloved by locals for its communal-style Southern breakfast. Adele's brunch buffet is the Reddit favorite for a special occasion.

Where do Nashville locals go for brunch?

Cafe Roze in East Nashville, Stay Golden in The Nations, and Dino's Bar & Grill are where locals eat. Avoid Broadway tourist traps.

Is Biscuit Love worth the wait?

Yes, but go early on a weekday. The Gulch location has the longest lines. The East Side Biscuit Bonanza and Princess Hot Chicken Biscuit are worth it.

How much does brunch cost in Nashville?

Budget $15-25 per person at casual spots like Biscuit Love and Pancake Pantry. Upscale brunch at Adele's or Henrietta Red runs $30-50 per person.

What is the most iconic Nashville brunch dish?

Hot chicken and waffles, biscuits with sausage gravy, and the Loveless Cafe's scratch-made biscuits are the signature Nashville brunch dishes.

Do Nashville brunch spots take reservations?

Adele's, Henrietta Red, Husk, and Noko accept reservations. Biscuit Love, Pancake Pantry, and Monell's are first-come, first-served.

What neighborhoods have the best brunch in Nashville?

East Nashville (Cafe Roze, Noko, Dino's), Germantown (Monell's, Henrietta Red), The Gulch (Biscuit Love, Adele's), and 12 South (Butter Milk Ranch) are the top brunch neighborhoods.

What is the most famous brunch spot in Nashville?

While 'most famous' can be subjective, <a href="#biscuit-love"><strong>Biscuit Love</strong></a> is arguably the city's most talked-about biscuit spot, drawing crowds for its innovative Southern breakfast. <a href="#the-loveless-cafe"><strong>The Loveless Cafe</strong></a> also holds legendary status as a historic Nashville institution, especially for its world-renowned biscuits.

Where can I find the best biscuits for brunch in Nashville?

For an undisputed biscuit experience, head to <a href="#the-loveless-cafe"><strong>The Loveless Cafe</strong></a>, where their scratch-made biscuits are truly legendary. <a href="#biscuit-love"><strong>Biscuit Love</strong></a> also offers exceptional, creative biscuit dishes, including their famous Princess Hot Chicken biscuit, making it another top contender.

Are reservations needed for brunch in Nashville?

For many of Nashville's popular and upscale brunch spots like <a href="#adeles"><strong>Adele's</strong></a>, <a href="#husk-nashville"><strong>Husk Nashville</strong></a>, or <a href="#henrietta-red"><strong>Henrietta Red</strong></a>, reservations are highly recommended and often essential, especially for weekend brunch. Walk-in spots like <a href="#the-pancake-pantry"><strong>The Pancake Pantry</strong></a> or <a href="#biscuit-love"><strong>Biscuit Love</strong></a> will likely have a wait.

What's a unique brunch experience in Nashville?

For a truly unique experience, <a href="#monells"><strong>Monell's</strong></a> offers a one-of-a-kind communal-table, family-style Southern brunch that fosters conversation among strangers. If you're into a different kind of vibe, <a href="#dinos-bar-grill"><strong>Dino's Bar & Grill</strong></a> provides an 'anti-brunch' dive bar atmosphere with surprisingly delicious hot chicken French toast.

Where do locals go for brunch in Nashville?

Locals often frequent neighborhood gems away from the main tourist hubs. <a href="#cafe-roze"><strong>Cafe Roze</strong></a> in East Nashville and <a href="#stay-golden"><strong>Stay Golden</strong></a> in The Nations are popular choices for a stylish yet less touristy brunch. <a href="#the-butter-milk-ranch"><strong>The Butter Milk Ranch</strong></a> in 12 South is also a beloved local bakery-cafe for pastries and savory dishes.

Can I find healthy brunch options in Nashville?

Yes, many Nashville brunch spots offer lighter or healthier alternatives. Places like <a href="#cafe-roze"><strong>Cafe Roze</strong></a> often feature fresh, ingredient-driven dishes, and most upscale establishments like <a href="#adeles"><strong>Adele's</strong></a> or <a href="#henrietta-red"><strong>Henrietta Red</strong></a> will have options like salads, vegetable-focused plates, or lighter protein choices on their menus.
