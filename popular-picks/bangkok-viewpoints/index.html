Quick answer

Bangkok is one of the world's great cities for rooftop views — a sprawling metropolis where glittering skyscrapers, ancient temple spires, and the winding Chao Phraya River create a panoramic canvas unlike any other.

  • Sky Bar at Lebua (State Tower): The iconic, bucket-list Bangkok rooftop bar experience
  • Vertigo and Moon Bar at Banyan Tree Bangkok: Romantic open-air rooftop dining with unobstructed 360-degree views

From ultra-modern observation decks with glass-floor walkways to legendary rooftop bars that have graced Hollywood films, from historic temple heights offering old-city panoramas to riverside lounges with Wat Arun sunset views, Bangkok delivers viewpoints for every budget and mood. The city's rooftop bar scene is arguably the finest in Southeast Asia, with new openings constantly raising the bar.

Frequently asked questions

What is the highest viewpoint in Bangkok?

The highest viewpoint in Bangkok is the King Power Mahanakhon SkyWalk. Located on the 78th floor at 314 meters, it offers a thrilling outdoor observation deck and glass tray experience with unparalleled panoramic views of the entire city. Tickets start around 880 THB ($25 USD). The Standard Bangkok Mahanakhon now occupies the rooftop bar space and claims the title of Thailand's highest rooftop bar.

Are there any affordable viewpoints in Bangkok?

Yes. The Golden Mount (Wat Saket) offers historic charm and expansive Old Bangkok views for just 100 THB ($3 USD). Most rooftop bars have no cover charge but require a minimum drink purchase (300-500 THB per cocktail). Tichuca is considered one of the more budget-friendly rooftop bars. Some hotel lobbies and shopping mall upper floors also offer free skyline views.

What is the best time to visit Bangkok's viewpoints?

The best time is just before sunset, around 5:30-6:30 PM depending on season. This lets you experience the city in daylight, witness the sunset, and then see Bangkok transform into a glittering nightscape. For the Mahanakhon SkyWalk, arrive by 5 PM for the full experience. For rooftop bars, arrive at opening (5-6 PM) on weekends to avoid waits. Weekdays are less crowded.

Is there a dress code for Bangkok's rooftop bars?

Yes, most upscale rooftop bars enforce a smart casual dress code. No shorts, sleeveless shirts (for men), flip-flops, or athletic wear. Sky Bar at Lebua and Vertigo are the strictest. Tichuca and Above Eleven are slightly more relaxed but still no flip-flops. The Golden Mount (a temple) requires covered shoulders and knees. Always check the specific venue's policy before visiting.

Which viewpoint is best for a romantic evening?

Vertigo and Moon Bar at Banyan Tree Bangkok is the top choice for romance — open-air dining 61 floors up with no glass barriers between you and the skyline. Sky Bar at Lebua is the classic bucket-list pick. For a more intimate riverside experience, Sala Rattanakosin's rooftop with its direct Wat Arun view is unmatched. ThreeSixty Jazz Lounge adds live music to the mix.

Are Bangkok's viewpoints suitable for families with children?

The King Power Mahanakhon SkyWalk is the most family-friendly option with engaging experiences for all ages. The Golden Mount (Wat Saket) is also suitable for families and offers a cultural experience. Most rooftop bars have age restrictions (usually 20+ after certain hours) and are geared toward adults. Some venues allow children during early hours before 8 PM — check ahead.

Do I need reservations for Bangkok rooftop bars?

Reservations are strongly recommended for Vertigo (the restaurant), Sky Bar (for VIP seating), and Red Sky Bar for dinner. Moon Bar, Octave, Tichuca, and Above Eleven are walk-in friendly but expect queues on Friday and Saturday evenings. Arriving at opening time (5-5:30 PM) is the best strategy to secure a prime spot without a reservation.

How much should I budget for a rooftop bar visit in Bangkok?

Expect to spend 800-2,000 THB ($23-$57 USD) per person for 2-3 cocktails at most rooftop bars. Sky Bar and Vertigo are the most expensive at 500-700 THB per cocktail. Tichuca and Above Eleven offer cocktails from 350-500 THB. The Mahanakhon SkyWalk costs 880 THB entry. The Golden Mount is just 100 THB. Budget 1,500-3,000 THB total for a memorable rooftop evening including drinks and snacks.

Planning your Bangkok viewpoint tour

The ideal Bangkok viewpoint itinerary combines daytime cultural panoramas with sunset rooftop experiences.

Start your day at the Golden Mount (Wat Saket) for a morning climb with fewer crowds and cooler temperatures. After exploring the old city — Grand Palace, Wat Pho — head to Sala Rattanakosin for a late-afternoon cocktail with Wat Arun views. For sunset, the King Power Mahanakhon SkyWalk (arrive by 5 PM) offers the ultimate daytime panorama.

For evening, choose your rooftop based on your vibe: Sky Bar at Lebua for the bucket-list experience, Vertigo & Moon Bar for romance, Octave for a local feel, or Tichuca for Instagram-worthy jungle vibes. ThreeSixty at the Hilton adds live jazz to the mix. Above Eleven on Soi 11 is perfect for late-night food and drinks.

Budget roughly ฿2,000–4,000 ($57–$115 USD) for a full day combining the SkyWalk entry plus 2–3 drinks at a rooftop bar, or keep it under ฿500 ($14) with just the Golden Mount and Sala Rattanakosin.
