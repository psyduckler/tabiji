Bodrum — Crusader castle, twin crescent harbors, and the Aegean-peninsula anchor of Turkey's summer party circuit — runs the country's most industrial-scale jet-ski and gulet-cruise scam scene. Volume is concentrated at the beach strips of **Gümbet**, **Yalıkavak**, and **Turgutreis**, at the **Bodrum Milas Airport (BJV)**, and around the **Yalıkavak marina** (Palmarina) where the sunset-restaurant scene lives.

The **jet-ski damage-deposit cycle** is the signature Bodrum scam and has been covered almost every summer for years in *Hürriyet Daily News* and r/Turkey. Operators on Gümbet, Yalıkavak, and Turgutreis beaches take €400 cash deposits on a 30-minute jet-ski rental and, at return, point at pre-existing scratches near the exhaust or the front hull and claim €150–€250 "repair fees." The deposit is held in cash and rarely returned in full. The defense: pay only with a chargeback-able credit card, photograph the jet ski from eight angles before riding (date/time-stamped on the phone), and refuse to accept a cash-deposit arrangement. Bodrum Turizm Polisi intervene in these disputes but cannot recover cash deposits after the fact — the credit-card chargeback is the only mechanism that works from abroad.

The risk radiates out in three corridors. The **Yalıkavak Palmarina sunset-restaurant unposted cover charge** adds €15–€30 per person "service" to a bill that already carries a visible 10% service line; the contract is ambiguous and most tourists pay. The **gulet-boat day-cruise downgrade** — a €80 per-person "private gulet" cruise booked off a beach chalkboard is downgraded at the marina to a 30-person budget trip; book through the marina's official office or Viator/GetYourGuide to avoid this. And the **Bodrum Milas Airport (BJV) flat-rate taxi** touts quote 2,200–3,000 lira for the 35-km run to Bodrum center; the metered fare is 1,000–1,300 lira and the official Havaş shuttle bus is 250 lira.

A safe default: take the **Havaş airport shuttle** from BJV to Bodrum (250 lira, 50 minutes); use **BiTaksi** for city rides; rent jet skis only from operators who accept credit-card deposits (never cash or passport); photograph every rental before riding; book gulet cruises only through the Bodrum Marina Booking Office or through Viator / GetYourGuide; and call **155** (Polis İmdat) or the Bodrum Tourist Police at **+90 252 313 1234** from the Marina's security post. The Bodrum Turizm Polisi desk is at **Atatürk Caddesi 3**, near the main harbor and castle entrance.
