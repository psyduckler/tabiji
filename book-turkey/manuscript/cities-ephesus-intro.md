Ephesus — the Roman-era ruins 60 km south of İzmir and one of the Mediterranean's most-visited archaeological sites — sits inside the Selçuk municipality. Volume is at the **main Ephesus site gate** (the upper and lower gates both see heavy traffic), at **Selçuk bus station** where cruise and Kuşadası arrivals meet freelance minibus drivers, and along the **Selçuk-to-Ephesus road** where freelance "guides" wait for tourists to accept a ride.

The **Ephesus freelance "official guide" scam** is the volume risk. Men in polo shirts at the main gate approach English-speaking tourists with laminated cards claiming to be "licensed guides," and offer walking tours for 500–800 lira per person. The real Ministry-licensed *turist rehberi* are available only at the ticket office (the combined "Ephesus + Terrace Houses" ticket runs approximately €52 total — roughly 1,800–2,400 TL at 2026 rates, sold separately at ~€40 Ephesus and ~€15 Terrace Houses — and a licensed guide can be added at the ticket desk), or through a Viator / GetYourGuide / Klook booking that names the guide's license number. Freelance "guides" at the gate are unlicensed; the Ministry of Culture and Tourism has posted Turkish and English warnings at both gates.

The risk radiates out in three corridors. The **Selçuk bus-station minibus-tour substitution** sells "Ephesus + House of Virgin Mary + Sirince Village" packages at 800–1,200 lira per person that substitute four commission stops (leather, carpet, olive oil, Turkish delight) for meaningful time at the actual ruins. The **"Terrace Houses combined-ticket" confusion** at the main gate — some touts claim the separately-priced Terrace Houses ticket (which is real, roughly €15 / 500–700 TL at 2026 rates) requires a "guided-entry only" surcharge, which is false. And the **post-visit "handmade olive-wood" gift-shop kickback circuit** — the Selçuk tour minibus stops at a single shop where items are 3–4× retail.

A safe default: take the **dolmuş** (shared minibus) from Selçuk bus station to Ephesus (35 lira, 15 minutes), not a tout-offered "tour minibus"; buy combined Ephesus + Terrace Houses tickets at the official ticket booths (the Ministry prices these in euros — ~€40 + ~€15 — payable in TL at the day's exchange rate); for a guided experience, book through the Ministry-licensed operators listed at goturkiye.com or through Viator / GetYourGuide; ignore every English-speaking approach at the main gate; and call **155** (Polis İmdat) or the Selçuk Jandarma at **+90 232 892 6001**. The Ephesus site has no Turizm Polisi desk; the nearest tourist-police presence is at the Selçuk bus station.
