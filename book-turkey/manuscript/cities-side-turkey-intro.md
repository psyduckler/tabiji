Side — Antalya-coast Roman-era town built around a spectacular Temple of Apollo on a stone-fringed peninsula — is quieter than its Alanya and Antalya neighbors but with a scam footprint shaped by cruise and package-holiday traffic. Volume is at the **theater-area harbor restaurant cluster**, at the **Apollo Temple promenade**, on **Side Beach** east of the old town, and along the **Side-to-Manavgat Waterfalls minibus route**.

The **theater-area harbor restaurant tourist menus** without posted prices are the volume risk. The restaurants clustered along Liman Caddesi between the ancient theater and the Temple of Apollo target the peak sunset window with "menu without prices" tables; the final bill routinely runs 40–60% higher than the posted-menu restaurants a block inland on Soğuksu Caddesi. The defense is simple: walk one street back from the waterfront and eat where the menu is on the door.

The risk radiates out in three corridors. The **"Roman coin" antique-shop scam** on Liman Caddesi — shops sell modern-struck "Byzantine" or "Seljuk" coins as genuine at 300–1,000 lira each; real antique coins in Turkey require a provenance certificate from the Ministry of Culture that the shop cannot produce. The **pre-payment beach umbrella and lounger scam** — young men on Side Beach collect "umbrella fees" (50–80 lira) from arriving tourists; they are not employed by the umbrella venue and the actual attendant re-collects the fee when the real venue becomes clear. And the **Side-to-Manavgat Waterfalls minibus "tour" package** sold by street touts at 250–400 lira per person typically includes a single carpet-shop kickback stop between Side and the waterfalls.

A safe default: eat one street back from Liman Caddesi (Soğuksu, Sümbül, Turgut Reis) where menus are posted; buy antiques only from shops that produce a Ministry-of-Culture provenance certificate; pay umbrella fees only to the uniformed venue attendant at the umbrella station, not to walking collectors; take the regulated *dolmuş* from Side to Manavgat Waterfalls (50 lira each way) rather than a tourist-minibus tour; and call **155** or the Side Jandarma at **+90 242 753 1020**. The nearest Turizm Polisi desk is in Manavgat, 15 km north; for urgent tourist-specific help, the Antalya Tourist Police at **+90 242 247 2400** dispatches regional support.
