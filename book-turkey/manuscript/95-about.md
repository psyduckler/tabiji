# About Tabiji {-}

Tabiji is a travel-safety research organization. We document scams, health risks, and local advisories for travelers across more than fifty cities worldwide, and we publish everything as a free public resource at **[tabiji.ai](https://tabiji.ai)**.

We started this series after noticing that most scam roundups online skip the one thing travelers actually need in the moment: the exact phrase, the exact move, that ends the interaction. Remembering a list of seventy-eight scams is hard when you are tired and under pressure. Remembering six patterns is much easier. That is the shape we have tried to give this book.

Every scam documented here was drawn from Turkish and Turkish-facing English press — *Hürriyet Daily News*, *Daily Sabah*, *Anadolu Agency*, *Cumhuriyet*, *Milliyet*, *Sabah*, *Sözcü*, *TRT World* — cross-referenced against Turkish National Police (*Polis İmdat* 155), Jandarma (156), Turizm Polisi advisory bulletins, Ministry of Culture and Tourism complaint records, and firsthand traveler accounts on Reddit's r/Turkey, r/Istanbul, r/Antalya, r/solotravel, and the destination-specific subreddits. Where we have named documented incidents, arrest dates, or specific lira or euro amounts, we cite them. Where a pattern has been reported but not formally investigated, we say so.

For new scams and live updates to the cases in this book, visit **[tabiji.ai/scams](https://tabiji.ai/scams)**. We maintain the database quarterly, and most corrections reach the site within a week of a credible report.

We hope you come home with stories about the bazaars, the Bosphorus sunset, the Cappadocia balloons, and the mezze-and-rakı nights.

— Tabiji
