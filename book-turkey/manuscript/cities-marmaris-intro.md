Marmaris — forested Mediterranean-Aegean crossroads, the Rhodes ferry port, and one of Turkey's busiest yacht-charter hubs — runs a scam scene that combines Bodrum's beach-rental patterns with a rowdier nightlife strip. Volume is at the **main Marmaris beach strip** (Uzunyalı), the **Içmeler** holiday village 7 km west, **Barlar Sokak** (Bar Street) behind the main quay, and at **Dalaman Airport (DLM)** 90 minutes northeast.

The **jet-ski and watersport damage-deposit cycle** on Uzunyalı and the Içmeler beaches runs the Bodrum script at a lower volume but with identical choreography: €300 cash deposit, disputed scratch, €150 deducted. The **Içmeler-to-Marmaris minibus (*dolmuş*) tourist-fare inflation** is more local: the regulated dolmuş fare from Içmeler to the Marmaris harbor is 35 lira; tourist-facing minibus drivers waiting outside Içmeler hotels sometimes quote 150 lira "foreigner tariff" for the same 15-minute run.

The risk radiates out in three corridors. The **Marmaris Bar Street (Barlar Sokak) drink-spike and inflated-bill pattern** targets young arrivals with flashing-neon bars offering "buy one get three" promotions that deliver a €300+ final bill and occasional reports of spiked drinks resulting in wallet theft — the r/Turkey community flags specific venues by name each season. The **"Turkish Night" dinner-show tourist packages** sold on the harbor promenade at €60 per person are sometimes delivered at a different, cheaper venue than the one advertised. And the **Dalaman Airport (DLM) flat-rate taxi** touts quote €80–€100 for the run to Marmaris; the regulated Havaş shuttle bus is €12.

A safe default: take the **Havaş shuttle** from DLM to Marmaris Otogar (€12, 90 minutes); from Içmeler, take the official dolmuş at the posted 35-lira tariff; rent jet skis only from operators accepting credit-card holds; avoid Barlar Sokak "buy-one-get-three" venues and drink only from sealed bottles served in front of you; buy Turkish Night dinner-shows only through your hotel concierge with a named venue on the receipt; and call **155** or the Marmaris Turizm Polisi at **+90 252 412 1020**. The Marmaris Tourist Police desk is at **İskele Meydanı 2**, at the main ferry quay.
