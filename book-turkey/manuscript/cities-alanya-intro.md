Alanya — castle-crowned peninsula, Cleopatra Beach, and the Turkish Riviera's eastern anchor — has a scam scene shaped by Russian, German, Dutch, and British package-holiday volume. Volume is concentrated at **Cleopatra Beach**, the **Alanya harbor "pirate boat" cluster**, **Damlataş Cave**, and **Gazipaşa Airport (GZP)** 40 km east.

The **Cleopatra Beach jet-ski damage-deposit cycle** runs the Bodrum-Marmaris-Fethiye script on the Alanya beach — €300 cash deposit, disputed scratch, €150 deducted. The **Alanya harbor "pirate boat" day-tour** is the local variant of the Fethiye 12-Islands hidden-fee boat. A €25 per-person harbor tour is sold on the dock chalkboards; on board, "free lunch," "free snorkel gear," and "drinks" each become €8–€15. Book through Viator / GetYourGuide for a transparent total; the marina's official booking office posts a published fare sheet that's worth the five-minute walk to find.

The risk radiates out in three corridors. The **Damlataş Cave "photography guide" shakedown** at the cave entrance — a freelance photographer follows groups through the 150-meter cave and demands 200+ lira for photos tourists didn't commission. The **Gazipaşa Airport (GZP) flat-rate taxi** touts quote 1,800–2,500 lira for the 40-km run to Alanya center; the regulated Havaş shuttle bus is 150 lira. And the **old-town Red Tower (Kızıl Kule) "Turkish Night" dinner-show packages** sold on the harbor at €55 per person sometimes deliver at a different venue with a surprise €30 bar-minimum.

A safe default: take the **Havaş shuttle** from GZP to Alanya center (150 lira, 50 minutes); use **BiTaksi** for city rides; rent jet skis only against a credit-card hold; buy Alanya harbor boat tours only at the marina's official booking office or through Viator/GetYourGuide; ignore Damlataş Cave photo-guide approaches; confirm Turkish Night dinner-show venues in writing before paying; and call **155** or the Alanya Tourist Police at **+90 242 513 1020**. The Alanya Turizm Polisi desk is at **Damlataş Caddesi 1**, near the castle base.
