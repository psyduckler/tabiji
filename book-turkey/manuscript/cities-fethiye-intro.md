Fethiye — the Ölüdeniz blue lagoon, the Babadağ paragliding ridge, the Lycian Way, and the 12-Islands day-cruise anchor — runs a narrower but well-rehearsed scam scene focused on two signature attractions: paragliding and gulet cruises. Volume is at the **Ölüdeniz beach** landing zone, at the **Paspatur** old-town restaurant cluster, and at the **Fethiye inner harbor** where 12-Islands day-cruise boats depart.

The **paragliding landing-fee add-on** is the most common complaint. A tandem-paragliding flight from Babadağ to Ölüdeniz beach is widely advertised at €65–€80 "all-inclusive" with video. On landing, the pilot or the ground crew produces a €150 "helmet cam rental" or a €100 "GoPro memory-card release" charge that wasn't disclosed in the initial pitch. The defense: book only through operators who publish a complete written price list with all on-ground add-ons named (Sky Sports, Gravity, Reaction Tandem Paragliding, Easy Riders are the commonly cited reputable operators), and confirm in writing that the video/photo package is included or priced separately.

The risk radiates out in three corridors. The **12-Islands day-cruise hidden-fee boat** is the signature harbor scam. A €40 per-person day cruise (typically a 12-island loop visiting Gemiler, Tersane, Yassıca, Kleopatra Hamamı, and the Butterfly Valley) is advertised with "free lunch" and "free snorkel gear"; on board, each of those becomes €15 separately billed, plus a €10 "harbor tax" the tout didn't mention. Book through the Fethiye Marina office or Viator / GetYourGuide to see the real complete fee structure in advance. **Ölüdeniz beach jet-ski damage deposits** run the Bodrum script at lower volume. And the **Paspatur old-town restaurant unposted cover-charge scene** — the old-town restaurant row adds a per-person "service charge" or "bread and meze" charge that isn't on the door menu.

A safe default: fly into **Dalaman (DLM)** and take the Havaş shuttle to Fethiye Otogar (90 lira, 45 minutes); book paragliding and 12-Islands cruises only through operators with a published full price list (Viator / GetYourGuide), not beach-chalkboard touts; rent jet skis only against a credit-card hold; eat in Paspatur only at restaurants that post full menus including bread/meze/service fees on the door; and call **155** or the Fethiye Tourist Police at **+90 252 614 1569**. The Fethiye Turizm Polisi desk is at **İskele Meydanı 1**, at the harbor.
