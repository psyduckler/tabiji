Pamukkale — "Cotton Castle" in Turkish, the surreal white-travertine terraces of mineral-water pools cascading down a hillside above Denizli — shares its scenic area with the ancient city of **Hierapolis** (a UNESCO World Heritage site). The combined site charges a single entry priced in euros by the Ministry (approximately €25–€30, payable in TL at the day's rate — roughly 900–1,100 TL as this edition goes to press) that includes unlimited travertine walking plus Hierapolis access. Volume is at the **main Pamukkale village entrance**, at **Denizli bus station**, and at the **Denizli Cardak Airport (DNZ)** 65 km east.

The **Denizli bus-station freelance "Pamukkale guide" scam** is the volume risk. Minibus drivers and freelance "guides" at Denizli Otogar approach arrivals with "Pamukkale + Hierapolis guided tour" offers at 400–700 lira per person. The promised tour substitutes a brief 30-minute travertine walk for the advertised full Hierapolis exploration, includes a single commission stop (at a "hand-loomed Pamukkale cotton" shop, which is the local kickback venue), and drops tourists at the Denizli bus station by 2 PM. The real Pamukkale–Hierapolis visit is self-guided, well-signposted in English, and takes 3–4 hours; the combined-entry ticket (500 lira) includes everything except the separately-priced Cleopatra Pool.

The risk radiates out in three corridors. The **Cleopatra Pool separate-entry scam** — this is real: the Cleopatra Antique Pool costs an extra charge (roughly €10, or 350–400 TL) beyond the combined entry and is sometimes sold by touts as a bundled "premium package" at two to three times that price. Buy it at the Cleopatra Pool counter directly or skip (the pool is fine but not essential). The **Denizli Cardak Airport (DNZ) flat-rate taxi** touts quote 1,500–2,200 lira for the 65-km run to Pamukkale; the regulated Havaş shuttle bus is 250 lira. And the **post-visit "hand-loomed Pamukkale cotton" gift-shop kickback circuit** — the minibus tours stop at a single shop where towels and throws run 2–3× retail.

A safe default: fly into **Denizli Cardak (DNZ)** and take the **Havaş shuttle** (250 lira, 90 minutes to Pamukkale); from Denizli Otogar, take the regulated *dolmuş* to Pamukkale village (35 lira, 40 minutes), not a freelance-minibus tour; buy the combined Pamukkale + Hierapolis ticket at the official booth (the Ministry prices in euros at roughly €25–€30, payable in TL at the day's rate); skip the Cleopatra Pool add-on unless you specifically want to swim; buy cotton textiles at the posted-price shops along Pamukkale village main street (not at the minibus-tour kickback stop); and call **155** or the Pamukkale Jandarma at **+90 258 272 2077**. For tourist-specific urgent help, the Denizli Tourist Information hotline is **+90 258 264 3971**.
