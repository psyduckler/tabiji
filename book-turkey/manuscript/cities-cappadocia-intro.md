Cappadocia — fairy chimneys, cave hotels, and Turkey's signature sunrise-balloon industry — is centered on the town of Göreme with Uçhisar and Ürgüp as the two secondary bases. Its scam scene is narrower than Istanbul's but concentrated at a single high-dollar-value operation: the **balloon-tour booking**. A typical 1-hour Cappadocia balloon flight runs €180–€250 through a licensed operator; the market supports roughly 200 balloons on peak-season mornings, and tickets sell out days ahead.

The **WhatsApp balloon-fraud circuit** is the headline. A Göreme hotel concierge shares a WhatsApp contact that is not affiliated with any real operator; the contact quotes €200, demands 100% payment by bank transfer to a Turkish IBAN, and books you for a launch time that either never happens ("canceled for wind") or pushes you onto a budget balloon with 28 other passengers rather than the promised 16-person basket. Refunds are impossible once the money has left your account; the Turkish Civil Aviation Authority (SHGM) licenses every legitimate operator, and the Ministry of Culture and Tourism publishes the current operator list at **goturkiye.com**. The three most commonly named scam-free operators: **Kapadokya Balloons** (kapadokyaballoons.com), **Butterfly Balloons** (butterflyballoons.com), and **Royal Balloon** (royalballoon.com).

The risk radiates out in three corridors. The **Göreme cave-hotel upgrade fee** scam adds 500–1,200 lira at check-in for an "upgraded cave-view room" already booked through the reservation platform; book only through Booking.com, Airbnb, or Agoda where the single-price contract is enforced. The **Uçhisar and Ürgüp "Iznik tile master" workshop visits** sell machine-printed tiles as hand-painted at 800–2,400 lira each; real hand-painted Iznik from licensed workshops carries an artist's signature stamp and a Kütahya/İznik provenance certificate. And the **Kaymaklı and Derinkuyu Underground City freelance "guide" approaches** demand 300–800 lira after a 20-minute accompaniment — the Underground Cities are self-guided and the 200-lira site entry includes access without a guide.

A safe default: fly into **Kayseri (ASR)** or **Nevşehir (NAV)** and take the Turkish Airlines / Pegasus official shuttle bus (60–100 lira to Göreme; the metered taxi from ASR runs 800–1,200 lira and from NAV 350–500 lira as this edition goes to press); book balloon flights only at **kapadokyaballoons.com**, **butterflyballoons.com**, **royalballoon.com**, or through Viator / GetYourGuide / Klook with published reviews; book accommodation only through major OTAs; decline every WhatsApp-shared contact that requires bank-transfer prepayment; and call **155** or the Cappadocia-region Tourist Information hotline at **+90 384 271 2558** from the Göreme Open Air Museum information desk during office hours. The Göreme Jandarma (rural police) post is on the main D302 road near the bus station.
