İzmir — Turkey's third-largest city, Aegean capital, and the staging point for Ephesus day trips — runs a quieter scam scene than Istanbul. Volume is concentrated in three areas: the **Kordon** waterfront promenade in Alsancak, **Konak Square** and its clock-tower approach, and **İzmir Adnan Menderes Airport (ADB)**.

The **Kordon waterfront tourist-menu overcharge** is the most common complaint. Restaurants along the Atatürk Caddesi / Birinci Kordon strip from Alsancak to Konak sometimes carry two menus — the Turkish-language menu with standard prices, and an English-language or photo menu that's 40–60% higher for the same dishes. The defense is the universal refusal: ask for the *Türkçe menü* and check Google Maps reviews before you sit down. Alsancak's backstreet restaurants off 1469 Sokak and 1453 Sokak carry honest prices and generally post them on the door.

The risk radiates out in three corridors. **Konak Square "free city tour" approaches** target cruise arrivals disembarking from the İzmir port (the Alsancak Cruise Terminal handles most Aegean cruise calls); the "tour" is a 30-minute walk ending at a single rug or leather shop with kickbacks. **ADB airport flat-rate taxi touts** quote 1,200–1,500 lira to central İzmir; the regulated metered fare is 500–700 lira and the İZBAN suburban rail from ADB airport to Alsancak is 45 lira. And the **Alsancak "Turkish coffee house" cover-charge scam** on tourist-facing side streets adds 180–250 lira "cultural service" to the bill when the waiter identifies a foreign accent.

A safe default: take the **İZBAN** from ADB airport (45 lira, 30 minutes to Alsancak); use **BiTaksi** for city rides; eat on Alsancak backstreets off 1469 and 1453 Sokak with posted Turkish-language menus; ignore cruise-day freelance "city tour" offers; and call **155** (Polis İmdat) or the İzmir Tourist Information hotline at **+90 232 489 9278** from inside a chain café. The İzmir Turizm Polisi desk is at **Atatürk Bulvarı 418, Konak**, near the Ministry of Culture and Tourism provincial office.
