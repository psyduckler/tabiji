Konya — central-Anatolian Seljuk capital, home of the Mevlana (Rumi) mausoleum, and the Whirling Dervishes' historic seat — runs the quietest scam scene of any city in this book. Volume is modest by Turkish standards. Volume is around the **Mevlana Museum (Mevlana Müzesi)** complex, along **Mevlana Caddesi** — the pilgrimage axis from Alaaddin Hill to the mausoleum — and at **Konya Airport (KYA)**.

The **Mevlana-area carpet-shop approach** is the signature Konya scam. Shops along Mevlana Caddesi and the adjacent Türbe Caddesi offer "Mevlevi-order prayer rugs" and "Dervish ceremony carpets" as themed variants of standard kilims, priced at 3× normal retail. The items are functionally identical to non-themed Turkish kilims; the Mevlevi branding is a selling point, not a material difference. A real hand-woven Mevlana-region kilim from a licensed workshop carries a Ministry-of-Culture provenance certificate and runs 1,500–3,500 lira for a typical 2×3 meter piece; tourist-marked "Mevlevi prayer rug" tags at 6,000–10,000 lira are the same item with branding.

The risk radiates out in three corridors. The **"Whirling Dervish ceremony" ticket scam** — the real *Sema* ceremony is held every Saturday evening at the **Mevlana Cultural Center (Mevlana Kültür Merkezi)**, 1 km from the mausoleum, with free entry to the public hall and a modest 100–150 lira reserved-seat ticket on the Culture Center's own website. Freelance touts around the mausoleum sell "Sema tickets" at 350–500 lira for a venue that isn't the Cultural Center. The **Konya Airport (KYA) flat-rate taxi** touts quote 800–1,200 lira for the 20-km run to central Konya; the regulated metered fare is 400–600 lira. And the **central-Konya tourist-menu restaurants near Mevlana Caddesi** quote prices in euros when the lira menu is significantly cheaper.

A safe default: take the **Konya Tramvay (line T2)** from Selçuk University / ALAEDDIN to Mevlana (12 lira); take the regulated taxi or BiTaksi from KYA to central Konya; buy kilims only from shops with a tax-ID and Ministry-of-Culture provenance certificate, at posted prices; buy Sema tickets only at the Mevlana Cultural Center's own website or box office; eat at restaurants with posted lira menus (not euro menus); and call **155** or the Konya Tourist Information hotline at **+90 332 353 4020**. There is no dedicated Turizm Polisi desk in Konya; the provincial Jandarma handles tourism complaints via the 155 dispatcher.
