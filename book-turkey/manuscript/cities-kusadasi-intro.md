Kuşadası — Aegean cruise port, Ephesus gateway, and a summer-heavy resort town — runs a scam scene shaped almost entirely by cruise-ship day-trippers. Volume is at the **Ege Ports Kuşadası cruise terminal** where Royal Caribbean, MSC, Celebrity, and Norwegian ships dock daily in season, at the **Barlar Sokak** (Bar Street) nightlife strip, and on the approach to **Pigeon Island (Güvercinada)** off the main promenade.

The **cruise-arrival taxi-tout scam** is the volume risk. Drivers at the cruise terminal exit quote flat euros for runs to Ephesus at €80–€120 round-trip when the regulated Turkish taxi fare from Kuşadası to Ephesus is roughly 600–800 lira (about €20–€25 at 2026 rates), and the Selçuk-bound *dolmuş* from Kuşadası bus station is 80 lira each way. The taxi driver almost always promises to "wait at Ephesus for you," which is legitimate for a tripled fare but — importantly — the ship's own shore-excursion desk and the Ephesus-combined-ticket offer from Viator / GetYourGuide are both priced transparently and include a licensed guide.

The risk radiates out in three corridors. The **Barlar Sokak "free entry / bottle minimum" scam** targets young cruise arrivals at night with "no cover charge" signs that conceal a €200+ per-table bottle-service minimum once you're seated. The **Pigeon Island "free tour" approach** walks you across the causeway toward a single leather shop where a carpet-shop script runs in a different product. And the **"Turkish carpet workshop visit" bundled into shore-excursion packages** — the cruise-line-sold excursions include a carpet-workshop stop that runs 2–3× direct retail; the ship's excursion includes a commission component, and buying from the excursion is materially more expensive than buying from a reputable Selçuk or İzmir shop.

A safe default: take a **dolmuş** from Kuşadası bus station to Selçuk (80 lira) and a second dolmuş to Ephesus (35 lira) rather than a cruise-terminal taxi; avoid Barlar Sokak bottle-service bars in favor of the posted-menu bars along Barbaros Hayrettin Paşa Bulvarı; ignore cruise-terminal "free tour" approaches; and call **155** or the Kuşadası Tourist Police at **+90 256 614 0910**. The Kuşadası Turizm Polisi desk is at **Barbaros Hayrettin Paşa Bulvarı 4**, a five-minute walk from the cruise terminal exit.
