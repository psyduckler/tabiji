# The Scams: City by City {-}

The chapters that follow document every scam reported in nine Japanese cities. Cities are ordered by visitor volume, starting with Tokyo and ending with Yokohama. Each chapter opens with a city-specific briefing — risk level, neighborhoods to watch, the nearest koban — followed by every documented scam in that city.

If you're traveling to only one or two cities, skip directly to those chapters. If you're visiting more, skim them all — many scams (touts, shirotaku taxis, fake monks) appear in multiple cities with local variations.

## The Nine Cities at a Glance {-}

![Tokyo — Shibuya crossing and Tokyo Tower](assets/cities/tokyo.jpg)

**Tokyo** — Shibuya, Shinjuku, and the neon heart of the country. Nightlife touts in Kabukicho; unlicensed taxis at the airports; fake monks on the approach to Senso-ji.

![Kyoto — Fushimi Inari torii tunnel at dusk](assets/cities/kyoto.jpg)

**Kyoto** — Temples, torii gates, and geisha districts. Photo-fee ambushes in Gion; aggressive photographer-guides around Kinkaku-ji; overpriced "tea ceremonies" off Kiyomizu-dera.

![Osaka — Dotonbori canal and Glico running-man sign](assets/cities/osaka.jpg)

**Osaka** — Dotonbori, Namba, and the country's loudest food street. Bottakuri bars behind the signboards; izakaya cover-charge surprises; rigged claw-machines in arcades.

![Sapporo — Sapporo TV Tower and Odori Park ginkgo](assets/cities/sapporo.jpg)

**Sapporo** — Susukino after dark, ski-season airport touts, Odori Park by day. Massage-parlor upsells; taxi meters that "forget" to start; snow-festival ticket resellers.

![Fukuoka — Hakata yatai row at dusk](assets/cities/fukuoka.jpg)

**Fukuoka** — Nakasu yatai stalls, Tenjin nightlife, the Kyushu gateway. Tonkotsu-cart price swaps; Nakasu hostess-bar charges; cruise-terminal "tour guides."

![Hiroshima — Itsukushima floating torii at high tide](assets/cities/hiroshima.jpg)

**Hiroshima** — Peace Park, Hondori, Miyajima day-trips. Ferry-ticket upsells; unlicensed guides at the Atomic Bomb Dome; temple-donation pressure on the island.

![Nara — Todai-ji Great Buddha Hall and deer](assets/cities/nara.jpg)

**Nara** — Todai-ji, the deer park, and the ancient capital. Deer-cracker overcharges; aggressive fortune-telling near Kasuga-taisha; taxi-tour pitches at Kintetsu Nara Station.

![Okinawa — Shuri Castle gate and turquoise sea](assets/cities/okinawa.jpg)

**Okinawa** — Naha, Kokusai-dori, and the Ryukyu beaches. Dive-shop add-ons; Chatan nightlife traps; rental-car "damage" claims at the airport.

![Yokohama — Minato Mirai and Cosmo Clock at dusk](assets/cities/yokohama.jpg)

**Yokohama** — Chinatown, Minato Mirai, the Bay. Chinatown fortune-tellers; bar-tour pitches near the stadium; counterfeit-goods stalls along the waterfront.
