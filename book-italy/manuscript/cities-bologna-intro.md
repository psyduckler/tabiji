Bologna's scam profile is sharper than its reputation. The city is smaller and less tourist-heavy than Rome, Venice, or Florence, which means the scammers work a tight corridor rather than a whole historic centre: Via dell'Indipendenza between Bologna Centrale and Piazza Maggiore is nine hundred metres of porticoed walk where a pair of borseggiatrici can work the flow all day. The signature script is the cigarette request — a 2016 Il Resto del Carlino arrest report documents two Roma women, ages 18 and 26, using this exact move on a Japanese tourist, with an off-duty officer making the arrest on the spot. A September 2025 r/bologna firsthand post from a local confirms the same script is still running.

The risk zone is Via dell'Indipendenza, the area under the Due Torri (Piazza di Porta Ravegnana), Bologna Centrale platforms and the taxi-queue forecourt, and the outdoor tables along Via Volturno / Via dei Mille where bag-snatch teams work the lunch crowd. A safe default: walk Via dell'Indipendenza on the portico-wall side with your bag inside the pillar line, photograph the menu and coperto at every restaurant before sitting, and use the Marconi Express monorail from Bologna Airport (€12.20) rather than negotiating the €12.80 official taxi fare.
