Verona's scams are tied to its two signature attractions — Casa di Giulietta and the Arena opera festival — plus the train-station pickpocket pattern that feeds Milan–Venezia day-trippers through Porta Nuova. Casa di Giulietta became ticketed on April 1, 2026 at €5 per courtyard entry, walking back a €12 December 2025 trial that caused a Comune emergency meeting; scammers are now exploiting the fee transition with fake "balcony viewing" upsells and resale-site markups. The Arena Opera counterfeit-ticket ring is well documented: a January 2020 Carabinieri operation in Vedelago arrested a 27-year-old Napoletana for online counterfeit Arena ticket sales; TripAdvisor and r/Verona collect reports of £350 Arena tickets that were never delivered.

The signature Verona street scam is the *finte sordomute* (fake-deaf-mute) petition ring on Piazza Bra and Via Mazzini — Polizia di Stato Verona has an on-record denuncia against four Romanian women working the piazza, and L'Arena has covered multiple waves going back a decade. This is a distraction-pickpocket scam and not a donation scam; signing the petition buys you nothing and usually costs you your wallet. A safe default: buy Arena tickets ONLY at **[arena.it](https://arena.it)** or the official Fondazione Arena box office on Piazza Bra; buy Casa di Giulietta entry only at the official courtyard kiosk; and treat every clipboard on the street as a theft cue.
