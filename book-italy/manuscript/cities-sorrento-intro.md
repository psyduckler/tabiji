Sorrento is the gateway hub for the Amalfi Coast and Capri, which means almost every scam in this chapter is a transit scam. The Capodichino airport transfer is the biggest exposure: the legitimate fixed fare to Sorrento is around €110 and should be agreed before the door closes; abusivi quote €180 and sometimes €220 for the same route, and a single 2024 Guardia di Finanza sweep fined six SUV abusivi €73,000 between Capodichino and the Sorrento port. The Circumvesuviana rail line — which runs Napoli–Pompei–Sorrento — is notorious enough that Il Mattino has called it *"un'aggressione al giorno"* (an attack a day), with ticketing fraud and platform pickpocketing both well-documented. The Sorrento-to-Amalfi private transfer market is reddit-infamous at €120–€180 for a route the SITA bus does for €2.10.

The risk zone is Sorrento's Circumvesuviana station, the Marina Piccola hydrofoil pier, and Piazza Tasso / Via San Cesareo for restaurant tourist-menu traps. A safe default: take the Alibus from Capodichino (€5) to Piazza Garibaldi, then the Circumvesuviana (€3.60) to Sorrento; buy hydrofoil tickets for Capri only at the Caremar, Snav, NLG, or Alilauro counters on Marina Piccola (not from street resellers); and take SITA Sud for any onward trip to Positano or Amalfi.
