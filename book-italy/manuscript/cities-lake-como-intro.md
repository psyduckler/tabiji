Lake Como's scam profile is gentler than Garda's but denser per visitor — the tourist footprint compresses into a handful of villages (Como, Bellagio, Varenna, Menaggio, Tremezzo, Lenno, Laglio) all served by the Navigazione Lago di Como (NLC) public ferries. The most common mistakes are ticketing: fake "hop-on hop-off" passes sold at piers that do not cover all stops, private water taxis quoting €100+ for twenty-minute crossings NLC runs for €11, and villa-ticket bundles layered on top of direct admission to Villa del Balbianello, Villa Carlotta, and Villa Melzi.

The oddest Lake Como scam — and the one that most consistently catches Americans — is the "George Clooney villa tour" sold out of Como and Bellagio. Clooney's villa in Laglio has no public tour, ever; every operator selling one is a scam. Parking in Bellagio is also a consistent trap: the town's few paid lots (near the bridge and the ferry dock) charge €5–€8/hr officially, but parcheggiatori abusivi direct tourists to unofficial spots at €20–€40/hr. A safe default: buy every ferry ticket at the NLC machine with the blue logo on the pier (not from a person), ignore every "Clooney tour" offer, and arrive in Bellagio or Varenna by boat or bus rather than by car.
