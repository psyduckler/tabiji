Siena is a perfect hill town with two sharp tourist traps that most guidebooks soften: the ZTL fines and the specchietto scam. The ZTL (*Zona a Traffico Limitato*) cameras ring the historic centre and fine unauthorised rental cars €80–€335 per entry, with rental companies passing fines through at an administrative surcharge that routinely puts the total past €200. Radio Siena TV reported a single afternoon in which vigili booted ten unauthorised vehicles — enforcement is aggressive and automatic. The specchietto crash scam has a dedicated arrest log on the Firenze–Siena raccordo, with a recurring white VW Golf named across multiple cronaca pieces.

The Palio, held on 2 July and 16 August, doubles both the beauty and the scam surface: fake bleacher tickets at €300–€500 appear on aggregator sites the week before, and the only real tickets come directly from the Contrade or licensed Siena hotels on the piazza. The tourist-menu trap on Piazza del Campo is pronounced during the Palio and subdued in off-season. A safe default: park at the free Parcheggio Il Campo outside the walls and walk in, book Palio tickets only through the Contrade or your hotel, and if another driver flags you over on any Tuscan SS road, do not stop.
