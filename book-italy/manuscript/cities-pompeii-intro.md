Pompeii is a highly concentrated scam environment because every visitor passes through a single funnel: the Pompei Scavi Circumvesuviana station to the Porta Marina gate. In that three-hundred-metre walk you pass a parade of storefronts labelled "Info Point" and "Pompeii Service" that are not the official entrance; multiple TripAdvisor reports titled *"TRUFFA! Info Point NON Ufficiale!"* document €150-per-person "guide" pitches for the ruins you can enter for €22 at the real gate. An r/ancientrome post titled "Pompeii Scam" warns to ignore anyone before the turnstile regardless of how official their lanyard looks.

The unlicensed-guide problem is so entrenched that it has become a formal Carabinieri enforcement programme: Il Mattino's *"lotta alle guide turistiche abusive"* coverage, internapoli.it's September 2025 denuncia of a 44-year-old from Castellammare, and Made in Pompei's August 2024 "tre guide turistiche abusive sanzionate" (roughly €5,000 in fines) show *esercizio abusivo della professione* arrests are a recurring event at the Scavi. In-park pickpocketing is also active; Napoli Today reported the Parco Archeologico's vigilanza arresting a serial wallet-and-phone thief working the Forum and Villa dei Misteri loop. A safe default: buy your ticket ONLY at **[pompeiisites.org](https://pompeiisites.org)** or at the Porta Marina entrance itself; ignore every "Info Point" storefront between the station and the gate; and hire guides only through the park's official authorised-guides list.
