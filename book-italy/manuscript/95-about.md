# About Tabiji {-}

Tabiji is a travel-safety research organization. We document scams, health risks, and local advisories for travelers across more than fifty cities worldwide, and we publish everything as a free public resource at **[tabiji.ai](https://tabiji.ai)**.

We started this series after noticing that most scam roundups online skip the one thing travelers actually need in the moment: the exact phrase, the exact move, that ends the interaction. Remembering a list of one hundred and forty-nine scams is hard when you are tired and under pressure. Remembering six patterns is much easier. That is the shape we have tried to give this book.

Every scam documented here was drawn from Italian-press coverage — La Repubblica, Il Corriere della Sera, Il Mattino, La Nazione, L'Arena, il Gazzettino, La Nuova Sardegna, Gazzetta del Sud, Il Resto del Carlino, Romatoday, Napoli Today, and the city dailies — cross-referenced against Carabinieri and Polizia di Stato arrest records, Guardia di Finanza bust coverage, consumer-body warnings from Cittadinanzattiva, and firsthand traveler accounts on Reddit's r/ItalyTravel, r/italy, and the city-specific subreddits. Where we have named perpetrators, arrest dates, or documented euro amounts, we cite them. Where a pattern has been reported but not formally investigated, we say so.

For new scams and live updates to the cases in this book, scan the QR code on the back cover or visit **[tabiji.ai/scams](https://tabiji.ai/scams)**. We maintain the database quarterly, and most corrections reach the site within a week of a credible report.

We hope you come home with stories about the food.

— Tabiji
