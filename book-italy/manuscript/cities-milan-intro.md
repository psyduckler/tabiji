Milan's scam profile is unusual because the city is wealthy, modern, and orderly — and yet the tre-campanelle shell game logged 125 denunce in four months on a single stretch between Via Torino and Via Dante, with crews caught holding up to €4,000 in victims' cash. Il Giorno's "100 arrests in 100 days" report on the Duomo-Corso zone for 2025 works out to one suspect cuffed every twenty-four hours. The tell of Milan is that the same tourist radius that produces the scam also produces the enforcement: plainclothes Polizia Locale and Carabinieri operate the same piazzas as the bracelet pairs.

The risk zone is almost entirely contained in Piazza del Duomo and Via Dante, the M1/M3 metro lines during commuter hours, and the Malpensa-airport transfer route. A fake-plainclothes-police wallet check is the third recurring scam in the Quadrilatero della Moda and around Stazione Centrale. A safe default: take the Malpensa Express (€13 single) rather than a taxi from the airport, photograph your outbound Italo or Trenitalia ticket QR code before boarding to beat ticket-machine "helpers," and walk two blocks off the Duomo axis for any meal. The Navigli nightlife district has its own tourist-trap mechanics on the aperitivo scene.
