Florence is the densest scam experience in Tuscany: compact enough that the Duomo, Uffizi, Ponte Vecchio, and San Lorenzo market are all a ten-minute walk apart, which means the scam operators on one loop can hit all four in a single afternoon. La Nazione and Firenze Today both run weekly arrest reports; the Duomo bracelet pair, the Uffizi painting ambush, the Ponte Vecchio tre campanelle, and the Santa Maria Novella station pickpocket ring are all prosecuted often enough to be documented recurring operations.

The single most underrated Florence scam is the San Lorenzo fake leather trade — a Guardia di Finanza "filiera del falso" investigation indicted 49 people, and one Campi Bisenzio pelletteria alone was estimated to gross roughly a million euros counterfeiting Armani, Balenciaga, Celine, Fendi, Givenchy, Gucci, Louis Vuitton, and Prada. The market survives on tourists who believe "Italian leather" on a San Lorenzo stall means what it says. The risk zone covers SMN station, the Duomo-to-Uffizi axis, Ponte Vecchio, and the San Lorenzo market itself. A safe default: buy leather only from a shop with a posted P.IVA number and a physical storefront, pay by credit card (never cash), and do not sign anything a bracelet-man hands you.
