# The Scams: City by City {-}

The chapters that follow document every scam reported in twenty Italian cities. Cities are ordered by visitor volume and reading utility — starting with the Grand Tour core of Rome, Venice, Florence, and Milan, then the major southern cities, the Tuscan day trips, the Amalfi region, the Liguria coast, the Veneto and the lakes, and finally the islands and the south.

If you are traveling to only a few of these cities, skip directly to the relevant chapters. If you are visiting more, skim them all — many scams (the friendship-bracelet duo, the tre-campanelle shell game, the finti poliziotti wallet check, the airport NCC abusivo, and the coperto-plus-pesce-al-etto restaurant trap) appear in multiple cities with local variations. Knowing the pattern in Rome makes you faster to spot it in Milan or Bologna.

## The Twenty Cities at a Glance {-}

![Rome — Colosseum and Roman Forum at golden hour](assets/cities/rome.jpg)

**Rome** — The Colosseum, the Vatican, and the country's largest tourist-scam ecosystem. Tre-campanelle shell games across the Centro Storico; gladiator photo ambushes outside the Colosseum; fake "skip the line" ticket sellers; POS-pirata contactless theft on the metro; fake five-star hotel wire-fraud rings.

![Venice — Rialto Bridge and grand canal gondola](assets/cities/venice.jpg)

**Venice** — Canals, carnival masks, and a pickpocket ring so large a Corriere del Veneto investigation named its 20-year-old leader "Shakira" and pegged the daily take at €2,500 per member. Fake vaporetto-ticket sellers at Ferrovia; gondola overcharging above the €90 official rate; Marco Polo water-taxi abusivi outnumbering licensed operators two-to-one.

![Florence — Duomo dome and Tuscan rooftops](assets/cities/florence.jpg)

**Florence** — The Duomo, the Uffizi, the world's most famous leather market — and a Guardia di Finanza *"filiera del falso"* investigation that indicted 49 people for counterfeiting in the San Lorenzo leather trade. Duomo bracelet pairs; the Ponte Vecchio tre campanelle; the painting-ambush scam outside the Uffizi; SMN taxi strike overcharges.

![Milan — Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II glass ceiling](assets/cities/milan.jpg)

**Milan** — Fashion, finance, and 125 tre-campanelle denunce in four months around Via Dante. Bracelet-and-selfie teams in Piazza del Duomo; metro pickpocket rings on M1 and M3; €95 Malpensa taxis quoted at €250; a plainclothes "police" wallet-check scam that runs from the Quadrilatero della Moda to the Navigli.

![Naples — Spaccanapoli alley and Vesuvius in the distance](assets/cities/naples.jpg)

**Naples** — The most-slandered great Italian city, and the one with the sharpest-edged scams. Moped *scippatori*, including a Naples-linked Rolex ring that works Marbella and Puerto Banús; the Piazza Garibaldi tre campanelle that has run for fifty years and still catches tourists; FreeNow app price-edits on airport transfers.

![Bologna — Due Torri and porticoed street](assets/cities/bologna.jpg)

**Bologna** — Porticoes, tortellini, and a Via dell'Indipendenza pickpocket corridor that has been running the same cigarette-request script since 2016. Due Torri begging-arm-grabs; outdoor-restaurant bag-snatches with immediate ATM cashouts; Fiera-district fake rentals for business travelers who book at 11pm.

![Palermo — Quattro Canti baroque crossroads](assets/cities/palermo.jpg)

**Palermo** — Baroque streets and street markets with a harder scam edge than anywhere else in this book. Moped scippo along Via Maqueda; the signature Sicilian *truffa dello specchietto* mirror-crash scam on Viale Regione Siciliana; Mondello beach-lido monopoly pricing; Punta Raisi airport taxi zones fixed at €39 / €44 / €50.

![Pisa — Leaning Tower from the Campo dei Miracoli](assets/cities/pisa.jpg)

**Pisa** — A day-trip destination with the densest scam-per-hour ratio in this book. Bracelet and rose sellers on the Via Santa Maria approach to the Tower; the LAM Rossa bus fare-checker shakedown; a dedicated Polizia Turistica on Piazza del Duomo from 2026 that exists specifically because of the tourist-theft density.

![Siena — Piazza del Campo and Torre del Mangia](assets/cities/siena.jpg)

**Siena** — A perfect hill town with a medieval ZTL that fines rental cars €80 to €335 per entry, a July-and-August Palio that draws every scalper in Tuscany, and the classic *specchietto* crash scam on the Firenze–Siena raccordo.

![Sorrento — Marina Grande and cliff harbor](assets/cities/sorrento.jpg)

**Sorrento** — The Amalfi and Capri gateway hub. Capodichino taxi overcharges; Circumvesuviana pickpockets; €200 Sorrento-to-Amalfi transfer gouges on a route the SITA bus does for €2.10; limoncello-factory bait-and-switch tours.

![Positano — classic pastel hillside](assets/cities/positano.jpg)

**Positano** — The most-photographed cliff in Italy, with an Italian-MP-level scandal about €14-per-hour parking and a documented clone-site hotel ring that defrauded ten wire-deposit victims. Spiaggia Grande private-lido gatekeeping; pesce al etto on the waterfront; Path of the Gods tour scams.

![Amalfi Coast — SS163 cliff road and lemon groves](assets/cities/amalfi-coast.jpg)

**Amalfi Coast** — Amalfi, Ravello, Praiano, and the SS163. Abusivi NCC drivers migrating from Naples on cruise days; sea-view coperto and pesce al etto; parcheggiatori abusivi at Amalfi town; the Meta €4-per-night tourism tax weaponized as "city fee in cash" on arrival.

![Capri — Faraglioni rocks from the sea](assets/cities/capri.jpg)

**Capri** — The Blue Grotto fee stack (four separate charges totaling €35 to €45 per person); Piazzetta cafés where a cornetto costs €5.50 at an outdoor table; Faraglioni private-beach minimum spends of €200 per person; and the April 2026 Comune ordinance that fines aggressive touts up to €500.

![Pompeii — Forum ruins with Vesuvius behind](assets/cities/pompeii.jpg)

**Pompeii** — The ruins, and the parade of fake "Info Point" storefronts between Pompei Scavi Circumvesuviana and the Porta Marina gate. Unlicensed guides prosecuted by the Carabinieri; fake skip-the-line tickets; in-park pickpocket rings caught by the Parco Archeologico's own vigilanza.

![Cinque Terre — Manarola pastel cliffs](assets/cities/cinque-terre.jpg)

**Cinque Terre** — Five villages, one bewildering ticket system. The Cinque Terre Card at €17.30 in low season versus €32.50 at peak; the Trenitalia digital-ticket check-in fine of €60 that catches travelers who do not press the in-app button; the €30 Monterosso–Riomaggiore ferry that quietly skips Corniglia.

![Verona — Roman Arena from Piazza Bra](assets/cities/verona.jpg)

**Verona** — Juliet's balcony (newly ticketed at €5 from April 1, 2026); the Arena opera festival with its counterfeit-ticket ring; and the signature Italian *finte sordomute* petition-pickpocket team arrested in a four-person Bardolino sweep.

![Lake Garda — Sirmione castle on the peninsula](assets/cities/lake-garda.jpg)

**Lake Garda** — Italy's largest lake, three regions, and a 2025 Corriere Brescia feature titled *"Sirmione salassi"* ("bloodletting") documenting €75 lunches and €8 single-scoop gelato. Also: the same finti sordomuti ring that works Verona, now operating out of Bardolino with multiple L'Arena arrests.

![Lake Como — Bellagio from the water](assets/cities/lake-como.jpg)

**Lake Como** — Bellagio, Varenna, Menaggio, and a constellation of waterfront tourist traps. Fake vaporetto passes at the piers; private water-taxi overcharging for 20-minute crossings; villa-ticket-bundle markups at Balbianello, Carlotta, and Melzi; the Laglio "George Clooney villa tour" rip-off.

![Sardinia — Costa Smeralda turquoise coastline](assets/cities/sardinia.jpg)

**Sardinia** — The island, the beaches, and Italy's most serious customs fine: Law n. 16/2017 makes taking sand or shells a criminal offense, and the Agenzia delle Dogane has seized over 900 kg in Olbia alone between 2020 and 2025. Plus: Costa Smeralda "minimum spend" restaurants, the Porto Istana €1,155 cheese bill, and the La Pelosa €3.50 reservation resold by middlemen at €25.

![Taormina — Teatro Antico with Etna behind](assets/cities/taormina.jpg)

**Taormina** — The cliffside town made famous by *The White Lotus*. Isola Bella's €5 real fee sold to tourists at €20 as "guided tours"; Teatro Antico restoration-scaffolding hidden from skip-the-line packages; unlicensed Etna guides working above the 2,500-meter certified-guide line.
