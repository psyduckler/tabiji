## Final notes

When uploading:

1. Title and Subtitle go in separate fields — never combine.
2. Author byline must match the byline shown on the cover and copyright page exactly: **Bernard Huang, Editor · Tabiji**.
3. Set "Adult content" to **No**.
4. ISBN: KDP will assign a free ISBN for the eBook; for paperback, accept their free ISBN unless you have your own.
5. Publishing rights: select "I own the copyright and I hold the necessary publishing rights."
6. Territories: All territories (worldwide).
7. DRM: Optional. Recommend **No DRM** for traveler-friendliness (readers can move the file to multiple devices).
