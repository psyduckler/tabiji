## The card

The man on the corner of Soi 4 and Sukhumvit was in his thirties,
wearing a button-down. He smiled and held up a laminated card.
Five hundred baht. Two beers. Lady company. Air conditioning.

He gestured down the *soi* (the side street) to a doorway with a
velvet rope and a small neon sign that read PINK PUSSYCAT.

Harry was in Bangkok on the third night of a long-planned solo
trip. He had spent the day at the Grand Palace and Wat Pho. He had
walked Sukhumvit looking for a quiet place to read and have a beer
before bed. The card the hustler was holding said five hundred baht.
Fifteen US dollars. The price felt wrong for the offer, and the
wrongness was exactly why he should have walked on.

He stepped inside.

The lights were low. Two women in cocktail dresses sat at the table
immediately. The bartender poured drinks without being asked. One
of the women ordered a bottle of water; she called it her drink.
There was no menu in sight. Harry asked for one.

The hostess shrugged and giggled. *"No menu, all friendly price,
mama-san takes care."*

An hour later the bill arrived folded on a tray. The total was
18,400 baht, about 540 US dollars. Itemized line items he had not
ordered: ten "lady drinks" at 800 baht each, a "champagne service"
at 6,000, a "mama-san fee" at 2,400. The card terminal was already
on the table. Behind it, two men in tight black t-shirts stood
between Harry and the door.

## The trick

The bar bill trap stacks two patterns: Captive-Position Lever on
top of Sub-Market Quote. The five hundred baht on the laminated
card is the bait (Sub-Market Quote). The drinks served without
ordering and the absence of a posted menu set the scene. The
bouncers at the door close the position (Captive-Position Lever).
The bill arrives at the moment when standing up to leave is no
longer feasible.

In the catastrophic-tier variants, the drink itself is spiked. A
"fellow tourist" buys you a round. Within fifteen to thirty minutes
your motor function is impaired. The next clear memory is morning
and your cards have run through five ATMs.

The defense is three lines, mapped to the three stages of the
trap.

The first line is drink discipline. Do not accept drinks you did
not watch poured from a sealed bottle. Scopolamine, GHB, and
benzodiazepines are administered at the moment of pour or in the
moments after. If the drink is poured in front of you and stays
within your line of sight, the variant cannot operate.

The second line is door discipline. Do not enter venues without
printed prices visible at the door. The lady-drink and host-club
variants both rely on the absence of a printed menu so the staff
can invent prices at the end. A bar that publishes prices on a
printed menu has nothing to gain from the trap. A bar that refuses
to show prices is operating it.

The third line is card discipline. Do not hand over your card for
any reason. The honeytrap variant in Vietnam and the host-club
variant in Tokyo Kabukicho are built around a pre-authorization
hold on your credit card. Once your card has been swiped for "the
bottle service," any amount can be charged. Pay cash for the
agreed-upon items only. If cash is refused, leave.

## The four mechanics

**Lady-drink padding (Bangkok / Pattaya).** Tout offers cheap entry.
Bar has no menu. Hostesses sit immediately and order their own
drinks billed at five to ten times normal. After 60–90 minutes the
padded bill arrives. Documented heavily in Bangkok Sukhumvit Soi 4,
Patpong, Soi Cowboy, Nana Plaza; Pattaya Walking Street; Phuket
Bangla Road. Typical bill: 12,000–25,000 THB (350–750 USD).

**Host-club catch (Tokyo Kabukicho).** Street hustler offers "3,000
yen all-you-can-drink" or similar. Venue is a "catch-bar" front for
an unlicensed host club. Hostess or host orders her own drinks at
10,000+ yen each. Bouncers block exit. Documented heavily in Tokyo
Kabukicho, Roppongi, Shibuya; Osaka Namba; Sapporo Susukino.
Typical bill: 50,000–300,000 JPY (300–2,000 USD). The Tokyo
Metropolitan Police publish ongoing catch-bar warnings.

**Scopolamine compliance (Bogotá / Medellín).** "Fellow tourist" or
attractive stranger approaches at an upscale bar. Buys a drink. The
target becomes compliant within fifteen to thirty minutes. The
perpetrator walks the target through ATM withdrawals, takes credit
cards, jewelry, phones, occasionally assaults. Scopolamine
(*burundanga*) is the most-documented agent. The US State
Department has issued repeated advisories. Documented in Bogotá
Zona Rosa, La Candelaria, Chapinero; Medellín El Poblado, Provenza;
Cartagena Old City. Typical loss: 800–5,000 USD.

**Honeytrap pre-authorized card charge (Hanoi / Saigon).** Solo
male traveler approached by attractive person at one bar. Relocated
to second venue. Bottle service ordered. Card pre-authorized at high
amount. "Service charge" added at exit. Bouncers enforce payment.
Documented heavily in Hanoi Bui Vien (Old Quarter) and Tran Hung
Dao bars; Saigon Pham Ngu Lao and Bui Vien. Typical loss: 800–2,500
USD.

## Where it runs (4 countries, Thailand and Japan dominant for nightlife volume)

Drink spiking and bar bill traps concentrate in nightlife districts
where tourist density is high and local police presence is light or
unevenly enforced. Thailand drives the lady-drink volume. Japan
drives the host-club closing prices. Colombia drives the scopolamine
catastrophic-tier variants. Vietnam runs the honeytrap pre-authorized
card variant.

You will see less of this in countries with strong nightlife
licensing and active police presence in tourist-bar zones (most of
Western Europe, Australia, the United States). The variants travel
with the laminated-card street hustler and the velvet-rope front-door
filter.

## The five red flags

Two or more in the first thirty seconds: walk on, find a different
bar.

- **A laminated card with a too-cheap-to-believe entry price.**
  Real bars do not need hustlers on the corner.
- **No menu visible at the door or on the bar.** Reputable
  nightlife venues post menus at the entrance.
- **Drinks poured without you ordering them.** Hostess "lady drinks"
  arrive without a request. Real bars wait for you to order.
- **A drink bought for you by a stranger.** This is the pour you
  did not watch. Decline it without exception.
- **Bouncers between you and the door at the moment the bill
  arrives.** This is the captive-position close.

## The phrases that shut it down

Said before entering, at the door:

| Language | Phrase | Translation |
|---|---|---|
| Thai | *Mai krap, yang mai khao.* / *Mai ka, yang mai khao.* | No, not going in. |
| Japanese | *Iie, kekkō desu. Hairimasen.* | No thank you, not entering. |
| Spanish (Colombia) | *No, gracias. No tomo bebidas que no vea servir.* | No thanks. I don't drink anything I don't see poured. |
| Vietnamese | *Không, cảm ơn. Tôi không vào.* | No, thank you. I'm not going in. |
| English | "I need to see a printed price list before I sit down." |  |

The "printed price list" line ends the lady-drink and host-club
variants because the bait depends on the absence of one.

## If this happens to you

The bill trap closed and you paid. Or worse: you accepted a drink
from a stranger in Zona Rosa and the next clear memory is morning.

For the bar-bill-trap variants (Bangkok lady-drink, Tokyo host-club,
Hanoi honeytrap):

Phone the local tourist police from the table. Thailand 1155
(English-speaking, twenty-four hours). Japan 110. Vietnam 113.
Colombia 123. Photograph the venue exterior, the bill, the card
terminal, the receipt if printed. Phone your card issuer
immediately to dispute the charge as "fraud or coercion at point
of sale." This is a recognized chargeback category at Visa,
Mastercard, and Amex. Most bar-bill-trap charges are reversible if
reported within thirty days.

For suspected drink spiking (catastrophic-tier scopolamine variant):

Get to a hospital as soon as possible for toxicology and forensic
documentation; some substances have short detection windows, and
clinicians can advise whether blood or urine testing is
appropriate.
Benzodiazepines and GHB clear in six to twelve hours. Scopolamine
clears in twelve to twenty-four hours. Phone your embassy. The US,
UK, Canadian, and Australian embassies in Bogotá, Mexico City,
Bangkok, and Hanoi all have twenty-four-hour duty officers who
handle drink-spiking cases.

Cancel cards immediately. Check ATM withdrawals on your statement.
File a police report with the tourist-police line and request a
copy in English for insurance.

If you were physically blocked from leaving and felt threatened:
phone the embassy duty officer in addition to local police. The US
State Department's Overseas Citizens Services line is +1 202 501
4444. The equivalent UK FCDO line is +44 20 7008 5000. Both will
coordinate with local police on your behalf.

The catastrophic-tier loss profile (drink spiking plus card-running
plus possible assault) makes this the chapter to read in full,
regardless of where you are traveling. The cost of being wrong is
not just money.

Harry walked out of the Pink Pussycat with a bill of 6,400 baht
(about 190 dollars) after he photographed the bouncer's face and
told the *mama-san* he was calling 1155 from outside the door. He
phoned from the corner. The bouncers did not follow. He blocked
his card from the hotel ten minutes later. He slept poorly and
flew to Chiang Mai the next afternoon.
