## A note on how to choose

If you are going to one specific country, get that country's book.
The local detail in any one country book is more useful for your
trip than ten chapters of this atlas combined.

If you travel across several countries in a year, this atlas is the
keeper. Read it once, refer to it often. The country books are the
follow-ups when you commit to a specific destination.

If you travel for a living and have not bought a country book yet,
start with whichever destination you visit most often. The
financial saving on a single avoided scam will pay for the full
country library.
