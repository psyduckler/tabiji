# About the Travel Safety Series

The Travel Safety Series is the publishing program of Tabiji, an
independent travel-safety publisher founded in 2024. The series
documents tourist scams country by country, sourced from police
records, local press, and verified traveler reports.

This book is the Field Guide. The umbrella reference. Thirty
high-impact scams that travel across borders, alphabetical by scam
name, with the seven patterns that underlie them and the recovery
paths after they run. The complete catalog of additional scam types
lives on the web atlas at tabiji.ai/scams/atlas, which is updated
continuously.

The destination volumes are the country books. Each one takes a
single country and runs through every scam in that country with the
local detail you need the week before your flight. As of this
edition, twenty-four countries are live on Amazon Kindle and in
paperback.

The complete country list:

- Argentina · Australia · Brazil · Canada · China · Colombia ·
  Costa Rica · Egypt · France · Germany · Greece · India · Indonesia
  · Italy · Japan · Malaysia · Mexico · Morocco · Portugal · Spain
  · Thailand · Turkey · United Kingdom · Vietnam

We aim to add one country per quarter. The 2027 edition of this
atlas will reflect any countries shipped between now and then.

## How the series fits together

Read this book first. It is the cross-country reference, the field
guide that prepares you for the patterns you will see anywhere.
Take ten minutes with Part I.

Read your destination volume next. The country book goes deep on
the local neighborhoods, the specific addresses, the local language
phrases, the local police hotlines. The week before your flight is
the right time to read the country book.

If you travel often, both volumes are worth keeping. The atlas is
the textbook. The country books are the field manuals.

also free to read on the web. The book is the curated, portable,
offline-capable version. The web is the always-current source-
verifiable index.
