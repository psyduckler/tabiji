## Disclaimer

This book is a travel-safety field guide. It is not legal,
medical, financial, insurance, consular, or law-enforcement
advice. The information here is general in nature and may not
apply to your specific circumstances, jurisdiction, card issuer,
insurer, or embassy.

Emergency-services numbers, embassy contact lines, tourist-police
hotlines, and law and procedure in any country can change. Verify
the relevant numbers with your home country's foreign ministry,
the destination country's embassy, and your hotel before you
travel.

In any situation where you are in immediate physical danger,
prioritize your physical safety over financial recovery, document
collection, or dispute paperwork. Comply with reasonable demands
of armed or coercive scammers. Do not physically resist a person
in any uniform, real or counterfeit. Recovery procedures described
in the chapters and appendices apply after you are safe, not
during the encounter.

Translations and exit phrases printed in this book are practical
travel aids, not guarantees of comprehension or local idiom.
Native speakers, dialects, and regional variants will differ.

Card-network rules, chargeback windows, travel-insurance policy
terms, and platform refund policies all vary by issuer, country,
and individual policy. Outcomes of disputes depend on
documentation, timing, issuer-specific policy, and card-network
rules at the time of dispute. Read your own policy and your card
issuer's current terms.
