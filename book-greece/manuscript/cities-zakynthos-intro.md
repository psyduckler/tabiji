Zakynthos — also called Zante in UK package marketing — is the southernmost of the Ionian islands, famous for Navagio Shipwreck Beach, the loggerhead-turtle nesting beaches around Laganas Bay, and the Blue Caves at Cape Skinari. Heavy UK package-tourism, German + Italian secondary; Athens-area domestic visitors fill the shoulder season. The scam economy here is documented in detail by the British tabloid press — the Sun, the Mirror, the Daily Mail, and the Metro have all published recurring summer exposés naming specific Laganas-strip clip-joints by name. The Coroner's Office in the UK tracks Zakynthos quad and ATV deaths separately because the rate has been distinctively high for a decade.

The risk radiates from three corridors. The Laganas resort strip runs the most aggressive nightlife scams in Greece outside the Athens-Plaka clip-joints — spiked-cocktail incidents, bootleg-vodka substitution at "VIP table" minimum-consumption bars, and the bracelet-hawker pattern on the main strip where €1 wristbands turn into €30 bills with crew enforcement. Across Laganas, Argassi, and Tsilivi resort strips, scooter and quad rental shops run an aggressive damage-deposit cycle — and unlike Mykonos or Kos where the deposit is the financial damage, here the physical-injury rate is genuinely elevated. The Marathonisi (Turtle Island) boat-tour operators routinely overcrowd vessels and run "guaranteed turtle" markups on what is supposed to be a protected-species observation. A safe default: stick to named, posted-price venues in Laganas (the strip is small enough to walk in twenty minutes), refuse unrequested drinks and never share a poured cocktail you didn't watch mixed, decline all quad and ATV rentals (rent a small car if you must), and verify any Marathonisi boat tour against the Zakynthos Marine Park's published licensed-operator list.
