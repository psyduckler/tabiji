# Appendix A: Greek Exit-Phrase Card {-}

These are the phrases most likely to end a scam in Greece. They are arranged by situation. For each phrase you will see the **English translation**, followed by the **Greek** (in Greek script), a **Romanized** version, and a simple pronunciation guide in italics.

A practical suggestion: before your trip, take a screenshot of this appendix on your phone, and bookmark any phrase you think you are most likely to need. Say the first three or four out loud a few times. You will feel much less awkward using them when the moment comes.

## A note on pronunciation

Greek is not a tonal language like Thai or Mandarin — the meaning of a word does not change with pitch. This makes Greek easier for most English speakers. The stress (emphasis) falls on one syllable per word, marked with an accent in written Greek. Get the stress roughly right and you will be understood.

A few pointers:

- Greek vowels are consistent: *a* is "ah," *e* is "eh," *i* is "ee," *o* is "oh," *ou* is "oo."
- The letter *γ* (gamma) before *e* or *i* sounds like a soft "y" — *γεια* (hello) is pronounced "YA."
- The letter *χ* is a guttural "h" or "kh" sound, like clearing your throat gently.
- Double consonants (ντ, μπ, γγ) represent single sounds: *ντ* = "d" or "nd," *μπ* = "b" or "mb," *γγ* = "ng."

## Asking before you commit

**How much is it?**  
Πόσο κοστίζει;  
Póso kostízei?  
*POH-soh koh-STEE-zee?*

**How much does the whole thing cost? (for fish by weight)**  
Πόσο κοστίζει όλο;  
Póso kostízei ólo?  
*POH-soh koh-STEE-zee OH-loh?*

Use this before ordering any fish priced by the kilogram. Wait for a number in euros before the fish is cooked.

**Please use the meter.**  
Παρακαλώ, το ταξίμετρο.  
Parakaló, to taxímetro.  
*Pah-rah-kah-LOH, toh tahk-SEE-meh-troh.*

Say this the instant you get into a taxi. If the driver refuses, get out. Greek law requires metered fares except for posted flat-rate routes.

**Can I see the menu with prices?**  
Μπορώ να δω το μενού με τις τιμές;  
Boró na do to menoú me tis timés?  
*Boh-ROH nah thoh toh meh-NOO meh tees tee-MESS?*

Use this before you sit down at any restaurant. If the menu has no prices, walk out.

**Is this free?**  
Είναι δωρεάν;  
Eínai dhoreán?  
*EE-neh thoh-reh-AHN?*

Use this when bread, olives, or water arrive without ordering. Wait for verbal confirmation before eating.

## Declining and leaving

**No, thank you.**  
Όχι, ευχαριστώ.  
Óchi, efcharistó.  
*OH-hee, ef-hah-ree-STOH.*

The single most useful phrase in this appendix. Said firmly and without slowing your pace. Greek people understand it as a complete refusal.

**I don't want it.**  
Δεν το θέλω.  
Den to thélo.  
*Then toh THEH-loh.*

Stronger than "no, thank you." Use this for persistent vendors, bracelet-sellers, or rose-sellers.

**I did not order this.**  
Δεν το παρήγγειλα.  
Den to paríggeila.  
*Then toh pah-REENG-gee-lah.*

Use this at the end of the meal when the bill includes bread, olives, or cover charges you never ordered.

**The price is too high.**  
Η τιμή είναι πολύ ακριβή.  
I timí eínai polý akrivó.  
*Ee tee-MEE EE-neh poh-LEE ah-kree-VEE.*

Use this to signal you know the price is inflated. Often produces a "discount" to the fair price.

**I'm leaving.**  
Φεύγω.  
Févgo.  
*FEV-goh.*

Short and clear. Use this to end a negotiation or leave a venue.

## When things go wrong

**I need help.**  
Χρειάζομαι βοήθεια.  
Chriázomai voítheia.  
*Hree-AH-zoh-meh voh-EE-thee-ah.*

**Call the police.**  
Καλέστε την αστυνομία.  
Kaléste tin astinomía.  
*Kah-LESS-teh teen ah-stee-noh-MEE-ah.*

**I want to call the Tourist Police.**  
Θέλω να καλέσω την Τουριστική Αστυνομία.  
Thélo na kaléso tin Touristikí Astinomía.  
*THEH-loh nah kah-LEH-soh teen Too-ree-stee-KEE Ah-stee-noh-MEE-ah.*

**I need a receipt.**  
Χρειάζομαι απόδειξη.  
Chriázomai apódeixi.  
*Hree-AH-zoh-meh ah-POH-theek-see.*

Greek law requires receipts for all transactions. Businesses can be fined for refusing to issue one.

**Where is the police station?**  
Πού είναι το αστυνομικό τμήμα;  
Poú eínai to astinomikó tmíma?  
*POO EE-neh toh ah-stee-noh-mee-KOH TMEE-mah?*

**I need a doctor.**  
Χρειάζομαι γιατρό.  
Chriázomai yiatró.  
*Hree-AH-zoh-meh yah-TROH.*
