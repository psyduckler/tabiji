Heraklion is Crete's capital and the gateway to Knossos, the Bronze Age palace of the Minoans. It is also the epicenter of Crete's car-rental damage-claim scam. The island has dozens of small rental storefronts — in Heraklion, Hersonissos, and the airport road — that rent vehicles with pre-existing cosmetic damage, "discover" scratches on return, and charge five to ten times the real repair cost. Carwiz has been specifically named in Greek market warnings. The pattern is so consistent that r/crete threads discussing rental safety outnumber threads about restaurants.

The risk concentrates in three zones. Heraklion Airport (HER) runs the rental-damage script and the taxi-overcharge quote (€30 for a €15 metered ride to the city). The tourist strip from Hersonissos to Malia runs the nightclub "minimum consumption" trap and beach-club sunbed ambiguity. The harbour restaurants in Heraklion run the standard welcome-bread and per-kg fish patterns. A safe default: rent only from major international brands at the airport (Avis, Hertz, Europcar, Sixt — not Carwiz), photograph every panel with timestamps before driving off, pay by credit card only, and use a travel card with primary rental insurance. For dining, eat at Peskesi, Parasties, or Ligo Krasi — all posted-price, all community-verified.
