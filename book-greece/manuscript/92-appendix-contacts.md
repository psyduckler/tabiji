# Appendix C: Emergency Contacts {-}

Every phone number in this appendix was verified current at the time of publication, but phone numbers do change, consulates move, and hospitals occasionally renumber their switchboards. If a number below fails to connect, search the organization's name plus *Greece* and you will almost always find the current line on the first page of results.

**Before you travel**, we strongly recommend you double-check your own embassy or consulate's current number directly on its official website. Save the main switchboard and the after-hours duty-officer line on your phone before departure, and screenshot this appendix so you can read it offline.

## The numbers that work everywhere in Greece

These are the lines to dial from any phone — mobile or landline, Greek SIM or roaming — anywhere in the country. All of them are free.

- **European Emergency Number:** **112**. Works from any mobile phone in Greece and routes to the appropriate service. Use this if you cannot remember the specific Greek numbers below.
- **Tourist Police:** **171**. English-speaking dispatchers available in major tourist areas. This is the *first* number to try for any scam, theft, harassment, or assault incident involving a foreign visitor.
- **Hellenic Police (general emergency):** **100**. The national police general-emergency line. The first operator may speak Greek only; repeat "English, please" or "Tourist Police" and you will be transferred.
- **Ambulance (EKAB):** **166**. The national emergency medical services dispatch. English is available at major dispatch centers in Athens, Thessaloniki, and the tourist islands.
- **Fire:** **199**. National fire-and-rescue dispatch.
- **Coast Guard:** **108**. For maritime emergencies, ferry incidents, or accidents at sea.
- **Greek National Tourism Organisation (EOT):** **1572**. Tourist information and orientation hotline. Not a fraud-report line, but useful for guidance on disputes with registered tour operators.
- **Hellenic Consumer Ombudsman:** **210 646 0862**. Complaints against shops, restaurants, tour packages, and consumer fraud. Website: **synigoroskatanaloti.gr**.

## Card issuer emergency lines (callable from Greece)

When you call, have your card number (or the last four digits), the approximate transaction amount, and the time of the fraudulent charge ready.

- **Visa Global Customer Assistance (collect from Greece):** +1 303 967 1096. 24/7, English.
- **Mastercard Global Service (collect from Greece):** +1 636 722 7111. 24/7.
- **American Express International Service Center (collect):** +1 336 393 1111. 24/7.

If your card was issued by a bank, the fastest route is the number printed on the back of the card. That line routes you to your own issuer's fraud desk and they can freeze the card in under a minute.

## Embassies in Greece for major passport nationalities

Greece hosts most countries' embassies in Athens. The United States also maintains a consular presence in Thessaloniki.

### United States

- **US Embassy Athens:** 91 Vasilissis Sofias Avenue, 10160 Athens. Main switchboard +30 210 721 2951. After-hours emergency line for US citizens: +30 210 721 2951 (press 0).
- **US Consulate General Thessaloniki:** 43 Tsimiski Street, 7th Floor, 54623 Thessaloniki. Main switchboard +30 2310 242 905.

### United Kingdom

- **British Embassy Athens:** 1 Ploutarchou Street, 10675 Athens. Main switchboard +30 210 727 2600.

### Canada

- **Canadian Embassy Athens:** 48 Ethnikis Antistaseos Street, 15231 Chalandri, Athens. Main switchboard +30 210 727 3400.

### Australia

- **Australian Embassy Athens:** Level 6, Thon Building, Kifisias & Alexandras Avenue, 11523 Athens. Main switchboard +30 210 870 4000.

### Ireland

- **Embassy of Ireland Athens:** 7 Leoforos Vasileos Konstantinou, 10674 Athens. Main switchboard +30 210 723 2771.

For all nationalities: the embassy's published after-hours emergency line is the number to call if you have been arrested, hospitalized, assaulted, or lost your passport on a weekend.

## City-by-city emergency contacts

### Athens

- **Tourist Police Athens:** Dimitrakopoulou 6, Koukaki (near Syntagma). Walk-in desk for foreign visitors. Also reachable via 171.
- **Major 24/7 hospitals:** **Evangelismos Hospital** (45-47 Ipsilantou Street, +30 210 720 1000) is the largest public hospital in central Athens. **Athens Medical Center** (+30 210 686 4100) and **Hygeia Hospital** (+30 210 686 7000) are private hospitals with English-speaking staff and direct billing with international insurers.

### Thessaloniki

- **Tourist Police Thessaloniki:** Dodekanisou 4, near the White Tower. Dial 171 for directions.
- **Hospitals:** **AHEPA University Hospital** (+30 2310 993 111) is the main public teaching hospital. **Interbalkan Medical Center** (+30 2310 400 000) is a private hospital with international-patient services.

### Santorini

- **Tourist Police Santorini:** Fira main square. Dial 171.
- **Hospital:** **Santorini General Hospital** in Fira (+30 22860 22222). For serious emergencies, medical evacuation to Athens may be arranged.

### Mykonos

- **Tourist Police Mykonos:** Mykonos Town (Chora). Dial 171.
- **Hospital:** **Mykonos Health Center** (+30 22890 23994). Limited capacity — serious cases transfer to Syros or Athens.

### Rhodes

- **Tourist Police Rhodes:** Rhodes Old Town. Dial 171.
- **Hospital:** **Rhodes General Hospital** (+30 22410 80000).

### Corfu

- **Tourist Police Corfu:** Corfu Town. Dial 171.
- **Hospital:** **Corfu General Hospital** (+30 26610 88200).

### Heraklion (Crete)

- **Tourist Police Heraklion:** Central Heraklion. Dial 171.
- **Hospital:** **Venizeleio General Hospital** (+30 2810 368000). **University Hospital of Heraklion (PAGNI)** (+30 2810 392111) handles complex cases.

### Chania (Crete)

- **Tourist Police Chania:** +30 28210 73333.
- **Hospital:** **Chania General Hospital** (+30 28210 22000).

### Paros

- **Tourist Police Paros:** +30 22840 21673.
- **Health Center:** **Paros Health Center** in Parikia (+30 22840 22500). Serious cases transfer to Syros or Athens.

### Naxos

- **Tourist Police Naxos:** +30 22850 22100.
- **Health Center:** **Naxos Health Center** in Chora (+30 22850 23333). Serious cases transfer to Syros or Athens.
