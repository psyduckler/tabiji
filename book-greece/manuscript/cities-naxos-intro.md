Naxos is the largest of the Cyclades — an island of marble villages, Byzantine churches, and the iconic Portara gateway to an unfinished Temple of Apollo. Its scam ecosystem is smaller than Mykonos or Santorini, but the patterns are present, and one operator has been specifically named. Matha Rent a Car has been flagged in r/GreeceTravel community warnings for the damage-claim script and post-return credit-card fraud.

The risk concentrates in three zones. Naxos port runs the taxi-overcharge script: €20 quoted for a 1-km walk to most Chora hotels. Naxos Chora waterfront and the Kastro (Venetian old town) run welcome-bread markup and tourist-menu pricing at the restaurants with the best views. The beach-chair concessions at Plaka, Agios Prokopios, and Alyko run "per chair per hour" pricing that is disclosed only at payment. A safe default: take the KTEL bus from the port (€1.80), eat at Metaxi Mas, Taverna Mouragio, or Apolafsis with posted menus, do not rent from Matha Rent a Car specifically, rent from Europcar at Naxos Airport (JNX), and ask beach-chair operators for the total price in writing before sitting — "full day, two chairs plus umbrella, confirmed."
