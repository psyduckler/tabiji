The Cosmetic Surgery Field Guide — Cover Direction Prototypes

Four comic-style directions, generated 2026-05-11 via Nano Banana Pro. Pick one to develop into the final 6″ × 9″ wrap.

Brief: calm, considered, protective tone. Not glamorous, not alarmist. The visual should feel like a serious nonfiction buyer-protection book a careful adult would trust. Title overlay will sit in the upper third in production.

Direction 1

Hergé / Tintin ligne-claire

Clean uniform black ink, flat saturated color, no shading. A traveler at an airport gate with passport, clipboard, and rolling suitcase; airliner on the tarmac beyond the window.

Confident Field-guide adventure Friendly Clear silhouette

Reads as: "the careful traveler" — a book about preparing well for a trip. The cleanest, most legible at thumbnail size. Risk: can read as too cheerful for the seriousness of the subject if the title type is wrong.

Direction 2

Saul Steinberg / New Yorker editorial line

Spare confident black-ink line on cream, single warm-red accent. A solitary figure at a small table at night, lamp glow, stacked papers, coffee. Generous negative space.

Sophisticated Restrained Intellectual High negative space

Reads as: a thinking person's book. The most "literary nonfiction" of the four. Risk: potentially too austere for a mass-market Amazon thumbnail. Strong on a shelf, weaker as a 1-inch icon.

Direction 3

Norman Rockwell / Saturday Evening Post

Warm naturalistic painting, honey-amber palette, soft brushwork. A person at a kitchen table at evening, leaning thoughtfully on one elbow, paperwork spread out, pendant lamp overhead.

Warm Narrative Trustworthy Identifiable face

Reads as: honest deliberation, the kitchen-table moment of decision. Most emotionally relatable. Risk: the generated figure shows a clearly identifiable face — production version would need regen with face shadowed or turned away to avoid likeness questions.

Direction 4

Push Pin Studios / Glaser conceptual

Bold flat color, hand-cut-paper feel, saturated mustard / deep teal / terra-cotta / cream. A passport opened to a page where the stamp area carries a stylized medical-chart silhouette.

Designer-y Conceptual Bold thumbnail VISA text in image

Reads as: a smart conceptual nonfiction jacket. Strongest at thumbnail. Risk: the model included "VISA" text on the stamp despite no-text rule; production version would need regen, or we accept the text as part of the design and adjust the title placement around it.

To decide: Each of these will need one or two refinements before becoming the production cover. The cleanest path is to pick a direction (1–4), then we regenerate with title-area refinements and any specific notes (face treatment, palette adjustment, text removal) before building the full 6″ × 9″ wrap cover for KDP. Files are also in the repo at book-cosmetic-surgery/assets/cover-prototypes/.
