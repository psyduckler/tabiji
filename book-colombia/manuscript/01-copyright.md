## Disclaimer

**This book is for informational purposes only.** It is not legal, financial, medical, or insurance advice. Scam patterns evolve, legal protections vary by country of travel, and venue-specific details may change between editions.

References to streets, plazas, bus terminals, neighborhoods, and business types describe patterns of scam activity reported by Colombian newspapers, local authorities, and travelers at the time of writing. Mention of a specific area does not imply that all venues or individuals operating there engage in the described behavior. Where this book references named operators or establishments, it does so on the basis of published news coverage or direct consumer reviews; any such reference is intended as consumer information and not as an accusation of wrongdoing against any individual business that was not the subject of a specific cited report.

Information is current as of the **2026 Edition**, reflecting the current Colombian tourism landscape: the continued dominance of scopolamine (*burundanga*) drink-spiking in nightlife districts, the proliferation of fake Tinder and Bumble match setups in Medellín and Cali, the 2025 surge of *paseo millonario* (express-kidnapping) taxis in Bogotá, and the coexistence of a DiDi and InDrive rideshare market alongside a street-taxi economy in which Uber operates in a legal gray zone. For newly reported scams and live updates, visit **[tabiji.ai/scams](https://tabiji.ai/scams)**.

If you are victimized, consult local authorities. Colombia's unified emergency line is **123** — English-speaking dispatchers are intermittent; persistence helps. The **Policía Nacional de Turismo** operates dedicated tourist-protection units in Bogotá, Cartagena, Medellín, Cali, and Santa Marta; this book lists the specific contact numbers and addresses city by city in the appendix. For major incidents, also contact the **Comando de Atención Inmediata (CAI) Turístico** nearest your location, your embassy or consulate, your credit-card issuer, and your travel-insurance provider. Do not rely on this book as your sole source of guidance.
