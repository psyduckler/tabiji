# The Scams: City by City {-}

The chapters that follow document every scam reported in eight Costa Rican cities. Cities are ordered by the arc most first-time visitors actually fly: the Central Valley gateway (San José), then north to the Guanacaste beach belt (Liberia, Tamarindo), up into the cloud forest (Monteverde), east to the Arenal volcano region (La Fortuna), back down to the Central Pacific surf and nightlife towns (Jacó, Manuel Antonio), and finally across to the Caribbean (Puerto Viejo). Every major Costa Rica itinerary touches three to five of these cities, and the regional scam profiles overlap more than they diverge.

Costa Rica has four tourism regions, each with a distinctive scam texture. The **Central Valley** around San José concentrates the country's volume crime: SJO airport pirate taxis, Coca-Cola bus terminal pickpocketing, the *cambista* counterfeit-swap on Avenida 2, and the Airbnb calendar-leak home invasions that the November 25, 2025 U.S. Embassy Security Alert put on the record. The **Pacific-Guanacaste belt** — Liberia, Tamarindo, Jacó — runs on rental-car scams (Avis LIR, Hertz SJO, the *pinchonazo* flat-tire robbery on Route 21), beach-parking *guachimán* extortion, and the nightlife drink-and-rob pipeline at Hotel Cocal and the Tamarindo casino. The **cloud forest and Arenal** region (Monteverde, La Fortuna) concentrates tour-booking fraud: the monteverdetourscr.com copycat domain, the "Roy's nature guide" advance-fee setup, Lava Land Tours' $160 sloth-tour bait-and-switch, and the Bookaway / Monteverde Shuttle Bus Company mid-route redirect incident. The **Caribbean** (Puerto Viejo) runs on violent-leaning property crime: machete robberies on the Volio / Bribrí waterfall trails, key-fob jammer car break-ins on Playa Cocles, and inside-job Airbnb burglaries on Playa Chiquita and Punta Uva.

If you are traveling to only a few of these cities, skip directly to the relevant chapters. If you are visiting more, skim them all — many scams (the LIR/SJO rental-car insurance stack, the SINPE/WhatsApp deposit fraud, the *guachimán* beach-parking shakedown, the RFID key-fob relay theft, the calendar-leak Airbnb invasion, the copycat-domain tour booking fraud) appear in multiple cities with local variations. Knowing the pattern in Liberia makes you faster to spot it in Jacó or Tamarindo.

## The Eight Cities at a Glance {-}

![San José — Teatro Nacional and Plaza de la Cultura](assets/cities/san-jose.jpg)

**San José** — The Central Valley capital and the country's busiest volume-crime hub. SJO airport pirate-taxi cartel (88-upvote "TAXI CARTEL IN SAN JOSE TARGETS FOREIGNERS" PSA; 40-upvote near-kidnap incident on the wrong-way highway); the Uber "driver doesn't end the trip" fare gouge (₡3,900 → ₡20,000); the SJO Expedia / Hertz / Fox "mandatory insurance" $2,000 ambush (109-upvote r/CostaRicaTravel thread); Airbnb calendar-leak home invasions in Escazú, Santa Ana, and Barrio Escalante (369-upvote Guanacaste shoot-out case); Coca-Cola terminal pickpocketing; Parque Central *cambista* counterfeit-bill swap; Claro / Kolbi SIM-card "activation fee" fraud at baggage claim.

![Liberia — Daniel Oduber Quirós airport and Guanacaste dry forest](assets/cities/liberia.jpg)

**Liberia** — The Guanacaste gateway airport (LIR) and the worst single rental-car branch in Costa Rica: Avis LIR has three separate 2025–2026 PSA threads with dollar losses attached (29-upvote "Dont rent from Avis liberia airport"; 34-upvote "Don't rent Avis in Liberia"; $123 → $444 mandatory-insurance ambush). Budget / Hertz / Fox / Europcar counter phantom-fee stacks ($800 rental becomes $1,800); the *pinchonazo* flat-tire robbery on Route 21 to the Guanacaste beach towns; unofficial airport porter luggage grabs; LIR taxi "broken meter" overcharge ($80 for a $45 Coco run); Global Exchange rate gouge; Delta / Recope gas-station "pump not zeroed" and colón–dollar short-change.

![Tamarindo — Playa Tamarindo surf break and Punta Langosta](assets/cities/tamarindo.jpg)

**Tamarindo** — Guanacaste's surf-and-party capital and the site of the July 2025 home-invasion shooting of Canadian life coach Christopher Deir at a Los Jobos rental (U.S. Embassy Security Alert anchor case). The yellow-vest *guachimán* parking-extortion racket on the main strip (305-upvote r/travel thread); RFID key-fob relay theft outside Witch's Rock / Volcano Brewing (187-upvote "ROBBERY WARNING," $14,000 in camera gear); beach-hustler surf-lesson $200 "board damage" ambush; LIR shuttle / pirate-taxi gouge; Banco Nacional / BAC ATM skimming plus counterfeit-bill swap; Monkey Bar / casino drink-spiking and "sexpat" $500 extortion script.

![Monteverde — cloud-forest canopy and hanging bridges](assets/cities/monteverde.jpg)

**Monteverde** — The cloud-forest reserve that moved to online timed entry in December 2024 ($29 per circuit at cloudforestmonteverde.com) and the copycat-domain epicenter: monteverdetourscr.com (NOT monteverdetours.com, which has operated since 2002) cost one Reddit poster $1,000 USD for a tour that was cancelled the day before and never refunded (52-upvote PSA). Fake / unlicensed "certified guide" ambush at the reserve gate; Bookaway / Monteverde Shuttle Bus Company wrong-pickup incidents; Santa Elena hostel-desk 30–50% commission markup on night walks and Don Juan coffee tours; jeep-boat-jeep double-booking and wrong-hotel pickup on the Arenal crossing; "Roy's nature guide" advance-fee TripAdvisor-flagged scam.

![La Fortuna — Arenal volcano and Catarata La Fortuna](assets/cities/la-fortuna.jpg)

**La Fortuna** — Arenal volcano's gateway town and the highest-voted Vrbo scam in the 2025 Costa Rica archive. Lava Land Tours "Julio" storefront $160 sloth-tour bait-and-switch (named-operator PSA); the 239-upvote "Vrbo host robbed us of all our electronics on our last day" Chachagua-corridor inside job (laptops, cameras, headphones, cash stolen during a "15-minute power cut"); red-taxi *María* meter tampering (₡17,500 for a $10 ride); roadside Rio Chollín "parking mafia" for a free public hot spring; La Fortuna Waterfall ADIFORT fake "parking permit" collectors; jeep-boat-jeep luggage-overage ambush; Societal La Fortuna Expedia freeze-out ($550 CAD lost).

![Jacó — Avenida Pastor Díaz and Playa Jacó sunset](assets/cities/jaco.jpg)

**Jacó** — The Central Pacific nightlife town *Tico Times* 2025 named as "a drug town taken by all sorts of shady people." Hotel Cocal & Casino drink-spiking and in-room-safe theft (TripAdvisor 187+ reviews; one $2,000-and-passport loss "while I was out fishing"); "Costa Joe" / Joseph Goodwin WhatsApp deposit fraud (named operator across cars, golf carts, Airbnbs, jet skis); beach-parking yellow-vest fake "no-park zone" extortion plus RF key-jammer break-ins at Jaco Walk; Mirador Jacó abandoned-villa robbery hotspot; ATV and golf-cart damage-inflation deposit trap on Avenida Pastor Díaz; SJO–Jacó shuttle overcharge ($200+ quoted for a $50–$65 pp Interbus run).

![Manuel Antonio — Playa Espadilla and the park turnaround](assets/cities/manuel-antonio.jpg)

**Manuel Antonio** — The most-scammed national-park approach in Costa Rica and the canonical "fake ranger" zone. Route 618 khaki-uniform fake-SINAC roadblock (146-upvote 2026 PSA, U.S. Embassy Security Alert anchor); fake paper SINAC ticket booths selling $20–$40 hand-printed "tickets" that don't work at the real turnstile ($18.08 online-only); unofficial "ICT-certified" guides at the gate charging $60 for a mangrove shortcut; Playa Espadilla "David's crew" parking-and-umbrella cash-only intimidation (metal-rod threats documented); Tracopa SJO–Quepos bus rest-stop overhead-bin theft; capuchin-monkey-and-human-accomplice beach-towel distraction snatch; Manuel Antonio strip "gringo menu" hidden 13% IVA + 10% service.

![Puerto Viejo — Playa Cocles and Calle 217](assets/cities/puerto-viejo.jpg)

**Puerto Viejo** — The South Caribbean village with the most physically dangerous scam profile in the country. Volio Falls / Bribrí waterfall masked-machete robberies (210-upvote "CRIME: The Machete guy did happen" with police-report photo); key-fob jammer car break-ins on Playa Cocles, Chiquita, and Punta Uva (62-upvote confirmed in Limón); "you want weed?" drug-dealer stalking and robbery-after-deal setup on Calle 217 after 9 PM; beach belongings snatch during swim/snorkel; Tuanis / Dogg Bikes and copycat bike-rental passport-as-deposit extortion; MEPE vs fake shuttle gouge ($12 bus vs $50+ phantom shuttle); Playa Cocles / Punta Uva Airbnb inside-job "power-outage" burglary.
