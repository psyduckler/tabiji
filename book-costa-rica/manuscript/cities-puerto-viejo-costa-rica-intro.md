Puerto Viejo de Talamanca is the village on Costa Rica's South Caribbean coast with the most physically dangerous scam profile in the country. The anchor is the machete-robbery corridor through Kekoldi / Bribrí Indigenous territory: u/SOdapop2980 opened the modern record in 2023 with "Machete guy in Volio falls? (Puerto Viejo)"; two years later, u/kentasasakado's 210-upvote "CRIME: The Machete guy did happen; proof attached" (July 2025) pairs a police report with a map circling the attack spot and the words "I got chased in the jungle last night and robbed." u/Nervous-Rabbit2253's 111-upvote "Puerto Viejo - Young ladies use caution" (March 2025) describes three men surrounding a car after stalking the OP; her mother had to run. u/jesuswastransright: "A family member was robbed and they held a machete to his throat just a few weeks ago on the beach." A 1,876-upvote r/news thread (February 2026) reframed the regional risk with the Cahuita home-invasion murder of American surfer Kurt Van Dyke. On the road, the key-fob jammer scam is the most economically damaging pattern in Limón province — u/Ok_Patience_7565's 62-upvote r/costarica PSA (November 2025) documents an RF jammer that blocks the fob signal so the car "looks locked" (lights flash, LED blinks) but the doors never actually engage; u/magicalbean420 confirmed it happened to him in Puerto Viejo; the same pattern cost u/fineartbydan $14,000 in camera gear in Tamarindo (187 upvotes) and runs identically on Playa Cocles, Chiquita, and Punta Uva. Drug-dealer stalking on Calle 217 after 9 PM runs the "you want weed?" robbery-after-deal setup described by u/Wooden_Marionberry40 and u/cassidylorene1 — dealers invite the tourist down a jungle path to complete the sale and produce a machete at the chokepoint or hand over oregano. MEPE bus fare gouging ($12 real vs $50+ phantom shuttle) and the Tuanis-style bike-rental passport-as-deposit ambush round out the daily catalog; and the Airbnb inside-job burglary at isolated Playa Cocles / Punta Uva villas (r/CostaRicaTravel 1fguymf and adjacent threads) lands the same "power cut while you're out to dinner" pattern as La Fortuna's 239-upvote Vrbo case.

The risk zones are any waterfall trail inland of Route 36 (Volio, Bribrí, Gandoca-Manzanillo back trails without a SINAC gate or paid entrance), the Calle 217 main drag and unlit jungle paths 50 meters off it after 9 PM, dirt pullouts along Route 256 to Manzanillo (Cocles, Chiquita, Punta Uva), and any remote jungle *cabina* Airbnb with a single-caretaker key. The safe default: hike waterfalls **only** with ICT-certified agencies — Caribe Shuttle (caribeshuttle.com), Terraventuras, Exploradores Outdoors, or the Jaguar Rescue Center — and stick to paid-entry refuges (Cahuita National Park at Kelly Creek or Puerto Vargas, the Manzanillo refuge core trail). Carry a decoy wallet with ₡10,000; passport in the hotel safe. At beach parking, always physically tug the door handle after pressing lock — if it opens, press again or use the key manually; carry an RFID Faraday pouch ($10 Amazon); park only in supervised lots (Rocking J's, Cocles Beach Guardado near Cafe Rico $3, Maxi's Manzanillo). Empty the rental completely — empty the trunk, leave the glove box open. Book lodging with 24/7 on-site staff — La Costa de Papito (Cocles), Azania Bungalows, Shawandha Lodge (Chiquita), Selvin's Cabinas (Punta Uva), Cariblue Beach & Jungle Resort — and skip isolated caretaker-only villas. For SJO transfers, MEPE from the Gran Terminal del Caribe is the real cheap route at ₡5,960–₡6,760 (~$12); Caribe Shuttle door-to-door is $54 — never a $30 "shuttle" from a Calle 217 tout. Do not buy recreational drugs in Puerto Viejo; walk in pairs after 9 PM; and if cornered, duck into Soda Lidia, Lazy Mon, Koki Beach, or Bread & Chocolate and ask staff to call 911. Dial Tourist Police **2586-4052** or Fuerza Pública Puerto Viejo (Calle 217) for *denuncia* reports required by travel insurance.
