Tamarindo is Guanacaste's surf-and-party capital and, since the July 11, 2025 home-invasion shooting of Canadian life coach Christopher Deir at a Los Jobos rental, the U.S. Embassy San José's headline case on vacation-rental armed robberies. OIJ reported that an intruder broke in and shot Deir in the hallway after his partner's screams drew him out. The 369-upvote "Armed Robbery + Shoot Out On Vacation" r/CostaRicaTravel thread (u/2Coneone, October 2025) and the August 2025 "Attempted Robbery on Honeymoon" Playa Negra VRBO case (u/creativethoughtsbaby, 5–6-flashlight invasion, piled-furniture defense) confirm the pattern is active across the full beach corridor. The risk zones are any isolated beachfront villa without 24/7 armed security (Los Jobos, Playa Negra, Playa Grande, Playa Langosta-beach sections outside guarded communities), the main strip parking pullouts between Plaza Tamarindo and the estuary, the Monkey Bar / casino nightlife corridor after 10 PM, and the unlit beach path from the main strip to Langosta.
