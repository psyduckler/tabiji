# Appendix C: Emergency Contacts {-}

Every phone number in this appendix was verified current at the time of publication. Phone numbers do change — if a number below fails to connect, search the organization's name plus *Costa Rica* and you will almost always find the current line on the first page of results.

**Before you travel**, we strongly recommend you double-check your own embassy or consulate's current number directly on its official website (the U.S. State Department's travel.state.gov, the U.K.'s gov.uk, Canada's travel.gc.ca, Ireland's dfa.ie, Australia's Smartraveller). Save the number and the after-hours emergency line on your phone before departure. Even a verified number can become a wrong number between editions.

## National (anywhere in Costa Rica)

- **Emergencies (police, ambulance, fire):** **911** from any phone anywhere in Costa Rica. English dispatch is available through the Tourist Police Operations Center (launched September 2025 with hubs in San José and Jacó); otherwise operators route through a translator.
- **OIJ tip line (fraud, theft, major crime):** **800-8000-645** ("800-8000-OIJ"), 24/7 nationwide.
- **Tourist Police 24/7 line:** **2258-1008** / **2258-1022** — English-friendly.
- **ICT (Instituto Costarricense de Turismo) tourist info + operator licensing:** **2299-5800** / **1-800-TOURISM** (1-800-868-7476) toll-free from U.S. and Canada / [visitcostarica.com](https://www.visitcostarica.com).

- **Red Cross (Cruz Roja Costarricense):** **128**.
- **Poison Control (Centro Nacional de Intoxicaciones):** **2223-1028** — for drink-spiking, scopolamine (*burundanga*), benzodiazepines, accidental ingestion.
- **MOPT (transport ministry — taxi fare disputes):** **2523-2000** / [ctp.go.cr/inconformidades](https://www.ctp.go.cr/inconformidades).

## Scam + fraud reporting

- **OIJ Fraud Unit:** tip line **800-8000-645**; email [oij_fraudes@poder-judicial.go.cr](mailto:oij_fraudes@poder-judicial.go.cr) for named-operator fraud (Costa Joe / Joseph Goodwin, monteverdetourscr.com, Lava Land Tours Julio, Avis Liberia counter scams). File a *denuncia* for the case number you will need on chargebacks and insurance claims.
- **Defensoría del Consumidor (Consumer Protection):** [consumidor.go.cr](https://www.consumidor.go.cr). For Ley 7472 violations — undisclosed service charges, "gringo menus" without printed prices, missing *factura electrónica*, rental-car mandatory-insurance ambushes.
- **ICT Operator Complaints:** **2299-5800** / [visitcostarica.com/en/contact-us](https://www.visitcostarica.com/en/contact-us). Can revoke tour-operator licenses; effective for ICT-registered businesses.
- **SUTEL (telecoms):** [sutel.go.cr](https://sutel.go.cr). For Claro / Kolbi / Liberty SIM fraud at SJO baggage claim.

## English-speaking medical care

Costa Rica runs a two-track healthcare system: the **CCSS** (*Caja Costarricense de Seguro Social*) public network covers residents and requires *cédula* registration; private hospitals accept travel insurance directly and handle foreigners best. For anything serious, go private.

- **Hospital CIMA San José** (Escazú, Autopista Próspero Fernández): **+506 2208-1000**. The top private hospital in Costa Rica, fully English-speaking, JCI-accredited, 24/7 ER. Most U.S. travel insurance carriers work direct-bill.
- **Hospital Clínica Bíblica** (central San José, Avenida 14, Calle Central): **+506 2522-1000**. Major private hospital, 24/7 ER, English-speaking.
- **Hospital La Católica** (Guadalupe): **+506 2246-3000**. Private, 24/7.

## A note on dialing 911

If you dial 911 from any Costa Rican phone, you will be asked which service you need — police (*policía*), ambulance (*ambulancia*), or fire (*bomberos*). Operators usually speak functional English, especially since the 2025 Tourist Police Operations Center rollout. State your location as a landmark: "*Hotel Cocal, Jacó*," "*Playa Espadilla, Manuel Antonio*," "*Parque Central Santa Elena, Monteverde*," "*Calle 217, Puerto Viejo*." If you are in a rental car with GPS, read the coordinates. Dispatchers can pull your approximate location from a mobile signal but the landmark saves time.

If you are calling from a mobile with no signal, drive or walk until you have one and try again — Costa Rica has no equivalent of Australia's 112 auto-routing. For rural areas (Kekoldi / Bribrí, El Castillo / Chachagua, Osa Peninsula back roads), Fuerza Pública response times can run 30+ minutes; for major town centers, response is typically under 15 minutes.

For non-emergency property theft or pickpocketing, go directly to the nearest **OIJ subdelegación** (for San José, Liberia, Quepos, Jacó, Puerto Viejo, Monteverde) or **Fuerza Pública station** to file a *denuncia*. The case number is mandatory for travel-insurance claims, credit-card chargebacks, and Airbnb/Vrbo/Booking refund requests.
