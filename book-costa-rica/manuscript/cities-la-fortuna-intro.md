La Fortuna is the gateway town to Arenal Volcano, the Costa Rican hot-springs capital, and the city that produced the highest-voted single scam thread in the 2025 Costa Rica archive: a 239-upvote r/CostaRicaTravel PSA, "Our Vrbo host robbed us of all our electronics on our last day in La Fortuna" (u/ljpeppers, May 2025). On the guests' final evening they went to dinner; they returned to find laptops, cameras, headphones, phones, watches, and cash gone, the house locked, the TV still playing Netflix, and no sign of forced entry. The host's explanation — "they shut the power off for 15 minutes" — contradicted the still-running TV; Find My tracked the stolen devices moving toward San Ramón. The property was a remote El Castillo / Chachagua-corridor villa with "Premier Host" status; a comment thread surfaced a second traveler who had been hit at the same property. Alongside that runs the named-operator tour-fraud case at **Lava Land Tours** (owner Julio, WhatsApp +506 8442-7337): an 11-upvote PSA (u/greatmrs, November 2023) documented $160 USD cash paid for a promised sloth tour with an English-speaking guide; what showed up was an older Costa Rican man with binoculars who spoke no English, walked 500 meters ahead of the group in silence, and produced no sloths; Julio refused a refund, shouted profanities on the main street, and subbed in a $10 hot-springs visit. The 76-upvote "TRAVELER WARNING FOR SOCIETAL LA FORTUNA" PSA (u/Jaded_Organization69, May 2025) logs a $550 CAD Expedia booking where the hotel "had local-government issues and couldn't host them" on arrival, emails reached no one, and Expedia denied a refund for five days. Adjacent to these named cases: the red-taxi *María* meter that already read ₡8,000 in the first minute and ₡17,500 at arrival for what Uber estimated at $10 (u/citibikefinder); the Rio Chollín "parking mafia" charging $5–$10 for a free public hot-spring river; the La Fortuna Waterfall "unofficial parking permit" collectors 500 meters before the ADIFORT gate (parking is included in the $18 entry); and the Jeep-Boat-Jeep Monteverde crossing "luggage over 15 kg $20 overage" ambush at the boat dock.

The risk zones are any remote El Castillo / Chachagua-corridor villa without on-site staff, the Route 142 approach to Rio Chollín and the access road to La Fortuna Waterfall, downtown Avenida Central cash-only storefronts demanding Zelle or SINPE deposits, and red-taxi stands at Parque La Fortuna. The safe default: book lodging with 24/7 on-site staff — Lomas del Volcán ($100–$150 volcano-view cabins), Arenal Observatory Lodge ($165+), Volcano Lodge, Hotel Magic Mountain, or Palacios Arenal Hostel for budget — and read every 1- and 2-star review for the phrases "power was out" or "felt watched." On your last night, take laptops, passports, jewelry, and cash to dinner (or transfer to your next hotel early); enable Find My iPhone / Find My Device with email and phone alerts before the trip. Book tours only through ICT-registered operators with TripAdvisor / Viator / GetYourGuide presence — Desafio Adventure Costa Rica, Eco Terra Costa Rica, Canoa Aventura, or your hotel front desk — and pay credit card only. For the airport transfer use Uber or DiDi (Uber more reliable in La Fortuna, $8–$14 typical in-town vs ₡17,500 red-taxi); for hot springs, know that **Rio Chollín is FREE** (park past the Tabacón bridge, daylight only) and paid resort rates are Tabacón $99, Baldi $51, Paradise $64, Titoku $52, EcoTermales $50. For SJO → La Fortuna use Interbus ($56–$75 shared) or a private shuttle ($140–$200) — never a WhatsApp "driver" demanding Zelle upfront. Program Fuerza Pública La Fortuna **2479-9288**, 911, and OIJ **800-8000-645** on arrival.
