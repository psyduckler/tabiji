Liberia is the Guanacaste gateway — Daniel Oduber Quirós International Airport (LIR) feeds Tamarindo, Playas del Coco, Playa Hermosa, and Flamingo — and it is the worst single rental-car location in Costa Rica. Avis at LIR is a named repeat offender across three separate 2025–2026 PSA threads: the 34-upvote "Don't rent Avis in Liberia" (u/Traditional-Ride-379, April 2025) documents a $350 online prepay plus a $350 counter "liability" demand; the 29-upvote "Dont rent from Avis liberia airport" (u/gshock7665, April 2026) calls it "a highly fraudulent and scamming location, they prey on tourists big time... staff and owners are all heavily involved and will use your credit card the way they want"; and the original "Rental car scams" thread documented a Hertz LIR online quote of $123 that ballooned to $444 at the counter. u/isnt_that_special: "Hertz Liberia did this to us too. Same scam. False damage claims, we even had photo & video proof the damage existed at pickup." The Budget / Fox / Europcar / NextCar stack runs parallel: a March 2025 "Car rental scam" thread (u/Antique_Resource_613) logs $1,372 demanded on arrival for one car booked through Economybookings at $639. Downstream of the counter scam is the *pinchonazo* (puncture) on Route 21: an accomplice pokes a tire valve at a Delta or Recope fuel stop on the Liberia belt road, a "good Samaritan" motorcyclist materializes within two minutes of your pullover, and the back seat is emptied while the "help" works the jack — typical loss $2,000–$8,000. The LIR airport curb runs unofficial "porters" ($20/bag demanded after the grab), fake pre-booked shuttle sign-holders quoting $120 for a prepaid $65 Interbus run, and taxi "broken meter" overcharges ($80 for what MOPT says is a $45–$55 Coco fare). Global Exchange inside the terminal runs ₡450/USD when the Banco Central posts ₡515–₡520 — a 6–10% skim.

The risk zones are LIR arrivals curb, any Avis / Hertz / Fox / Budget / Europcar counter inside or along the airport access road, Delta and Recope gas stations on the Route 21 belt, and BAC / Scotiabank street-side ATMs in Liberia city after dark. The safe default: book rental cars DIRECT with **Adobe** (adobecar.com; MyTanFeet $25 off) or **Vamos** (vamosrentacar.com; 10% cash discount), both ICT-licensed and 4.5+ Google; if you need a major U.S. brand, use the U.S. Alamo site (alamo.com, not alamo.cr — documented $300 savings). Bring a printed Chase / Capital One Venture X / Amex Platinum Costa Rica CDW letter; accept only the mandatory TPL (~$10–$20/day) and decline everything else in writing. After LIR or any Liberia fuel stop, drive 200 meters and inspect all four tires; on a wobble, keep driving to the next populated area and call Adobe's 24/7 line (**2542-4800**) or Vamos's (**4000-0557**). Pre-book LIR transfers via interbusonline.com, caribeshuttle.com, or iltcostarica.com rather than taking a curbside cab. Withdraw colones only at BAC, Scotiabank, or Banco Nacional inside-lobby ATMs during business hours, and skip the Global Exchange kiosk entirely.
