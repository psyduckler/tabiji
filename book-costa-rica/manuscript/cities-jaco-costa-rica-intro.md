Jacó is the Central Pacific nightlife town that *Tico Times*' 2025 "Danger In Costa Rica? Rising Crime" series named as "a drug town taken by all sorts of shady people" — a 45-upvote r/CostaRicaTravel mirror thread (u/Own-Cryptographer534) put the quote on the record. The city's headline risk is the Hotel Cocal & Casino drink-and-rob pipeline: TripAdvisor catalogs 187+ reviews describing "prostitutes and thieves" at the pool bar, including one reviewer "robbed of $2k and my passport while I was out fishing"; the 2025 "Solo trip to Jaco, planning to go to Hotel Cocal" r/CostaRicaTravel thread (u/IntrepidSalad3242) collects field advice from regulars: "watch your drink, have the bartender open the beer in sight"; "your largest safety concern is the people you will let in the room — their mission is to steal your wallet/phone/key card. Also, if you have a payment dispute, their male friends will be very effective in collections"; "lots of post clients belongings disappearing even out of the hotel room safes." Alongside it runs the 35-upvote "'Costa Joe' scam" PSA (u/StartupsAndTravel, 2025), which identifies a named operator — real name **Joseph Goodwin**, mid-twenties, WhatsApp-based — who sets up multiple Facebook profiles tagging each other as "references" and takes $200–$800 deposits via SINPE Móvil, Zelle, or crypto for car rentals, golf carts, Airbnbs, jet skis, and fishing charters that never materialize. The 52-upvote "Monteverde Tours Costa Rica - Buyer Beware" PSA (u/rmh901, November 2024) documents the copycat-domain variant that drains deposits from Jacó-bound travelers — monteverdetourscr.com (the scam) vs monteverdetours.com (the real operator since 2002) — costing u/rmh901's group $1,000 USD for a tour cancelled the day before and never refunded. Adjacent: the beach-parking yellow-vest fake "no-park zone" fine (u/SignificanceFast9207, r/CostaRicaTravel 2024) stacked with RF key-fob jammer break-ins at Jaco Walk plaza (62-upvote r/costarica PSA); the SJO → Jacó arrivals-hall shuttle overcharge ($200–$300 quoted for what Uber runs at $70–$100 and Interbus at $50–$65); the 9-upvote warning on the Mirador Jacó abandoned-villa hike: "Stay away from the Mirador Jaco - tourists get robbed there all day, every day"; and the ATV / golf-cart damage-inflation ambush on Avenida Pastor Díaz that u/q2fgyt's trip report flagged at up to $2,700 in fake return charges.

The risk zones are Hotel Cocal & Casino's pool bar and in-room safes, the Avenida Pastor Díaz nightlife strip (Beatle Bar, Le Loft, Orange Pub) after midnight, the Jaco Walk plaza and main beach parking pullouts, the Mirador Jacó hike after 4 PM, and any WhatsApp or Facebook "fixer" quoting a rental deposit via SINPE or Zelle. The safe default: skip Hotel Cocal for lodging entirely; book Hotel Club del Mar, Crocs Resort (family side), DoCe Lunas, or Los Sueños condos instead. At the bar, order bottled / canned drinks only and watch them opened; never bring a nightlife companion back to your room; keep your passport in the lobby safe-deposit box (not the in-room safe) and carry only a color copy plus one card to the strip. Rent ATVs and golf carts only at Avenida Pastor Díaz storefronts (AXR Jaco, Aventuras AVT Rentals, JacoLuxe, Adventure Tours CR) with a credit-card hold and a date-stamped 360° video walkaround narrating defects before you leave the lot. Refuse every WhatsApp / Facebook deposit request from individuals; book cars only with Adobe, Vamos, Budget, Alamo, or Economy direct at the SJO counter. For the SJO transfer, use Uber ($70–$100), a Taxi Aeropuerto orange-and-yellow cab (~$100–$130), or pre-booked Interbus ($50–$65 pp shared). Dial 911 at the first sign of sudden dissociation or slurred speech 20–40 minutes into a drink; the Tourist Police Operations Center opened in Jacó in September 2025 with English dispatch.
