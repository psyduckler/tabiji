Florianópolis — universally called "Floripa" — is the capital of Santa Catarina, an island city on Ilha de Santa Catarina with 500,000 year-round residents and 42 named beaches. For most international visitors, Floripa is a beach-and-adventure destination: surfing at Praia Mole and Joaquina, beach-club nightlife at Jurerê Internacional, Lagoa da Conceição lagoon kayaking and sunset dining, day-trips to Ilha do Campeche by boat, and occasionally cross-border routes to Argentina and Uruguay.

The scam surface area in Floripa has a distinct high-season / low-season rhythm. The peak summer is December through March, with rental prices 4–10× normal; Réveillon on Jurerê Internacional is a mega-event drawing 100,000+ attendees. The winter low season (June–August) sees beach infrastructure wound down and many restaurants closed, which is its own visitor problem.

First, Hercílio Luz airport (FLN) is 12 km from Centro, and the "Special Taxi" kiosk quotes flat R\$ 150+ to Centro / Lagoa / Praia Mole when Uber is R\$ 50–R\$ 100. Second, the aluguel-de-temporada / Airbnb high-season cancel-and-relist fraud is a documented 2025 pattern — r/florianopolis "Tá difícil Airbnb pro verão" (2025) and "Help report this scam" (2025) are the canonical community anchors. Third, and with exceptional caution: the **"Mega Model" beach-recruit scam** targets young female tourists at Praia Mole, Praia Brava, and Jurerê Internacional with "are you a model? our agency Mega Model is doing a campaign — free accommodation in our model house during the shoot" openers. The pattern is a documented trafficking template, flagged by r/florianopolis "Has anyone been approached by Mega Model scouts?" (2025). Brief every female member of your travel group specifically on this pattern. Disque 100 (federal anti-trafficking hotline) handles reports.

Fourth, the upscale beach clubs at Jurerê Internacional — Café de la Musique, Donna Floripa, P12, Taikô — charge R\$ 150–R\$ 500 for beach chair + umbrella + minimum spend, which is legitimate; but some also upsell "VIP couples bed R\$ 1,500+" on beaches that are 100% public federal property. Fifth, the Lagoa da Conceição restaurant strip sells standard Brazilian couvert + serviço padding. Sixth, the north-island schooner tours to Canasvieiras, Ponta das Canas, and Ilha do Campeche run an unlicensed-operator safety problem, with some boats lacking Marinha do Brasil registration and life jackets.

Florianópolis violent-crime incidence is the lowest among Brazilian tourist cities; scams here are almost entirely financial, with the trafficking-template beach-recruit exception that justifies its own separate warning chapter.
