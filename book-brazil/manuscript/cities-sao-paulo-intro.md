São Paulo is Brazil's economic capital — 12 million residents in the city proper, 22 million in the metropolitan area, the Western Hemisphere's largest urban agglomeration outside of Mexico City. For the international visitor, São Paulo is a business-travel city, a weekend layover on the way to Rio or Foz do Iguaçu, or a food-and-arts stopover (the MASP, the Pinacoteca, the Mercado Municipal, the Beco do Batman street-art corridor in Vila Madalena). Unlike Rio, São Paulo's tourist corridor is diffuse — there is no Copacabana equivalent, and visitors distribute across Paulista, Jardins, Itaim Bibi, Vila Olímpia, Vila Madalena, Pinheiros, and (more cautiously) Centro Histórico.

The scam surface area reflects this sprawl. First, the airports: Guarulhos (GRU) is the international hub 25 km east of the city, and Congonhas (CGH) is the downtown domestic airport 8 km south. Both run "Special Taxi" kiosks at R\$ 250–R\$ 500 flat quotes to Paulista when Uber / 99 / Cabify are R\$ 90–R\$ 180 (GRU) and R\$ 30–R\$ 70 (CGH). Second, Avenida Paulista — the 2.8 km commercial spine with 1.5 million daily foot-traffic — hosts two flagship distraction-pickpocket scams: the "os palhaços" (the clowns) coordinated three-to-five-man team documented by r/saopaulo "I fell for the scam of the clowns on Paulista Avenue" (2025), and the "old lady blood-pressure" distraction-theft variant where a well-dressed older woman feigns a medical emergency while a partner lifts wallet and phone. Third, the Centro Histórico — Sé, Anhangabaú, Estação da Luz, República, 25 de Março — contains the city's most-photographed colonial-era sights and its highest tourist-pickpocket density.

Fourth, São Paulo's restaurant industry has a documented 2025 "abusive practices" problem around couvert artístico and serviço padding — r/saopaulo "Abusive practices of restaurants and bars" (2025) is the canonical anchor, and the PROCON-SP consumer-protection office publishes advisory notices on it. Fifth, São Paulo's rideshare ecosystem is excellent overall, but a specific off-app cancel-and-recontract fraud runs at GRU and CGH where drivers accept the in-app ride, cancel at the kerb, then quote cash R\$ 250+.

São Paulo has meaningful violent-crime incidence outside the tourist corridors, but in the Paulista / Jardins / Itaim / Vila Madalena / Pinheiros belt that accounts for 95% of visitor time, street safety is comparable to most large Latin American cities during daytime. The DEATUR office at R. Sá e Souza 99 in Vila Olímpia is staffed 24/7 and handles foreign-visitor reports in English.
