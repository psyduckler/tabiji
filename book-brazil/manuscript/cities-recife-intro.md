Recife — the capital of Pernambuco, nicknamed "the Brazilian Venice" for its network of rivers and bridges — is the third-largest urban area in Northeast Brazil. For international visitors, Recife is typically paired with Olinda (6 km north, UNESCO-listed colonial hill-town) and Porto de Galinhas (1 hour south, Brazil's gold-standard safe beach with natural tide pools). Most Recife itineraries run three to five nights.

The scam surface area is characteristic of a Northeast-coast tourism city, with one extraordinary safety issue that justifies its own section: the Boa Viagem shark-attack zone.

First, the airport: Recife Guararapes (REC) sits 11 km from Boa Viagem beach, and the "Special Taxi" kiosks quote R\$ 120–R\$ 300 to Boa Viagem when Uber is R\$ 25–R\$ 45. Second — and this is the section to read twice before you swim — **Praia de Boa Viagem has one of the world's most documented shark-attack risks**. CEMIT (Comitê Estadual de Monitoramento de Incidentes com Tubarões — the Pernambuco state shark-attack monitoring committee) has logged 60+ attacks and 25+ fatalities at Boa Viagem since 1992; the cause is a combination of the Suape Port construction in the 1990s disrupting bull-shark migratory routes along the reef channel and an active tiger-shark population in the offshore reef system. The broader Pernambuco metro region remains active — in January 2026, 13-year-old Deivson Rocha Dantas was fatally attacked at Praia Del Chifre (Olinda / Paulista side, 20 km north of Boa Viagem). Red-and-yellow warning signs are posted at all Boa Viagem beach access points. They are not decoration. Swim only in the natural tide-pool zone inside the reef at low tide, or — far better — travel to Porto de Galinhas or Praia do Paiva for ocean swimming.

Third, Recife Antigo (Marco Zero, Casa da Cultura, Rua Bom Jesus) is the safe daytime Centro Histórico, patrolled by tourist police; after dark, pickpocket + phone-snatch risk rises. Fourth, the Olinda day-trip is the signature side-trip, and it runs an active "mandatory guide" pressure scam on Alto da Sé. Fifth, Boa Viagem and Recife Antigo dining host standard Brazilian couvert + serviço padding plus a "*frutos do mar preço de mercado*" seafood trap. Sixth, Carnaval in Recife and Olinda is Brazil's most authentic frevo + maracatu street celebration, with the Galo da Madrugada bloco holding the Guinness World Record as the largest Carnaval bloco globally — and a corresponding counterfeit-ticket + off-platform booking-fraud ecosystem.

Recife violent-crime incidence is higher than the coastal Southeast but lower than Salvador for tourists in the Boa Viagem / Recife Antigo / Olinda corridor. DEATUR Pernambuco is at Rua Aurora, 1028.
