# The Scams: City by City {-}

The chapters that follow document every scam reported in twelve Brazilian destinations. Cities are ordered by how most visitors actually travel: starting with Rio de Janeiro, the flagship destination and densest scam ecosystem, then out to the beach-peninsula of Búzios and the colonial port of Paraty on the Costa Verde; north to São Paulo and down to the island capital of Florianópolis; up to the northeast coast through Salvador (the first colonial capital), Recife (with its Boa Viagem shark-attack zone), and Fortaleza; into the Amazon at Manaus; west to the Iguaçu Falls tripoint at Foz do Iguaçu; and finishing in the interior with the modernist capital of Brasília and the UNESCO colonial gold-rush town of Ouro Preto in Minas Gerais.

If you're hitting only a few of these, skip to the chapter you need. If you're doing a longer loop, skim them all — the same core scams recur with local variations. The "Special Taxi" kiosk mafia at Rio's Galeão (GIG) runs identically at São Paulo's Guarulhos (GRU), Salvador (SSA), Recife (REC), Fortaleza (FOR), Manaus (MAO), Foz do Iguaçu (IGU), Brasília (BSB), and Florianópolis (FLN). The aluguel-de-temporada cancel-and-relist pattern runs at Rio, Búzios, Paraty, Florianópolis, Fortaleza, Brasília, and Ouro Preto Carnaval. The couvert artístico + serviço padding runs in every Brazilian restaurant chapter in this book. The unlicensed boat-tour operator problem threads through Búzios, Paraty, Salvador (Morro de São Paulo), Florianópolis (Ilha do Campeche), and Manaus (Encontro das Águas). Learn the pattern in one city, and you'll spot it the instant you arrive in the next.

## The Twelve Cities at a Glance {-}

![Rio de Janeiro — Sugarloaf silhouette and Copacabana crescent at sunset](assets/cities/rio-de-janeiro.jpg)

**Rio de Janeiro** — The flagship. The GIG airport "Special Taxi" mafia that quotes R\$ 250–R\$ 400 to Ipanema when Uber is R\$ 90–R\$ 140; the Copacabana and Ipanema beach *arrastão* (coordinated sweep theft); the Lapa / Copacabana R\$ 10,000 caipirinha-bar honeypot that traps tourists with "my friend, Rio's best caipirinha"; the Christ the Redeemer and Sugarloaf "skip-the-line VIP" tour-operator fraud; the Rocinha / Vidigal unethical favela tour with fake "community fund" pitches; and the Carnaval + Réveillon Booking.com / Airbnb cancel-and-relist cycle.

![Búzios — João Fernandes bay arc and Rua das Pedras cobblestones](assets/cities/buzios.jpg)

**Búzios** — Rio's coastal weekend getaway, 175 km east, a 2.5-hour drive. The Rio-to-Búzios private-transfer overcharge (R\$ 1,200+ for a trip the 1001 bus does for R\$ 60); the beach-hopping schooner tour with unlicensed boats and no life jackets; the Rua das Pedras tourist-menu + couvert artístico bill-padding; the João Fernandes / Geribá "VIP beach bed" R\$ 1,500 upcharge on beaches that are 100% free public federal property; the pousada Carnaval + NYE cancel-and-relist at 4–8× normal rates; and the Arraial do Cabo + Cabo Frio combined day-trip commission scam.

![Paraty — UNESCO cobblestones and schooner masts in the bay](assets/cities/paraty.jpg)

**Paraty** — UNESCO colonial port on the Costa Verde, 250 km southwest of Rio. The Rio / São Paulo-to-Paraty bus + private-transfer overcharge on the 250-curve BR-101 highway; the Paraty Bay schooner tour overcharge with unsafe operators; the Centro Histórico restaurant tourist-menu + couvert padding; the cachaça distillery "premium tasting" commission scam (real R\$ 30–R\$ 80 per 500ml artisanal, tourist-shop R\$ 150+); the Trindade + Angra + Ilha Grande day-trip commission stops; and the pousada Carnaval + FLIP literary-festival cancel-and-relist.

![São Paulo — Avenida Paulista spine and MASP silhouette at dusk](assets/cities/sao-paulo.jpg)

**São Paulo** — Brazil's economic capital and 12-million-resident metropolis. The GRU airport "Special Taxi" mafia at R\$ 250–R\$ 500 to Paulista; the Avenida Paulista "clowns" distraction-pickpocket ring documented across dozens of r/saopaulo reports; the "old lady blood-pressure" distraction-theft pattern; the Centro Histórico (Sé / Anhangabaú / Luz / República) phone-snatch corridor; the abusive restaurant couvert artístico + serviço padding documented by r/saopaulo "Abusive practices of restaurants and bars"; and the Uber / 99 off-app cancel-and-recontract cash scam at GRU and CGH.

![Florianópolis — Lagoa da Conceição and Ponte Hercílio Luz at twilight](assets/cities/florianopolis.jpg)

**Florianópolis** — Ilha de Santa Catarina. The FLN airport "Special Taxi" + beach-transfer overcharge; the aluguel-de-temporada / Airbnb high-season (Dec–Mar + Réveillon) cancel-and-relist fraud; the "Mega Model" beach-recruit scam targeting young female tourists at Praia Mole / Jurerê with a trafficking-template modus operandi documented by r/florianopolis; the Praia Mole + Jurerê beach-club "VIP couples bed R\$ 1,500" upcharge; the Lagoa da Conceição restaurant couvert + serviço padding; and the north-island schooner / Ilha do Campeche boat-operator scam.

![Salvador — Pelourinho pastel facades and Elevador Lacerda](assets/cities/salvador.jpg)

**Salvador** — Bahia's capital, Brazil's first colonial capital. The SSA airport taxi mafia + late-night Uber supply gap; the Pelourinho *fita do Senhor do Bonfim* ribbon-tying forced-tip scam; the Pelourinho / Centro Histórico pickpocket + phone-snatch ring; the Morro de São Paulo / Tinharé schooner day-trip overcharge (private R\$ 800 versus real catamarã R\$ 130–R\$ 220 round trip); the Pelourinho / Rio Vermelho restaurant couvert + serviço padding; and the Carnaval camarote / trio elétrico counterfeit-ticket + booking fraud.

![Recife — Marco Zero and Instituto Ricardo Brennand silhouette](assets/cities/recife.jpg)

**Recife** — Pernambuco's capital. The REC airport taxi + Boa Viagem transfer overcharge; the **Boa Viagem beach shark-attack risk** (the beach sits on one of the world's most documented shark-attack coastlines; CEMIT monitoring data; 2026 fatal incident at Praia Del Chifre nearby); the Recife Antigo / Marco Zero pickpocket + phone-snatch ring; the Olinda day-trip "mandatory guide" scam and tour-operator commission stops; the Boa Viagem restaurant couvert + serviço + "*frutos do mar preço de mercado*" seafood trap; and the Carnaval frevo + Galo da Madrugada counterfeit-ticket booking fraud.

![Fortaleza — Beira Mar arc and Pedra do Meireles silhouette](assets/cities/fortaleza.jpg)

**Fortaleza** — Ceará's capital, 2.7 million residents. The FOR airport taxi mafia + Uber pickup-zone confusion; the Terminal da Parangaba walkway distraction-pickpocket ring (the canonical 2025 r/Fortaleza "I fell for a scam on the Parangaba terminal walkway" anchor); the Beira Mar / Praia de Iracema motorbike phone-snatch ring; the Jericoacoara + Canoa Quebrada buggy day-trip overcharge + commission scam; the Beira Mar + Praia do Futuro restaurant couvert + serviço + "*preço de mercado*" seafood trap; and the Réveillon (Aterro da Praia de Iracema) + Fortal Carnaval booking fraud.

![Manaus — Teatro Amazonas dome and Rio Negro confluence](assets/cities/manaus.jpg)

**Manaus** — gateway to the Brazilian Amazon, 2.1 million residents. The MAO airport taxi + late-night transfer overcharge; the Amazon jungle-lodge booking fraud + commission-stop day-trip scam (the canonical 2025 r/Brazil "How do I tell which Manaus Amazon tour is legit?" anchor); the Encontro das Águas boat-tour overcharge + unlicensed-operator safety risk (with the 2025 IBAMA pink-dolphin contact ban and Marinha do Brasil vessel-registration requirement); the Centro Histórico + Mercado Adolpho Lisboa pickpocket + phone-snatch ring; the Ponta Negra + Centro restaurant couvert + serviço + "*peixe do dia preço de mercado*" trap; and the Amazonas regional Uber + mototaxi coordinated scam pattern (Santarém / Alto do Chão template).

![Foz do Iguaçu — Falls panorama and Ponte da Amizade silhouette](assets/cities/foz-do-iguacu.jpg)

**Foz do Iguaçu** — tripoint between Brazil, Argentina, and Paraguay. The IGU airport taxi + late-night transfer overcharge; the Parque Nacional do Iguaçu tour commission + "skip-the-line" fraud at the park entrance; the Ciudad del Este day-trip pickpocket + counterfeit-goods risk (crossing the Ponte da Amizade into Paraguay); the Itaipu Dam tour booking fraud + commission-markup hotel packages; the Foz restaurant couvert + serviço + "*churrascaria rodízio*" price-inflation pattern; and the Argentine-side crossing (Puerto Iguazú) with border-transfer confusion and the "Argentine reciprocity fee" demand (abolished since 2016 for US / EU / Canadian citizens).

![Brasília — Congresso Nacional domes and Esplanada dos Ministérios](assets/cities/brasilia.jpg)

**Brasília** — Brazil's purpose-built modernist capital since 1960. The BSB airport taxi + late-night transfer overcharge; the Esplanada dos Ministérios + Congresso Nacional pickpocket + "official tour guide" scam (the real tour is FREE at *tours.congresso.leg.br*); the restaurant couvert + serviço + "*comida por kg*" INMETRO-scale weighing trap; the aluguel-de-temporada + hotel booking fraud for peak dates (Independência Sep 7; Réveillon; presidential inaugurations every 4 years); the Lago Paranoá + Pontão pickpocket + phone-snatch ring; and the Uber coordinated scam + cross-superquadra route-padding in the Plano Piloto grid.

![Ouro Preto — Igreja São Francisco de Assis and cobblestone staircase](assets/cities/ouro-preto.jpg)

**Ouro Preto** — Minas Gerais, Brazil's first UNESCO heritage site (declared 1980). The Belo Horizonte Confins (CNF) airport-to-Ouro Preto transfer overcharge on the 130-km BR-040 run; the Centro Histórico "mandatory guide" + church-entry "skip-the-line" fraud; the soapstone (pedra-sabão) + emerald / topaz gemstone tourist-shop overcharge + counterfeit-certificate risk (IBGM / GIA / IGI certification authorities); the Carnaval *repúblicas estudantis* (UFOP student-house) booking fraud + peak-date accommodation scams; the restaurant couvert + serviço + "*comida mineira*" buffet weighing trap; and the Mariana day-trip + Minas Gerais colonial-circuit commission scam.
