Manaus — the capital of Amazonas, 2.1 million residents, the gateway to the Brazilian Amazon — is unlike any other Brazilian city in this book. Founded in 1669 and enriched by the late-19th-century rubber boom, Manaus sits 1,500 km from the Atlantic up the Amazon river system, 3 degrees south of the equator, on the north bank of the Rio Negro. For international visitors, Manaus is a staging city: the primary mission is the Amazon jungle-lodge or river-cruise trip that begins and ends here, with one or two nights in the city bracketing the lodge days.

The scam surface area reflects this staging-city dynamic. The city-level scams are a city-level problem, but the lodge-booking scams are where the serious money is at risk — five-figure US-dollar packages sold through Instagram and WhatsApp that don't exist.

First, the airport: Eduardo Gomes (MAO) sits 14 km north of Centro Histórico and 11 km north of Ponta Negra (the main tourist district), and the "Special Taxi" kiosks quote R\$ 150–R\$ 280 when Uber is R\$ 45–R\$ 75. Late-night Uber supply thins after 23:00 — MAO has many international connections arriving after midnight — and unlicensed mototaxis fill the gap. Second, the **Amazon jungle-lodge booking fraud** is Manaus's signature scam: airport tour-desks, hotel lobbies, and Instagram / WhatsApp "operators" sell "exclusive 3-night packages R\$ 8,500" for lodges the standard direct-booking price is R\$ 1,800–R\$ 3,500. Reputable lodges — Juma Amazon Lodge, Anavilhanas Jungle Lodge, Uacari Lodge, Amazon Eco Park Lodge, Tariri Amazon Lodge — all have direct websites with verified Marinha do Brasil vessel registration and FUNAI compliance. Book only through those. Pay by credit card, never PIX — PIX is irreversible and the Instagram-operator pattern is 100% PIX-dependent. The AMAZONASTUR (state tourism board) verification hotline at **+55 92 2123 8800** can verify any operator before booking.

Third, Encontro das Águas — the confluence 10 km downstream where the black Rio Negro meets the café-au-lait Rio Solimões to form the Amazon proper — is the flagship half-day boat excursion. Reputable group operators (Gero Tours, Viverde, Amazonastur) run it at R\$ 250–R\$ 400 per person including Janauari Ecological Park + piranha fishing + lunch. Unlicensed speedboats without life jackets at Ponta Negra pier run the alternative at R\$ 800 private. And — critically — the 2025 IBAMA decree banned all tourist pink-dolphin contact experiences: reputable operators decline "pink-dolphin swim R\$ 300 extra" offers. If a Manaus operator sells it, they are operating outside IBAMA regulation.

Fourth, the Centro Histórico (Teatro Amazonas, Largo de São Sebastião, Mercado Adolpho Lisboa, Porto de Manaus) is the mandatory half-day tourist walk and hosts a 2025 pickpocket + phone-snatch ring. Teatro Amazonas guided tours are R\$ 20 at the interior ticket window — refuse every R\$ 80 "guide" on the exterior steps. Fifth, the Ponta Negra waterfront and Centro dining run the standard couvert + serviço + "*peixe do dia preço de mercado*" pattern, with a distinctive Amazonian fish menu (tambaqui, pirarucu, tucunaré, matrinxã). And sixth, the **Amazonas regional Uber coordinated-scam pattern** — documented in r/Brazil "Uber in Santarém and Alto do Chão – Coordinated Scam" (2025) — applies to any side-trip into Pará or remote Amazonas.

Manaus violent-crime incidence is the highest of any city in this book. Stay in Ponta Negra (safest 5-star zone), use Uber after dark, avoid the waterfront after 20:00. DEATUR Manaus is at **+55 92 3584 1373**.
