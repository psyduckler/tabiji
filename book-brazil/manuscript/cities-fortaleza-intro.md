Fortaleza — the capital of Ceará, 2.7 million residents, Brazil's fifth-largest city — is the Northeast beach-vacation capital for Brazilian domestic travelers and increasingly for international visitors. Most itineraries base out of the Beira Mar / Meireles beachfront corridor and use Fortaleza as a staging point for three iconic day-or-overnight trips: Jericoacoara (300 km NW, Brazil's most famous windswept beach-dune village), Canoa Quebrada (160 km SE, cliff-top beach + buggy country), and Cumbuco (40 km NW, kitesurfing mecca).

The scam surface area has a specific structure shaped by Fortaleza's geography. First, Pinto Martins airport (FOR) is only 6 km south of Centro and 10 km south of Beira Mar — closer than most Brazilian capital-city airports to the tourist zone — and the "Special Taxi" kiosks quote R\$ 100–R\$ 250 when Uber is R\$ 25–R\$ 45. Second, Fortaleza has one extraordinary urban-pickpocket hot-spot: the **Terminal da Parangaba walkway**, a major bus + metro interchange in northern Fortaleza whose elevated pedestrian connector is a documented 2025 distraction-pickpocket chokepoint. r/Fortaleza "I fell for a scam on the Parangaba terminal walkway" (2025) is the canonical first-person anchor. Most visitors will not go to Parangaba — it is a local commuter hub, not a tourist attraction — but if you plan to use the metro, know it.

Third, the Av. Beira Mar orla (3-km beachfront promenade from Praia do Mucuripe through Meireles to Praia de Iracema) is the primary tourist corridor and hosts a motorbike phone-snatch ring that targets tourists holding phones for sunset photos. Fourth, the Jericoacoara + Canoa Quebrada day-trip market runs a buggy + commission-stop scam, with hotel concierges quoting "VIP private buggy day-trips R\$ 1,200" for packages the group-bus operators (Fretur, Litoral Tours, Tour Shop) do at R\$ 120–R\$ 180. Fifth, the Beira Mar restaurant strip and the Praia do Futuro barraca (beach-kiosk) corridor run the standard Brazilian couvert + serviço padding plus a "*preço de mercado*" seafood trap (tambaqui, pirarucu, tucunaré, lagosta). Reputable barracas at Praia do Futuro: Chico do Caranguejo (iconic for Wednesday crab nights), Itapariká, Crocobeach.

Sixth, Fortaleza hosts two mega-events that drive nationwide booking fraud: **Réveillon** on Aterro da Praia de Iracema (2+ million attendees for the NYE fireworks) and **Fortal Carnaval** in late July (Brazil's largest out-of-season Carnaval, 4 nights at Cidade Fortal stadium, 20 km south of Centro).

Fortaleza's violent-crime incidence is higher than Southeast tourist cities but lower than Salvador for visitors in the Beira Mar / Meireles corridor with Uber after dark. DEATUR Ceará is at Av. Beira Mar, 3001.
