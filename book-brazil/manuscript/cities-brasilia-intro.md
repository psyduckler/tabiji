Brasília — Brazil's purpose-built modernist capital since 1960, Lúcio Costa's planned city inscribed on the UNESCO World Heritage list — is unlike any other Brazilian city in this book. It is a capital built from scratch in four years on the central high plateau, designed at scale for the automobile, organized into numbered "superquadras" rather than a traditional city grid, and anchored by the Eixo Monumental (the ceremonial east–west axis) culminating in Oscar Niemeyer's Praça dos Três Poderes.

For international visitors, Brasília is an architecture-and-politics stopover — typically two or three nights — with a compact sightseeing set: the Esplanada dos Ministérios, the Congresso Nacional, the Palácio do Planalto, the Palácio do Itamaraty (foreign ministry), the Catedral Metropolitana, the Memorial JK, the Pontão do Lago Sul at sunset, and the Brasília Palace Hotel (the Niemeyer original from 1960) as a reference site.

The scam surface area in Brasília is narrower than in other Brazilian capitals — the city has the country's highest-income demographic and a transient political class rather than a permanent tourism economy — but the scams that exist are distinctive.

First, the airport: Juscelino Kubitschek (BSB) sits 12 km SW of the Plano Piloto and the "Special Taxi" / "Taxi Executivo" kiosks quote R\$ 200–R\$ 350 to Asa Sul when Uber is R\$ 40–R\$ 65. Second, the Esplanada dos Ministérios runs two distinctive scam patterns: the "official tour guide R\$ 80–R\$ 150" offers on Praça dos Três Poderes (the real Congresso Nacional guided tour is FREE, bookable at *tours.congresso.leg.br* 1–5 days ahead; Palácio do Planalto is FREE Sunday mornings 9:30 AM–1:30 PM at *planalto.gov.br*), and a phone-snatch ring that targets tourists taking photos of the twin ministry buildings.

Third, Brasília's dining is dominated by weekday "*comida por kg*" (pay-by-weight) buffets — a uniquely Brasiliense format that runs an INMETRO-scale weigh trap: tourist-zone buffets in Setor Comercial Sul charge R\$ 110–R\$ 180 per kg for what legitimate Asa Sul buffets (Universal Diner, Zuu aA, Buffet Estoril) price at R\$ 70–R\$ 110 per kg, often on uncertified scales that pad weight 10–25%. Verify the INMETRO 2025/2026 calibration sticker on the scale before plating.

Fourth, the Brasília aluguel-de-temporada / Airbnb + hotel booking-fraud market has three peak-demand windows: Independência (September 7 military parade on the Esplanada), Réveillon (Pontão Lago Sul fireworks), and presidential-inauguration dates every four years. Fifth, the Pontão do Lago Sul waterfront promenade (Brasília's default evening tourist destination, 3 km from Plano Piloto across the Ponte JK) hosts a phone-snatch ring targeting sunset photography. Sixth, Brasília's Plano Piloto superquadra grid is uniquely confusing to non-residents, and 2025 Uber drivers exploit this via cross-superquadra route-padding (e.g., Asa Sul 108 to Asa Sul 308 via Eixo Rodoviário + W3 Sul instead of the direct L2 Sul).
