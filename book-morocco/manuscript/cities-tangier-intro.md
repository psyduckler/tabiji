Tangier is Morocco's gateway from Spain — the ferry-port arrivals from Algeciras and Tarifa, the high-speed Al Boraq train south, and Ibn Battuta Airport (TNG) on the city's southwest edge. The scam ecosystem here is shaped by that arrivals geography: most of the volume is concentrated in the first hour of a tourist's visit, with the ferry port and the airport as the highest-density nodes, and the Petit Socco / Grand Socco medina as the second wave.

The risk concentrates at four nodes. **Tangier Ville ferry port** and **Tangier-Med ferry port** (40 km east on the coast) taxi ranks and the curb just outside. **Ibn Battuta Airport (TNG)** taxi rank. The **Petit Socco** and **Grand Socco** medina entrances, where the faux-guide ecosystem inherited from the international-zone era still operates. And the medina's **Rue es-Siaghin** and **Rue Caza Bel'Beis** restaurant strips, where the no-menu price trap runs hardest.

The **Medina Fake Guide Ambush** is the city's volume risk. Within ten minutes of stepping out of the ferry terminal or the train station, a friendly man speaking decent English will say his cousin owns a riad / his brother knows the best leather shop / he can show you the way through the medina to the kasbah. Twenty turns later you are in a carpet showroom with mint tea on a tray and a stack of rugs being unrolled, and the *"guide"* is on commission. The defense is to refuse all walked-with help in the first five hundred meters from the ferry exit, ignore openings about your hotel, and walk straight to your accommodation with offline maps.

The risk radiates out in three corridors. The **Taxi Meter Manipulation at the Ferry Port** quotes 200–500 MAD into the medina (the metered fare is 30–50 MAD from Tangier Ville, or 250–350 from Tangier-Med). The **Counterfeit Currency Exchange** runs at the curb outside the ferry exit and on the informal-exchange tables near the Grand Socco — old or out-of-circulation Moroccan dirhams are pressed in among real notes, and the rate is 15–25% below market. The **Forced Henna Tattoo Hustle** runs at Petit Socco's seating areas and the alleys near the Kasbah Museum with the same wrist-grab script as Marrakech. The **Fake Argan Oil and Spice Shop Swindle** in the Berber-pharmacy concentration on Rue es-Siaghin runs the diluted-product / euro-pricing combination. The **Restaurant No-Menu Price Trap** near the port market runs 800–1,500 MAD for two on dishes that should be a third of that.

A safe default: at Tangier Ville ferry port, walk past the touts directly to the official taxi rank and demand the meter; at Tangier-Med, the **CTM intercity bus** to Tangier center is 25 MAD and the most reliable transfer. Exchange currency only at the in-city Attijariwafa Bank or BMCE branches, never at curb-side stalls or the airport's Global Exchange. Ignore everyone who opens with *"my friend, where are you going?"* in the first hour after arrival. The Brigade Touristique desk shares the central commissariat at **Place de France** in the Ville Nouvelle near the Café de Paris.
