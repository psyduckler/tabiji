Chefchaouen is the blue mountain town two hours south of Tangier in the Rif Mountains. It is small — a medina you can walk in under an hour — and most visitors come for the Instagram-friendly indigo-painted alleys and the mountain hike to the Spanish Mosque viewpoint. The scam ecosystem here is small in absolute terms but contains the single most well-known Moroccan scam in the country, and the geography (the Rif is the country's cannabis-growing region) shapes it.

The risk concentrates at three nodes. The **medina alleys around Plaza Uta el-Hammam**, especially after dark. **Bab al-Ain**, the main medina entrance from the Ville Nouvelle. And the path up to the **Spanish Mosque**, the sunset-photo destination on the hill east of town.

The **Hash Tout Police Shakedown** is the volume risk and the city's signature scam, well documented since at least 2018 on r/Morocco and warned about directly by the US State Department and the British Foreign Office. A young man on a medina alley offers cannabis with a friendly *"you want hash, my friend?"* and a small pre-rolled chunk to inspect. Within two to ten minutes — sometimes immediately — a man in a short jacket steps out and identifies himself as a police officer. He may flash a real-looking ID. He says cannabis is illegal in Morocco (which it is) and that you are going to the station unless you settle here. The opening number is 2,000 MAD ($200); the closing number depends on what is in your wallet. The defense is preemptive: decline every street offer with *"la, shukran"* and walk on. If you do get stopped on the street and someone demands cash, ask firmly to be taken to the central Sûreté Nationale station on **Avenue Hassan II** — real officers will take you, fake ones will disappear.

The risk radiates out in three corridors. The **Tangier–Chefchaouen Grand Taxi Overcharge** at the Tangier and Tetouan bus stations and at the Chefchaouen Bab al-Ain taxi rank quotes 700 MAD per person against the 70-MAD shared per-seat rate, or alternatively pushes the *"private taxi"* price up. The **Medina Guide Maze Trap** at Bab al-Ain walks tourists through deliberately confusing blue alleys and demands 100–200 MAD at the destination. The **Plaza Uta el-Hammam Cafe Overcharge** runs 400 MAD for two on dishes that cost 80–120 MAD uphill or one street back. The **Henna Tattoo Ambush** runs the Marrakech wrist-grab script on Plaza Uta el-Hammam and at Bab al-Ain at 100–200 MAD. The **Spanish Mosque Sunset Tip Demand** has teenagers on the path claiming the route is dangerous and offering unrequested help for 50–100 MAD; children pose for selfies and demand 20–50 MAD afterward.

A safe default: arrive by **CTM bus** from Tangier (50–80 MAD, scheduled, no negotiation) rather than by grand taxi. Decline every street offer of cannabis without engaging — possession is illegal regardless of the Rif's growing reputation, and the legal exposure is what makes the fake-cop threat work. Demand a printed menu before sitting at any Plaza Uta el-Hammam terrace. Hike to the Spanish Mosque before sunset and walk independently, declining all offered "help." The central police station is on **Avenue Hassan II** about 200 meters west of Plaza Uta el-Hammam in the Ville Nouvelle.
