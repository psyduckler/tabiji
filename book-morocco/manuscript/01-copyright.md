**This book is for informational purposes only.** It is not legal, financial, medical, or insurance advice. Scam patterns evolve, legal protections vary by country of travel, and venue-specific details may change between editions.

References to streets, *souks*, medina alleys, neighborhoods, and business types describe patterns of scam activity reported by Moroccan newspapers, the Brigade Touristique, the Sûreté Nationale, US and UK embassy advisories, and travelers at the time of writing. Mention of a specific area does not imply that all venues or individuals operating there engage in the described behavior. Where this book references named operators or establishments, it does so on the basis of published news coverage or direct consumer reviews; any such reference is intended as consumer information and not as an accusation of wrongdoing against any individual business that was not the subject of a specific cited report.

Information is current as of **2026 Edition**. For newly reported scams and live updates, visit **[tabiji.ai/scams](https://tabiji.ai/scams)**.

If you are victimized, consult local authorities (dial **19** for the Sûreté Nationale in any Moroccan city — the dispatcher will route you to the nearest police station; **177** reaches the Gendarmerie Royale on highways and in rural areas; **15** is the SAMU medical emergency line; the Brigade Touristique operates dedicated tourist-police desks in Marrakech, Casablanca, Fez, Tangier, Agadir, and Rabat), your embassy or consulate, your credit-card issuer, and your travel-insurance provider. Do not rely on this book as your sole source of guidance.

A note on names. This book uses the English-language conventions for Moroccan place names — Marrakech (not Marrakesh), Fez (not Fès), Tangier (not Tanger), Casablanca (also called Dar el-Beïda in Arabic). Street names follow the form most commonly used by the Brigade Touristique and major Moroccan newspapers, generally French (Avenue Mohammed V) with the Arabic original given on first mention where it materially helps a reader find the place.
