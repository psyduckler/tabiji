Ouarzazate is the gateway to the Aït Benhaddou ksar and the southern desert circuit — four hours from Marrakech over the Tizi n'Tichka pass through the High Atlas. It is also Morocco's film-industry hub, home to Atlas Studios where everything from *Game of Thrones* to *Gladiator* was filmed. The scam ecosystem here is moderate, concentrated almost entirely on Aït Benhaddou itself, the Atlas Studios tour, and the desert-tour booking step that overlaps with Merzouga's.

The risk concentrates at four nodes. The **Aït Benhaddou** parking-area approach, where the fake-entry-fee operator works the gate. The **Atlas Studios** entrance and tour, with its guide-tip pressure and pottery-cooperative upsell. **Berber villages** along the desert-tour route between Ouarzazate and Erfoud, where the carpet-shop detour is built in. And the **Ouarzazate tour-agency strip** along Avenue Mohammed V, where unverified operators sell the same Marrakech-to-Merzouga bait-and-switch tour from a different starting point.

The **Desert Tour Bait-and-Switch** is the volume risk. Tour agencies along Avenue Mohammed V and online booking platforms based in Ouarzazate sell 2-to-4-day Sahara packages with photographs of luxury tents, private 4×4s, and exclusive sunrise-camel routes. What arrives is the same basic-tent shared minivan tour as the Marrakech-originating version, often the same operator running it under a different name. The defense is identical to Merzouga's: book only through operators with a verifiable office and recent independent reviews (Tripadvisor, Get Your Guide), and pay by credit card with chargeback rights.

The risk radiates out in three corridors. The **Aït Benhaddou Fake Entry Fee** is the most-reported single Ouarzazate scam: a man at the parking-area approach to the ksar collects 20–50 MAD as an "entry fee" before the (free) crossing of the riverbed. Aït Benhaddou has no entry fee — the village is open access; the Kasbah Caïd Ali inside it has a small admission charge but it is collected at the kasbah door, not the riverbed. The **Carpet Shop Detour** on the Ouarzazate-Merzouga and Ouarzazate-Aït Benhaddou desert routes is built into many guided tours — the driver receives commission whether you buy or not. The **Fake Fossil Racket** runs on the roadside stalls between Ouarzazate and Erfoud at museum prices for cement casts. The **Self-Appointed Kasbah Guide** at the Taourirt Kasbah and the El Glaoui residence rooms offers a *"guided tour"* for 100–200 MAD on top of the official admission. The **Atlas Studios Upsell** has guides who add a pottery-cooperative or carpet-cooperative stop to the official tour itinerary with 100–300 MAD pressure-sales pitches.

A safe default: walk past anyone collecting *"entry fees"* at the Aït Benhaddou riverbed approach (the only legitimate paid attraction inside the ksar is the Kasbah Caïd Ali, and that ticket is sold at the kasbah door); book Atlas Studios tours through the official ticket office only; refuse driver-suggested carpet-cooperative detours; and book desert tours only through operators with a published office and visible reviews. The central Sûreté Nationale commissariat is on **Avenue Mohammed V** in the Ville Nouvelle; **Hôpital Bouafer Ouarzazate** is the public hospital on the same avenue.
