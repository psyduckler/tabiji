Dahab is the Sinai's dive-tourism town — a low-rise bedouin coastal village on the Gulf of Aqaba, an hour and a half north of Sharm El Sheikh, oriented almost entirely around the Blue Hole, the Canyon, and Bells. The scam profile reflects that geography. Six documented hustles, two rated high-severity, and the headline risk is structural rather than commercial. The Blue Hole Dive-Op Safety Cuts run on the Mashraba strip at $25 for an intro dive — half what Reef 2000, Big Blue, or H2O charge — with regulators that taste metallic, BCDs that won't hold air, and "instructors" who never show a PADI card. The Blue Hole has a documented death toll measured in the hundreds; most fatalities trace to operators who rented narcosis-prone divers tanks for the deep Arch pass-through with no chamber nearby. The Sharm Airport to Dahab Taxi Quote Switch lands a $70 dollar quote on a 90-minute transfer that should run 1,500 to 1,800 EGP through a hotel pickup, with "checkpoint fees" added en route to push the curbside drop past $80.

The risk is concentrated on the Mashraba strip between Lighthouse beach and the GO Bus station — every dive-shop equipment-cut, every camel-ride ransom on the promenade, every Bedouin tea-camp bill inflation runs on this corridor. The Mashraba and Lighthouse beachfront tents pull tourists in for "free tea" at sunset, then unsolicited dates, hookah, and a fish platter land on the low table; the bill runs 800 to 1,200 EGP for hospitality framed as a cultural experience. The Mashraba bungalow strip runs the hidden-charge trap: 200 EGP per night with "everything included" becomes 1,400 over three nights once the towel, AC, water, and service fees materialize. Assalah Square money changers run the short-count — 18 hundred-pound notes counted into your hand at high speed against $200, when 20 were promised.

A safe default for first-timers: dive only with Reef 2000, Big Blue, or H2O — Dahab's three established operators with chamber-evacuation protocols — and pre-book your Sharm-to-Dahab transfer through your hotel before you fly.
