Hurghada's scam profile is the all-inclusive resort culture turned into a hustle index: seven documented scams, three rated high-severity, almost all of them keyed to the gap between the resort wristband and the El Mamsha promenade outside the gate. The Airport Visa Counter Scam intercepts you in the 90-second corridor between Hurghada passport control and baggage claim — a man in a navy polo with a printed Coral Travel or Alltours lanyard takes $35 cash for a visa that costs $25 at the official bank window. The Resort Doctor Overcharging routine diagnoses a £1,000-to-£1,500 condition (roughly $1,250 to $1,900) from a routine sunburn or stomach bug and dispenses medication that, per NHS reports, often does not match the actual ailment. The All-Inclusive Wristband Shop Scam turns a publicly visible band color into rapport: the shopkeeper greets you by your hotel's name, claims to work at reception, and offers a "guest-only" deal that bypasses the front desk entirely.

The risk is concentrated along El Mamsha promenade (Sheraton Road) and through the Sekalla tourist strip — the wristband-recognition pattern, the camel and horse ride ransom on the beach, the perfume and papyrus factory tours all run on this corridor. Hurghada Marina carries the Snorkeling Boat Trip Bait and Switch: a $20 "luxury yacht" pitch that delivers an overcrowded fishing vessel with no life jackets and a cascade of dock-side extras. El Dahar's older market stalls run the currency-confusion swap — counterfeit British pound notes, Moroccan dirham coins slipped in as one-pound coins, the 50 piastre and 50 EGP notes exploited for short change. The on-site resort doctor sits inside the resort perimeter; the conflict of interest is structural — he pays the hotel a commission for access to your room.

A safe default for first-timers: pay the visa in exact $25 cash at the official bank counter window before passport control, buy comprehensive travel insurance with direct-billing partners before your trip, and call your real hotel reception from the room phone before agreeing to any "guest-only" shop deal outside the gate.
