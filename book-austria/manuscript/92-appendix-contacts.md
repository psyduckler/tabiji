# Appendix C: Emergency Contacts {-}

Every phone number in this appendix was verified current at the time of publication. Phone numbers do change — if a number below fails to connect, search the organization's name plus *Telefon* and you will almost always find the current line on the first page of results.

**Before you travel**, we strongly recommend you double-check your own embassy or consulate's current number directly on its official website (the US State Department's travel.state.gov, the UK's gov.uk, Canada's travel.gc.ca, Australia's smartraveller.gov.au, etc.). Save the number and the after-hours emergency line on your phone before departure. Even a verified number can become a wrong number between editions.

## National (anywhere in Austria)

- **European emergency number (all services):** **112**. Works from any phone in Austria, including a locked mobile with no SIM, and connects you to police, ambulance, or fire. English-speaking operators are available. State your location as a landmark — "Stephansplatz," "Hauptplatz Graz," "Hallstatt Marktplatz" — rather than a street address that is hard to pronounce.
- **Police (Polizei):** **133**.
- **Ambulance (Rettung):** **144**.
- **Fire brigade (Feuerwehr):** **122**.
- **Mountain rescue (Bergrettung):** **140**. Relevant for the alpine areas in this book — Zell am See, Bad Gastein, Innsbruck, the Salzkammergut around Hallstatt.
- **Euro-wide medical advice / health hotline:** **1450** (Austria's nationwide non-emergency health-advice line; some English support).

## Online crime reporting

- **Austrian police online reporting (Online-Anzeige):** via the Federal Ministry of the Interior at **[bmi.gv.at](https://www.bmi.gv.at)**. You can file an initial report for theft or fraud, but for anything you need documented quickly (for a chargeback or a flight the same day), going to a *Polizeiinspektion* in person is usually faster.

## Consumer protection (for overcharging, DCC, rental, and taxi disputes)

- **VKI — Verein für Konsumenteninformation:** **[konsument.at](https://www.konsument.at)**. Austria's main consumer-information body; takes complaints and publishes warnings.
- **Arbeiterkammer (AK) consumer advice:** **[arbeiterkammer.at](https://www.arbeiterkammer.at)**. Each province (Bundesland) runs its own AK consumer-advice line.
- **European Consumer Centre Austria (Europäisches Verbraucherzentrum):** **[europakonsument.at](https://europakonsument.at)**. For disputes with businesses based in another EU country.

## Card issuer emergency lines (callable from Austria)

When you call, have your card number (or the last four digits), the approximate transaction amount, and the time of the fraudulent charge ready.

- **Visa Global Customer Assistance:** +1-303-967-1096 (call collect from Austria). 24/7, English.
- **Mastercard Global Service (Austria toll-free):** 0800-21-8235. 24/7. International collect: +1-636-722-7111.
- **American Express (international, call collect):** +1-336-393-1111. 24/7.

If your card was issued by a bank (not directly by Visa / Mastercard / Amex), the fastest route is to call the number printed on the back of your card. That line routes you to your own issuer's fraud desk, and they can usually freeze the card in under a minute. This is also the route to dispute a Dynamic Currency Conversion overcharge.

## A note on calling 112 or 133 with limited German

If you dial **112** or **133** and cannot explain what is happening in German, say *"Englisch, bitte"* clearly and wait. Austrian emergency dispatchers handle English routinely. State your location as a landmark — *"Stephansplatz,"* *"Getreidegasse,"* *"Hallstatt Marktplatz"* — rather than a street address that is hard to pronounce. If you are calling from a mobile phone, the dispatcher can often pull your approximate location automatically, so even a rough *"I am near the Goldenes Dachl in Innsbruck"* is enough to get help moving.

For travelers who have just been pickpocketed, the word that tells every officer what you need is **Anzeige** — the crime report you will use for your insurance claim and your card chargeback.
