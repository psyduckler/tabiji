# The Scams: City by City {-}

The chapters that follow document every scam reported across nine Austrian cities and regions. They are ordered for reading utility — starting with Vienna, then following the Danube up through the Wachau to Linz, west to Mozart's Salzburg, into the lake-and-alpine resort country of Hallstatt, Bad Gastein, and Zell am See, over to Tyrol's Innsbruck, and finishing in Styria's Graz.

If you are traveling to only a few of these, skip directly to the relevant chapters. If you are visiting more, skim them all — many scams (the Euronet currency-conversion trap, the off-meter airport taxi, the lakeview view-tax bill, the clipboard-petition distraction, and the reseller ticket markup) appear in several places with local variations. Knowing the pattern in Vienna makes you faster to spot it in Salzburg or Graz.

## The Nine Destinations at a Glance {-}

![Vienna — St. Stephen's Cathedral and the Hofburg at golden hour](assets/cities/vienna.jpg)

**Vienna** — The imperial capital, the coffee houses, the Klimt gold — and the densest scam ecosystem in the country. Costumed "Mozart" ticket sellers on Stephansplatz; roughly 5,300 pickpocket cases a year concentrated on the U1 and the 1st-district triangle; fake-police "ID check" PIN theft documented against tourists by Der Standard; off-meter 50-to-70-euro airport taxis; the 105-euro airport-train zone fine; and an apartment-rental fraud wave the Federal Criminal Police peg at about 30 cases a week.

![The Wachau — Dürnstein's blue church tower above the Danube and terraced vineyards](assets/cities/wachau.jpg)

**The Wachau** — The UNESCO Danube valley of vineyards, apricot orchards, and Dürnstein's blue-towered abbey, swamped on cruise-and-coach days. The Dürnstein terrace rush on the one narrow Hauptstraße; wrong-boat tickets between Krems and Melk; the app-only Dürnstein parking maze; "homemade" Marille jam and roadside Schnaps that isn't; Melk Abbey reseller markups; and the Euronet conversion trap by the Krems and Melk docks.

![Linz — the main square and Pöstlingberg church above the Danube](assets/cities/linz.jpg)

**Linz** — The Danube cruise port, the Ars Electronica city, the vast baroque Hauptplatz. Off-meter taxis to Hörsching airport; overpriced Salzburg shore excursions sold off the cruise dock; Hauptplatz terrace upsells and padded bills; the Euronet DCC trap on Landstraße; marked-up Pöstlingbergbahn and attraction tickets; and the clipboard-petition pickpocket distraction on Hauptplatz.

![Salzburg — the Hohensalzburg Fortress above the old town and Salzach river](assets/cities/salzburg.jpg)

**Salzburg** — Mozart's birthplace, the baroque old town, *The Sound of Music*. Aggressive scam restaurants off the Getreidegasse with a host waving you in; private parking-lot extortion near the riverbank; a sympathy approach at the Hauptbahnhof; counterfeit Mozart memorabilia; Sound of Music tour overcharges and rebadges; and the orange Euronet ATMs on Linzergasse and Getreidegasse.

![Hallstatt — pastel lakeside houses and church spire beneath alpine cliffs](assets/cities/hallstatt.jpg)

**Hallstatt** — The most-photographed village in Austria, a UNESCO lakeside of eight hundred residents drowning in ten thousand day-trippers. The drive-past-the-barrier no-car-zone fine; steep pay-by-the-hour parking and lost-ticket stings; the cash-only ferry and private-boat upsell; the lakeview-restaurant bill and card tricks; drone-photo fines at the classic viewpoint; and coach day tours that give you two hours on the ground.

![Bad Gastein — Belle Époque hotels stacked above the waterfall in the alpine gorge](assets/cities/bad-gastein.jpg)

**Bad Gastein** — The Belle Époque spa town stacked dramatically around its waterfall, half grand-hotel revival and half ski resort. Fake holiday-apartment deposit-wire listings around Straubinger Platz; a mountain-restaurant cover-charge pile-on; hidden Felsentherme thermal-spa add-ons; unofficial 100-kilometer Salzburg-airport transfers; radon-gallery therapy upsells; and the standalone Euronet conversion trap by the station.

![Zell am See — the lakeside town beneath the snow-capped Kitzsteinhorn glacier](assets/cities/zell-am-see.jpg)

**Zell am See** — The lake-and-glacier resort beneath the Kitzsteinhorn, a summer-and-ski hub roughly 90 kilometers from Salzburg Airport. Unlicensed "black taxis" at the Bahnhof; fixed-price airport-transfer shakedowns; Esplanade café water-and-bill padding; no-meter day-trip taxi quotes to Kaprun and beyond; a cable-car counter upsell over the free Summer Card; and overpriced private Grossglockner and Krimml Waterfalls tours.

![Innsbruck — colorful Maria-Theresien-Strasse facades beneath the Nordkette mountains](assets/cities/innsbruck.jpg)

**Innsbruck** — The Tyrolean capital under the Nordkette, the Golden Roof, the gateway to the Alps. The "broken camera blame" near the Goldenes Dachl; a friendly nightlife lure toward a "much better place" and a padded bar bill; the ATM currency-conversion trap; the discount ski-pass middleman; off-meter 30-to-35-euro airport taxis; and a marked-up Swarovski Crystal Worlds shuttle package from old-town tour desks.

![Graz — the Schlossberg clock tower above the red-roofed old town](assets/cities/graz.jpg)

**Graz** — Austria's second city, a UNESCO old town of red rooftops crowned by the Schlossberg clock tower. Jakominiplatz tram-crush pickpocketing; a Schlossberg-terrace view-tax markup; the Euronet DCC trap on Herrengasse; off-meter airport and late-night taxi overcharges; a stricter-than-it-looks Blue Zone parking fine; and the Herrengasse clipboard-and-bracelet distraction.
