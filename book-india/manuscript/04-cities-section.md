# The Scams: City by City {-}

The chapters that follow document sixty scams reported across twelve Indian cities. Cities are ordered by the typical first-time itinerary — starting with Delhi and the capital region, sweeping the Golden Triangle through Agra and Jaipur, slipping into Rajasthan at Udaipur, dropping east to Varanasi on the Ganga and Kolkata on the Hooghly, then west to Mumbai and the Goan coast, south to Bangalore, Hyderabad, and Chennai, and finally up to Rishikesh in the Himalayan foothills.

If you are traveling to only a few of these cities, skip directly to the relevant chapters. If you are visiting more, skim them all — many scams (the auto-rickshaw commission detour, the fake-tourism-office hotel hijack, the brand-mimicry storefront, the temple-priest donation book, the made-up monument closure, the boat-fare price-explosion mid-ride) appear in multiple cities with local variations. Knowing the pattern in Delhi makes you faster to spot it in Jaipur, Mumbai, or Hyderabad.

## The Twelve Cities at a Glance {-}

**Delhi** — The Indian capital, the Red Fort, India Gate, Qutub Minar, and the country's busiest scam ecosystem. The New Delhi Station Fake Tourist Office, where polo-shirt "Tourism" reps walk arrivals to a Paharganj storefront and rebook them into a $300 to $500 package; the 'Closed Attraction' Rickshaw Redirect that converts a Red Fort visit into a silk-emporium tour; the Paharganj Overpriced 'Guesthouse Full' Hustle that confuses arrivals with similar-named guesthouses; the Connaught Place Friendly-Stranger Tea Trap; the Fake Delhi Metro Token Tout at Rajiv Chowk; the Overpriced Tuk-Tuk to Agra Road Vendors with their "mandatory rest stops" on the Yamuna Expressway.

**Agra** — The Taj Mahal, Agra Fort, Fatehpur Sikri, and a marble-shop scam economy that rivals any in India. The Marble Factory Commission Tour Overcharge, where Tajganj drivers route the day to "Karkhana" workshops pushing ₹37,000 inlay bowls; the Fatehpur Sikri Fake Guide and Chadar Shakedown at the Sheikh Salim Chishti dargah, where "donations" run ₹15,000 to ₹90,000; the Taj Mahal lookalike ticket-website that sells $35 invalid passes against the ₹1,300 ASI rate; the autorickshaw "free tour" commission shop circuit; the Uber Intercity Delhi-Agra toll-tax surcharge that demands ₹4,000 in fictitious tolls.

**Jaipur** — The Pink City, Hawa Mahal, City Palace, Amber Fort, and the most consistently-reported textile and gem scams in Rajasthan. The Johari Bazaar Gem Export Pitch (₹45,000 of "investment rubies" that turn out to be glass); the Hawa Mahal Rickshaw Textile Detour to a "cousin's emporium" in the suburbs at 5x to 10x Bapu Bazaar prices; the Jaipur Station Fake Tourist Office that mimics RTDC; the Elephant Ride Bait-and-Switch at Amber Fort, where ₹600 becomes ₹2,000 mid-ramp; the Hotel-Canceled Tout outside Jaipur Railway Station; the Hawa Mahal Free-Henna Pressure Hustle that grabs your wrist before any price is named; the Amber Fort Milk-and-Formula Sympathy Hustle.

**Udaipur** — The "Venice of the East," Lake Pichola, City Palace, the Lake Palace, and the smallest, gentlest scam set in this book. The Lake Palace Boat Photography Scam, where a ₹200 sunset ride becomes a 20-photo demand at ₹500 each; the City Palace Miniature Painting Pitch, where a 20-minute demonstration is followed by a factory-reproduction sale.

**Varanasi** — The Ganges, Dashashwamedh Ghat, Manikarnika Ghat, the Vishwanath Temple lanes, and three temple-and-river scams that work specifically on the spiritual visitor. The Ganga Offering Shake-Down, where a saffron-robed "priest" places oil lamps on your lap during the Aarti and produces a ₹2,000 to ₹5,000 donation book; the Ganges Sunrise Boat Ride Price Inflation, where ₹100 becomes ₹1,500 with mid-ride "viewing fees"; the Vishwanath Lanes Silk Shop Detour, where a "shortcut" through the temple maze passes through a polyester-as-Banarasi shop.

**Kolkata** — The City of Joy, Howrah Station, Kalighat Temple, New Market, the Maidan, and seven scams that range from station touts to organized child pickpocket rings. The Howrah Station Ticket Trap, where touts intercept arrivals at India's busiest railway hub; the Kalighat Temple Donation Scam with its donation book of fake foreign-name entries; the New Market Commission Guide; the CCU Airport Prepaid-Taxi Hotel Diversion; the Sudder Street Pickpocket Ring; the Victoria Memorial Rickshaw Fare Explosion with its team of backup pullers; the Kolkata Driver Currency-Note Swap.

**Mumbai** — The Gateway of India, CST, Marine Drive, Bandra, Andheri, and seven scams that span taxis, dating apps, antique markets, and the Elephanta ferry. The CST Black-Taxi Meter Scam where ₹180 becomes ₹800 on a Bandra-Colaba ride; the Dhobhi Ghat 'Free Guide' Commission Trap; the Dating App Café Bill Scam that hit headlines after 22 victims at two Andheri pubs in 2024; the Fake 'Your Hotel Is Closed' airport redirect; the Overpriced Colaba Causeway 'Antique' Sellers; the Elephanta Island Boat Ticket Touting that flips ₹200 government ferry into ₹500-₹800 "VIP" tickets; the CSMT Train Ticket 'Reservation Assistance' Scam.

**Goa** — Baga Beach, Anjuna, Vagator, Aguada Fort, Old Goa, and six scams from a state that successfully blocked Uber and built a unique cartel ecosystem. The Taxi Mafia Fixed-Rate Extortion, with ₹800-₹1,200 quotes for ₹150-equivalent rides; the Aguada Fort Scratch-Card Resort Membership Scam (₹6 lakh memberships dressed as "free holidays"); the Fake Hotel Listing Booking Fraud that defrauded 500+ tourists in early 2025; the Anjuna Drug Sting and Police Bribe Setup; the Beach Shack Chair and Umbrella Ambush at Baga; the North Goa Motorbike Rental Damage Claim.

**Bangalore** — Silicon Valley of India, Vidhana Soudha, Cubbon Park, Kempegowda Airport, and five scams that target the IT-and-business traveler corridor. The Silk Board Auto Meter-Speed Trick, where aftermarket meter chips double the per-km tick; the HSR Layout Driver Hospital-Photo Hustle, where the same wife-and-twins photo circulates across drivers; the Commercial Street Gem Export Pitch; the Kempegowda Airport Prepaid-Tout Hustle; the Majestic Tourist-SIM Markup that sells ₹300 SIMs at ₹1,500 with potentially fraudulent registration.

**Hyderabad** — Charminar, Golconda Fort, Hussain Sagar, Old City, Banjara Hills, and five scams ranging from pearl counterfeits to biryani bait-and-bills. The Laad Bazaar Pearl Sting near Charminar, where ₹12,000 "Hyderabadi pearls" anchor and "discount" to ₹4,000 plastic beads; the RGIA Airport Shared-Taxi Hustle that splits your luggage to a second vehicle and turns 45 minutes into 3 hours; the Golconda Fort Unofficial Guide; the Old City Rickshaw Runaround through attar, bangle, and pearl shops; the Charminar Biryani Bait-and-Bill, where copycat "Bawarchii" and "Shahh Ghouse" restaurants charge ₹800 for a single plate.

**Chennai** — Marina Beach, Kapaleeshwarar Temple, T. Nagar, Mahabalipuram, the Tamil heartland, and five scams across station, temple, beach, market, and travel-agent channels. The Chennai Central Auto Commission Loop, where ₹50 quotes turn into two-hour silk-and-jewelry circuits; the Kapaleeshwarar Temple Blessing Shakedown in Mylapore; the Marina Beach Parking Extortion run by men in fluorescent vests with no city authority; the T. Nagar Silk Saree Switcheroo, where a Kanchipuram swap happens during the wrap; the Egmore Fake Tour Agent Vanish that vanishes on the morning of the trip.

**Rishikesh** — The "Yoga Capital of the World," Tapovan, Laxman Jhula, the Ganga at the Himalayan foothills, and two scams aimed specifically at the spiritual-and-wellness visitor. The Tapovan Fake-Ashram YTT Trap, where $500 200-hour Yoga Teacher Trainings produce certificates from made-up alliances; the Rishikesh Fake-GST Billing that adds 5%-18% "tax" to bills with no GSTIN registration.
