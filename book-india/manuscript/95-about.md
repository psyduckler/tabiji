# About Tabiji {-}

Tabiji is a small team that publishes practical travel-safety guides for first-time visitors to scam-prone destinations. We read every relevant Reddit thread, every regional news report, and every embassy advisory we can find, then write what we wish we had read before our own first trip.

This is the fifth volume in the Travel Safety Series, after Japan, Italy, Costa Rica, and Egypt. More volumes are in production — see [tabiji.ai/books](https://tabiji.ai/books) for the latest list.

If you have a scam to add, a correction to make, or a city you'd like us to cover, write to us at hello@tabiji.ai. We read every email.
