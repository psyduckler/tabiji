# Appendix: Emergency Contacts {-}

All numbers verified current as of April 2026. Phone numbers are dialed from within India unless otherwise noted; international callers add **+91** and drop the leading zero.

## Pan-India emergency lines {-}

| Service | Number | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **All-Emergency Response** | **112** | Single-window emergency dispatch. Police, fire, ambulance, women's helpline, all routed from this number. Works in 36 states and union territories. |
| **Police** | **100** | Direct police dispatch. Falls back to 112 in some states. |
| **Tourist Police helpline** | **1363** | All-India 24/7 multilingual line, 12 languages. Can mediate scam disputes locally. |
| **Cyber Crime Helpline** | **1930** | Online fraud, fake hotel listings, UPI fraud. Web portal: [cybercrime.gov.in](https://www.cybercrime.gov.in/) |
| **Women's Helpline** | **1091** | 24/7 emergency support for women travelers. |
| **Ambulance** | **102** or **108** | 102 is the national service; 108 the GVK-EMRI service in many states. |
| **Fire** | **101** | National fire dispatch. |
| **National Consumer Helpline** | **1915** | Consumer-fraud disputes. Web: [consumerhelpline.gov.in](https://consumerhelpline.gov.in/) |

## Travel-fraud-specific reporting routes {-}

- **Fake hotel/tour listings:** Cyber Crime helpline **1930** + [cybercrime.gov.in](https://www.cybercrime.gov.in/) within 24 hours improves the chance of UPI/account freeze.
- **Tourist-shop counterfeit gems / "antiques":** Consumer Helpline **1915** for amounts over ₹20,000; below that, document for travel insurance.
- **Auto/taxi meter tampering:** complain to the Regional Transport Office (RTO) of the city — most accept photo evidence and the vehicle plate. Mumbai: rto-mh.gov.in. Bangalore: transport.karnataka.gov.in. Delhi: transport.delhi.gov.in.
- **Tourist-police harassment / extortion:** Tourist Police helpline **1363** itself accepts complaints against officers.
