Hyderabad is the city of pearls, biryani, and Charminar's four minarets — and five scams that work the corridors a first-time visitor will trace exactly. Risk concentrates at Laad Bazaar near Charminar (the pearl district), Rajiv Gandhi International Airport (RGIA) arrivals, the entrance to Golconda Fort, the Old City rickshaw routes between Charminar, Mecca Masjid, and Salar Jung Museum, and the biryani restaurants around Charminar. The Laad Bazaar Pearl Sting is the marquee scam — a shopkeeper anchors at ₹12,000 for a "genuine Hyderabadi pearl" strand, drops to ₹4,000 "because you seem like a nice person," and the strand is plastic beads with a pearlescent coating shipped from Nepal and Tibet. Market reports estimate over 25% of Hyderabad's pearl trade is counterfeit. At RGIA, the Shared-Taxi Hustle splits your luggage into a second vehicle and turns a 45-minute Banjara Hills ride into a three-hour collection-tour. At Golconda Fort, "ASI-certified" guides with laminated badges latch onto arrivals and demand ₹2,000 at the exit against the real ₹500 to ₹600 rate.

The five Hyderabad defenses, ranked: buy pearls only at named flagship stores — Krishna Pearls, the original Jagdamba on Basheerbagh, or Mangatrai — with a gemological certificate plus a GST-compliant receipt, and do the tooth test (real pearls feel slightly gritty, fakes feel smooth); use only the official prepaid taxi counter or the Ola/Uber pickup zone at RGIA, never let a stranger handle your luggage, and verify the car's commercial license plate matches your booking before climbing in; hire guides only at the Golconda Fort official counter inside the entrance, agree on the price in writing before the tour starts; skip street autos in the Charminar corridor entirely (book Ola or Uber Auto for fixed in-app pricing, or walk between Charminar, Mecca Masjid, and Laad Bazaar — they are within a 10-minute stroll); and verify biryani-restaurant authenticity on Google Maps before sitting down — the real Bawarchi is at Lalita Bagh in Begumpet, the real Shah Ghouse is at Tolichowki, and the look-alike spelling variants near Charminar are not affiliates.
