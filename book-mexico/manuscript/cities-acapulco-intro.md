Acapulco is the original Mexican Pacific resort city — the bay that built the *jet-set* template in the 1950s, anchored by La Quebrada cliff-divers, Acapulco International (ACA), and a coastline that runs from **Old Acapulco** east along the *Costera Miguel Alemán* arc to the **Diamante** resort strip. The scam ecosystem concentrates on three corridors: **ACA arrivals and the cruise pier**, the **Costera beachfront** from Playa Hornos through Playa Hornitos, and the **Diamante resort welcome desks**. Patterns are documented in *El Sur* and *Quadratín Guerrero*. One important note: Hurricane Otis flattened large sections of the city in October 2023 and the state of Guerrero carries an active US State Department Level 4 advisory; the Diamante and Costera tourist zones are statistically separate from rural-Guerrero cartel risk, but verify current zone-by-zone status with your embassy and travel insurer before booking, and stay out of the rural interior entirely.

The **ACA arrivals taxi-mafia overcharge** is the volume risk. Touts inside the airport quote MX$1,200–$2,500 for a ride to a Costera hotel that runs MX$300–$450 on Uber and MX$450–$600 on the official **Transporte Terrestre** prepaid booth inside arrivals. The same crew works the cruise pier — "authorized taxi US$50 to the cliff divers" for a trip the metered SECTUR stand fifty feet behind them runs at MX$200–$300. The defense: walk past the curb, use the prepaid booth or Uber, and refuse every quoted fare on the sidewalk.

The risk radiates out in three corridors. The **La Quebrada "VIP" cliff-diver tout** intercepts you on the climb to the *Mirador* before the 9:30 PM torch dive and sells a "premium platform ticket" at MX$300–$500 that does not exist — the official ticket at the gate is MX$50–$80, and the best angle is a MX$200 margarita on the La Perla terrace at Hotel El Mirador. The **Costera beachfront "*mariscos* market price" bill-padding** runs USD-only menus, weighs lobster out of sight at MX$1,800 for a 1.5-kg fish, and closes with an 18% pre-added gratuity plus a 10–15% card surcharge; ask for the *MX$* menu and agree per-kilogram price in writing. And the **Diamante welcome-desk "hurricane recovery" timeshare** at Princess Mundo Imperial and Vidanta Acapulco runs 3-to-5-hour pitches with US$25,000 "rebuild Acapulco" fractional ownership and a fictitious "US$5,000 *fideicomiso* admin fee" that real Mexican *notarios* charge at MX$10,000–$25,000.

A safe default: book ACA transfers at the **Transporte Terrestre** prepaid booth or via Uber; pay La Quebrada at the gate, never on the climb; eat where prices are posted in pesos — La Cabaña de Caleta, El Cabrito, 100% Natural; refuse every "free breakfast plus US$200 credit" pitch at resort check-in, and if you have already signed, file a **PROFECO** complaint at profeco.gob.mx within five business days; refuse every Instagram or WhatsApp "authentic Guerrero" tour offer; and call **911** for emergencies, or the Acapulco *Policía Turística* direct line at **+52 744 484 4416**. The Guerrero *Fiscalía General* handles formal *Ministerio Público* (**MP**) complaints, and the US Embassy 24-hour line is **+52 55 8526 2561**.
