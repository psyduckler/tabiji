Holbox (pronounced *OLE-boche*) is the sand-street eco-island at the northern tip of Quintana Roo, reached by 20-minute ferry from Chiquilá 160 km north of Cancún, sitting inside the Yum Balam *Reserva* and serving as Mexico's flagship for whale-shark snorkeling and flamingo sightings. There are no cars on the island — only golf carts and bicycles on unpaved sand streets — and the scam ecosystem clusters in three corridors: the **Chiquilá mainland ferry pier and parking lots**, the **Avenida Tiburón Ballena (Whale Shark Street) tour-booth strip** running through the island Centro, and the **Punta Cocos and Punta Mosquito** beach approaches at either end. Quintana Roo's *fiscalía* and SECTUR's **078** hotline cover complaints, and *Riviera Maya News* has documented the cross-island ferry and tour patterns through 2025.

The **whale-shark-tour unlicensed-operator and off-season "guarantee" fraud** is the volume risk and Holbox's most-dangerous scam. Legitimate season is strictly **May 15 – September 15**, with SEMARNAT-permitted operators (VIP Holbox, Holbox Whale Shark Tours, Contoy Adventures) running 5–7 hour tours at US$150–$220 per person, including the 1.5-hour boat transfer to the Punta Mosquito approach zone, regulated single-group snorkel time, and stops at Isla Pajaros and *Cenote* Cabo Catoche. The 2025 scams: "whale-shark guarantee" pitches sold October–April when sharks have migrated and no operator can deliver them; unlicensed boats without SEMARNAT permits, life jackets, or insurance (with documented drownings in Gulf currents); MX$8,000 "private tour" upsells over a regulated group cap of ten; and mid-trip "fuel surcharges" of MX$500. The defense: verify the SEMARNAT number on paperwork before paying, confirm life jackets and the captain's license are displayed on the boat, and reject every off-season offer outright.

The risk radiates out in three corridors. The **Chiquilá ferry-pier overcharge and parking-lot extortion** sells "VIP ferry MX$800" tickets when 9 Hermanos / Holbox Express round-trips are MX$600, redirects drivers from the official terminal lot at MX$100/day to unofficial lots at MX$300–$500 (sometimes with cars rifled), and pushes Cancún private vans at MX$2,800 when the ADO bus to Chiquilá is MX$450 plus the MX$300 ferry. The **Avenida Tiburón Ballena artisan-stall *chaquira*-bead "all-handmade" markup** runs alongside hotel-lobby tour desks and Punta Cocos "private skipper" pitches at MX$6,000 for bioluminescence trips that are MX$1,800 in a group — and only run on dark-moon nights from May to October, never during full moons or off-season when operators "guarantee" the glow. And the **Holbox golf-cart and Airbnb overwater-bungalow fraud pair** delivers "sand stuck" and "beach cleaning" return fees of MX$1,500–$3,000 on perfectly normal sand-road usage, and Instagram listings using stolen Maldives photos for a sandbar island where no overwater bungalow exists.

A safe default: take the ADO bus from Cancún to Chiquilá at MX$450 and buy ferry tickets only at the official 9 Hermanos / Holbox Express window; withdraw MX$3,000–$5,000 cash on the mainland before boarding, because Holbox has only two or three unreliable ATMs and hotel-reception exchange runs 12–15% padded; book whale-shark tours only May 15 – September 15 through SEMARNAT-licensed operators with the permit number on paperwork; rent golf carts only at Google 4.5+ shops (Rentadora Holbox, Caribe Rent) with a credit-card pre-auth and timestamped photos; reserve hotels through Booking.com or direct websites at Las Nubes, Palapas del Sol, Hotel Villas Flamingos, Hotel Mawimbi, or Casa Sandra — never via WhatsApp bank transfer; and call **911** for emergencies, **078** for SECTUR, or the Quintana Roo *fiscalía* through SECTUR for written complaints.
