# The Scams: City by City {-}

The chapters that follow document every scam reported in nineteen Mexican destinations. Cities are ordered by how most visitors actually travel: starting with Mexico City, the country's flagship and the densest scam ecosystem, then through the central highland heritage cities (Puebla, Oaxaca, Guanajuato, San Miguel de Allende), west to Guadalajara, east to the Yucatán capital of Mérida and the Chiapas highland of San Cristóbal, on to the Caribbean (Cancún, Playa del Carmen, Tulum, Cozumel, Isla Mujeres, Holbox), and finishing along the Pacific coast (Puerto Vallarta, Mazatlán, Acapulco, Cabo San Lucas, Puerto Escondido).

If you're hitting only a few of these, skip to the chapter you need. If you're doing a longer loop, skim them all — the same core scams recur with local variations. The Mexico City Zócalo Aztec-dancer photo-tip extraction runs almost identically on the Puerto Vallarta Malecón. The Cancún Hotel Zone time-share "free breakfast" sign-up runs in Cabo San Lucas, Puerto Vallarta, and Playa del Carmen. The airport flat-rate taxi refusal starts at Mexico City's Benito Juárez (MEX) and repeats at Cancún (CUN), Puerto Vallarta (PVR), Los Cabos (SJD), Mérida (MID), and Mazatlán (MZT). Learn the pattern in one city, and you'll spot it the instant you step off the plane in the next.

## The Nineteen Cities at a Glance {-}

![Mexico City — the Catedral Metropolitana and Zócalo flag at golden hour](assets/cities/mexico-city.jpg)

**Mexico City** — Mexico's capital and the densest approach-scam ecosystem in the country. The Zócalo Aztec-dancer "photo tip" shakedown that the Policía Turística have been issuing English-language warning cards about for years; the Centro Histórico fake-tourist-police "currency check" that runs almost daily near the Templo Mayor; the Benito Juárez (MEX) airport "authorized" *sitio*-taxi overcharge that *Reforma* and *El Universal* have covered for over a decade; the Roma Norte chalkboard tasting-menu inflation; and the Polanco "off-app" Uber that picks you up after a real ride is canceled.

![Puebla — Talavera-tiled facades and Popocatépetl on the horizon](assets/cities/puebla.jpg)

**Puebla** — Colonial heritage city, *talavera* ceramics capital, and the gateway to the Sierra Madre Oriental. The Centro Histórico *talavera*-shop "master artisan" markup on factory-line pieces; the Cholula pyramid "free guide" approach that ends at a kickback gift shop; Puebla International Airport (PBC) flat-rate taxi touts; and the *mole poblano* "tasting menu" overcharges in the central Zócalo restaurants where the prices are not posted.

![Oaxaca — Templo de Santo Domingo above jacaranda trees](assets/cities/oaxaca.jpg)

**Oaxaca** — UNESCO heritage capital and the heart of Mexican mezcal. The Templo de Santo Domingo "free history guide" tip extraction; mezcal *palenque* tour kickback shops in the surrounding Valles Centrales; Monte Albán archaeological-site freelance guide overcharges; *huipil* and Zapotec rug "all-handmade" markup at central market stalls that source from machine-loom imports; and the *Día de los Muertos* festival pricing inflation on cemetery-tour packages.

![Guanajuato — Callejón del Beso and pastel hillside houses](assets/cities/guanajuato.jpg)

**Guanajuato** — Silver-mining heritage city, university town, and the *callejones* labyrinth. The *callejoneada* (musical alley walk) "private group rate" overcharge; *Mummy Museum* freelance "express line" tickets sold at 3× the official rate; Bajío area minibus-driver "tour package" upsells with a single-silver-shop kickback stop; and the central Jardín de la Unión restaurant tourist menu priced in dollars when the peso menu is significantly cheaper.

![San Miguel de Allende — pink Parroquia rising over the Jardín](assets/cities/san-miguel-de-allende.jpg)

**San Miguel de Allende** — *Pueblo Mágico*, expat magnet, and one of the most-photographed colonial cities in Mexico. The Jardín Principal "free walking tour" approach that ends at a hot-springs / silver-shop combination; the Atotonilco hot-springs day-pass overcharge sold at the *centro* hotel desks; San Miguel Centro real-estate "investment seminars" targeting expats; and the *Calle del Chorro* artisan-shop *otomí*-textile "all-handmade" markup.

![Guadalajara — Catedral twin spires above Plaza de Armas](assets/cities/guadalajara.jpg)

**Guadalajara** — Mexico's second city, mariachi capital, and the gateway to Tequila country. The Plaza de los Mariachis "song request" overcharge where a single song quoted at MX$200 becomes a MX$2,000 set bill; the Tlaquepaque artisan-shop "antique" overcharge on machine-pressed pieces; Guadalajara International (GDL) airport "private transfer" touts at the *Llegadas* hall; and the Tequila distillery-tour package with hidden tasting-room minimums.

![Mérida — Plaza Grande and Casa de Montejo at midday](assets/cities/merida.jpg)

**Mérida** — Yucatán capital, white-stone heritage city, and the base for Chichén Itzá and the *cenotes*. The *Mérida en Domingo* tourist-restaurant Yucatecan "tasting menu" overcharge; *Hacienda*-tour kickback shops on the Mérida-Izamal-Valladolid loop; central plaza "free Mayan ceremony" donation push; Chichén Itzá freelance "guide" overcharges sold at the gate against the licensed INAH operators; and Mérida International Airport (MID) flat-rate taxi touts.

![San Cristóbal de las Casas — Templo de Santo Domingo and pine highlands](assets/cities/san-cristobal-de-las-casas.jpg)

**San Cristóbal de las Casas** — Chiapas highland *Pueblo Mágico*, gateway to the Tzotzil and Tzeltal communities. The *Real de Guadalupe* textile-shop "all hand-woven" markup on machine-loomed *huipil* pieces; San Juan Chamula village-tour "compulsory donation" pressure; *Sumidero Canyon* boat-tour package overcharges; and the central plaza "free coffee tour" approach that ends at a single high-margin coffee shop.

![Cancún — turquoise Hotel Zone curve at golden hour](assets/cities/cancun.jpg)

**Cancún** — The Caribbean flagship, the Hotel Zone, and the highest-volume tourist economy in Mexico. The Cancún International (CUN) "authorized taxi" overcharge that adds MX$800 to every airport ride; the Hotel Zone time-share "free breakfast" presentation that PROFECO has annulled hundreds of contracts from; the *Playa Delfines* jet-ski damage-deposit cycle; the Hotel Zone beach-club "house margarita" no-price overcharge; and the Mercado 28 silver-shop "antique" markup.

![Playa del Carmen — Quinta Avenida pedestrian street](assets/cities/playa-del-carmen.jpg)

**Playa del Carmen** — Riviera Maya hub, ferry port for Cozumel, and one of the densest tourist corridors in the Caribbean. The Quinta Avenida bracelet-drop scam; the Cozumel-ferry-terminal "official taxi" flat overcharge to the Hotel Zone; *Coco Bongo* and Mamita's Beach Club drink-up pricing without a posted minimum; the *5ta Avenida* time-share "free dinner" sign-up; and the Playa-Tulum ADO-bus "express ticket" upsell that touts sell at the bus station.

![Tulum — El Castillo Mayan ruin above the Caribbean](assets/cities/tulum.jpg)

**Tulum** — Cliffside Mayan ruins, bohemian beach road, and one of the fastest-rising scam corridors in the Riviera Maya. The Tulum cenote-road "park ranger" entry-fee shakedown that the *Quintana Roo fiscalía* has issued multiple advisories on; the Tulum beach-road no-price seafood overcharge; the cliffside "Mayan blessing" *chamán* donation scam; the Tulum-Pueblo ATV-tour deposit-hold; and the Tulum INAH-ruins "Express Line" ticket markup.

![Cozumel — San Miguel waterfront and reef-edge ferry pier](assets/cities/cozumel.jpg)

**Cozumel** — Caribbean reef-diving capital and the Riviera Maya's largest cruise port. The cruise-arrival taxi flat-rate to the Punta Sur lighthouse; the snorkel-set "cracked-mask" damage-fee at the malecón rental booths; the Punta Sur "private guide" overcharge sold inside the park; the *San Miguel* main-strip silver-shop "Taxco antique" markup; and the Mr Sancho's beach-club "free transfer" that adds an undisclosed minimum spend.

![Isla Mujeres — Playa Norte and golf-cart boardwalk](assets/cities/isla-mujeres.jpg)

**Isla Mujeres** — Caribbean day-trip island and the southern endpoint of the Cancún Hotel Zone. The Punta Sur sculpture-park "private tour" overcharge; the golf-cart rental damage deposit; the *Garrafón* Reef Park add-on minimum spends; the *Hidalgo Avenue* tourist-restaurant menu in dollars trick; and the Cancún-Isla ferry "VIP upgrade" sold by touts at the Puerto Juárez and Gran Puerto terminals.

![Holbox — sand streets and lagoon-side flamingos](assets/cities/holbox.jpg)

**Holbox** — Sand-street eco-island, flamingo-and-whale-shark flagship, and one of Mexico's quieter Caribbean destinations. The whale-shark-tour package "all-inclusive" downgrade where lunch and snorkel become add-ons; the Yum Balam *Reserva* "park ranger" entry-fee shakedown; the *Calle Tiburón Ballena* artisan-stall *chaquira*-bead "all-handmade" markup; the *Punta Cocos* boat-tour "private skipper" overcharge; and the Chiquilá-Holbox ferry tout upsells at the Chiquilá pier.

![Puerto Vallarta — Malecón seawall sculptures and Banderas Bay](assets/cities/puerto-vallarta.jpg)

**Puerto Vallarta** — Pacific-coast flagship, Banderas Bay, and one of the longest-established Mexican beach destinations. The Marietas Islands boat-tour hidden-fee package; the Romántica Zone time-share "free dinner" sign-up; the Malecón "free tequila tasting" tout that ends at a single high-markup tequila shop; the Olas Altas "lobster by weight" overcharge; and the Puerto Vallarta International (PVR) "authorized" flat-rate taxi overcharge to the Romántica Zone.

![Mazatlán — Old Lighthouse and Olas Altas at golden hour](assets/cities/mazatlan.jpg)

**Mazatlán** — Sinaloa Pacific-coast city, *Pueblo Mágico*-adjacent old town, and a major cruise port. The cruise-arrival "Old Town tour" taxi-flat overcharge; the Stone Island day-trip "private boat" upsell; the *Plazuela Machado* tourist-restaurant menu-in-dollars trick; the *Cerro de la Nevería* "private guided hike" overcharge; and the Mazatlán International (MZT) airport flat-rate taxi touts.

![Acapulco — La Quebrada cliff divers above the Pacific bay](assets/cities/acapulco.jpg)

**Acapulco** — Pacific Bay, La Quebrada cliff-divers, and the original Mexican Pacific resort city. The La Quebrada "front-row" *peso-tip* shakedown after the dive show; the Costera Miguel Alemán beach-vendor *henna-tattoo* over-pricing; the *Mercado de Artesanías* "antique" silver markup; and the Acapulco International (ACA) airport flat-rate taxi overcharges (operating in a city that has seen volatile security conditions in recent years — verify your hotel zone is currently green-flagged with your embassy or travel insurer before booking).

![Cabo San Lucas — El Arco and Land's End](assets/cities/cabo-san-lucas.jpg)

**Cabo San Lucas** — Baja California Sur tip, El Arco rock arch, and one of Mexico's most US-tourist-saturated Pacific destinations. The Marina time-share "free breakfast" sign-up that mirrors Cancún's pattern; the El Arco glass-bottom-boat hidden-fee package; the *Médano Beach* jet-ski / ATV damage-deposit cycle; the Marina yacht-charter "captain Pedro" tout that pulls you into a third-party operator; and the Los Cabos International (SJD) "private transfer" flat-rate overcharge.

![Puerto Escondido — Zicatela Beach Pipeline at golden hour](assets/cities/puerto-escondido.jpg)

**Puerto Escondido** — Pacific-coast surf town, Zicatela Beach, and one of the most-rapidly-developing Oaxaca-coast tourist economies. The Zicatela paddleboard / surfboard rental "fin damage" deposit-hold; the *Playa Carrizalillo* boat-tour package with hidden lunch fees; the *Mercado Benito Juárez* mezcal-tasting "antique bottle" overcharge; the *La Punta* surf-school "private lesson" overcharge against the posted group rate; and the Puerto Escondido (PXM) airport flat-rate taxi touts.
