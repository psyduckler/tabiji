Cabo San Lucas sits at the southern tip of Baja California — anchored by **El Arco** at Land's End, Los Cabos International (SJD), and a US-tourist-saturated coastline running from **Playa Médano** east along the Tourist Corridor toward San José del Cabo. The scam ecosystem concentrates on three corridors: the **SJD "Shark Tank"** arrivals hall, the **Marina** boat-tour boardwalk, and **Playa Médano** with its beach-club strip. Patterns are documented in *Tribuna de los Cabos* and *BCS Noticias*. Baja California Sur is one of Mexico's safest states, with no active US State Department travel restrictions on the tourist zones — the risk here is commercial rather than physical, but the commercial pressure is among the most aggressive in the country.

The **SJD "Shark Tank" timeshare and fake-taxi ambush** is the volume risk. Salespeople in polos and laminated badges intercept arrivals between customs and the curb, asking "Where are you staying?" before pitching a "free shuttle" that locks you into a 90-minute-to-5-hour presentation at Pueblo Bonito Sunset Beach, Garza Blanca Los Cabos, or Villa del Palmar. Adjacent kiosks quote US$80–$150 for a Cabo transfer that runs MX$900–$1,500 via Uber from the signposted pickup zone and US$20–$30 per person via the official **Cabo Yellow Bus** shared shuttle. The defense: walk straight through, do not engage, and meet your Uber at the marked zone.

The risk radiates out in three corridors. The **Marina "private yacht" El Arco upsell** sells a "private yacht to El Arco" at US$400 per person when the official **Cabo Water Taxi** (yellow boats) runs the same route to El Arco and Lovers Beach at US$20–$30 round-trip, and reputable sunset operators (Cabo Rey Sailing, Cabo Escape) run group cruises at US$60–$90; refuse "guaranteed whale sighting" upcharges (whales migrate, December through April only). The **Playa Médano vendor harassment and "free drink" bait** runs aggressive 30-to-60-second pitch cycles for *henna*, sarongs, jewelry, parasails, and beach-club covers — book massage inland at Spa Nowadays or Me Cabo Spa for MX$1,000–$1,600 and ask any beach-club cover charge before sitting. And the **rental-car deposit overcharge** at SJD agencies inflates a US$12/day base into US$80–$120/day via "mandatory insurance," holds US$2,500 deposits for 30 to 60 days, and invents "pre-existing damage" at return — book through Expedia Cars with the total visible upfront and photograph every panel before driving off.

A safe default: walk straight through the SJD "Shark Tank" without speaking; book your transfer at the signposted Uber zone or **Cabo Yellow Bus** shuttle; use **Cabo Water Taxi** yellow boats for El Arco and Lovers Beach; eat off the Marina at La Lupita, Los Tres Gallos, or El Farallon where prices print in pesos; decline every welcome-desk "Explore rewards" at Pueblo Bonito, Garza Blanca, and Villa Group, and file a **PROFECO** complaint within five business days if you have already signed; and call **911** for emergencies, or **078** for the federal SECTUR tourist line. The Baja California Sur *Fiscalía General* handles formal *Ministerio Público* (**MP**) complaints, and the US Consular Agency Cabo San Lucas is reachable at **+52 624 143 3566**.
