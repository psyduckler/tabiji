## Keywords (7 total — KDP search field)

KDP allows 7 keyword phrases. These are search phrases real buyers type into Amazon search. Order matters slightly — list them in priority of search volume × relevance.

```
1. medical tourism guide
2. surgery abroad
3. healthcare abroad
4. how to choose a doctor abroad
5. medical tourism safety
6. patient buyer protection
7. medical tourism insurance
```

> Notes for refinement: validate with Publisher Rocket or KDP keyword research before launch. Avoid keywords that are also category names (Amazon double-counts those). Avoid using your title or author name as a keyword. "Medical tourism" alone is one of the highest-volume single phrases in the category; "medical tourism guide" claims the buyer-protection long-tail more cleanly. "Surgery abroad" picks up cosmetic, orthopedic, bariatric, and cardiac searchers. "Healthcare abroad" is broader and captures the foundation-volume positioning that distinguishes V3 from V1 (dental) and V2 (cosmetic surgery).

## Other KDP fields

- **Language:** English
- **Publication date:** Set to the day you click "Publish" or schedule a date.
- **Publishing rights:** "I own the copyright" (assuming Tabiji owns rights)
- **Adult content:** No
- **DRM:** Off recommended (allows lending and library distribution; supports word-of-mouth for a consumer-protection book). DRM cannot be changed after publication.
- **KDP Select enrollment:** **Opt out for launch.** Select requires 90 days of Amazon exclusivity in exchange for Kindle Unlimited inclusion. For a series flagship volume, opting out preserves the ability to distribute via IngramSpark, Apple Books, Kobo, etc. — which matters more long-term than the Select boost.

## ISBNs

- **Kindle ebook:** Amazon assigns a free ASIN; no separate ISBN needed.
- **Paperback:** KDP can assign a free Amazon-only ISBN, OR you can supply your own purchased from Bowker (US) / Nielsen (UK). Use **your own ISBN** if you intend to distribute outside Amazon (IngramSpark, bookstores). KDP-assigned ISBNs lock the paperback to Amazon-only distribution.

## Series enrollment

KDP supports series enrollment for grouped volumes. Once V3 is live:


Enrolling V3 in the same Series Name as V1 and V2 means the three volumes appear together on each book's product page ("Other books in this series") and on Amazon search-result page suggestions.
