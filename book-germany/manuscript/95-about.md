# About Tabiji {-}

Tabiji is a small travel-safety publisher based on one stubborn idea: that the most useful thing a traveler can carry is a short, accurate, current list of the specific ways they are likely to be separated from their money in the specific city they are in.

We maintain [tabiji.ai/scams](https://tabiji.ai/scams), a free, continuously-updated directory of tourist scams drawn from local press, police reports, and consumer reviews across every major destination country. When we've documented enough depth in a country to package it usefully, we ship a book like this one — with a comic illustration for every scam so you can recognize the pattern visually, a recovery playbook for when the worst happens, and an exit-phrase card in the local language.

We do not accept tourism-board money. We do not accept advertising from hotels, tour operators, or rental companies. We source every scam from published reporting — German-language newspapers like *Der Spiegel*, *Süddeutsche Zeitung*, *Bild*, *Frankfurter Allgemeine Zeitung*, *Die Welt*, and *Tagesspiegel*; English-language German press like *Deutsche Welle*, *The Local Germany*, and *Deutsche Presse-Agentur (dpa)*; official Bundespolizei, Landespolizei, and Ministry of Tourism incident reports; and real traveler accounts on r/germany, r/berlin, r/munich, r/cologne, and r/solotravel.

If a scam described in this book has happened to you, or if you've seen one we haven't documented yet, email **tips@tabiji.ai**. We read every one.

— The Tabiji Team, 2026
