Heidelberg is the university town on the Neckar with Germany's most-photographed castle ruin — the red-sandstone Schloss above the Old Town, the Alte Brücke, and the Philosophenweg walking path. The scam ecosystem is narrow and concentrates on two corridors: the **Altstadt Hauptstraße** (Europe's longest pedestrian shopping street, one kilometer from Bismarckplatz to the Karlstor) and the **Heidelberg Schloss** castle grounds accessible by funicular from Kornmarkt. *Rhein-Neckar-Zeitung* and *Mannheimer Morgen* cover the patterns in their weekly crime bulletins.

The **Heidelberg Altstadt restaurant tourist-menu bill-padding** is the signature Heidelberg scam. The Hauptstraße section between the Universitätsplatz and the Heiliggeistkirche runs dozens of restaurants with aggressive multilingual greeters who quote "around €25" for a schnitzel that lands at €44 on the bill, or a "Heidelberger Student" seasonal drink at €12 when the posted menu lists it at €6. Tourists eating on the Hauptstraße choke point routinely spend €70–€120 per person for meals that cost €25–€45 one street over on Untere Straße. The pattern has been covered in the RNZ's *Gastro-Kritik* weekly column.

The risk radiates out in three corridors. The **Heidelberg Schloss ticket-reseller** scam mirrors the Sanssouci and Neuschwanstein patterns: third-party "skip-the-line" sites sell €22 slots for a €9 castle ticket, with QR codes that either duplicate legitimate slots or don't scan. The **Philosophenweg bike-rental damage claim** takes a €80 cash deposit and holds €40 back for "scratches" that were on the pickup photo. The **Hauptstraße "original Käthe Wohlfahrt" souvenir-shop** sells machine-imported SKUs at 3× the chain's own retail when the real Käthe Wohlfahrt Heidelberg branch is two doors down.

A safe default: book Schloss tickets only at **schloss-heidelberg.de** (€9 adult, €4.50 student, includes the funicular) or at the Schloss Kasse itself; eat on the Untere Straße side streets rather than the Hauptstraße choke point; rent bikes only from Heidelberg's **Stadtwerke Heidelberg Nextbike** (app-based, posted tariff, no cash deposit); and call **110** or visit the **Polizeirevier Heidelberg-Mitte** at Römerstraße 2–4 — seven minutes from Bismarckplatz.
