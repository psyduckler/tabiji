Bremen is a Hanseatic trade city with a well-preserved Marktplatz, a beloved Town Musicians of Bremen statue, and a notably lower scam intensity than Hamburg or Berlin — but the patterns that do appear cluster tight around two corridors: the **Marktplatz and Böttcherstraße** Altstadt axis and the **Bremen Hauptbahnhof** main entrance. The Bremen Polizei's central district publishes a *Tatort-Tourismus* bulletin every quarter; the Bremen numbers are small but the corridor density is real.

The **Marktplatz and Böttcherstraße pickpocket distraction** is the signature Bremen scam. A group photo at the bronze Town Musicians statue (the stacked donkey-dog-cat-rooster pyramid) is the peak-risk moment: a second team member asks you to take their photo, you hand over your phone, and while you are focused on framing the shot a third team member eases the wallet or phone from your pocket. Losses run €200–€800 per incident. The *Weser-Kurier* and *Bremen 1 radio* have covered the pattern every summer since 2019.

The risk radiates out in three corridors. The **Bremen Hauptbahnhof sob-story approach** targets arriving tourists at the tram-stop exit with the recurring "€30 for a train ticket home" pitch — the same script that runs at every major German Hauptbahnhof but with a lower hit rate in Bremen's smaller foot-traffic environment. The **fake VBN/BSAG Kontrolleur** on the tram demands a €60 on-the-spot cash fine; real Bremen-area ticket inspectors travel in pairs, carry a visible VBN or BSAG ID, and issue the fine by invoice. The **Schnoor quarter "handcrafted amber" markup** at the narrow Schnoorviertel tourist-shop lanes sells machine-pressed amber pieces at 3× fair retail; the Baltic-amber scam is documented on consumer-protection blogs and was covered by *Buten un Binnen* (Radio Bremen's city news) in 2023.

A safe default: keep valuables crossbody and close when photographing the Town Musicians or walking the Böttcherstraße jewel-box alleys; take the tram 4 or 6 from Hauptbahnhof into the Altstadt (€2.90, five minutes); for amber and *Bremer Kaffee* gifts, buy from posted-price shops only and pay by credit card; and call **110** or visit the Polizeirevier Mitte at Am Wall 196 — seven minutes from Marktplatz.
