Hamburg is Germany's second-largest city, a working port, and home to the Reeperbahn — the country's most notorious red-light district. The scam ecosystem concentrates on three corridors: the **Reeperbahn and Große Freiheit** in St. Pauli (bars, clubs, and the *Animierbars*), the **Hamburg Hauptbahnhof** main concourse and Wandelhalle, and the **HafenCity / Landungsbrücken** harbor-cruise tout strip. The Hamburg Polizei's district 11 (PK 11) covers St. Pauli specifically and publishes ongoing English-language warnings about Reeperbahn bill-shock; *Der Spiegel* and *Bild* have run the Reeperbahn scripts every year since the early 2010s.

The **Reeperbahn *Animierbar* bill-shock** is the signature Hamburg scam. A friendly English-fluent woman (an *Animierdame*) invites you to buy her a drink; what arrives as "one cocktail" is a bottle of sparkling wine at €250–€800 that shows up on the bill at the end of the evening. Bouncers block the exit until you pay by card. Losses in Hamburg Polizei case files routinely run €500–€3,000 per victim. The script is documented in *Hamburger Morgenpost* and on r/hamburg in continuous variants; the PK 11 St. Pauli desk at Davidstraße 1 handles dozens of these incidents every weekend of the summer.

The risk radiates out in three corridors. The **Hamburg Hauptbahnhof pickpocket team** works the Wandelhalle concourse and the platforms for the S-Bahn S1 and S3 to the airport — the ICE-to-Hauptbahnhof arrival from southern Germany is the peak window. The **Rathausmarkt "fake charity petition"** is Berlin's Brandenburg script ported north — clipboards, €50 asks, and an accomplice at your back pocket. The **HAM Airport flat-rate taxi tout** at Fuhlsbüttel quotes €60–€80 for the ~10-km run to the Hauptbahnhof (metered: €30–€38; the S-Bahn S1 is €3–€4 in 25 minutes).

A safe default: take the **S-Bahn S1** from HAM into the Hauptbahnhof; avoid every *Animierbar* (no sign you can recognize — the scam is that the sign looks like a normal bar); for Reeperbahn nightlife stick to venues with posted entry prices and drink-menu cards in the window (Molotow, Gretchens Villa, Beatlemania); keep your phone and wallet on your front or inside pocket through Hauptbahnhof and Landungsbrücken; and call **110** the moment an exit is blocked. The Polizeikommissariat 11 is at Davidstraße 1 — three minutes from the Reeperbahn axis. Service line: **+49 40 4286-56110**.
