# Stay Safe, and Enjoy Germany {-}

If you have read this book before your trip: thank you. You will almost certainly not need most of it. Germany is an extraordinary country — the Brandenburg Gate at dusk, the Elbphilharmonie glass sail reflected in the Hamburg harbor, a Bavarian Oktoberfest evening inside a hundred-year-old beer tent, the Cologne Cathedral spires above the Rhine, the Neuschwanstein silhouette at dawn against the Alps. We want you to come home with those stories.

If one of the scams in this book happened anyway: we are sorry. It happens to careful travelers every day. Turn to the recovery appendix, work through the first fifteen minutes and first hour, dial **110** if you need the Polizei or **112** for medical or fire emergencies, and take care of yourself first. The money is recoverable more often than you'd expect.
