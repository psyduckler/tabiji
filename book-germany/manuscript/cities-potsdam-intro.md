Potsdam sits twenty-five minutes by S-Bahn from Berlin, and its scam ecosystem is a quieter cousin of the capital's — concentrated in two corridors: the **Sanssouci Palace grounds and Park Sanssouci** (the Prussian palace circuit, UNESCO-listed) and the **Potsdam Altstadt / Dutch Quarter (*Holländisches Viertel*)**. The Brandenburg Landespolizei logs Potsdam separately from Berlin, and the numbers are small — a few hundred tourist-incident reports per year compared with Berlin's tens of thousands — but every one of them clusters on the Sanssouci-Altstadt axis where day-trippers arrive.

The **Sanssouci skip-the-line ticket reseller** is the signature Potsdam scam. Third-party websites with urls like "sanssouci-skipline.com" or "potsdam-tickets-official.eu" sell €35 "priority entry" slots for a palace that charges €14 at the ticket window and €8 for the audio guide. The QR codes either don't scan at the Sanssouci *Kasse* or duplicate legitimate slots already sold. The Bavarian and Prussian palace administrations ran a joint press release in *Süddeutsche Zeitung* in 2024 flagging this specific pattern across Neuschwanstein, Sanssouci, and Linderhof.

The risk radiates out in three corridors. The **Berlin-to-Potsdam day-trip package** sold by sidewalk agencies at Berlin's Alexanderplatz quotes €49 per person for a "guided tour" that bundles €4.40 of RE1 train fare plus a brisk walk past the palace exterior — the guided portion is the same public footpath any S-Bahn arrival can walk for free. The **Potsdam Altstadt tourist-menu bill-padding** hits the restaurants on the Brandenburger Straße pedestrian mall: a "Prussian special" priced verbally at "around €22" arrives as a €48 line item on the bill. The **Potsdam Hauptbahnhof approach scams** include sob-story cash requests (the "€50 for a train ticket home" pitch) that recur almost daily on the tram-stop benches.

A safe default: book Sanssouci tickets only at **spsg.de** (the Stiftung Preußische Schlösser und Gärten, the official Prussian palace administration); take the RE1 or S7 from Berlin Hauptbahnhof to Potsdam Hauptbahnhof for €4.40 and walk the fifteen minutes through Park Sanssouci yourself; eat on the Gutenbergstraße side streets rather than the Brandenburger Straße main drag; and dial **110** for Polizei escalation. The Polizeiinspektion Potsdam is at Henning-von-Tresckow-Straße 9–13, a ten-minute walk from the Altstadt. Service line: **+49 331 5508-0**.
