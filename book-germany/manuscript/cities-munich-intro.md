Munich is Germany's second-densest scam ecosystem after Berlin, and the reason is two events: the **Marienplatz / Viktualienmarkt** daily tourist corridor (8 AM to 10 PM most days of the year) and the two-week **Oktoberfest** on the Theresienwiese every late September through the first weekend of October. Add the **Munich Hauptbahnhof** late-night corridor and you have the three places where the Bavarian Landespolizei logs the bulk of foreign-visitor scam incidents. *Süddeutsche Zeitung*, *Münchner Merkur*, and *Tz München* cover new Oktoberfest and Altstadt scams every season.

The **Oktoberfest "share my table, I'll buy the first round"** is the signature Munich scam. During the two-week Wiesn, an English-fluent stranger (often claiming to be a fellow American or Brit) invites you to share their table in a crowded Hofbräu, Paulaner, or Löwenbräu tent, buys the first *Maß* (stein, €14.50–€15 by 2026 festival tariff), and disappears by the second round — leaving you responsible for a bill that includes four or five beers you never saw arrive, at €60–€180 total. The script is well-documented in *Bild* every late September. Pair it with the parallel **Oktoberfest ticket-reservation fraud** — fake "VIP tent" resale sites like *oktoberfestreservation.com* or *staykooook.com* — and Munich's September–October scam surge is Germany's single busiest tourist-fraud window.

The risk radiates out in three corridors. The **Marienplatz clipboard pickpocket** runs Berlin's Brandenburg script in the daily-crowd layer between the Rathaus and the Frauenkirche. The **Viktualienmarkt "taste before you buy" pressure sale** pushes tourists into buying €35 "artisan Obatzda" (cheese spread) that is €8 at the next stall. The **Munich Hauptbahnhof late-night Tarif-2 taxi overcharge** targets ICE arrivals between 22:00 and midnight — a ride to the Altstadt that meters at €12–€16 on Tarif 1 is run on Tarif 2 and billed €25–€35.

A safe default: for Oktoberfest tent reservations, book only through the brewery's own site (hofbraeu-festzelt.de, paulaner-festzelt.de, augustiner-festhalle.com, etc.); never accept a "shared table" invitation from an English-fluent stranger; at MUC airport take the **S1 or S8** to the Hauptbahnhof for €13–€14 in 45 minutes rather than the €110–€140 flat-rate taxi; on the daytime Marienplatz corridor keep wallets crossbody and phones in front pockets; and call **110** or walk to the **Polizeiinspektion 11** at Arnulfstraße 20 — four minutes from Hauptbahnhof. During Oktoberfest, the **Wiesn-Wache** is on-site at the Theresienwiese. Service line: **+49 89 2910-0**.
