Dresden is Saxon baroque at its most photogenic — the Zwinger, the Frauenkirche, the Semperoper, and one of Germany's three most-visited Christmas markets (the *Striezelmarkt*, running since 1434). The scam ecosystem concentrates on three corridors: the **Altstadt Neumarkt and Frauenkirche square**, the **Striezelmarkt on Altmarkt** during the December *Weihnachtsmarkt* run, and the **Dresden Hauptbahnhof and Prager Straße** shopping axis. The Saxon Landespolizei's Dresden district publishes a seasonal *Weihnachtsmarkt-Kriminalität* bulletin every December; pickpocket density on Altmarkt during the Striezelmarkt rivals Nuremberg.

The **Striezelmarkt pickpocket ring** is the signature Dresden scam. The 200-stall *Weihnachtsmarkt* draws two-million-plus visitors in the four weeks before Christmas, and organized teams of three or four work the densest choke points — the Stollen-cutting stage, the giant pyramid, the Glühwein counters. One distracts by asking directions or spilling Glühwein; a second eases the wallet or phone from a coat pocket while the target wipes the spill. Losses run €400–€1,200 per incident. The *Sächsische Zeitung* and *Dresdner Neueste Nachrichten* cover new variants every December.

The risk radiates out in three corridors. The **Neumarkt and Frauenkirche "audio-tour helper"** scam fronts as a uniformed attendant offering the free audio tour "with priority access" for €15 — the real audio guide is €4 inside the church. The **fake VVO Kontrolleur** on the Dresden tram demands a €60 on-the-spot cash fine; real VVO (Verkehrsverbund Oberelbe) inspectors travel in teams, carry visible IDs, and never collect cash. The **Prager Straße "Schnell-Wechselstube" currency-exchange kiosk** charges 12–18% below the xe.com rate on cash conversion; use an ATM inside a Sparkasse or Commerzbank branch instead.

A safe default: through the Striezelmarkt run, keep phones and wallets in zipped inner pockets and stay out of the three-deep Glühwein counter queues; buy Stollen and Pflaumentoffel from posted-price vendors only and check the per-kilo rate before the knife touches the stollen; at Frauenkirche, buy tickets only at the church's own *Besucherzentrum* (€10 adults, €5 audio guide); take the S1 / S2 / S3 from Hauptbahnhof to Altmarkt for €2.70; and call **110** or visit the Polizeirevier Dresden-Altstadt at Schießgasse 7 — four minutes from Neumarkt.
