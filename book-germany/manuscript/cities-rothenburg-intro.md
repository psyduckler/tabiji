Rothenburg ob der Tauber is the Romantic Road's photogenic climax — the iconic Plönlein fork, the unbroken city wall walk, the *Käthe Wohlfahrt* year-round Christmas shop, and the *Nachtwächter* (night-watchman) tour. The whole town is essentially a tourist destination; the scam ecosystem concentrates tight on three corridors: the **Marktplatz and the Plönlein** photo-op axis, the **Reiterlesmarkt** (Rothenburg's winter Christmas market, late Nov through Dec), and the **day-trip bus coach arrival zone** at the Am Ziegelhüttenplatz bus parking. *Nordbayerische Nachrichten* and *Fränkische Landeszeitung* cover the seasonal patterns.

The **Rothenburg Reiterlesmarkt and year-round Christmas shop overcharge** is the signature Rothenburg scam. *Käthe Wohlfahrt* operates a legitimate flagship shop at Herrngasse 1 with posted prices — but a second class of "Christmas shops" on Herrngasse and Obere Schmiedgasse sells machine-imported stein, Nutcracker, and cuckoo-clock SKUs at 2–3× Wohlfahrt's own retail, often with "handmade in Germany" signs that don't survive inspection. During the Reiterlesmarkt Christmas run, temporary stall overcharges on Schneeballen (Rothenburg's signature fried-dough pastry) add a "decorative" markup that triples the per-item price.

The risk radiates out in three corridors. The **Plönlein "photo-op donation"** approach targets coach-tour arrivals at the most-photographed intersection — a "local in costume" asks for €5 per photo after posing for one. The **Rothenburg tour-bus package overcharge** from Munich, Nuremberg, or Frankfurt runs €89–€120 per person for a five-hour day-trip that includes €7 of Deutsche Bahn train fare and a guided walk that is the same public-footpath tour any independent visitor can take for free. The **Rothenburg "medieval inn" accommodation overcharge** hits Kleinanzeigen off-platform rental listings with €300–€500 per-night "authentic medieval guesthouse" rooms at properties that don't exist.

A safe default: for Christmas-gift shopping, buy at the **Käthe Wohlfahrt flagship at Herrngasse 1** or **Weihnachtsdorf** (both have posted prices and a printed catalogue); take the Deutsche Bahn Regionalbahn RB88 from Steinach for €7 rather than a €90 bus-tour package; book accommodation only through Booking.com or Expedia for Rothenburg stays; decline every "costume photo" tip demand; and call **110** or visit the **Polizeiinspektion Rothenburg** at Erlbacher Straße 5 — four minutes from Marktplatz.
