Düsseldorf is North-Rhine Westphalia's business capital — Messe-week trade fairs (Boot, drupa, Interpack), the Königsallee luxury-shopping spine, and the Altstadt's "longest bar in the world" bar strip. The scam ecosystem concentrates on three corridors: the **Altstadt between Burgplatz and Bolkerstraße** (the bar strip), the **Königsallee ("Kö") shopping and business-lunch axis**, and **DUS Airport** / the **Düsseldorf Messe** trade-fair grounds. *Rheinische Post* and *Westdeutsche Zeitung* cover Messe-week surges and Altstadt bill-shock every season.

The **DUS Airport taxi flat-rate overcharge** is the signature Düsseldorf business-traveler scam. Business travelers arriving for Messe sessions or Kö meetings get quoted €70–€100 for the 10-kilometer run to the city center (metered fare: €30–€40, and the S-Bahn S11 is €3.20 in 14 minutes). The "executive car" premium-service markup runs deeper — €140+ for a ride that Uber-black would quote at €70. The DUS taxi dispatch and the *Verkehrsverbund Rhein-Ruhr* (VRR) both publish the tariff schedule, and any quote that ignores it is the tell.

The risk radiates out in three corridors. The **Altstadt "Killepitsch shot round"** at the Bolkerstraße bar strip inflates a round of six 2-cl herb-liqueur shots (standard €18) to €60+ by charging a "service fee" and rounding up. The **Königsallee "shopping-tour guide" commission** pitches foreign business travelers on a walk through the Kö luxury windows and steers them into specific jewellery and watch shops that pay 10–15% kickback. The **Düsseldorf Messe-week apartment-rental fraud** targets Boot, drupa, and Interpack attendees with fake off-platform listings at €600–€2,000 per week for properties that don't exist; the Messe weeks produce Germany's highest per-capita tourist-fraud density.

A safe default: take the **S-Bahn S11** from DUS to Düsseldorf Hauptbahnhof for €3.20; at the Altstadt bar strip, pay per round and count the shots as they come (Killepitsch is posted at €3 per 2-cl shot at reputable venues); on the Kö, walk yourself — the shop windows are the attraction, and a "guide" adds only commission; book accommodation only through Booking.com, Expedia, or HRS during Messe weeks; and call **110** or walk to the **Polizeiinspektion Mitte** at Jürgensplatz 5–7 — eight minutes from Altstadt. Service line: **+49 211 870-0**.
