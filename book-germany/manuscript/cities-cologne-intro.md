Cologne (*Köln*) runs on three tent-pole events — **Karneval** (the "Fifth Season," Thursday-through-Wednesday in late winter), **Gamescom** (August, Europe's largest video-game trade fair), and the Cologne Cathedral **Weihnachtsmarkt** on the Roncalliplatz. The scam ecosystem concentrates on three corridors: the **Kölner Dom / Domplatte** plaza directly in front of the cathedral, the **Cologne Hauptbahnhof** connected to the cathedral by a two-minute walk, and the **Altstadt Brauhaus quarter** (the Kölsch-serving traditional breweries on Heumarkt, Alter Markt, and Heinzelmännchenbrunnen). *Kölner Stadt-Anzeiger* and *Express Köln* cover the seasonal surges every February (Karneval), August (Gamescom), and December (Weihnachtsmarkt).

The **Kölner Dom pickpocket ring** is the signature Cologne scam. The Domplatte between the Hauptbahnhof exit and the cathedral's north portal handles twenty million annual visitors, and during the Weihnachtsmarkt run the density rivals Dresden's Striezelmarkt. Teams of three to five work the choke points — the Domwall descent, the Domplatte performance circle, the Cologne Cathedral audio-guide entrance line. One "trips," another apologizes and dabs at your coat, a third eases the wallet or phone. The Cologne Polizei's dedicated Dom-Wache (cathedral-plaza substation) is open for exactly this pattern.

The risk radiates out in three corridors. The **Heumarkt / Alter Markt Kölsch "Köbes" bill-padding** is the Cologne Brauhaus peculiarity — a *Köbes* (the traditional Brauhaus waiter in a blue smock) brings fresh 0.2L Kölsch glasses to your table automatically as you drink, marking each on your coaster; tourists who don't understand the ritual end the evening with eight or nine Kölsch charged at €2.50–€3 each plus service when they intended to order two. The **Alter Markt Rhine-cruise resale scam** sells walk-up "Rhine-valley cruise" tickets at €45 for what is actually a €15 45-minute puddle-jumper. The **Karneval "VIP parade viewing" resale fraud** sells fake tickets for the Rosenmontagszug processional route that are not transferable under the festival's byelaws.

A safe default: flip your coaster upside-down on the Brauhaus table when you want your Kölsch flow to stop (this is the universally-respected Cologne signal); book Rhine cruises only through **KD (Köln-Düsseldorfer)** at kd.com or at the posted KD pier office; buy Weihnachtsmarkt entry never — entry is always free, so "skip-the-line entry" is by definition fraud; and call **110** or walk to the **Polizeiinspektion 1** at Walter-Pauli-Ring 2 — seven minutes from the Dom. Bundespolizei Köln Hauptbahnhof service line: **+49 221 16095-0**.
