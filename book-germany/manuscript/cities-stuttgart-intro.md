Stuttgart is the Swabian capital — home of Mercedes-Benz, Porsche, the state parliament, and the *Cannstatter Volksfest* (Stuttgart's Oktoberfest analogue, running every late September into early October on the Cannstatter Wasen). The scam ecosystem is narrower than Berlin's or Munich's but concentrates on three corridors: the **Königstraße pedestrian shopping axis** from Hauptbahnhof to the Schlossplatz, **Stuttgart Hauptbahnhof** itself, and the **Cannstatter Volksfest** during its sixteen-day festival run. *Stuttgarter Zeitung* and *Stuttgarter Nachrichten* cover seasonal variants.

The **Stuttgart Hauptbahnhof and Königstraße approach scams** are the signature Stuttgart pattern. The station's current underground reconstruction (*Stuttgart 21*) has produced a busy, confusing platform and concourse environment that favors the sob-story cash ask and the yellow-vest ticket-machine helper. Down the Königstraße pedestrian spine — one of the longest pedestrian shopping axes in Europe — clipboard distractions and "free hug" bracelet approaches recur daily. Losses cluster at €50–€400. The Stuttgart Polizei logs a seasonal spike during Cannstatter Volksfest and the IAA auto show.

The risk radiates out in three corridors. The **Cannstatter Volksfest bill-shock and ticket fraud** runs Munich's Oktoberfest script with Swabian variations: the *Besen* (broomstick) family-friendly tents have fewer bill-shocks than the big Dinkelacker and Schwabenbräu marquees, where "shared table" invites convert to €80–€150 surprise bills at the end of an evening. The **STR Airport taxi overcharge** with Bolt/Uber confusion targets business travelers landing at Stuttgart — "Uber is not available in Stuttgart" is usually true, so flat-rate touts step in at €60+ when the S-Bahn S2/S3 is €4.20 in 27 minutes. The **Markthalle "Maultaschen by weight" scale overcharge** at the stone-vaulted indoor market tips scales to favor the vendor on tourist-language interactions.

A safe default: take the S-Bahn S2 or S3 from STR to Hauptbahnhof for €4.20; keep phones front-pocketed on the Königstraße shopping spine; at Cannstatter Volksfest stick to posted-price family *Besen* rather than the marquee tents if you want predictable billing; for Maultaschen and Swabian gifts, buy from stalls with posted per-kilo signs; and call **110** or visit the **Polizeirevier 1** at Theodor-Heuss-Straße 11 — six minutes from Hauptbahnhof.
