# Appendix C: Emergency Contacts {-}

Every number in this appendix has been verified against the agency's published 2026 directory. Numbers drift between editions; if one does not connect, dial **115** (the German federal government-services line, 08:00–18:00 Mon–Fri) and ask for the correct current contact.

## Nationwide emergency numbers

- **110** — *Polizei* (police, 24/7, all states, English-capable dispatchers in major cities)
- **112** — *Rettungsdienst / Feuerwehr* (ambulance / fire, pan-European, 24/7, English-capable)
- **115** — *Behördennummer* (general government services, Mon–Fri 08:00–18:00, English on request)
- **116 117** — *Ärztlicher Bereitschaftsdienst* (non-emergency medical on-call, 24/7)
- **0800 1110 111** — *Telefonseelsorge* (free crisis counseling, English available, 24/7)
- **+49 800 6 888 000** — *Bundespolizei* service line (federal police — airports, trains, borders)

## Consumer-protection agencies

- **Verbraucherzentrale** (federal hub): **verbraucherzentrale.de** — file online in English
- **BaFin** (Federal Financial Supervisory Authority) — **bafin.de**, for banking / payment disputes
- **Ordnungsamt** (local regulatory office) — file transport complaints with the city's Ordnungsamt; search "Ordnungsamt" plus the city name
