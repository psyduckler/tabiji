Füssen is the Romantic Road finale and the base for Bavaria's two most-visited castles — **Neuschwanstein** and **Hohenschwangau**. The town itself is small; the scam ecosystem is entirely organized around the castle-access economy in the Hohenschwangau village two kilometers east. Risk concentrates on three corridors: the **Hohenschwangau ticket office** (the single *Ticketcenter* that serves both castles), the **Füssen Altstadt tourist restaurants** on Reichenstraße, and the **Neuschwanstein shuttle / horse-carriage uphill transport**. *Allgäuer Zeitung* and *Kreisbote* cover the seasonal patterns; the Bavarian Palace Administration publishes ticket-scam warnings every tourist season.

The **Neuschwanstein skip-the-line ticket resale** is the signature Füssen scam. Third-party resellers — domains like neuschwanstein-tickets.eu, bavarian-castle-skipline.com, and similar — sell €65 "skip-the-line" slots for a €23 castle ticket that must be issued through the official Ticketcenter Hohenschwangau. The resold QR codes either duplicate legitimate slots or don't scan at the castle gate (Neuschwanstein is admission by timed entry only — there is no skip-the-line anywhere, by Bavarian Palace Administration policy). Losses run €40–€200 per victim per attempted visit. The BPA has run English-language newspaper warnings in *Der Spiegel*, *Süddeutsche Zeitung*, and *The Local Germany* every summer since 2022.

The risk radiates out in three corridors. The **Munich-to-Füssen day-trip package** sold by Munich sidewalk agencies quotes €89–€139 per person for a trip that includes €44 of BayernTicket train fare (which covers five people for €44 total) plus a guided walk that doesn't add value over the audio guide. The **Hohenschwangau horse-carriage uphill** to Neuschwanstein charges €10 per person with no posted return pricing, and "premium" carriage upsells run €20–€40 per person. The **Füssen Altstadt tourist-menu restaurant** quotes verbal "around €20" Weißwurstfrühstück prices that bill at €35–€45.

A safe default: book Neuschwanstein and Hohenschwangau tickets only at **hohenschwangau.de** — the official Bavarian Palace Administration portal — or at the Ticketcenter itself on arrival; for transport from Munich, buy a **BayernTicket** at the Munich Hauptbahnhof DB Reisezentrum (€29 solo, €44 up to 5 people) and take RB to Füssen + bus 73/78 — €7 cheaper than any "day tour"; decline horse-carriage upsells (the uphill walk is 30 minutes and included free); eat on the Schrannenplatz or Franziskanerplatz side streets rather than the Reichenstraße tourist strip; and call **110** or visit the **Polizeiinspektion Füssen** at Rupprechtstraße 12 — five minutes from the Altstadt.
