⚡ Before You Go — Essentials

🛂 Visa

Most nationalities can get an e-visa (25 USD, single entry, 30 days) online. Apply at least 5 business days before travel.

💵 Currency

Vietnamese Dong (VND). 1 USD ≈ 25,000 VND. ATMs widely available; carry cash for street food and markets.

🌦️ Weather

June is hot and humid (30–35°C) with occasional rain. Pack light clothes, a rain jacket, and sunscreen.

🚗 Getting Around

Domestic flights between cities (VietJet, Bamboo). Grab app for taxis. Trains on the Reunification Express.

📱 Connectivity

Buy a Viettel or Mobifone SIM at the airport (5–10 USD for 30 days, 4G). Wi-Fi at most hotels and cafes.

🏥 Health

Hep A/B and Typhoid recommended. Drink bottled water only. Travel insurance strongly recommended.

Day 1 Hanoi · Old Quarter · Hoan Kiem

Afternoon

Arrive in Hanoi

Check into your hotel in the Old Quarter. Take a slow walk around Hoan Kiem Lake as jet lag settles.

Noi Bai Airport → Old Quarter: ~45 min by Grab (~200,000 VND)
Evening

Old Quarter Walking Tour

Wander the 36 ancient streets, each named after the goods once sold there. Soak in the energy of motorbikes, vendors, and lantern-lit alleys.

🍜 Dinner
Phở Thìn Bờ Hồ
Legendary beef pho with a rich, slightly charred broth — a Hanoi institution.
13 Lò Đúc · ~50,000 VND

⚡ Power & Water

  • Type A/C plugs, 220V — bring a universal adapter
  • Never drink tap water — bottled water is 5,000–10,000 VND everywhere

🗣️ Language & Communication

  • Vietnamese is the official language
  • English spoken in tourist areas but limited elsewhere
  • Download Google Translate offline Vietnamese pack

💰 Money Tips

  • Tipping not expected but appreciated — round up at restaurants
  • 50,000–100,000 VND for guides/drivers per day
  • ATMs widely available — Vietcombank and BIDV have lowest fees
  • Max withdrawal usually 2–5 million VND

🛡️ Safety & Etiquette

  • Vietnam is very safe for tourists
  • Watch for petty theft in crowded areas
  • Crossing streets: walk steadily and motorbikes go around you
  • Ask permission before photographing people
  • No photos at military installations
