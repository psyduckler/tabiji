A 13-Day First-Timer Japan Adventure: Tokyo neon, Hakone onsen, Kyoto temples, Osaka street food, and one perfect Nara day

A balanced Japan route for two travelers who want adventure, food, and nightlife without missing the classics: Tokyo for scale and energy, Hakone for a soft onsen reset, Kyoto for atmosphere, Osaka for food and nights out, and Nara for a final cultural day trip.

Duration: 12 nights
Dates: Sep 15 – Sep 27, 2026
Budget: $$–$$$
Pace: Balanced adventure
Best for: First-time Japan travelers who like food, neighborhoods, and nights out

⚡ Before You Go — Essentials

☔ September weather

Expect warm humid days, possible rain, and occasional typhoon disruption. Pack breathable clothes, a compact umbrella, and keep one evening flexible.

🚄 Transport

Use IC cards for local transit and reserve Shinkansen seats for Tokyo → Odawara/Kyoto and Kyoto/Osaka transfers. Luggage forwarding makes this route much easier.

♨️ Ryokan etiquette

For Hakone, book a ryokan with dinner and breakfast included. Check tattoo policies if relevant and learn basic onsen etiquette before arrival.

🍜 Food strategy

Do not overbook restaurants. Mix one or two reservations with spontaneous markets, ramen, izakayas, and department-store food halls.

Day 1 Shinjuku · Golden Gai

Arrival in Tokyo & Neon First Night

Arrive, settle into Tokyo, and keep the first evening easy but memorable in Shinjuku.

Afternoon

Arrive and check in

Transfer from Haneda or Narita, pick up an IC card/mobile Suica setup, and settle near Shinjuku, Ginza, or Tokyo Station.

Grab konbini supplies for breakfast and jet lag.
Evening

Omoide Yokocho and Golden Gai

Eat yakitori in Omoide Yokocho, then choose one small Golden Gai bar if energy allows.

Avoid touts and keep the night short if jet lag hits.
🍢 Dinner
Omoide Yokocho
Tiny grilled-skewer shops and first-night highballs.
$$ · Casual
Day 2 Shibuya · Harajuku · Omotesando

Shrines, Street Fashion & Shibuya Nightlife

A west-side Tokyo day that moves from quiet forest shrine to maximum city energy.

Morning

Meiji Jingu and Yoyogi Park

Start with the forested shrine approach before Harajuku wakes up fully.

Go early for softer light and fewer crowds.
Afternoon

Harajuku, Cat Street and Omotesando

Browse Takeshita-dori, backstreets, design shops, cafés, and streetwear.

Use cafés as cooling breaks.
☕ Lunch
Harajuku or Omotesando café
Pancakes, sandos, curry, or stylish café plates.
$$
Evening

Shibuya Scramble, Nonbei Yokocho and rooftop view

See the crossing, book Shibuya Sky if weather is clear, then drink or snack around Nonbei Yokocho.

Reserve Shibuya Sky sunset ahead if this matters.
🍻 Dinner
Shibuya izakaya
Shared plates, grilled fish, karaage, and drinks.
$$
Day 3 Tsukiji · Ginza · TeamLab

Market Breakfast, Ginza & Digital Art

Food-first Tokyo morning, polished Ginza afternoon, immersive art and waterfront views at night.

Morning

Tsukiji Outer Market breakfast

Graze sushi, tamagoyaki, grilled seafood, onigiri, and coffee at the outer market.

Go before 10am for the best food energy.
🍣 Breakfast
Tsukiji Outer Market
Fresh seafood and street snacks.
$$
Afternoon

Ginza shopping and depachika food halls

Explore flagship stores and department basement food halls for sweets and gifts.

Great place for air-conditioned breaks.
Evening

teamLab Planets and Toyosu waterfront

Book teamLab ahead, then eat around Toyosu or return to Ginza/Tsukiji.

Wear clothes that work for mirrored/water installations.
🍜 Dinner
Ginza ramen or Toyosu sushi
Choose polished ramen, sushi, or izakaya depending on appetite.
$$–$$$
Day 4 Asakusa · Ueno · Akihabara

Old Tokyo to Electric Town

Classic Tokyo temples and snacks, museum/market time, then Akihabara games and anime.

Morning

Senso-ji and Nakamise-dori

Visit Tokyo’s oldest temple and snack through the approach street.

Go early for Kaminarimon photos.
Afternoon

Ueno Park and Ameyoko

Choose a museum or simply wander the park, then snack through Ameyoko market.

Tokyo National Museum is excellent if you want depth.
🍡 Lunch
Ameyoko Market
Casual market food, fruit, skewers, and seafood.
$
Evening

Akihabara arcades and shops

Play rhythm games, browse retro games, anime goods, electronics, and gacha.

Compare prices on collectibles before buying.
🍛 Dinner
Akihabara curry or ramen
Easy casual dinner before heading back.
$$
Day 5 Hakone · Lake Ashi · Gora

Hakone Onsen Reset

Leave the city for mountains, volcanic valleys, lake views, and a ryokan dinner.

Morning

Train to Hakone and Open-Air Museum

Travel via Odawara/Hakone-Yumoto, drop bags, and visit the Hakone Open-Air Museum.

Consider the Hakone Free Pass.
Afternoon

Ropeway, Owakudani and Lake Ashi

Ride the ropeway over volcanic vents, try black eggs, and cruise Lake Ashi if weather cooperates.

Fuji views are weather-dependent; enjoy the loop regardless.
Evening

Ryokan onsen and kaiseki dinner

Check into a ryokan, soak, wear yukata, and enjoy a multi-course dinner.

Follow onsen washing etiquette before entering baths.
🍱 Dinner
Ryokan kaiseki
Seasonal multi-course dinner at your inn.
$$$ · Book ahead
Day 6 Hakone · Kyoto · Gion

Hakone Morning to Kyoto Lanterns

Wake slowly in Hakone, then ride onward to Kyoto for an atmospheric first evening.

Morning

Final onsen and breakfast

Enjoy ryokan breakfast and one last bath before checkout.

Forward luggage if you want an easier transfer.
Afternoon

Shinkansen to Kyoto

Travel via Odawara to Kyoto and check in near Kawaramachi, Gion, or Kyoto Station.

Reserve seats and keep snacks handy.
Evening

Gion and Pontocho walk

Stroll Yasaka Shrine, Hanamikoji, and Pontocho as lanterns come on.

Be respectful around geiko/maiko; no chasing photos.
🍶 Dinner
Pontocho Alley
Kyoto small plates and sake by the Kamo River.
$$–$$$
Day 7 Fushimi Inari · Higashiyama

Torii Gates & Kiyomizu Sunset

Kyoto icons done with smart timing: early shrine, slow temple streets, golden-hour views.

Morning

Fushimi Inari early

Walk through the torii gates before the heaviest crowd arrives.

Continue uphill for quieter sections.
Afternoon

Higashiyama lanes

Explore Sannenzaka, Ninenzaka, tea shops, ceramics, and Yasaka Pagoda.

Great souvenir area but very crowded midday.
🍵 Snack
Matcha café in Higashiyama
Tea, wagashi, or parfait break.
$$
Evening

Kiyomizu-dera and Yasaka Shrine

Time Kiyomizu for late light, then walk back toward Gion after dark.

Check temple closing hours closer to travel.
🍜 Dinner
Kawaramachi ramen or obanzai
Casual Kyoto dinner after a long walking day.
$$
Day 8 Arashiyama · Nishiki · Kiyamachi

Bamboo, River Views & Kyoto Kitchen

Western Kyoto scenery in the morning, market grazing in the afternoon, and casual bars at night.

Morning

Arashiyama Bamboo Grove and Tenryu-ji

Start early at the bamboo grove, then Tenryu-ji garden and Togetsukyo Bridge.

If crowds are intense, walk toward quieter temples.
Afternoon

Nishiki Market tasting route

Return central and snack through Kyoto’s kitchen: yuba, pickles, tamagoyaki, sweets, and tea.

Many stalls close by late afternoon.
🍡 Lunch
Nishiki Market
Grazing lunch across market stalls.
$–$$
Evening

Kiyamachi and cocktail bars

Try a relaxed Kyoto night around Kiyamachi or a polished cocktail bar.

Kyoto nightlife is quieter; lean into it.
🍽️ Dinner
Obanzai restaurant
Kyoto home-style small dishes.
$$–$$$
Day 9 Nara · Osaka · Dotonbori

Nara Deer & Osaka Neon Arrival

Day-trip through Nara’s deer park and Great Buddha, then roll straight into Osaka’s food capital energy.

Morning

Nara Park and Todai-ji

Train to Nara, feed deer carefully, and visit the Great Buddha Hall.

Keep maps and snacks away from deer.
Afternoon

Kasuga Taisha then Osaka check-in

Walk the lantern shrine paths, then travel to Osaka and check in near Namba or Umeda.

Nara to Osaka is easy by Kintetsu/JR.
🍣 Lunch
Nara local snacks
Kakinoha-zushi, mochi, or soba near the park/station.
$$
Evening

Dotonbori food crawl

Glico sign, canal lights, takoyaki, okonomiyaki, gyoza, and people-watching.

Order small and share to try more.
🐙 Dinner
Dotonbori crawl
Osaka street-food greatest hits.
$–$$
Day 10 Osaka Castle · Umeda · Tsuruhashi

Osaka Castle, Skyline & Yakiniku Night

Modern Osaka, a little history, big city views, and a food-heavy night in Koreatown.

Morning

Osaka Castle Park

Walk the castle grounds and museum area before the day heats up.

The exterior/park often matters more than the museum if time is tight.
Afternoon

Umeda Sky Building and department stores

See the city from the Floating Garden Observatory, then explore Osaka Station’s food halls.

Great indoor option if it rains.
Evening

Tsuruhashi yakiniku

Head to Osaka’s Koreatown for grilled beef, kimchi, smoke, and a very different night-food vibe.

Reserve if choosing a famous spot.
🥩 Dinner
Tsuruhashi yakiniku
Grill-it-yourself beef and Korean-Japanese sides.
$$–$$$
Day 11 Kuromon · Den Den Town · Shinsekai

Markets, Anime Shops & Retro Shinsekai

A very Osaka day: market breakfast, otaku shopping, retro tower streets, and kushikatsu.

Morning

Kuromon Ichiba Market

Graze seafood, fruit, wagyu skewers, tamagoyaki, and coffee.

Earlier is calmer and fresher.
🍣 Breakfast
Kuromon Ichiba
Market grazing breakfast.
$$
Afternoon

Den Den Town and Nipponbashi

Browse games, anime, figures, electronics, and hobby shops.

Good backup for Akihabara-style shopping with Osaka prices.
Evening

Shinsekai and Tsutenkaku

Retro arcades, tower lights, kushikatsu, and old-school Osaka atmosphere.

No double-dipping if communal sauce appears.
🍢 Dinner
Shinsekai kushikatsu
Deep-fried skewers, doteyaki, beer, and retro dining rooms.
$–$$
Day 12 Himeji · Kobe · Osaka

Choose-Your-Adventure Final Full Day

Either go big with Himeji Castle and Kobe beef, or keep it relaxed in Osaka. The main route uses Himeji/Kobe because it is memorable and efficient.

Morning

Himeji Castle day trip

Take the train to Himeji and tour Japan’s most spectacular original castle.

Start early; the castle deserves unhurried time.
Afternoon

Kobe harbor and beef lunch/early dinner

Continue to Kobe for harbor views and a beef meal before returning to Osaka.

Reserve a reputable Kobe beef restaurant if this is a priority.
🥩 Lunch
Kobe beef restaurant
Splurge meal in Kobe, or choose a casual steak lunch if budget-conscious.
$$$
Evening

Final Osaka drinks

Return to Osaka for one last Namba or Umeda night, depending on hotel location.

Pack before going out so departure morning is painless.
🍻 Dinner
Namba farewell izakaya
Final shared plates and drinks.
$$
Day 13 Namba · Kansai Airport

Final Breakfast & Departure

A simple final day: breakfast, souvenirs, airport transfer, and one last konbini run.

Morning

Slow breakfast and souvenir sweep

Pick up snacks, KitKats, tea, stationery, or last-minute gifts around Namba or Osaka Station.

Check tax-free packing rules before sealing luggage.
☕ Breakfast
Japanese café or konbini picnic
Keep it easy and close to your hotel.
$
Afternoon

Transfer to airport

Take Nankai from Namba to KIX, JR from Osaka, or Shinkansen back to Tokyo if your flight departs there.

Leave more buffer than you think, especially with luggage.
