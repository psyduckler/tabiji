Toyokoro & the Tokachi Heartland: Autumn in Hokkaido's pastoral frontier — dairy farms, wild onsen, marshlands & fall foliage

An 11-day journey through eastern Hokkaido's Tokachi region and beyond. Start in Obihiro with legendary butadon and artisan cheese, venture into the highland lakes for onsen soaking, explore Kushiro's pristine marshlands and Ainu culture, chase autumn colors through Furano and Biei, and finish in Asahikawa with one of Japan's best zoos and iconic ramen.

Duration: 11 days
Dates: Sep 30 – Oct 10, 2026
Budget: $2,000–5,000 for 2 people
Pace: Relaxed with active day-trips

⚡ Before You Go — Essentials

✈️ Getting There

Fly into Tokachi-Obihiro Airport (OBO) from Tokyo Haneda (~1h40m). Rent a car at the airport — essential for rural Hokkaido.

🚗 Getting Around

A rental car is mandatory. Roads are well-maintained and scenic. Reserve early for autumn season. International driving permit required.

🍂 Season

Late September to early October is peak autumn in Tokachi. Expect daytime highs of 15-20°C and crisp mornings around 5-8°C. Leaves peak mid-October at lower elevations.

💴 Money

Rural Hokkaido is still heavily cash-based. ATMs at convenience stores (7-Eleven, Lawson) accept international cards. Carry ¥20,000+ at all times.

📱 Connectivity

Get a pocket Wi-Fi or eSIM before arrival. Cell coverage can be spotty in mountain areas around Shikaribetsu and Nukabira.

♨️ Onsen Etiquette

Wash thoroughly before entering the bath. No swimsuits. Tattoo-friendly onsen exist but call ahead. Small towel OK on your head, not in the water.

Day 1 Obihiro · Arrival Day

Afternoon

Arrive at Tokachi-Obihiro Airport

Pick up your rental car and drive into Obihiro city (20 min). Check into your hotel and stretch your legs after the flight.

Obihiro Butadon Street

Explore Obihiro's signature dish — charcoal-grilled pork rice bowls. Pancho and Ippei are the iconic rivals; lines are worth it.

Lunch/Dinner
Pancho (ぱんちょう)
The original butadon shop since 1933. Thick-cut pork glazed in sweet soy over rice.
¥1,100 · Cash only
Evening

Obihiro Yatai Village

A cluster of tiny izakaya stalls in the city center. Great for local craft beer, grilled Tokachi beef, and chatting with locals.

Day 2 Tokachi · Dairy & Sweets

Morning

Tokachi Hills (十勝ヒルズ)

A beautiful garden complex overlooking the Tokachi Plains. Walk through herb gardens and flower fields with early autumn colors starting to show.

Breakfast
Hotel Breakfast or Convenience Store
Grab fresh milk and onigiri from a local Seico Mart — Hokkaido's beloved convenience chain.
¥500–800
Afternoon

Rokkatei Garden & Factory

Tour the famous Rokkatei confectionery's flagship garden in Nakasakaecho. Free tastings of their Marusei butter sandwich and seasonal sweets.

Tokachi Wine & Cheese

Visit Ikeda Wine Castle (ワイン城) for local Tokachi wine tasting and panoramic views from the rooftop. Pair with artisan cheese from nearby farms.

Lunch
Ikeda Wine Castle Restaurant
Local wine paired with Tokachi beef steak or cheese plates.
¥1,500–2,500
Day 3 Toyokoro · Coastal Village

Morning

Toyokoro Town Exploration

Drive to the coast and explore Toyokoro, a small fishing village on the Pacific. Visit the Harappa roadside station for fresh local produce and seafood.

Toyokoro Jewelry Ice Beach

While the famous ice phenomenon is winter-only, the Otsu coast is stunning year-round. Walk the wild Pacific beach and watch fishing boats return.

Breakfast
Michi no Eki Cosmo-ru Toyokoro
Roadside station with fresh local dairy products and baked goods.
¥600–1,000
Afternoon

Toyokoro Salmon Fishing Observation

In early October, watch salmon run up the Toshibetsu River. Locals gather to watch the spawning — a raw, powerful natural spectacle unique to Hokkaido.

Lunch
Local Seafood at Toyokoro Port
Fresh shrimp, hokki clams, and grilled fish at a portside eatery.
¥1,200–2,000
Day 4 Lake Shikaribetsu · Highland Retreat

Morning

Drive to Lake Shikaribetsu

Head north through golden larch forests into the Daisetsuzan highlands. Lake Shikaribetsu (然別湖) is Hokkaido's highest-elevation natural lake at 810m.

Breakfast
Pack from Obihiro bakery
Grab pastries and coffee before the mountain drive.
¥500–800
Afternoon

Lake Shikaribetsu Nature Cruise

Take a sightseeing boat across the crystal-clear lake surrounded by virgin forest. In early October, the surrounding mountains blaze with red and gold.

Shikaribetsu Nature Center

Learn about the endemic Miyabe charr fish and the lake's unique ecosystem. Short hiking trails loop through the lakeshore forest.

Lunch
Shikaribetsu Lakeside Café
Simple but satisfying curry rice or soba with lake views.
¥900–1,200
Day 5 Nukabira · Onsen & Forest

Morning

Nukabira Onsen Village

Soak in one of Nukabira's forest onsen. Nakamuraya is a classic ryokan with outdoor baths overlooking the Nukabira River gorge. The autumn foliage reflecting in the water is unforgettable.

Breakfast
Ryokan Breakfast
Traditional Japanese breakfast at your onsen inn — grilled fish, miso soup, pickles, rice, and fresh Tokachi milk.
Included with stay
Afternoon

Taushubetsu Bridge Viewpoint

The "phantom bridge" — a 1930s concrete arch bridge that appears and disappears with the dam water level. October is often a good time to see partial arches. Reserve a guided tour in advance.

Forest Railway Ruins Walk

Walk sections of the abandoned Shihoro Line railway through the forest. The old tracks and tunnels are atmospheric and photogenic.

Lunch
Nukabira Lodge Cafe
Venison curry or Tokachi pork katsu at a mountain lodge.
¥1,000–1,500
Day 6 Kushiro · Marshlands

Morning

Drive to Kushiro

Head southeast through the Tokachi countryside to Kushiro (about 2.5 hours). The drive itself passes through rolling farmland and forests turning gold.

Kushiro Shitsugen (Marshlands)

Japan's largest wetland. Start at the Hosooka Observatory for panoramic views over the endless marsh. Watch for red-crowned cranes (tancho) — they're here year-round.

Lunch
Kushiro Washo Market
Build your own seafood bowl (katte-don) at Washo Market — buy rice, then walk to different stalls to top it with salmon, crab, uni, and ikura.
¥1,500–3,000
Afternoon

Kushiro Marsh Canoe Trip

Paddle down the Kushiro River through the wetlands. The 2-hour guided canoe tour passes through reed beds alive with birds. Serene and unique to Hokkaido.

Day 7 Akan · Lake & Ainu Culture

Morning

Lake Akan & Marimo Exhibition Center

Drive to Lake Akan (1.5 hours from Kushiro). See the rare marimo — spherical moss balls found only here and in Iceland. The exhibition center has live underwater displays.

Breakfast
Hotel in Kushiro
Western or Japanese breakfast before the drive.
Included or ¥800
Afternoon

Ainu Kotan Village (Lake Akan Ainu Theater Ikor)

Visit the Ainu cultural village on the lakeshore. Watch traditional dance performances (UNESCO Intangible Heritage), browse woodcarving workshops, and learn about Hokkaido's indigenous culture.

Lake Akan Onsen

End the day soaking in an onsen overlooking the lake. Many hotels offer day-use bathing with stunning autumn views.

Lunch
Ainu Cuisine Experience
Try ohaw (traditional Ainu stew) and venison dishes at a restaurant in Ainu Kotan.
¥1,200–2,000
Day 8 Furano · Autumn Colors

Morning

Drive to Furano

A long but scenic drive from Akan to Furano (about 4 hours via Daisetsu). Consider stopping at Sounkyo Gorge for a break — the canyon walls turn brilliant red in October.

Breakfast
Roadside Stop
Grab onigiri and coffee at a Seicomart along the way.
¥400–600
Afternoon

Furano Winery & Cheese Factory

Sample local Furano wine (made from grapes grown in this unlikely northern latitude) and taste fresh cheese and butter at the adjacent factory. Great views of the Daisetsuzan range.

Ningle Terrace

A charming cluster of tiny log-cabin craft shops in the forest behind New Furano Prince Hotel. Browse handmade candles, glass art, and leather goods under the trees.

Lunch
Furano Curry & Omelette
Furano is famous for its omu-curry (omelette curry) made with local eggs and vegetables.
¥900–1,300
Day 9 Biei · Patchwork Hills

Morning

Biei Patchwork Road

Drive the famous "Patchwork Road" — a rolling landscape of colorful farm fields stitched together like a quilt. In autumn, harvested fields mix gold, brown, and green. Stop at iconic trees: Ken & Mary, Seven Star, Mild Seven Hill.

Breakfast
Bi.ble Bakery (Biei)
Artisan bakery using Biei-grown wheat. Crusty bread, croissants, and coffee.
¥500–900
Afternoon

Shirogane Blue Pond (青い池)

The famous electric-blue pond created by volcanic mineral runoff. Surrounded by silver birch trees turning gold in October — the contrast is extraordinary.

Shirahige Waterfall

A short walk from the Blue Pond, this waterfall cascades from underground springs through the rock face into the turquoise Biei River below.

Lunch
Biei Farm Restaurant
Farm-to-table meal with local corn, potato, and pork in a hilltop restaurant.
¥1,200–1,800
Day 10 Asahikawa · Zoo & Ramen

Morning

Asahiyama Zoo

Japan's most innovative zoo. Famous for behavioral exhibits — watch penguins walk through tunnels beneath you, polar bears swim overhead, and red pandas navigate aerial bridges. Arrive at opening (9:30 AM) to avoid crowds.

Breakfast
Hotel in Asahikawa
Fuel up before a full morning at the zoo.
¥800–1,200
Afternoon

Asahikawa Ramen Village (あさひかわラーメン村)

Eight competing ramen shops under one roof, all serving Asahikawa's signature soy-based double-broth style. Try Aoba or Tenkin for the richest bowls.

Lunch
Asahikawa Ramen (Aoba or Tenkin)
Asahikawa-style shoyu ramen with pork and seafood double broth. Rich, savory, and perfect for autumn.
¥850–1,100
Day 11 Asahikawa · Departure

Morning

Asahikawa Morning Market & Souvenir Shopping

Browse the morning market for last-minute Hokkaido gifts: Rokkatei sweets, Furano jam, lavender goods, and carved wooden bears. Asahikawa is also known for its wood furniture craft.

Depart from Asahikawa Airport

Return your rental car at Asahikawa Airport (AKJ) and fly to Tokyo Haneda or Narita (~1h45m). Alternatively, take the JR limited express to Sapporo (1h25m) for a New Chitose connection.

Breakfast
Café at Asahikawa Station
Last Hokkaido coffee and pastry before departure.
¥500–800

🚗 Rental Car

  • Book through Toyota Rent a Car or Times Car Rental at Obihiro Airport. Reserve at least 1 month ahead for autumn.
  • An ETC card for highway tolls is useful but not essential — most driving is on free roads.
  • International driving permit (IDP) required for non-Japanese licenses.

🏨 Accommodation

  • Mix of business hotels in cities (Obihiro, Kushiro, Asahikawa) at ¥5,000–8,000/night.
  • Onsen ryokan at highland lakes run ¥12,000–20,000/night with meals included.
  • Book ryokan early — autumn weekends fill fast.

🗣️ Language

  • Very little English is spoken in rural Tokachi.
  • Google Translate camera mode is invaluable for menus and signs.
  • Download Japanese offline for Google Maps and Translate before arriving.

🐻 Wildlife Safety

  • Brown bears (higuma) are active in Hokkaido forests, especially autumn.
  • Stick to marked trails, carry a bear bell, and never leave food outside.
  • Watch for deer on roads at dusk — they are common.

🌡️ Weather & Packing

  • Late September–early October: 15–20°C days, 5–10°C nights.
  • Pack layers, a light waterproof jacket, and comfortable walking shoes.
  • First frost possible at higher elevations around Shikaribetsu.

🚃 JR Hokkaido

  • The JR Hokkaido rail pass covers limited express trains between major cities.
  • Bus/train service to Lake Shikaribetsu, Nukabira, and Biei is infrequent — a car is strongly recommended.
