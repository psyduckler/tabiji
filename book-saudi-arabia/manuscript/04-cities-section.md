# The Scams: City by City {-}

The chapters that follow document sixty-four scams reported across ten Saudi cities. Cities are ordered by the way a first-time visitor tends to move through the Kingdom — starting in the capital, Riyadh, then west to the Red Sea at Jeddah and on to the holy cities of Mecca and Medina and the rose-and-mountain town of Taif, north to the Nabataean desert at AlUla and Tabuk, south to the green Asir highlands at Abha, and finally east to the Gulf coast at Dammam and Al Khobar. The full reading order is: **Riyadh, Jeddah, Mecca, Medina, Taif, AlUla, Tabuk, Abha, Dammam, and Al Khobar.**

If you are traveling to only a few of these cities, skip directly to the relevant chapters. If you are visiting more, skim them all — many scams (the broken-meter taxi at the airport, the phantom rental-car scratch, the fake gold or oud or honey at the souq, the third-party ticket markup, and the WhatsApp "confirm your card" message before you have even landed) repeat from city to city with local variations. Knowing the pattern in Riyadh makes you faster to spot it in Jeddah or Dammam, and the financial-not-physical rule from the opening chapter holds in every one of them: guard your wallet, not your safety.
