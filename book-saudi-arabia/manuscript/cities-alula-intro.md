AlUla is a tourist trap of a single kind: six documented scams, all in the tourist-trap category, every one of them organized around the gap between AlUla's one official seller — the Royal Commission's experiencealula.com — and the resellers, packagers, and freelancers who price the same product two and three times higher. The Lookalike Hegra Ticket Reseller Markup turns a roughly 95-SAR official Hegra Day Tour, the same coach and the same Rawi storyteller, into 250-to-300 SAR sold through Booking, GetYourGuide, or Klook. The Madain Saleh All-Inclusive Package Triple-Markup wraps permits, transfers, guide, and a night or two into one convenient round number that a long-running TripAdvisor review says rose "by almost 3 times for the same trip" after the site won UNESCO status — a SR3,000 two-person trip quoted at SR6,500-7,000. The No-Show Rawi and Vanishing Refund is the high-severity version: one reviewer paid 250 euros for a private Land Rover tour, no guide appeared, and a month of refund emails went unanswered.

The risk is concentrated at two chokepoints — the airport and the ticket window. AlUla International (ULH) sits some 35 kilometers from the Ashar Valley resorts and Old Town, and because Careem and Uber barely function here the AlUla Airport Transfer Gouge thrives: travelers report being quoted around 1,000 SAR for a round-trip airport-to-hotel transfer and roughly 650 SAR for a hotel-to-Winter Park round trip, figures visitors flatly call "extortionate" against an honest single pickup near 80 SAR. The rental desks at ULH — Lumi, Budget, Yelo — run the Rental-Car Phantom Damage Charge, pointing at a scuff at drop-off and billing for it, in one case inventing an "accident" an hour after a clean return. The Hegra Welcome Center and Winter Park ticket office are where the reseller markup lands in person, and the Booking.com 'Confirm Your Card' Phishing Trap reaches even the $2,000-a-night Banyan Tree tented villas by message, email, and WhatsApp, pre-filled with your real name, property, and dates.

A safe default for first-timers: buy every Hegra ticket, permit, and tour only at tickets.experiencealula.com and screenshot the roughly 95-SAR official price before you compare anything elsewhere — that one habit defeats the reseller markup, the triple-markup package, and most of the no-show risk in one move. For the airport, agree a flat pickup rate with your hotel before you fly or install the AlUla Taxi (Experience AlUla) app rather than taking the concierge's first number, and if you rent, film a dated full walkaround of the car and fuel gauge before driving off. Never re-enter card details from a link in any booking message; call the property's official number to verify a "payment problem."
