Abha's scams run on altitude, crowds, and the summer festival. Seven documented scams, all tourist-trap category, one rated high-severity, and most of them feed on the June-to-August Aseer Season crush when the "Sound of Abha" festival alone is projected to draw between 900,000 and 1.4 million visitors over 91 days. The high-severity pull is the Aseer Season Chalet & Istiraha Booking Fraud: a gorgeous mountain chalet near Al Soudah priced below every hotel in town, a "hold it" bank transfer or app payment, and an arrival address that turns out to be a vacant lot. The signature daytime trap is the Cable Car "Per Head" vs "Whole Cabin" Switch, where Abha's three teleferiks blur a per-person fare of roughly SAR 50-90 against a "private cabin" rate, and the busy summer operator quotes whichever nets more — one family expecting SAR 80 a head was told their party of three was SAR 550. The Revolving-Restaurant "Surprise Bill" completes the trio: a reviewer at the Assalam Palace Hotel's 10th-floor revolving restaurant was refused a drinks menu and then billed SAR 450 for three after a poor buffet.

The risk concentrates on the escarpment viewpoints and the mountain roads that connect them. Abha International (AHB) and the ranks near Abha Dam Lake, Al-Muftaha, and the High City viewpoints carry the Abha Airport & Mountain-Road Off-App Taxi Overcharge, where an honest SAR 50-80 metered run into town becomes two or three times that from a tout — and on the twisting roads, with no meter and no GPS trail, you cannot argue once moving. Al Soudah, Abha Lake, High City, and the Jabal Thera lookouts are also where the low-severity Viewpoint Camera-Drop & Photo Tip plays out: a stranger hands you his camera, it "slips," and he turns aggressive over the damage. The Tuesday Market and the Al-Muftaha Arts Village stalls, plus roadside vendors toward Rijal Almaa, run the Fake "Pure Asir" Sidr Honey & Tourist-Grade Jambiya, cutting or mislabeling a honey that genuinely sells for SR350-500 per kilo. The rental desks at AHB and in the city carry the Rental-Car Pre-Existing Damage Charge on cars already battered by steep-road driving.

A safe default for first-timers: use Careem, which has the best Abha coverage, for every trip and expect SAR 50-80 from the airport into the center — waiting the ten or fifteen minutes for an app pickup defeats the off-app overcharge outright. Book any festival chalet only through a platform with buyer protection, at least four weeks ahead, and never by direct transfer or WhatsApp deposit, verifying the exact address on a map before paying. At the cable cars, ask out loud "per person or per whole cabin?" and pay only after the rate is stated for your exact party; demand a printed, priced menu before ordering anywhere with a view; and decline to hold any stranger's camera.
