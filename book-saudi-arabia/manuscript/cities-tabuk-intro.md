Tabuk's scam profile is dominated by one thing the rest of the Kingdom doesn't have: NEOM. Seven documented scams, all tourist-trap category, four of them rated high-severity, and the two heaviest both weaponize the world's biggest construction project. The Fake NEOM Job Offer & Recruitment-Fee Trap runs on LinkedIn, WhatsApp, and Telegram, demanding a "refundable" processing, medical, or training fee before a job that doesn't exist — Arab News documented a young applicant named Ammar scammed out of SR15,000 by fraudsters using the NEOM name, and the Saudi Central Bank formed a team to probe the wave. The NEOM Vendor-Tender & 'NEOM Crypto' Investment Fraud arrives as a "NEOM GIGA PROJECT VENDOR REGISTRATION" email from a lookalike domain or a social ad selling a "NEOM coin," both 419-style frauds that harvest company data or extract fake "fees and taxes." Layered on top is the Fake Saudi eVisa & Vacation-Rental Booking Site — portals like saudisevisa.com that took £172 for a visa that never came.

The on-the-ground risk concentrates at the airport, the wadi, and the souq. Prince Sultan bin Abdulaziz Airport (TUU) is only about 6 kilometers from the city, an honest taxi near $18, but the Tabuk Airport & Street-Taxi Overcharge intercepts arrivals before the official rank with a "broken meter" and a flat fare several times that. The drive to Wadi Al Disah hits the Wadi Al Disah Informal 4x4 'End of the Road' Markup: where the paved road ends roughly 10 kilometers short of the canyon at Disah village, local men in open-top 4x4s quote 300 SAR for about an hour, negotiable to 200. The rental desks in Tabuk and at AlUla airport carry the Tabuk / AlUla Rental-Car Damage & Deposit Trap, with one Lumi customer chased down post-return over parking-lot scratches and told the insurance was void without a police report. The lowest-stakes pull, the Fake Oud & Inflated Souvenir Markup, lives in the perfume shops, where weight manipulation and adulterated agarwood inflate one of the most expensive scents on earth.

A safe default for first-timers: treat every "NEOM" contact as a fraud until proven otherwise — apply for roles only at the official careers.neom.com portal, verify any procurement only through neom.com domains, and refuse to pay any fee, because legitimate employers cover visa and recruitment costs and NEOM has publicly stated it has no cryptocurrency. Get your tourist eVisa only at visa.visitsaudi.com and use Nusuk for any Hajj or Umrah booking, never a third-party agent. On arrival, order an Uber so the price is fixed before you confirm, and at Wadi Al Disah agree both price and duration before you get in the jeep.
