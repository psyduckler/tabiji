Portland's scam landscape is less about con artists than about property crime and a few opportunistic hustles, and a tourist car is the single biggest target. Of the seven scams documented here, the costliest cluster around vehicles: smash-and-grab break-ins (KOIN News logged 5,474 car thefts in 2024) and predatory towing both run high, and both feed on out-of-state plates and rental stickers that signal "tourist with luggage, gone for hours." The rest split between digital fraud — fake Airbnb listings and the Venmo and Cash App street grab — and street-level nuisance: aggressive panhandling, an organized bike-theft ring, and counterfeit "Portland-made" goods at the edges of Saturday Market.

The trouble concentrates in a handful of zones. Downtown garages, Pearl District street parking, and the isolated trailhead lots at Forest Park and Washington Park are where the vehicle break-in and smash-and-grab crews work, while predatory towing operators haunt private lots off Division Street, the Pearl, and near the Rose Quarter, with signage angled to be unreadable until you've already walked away. Old Town/Chinatown, Pioneer Courthouse Square, and the Alder Street food-cart pods draw the aggressive panhandling crews and the Venmo and Cash App street scam, where a $5 "sports team donation" ends with someone transferring $2,500 off your unlocked phone. Bikes get cut from racks on Alberta and Hawthorne, and the counterfeit goods scam sets up on the sidewalks just outside the vetted Saturday Market footprint at Waterfront Park.

For a first-timer, the safe defaults are simple. Leave your car empty and free of visible clutter — a jacket or charging cable is enough to invite a smashed window — and lean on TriMet MAX or rideshare to reach trailheads and downtown instead of parking a tagged rental. Pay for parking only through ParkMobile or SpotHero, never at a hand-lettered private lot. Never hand your unlocked phone to a stranger, and book lodging exclusively through Airbnb, VRBO, or Booking.com's native payment system — never a wire or Venmo to a "host" directly.
