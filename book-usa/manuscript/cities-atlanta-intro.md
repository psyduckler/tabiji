Atlanta's scam profile is less about pickpockets and more about money: of the six common schemes documented here, the dangerous ones are financial cons that separate you from cash before you ever set foot on a sidewalk. Two carry the highest severity — the "US Customs and Border Protection" phone scam, where a spoofed 404 number claims a package in your name was seized at the airport and demands $1,000 to $5,000 by Zelle or gift card, and Atlanta Airbnb & convention-season rental fraud, which floods Facebook Marketplace with phantom Midtown apartments during Dragon Con, Music Midtown, and the SEC Championship. The rest are markup and tout schemes: overpriced tickets, padded fares, and tourist-menu pricing. There's no high-crime mugging epidemic in these pages — the threat is paperwork, screens, and smooth talkers.

The clusters are predictable. Hartsfield-Jackson, the busiest airport on the planet, is ground zero: unlicensed "black car" touts work baggage claim with flat $60–$120 quotes to downtown, and fake MARTA agents hawk overpriced passes inside the terminal. The second hotspot is the tourist core around Centennial Olympic Park and Pemberton Place, where Georgia Aquarium & Coca-Cola museum reseller scams push $75–$120 tickets for a $49.95 aquarium, and touts intercept families before the booths. The third is the city's heritage and food trail — Sweet Auburn, where touts charge $30–$80 for "private tours" of the entirely free MLK Center and Ebenezer Baptist Church, and Ponce City Market & the BeltLine, where atmosphere comes at a 40–60% markup over neighborhood prices.

Three defaults beat almost everything here. From the airport, skip the curb entirely: the MARTA Red or Gold Line runs ATL to Five Points downtown in 20 minutes for $2.50. Buy every attraction ticket only at the official domain — georgiaaquarium.org, citypass.com — and scroll past the sponsored Google results. And never pay a "host," "officer," or tout by Zelle, Venmo, or gift card; CBP communicates by USPS mail, and legitimate rentals book through the platform with payment protection intact.
