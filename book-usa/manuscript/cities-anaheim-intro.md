Anaheim is a one-resort town, and its scams orbit the same gravity well: every one of the six hustles documented here exists because Disneyland draws millions of cost-conscious families who don't know where Disney's authority ends and the open city begins. The risk is rarely violent and almost never about pickpockets — it's financial, built on price confusion and "official"-sounding language. Ticket fraud, padded accommodation costs, and bogus parking dominate the list, with the single high-severity threat being resale ticket scams that can vaporize a few hundred dollars before you ever reach a turnstile.

The clearest cluster is the Downtown Disney esplanade, the public walkway between the hotels and the gates. Because it sits outside Disney's jurisdiction, it's open season for the "Parrot Scammer" and the Downtown Disney photo hustle — operators drape a parrot or iguana on your kid's shoulder, snap a picture, then demand $20–$40 and turn aggressive if you balk. The second cluster is the resort perimeter itself: Harbor Blvd, Katella Ave, and Disneyland Drive, where the Disney-area parking and off-Disney shuttle scam pitches "$15 Disney parking" with a phantom free shuttle that runs every 60–90 minutes, and where resort-fee and short-term-rental booking fraud hides $20–$45 nightly fees behind cheap headline room rates. The third is the indoor variant — hotel lobbies and concierge desks running the Magic Key and Lightning Lane upsell, fake VIP tours, and the timeshare presentation that trades a "free Disney ticket" for three to five hours of pressure.

For a first-timer, three defaults cover most of it. Buy tickets only at disneyland.com or the gate booth — anything $50 or more below that price is fraudulent no matter how real the QR code looks. Park in Disney's own Mickey & Friends or Toy Story structures at $35/day, and pay any later citation only at anaheim.net, never via a windshield QR code. And outside the gates, refuse every unsolicited photo, "free ticket," or concierge upsell before it starts — Disney's real character meets are inside the parks and included with admission.
