San Antonio's scam scene is built almost entirely on its own tourism gravity. Across six documented schemes, none involves pickpockets or street muggers — the city's hustles are markups, misdirection, and paperwork. Half are classic tourist traps clustered where the crowds are thickest, with the rest split across the airport, the rental-car counter, and short-term rentals during Fiesta. The pattern is consistent: the closer you stand to a marquee landmark, the more you'll be quietly overcharged for things that are cheaper — or free — a ten-minute walk away.

The River Walk is the epicenter. The San Antonio Riverwalk Restaurant Overcharge & "Scenic" Menu Markup runs prices 30–60% over comparable in-town spots, tacks on undisclosed auto-gratuity and "river view" fees, and pushes $40 "VIP" cruise packages when the official narrated boat tour is $15 direct from riosanantonio.com. A few blocks away at Alamo Plaza, the Alamo Mission "Ticket" & Historic Site Tour Confusion preys on people who don't realize the Alamo is free — third-party sites sell fake "skip-the-line" tickets, and curb touts hawk $40 Alamo-plus-River-Walk "combo tours" bundling two free attractions. The trouble extends well past downtown: the SAT Airport Rideshare, Taxi & "Airport Parking" Tow Scam includes off-airport lots that tow your car after 24 hours and demand $300–$800 cash, the Rental Car "Damage Claim" & Alamo Upsell Scam can hit weeks later with $2,000–$3,000 invoices for phantom scratches, and the Texas Hill Country "Texas Winos" Wine Tour Scam takes Groupon money for Fredericksburg tours that never materialize.

For a first-timer, a few defaults cover most of it. Book the River Cruise and your free timed-entry Alamo tickets directly from the official sites, never a reseller. From the airport, take Uber, Lyft, or VIA Route 5 with a fare screenshot, and refuse anyone soliciting at baggage claim. Park only in on-airport SAT lots. If you rent a car, shoot a narrated video walk-around at pickup. And book lodging — especially during Fiesta in late April — only through Airbnb, VRBO, or Booking.com, never by Zelle or wire.
