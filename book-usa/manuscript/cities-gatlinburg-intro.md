Gatlinburg's scam risk is best described as low-violence but high-dollar: of the six scams documented here, none involves a stranger picking your pocket, yet three are rated high severity because they can cost you hundreds to tens of thousands of dollars in a single afternoon. The pattern is driven by the town's economics. This is a national-park gateway built almost entirely around out-of-state visitors, many of them retirees bringing grandkids to the Smokies, and the predators have organized accordingly. Two of the worst scams here are timeshare hustles, one is a predatory tow operation, and the rest are tourist-strip overcharges, a national-park parking-pass racket, and off-platform cabin-rental fraud. The common thread: high-pressure or fine-print traps aimed squarely at people who only pass through once.

The danger clusters in three zones. Along the Gatlinburg Parkway (US-441) and the Pigeon Forge malls, promoters run the "VacationVIP" & Free Tickets Timeshare Presentation Hustle, dangling "free Dollywood tickets" or a "free cabin stay" to funnel you into a Wyndham, Bluegreen, or Westgate sales office where a "90-minute tour" becomes a six-hour push for a $10,000-plus purchase. The same Parkway hides private lots wired for the Everything Auto predatory tow scam, where a car parked five minutes in an unmarked "open" lot reappears at a yard fifteen miles out demanding $300 to $700 in cash. And at the Great Smoky Mountains National Park gates, third-party sites and fake "attendants" sell the GSMNP Parking Tag and Fake Park Permit at ten times the real $5 price.

Three defaults protect a first-timer. Refuse every "free ticket" offer that requires a presentation, and buy Dollywood passes direct at dollywood.com. Park only in city-operated lots like the Gatlinburg City Parking Garage, and never in a fine-print "customers only" lot. And buy the Smoky Mountain Parking Tag only at a visitor center or smokiesinformation.org, just as you should book any cabin only through Airbnb or VRBO platform payment, never by Zelle.
