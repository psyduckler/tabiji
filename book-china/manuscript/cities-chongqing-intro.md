Chongqing — the mountain megacity at the Yangtze and Jialing confluence — has a scam scene shaped by two things: its role as the primary embarkation point for Yangtze / Three Gorges cruises, and the explosive tourist growth of photogenic "old-city" spots like **Hongya Cave** (洪崖洞) and **Ciqikou Ancient Town** (磁器口). What was a quiet inland transit hub ten years ago is now one of China's top-five domestic tourism destinations, and its tourist-quarter scams have scaled with the volume.

The **Three Gorges cruise pier-side tout scam** is the highest-dollar-value risk. Outside the Chaotianmen cruise port and at the Chongqing Cruise Center (重庆朝天门游轮中心), walking touts sell "same-day upgraded-cabin tickets" on legitimate cruise lines (Victoria Cruises, Yangtze Gold, Chongqing New Century) at 50-100% markups. The cabin often exists but has been downgraded or is on a departure two days later; refunds from a pier-side tout are effectively impossible. Book cruises through Victoria Cruises' own website or through Trip.com at least two weeks before arrival.

The risk radiates out in three corridors. **Hongya Cave and Ciqikou hotpot restaurants** quote "tourist menus" at 15-20% markup over the Mandarin menu on the same counter; hotpot specifically is susceptible to "service fee" line items of 50–120 RMB that aren't disclosed. **Chongqing Jiangbei International Airport (CKG) taxi detours** add 80–150 RMB per ride by taking the slow road rather than the expressway, especially on 25–40 RMB metered runs that should clock at 80–140 RMB — foreign passengers are the usual targets. And the **Liziba "light-rail-through-the-building"** photo-spot touts at the Liziba station demand 50–100 RMB for photos they take of tourists without asking, then block the subway-station exit until the tourist pays.

A safe default: take Chongqing Metro Lines 1/2/3 everywhere (1 yuan-per-ride); from CKG airport, take the Metro Line 10 to central Chongqing (8 RMB) or the official airport taxi rank; eat hotpot at chain locations (Hai Di Lao, Xiao Long Kan, Shu Jiu Xiang) with posted menus and explicit service-fee line items; book Three Gorges cruises only through the official cruise-line websites or top-tier OTAs; ignore every Liziba photo-tout approach; and call **110** or the Chongqing tourist-police line at **+86 23 6351 6161** from inside a subway-station supervisor's office if an approach escalates.
