Harbin — northeast China's ice city, former Russian-railway hub, and host to the Harbin International Ice and Snow Festival — has a seasonally-concentrated scam scene. Between December and February, when the Ice and Snow World draws a million domestic and foreign visitors, volume spikes dramatically. Outside the festival season Harbin is quiet. Volume is concentrated at the **Harbin Ice and Snow World** (冰雪大世界) entrance on Sun Island, along **Central Street** (中央大街, *Zhōngyāng Dàjiē*) in the former Russian concession, and at the **Harbin Taiping International Airport (HRB)**.

The **Ice and Snow World ticket markup scam** runs only during January and February. The official festival ticket sold through the event website or at the Sun Island box office costs 330–500 RMB for adult daytime entry depending on the month. Touts clustered at the bus-stop approaches and along Songbei Tourism Road sell same-day entry at 800-1,100 RMB; the ticket is genuine but the markup is 2-3×. The Ice and Snow World has an official "scan-the-QR-on-the-stanchion" check-in system that confirms ticket-authenticity — foreigners should verify the QR purchase route before leaving the hotel.

The risk radiates out in three corridors. **Russian "amber" and "fur" shops on Central Street** sell plastic amber and synthetic fur at real-amber and real-fur prices; real Russian amber carries a Russian Gemological Association certification that can be looked up by number, and real fur has a felt-side texture a blended synthetic lacks. Central Street has several honest state-run Russian-goods shops (the **Qiulin Group** chain, in particular), recognizable by the Qiulin red logo on the storefront. **Central Street Russian-cuisine tourist menus** overcharge for borscht-and-dumplings sets at 150–250 RMB that are 60–90 RMB on the same restaurant's Mandarin menu; always ask for the 中文菜单. And the **Harbin Taiping Airport (HRB) taxi flat-rate tout** quotes 200–280 RMB for metered runs that clock at 100–140 RMB.

A safe default: buy Ice and Snow World tickets only through the **festival's official WeChat Mini-Program** (冰雪大世界 mini-program inside WeChat) or through Trip.com; take Airport Express Line 1 from HRB (20 RMB, 40 minutes) or Harbin Metro Line 2 once it connects further; buy Russian amber and fur only at the Qiulin chain on Central Street; eat Russian cuisine at the **Moderne (马迭尔) hotel restaurant** or the **Lu Lin (露林)** — both with posted English-language menus; and call **110** or **12301**. The Harbin tourist-police desk is at Central Street's north intersection with Jing Wei Avenue.
