Xi'an is the ancient capital — the start of the Silk Road, home to the Terracotta Warriors, and wrapped inside the best-preserved Ming-dynasty city wall in China. Its scam ecosystem is concentrated in three places: the **Muslim Quarter** (回民街, Huímín Jiē) running north from the Bell Tower, the **North Railway Station (Xī'ān Běi Zhàn)** where high-speed rail arrivals meet the city, and the access corridor to the **Terracotta Warriors Museum** in Lintong District.

The **Muslim Quarter "auntie-tour"** is the volume approach. Friendly older women ("auntie" — 阿姨, *āyí*) who speak unexpectedly good English offer to "show you the real Muslim Quarter" for free. The tour wanders through the food lanes for 15-20 minutes and ends at a single calligraphy shop or a single "imperial jade" shop where the auntie earns a kickback. The shop presents laminated "certificates" naming the piece as imperial-era or as carrying an "AMR appraisal"; the certificate is printed in-house. Prices run 800-8,000 RMB for pieces that a reputable Beijing or Shanghai jade market would price at 50–500 RMB.

The risk radiates out in three corridors. The **Xi'an North Railway Station minibus-tour scam** is the most common transport trap: freelance minibus drivers at the station exit quote 200–300 RMB per person for a "Terracotta Warriors day tour" that substitutes four commission-shop stops (jade, silk, tea, "imperial medicine") for meaningful time at the actual site; the official combined-entry public bus to the Terracotta Warriors (Bus 游5 / 306, *yóu 5 / sān líng liù*) departs from Fangzhicheng Coach Station (纺织城客运站) at 7 RMB and takes 80 minutes; a short Metro Line 9 transfer connects Fangzhicheng to downtown Xi'an. The **Bell Tower and Drum Tower area fake-ticket reseller** sells Muslim Quarter "cultural performance" tickets to events that do not exist, or that are canceled without refund. And the **riverside seafood-and-noodle per-jīn weighing trick** bills 1,000–1,400 RMB for a "small" fish that turned out to be 1.5-1.8 jīn (750-900g).

A safe default: take the official Xi'an Metro Line 2 to the Bell Tower and Line 4 to the North Railway Station; buy Terracotta Warriors tickets only through **the official site (bmy.com.cn)** or Trip.com; refuse every English-speaking Muslim Quarter approach; buy calligraphy and jade only from the large Cui Hua Tang department stores or from the Shaanxi Cultural Relics state-owned stores (not from small Muslim Quarter shops); and call **110** or **12301** from inside a Muslim Quarter or Bell Tower area chain-store (Starbucks at the Bell Tower is a reliable anchor). Xi'an PSB's tourist-police booth is at the Bell Tower subway underground concourse, signposted in English.
