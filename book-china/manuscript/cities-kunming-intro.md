Kunming — Yunnan's provincial capital and the "City of Eternal Spring" — is the launching point for most Yunnan itineraries (Dali, Lijiang, Shangri-La, Xishuangbanna). Its scam scene is narrower than Beijing or Shanghai but concentrated where the most foreign visitors pass through: the **Kunming Changshui International Airport (KMG)** and the adjoining **Kunming South Railway Station**, the **Stone Forest** (石林, Shílín) UNESCO site an hour east, and the historic **Green Lake** (翠湖) park downtown.

The **Stone Forest "ethnic-minority guide" tout scam** is the volume risk. Women in Sani-minority costume at the Stone Forest entrance approach foreign tourists with "I'm your tour guide today" openings and walk alongside for 20-30 minutes through the karst pinnacle formations, explaining Sani legends. At the end of the tour they demand 300–500 RMB per person in cash; they are not employed by the scenic area, and the visit does not require a guide (the park provides Mandarin-English bilingual signage at every major viewpoint). The Yunnan Cultural and Tourism Department posts warnings in English at the Stone Forest north gate and at the Stone Forest Bus Station.

The risk radiates out in three corridors. **KMG airport and Kunming South Railway Station minibus "Dali + Lijiang tour" substitutions** — freelance operators offer "4-day Yunnan tour" packages at 1,200–1,800 RMB per person that substitute one-day kickback-shop circuits for the advertised multi-day itinerary; legitimate Yunnan tours book through Trip.com or Klook at 2,500–4,500 RMB per person for comparable real itineraries. The **Green Lake tea-house tourist-menu** — small tea houses on the lakeshore charge 200–400 RMB per pot for what a nearby locals' tea house sells at 30–60 RMB per pot. And the **"Pu'er tea" pressed-cake "aged" scam** — shops at the Jinbi Square tourist quarter sell generic Yunnan tea pressed into cake form at 1,500–4,000 RMB per cake, claiming 20-year-aged provenance; real aged Pu'er comes from audited warehouses and carries an encoded QR traceability code from Yunnan's Tea Association.

A safe default: take the Kunming Metro Line 6 from KMG airport (6 RMB, 38 minutes to downtown) or the high-speed-rail transfer from Kunming South Railway Station; take the dedicated Stone Forest Tourist Bus from Kunming East Bus Station (34 RMB one-way, 70 minutes each way) rather than a freelance-minibus tour; buy Pu'er tea only from the **China Tea (中茶)** state-owned chain or the Yunnan Tea Cooperative stores; decline every "I'm your guide" approach at scenic areas; and call **110** or **12301**. Kunming's tourist-police desk is at the Stone Forest north gate, signposted in English.
