Hangzhou — capital of Zhejiang Province, UNESCO West Lake, and the birthplace of Longjing (Dragon Well) green tea — runs one of mainland China's more contained scam scenes, concentrated almost entirely around the tea-country villages west of West Lake. Volume is at the **Longjing Village** tea-farm tourist zone, along the **West Lake lakeshore** (especially at the Broken Bridge and Su Causeway piers), in the historic **Hefang Street** (河坊街) pedestrian quarter, and at the **Lingyin Temple** (灵隐寺) complex.

The **Longjing tea-village per-gram overcharge** is the signature risk. Freelance tea-farm operators along the road leading from West Lake into Longjing Village offer "free Longjing tea tastings" that end with a scripted pitch for 1,200–3,000 RMB canisters of what is presented as "first-picking Mingqian Longjing" but is generally later-season or non-Longjing-region tea. The Zhejiang Provincial Administration for Market Regulation has sampled and sanctioned several village-entrance tea shops in 2024-2025 reports; the *Xinhua* Hangzhou desk has covered the practice. Real first-picking Mingqian Longjing sold through the official **China Tea (中茶)** chain or the **Artisan Tea Cooperative** state shops at West Lake's Xihu Tianda and at Longjing Village itself runs 800-1,500 RMB per jīn.

The risk radiates out in three corridors. The **West Lake private-boat quotes that double mid-trip** — dock-side operators quote 200–400 RMB for a "1-hour private boat" that becomes 400–800 RMB once you're on the water, citing "fuel" or "overtime" charges the tourist has no way to verify. The **Hefang Street "pure silk" shops** sell polyester-and-silk blends labelled as 100% silk at 4-5× the fabric's actual value. And the **Lingyin Temple "blessing donation"** approach — freelance non-clerical operators in monk-like robes near the temple's Feilai Feng (Peak Flown From Afar) grottoes ask foreign tourists to receive a "blessed jade pendant" in exchange for a "donation" of 500-2,000 RMB; real Lingyin monks do not solicit cash.

A safe default: take the Hangzhou Metro Line 19 from the Hangzhou East Railway Station to West Lake; use the official West Lake electric-boat service (clearly signposted, posted pricing) or the regulated pier boats marked with the official Zhejiang tourism QR code; buy Longjing tea only at the official China Tea shop or at Longjing Village's cooperative store (not from roadside stalls); decline every "blessed pendant" offer at Lingyin Temple; ignore "private boat" approaches at non-official docks; and call **110** or **12301**. Hangzhou's tourist-police desk is at West Lake's Tourist Service Center on Nanshan Road.
