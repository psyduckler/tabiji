Guilin — the gateway city to the Li River karst landscape — handles the majority of Li River cruise traffic, and its scam scene is shaped by that single tourist flow. Volume is at the **Guilin North Railway Station** (桂林北站) where high-speed rail arrivals meet freelance minibus touts, at the **Reed Flute Cave** (芦笛岩) scenic area, at the Guilin Liangjiang International Airport (KWL), and at the downtown piers where Li River cruises embark.

The **North Railway Station freelance-minibus "Li River tour" substitution** is the headline scam. Freelance drivers outside the station exit approach tourists with English signs offering "Li River Cruise Day Tour" at 200–350 RMB per person. The promised cruise is a 4-hour large-boat trip from Mopanshan pier to Yangshuo. What the tourist actually gets: a 70-minute rubber-raft trip on the Yulong River (a Li River tributary that does not reach Yangshuo), a stop at two commission shops ("imperial jade," "tea master"), and a dropoff in central Guilin at 4 PM with no refund. The legitimate Li River cruise is booked through official operators (China Li River Cruise — 中国漓江游船) at the Mopanshan pier ticket office and is clearly marked by class: 3-star boats at roughly 215 RMB, 4-star boats at roughly 360–480 RMB for the full 4-hour genuine route.

The risk radiates out in three corridors. **Reed Flute Cave "photo-guide" freelancers** attach to visitors inside the cave offering to photograph them at the famous illuminated sections and demanding 100–200 RMB per photo afterward; the cave has its own (optional) 30-RMB photo service at the official Crystal Palace viewing point. The **Guilin Liangjiang Airport (KWL) taxi flat-rate quotes** run 200–300 RMB for a metered 90–120 RMB ride into central Guilin. And the **Yangshuo-bound fake-minibus** substitution — freelance buses at Guilin North Railway Station quote 45–60 RMB for the 1.5-hour run to Yangshuo and route through kickback shops along the way; the regulated Guilin-Yangshuo bus from the **Qinxing Bus Station** (琴兴汽车站) is 40 RMB express and goes direct.

A safe default: take an official DiDi from Guilin North Railway Station (5-8 minutes to central Guilin); buy Li River cruise tickets only at the Mopanshan pier ticket office or through Trip.com / Klook; refuse every freelance-minibus "tour" offer; ignore Reed Flute Cave photo-guide touts; and call **110** or **12301** from inside a hotel lobby if an approach pressures. For Yangshuo, take the express bus from Qinxing Bus Station or a fast train from Guilin West Railway Station to Yangshuo Station (15-minute trip, 30 RMB). Guilin's tourist-police desk is at the entrance of the Elephant Trunk Hill scenic area, signposted in English.
