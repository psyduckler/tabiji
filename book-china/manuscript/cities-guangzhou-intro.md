Guangzhou — trade capital of southern China, Cantonese-food heartland, and the primary business-travel gateway to the Pearl River Delta — sustains a scam ecosystem focused on English-speaking business visitors rather than leisure tourists. The concentration is at **Baiyun International Airport (CAN)**, the historic foreign-concession island of **Shamian** (沙面), the **Beijing Road Pedestrian Street**, and the **Shangxiajiu Pedestrian Street** dim-sum district.

The **Baiyun Airport (CAN) freelance-taxi flat-rate scam** is the volume approach. The regulated airport-to-downtown fare from CAN to Tianhe or the Pearl River is 90–130 RMB metered. Freelancers approach arrivals inside Terminal 1 and 2 with "300 RMB, no meter" offers; the regulated rank is clearly signposted and uses a supervisor-queue system. The Airport Express subway (Line 3 extended) runs to Tiyu Xilu in 55 minutes for 8 RMB.

The risk radiates out in three corridors. The **Shamian Island walking-tour-and-gallery circuit** targets English-speaking visitors staying at Shamian-adjacent five-star hotels (White Swan, Swiss Grand) with unsolicited "historical walking tour" offers that route through a single art or silk gallery with kickbacks. The **Beijing Road Pedestrian Street "art student" invitation** is the Wangfujing Beijing script ported to Guangzhou, same venue-type, same 2,000–4,000 RMB catalog close. And the **Shangxiajiu dim-sum overcharge** bills a "table fee" (台位费) and a "tea fee" (茶位费) on top of the per-basket menu price, adding 50–150 RMB to any modest meal; both charges are legal in Cantonese dim-sum culture but are often unpublicised on tourist-oriented venues at 2-3× the local rate.

A safe default: take the Airport Express subway (Line 3) from CAN, or the official airport taxi rank; book DiDi for city rides; ignore Shamian walking-tour and Beijing Road gallery approaches; eat dim-sum at chain venues (Tao Tao Ju, Lian Xiang Lou, Guangzhou Restaurant) with posted menus — expect a ~10 RMB *chá wèi fèi* per person as normal; confirm any hotel booking's total in RMB through WeChat or a hotel confirmation screenshot before arrival; and call **110** or the Guangzhou tourist-complaint line at **+86 20 962 020** from inside a lobby if an approach turns physical. Guangzhou's tourist-police desk at Shamian Island is at the corner of Shamian Street and Huadi Boulevard.
