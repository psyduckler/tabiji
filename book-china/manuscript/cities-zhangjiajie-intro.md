Zhangjiajie — the sandstone-pillar national park in Hunan that inspired the floating-mountain landscape of *Avatar* — has an atypical scam scene because the park itself is well-regulated, but the approach to the park is dominated by freelance "tour package" touts. Volume is at **Zhangjiajie Hehua International Airport (DYG)**, the **Zhangjiajie Railway Station**, the **Tianmen Mountain cable-car base station** in Zhangjiajie City, and the **Wulingyuan Scenic Area access villages**.

The **combined-ticket confusion scam** is the signature risk. The Zhangjiajie National Forest Park (张家界国家森林公园) charges a 227-RMB four-day peak-season pass that covers the park itself, the shuttle buses inside, and most (but not all) gondola and elevator rides. The Bailong Elevator and the Yuanjiajie-to-Tianzi Mountain cable car require separate tickets (72 RMB one-way and ~65 RMB one-way as of this edition). Tianmen Mountain (the glass-skywalk peak above Zhangjiajie City) is a separate park at roughly 285 RMB, which includes the main cable car, the fast-line cable, shuttle bus, and upper escalator. Freelance touts at DYG airport and the railway station sell "Avatar Mountain Combined Pass" packages at 800-1,400 RMB claiming to cover everything — the packages routinely substitute one-day visits for the advertised four-day park access and miss at least one of Bailong, the cable car, or Tianmen.

The risk radiates out in three corridors. **"Ethnic village performance" tickets** sold by touts at Wulingyuan access villages promise a Tujia-minority song-and-dance show that is canceled or empty by the time visitors arrive; real Tujia cultural performances run at the Wulingyuan's official Zhangjiajie Daxiangguo venue with posted schedules. **Tianmen Mountain "skywalk photographer" fees** — tourists walking the glass skywalk are photographed by "official photographers" who demand 80–200 RMB per photo after delivery; the Tianmen cable-car station has an official photo service at the summit at 60 RMB per photo. And the **DYG minibus "all-day Avatar tour" substitution** — freelancers at Zhangjiajie Hehua Airport offer "all-day Avatar Mountain tour" at 350–500 RMB per person that substitutes souvenir-shop stops for park time.

A safe default: fly into DYG and take the airport shuttle bus (25 RMB, 50 minutes to Zhangjiajie City) or the high-speed rail from Changsha to Zhangjiajie West Station (ZX) and then a DiDi; buy Zhangjiajie National Forest Park + Bailong Elevator + cable car tickets only at the park's official Wulingyuan ticket counter or through Trip.com / Klook (the park's separate-ticket structure is transparent on these platforms); buy Tianmen Mountain tickets only at the official Tianmen Mountain Cable Car ticket office in central Zhangjiajie City; refuse every DYG airport or railway-station "combined tour" offer from touts; and call **110** or **12301**. Zhangjiajie's tourist-police desk is at the Wulingyuan Scenic Area south gate and at the Tianmen Mountain cable-car station.
