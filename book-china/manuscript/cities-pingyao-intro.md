Pingyao — UNESCO World Heritage Ming-Qing walled city in central Shanxi — runs a quieter scam scene than the major-capital cities. Volume is modest by mainland standards and concentrated on three elements: the **combined-ticket / individual-attraction ticket confusion**, Pingyao-style courtyard-hotel (客栈, *kèzhàn*) pricing surprises on check-in, and the area around the famous **Qiao Family Courtyard** (乔家大院) outside town.

The **combined-ticket duplication scam** is the headline. The official Pingyao "Walled City Pass" (平遥古城通票) costs 125 RMB and covers 22 attractions inside the wall for two days, including the city wall itself, the Rishengchang bank, and most Ming-Qing courtyards. Freelance touts outside the Pingyao Railway Station and at the Ancient City's north gate sell "Pingyao all-access" upgraded tickets at 250–350 RMB that duplicate coverage — they promise entry to things already in the 125-RMB pass, plus a "free guide" that isn't free. The official pass is sold at six clearly-marked ticket booths, each with green tourism-bureau signage in English and Mandarin.

The risk radiates out in three corridors. **Courtyard-hotel upgrade surprises** — small courtyard inns booked through WeChat or a domestic-Chinese site sometimes add 100–300 RMB per night at check-in for an "upgraded courtyard view" or "first-floor fee" not disclosed on the original booking; book only through Trip.com or Booking.com, which enforce single-price contracts. **Antique-shop calligraphy-scroll "hand-painted" claims** — vendors along the West Street (西街) shopping lane sell printed reproductions for 400-2,000 RMB as "hand-painted originals"; a real hand-painted scroll from a Shanxi calligraphy master has visible brush-texture under magnification and costs 3,000–15,000 RMB with a formal provenance receipt. And the **Qiao Family Courtyard "photographer-guide"** freelancer scene — touts at the courtyard entrance demand unposted 100–200 RMB "photographer fees" for following-and-shooting visitors who never asked.

A safe default: take a taxi or DiDi directly from Pingyao Railway Station to your hotel (the station is 6 km from the ancient city — walking is unsafe at night); buy the 125-RMB Pingyao pass at one of the six official booths (printed confirmation in English and Mandarin; booths are at each of the four old gates plus two central points); book accommodation only through Trip.com, Booking.com, or Agoda; buy calligraphy at the state-run Shanxi Cultural Relics store on North Street rather than West Street; and call **110** or the Pingyao Tourism Bureau hotline at **+86 354 568 9000**. Pingyao's tourist-police desk is at the East Gate ticket-checkpoint, signposted in English.
