Lijiang — UNESCO World Heritage Old Town, Naxi-minority cultural capital, and the base for Jade Dragon Snow Mountain day trips — has a scam scene shaped by overnight domestic-tourist volume. Volume is in the **Lijiang Old Town (Dayan)** near Sifang Street (四方街) and along the Old Town's canal-laced lanes, at the Jade Dragon Snow Mountain scenic-area access road, and at the Lijiang Sanyi International Airport (LJG).

The **Old Town tea-house "free entry / 400-RMB minimum" scam** is the volume risk. Many Old Town tea houses advertise "free Naxi-style tea ceremony" on chalkboards outside; the minimum per-person spend (最低消费, *zuìdī xiāofèi*) is disclosed in small Mandarin characters inside, typically 400–600 RMB per person, and includes a forced-purchase "tea package" that is already-stacked on your table when you sit down. Legitimate Naxi tea houses (the kind local families actually visit) have no minimum, display the per-cup price in Mandarin and English on the wall, and charge 30–80 RMB per cup for serious-quality Pu'er.

The risk radiates out in three corridors. **Jade Dragon Snow Mountain (玉龙雪山) combined-ticket touts** outside the Old Town's north gate sell "all-inclusive" packages at 700–900 RMB that substitute a rushed glacier-park visit and three souvenir-shop stops for the advertised "Blue Moon Valley + Glacier Park + Impression Lijiang" itinerary; the official combined ticket from the Jade Dragon Snow Mountain scenic-area office is 390 RMB and takes ~6 hours. The **Naxi-dress photo-costume touts around Sifang Street** rent traditional costumes for 30–60 RMB per hour on posted signs but demand 200–400 RMB at "checkout" claiming the photographer's service was separately billed. And the **LJG Airport flat-rate taxi** scam — 300–400 RMB for a 90–130 RMB metered ride into Old Town.

A safe default: walk through the Old Town's free public paths (Mu Palace and Black Dragon Pool require separate tickets; Mu Palace is 55 RMB, Black Dragon Pool free when Lijiang maintenance fee has been paid); buy the Jade Dragon Snow Mountain ticket only at the scenic-area's official Mu Chu ticket office or through Klook/Trip.com (never from Old Town touts); rent Naxi costumes only from shops with posted per-hour prices and a "no photographer surcharge" sign; take DiDi from LJG airport or the official airport-taxi rank; and call **110** or **12301**. Lijiang's Old Town PSB sub-station is on Xinhua Street.
