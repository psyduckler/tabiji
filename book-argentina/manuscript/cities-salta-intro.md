Salta "la Linda" (the Beautiful) is the capital of Salta Province, Argentina's cultural heart of the northwest, and — with a population of 620,000 — a manageable base for exploring the pre-Colombian altiplano. The city's Plaza 9 de Julio remains one of the country's most photographed colonial plazas: the pink-facade Catedral Basílica on the north, the 18th-century Cabildo on the west, and a ring of palo-borracho trees around a bronze Belgrano. From here, a standard northwest circuit runs north to **Quebrada de Humahuaca** in Jujuy Province (a UNESCO heritage corridor), south to the **Valles Calchaquíes** and the boutique wine region of **Cafayate**, and east to the **Tren a las Nubes** — the legendary Train to the Clouds that climbs the Andean escarpment to 4,220 meters at La Polvorilla viaduct.

Tourist-scam exposure in Salta is narrower than in Buenos Aires or Córdoba but has two distinct high-intensity clusters. The first is the **Tren a las Nubes**: the state operator prices foreign-extranjero fares at ARS 187,500 (roughly USD 150 at 2026 exchange), but downtown travel agencies and international resellers (Viator, daytours4u, GetYourGuide) routinely bundle this into packages at USD 350–500 per person — the canonical example is a TripAdvisor "$700 for 3 persons" complaint that has been in that review ever since 2024. The second is the **Cafayate wine-tour ecosystem**, where hotel-concierge pairings and "premium private tastings" carry 2–4x the direct-winery price.

Inside the city itself, the scam profile is classic: Calle Balcarce restaurant tourist-menu markup on empanadas and tamales (compare Balcarce prices to the Calle Caseros or Mercado San Miguel per-unit rates); SLA airport "flat fare" tout quotes (the legitimate posted rate is EUR 42, roughly USD 45); and a Plaza 9 de Julio cambio-tout cluster that picks up where Florida Avenue and Peatonal Sarmiento leave off. Salta is otherwise among the safer Argentine cities at night; most issues are financial rather than physical.

Emergency: **911**; Comisaría del Turista Salta (Alvarado 581, +54 387 437-2555); English-speaking tourism help via **Ente de Turismo de Salta** (+54 387 431-0950).
