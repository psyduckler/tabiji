San Carlos de Bariloche — usually just "Bariloche" — is the country's Patagonian Alpine jewel, a 110,000-resident town on the south shore of Lake Nahuel Huapi, at the foot of Cerro Catedral, and the eastern gateway to both Andean Patagonia and the Seven Lakes route north to Villa La Angostura and San Martín de los Andes. Tourist traffic is bimodal: July–September ski season at Cerro Catedral (South America's largest ski resort by skiable terrain), and December–April summer season for Lago Nahuel Huapi, Circuito Chico, Mount Tronador, and the chocolate factories along Av. Bartolomé Mitre. Bariloche International Airport (BRC) is fifteen kilometers east of downtown.

Bariloche is also the single worst rental-car smash-and-grab hot-spot in Argentina. The sixty-kilometer Circuito Chico scenic loop — the most-photographed drive in the country — has a half-dozen documented break-in spots: Cerro Campanario, Llao Llao, Punto Panorámico, and Bahía López in particular. Organized crime groups based on the outskirts of Bariloche target rental vehicles parked at viewpoints, break windows in sixty seconds or less, grab anything visible, and disappear before tourists return from the overlook. The Policía de Río Negro acknowledges the pattern publicly. The rental companies pass both the deductible and the glass-replacement cost to the tourist. The defensive habit is absolute: *never leave anything visible in the car at any Circuito Chico viewpoint*. Take every backpack, jacket, and cable with you at every stop. Alternative: use a guided Circuito Chico bus tour (Via Bariloche, Cono Sur Travel) and skip the rental-car trap entirely.

The second layer is the **chocolate-shop markup on Av. Bartolomé Mitre**. Bariloche's Swiss-settler chocolate tradition is real, and the street's big-brand shops (Rapa Nui, Mamuschka, Abuela Goye, Del Turista, Fenoglio) do sell good product — but at "artisan" prices that are 30–50 % higher than the same brands' factory outlets (Rapa Nui Fábrica, Mamuschka Fábrica) ten minutes away by taxi. The signature mistake is treating Av. Mitre as the definitive buy; the factories are the bargain.

Other risks: Cerro Catedral ski-lift "last-run" ticket overcharges; Villa La Angostura summer-rental WhatsApp deposit fraud; and BRC airport rideshare-blocking (smaller than Córdoba, but present).
