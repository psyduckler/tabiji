El Calafate is Santa Cruz Province's Patagonian gateway — a planned tourist town of roughly 25,000 residents built on the south shore of Lago Argentino specifically to service access to Los Glaciares National Park and Perito Moreno Glacier. Comandante Armando Tola International Airport (FTE) is twenty-three kilometers east of the town center; the only way in and out by road is Ruta Nacional 40 heading north to Bariloche or Ruta Provincial 11 out to the park. The town itself is compact: Av. del Libertador San Martín is the main commercial strip, with every restaurant, tour operator, and ice-trek outfitter on a four-block stretch.

The core experience here is **Perito Moreno**, the 250-square-kilometer glacier seventy kilometers west of town that is one of the few advancing glaciers on Earth. Access splits two ways: a day-trip to the park's viewing platforms (three hours of catwalks with free binoculars and the famously ice-calving glacier face) or a full-day ice-trek with **Hielo y Aventura** (the single concessionaire, running both the "Minitrekking" and the longer "Big Ice" products out of Puerto Bandera). Any other operator selling an ice-trek is reselling Hielo y Aventura's inventory at a markup, or outright not delivering the product.

The scam profile in El Calafate reflects its single-industry nature. The first layer is the **third-party ice-trek reseller** who charges USD 350 for an experience Hielo y Aventura sells at ARS 384,000 (roughly USD 260 at 2026 rates) — and who sometimes delivers nothing. Book only via **hieloyaventura.com**. The second is the "park entrance included" tour that turns out to exclude the ARS 45,000 (roughly USD 32) foreigner-extranjero gate fee — always ask explicitly whether the fee is in the quoted price. The third is the **Av. del Libertador restaurant tourist-menu** pattern: the waiter hands you an English menu with prices marked up 40–60 % over the Spanish menu that sits at the bar; ask "*¿Puedo ver el menú en español, por favor?*" Last is the FTE airport "private transfer" overcharge: VES shuttle is USD 5–7 per person; taxi counter is roughly ARS 18,000 per vehicle. Arrivals-hall "private" touts quote USD 33 solo.

Emergency: **911**; Comisaría del Turista El Calafate (+54 2902 491-322); Bomberos **100**.
