If you are victimized, consult local authorities. Argentina's unified emergency line is **911** — English-speaking call-takers are available in the city of Buenos Aires and some provincial capitals. **Policía Federal** reaches the national force directly at **134**. For tourist-specific help in Buenos Aires, the **Comisaría del Turista** (Av. Corrientes 436, CABA, +54 11 4346 5748) operates 24/7 with English-speaking officers; this book lists equivalent provincial contacts city by city in the appendix. Also contact your embassy or consulate, your credit-card issuer, and your travel-insurance provider. Do not rely on this book as your sole source of guidance.
