# About Tabiji {-}

Tabiji is a travel-safety research organization. We document scams, health risks, and local advisories for travelers across more than fifty cities worldwide, and we publish everything as a free public resource at **[tabiji.ai](https://tabiji.ai)**.

We started this series after noticing that most scam roundups online skip the one thing travelers actually need in the moment: the exact phrase, the exact move, that ends the interaction. Remembering a list of sixty-six scams is hard when you are tired and under pressure. Remembering six patterns is much easier. That is the shape we have tried to give this book.

Every scam documented here was drawn from Argentine-press coverage — *Clarín*, *La Nación*, *Página/12*, *Perfil*, *Infobae*, *Rosario/12*, *Los Andes* (Mendoza), *La Gaceta* (Tucumán), *La Voz del Interior* (Córdoba), and the regional dailies of Patagonia and the northwest — cross-referenced against Policía Federal, provincial Policía Turística, Prefectura Naval, and Gendarmería Nacional reports; Defensoría del Consumidor sanction records; and firsthand traveler accounts from online travel communities and city-specific forums. Where we have named perpetrators, arrest dates, or documented peso and dollar amounts, we cite them. Where a pattern has been reported but not formally investigated, we say so.

This edition reflects the 2026 economic landscape — the post-Milei *dolar-blue* collapse, the 2024 introduction of the 20,000-peso banknote, the 2025 legalization of Uber, Cabify, and DiDi in most major cities, and the continuing 2025–2026 wave of COR airport taxi-mafia incidents. We expect to revise substantial portions of this book for the 2027 edition.

For new scams and live updates to the cases in this book, visit **[tabiji.ai/scams](https://tabiji.ai/scams)**. We maintain the database quarterly, and most corrections reach the site within a week of a credible report.

We hope you come home with stories about the bife de chorizo, the Perito Moreno glacier, a Mendoza Malbec, and a San Telmo tango night.

— Tabiji
